# LEDGER: the plan to the friends' build, then the town

Adopted 5 October 2026 from the outside audit's draft (production/audits/2026-10-04-plan-draft.md) with Jafar's edits; it replaces every earlier list. NOW.md carries the current phase.

Numbers are **ceilings and estimates**. W = one weekly allowance: at most 0.70W production and 0.10W review a week; 0.20W is Jafar's; he reads the meter. Each task's time is recorded (tools/timelog.py); phase 1 measures an accepted family's cost and reuse time before town production is extrapolated.

Every exit bundle names the commit and package, raw logs, fixed-camera images, a walking recording and the failures. A fresh model gets the requirements and outputs, never the maker's verdict, and verifies the exit; Jafar makes taste decisions. Assets are done by production/research/pre-production/5-DONE.md.

## Phases, in order

**0. Recover control.** Ceiling 0.20W, two to three working days, nothing bought.
- TOWN.md and CLOTHES.md within their caps; superseded lists and questions retired.
- Recover what exists unseen: every GitHub branch, his drives' project folders, the approval pages' answers, the old game-studio repository; each marked in main, unique and kept, or archivable. Taste or identity (the September atlas, art/atlas-01) reaches him on one page before merging.
- CATALOGUE.md: maps, district plans, concept images, research topics, approved assets; and the homes table, one home per kind of fact (map, cast, simulation rules, the street's people, the look). Disagreements listed, first the built street against the atlas, the C# simulation against the Unreal port, the game's cast against the casting sheets, the walkers against the residents.
- Checks failing the build: a branch over a week old neither merged nor archived; a derived thing disagreeing with its home; new art or research without a catalogue line. Approval answers read back nightly; the morning pictures made by 07:30.
- Every friends-build ruling mapped to one item, owner and packaged proof. Verified: P3's checking under caps, P14's source records, evening limits surviving a restart. One Shipping launch from the shortcut on his own account, first-run preparation included; no second account.
- The history cleaned: its plan and all it touches told to him first; every session stopped.
- **Exit:** a fresh reviewer checks each of these before phase 1. **His checkpoint:** only open money and identity choices.

**1. Prove the bottlenecks.** Ceiling 1.00W, one to two weeks, $1 a day measuring.
- First proof, Mickey's front office: the room Tom walks into, from the room kit, researched first; real glass reflecting the street; a 1990 cab office's wear and clutter, radio set, ashtrays, kettle, telephone, scuffed counter; no glitches. With it one frontage of correct geometry, material and wear, its window of verified CC0 goods, and three fixed street views.
- One complete Tom and one speaker walking, sitting and turning.
- P2, the voice: two measured approaches at most (shared graphics card; CPU or quantised), timed beside checked talk. P1 completed packaged: day, night, walking, Windows memory, sustained speech.
- **Exit:** the samples meet the bar; the render and voice pairing has measured headroom; median first meaningful speech two seconds or less (else state-grounded prepared opening beats are tested; generic thinking sounds do not count).
- **His checkpoint:** one whole-frame and moving-character review; frozen identities preserved.
- **Binding stop:** a sample that cannot pass within this ceiling halts expansion; the failed capability is reported plainly, never dressed as another plan.

**2. Produce Quay Street from proved families.** Ceiling 2.00W, three to four weeks, no new spending.
- From the asset plan (production/research/asset-plan/SUMMARY.md): the approved building kit re-seeded; mapped upper rooms; real near shop rooms; ground, wear, signage, furniture and food, vegetation, the hill's distance versions. Only approved samples multiplied. P7 walking, P6 plain clothes, P20 exposure, P13 ambience. The wet street's seam and icy near lane, and the hill, return here with their research. No cars unless P9 passes.
- **Exit:** a whole-street day and night walk passes the asset, source and budget checks, reverse views and the office camera (P15) included. **His checkpoint:** street, people and ambience, at most three decisions.

**3. Make thirty minutes hold.** Ceiling 0.75W, one to two weeks, the existing talk caps.
- N3 ported, with only the first-week content P4's gaps need: visible arrivals and deliveries, consequence, a useful office and ledger, prompts. Every visible person has a simulation identity. The weather proof (below). Debug keys, typing and collision faults removed.
- **Exit:** two different thirty-minute newcomer routes and one continuous checked-live route within the cap; no critical fault; at most five empty minutes; a consequence seen and kept after restart. **His checkpoint:** he plays thirty minutes and judges it.

**4. Friends' candidate.** Ceiling 0.50W, about a week, at most $5 an evening.
- The package frozen; P5 on his own account and shortcut; source credits; voice and save separate; packaged performance in rain, at night, with speech; the two routes repeated on that package.
- **Exit:** the twenty basics proved by output; no visible placeholder, hole or debug display; no development tools needed. The friends' first thirty minutes logged, blockers fixed before scope is added. **His checkpoint:** appearance and the evening's spend.

**5. First town.** A 1W pilot, then 6 to 12W provisional (three to six months).
- One adjacent block from existing kits; then the 14 principals, about 30 regulars and the population in batches, load-tested at 0, 5, 10 and 20 visible people first; every story or routine tied to playable evidence.
- **Exit per batch:** new-seed variety, weather attendance, witness, memory, reload and performance hold; no unproved family multiplied.

This consolidates P1 to P22 (production/research/pre-production/3-PROOFS.md); P12's face solver only if the conversation sample fails; P17 combat and P18 music deferred. **Friends: eight to twelve weeks, conditional on phase 1**, low confidence; a failed gate voids the date.

**Performance exit.** Median frame at most 16.7 ms, p99 25 ms, dedicated memory 6 GB with the voice on the card, at native output with speech, a wet night and the full population. P1's High at 55% leaves about 2.5 ms by day, 1.4 ms at night: shared, rebalanced at each accepted addition.

**Weather proof.** One saved wetness value driven by dry, drizzle and rain, wetting and drying timed independently; a recorded dry-rain-dry walk with surface, sound and visibility changes; same-seed runs show changed attendance and consistent witnesses, nobody vanishing in view; reloading mid-change reproduces it. No sunshine in the friends' build.

- Sonnet for routine implementation; Opus for bounded diagnosis, planning and each gate's fresh review; Haiku only for mechanical extraction. Models do not solve art.

- Stopped for the builder: wardrobe and town multiplication, whole-street parameter sweeps.
