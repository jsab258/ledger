# LEDGER: the production charter

Adopted 5 October 2026 from the outside audit's draft (production/audits/2026-10-04-charter-draft.md) with Jafar's edits. PLAN.md carries it out; CLAUDE.md says how the work runs.

**Goal.** Thirty minutes of Quay Street that Jafar wants to play and his friends can launch on his PC, on his own account. They accept the street's appearance, see the town remember what they did, and find the conversation responsive.

**Scope.** One continuous playable street, Mickey's usable front office, the three existing conversational principals (Ron, Sheila, Darren), and only the further people and places needed to make the approved first-week beats visible (game-design/first-hour-2026-09-29.md). A deed produces an intelligible later consequence that survives reload. Every visible person takes part in perception and memory unless ruled scenery. No new acts, combat programme, district production or crowd of hundreds before this works.

**Bar.** Hook-quality street composition, materials, depth, wear and atmosphere in the moving game, reverse views included. Approved identities and voices are preserved. Clothes keep their exception and floor exactly as RULINGS.md has them. A static approved picture never approves a broken walk.

**Weather (his ruling).** A grey range: dry overcast, drizzle and rain; the street drying and wetting over time; rain changing who is out, what is heard and what is seen. Sunshine never in the friends' build.

**Constraints.** AI does the implementation; no hiring, new subscriptions or unapproved purchases. Only verified AI-permitted sources; nothing NoAI in the game, the project or the references. One RX 6700 (10 GB), shared with the voice. Each week's allowance: 70% production, 10% independent review, 20% kept for Jafar; he reads the meter. The $1-a-day measurement and $5-a-friends'-evening caps stand. No reset or paid overage is assumed.

**Authority.** Jafar decides taste, identity and money, and gives orders on Mondays, except for a broken game or PC or a deliberate scope change that states what it displaces and its cost. One production session owns implementation, sequencing within the phase, evidence and corrective work; town and clothing work are bounded tasks it hands out.

**Stop condition (binding).** If a representative street-and-person sample cannot pass the bar within its capped proof budget, the failed capability is reported plainly. Failure is never disguised as another plan, and a town is never promised on an unproved method.
