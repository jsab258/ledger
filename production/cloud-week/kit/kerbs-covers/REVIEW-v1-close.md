Review of the close view of kit piece 3.7, Quay Street's kerbs and covers (the dropped crossover, its ramp, flanks and channel). Fresh reviewer, 12 October 2026. I checked the render `close.png` against TARGET.md and target.json, the reduced photographs in production/previews/cloud-week/refs/kerbs-and-covers/ and five Geograph photographs I found myself. To check things the view does not show, I made my own renders of review_scene.glb (from straight above, low beside the left flank, and looking down from the footway) and of each out-of-view piece. I read NOTES.md only after forming this view, and did not read the other reviews.

Verdict: the crossover's shape matches the photographed crossing (urban_street_03) in every main part, and the proportions are right. At this view's distance a player sees nothing wrong. Below are four narrow points, each with an exact fix, plus one class of fault in the pieces the views leave out.

## Narrow points (close view), worst first

1. **The crossover's joints are open to the void, with no fill and no bed (build).** These are the 12 mm joints between the ramp and the lip row (y -124 to -112), between the ramp and both flank strips and end-block ends (x ±1488 to ±1500), the 12 x 140 mm slot between each end block's back and its flank strip's front (y -170 to -182), and every joint between the 19 lip setts. From straight above, with a coloured sky, they show the sky as bright lines (my top render). At this view's low angle they read as dark lines, so they are not visible here. A player standing on the footway and looking down could see light through them if nothing lies below. The target asks for "12 mm bitumen" joints and lip setts "with dark mortar"; the channel has its recessed mortar bed, the crossing has none. Fix: add `bitumen_joint` strips that fill the ramp, flank and end-block joints and the end-block-to-flank slot to 3 mm below the tops beside them. Add a mortar bed under the lip row like the channel's `mortar` object: top at z +4 (8 below the lip top), x ±1.496, y -0.112 to 0.
2. **The left flank's inner face is not splayed (build).** Target 4.4: "the left strip's inner face splayed, a dark band about 40 wide". Both flank strips have vertical inner faces at x ±1.500 (vertices at x -1503 and -1500). The photograph (urban_street_03 crossover-top ortho, left side) shows a dark band along the ramp's left edge that widens toward the kerb. Fix: move `flank_l`'s inner top edge back to x -1.540 and keep its foot at -1.500 (a 40 mm splay), or record in NOTES that the one-of-each choice was dropped.
3. **The setts are too crisp and too regular (target's: it gives no round for the sett arris).** The channel setts are exact rectangles: straight sides, a small chamfer and a low faceted dome. In urban_street_03 (crossover-gully ortho, the two courses under the lip), the channel setts are worn round at every arris, with irregular outlines and joints 10 to 20 wide. Penzance 1988 and St Albans 2022 (below) show the same. At 2.4 m the channel reads a little like new concrete pavers; wear masks will not round a silhouette. Fix: the target should add, and the builder apply, a top-arris round of R 10 on the channel setts (R 5 on the lip row, which the photograph shows crisper), plus a ±4 mm jitter on each sett's plan corners so that no two joints run exactly parallel.
4. **The end arris of each end block kinks (build, small).** The 10 mm round on the end blocks' crossing ends kinks where the face's batter begins (z 45). Seen close from beside the left flank (my render from (9.3, 0.9, 0.75)), this shows as a notch in the highlight on the left end block. Fix: bevel the end edge after the profile is made, with the same segments as the profile, and weight or harden the normals across the kink.

## The pieces the views leave out (my own renders: from straight above and at 45 degrees, each piece set in a cut surface at z 0)

One fault runs through all of them (build): **every opening goes straight through to the void.** This covers the gap between lid and frame, the 15 mm seam between the leaves of the road cover, the key slots of the stopcock and gas lids, the hairline gaps round the infills, and gully grate B's two lifting holes, which lie outside its pot (pot x ±0.158; holes at x ±0.166). With the surface cut round the piece, as the review scene does, all of these show the sky. On the double-leaf road cover a player would see this: a bright line across a cover in the road. The fix is one change: give each cover a closed dark floor, a `pot_black` plate or box under the whole frame opening, 40 to 60 mm below the lid top (the frame's depth). For grate B, widen the pot to x ±0.180, or give each lifting hole a blind bottom 30 mm down.

- **cover_recessed_footway: sound.** Outer 1060 x 600, the outer band level with the flags, the lugged band stepped down, the pale infill 860 x 400 sitting 8 below the flags, three columns of lugs at the left end and two at the right. It matches the urban_street_03 footway-cover photograph: a dark lugged frame round a pale concrete tray.
- **cover_recessed_road: sound in size (646 x 670), with one narrow point (build).** It shows a 35 mm cast-iron band all round (frame ±323 x ±335 against infill ±288 x ±300). The target and the photograph (urban_street_02 tarmac-infill ortho) show only a dark hairline about 10 mm wide in the tarmac, with grass at a corner. Fix: bring the tarmac infill out to within 10 mm of the frame's outer edge (infill ±0.313 x ±0.325), or set the frame top 3 mm under a tarmac skim.
- **cover_road_double_leaf: size and tread right, but it has the seam fault above.** It is 1710 x 570, two leaves with a 15 gap, and a fine 45-degree stud tread. Narrow point (build): the target's 140 mm band of pale mortar round it is not in the piece (the telecom lid carries its own surround). Without it, the cover does not match the urban_street_04 photograph, where a pale band rings the cover. Fix: add a `mortar_pale` band 140 wide at z 0, as on service_telecom.
- **service_stopcock: sound apart from the openings.** Frame 176 across, lid 134, a 30 x 8 slot.
- **service_gas: sound apart from the openings.** Frame 290 x 180, lid 240 x 130, a slot at each end.
- **service_telecom: sound.** Frame 386 x 196, a flag infill 336 x 150 set slightly below the top, a 28 mm pale mortar surround. Only the hairline round the infill is open.
- **gully_grate_B: sound apart from the lifting holes.** It is 356 x 326, with seven 20 mm slots trimmed to 91, 182, 255, 288, 255, 182 and 91, and two round lifting holes on the long axis. This is the oval-trimmed field of the Birbeck Street photograph. The slots read black over the pot; the holes do not.

## What is right (close view)

- **End blocks:** 3.0 m between the end-block faces (x 8.900 to 11.900). The end blocks stop square at full height (115) with the half-battered face and R25 top arris carried to the end. There is no taper block, just the abrupt step of the photographed crossing (urban_street_03 kerb-end-flank view and crossover-top ortho).
- **Lip row:** 19 setts, about 155 along and 112 across, top 12 above the channel. They are blue-grey, pale and one pink-grey, with the front edge on the kerb face line. The first and last setts meet the end blocks' faces with a small joint (PM07 to PM09).
- **Ramp:** one in-situ plane rising from 12 to 110 over 721 (1 in 7.4), its back edge at y -845 meeting the footway at 110 with no step (PM11). It is a flat plane, as in-situ concrete is.
- **Flank strips:** 140 x 663, top 110, laid behind the end blocks and flush with the footway. Their inner faces stand above the ramp as long triangular granite cheeks, about 98 high at the front and nothing at the back. This is the dark band along the ramp's edge in the photograph (PM12).
- **Channel:** two courses (104 + 100 = 204) run unbroken across the crossing and past it, in running bond, with dark joints recessed. The photographed crossing (PM06) and the period photographs from Penzance and Plymouth show the same continuous channel.
- **Levels:** kerb top 115, flags 110, the road's edge at 6. Nothing floats, sinks or overlaps where the view can see it; the end blocks' feet sit in the setts. There is no tactile paving, which is right for 1990.
- **Proportions:** the ramp, the kerb top, the lip and the channel read in the same ratios as the crossover-top ortho of urban_street_03. Once the engine adds speckled concrete, granite and grime, this view would pass as an old British house crossing.
