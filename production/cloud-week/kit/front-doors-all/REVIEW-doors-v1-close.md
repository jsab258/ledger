# Front doors for every house kind (unit 9, part 2), v1: fresh review of the CLOSE view, try 1

Reviewer: a fresh subagent, 13 October 2026. I did not write the target or build the kit, and I did not open the street review. Judged: the 13 close pictures in `production/previews/cloud-week/front-doors-all/` (T1, T2, T2-close_left, T3, F1 ×3, F2, F3, F4, F5, F6, F7; 1600 × 1200, viewed at full size, then cropped and enlarged). References: P1 and P2 (`refs/front-door/`), D1 to D10 (`refs/front-doors-all/`, previews only). Files: the 12 door glbs and 29 fitting glbs, `houses.json`, `target.json`, `check_doors_v1.json`. Pixel places below are in the delivered pictures. My own crops, scripts and three test renders are in the session scratchpad (`rev-close/`), not in git.

In short: the close work is careful and nearly every fitting sits exactly where the target says. One fault in the files, F7's board shading, fails the view. It is a one-line fix.

## Fault

1. **F7's boards are shaded as rounded staves, not flat boards (builder's).** Where: `F7-close`, every board between the joints at x 189, 404, 624 and 846, top to bottom. Along row 300 each board brightens from about 54 at its left joint to 61 at its right (0 to 255), then drops at the next joint. The same ramp repeats on all six boards, so the leaf reads faintly fluted and the V joints read as soft hairlines, not as Vs.

   Cause: in `door_F7.glb` and `door_F7_kept.glb`, each `leaf_board_*` front face carries the averaged normals of its V-joint chamfers, (±0.29, 0, 0.957). That is 17 degrees off the flat face across about 0.28 m² per board. I found this by reading the normals and checking every flat face larger than 100 mm² in all 41 files. Only these boards show it on a large flat face. The mouldings' smaller deviations belong to their curves; I rendered F3's ovolo both ways under the same light and saw no visible difference.

   The engine will import these normals. To test them I lit the F7 close camera with one light raking at 70 degrees, like a street lamp beside the door. As delivered, each board ran from 69 down to 14 across its width: a corrugated sheet. With the boards shaded flat it ran from 41 to 36, and the V joints read as crisp lit and shadowed lines (`rev-close/render/f7_raking_pair.png`). At night under the street's lamps a player will see the stripes.

   **Fix:** in `doors_kinds.py`, split the normals at each board's arrises (mark the edges between the face and the chamfers sharp, or shade the boards flat) for F7 and F7_kept. Then add a GLB row to `check_doors.py` that fails when any planar face over 100 mm² has a vertex normal more than 5 degrees off its face normal. The present `GLB:*:normals` row only checks that normals exist.

## Narrow points (each to be fixed before the next build; none fails the view alone)

1. **T2's small dark fitting on the hinge stile is missing, and the omission is not recorded (builder's).** `target.json` kinds.T2.small_fixings `T2_hinge_fixing` asks for it at u 753, v 1390, 20 × 60, 8 proud, sRGB (40, 40, 42). TARGET.md section 5 lists it as "added". `door_T2.glb` has 23 parts and none of them is this fitting. In `T2-close` that place (x 935 to 965, rows 350 to 450) is plain maroon (median 70, 37, 44). NOTES.md does not mention it, and no check row looks for it. **Fix:** add two dark blocks 20 × 25, 8 proud, 10 apart, inside the 20 × 60 box centred at u 753, v 1390, in steel_dark. Also add an FU row. Otherwise, write the departure into NOTES.md.
2. **LP1 and LP2: the dished flap touches the dark liner along its centre line (builder's; the Rita's-shop class, but only along one line).** The flap's front face dishes from y −3.5 at its ends to −0.5 at its middle, which is exactly the liner's front face (−0.5). On LP2 the two lie within 0.1 mm of each other over 1,024 mm² (x ±13.8 about the centre). On LP1 the strip is narrower. In a rasteriser this can flicker as a dark line down the middle of the brass flap (`F2-close`, x 275 to 535, rows 240 to 305, centre x about 405). The builder's `GLB:F2@east_parade_bay1:no_shared_face_plane` row reports 0. **Fix:** move the liner of LP1 and LP2 to y −0.25 to −0.05, which puts it 0.25 behind the flap's deepest point. Also widen the GLB row to catch faces within 0.1 mm whose normals differ by under 1 degree.
3. **T2's bar pull HD3 reads as a modern matt-black flat bar (builder's Judgement).** In `T2-close_left` (x 775 to 817, rows 355 to 795) it is a flat front face with rounded ends, and its posts and stand-off cannot be seen. That is the look of a recent composite-door pull, not the 1930s pull D3 shows. **Fix:** keep the 25 × 270 overall size. Make the grip 19 mm round (or a 22 × 16 oval) so it carries a highlight along its length, and flare the two posts into 25 mm roses with a 30 mm stand-off.
4. **F7's thumb latch HD4 reads as a modern pull on a rounded plate, not a thumb latch (builder's Judgement).** In `F7-close` (x 880 to 940, rows 375 to 680), the thumb piece is a block on top of the bar. **Fix:** keep the 38 × 190 plate but give its ends a cusped or arrow shape. Make the grip a 19 mm round bow standing 30 off on posts 120 apart. Make the thumb piece a flat lever 14 wide × 45 long that rises through a slot in the plate's head at 15 degrees.
5. **The three levers HD2a, HD2b and HD2c have a straight, flat, parallel-sided bar on a slim neck (builder's Judgement).** This is a 2000s minimal lever, not a late-1980s one. See `T3-close` (x 830 to 995, rows 55 to 125) and `F6-close` (x 750 to 965, rows 425 to 505). **Fix:** taper the bar from 16 at the neck to 12 at the end and curve its last 25 mm back toward the door. Keep the round rose (D5 shows a lever on a rose).
6. **F7's nail heads are black dots on a painted door (builder's).** See `F7-close`, row 545, x 80, 295, 514, 735 and 958. Wrought nails on a painted ledged door are painted over with it. **Fix:** give `iron_nail_heads` the door's paint material, and leave any rust bleed to the wear layer.
7. **The glass is tinted dark in the file, so T2's nets read as slate panels (3.1 kit's material).** The `glass` material has base colour (121, 131, 139) at full transmission. In `T2-close` the four panes have a median of (75, 83, 95): flat blue-grey boards, where D3 shows pale nets behind clear glass. **Fix:** set the glass base colour to (235, 238, 236) and let the dark hall supply the darkness. The PC owes its own glass anyway (NOTES).
8. **The numerals' 7-segment stroke font (builder's Judgement).** Its square bowls, such as the foot of F3's painted "5" (`F3-close`, x 360 to 470, rows 0 to 45), look like the digits of an electronic display, not a sign-writer's or a brass numeral's. **Fix:** give 2, 3, 5, 6 and 9 round bowls, as the stroke skeleton of a plain sans. It stays strokes, not a real typeface.
9. **F6's spyhole is a bare 14 mm hole with a glass disc and no flange (target's: "no ring is given").** In my test render from 1 m it reads as a drilled hole. Every door viewer has a metal flange. **Fix:** add a ring 25 across, 2.5 proud, in chrome_nickel, round the lens.
10. **The plugged keyhole stands 0.8 proud instead of being sunk 2 mm (known; builder's).** From the front it reads as P2 shows (`F1-close`, x 970 to 995, rows 240 to 300), but in raking light it will catch an edge. **Fix:** F1 and F2 always carry KH1, so cut a blind keyhole-shaped pocket 2 mm deep in those two leaves (not through, so nothing can show through). Set the plate's face 2 below the leaf face and the blank 0.5 below it.
11. **The close pictures do not show the lock-side fittings of the left-handed kinds (builder's; the target's cameras).** The close camera looks at the right half of every door. So no close picture shows F3's bar pull, F4's and F5's levers, the low plate LP5 (T2, F4), T2's numerals, F2's "246", T1's and F3's letter plate LP1 in full, F6's spyhole, or T3's small light. T2 alone has an aid. **Fix:** render the gate's `close_plate` view for every kind (target.json gate.views already has its cameras), plus a `close_left` aid like T2's for F3, F4 and F5.
12. **Corrections to the target the builder flagged; the builder was right in each case (all target's, all narrow).**

    - (h) Two more of my own. LP2: P2's plate is chrome-coloured, not brass. Either use chrome_nickel or record why brass is kept. F5's 1 mm hairline 6 mm in from the edge lies under the stops' 20.4 mm lap on three sides and shows only at the foot, so drop it or say so.
