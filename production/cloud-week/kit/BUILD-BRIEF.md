# The brief every kit builder gets (cloud week 42)

You build one kit piece by script, from its exact target alone (production/cloud-week/targets/<family>/: TARGET.md, target.json, and TARGET-REVIEW.md, the review it passed). You did not write the target. A fresh reviewer who neither wrote the target nor built the piece will then judge your renders against the target's photographs, one view each.

## How

- **By script, never by hand:** Blender 5.2.2 as a Python module (bpy), headless: `/home/user/.bpyenv/bin/python build_<piece>.py ...`. Everything the piece is comes from target.json's numbers; a number you need that the target lacks is a fault in the target: write it down in NOTES.md as a judgement, do not invent quietly.
- **Shared tools** in production/cloud-week/kit/tools/: blender_parts.py (closed prisms and meshes from a parts list, exported to .npz for the check), joinery.py (mitred moulding sweeps, panels), outline.py (silhouettes and sections from triangles, rasterised and compared without a renderer: IoU and outline distances), review_render.py (the four standard views, Cycles on the CPU, an overcast light the camera does not see). The lab's door (git show origin/lab:production/lab/5-front-door/) is the worked example of target, build, check and review.
- **The model:** metres, z up, scale 1, the pivot at the base's centre on the ground (wall-mounted pieces: at the back face's bottom centre, the wall at y = 0, the street toward -y). Real geometry where the target shows edges, profiles, fixings and furniture: a profile the photographs show is modelled, not painted. Arrises get the bevel the target gives (else 2 to 3 mm on metal and stone, 1 to 2 mm on joinery) so they catch light; smooth shading by angle, sharp edges kept. Material slots named by what they are (paint_<colour>, cast_iron, galvanised_steel, concrete, granite, glass, brass, timber...), each with the target's base colour, roughness and metallic as starting values: the look itself is developed in Unreal, never judged here. UVs at real scale (1 UV unit = 1 m, box or cube projection per part) so the builder's tiling materials work. Mid-poly: aim under 20,000 triangles for street furniture, under 60,000 for a door or shopfront part. No hidden or overlapping faces (overlaps show as diagonal hatching: the door's second review found them), no gaps where parts meet.
- **Files:** scripts and checks in production/cloud-week/kit/<piece>/ (build_<piece>.py, check_<piece>.py, checks/check_vN.json, NOTES.md); the model as production/assets/cloud-week/<piece>/<piece>[_variant].glb, each file under 1 MB (the push guard refuses larger; if it cannot fit, the script that rebuilds it exactly is enough and the .glb stays out of git). Renders to the scratch folder you are given; reduced previews (tools/make_preview.py, or JPEG at most 1600 px and under 500 KB) in production/previews/cloud-week/<piece>/ named <piece>-<view>-v<N>-2026-10-<dd>.jpg.
- **The automatic check** (check_<piece>.py): the built mesh cut where the target draws it (outline.py), compared with the target's drawing (target_drawing.py's JSON), plus every check listed in target.json. The pass rule unless the target sets another: IoU at least 0.97, outline distance p95 at most 2 mm and worst at most 6 mm, every overall dimension within 1 mm (within 3 mm for pieces over 2 m). Write the result as checks/check_vN.json. A failing check is fixed before any render goes to a reviewer.
- **Renders for review** (Jafar, 11 October: two views a piece): review_render.py's street view (street distance) and close view, 1600 × 1200, at least 64 samples, `LEDGER_REVIEW_HDRI=/home/user/cache/hdri/bethnal_green_entrance_2k.hdr`. Front and low only as your own aids, not for review.
- **Photographs** (the network is open from 11 October): besides the target's own, real period photographs on Wikimedia Commons, Geograph and archive.org may be opened to see a detail the target leaves unclear (free licences only; date and page in NOTES.md). Where one shows the target wrong, build to the target and write the departure in NOTES.md for the reviewer; do not edit the target.

- how to reach them from the cloud session (tested 11 October): Commons' API (`https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/api.php?action=query&list=search&srnamespace=6&srsearch=...&format=json`, then `prop=imageinfo&iiprop=url|extmetadata` for the file, its date and licence; images from upload.wikimedia.org); Geograph's search feed (`https://api.geograph.org.uk/syndicator.php?q=...&format=JSON`, its pages with a browser user agent; photographs CC BY-SA 2.0, each page gives "taken" date); archive.org's advancedsearch.php. Send a user agent naming the project. Most Geograph photographs are after 2005: prefer those whose taken date is before 1995, else say the date.
Do not commit or push; do not edit the target. Reply with the check's result, the files, the triangle count and size of each .glb, and anything the target did not say that you had to decide.
