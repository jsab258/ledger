# The brief every fresh reviewer of a kit piece gets (cloud week 42)

You judge ONE view of one kit piece against the photographs of the real thing. You did not write its target and did not build it; you have not seen any other reviewer's verdict. The bar: would this piece, seen this way, pass as the real 1990 British object in a photoreal 2026 game, once it is given its final materials in the engine? The render is a judging aid lit by a plain overcast sky in Blender, not the game: judge form, proportion, profiles, edges, fixings, furniture, construction and the colours' kind, not the render's light, its pale ground or the absence of wear (wear comes from masks in the engine), unless the target asks for geometric damage.

## What you are given

- the render of your view (a path to a PNG);
- the photographs of the real thing: reduced previews in production/previews/cloud-week/refs/<family>/, and their source pages listed in production/cloud-week/targets/<family>/TARGET.md (open the source pages where you can for the full size; an unreached page is not evidence);
- the target (TARGET.md, target.json), so you can say whether a fault is the target's (it says so, and the photographs show otherwise) or the build's (the target says otherwise).

## What you hand over

A reply, and the same text written to production/cloud-week/kit/<piece>/REVIEW-v<N>-<view>.md: the first line PASS or FAIL. Then the faults, worst first, each: what is wrong, where in the view, what the photograph shows instead (which photograph, where), and whether it is the target's or the build's. Then what is right. FAIL for anything a player would see as wrong at this view's distance: a wrong proportion or profile, a missing part the photographs always show, a visibly modern or wrong-period form, a visible build fault (holes, gaps, overlapping faces, floating parts, faceting where it should be round). Narrow points that would not be seen at this distance go under PASS as notes. Do not edit any file but your review; do not commit or push. About 10 minutes.
