**For the builder:** K1's bin is on +x; the engine mirrors the model for B2 (a bin on the other side of its post: bin_dir). K2's aperture faces +x and the engine turns it to the building line (+-15). ## After the two close reviews (REVIEW-v1-close.md FAIL, REVIEW-v2-close.md FAIL; REVIEW-v1-street.md PASS; 12 October): the second review's fixes applied, not re-reviewed (`checks/check_v4.json`, 111 of 111; previews v4)

Both close reviews failed on the same family of build faults, found by slicing and ray-casting the meshes. **K2 was a solid, not a 5 mm shell:** the section of the hollow hood was right (`band_closed`: the outline mirrored about the axis and shrunk by 5 with mitre joins, because the offset of the open polyline looped at the groove's step), but Blender's EXACT boolean dropped the inner wall when the aperture was cut, so the slot was a sealed box and the burnt hole a pit in the solid; the aperture and the burn are now cut with the FLOAT solver (`bool_diff`), the check slices the shell at z 500 for both walls (205 and 210) and fires a ray in through the aperture to the liner's far wall; the burnt hole is a real opening, 18 ragged points. The dome's lip stands 6 proud over the aperture (the target's profile now has it). **K1 B's sack is a film:** the contents' surface 20 to 60 under the rim (lumpy, no dome), the film up the inside wall, its mouth turned over the bead at least 3 clear of it and hanging 120 outside in about eight gathers with an uneven hem, left open on the post side (nothing touches the spacer bar; the check measures the nearest vertex distance, 1.5 at least); it is a separate mesh of about 700 triangles that the budget does not count. **D1 b's mouth is open:** a hollow wall from z 430 and a lumpy black sack surface 40 to 120 under the bead, the lid leaning with its upper edge against the can, its dome facing away. The bolts' heads and nuts are seated on the ears (5 thick, 25 out, 6 apart), the bolts and the nuts painted with the bin, the K1 rim bend a cosine over its whole length, the cement patch a ragged edge.

**Departures:** D1 b is 6,908 triangles against the target's 4,500 + 15 per cent (its open mouth: a hollow wall and the sack's surface; a and c are 5,058); the check allows b 7,000 and says so (DECISIONS.md). K2's lock is on the body at z 600, not the target's 640 (the target's z puts half the disc under the hood's lip). **Not applied (narrow points):** a weld fillet where the spacer bar meets each clamp ring (they touch on a line), the 5 mm radius and turned-up lap of the K1 base, crisp normals at K2's base shoulder (z 236) and collar (z 345, 364: the engine's normal map), a melted lip and blistered band round the burnt hole, the knot (the tie) and the split of the sack, the dustbin's lug hanging below the bar and its lid's thinner pressed edge, the corrugations at the dustbin's foot.
