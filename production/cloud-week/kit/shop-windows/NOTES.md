## How to rebuild

`python3 build_ritas.py` from this folder. Heavy outputs go to SCRATCH (`/home/user/ledger-scratch`, override with `LEDGER_SW_SCRATCH`; F:/LedgerTools on the PC). Times on four CPU cores, no GPU: front 7 s, display 15 s, room (calibration about 4 minutes, then day 1004 s, night_lit 831 s, night_dark 835 s at 64 samples; 46 minutes with the calibration), gate (32 samples, 2560 x 1440: STREET_SQUARE day 236 s and night_lit 114 s, STREET_30 148 s and 112 s, CLOSE_35 252 s and 206 s, CLOSE_35_B 256 s and 196 s; the 28 check renders 184 s; the stage with the bakes through `trace` 1844 s), checks 94 s. `LEDGER_REUSE_CAL=1` reuses the light strengths of the last calibration; `LEDGER_ROOM_SAMPLES` and `LEDGER_GATE_SAMPLES` set the samples.
