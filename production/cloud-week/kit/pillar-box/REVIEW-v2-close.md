# Pillar box (kit 3.6), try 2: the close view, a fresh review (12 October 2026)

Judged: the close render (camera 1.45 m out, 0.6 m to the box's own right, eye 1.3 m, looking at z 1.12), zoomed, against seven photographs (below). Formed before reading NOTES.md; no other review opened. Verdict: it reads as a George VI / Elizabeth II cylindrical box at arm's length, with the right parts in the right order and no build fault. Four narrow points, each a small detail with an exact fix, plus two known ones (dentils, tablet) that the photographs show matter more than a decal can carry. Worst first.

## Narrow points (for the builder)

1. **Bare body between the hood and the collar; no panel, no tablet.** In the view: a 17 mm strip of plain cylinder between the hood's top (z 1333) and the collar ring (1350), and the hood is a thin lip (22 mm face, 30 proud). The photographs show the aperture's raised panel running right up to the collar in every box: on the George VI boxes (G2 Presteigne, G3 Caernarfon) the panel above the hood lip carries the small tablet in a frame, with cast words beside it; on the Elizabeth II boxes (G1, P1, P2) the hood itself is a deep box about 50 to 60 high in its face, standing about 40 proud, with the tablet set in its face and its top under the collar. Exact fix (George VI pattern, keeping the lip): carry the slot frame's raised panel (12 proud) up from z 1322 to the collar's underside at 1350, and set the tablet in it, centred, a frame about 55 x 40 round a 40 x 30 window. A decal on the 17 mm strip cannot hold a 40 high tablet. Whose: the target's (it has no tablet, its section 10, and puts the hood's top 10 under the cap); the build follows it.
2. **The cap's rim band is shallow and plain; the flare dominates.** In the view: rim band 45 deep (1440 to 1485) with only rounded edges, under it a 60 deep flare, so the cap reads as a smooth trumpet with a thin lid. The photographs show the rim band as deep as or deeper than the flare, with a rounded bead along its top edge and the dentil row on its face: rim, flare and collar about 60 : 40 : 35 in G1 (front close-up) and G3, about 55 : 50 : 30 in P1. Exact fix: rim band 1425 to 1485 (60 deep), a torus bead about R8 along its top edge in geometry, the flare 1380 to 1421; the dentil row (about 60 blocks round, as NOTES has it) as the normal map on the band's face. Whose: the target's (PHOTO-CHECK addendum's 1440 to 1485); the build follows it.
3. **The door handle is a small tab; the keyhole is a slit.** In the view (lower right, near the box's edge): a 40 x 100 escutcheon with a 4 x 12 slot and a 25 x 8 lever inside the escutcheon. G1 (the lock close-up), P2, G2 and G3 all show a separate rounded grip, about 70 wide, 25 high and 25 proud, its centre about 60 to 90 under the escutcheon, next to the door's edge; the escutcheon about 45 x 65 (G1) to 30 x 75 (P2), with a true keyhole (round top and slot, about 10 x 20) that reads clearly at this distance. Exact fix: the grip as its own part at z about 740, the escutcheon 45 x 70 against the door's edge, the keyhole shape cut 10 x 20. Whose: the target's (a disc, a slot and a shutter, no handle); the build's lever is too small.
4. **The door is narrower than the photographs'.** In the view: the door's near edge at about 38 degrees from the front, leaving a wide strip of plain body on the left of the door. G1 and G3 (front views) show the door's edges at about 45 to 50 degrees (the door about 0.8 of the box's seen width, about 290 to 300 across). Exact fix: the target's 300 wide, which fits the 390 body (plus or minus 50 degrees); the 0.8 scaling was only to be used where it would not otherwise fit. Whose: the build's.

Notes, not faults:
- The dome cannot be judged here: the eye (1.3 m) is under the cap's top. The base's top roll is out of frame.
- The slot (45 high) looks a little tall against G1 (about 32) and G3 (about 40); it is the target's and the photo check agreed it; leave it.
- The plate frame (130 x 240) is at the narrow end of the photographs (G1 about 150 x 205, G3 about 155 x 245, P2 the same shape as the build's); fine for an older plate, and 1990's plate size is unconfirmed (no period photograph reached).

## What is right

- The proportions: the cap rim 1.37 times the body (photographs 1.31 to 1.43); a collar ring just over the hood, a concave flare, no shelf or neck; the cap's silhouette smooth, no faceting at 96 sides.
- The plate frame upright and centred right under the slot frame, a flat-faced frame standing off the curved door with the white panel sunk in it (G1, G3, P2).
- The lock on the viewer's right, on the door, near its edge (all photographs).
- The door's joint: a fine 3 mm groove up its side and across its head under the slot frame; reads as cast iron.
- The slot cut through, dark inside; a raised frame round it with a hood lip that projects past the frame.
- Construction: every part seated in the body; no gaps, floating parts, overlapping faces, or parts too thin or too thick at this distance. No lettering, crown or brand; the reserved areas are out of this frame. Colours of the right kind (red paint, white enamel, black slot).
