# Kit piece 3.6, Quay Street's pillar box: review of the STREET view, v1

12 October 2026, a fresh reviewer (did not write the target, did not build the piece, has seen no other verdict). Judged: `render/street.png` (1600 x 1200, camera 6 m out, eye 1.6 m, a little to the box's right), cropped and enlarged, set beside five photographs at the same box height.

**In one line:** from across the street it reads as a pillar box, but not as the real one. The top of the box (the cap) has the wrong profile, and the white collection plate is the wrong shape in the wrong place. Both come from the target, and the build follows it faithfully. No build fault is visible.

## Faults, worst first

**1. The cap has the wrong profile. Where: the top 160 mm of the box. Cause: the target (its cap profile; the 60 mm dome is PHOTO-CHECK's figure). Not a narrow point: it is the top of the silhouette, the box's most recognisable part.**
- *The render:* the rim is a plain rounded band whose underside is almost flat (the cove rises 20 mm over a 72 mm overhang, z 1342 to 1362) and sits straight on the body. Above the rim come a flat shelf, a neck and a dome rising 80 mm in all, set in from the rim's edge. From the street it reads as a lid with a second, smaller dome on top: a mushroom.
- *The photographs (all five boxes):* under the rim, a deep concave flare runs down to a projecting collar ring round the top of the body, just above the slot's hood. The rim band has a beaded (dentil) lower edge. The dome is low (about 20 to 40 mm) and rises straight from just inside the rim's top bead, with no shelf or neck. See P3 (Rhayader), top of the box: dentil rim, flare, collar, then the slot. See P2 (Bridlington): the same three tiers above the hood. In P5 (Chester) the top is nearly flat. In P1 (Plymouth), the flare and collar are clear on the left side of the cap.
- *Measured on P3, as fractions of the height:* rim top 0.99, rim bottom 0.96, flare down to 0.92, collar down to 0.90, slot centre 0.85. Times 1500 that is: collar about 1345 to 1375, flare 1375 to 1435, rim band 1435 to 1485 (about 50 deep), dome 1485 to 1500. The build has its rim at 1362 to 1420 and its dome from 1440 to 1500, so the rim sits 60 to 70 mm too low and the dome stands too high.
- *Fix:* redraw the profile (the cylinder's turned outline) from z 1340 up, as above. Keep the 1500 height, the 535 rim and the slot at 1278: the hood's top (1333) then sits just under the collar, as in P2 and P3.

**2. The collection plate's frame is the wrong shape and in the wrong place. Where: the white panel in the middle of the door. Cause: the target (its 210 x 125 landscape frame at z 950); the build scaled it to 168 x 100. Not a narrow point: from the street the white plate is the brightest thing on the box.**
- *The render:* a landscape white panel centred on the door at z 950, its top about 255 mm below the slot, with blank red between.
- *The photographs (P1 to P5, six boxes):* the frame is upright (portrait), about 120 to 150 wide and 220 to 250 tall, centred under the slot. Its top is just below the slot's frame, about 40 to 60 mm under the slot. P2: 0.3 of the body wide, 0.15 of the height tall. P3: from 0.69 to 0.81 of the height. On every box it is a raised red frame of the same size, which suggests it is part of the door casting, so a 1990 box would have had it. No 1975 to 1995 photograph was reached, though, so the plate inside it in 1990 is not shown.
- *Fix:* an outer frame of about 130 x 240, on the axis, its top at about 1210. The door's head rises to just under the slot's frame (about 1235) so that the frame sits on the door. The plate's words stay an engine texture, and the five allowed strings fit an upright plate.
- *A consequence to note now, though it does not show in this view:* on every box photographed, the crown and cypher come below the plate (about 0.38 to 0.45 of the height), with the lettering below them (about 0.23 to 0.30). The target puts the cypher area above the plate (z 1088) and the lettering band above the door (1190 to 1230). Both reserved areas should move down before canon's cypher is placed in them.

## Narrow points (a PASS alone; for the builder)

- **The lock is on the wrong side (the build's).** The escutcheon shows at the left of the door. With z up and +y the front, +x is the viewer's LEFT; the target's frame line "+x is to the viewer's right" is mirrored, and LOCK_X = +90 followed it. Every photograph with the lock in view (P2, P3, P5, both P4 boxes) has it on the right, as an upright oblong escutcheon about 40 x 100 mm with a small lever handle under it (P2, P3, P5). Fix: x = -90 and the oblong shape.
- **The slot has no frame.** Every photograph shows a thin raised frame round the slot, with a small sill. P1 and P2 have a deep box hood with side cheeks (one pattern). P3, P5 and P4's right-hand box have a shallow hood like the build's lip. The lip is acceptable as the shallow pattern; add the frame and sill.
- **The small tablet above the slot is missing (the target's).** All six boxes show a small raised frame (about 50 x 30) with a plate, between the collar and the hood (brass in P2).
- **The top of the base:** P2 and P3 show a rounded roll where the black base meets the body. The build's 4 mm collar reads as a plain line.
- **The rim's beaded underside** belongs with fix 1 (geometry or a normal map).

## What is right

- **Height to width:** the build's 3.85 matches the narrow boxes of P1 (3.68), P2 (3.98) and P4's left-hand box (about 4.2, in pixels). The box reads neither squat nor thin.
- **Cap rim width:** 1.37 of the body, which reads right against P2 and P3.
- **Black base:** height (300, a fifth of the box) and width (398) agree with P2, P3 and P4.
- **Slot:** its height (centre 0.85, as in P3) and width (0.64 of the body) read right.
- **Door:** flush, its joint a fine line. The blank cypher and lettering areas are invisible from here, as intended.
- **Colours' kind:** a red body and cap, a black base, a white plate.
- **No build fault at this distance:** the cylinder, rim and dome edges are round (no faceting); there are no gaps, floating parts or overlapping faces; the hood sits on the body.
