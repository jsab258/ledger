## Notes (narrow points, worst first)

1. **The threshold's front may be too tall. This is the target's point, and I am not certain of it.** In front_foot (rows 752-815) the threshold shows a 76 mm front.
   - P1's camera is about 1.3 m above the threshold: the tread top shows 18 px, rows 562-580.
   - From that height, the 188 mm of threshold top in front of the leaf should show as 7-9 px, and the weatherboard's foot should sit about 9 px above the threshold's front edge.
   - P1 has the weatherboard's foot at row 529.5, a dark band at 530-533, its brightest line (a nose highlight) at 537-539, and the shadow under the nose at 548.
   - Read that way, the top is rows 530-537, and the front with its nose is only about 10 px (50 mm). The target reads 534-547.5 as all front, 70 mm.
   - A 76 mm stone threshold is still an ordinary British one, so this does not look wrong here. Check it on the street and low views before the target is used again.

2. **The transom bar is about 9 mm taller than P1's, and the fanlight glass sits about 8 mm higher. This is the target's.**
   - In the render, the white bar between the glass and the leaf is 73 mm: the transom's 65 plus the glazing bead's 10.
   - P1, averaged over x 140-240: the bar is rows 143-155.5 (12.5 px = 64 mm), the clear glass 95.5-142.5, with a dark 2 px line under the head at 93-95.
   - The render, scaled the same way: the bar is 141.5-155.7 and the glass 93.5-141. The glass is the same height; only the split between the head and the transom moves.
   - The target reads that same bar as the transom alone (143.3-155.5) and also runs its clear glass down to 142.5, which leaves no room for its own 10 mm bead.
   - The difference is 1.5 px on P1 and 3-4 px in this view.

3. **The bolection mouldings are about 8 mm narrower than P1's. This is the build's (D7), and within the photograph's error.**
   - Each moulding is 8.5-9.5 px on the scaled render, against 10-10.5 px on P1: upper-left panel top 173.5-184 and bottom 346-356.5, lower-left bottom 483-494. That is 44-49 mm against 51-54 mm, each edge ±1 px.
   - P1 also shows three highlights across each upward-facing moulding (rows 346.5, 349.5, 353 and 484.5, 487.5, 491) where the render gives two. P1's section is a little busier, probably a fillet at the panel's field.
   - Neither shows at this distance.

4. **The lock-rail band reads more reeded than P1's. This is light and material; look again in the engine.**
   - Render, rows 707-734: three crests at 1.5-1.9 times the flat leaf, with creases darker than the leaf (about 30 against 39).
   - P1, rows 363.5-378: the highlights and creases fall on the same rows within 1 px, but the creases stay brighter than the leaf (about 137 against 100), so the band reads as one broad lit moulding.
   - The profile is right. Black gloss exaggerates how each face is turned; judge it again with the final paint in the engine.

5. **The bell push has a brass ring that P1 does not show. This is the build's judgement (D13).**
   - Render, x 963-971, rows 576-604: a black oblong, 22 x 72 mm, with a round brass bezel of 17 mm.
   - P1 at 16x (x 271-276, rows 300-314): a dark grey oblong with pale edges and a darker horizontal band across its middle (rows 308-309), and no brass ring.
   - In this view the ring is a 7 px gold dot. Make it dark or plain.

6. **The foot gap is narrower than P1's dark band.** P1's dark band under the weatherboard is 3.5-4 px (rows 530-533.5); the render's gap is 10 mm, which is 2 px when scaled. This follows from note 1 and is not a separate fault.

7. **There is a notch at the foot of each plinth return's splay. This is the build's, in the context wall.**
   - front_foot, x 480-488, rows 785-795, and mirrored at about x 1112-1120: the 45 degree splay stops about 9 mm short of the return's vertical face, and a sliver of the threshold shows in the step.
   - It is about 4 px in this view. The wall belongs to the wall kit, but the doorway's wall should close the notch.

8. **There is no house number. The target makes it optional and leaves the number to the town.** P1 has "69" stacked on the muntin (rows 263-296), and P2 shows its own number too. This is not the kit piece's fault, but since both photographs have one, the street must put a number on or beside every door.

9. **The lock's collar is brass, while P1's reads dull grey-olive.** This is material, as the builder already noted.

## What is right

- **The elevation lies on P1.** The frame, leaf, rails, muntin, panel openings, band, weatherboard, plate, lock and jamb plate all match within 1-2 px. Width and height agree independently, so the door's proportion is P1's.
- **Frame.** It is flat and square-edged, with 55 mm showing each side. The head is cambered to follow the arch. In elevation there is correctly no soffit band: P1's dark band at rows 70.5-77.5 is the 114 mm reveal seen from below. No joint or shading break shows where the head meets the jambs; the shade grades smoothly.
- **Transom.** The order of slope, face, crease and lip matches P1's rows: bright slope 143-147, face 148-152, crease 153, lip 154-155. It runs 10 mm past each stop with P2's 45 degree splayed lip, which is clean at both ends.
- **Fanlight.** A single pane with a bead all round and mitred corners; the dark hall behind it reads as a hall.
- **Leaf.** The four bolection-moulded panels have the right lap and fall. The 1.6 mm margins and the dark 10 mm foot gap are there. The rail, stile and muntin joints show as faint hairlines without drawing the eye.
- **Lock-rail band and weatherboard.** Both are where P1 has them, with P1's internal structure. The weatherboard has its top roll, hollow, broad sky-lit face and lower arris (P1 rows 514-517, 518-519, 520-527, 528-529). Both stop under the stops.
- **Ironmongery.**
  - The letter plate is vertical on the muntin, with a framed, chamfered rim, a recessed aperture and a dished flap, at P1's size and place (x 186-201, rows 192-239).
  - The lock is a round collar standing proud, 42 mm across, at P1's centre (258.75, 290.3).
  - The jamb oblong is at P1's place and size.
- **Threshold, riser and step.**
  - The threshold's ends die into the plinth returns on 45 degree diagonals, as in P1 on both sides.
  - The recessed riser shows 742 mm between the returns, which is P1's.
  - The step's half-round nose over its undercut runs round both ends, rounded in plan. Its overhang past the riser, 118 mm, against P1's 140 ±10 at the step's nearer plane, is consistent with the perspective.
- **No build faults seen.** There is no faceting on the step nose, lock collar, bell push or moulding curves, and no gaps, holes, floating parts or overlapping faces. The arrises are crisp, and nothing is a modern or wrong-period form.
- **Context.** A 13-brick segmental arch with radial joints and a curved extrados rests on the top quoin blocks. The quoins are in blocks of three courses, long and short, in step on both sides. The plinth is splayed. Everything sits where P1 has it once the wall plane's nearer scale is allowed for.
