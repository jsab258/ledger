## Notes (narrow points, not seen as wrong at 4 m)

1. **The threshold reads deeper than P2's.** Where: the dark slab across the foot of the opening. Its top shows about twice the height of its 45 mm front face (about 35 px of top against 15 to 18 px of face). The build follows the target: front at the wall face, back at 241.3, frame set 114.3 back. P2 instead (rows 946 to 979, crop p2_threshold) shows a top only about 0.65 times the height of its front face (13 px against 20 px). That suggests a sill projecting roughly 60 to 100 mm in front of the leaf, not 188, with the frame nearer the pilaster's face (P2 shows only a dark groove between the jamb strip and the render, crop p2_leftjamb). Fault: the target's (`step.threshold.front_y_mm` 0 with `opening.reveal_depth_mm` 114.3, both carried over from T1). It still reads as a plausible deep timber sill, so this is not a fail. The horns notched into the render at each end belong to the context.
2. **The near (left) jamb does not show at all.** Where: the leaf's left edge meets the render's corner directly, and the left end of the transom is hidden too. This is correct optics for a 114.3 reveal seen from 2 m to the left: about 50 mm is hidden and the jamb shows 49. The cause is the same reveal depth as note 1. If the shopfront sets the frame nearer flush, as P2 suggests, the jamb would show from this side. Fault: the target's or the context's, not the piece's.
3. **The centre knob's turned rings do not read.** Where: the black knob on the lock rail. In the render it reads as a smooth black cone ending in a small pointed brass tip. P2's knob (8x crop, p2_knob) is a beehive with five or six bold stepped rings, each catching a highlight, and a small flat round boss at the front, not a point. The target's profile (a taper to r 2 at z 62 under a 4 mm cap, grooves 1.5 deep) is followed. The grooves are too shallow to read, and the cap reads as a nipple. Fault: mostly the target's (profile and groove depth), and it can be fixed in the build by stepping the rings. It is not visible as a fault at 4 m, but it will be at the "close" view.
4. **The black "keep" on the right jamb.** Where: the small black rectangle on the jamb's front face, just below the cylinder. P2's jamb is plain at that height. An inward-opening door's latch keep is on the inside of the frame, so from the street this reads as a bell push, not a keep. That is acceptable for a flat's side door, but it should be called a bell push and given a button. Fault: the target's (copied from P1 for F1).
5. **The cylinder lock is a plain grey disc.** Where: the right stile, above the knob. P2 (crop p2_lock) shows a scalloped, serrated collar around a small plug. The build places 37 mm at 46.3 from the stop face, as the target says, but the 12 serrations do not read at this distance. Fault: none at 4 m. Check it at the close view.
6. **No house number.** Both photographs carry one: P1 has "69" stacked on the muntin, P2 has "178" on the top rail. The target makes it optional because the town assigns it, so this is not a missing part of the piece. The street instance should carry one.

The upper panel's apparent height-to-width is 4.5, against the target's 3.89 times the about 1.15 foreshortening. The muntin reads wider than the far stile in the right ratio (35 px against 25 px; 0.71 measured, 0.73 expected). It has bolection-type moulds with a crisp outer nose falling to a flat field, closed mitres and a square-edged leaf. This matches P1's leaf and P2's "bold bevel to a flat field".
- **Transom.** It is P2's: from the glass down, a weathered slope, a flat face, a dark quirk and a full round nose standing well proud over the leaf head. The slope stops at the stop edge while the face and nose run on about 28 mm over the jamb face, and the nose's end is cut back on the splay. The build's vertices run from the stop edge at z 4 to 874.2 at z 32, exactly as P2's crop c2_endR shows.

The centre knob is on the lock rail's centre line. The vertical nickel letter plate is on the muntin near the top of the upper panels, with its flap reading, as P1 places it. There are no hinges, knocker, band or weatherboard, as neither photograph shows them on this door.
- **Threshold.** It stands 45 mm above the paving with a slightly rounded nose, as P2 measures. There is no step or plinth.
- **Build quality.** The build is clean throughout: the knob and plate silhouettes are smooth and the edges are crisp, with no gaps between leaf, frame, transom and sill.
