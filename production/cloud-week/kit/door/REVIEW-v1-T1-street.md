# Front door 3.1, variant T1, view "street": fresh review (cloud week 42, v1)

**What was judged.** The render `door-build/v1/T1/street.png` (1600 x 1200; camera 2.0 m left of the door's centre, 3.5 m out, 1.6 m high, about 4 m off), against photograph 1 (Teignmouth, 480 x 640, T1's model) and photograph 2 (Tottenham) as a cross-check. The source pages on Geograph and Wikimedia cannot be opened from this cloud, so the judgement is made on the repository copies, cropped and enlarged with PIL. Pixel places below are in those files. The piece is the leaf, frame, transom, fanlight, ironmongery, threshold and step. The wall, quoins, arch and plinth are context. Light, the black paint and the lack of wear were not judged.

## Faults a player would see at this distance

None.

## Notes (narrow at 4 m; for the close and front views to settle)

1. **The lock-rail band reads as three separate rounds; the photograph's reads as one broad rail.**
   - Where: render rows 654-680, across the leaf.
   - Three crests of nearly equal height, each lit, with dark coves between them about 7 mm deep (a reeded look).
   - Photograph 1 (rows 363-378, column average x 148-168) shows one broad rail: a rounded top, a fine crease, a wide flat face, a lower lip, then a crisp shadow. Its brightness dips are shallow (about 10-14% against the crests); the render's dips fall to about 25% of the crests.
   - The black gloss against the photograph's red exaggerates this, so it cannot be settled from this view.
   - If the close view confirms it, the fault is the target's: its band profile has coves to 13 and 14.5 under crests of 20.5-22. The build follows the target.
2. **The panel mouldings may be a little narrow.**
   - Photograph 1 shows the bolection as two concentric lines about 8 px apart (left upper panel x 133-145 and 172-182; panel feet rows 346-357 and 483-495). That is about 40-50 mm on the face, with the flat field about 140 mm wide.
   - The build has 36 mm and a 170 mm field (target's, inside its own 34 +-8). Both read as a properly moulded four-panel door at 4 m.
3. **The cylinder lock reads as a plain brass disc.**
   - Where: render x 865, row 523.
   - Photograph 1 (x 255-262, rows 287-294) shows a pale dull ring round a darker plug.
   - The target's 28 mm plug inside the 43.5 mm collar does not separate at this distance. Check the plug's edge and the collar's metal in the close view (build's).
4. **The keep is a plain black block.**
   - Where: render x 903, rows 541-556.
   - Photograph 1 (x 270-275, rows 300-314) shows a dark plate with a paler slot. This is narrow at 4 m (build's).

## Context (not this piece; for whoever owns the wall)

- **Grey slabs at the jamb feet.** The plinth's level return tops render as thin grey slabs sitting over the threshold's ends (render x 893-930, rows 925-945, and x 650-690, rows 955-975). Photograph 1 shows red brick right up to the white jamb (rows 527-535, x 100-117 and 265-280). This is the wall's, as TARGET-REVIEW.md left it.
- **Wrong bond.** The context wall is laid in stretcher bond. Photograph 1's wall is Flemish bond, with a header between stretchers in every course (x 320-480, rows 250-450). Stretcher bond belongs to a 20th-century cavity wall and would look wrong on a Victorian terrace in the assembled street.
- **Arch bricks.** They render a cool grey-white with very thin dark joints. Photograph 1's are buff, with pale, irregular joints.

## What is right

- **The leaf's elevation matches photograph 1 rail by rail.** The render was measured down column x 720 (0.344 px a mm, level camera) and set against photograph 1 down x 160 (5.17 mm a px). Heights above the threshold, render first:
  - top of the upper-panel moulding: 1836 (target 1836, photograph about 1840);
  - foot of the upper panels: 898 (photograph 900);
  - band: 784-860 (photograph 791-863);
  - lower panels: 680 to 195 (photograph 688 to 186);
  - top of the weatherboard: 93 (photograph 88).
- **The leaf's parts are all there and match photograph 1 in form.** It has four panels, the upper ones tall, with bolection mouldings, a moulded band and a weatherboard with a belly, a hollow and a rounded nose. Its 10 mm foot gap sits in shadow.
- **The frame is flat and square-edged.**
  - On the right, the depths read true for this angle: the stop's inner face (13 px for 74 mm), the 55 mm jamb face (16 px) and the buff reveal return (21 px for 114 mm).
  - On the left, the reveal hides the jamb, as it must from 2 m to the left.
- **The transom and fanlight are right.**
  - The transom shows its lip, face and slope, and its end is returned on a splay just past the stop.
  - The fanlight is a single clear pane with a bead on all four sides.
  - The frame head shows about 70 mm under the arch soffit (render rows 141-165 at x 780), as photograph 1 shows.
- **The ironmongery is where photograph 1 has it.**
  - The letter plate stands vertical on the muntin with a raised rim and a sunk flap, centre about 1607 above the leaf bottom (target 1617).
  - The cylinder is on the lock stile at about 1231 (target 1235).
  - The keep is on the right jamb face.
  - There are no hinges, knocker or chain outside.
- **The foot follows photograph 1's sequence (rows 534-610).**
  - The stone threshold has a rounded nose (about 26 px, so about 76 mm), with a dark crease under it.
  - The riser is recessed (about 21 px, so about 72 mm).
  - The tread has a half-round nose (about 32 px) over an undercut, then the shaded base face (about 28 px) down to the pavement.
  - The step runs past the opening, its back corners butt the plinth's fronts, and its right end is rounded in plan.
- **No build faults were found.** There are no holes, gaps, floating parts, overlapping faces or faceting. The plate, lock, transom nose, band and step nose are smooth.
