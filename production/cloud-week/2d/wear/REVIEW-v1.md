# Wear masks of Quay Street (unit 4.5): fresh review, one view (the masks as wear)

12 October 2026. The reviewer looked at the contact sheets, the composed foot and head, and the full masks of 14 kinds, measuring some in `wear2/v3`. `checks/check_v1.json` reads 377 rows, 0 failing. Most kinds read as their wear: soot, damp, salt, cracks, gum, ends, oil, blots, flags, infill, lines, iron, grates, lichen, handles, sign ghosts, buffs. Findings 1 to 3 fail it: they are seen at every downpipe, on every repaired facade, and on a third of sills.

## Findings, worst first

1. **algae_downpipe: the patch floats, and the run and the moss are ruled shapes.** WHAT: In 4 of 5 variants the patch misses the shoe. Its lowest point is 0.15 m (v3, v4), 0.25 m (v1) or 0.53 m (v2) up, so it reads as a leaf-shaped stain hanging on the wall. The patches are 0.10 to 0.34 m wide; the rule says 0.3 to 0.9 m, wider low down. The run (v0, v2) is a flat-topped, straight-sided box faded in 8 flat steps; the composed preview shows it as a dark rectangle. The moss is 9 mm bars, 0.15 to 0.45 m long, scattered over the whole 1.4 m frame. WHERE: v0 to v4; `wear-composed-foot-head`, x 0.8 to 1.0 m. WHOSE: the target's envelope (`blob_field_polys`: centre y from 0.4 eqd to 0.6 H, the moss bars, `streak(slices=8)`), against its own rule text and moss_patch_eqd (p50 10.2 mm). FIX: (a) The first blob's lower edge on y = 0, 0.3 to 0.9 m wide, centred within 0.1 m of x 0. Other blobs overlap it and narrow upward. A new check: mask >= 0.5 over at least 0.3 m at 0.02 m up, in every variant. (b) Moss as patches of eqd 5.5 / 10.2 / 31.5 mm on bed joints (y = 75 n mm) and perpends, only within 0.15 m of the patch, none above 1.2 m. (c) The run 0.03 to 0.05 m wide at its top, widening to 0.1 to 0.3 m, with a continuous fade (finding 3).

2. **render_patch: all six variants are one lollipop.** WHAT: A ragged square with a hard 20 to 50 mm spike from the centre of its lower edge (correlation up to 0.95), so every repaired facade repeats one stamp. There is no L shape, chase, loss cluster or single brick, and the loss blobs (0.9) never show under the 1.0 patch. WHERE: v0 to v5. WHOSE: the target's envelope (`rect_patch_polys` always draws the tail, since `drip_tail.length_m` starts at 0.05); its six-type variant list is not drawn. FIX: v0 a rectangle with no tail. v1 an L (one corner cut, 40 to 60 % of each side). v2 a chase 0.06 to 0.12 by 0.4 to 0.7 m. v3 a square with a smear tail (level 0.4, 40 mm edge, offset up to 0.3 w). v4 4 to 9 loss blobs (33 / 67 / 294 mm) and no rectangle. v5 one brick 0.215 by 0.065 m.

3. **Streaks fade in steps, not as run-off.** WHAT: The fade is 14 flat slices. Down the end streak of `streak_sill_v0` (column 298) the mask holds for 50 to 80 mm, then drops 0.05 to 0.10 (0.66, 0.66, 0.67, 0.66, then 0.62, 0.57, 0.55 ...), rippling up to 0.04 around a smooth fade (v1 0.19). That shows as horizontal banding on a third of sills, worst on render. The same steps are in streak_coping, rust_bleed and the algae and bird runs. WHOSE: the target's envelope (`streak()`, `slices=14`, then a 20 mm blur). FIX: slices = ceil(length_mm / 5), or the outline at level 1 times the fade evaluated per pixel along the axis.

4. **poster_remnant: whole flat sheets with sawtooth edges.** WHAT: All five are one solid shape at 1.0, and v0 is a star of straight spikes. None is 20 to 70 % intact; there are no layers, paste ghost, torn corner or strips. WHOSE: the target's envelope (the 1.0 sheet hides its 0.5 to 0.95 axis-aligned layers; 8 noisy points per side make the zigzag). FIX: the sheet only as a paste-ghost rim, level 0.25, 10 to 40 mm wide. Layers at 0.6 to 1.0 cover 0.2, 0.4 or 0.7 of it by variant, each cut by 1 to 3 straight tears at 30 to 60 degrees. v4 is 2 to 5 strips, 30 to 120 mm wide. A new check: coverage at 0.5 equals the intact fraction +/- 0.1.

5. **tyre_scuff: four copies of one clean arc.** WHAT: Correlation 0.99. Each is an even band cut square at full strength, like a painted curve; there is no near-straight one and no 2, 3 or 5 m radii. WHOSE: the target's envelope (`scuff_polys` ignores the variant). FIX: radii 2, 3 and 5 m and straight by variant. Strength 0.4 + 0.6 x smooth noise (0.3 to 0.8 m) along the arc. Ends fade to 0 over 0.25 m, and chained stamps overlap by 0.25 m.

6. **gutter_grime: ruled trapezoids every 2 m.** WHAT: Two flat-topped, straight-sided trapezoids (30 to 120 mm, level about 0.45) per tile, some off the joint line (v0, v3). The tile repeats them every 2 m for 48 m, near the camera. The rule names no such mark; weeds are separate stamps. WHERE: v0 to v3, 0.20 to 0.40 m from the kerb foot. WHOSE: the target's envelope (`fingers` n_per_m 1.0). FIX: `fingers.n_per_m` 0 for gutter_grime.

7. **paint_flake: the flakes ignore the cracks; crazing is in every variant; the big sheet repeats every metre.** WHAT: Smooth lenses scattered regardless of the cracks, with round big sheets. The target's photograph (`ph-peeling_painted_wall`) shows losses opening along the cracks and sheets bounded by crack cells. All 6 variants carry render crazing (the target says on/off), so timber gets it too. On a stallriser the 1 m tile repeats its big sheet every metre (v1, v3, v4, v5). WHOSE: the target's envelope (`flake_field_polys`). FIX: craquelure off in v3 to v5, for timber. 60 % of flakes centred on a crack, long axis along it (+/- 10 degrees). The stallriser tile takes only variants without a big sheet.

8. **Narrow points (pass):**
   a. **The infill lets the flags show through.** footway_infill and graffiti_buff interiors vary 0.89 to 1.0 (the maker's strength variation), so flag joints ghost through an opaque infill by up to 11 %. FIX: add both to `HARD`.
   b. **The notes overstate the warp.** They say "a few tenths of a millimetre"; it is 0.35 to 1.6 texels, from 0.5 mm (flakes) to 18 mm (soot) and 40 mm (paint_fade). At 0.5 to 2 mm it cannot unrule a straight side (findings 4 and 6). FIX: change the sentence to say so.
   c. **The foot and head bands step in blocks.** The profile shifts by courses in flat segments 0.3 to 0.6 m wide (splash v1 crosses 0.5 at 0.375, 0.45 and 0.525 m); the sheet steps brick by brick. Under the brick's per-brick tone it passes. Placer note: one 2 m tile repeats 3 times per 6 m bay.
   d. **Bird droppings are plain blobs.** The splats are smooth solid ovals with no splatter. They are small and high; they pass.
   e. **Road patches are all squares.** All road_patch variants are square covers (a 1.2 m frame holds no trench). They pass at 1 to 3 per street.
