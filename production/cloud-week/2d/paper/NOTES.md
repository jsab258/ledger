# Paper, cards, boards and plates of Quay Street (units 4.2, 4.3 and 4.4): build notes

12 October 2026, cloud week 42. Built by script from `production/cloud-week/targets/posters-boards-plates/target.json` (and `TARGET.md`), the target as it stands after its second review and Jafar's ruling of 9 October (its `self_check.py` reader is imported, not edited). The target is untouched. The pictures are not in git (`production/previews/cloud-week/paper/` holds the review previews); one command rebuilds them.

## What was made

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `make_paper.py` | the one command. Reads `target.json` and the fonts; writes every picture, the glyph manifests and `manifest.json` into the folder it is given |
| `paper_common.py`, `paper_ink.py`, `paper_render.py`, `paper_wear.py` | what it uses: the target's own ageing formulas and noise (common); each item's shapes and lines laid at its own scale, the inks' look by process (ink); the compositing over the aged stock, relief and rolled edges (render); the weather, the wall and the fixings by the target's wear tables (wear) |
| `make_board.py` | the newsagent's window board SB1: the fifteen cards taped inside the glass, composed from the delivered pictures at 6 px/mm |
| `placed_decals.py` | the paste plan joined to the pictures: `placed_decals.json` (46 default placements, 16 held) |
| `paper_reader.py`, `check_paper.py` | the independent check (below); `checks/check_v1.json` is its result |
| `make_previews.py` | the reduced JPEG previews: contact sheets by group at one scale, and close crops at 1.5 to 6 px/mm |
| `production/fonts/archivo/`, `production/fonts/courier-prime/` | the two OFL families the target asked the builder to add (with their `OFL.txt`); THIRD-PARTY.md and tools/attribution-check.py name them |

The set (rebuilt into the scratch folder given to the command): **84 items** (every item of the target, the held twins and stand-ins too: `held` in the manifest), 140 delivered pictures (an item's classes are the ones the target lists in `items[].wear.classes`; `--classes all` makes A to D), each with a base colour, a 16-bit height map and an ORM map, one `<ID>.glyphs.json` per item, and **SB1**. Per picture:

- `<ID>_<class>_basecolour.png`: sRGB 8-bit, **RGBA where the item has an outline or a loss** (a star card, a hole, a torn or lost edge, a sleeve's clear band, a die-cut or rounded corner), else RGB. Row 0 is the TOP, column 0 the viewer's LEFT, **one pixel scale per item** (`items[].px_per_mm`, 2 to 12, as the target chose it so that every glyph can be told from every other), **square-on**: no skew and no rotation is baked in; the placement's `rot_deg` is the only turn.
- `<ID>_<class>_height.png`: 16-bit, 32768 is the face; 25600 units a millimetre (the fascia maps' own scale), **8192 on the cast and pressed plates** (their letters and border stand 3 and 1.5 mm; the manifest gives `units_per_mm` for each file). Paper cockle and impression, the ink film (+0.03 mm screen, -0.06 mm letterpress), torn edges, curl, tape (+0.12), the plate relief with ten degrees of draft and a rounded top, rolled edges.
- `<ID>_<class>_orm.png`: R 255 (no occlusion drawn), G roughness (paper 0.8 to 0.9, toner 0.6, litho 0.7, enamel 0.12, agent's board 0.35, cast aluminium 0.5, a sleeve's polythene 0.25), B metal (bare aluminium where the paint has flaked).

## How a picture is made (stages, in order)

1. **Layout** (`paper_ink.build`): the card's outline (a star's 14 points, rounded corners of a plate, a sticker's 2.5 mm die-cut corner, the hanging hole of K03), every shape of the target at its pixel scale (boxes with exact fractional coverage, the rest supersampled four times), the art slots, and every line of lettering from the target's own glyph layout (`self_check.layout_glyphs`) or, for the 29 hand cards, its jitter (`jitter_glyphs`: size, rotation, baseline and word-gap jitter of the hand style). The **glyph manifest** is written from exactly these glyphs, registration offset and jitter included.
2. **The ink's look, by process** (`LOOK` in `paper_ink.py`, from the target's `processes`): edge softness (never more than 0.6 px: more closes the small counters of an e at 12 px/mm); screen and litho and sticker mottle (4 to 12 per cent density loss at 4 to 8 mm), letterpress mottle with a darker rim at the letter edges, a ragged edge and the impression pressed into the height map (-0.06 mm), the screen's pinholes, the photocopier's toner density 0.92 with its slight fattening, the typewriter's strike varying from one character to the next, the felt pen's darker pooled edge, the ballpoint's pressure and its blots. The second colour of a two-colour job (red or blue) is off register by 0.28 to 0.36 mm along the line and 0.05 to 0.25 mm up.
3. **Colour, by class** (`paper_render`): the stock, ink and paint colours of the class by the target's own ageing formulas (`paper_common.Palette`, which asserts that it reproduces the target's tables on every run): fade by tau towards the paper, yellowing, a grime film; the red letting boards fade towards chalk pink. Elements are composited over the aged stock in the order the target lists (shapes, then lines), with the paper's fibre and a 6 mm cloudiness.
4. **Relief and edges**: the raised letters and border of S01 to S02 (3 and 1.5 mm, ten degrees of draft, a rounded top), the rolled edge of every plate and board, and the 16-bit height.
5. **Wear and fixings** (`paper_wear`): by the item's wear table, scaled by class (A none, B a little, C more, D most), every number from the target's `age_rules` and `wear_tables`:
   - *bill_pasted* (22 items): cockle (0.4 to 1.5 mm, wavelength 12 to 40 mm, stronger down the sheet), up to three lifted corners (the back of the sheet shows, with a fold and a shadow), rain runs from the top edge, a paste halo round the edges (C, D), tears from an edge, and the loss of paper: 2 to 5 torn patches with ragged outlines, the lower corners first (B up to 5 per cent and only the corners, C 3 to 20, D 30 to 60), small ragged bites from the edges, and in C and D a pale ghost of the paste (214, 205, 185 at 0.3) where the paper is gone.
   - *glass_bill* (P04, J01, D01 and twin): condensation runs and a stain in the lower third, the lower edge curling out 3 to 8 mm, four tabs of yellowed tape at the corners, the sun-fade 30 per cent faster (tau x 0.7).
   - *notice_sleeve* (C01 to C03, H03 to H05): fog on the inside in the lower third, one or two creases, 6 to 20 water beads, the sheet slid down 5 to 20 mm (the sleeve's clear band shows transparent at the top, within the margin) and two cable ties; C01a also carries its four tabs.
   - *card_felt*, *card_ballpoint*: warp or curl, dog-ears, a finger smudge, the top third bleached; every taped card carries ONE tab at the top-LEFT, every string-hung card the sucker and knot at the top-left, and nothing on the right half.
   - *sticker*: an edge lifting, scratches, loss in C and D.
   - *plastic card* (K03a, K03b, the OPEN and CLOSED sign): no folds, curl, bleaching or smudge; 6 to 20 scratches of 0.2 to 0.5 mm and 5 to 40 mm, grime at most 5 per cent; three seeds of it (`-s2`, `-s3`) for the eight doors.
   - *enamel_plate* (H01, H02): bolt heads with a rust halo, chips to black steel (12 to 30, 2 to 9 mm), crazing round the bolts, rust tears, a bird-lime streak, salt bloom (H02). *cast_aluminium_plate* (S01): the paint of the raised faces lifts in patches, first at the edges, showing bare aluminium (metal 1 in the ORM map), a grime gradient, rust under the screws, lime. *pressed_plate* (S02, S03): chips at the corners and screws only, a grime gradient, no flaking of the letters. *letting_board* (L01 to L04): grime streaks from the top edge, rust runs under the screws, the foot peeling in bites, algae in D.
   - the copier's own marks on every photocopy: toner speckle, a grey band 3 to 6 mm along one edge, one to three streaks.

The weather keeps off the lettering in classes A and B (the reader must still read them): a tear, a lifted corner or the loss of paper never comes within 4 mm of a line's reading window (8 mm along, 3 mm up and down), whose outline it follows round; the printer's imprint is not protected (the target: illegible by design); in C and D the weather may cross anything.

## What is judgement, and what the maker changes from the target (each one small and named)

- **Registration** of the second colour is the bottom of the target's range: 0.28 to 0.36 mm along the line and 0.05 to 0.25 up (the process range is 0.3 to 0.8; the target's own line reader fails a print line shifted more than about 0.4 mm at 4 px/mm), inside the manifest's print envelope and declared in it (`registration_offset_mm`).
- **The copier's speckle, edge shadow and streaks, the fixings and the weather are laid after the class-A picture "before wear"** that the target's ITEM.clean reads; `_check/<ID>_A_clean.png` is that picture. Toner speckle is ink-coloured pixels outside the lettering, which ITEM.clean counts as stray ink by definition.
- **A tab of tape is a translucent filter**, calibrated so that bare card under it reads the target's tape colour at its 0.85 opacity; the target puts the first letter of fourteen ballpoint cards and the K09 tickets under the tab (its own `tab_centre_mm`), and an opaque tab would hide it.
- **A rule that runs through lettering is cut 2 mm round it** (F01's column rule ran through MONDAY TO SATURDAY and SUNDAYS). The independent reference of the check draws it the same way.
- **The art slots are flat stand-ins**: G01, T01, T02 and their twins have the picture the image model is to make and nothing here may stand in for it. Each slot is the target's nominal colour (aged as a printed colour), with the zone the target guarantees behind a line (a title on sky, T02's tag on sand: 10 mm round the line, feathered), under the target's scrims. They look flat; they are not pictures. A fresh reviewer looks at each real picture at 1:1 before any text is laid (the target's ART.eye).
- **F02** (the summer sheet under F01) has no legible table and none of its three ghost words: the target says only "through a tear" and the game lays F01 over it. **K02** (not placed) has the hour hand at two o'clock (the target's other variant is "at 12"); its string-hung sucker lies over the B of BACK AT, the target's own geometry (centre 14, 14 from the corner, 22 mm across; the first letter starts at 12 mm).
- **K03's hole** is transparent (alpha 0); a die-cut sticker has rounded corners of 2.5 mm (the target gives none); **the K01 and K06a string** runs only from the sucker to the top edge of the card (the loop is beyond the card).
- **Plates' height scale** is 8192 units a millimetre (the 25600 of the fascia maps holds only 1.28 mm).
- **The loss of paper in classes A and B** is smaller than the top of the target's range where lettering stands in the way (it keeps to the margins).

## What is not made, and shortfalls

- **No 1990 photograph of a bill, a notice, a letting board or a plate was reached** (the target says so; this session's search of Geograph for 1988 to 1992 found few: an estate agent's development board in Belfast (1990), a small street-name plate above a Beverley pub (1992), a TO LET board on a fence (1991), a street plate in Southwark (1988), listed in the review brief). Sizes, colours, ageing and wear are the target's Judgement; the reviews are the check on them.
- **The three pictures** (above). **The brick relief** under a bill on the gable (the target's `gable_extra`: mortar lines as 0.5 mm relief at 75 mm pitch, soot streaks, splash-back) is not in the maps: the gable is bare by the target's own ruling, and the pier bill M01 gets no brick relief; the game's wall carries it.
- **The wet look** (`wet`: darken 0.12, saturation 1.1, roughness 0.55) is a runtime hint, not baked.
- **Overposting** (a newer bill over an older one's tear, glue stains) is not drawn: the default street has one layer.
- **The printed halftone** of the quads is drawn as continuous tone (the target's own call).
- The cards' string loops, the Harbour Board's case and the ferry's board (HC1, FC1) are not built (the target: not placed).
