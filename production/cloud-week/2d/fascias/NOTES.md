## Rebuild on the PC: one command

`python production/cloud-week/2d/fascias/make_fascias.py F:\LedgerTools\scratch\fascias` (any Python 3.11 or later with `numpy`, `scipy` and `Pillow` 10.1 or later; here it is `/home/user/.bpyenv/bin/python`). About a minute on four cores (`--jobs N`). The fonts are read from `production/fonts/`, so no network is needed.

- Same seed, same pixels: every random draw comes from a numpy Generator seeded from (seed base, board name); `--seed-base N` (default 1) gives a different set. `make_fascias.py OUTDIR --verify` rebuilds into `OUTDIR_verify` and compares every hash with `OUTDIR/manifest.json`. Tested here on a rebuild of the whole set in a second process: 131 of 131 files identical.
- The SHA-256 of a PNG matches only on the same library versions (the manifest records them: Pillow 12.3.0 with FreeType 2.14.3 and Raqm 0.10.5, numpy 2.5.3, scipy 1.18.1). `sha256_pixels` is the hash of the raw array. On the PC check `python -c "from PIL import features; print(features.check('raqm'))"`: if it prints False, Pillow falls back to basic layout, the kerning differs a little, the hashes will not match and the pictures are still right (the checks still pass); nobody has run this on Windows yet.
- `--pack pad` or `--pack stretch` also writes power-of-two copies next to each map (8192 x 1024 with the picture top-left and its edge pixels repeated, `uv_scale` 0.6604 x 0.53711; or 5120 x 512). **Not decided here**: the target (section 3 and 11, item 8) says to check FIRST in the installed 5.8.2 source whether a 5410 x 550 texture gets mips. That source is not on this machine and nothing was checked; the builder does it (file and line, RULINGS 8 Oct) and then picks.

## The two fixes of the target's second review, and the grocer's number

Applied exactly as `TARGET-REVIEW.md` writes them (`fascia_common.AMENDMENT_TEXT`; every one is also in `manifest.json` and in the check):

1. **Hand jitter.** Drawn at baseline SD **1.0 mm**, each glyph clipped at +-1.6 mm (not a range); judged **per board**, the flat-bottomed glyphs of all its painted and gilded blocks pooled, accepted at **0.45 to 2.0 mm**; vinyl, applied and glass blocks, pooled, at most 0.6 mm. (The pooled SD removes the line fit's degrees of freedom; glyphs under 0.85 of the cap, the round-bottomed lower-case letters of "Tea Rooms", are not baselines.) The per-block `.jitter` ids are answered by the board's pooled value.
2. **Advance jitter does not accumulate:** each glyph's pen is off by at most 1.5 per cent of its own advance from its true pen position. Rotation 0.35 degrees and stroke 3 per cent are drawn too (all four, with each board's wear, in the false-failure test).
3. **Glyph mask (G10) 3.5 mm** for hand-painted letters. Taken literally, as a Euclidean disc of 3.5 mm (a square of 4 pixels would reach 5.7 mm on the diagonals). See the judgement on the mirror margin below.
4. **Ink bottoms** of a string with no flat-bottomed glyph (Rita's 5s) are compared with `ink_box_mm[1]`, +-3 mm, not with the baseline.
5. **Tea Rooms sized by its H**, like every other block: 285.31 px an em, cap 200 mm meaning the H (the T then measures 204.8 mm; the cap check says so).
6. **The grocer's "11" goes on the SHOP door's fanlight**, not on a side-door fanlight the grocer does not have (`BAY_WITHOUT_SIDE_DOOR = 5`). The target's glass spec allows it: the kit's shop-door fanlight is x +-0.453 m, z 2.131 to 2.400, and the figures stand at z 2.18 to 2.29 (cap 110). ## Judgements: what the target left to me, and what I did with it

1. **Weather and wear numbers.** The counts, lengths, sizes and loss fractions are each shop's `age` numbers; the SHAPE of paint loss is P2's (median aspect 3.6, median equivalent diameter 9 mm); the pixels read 3.3 to 3.4 and 4.4 to 6.7 mm (see the result above: inside the check's band, under P2's middle). Loss is not even: it follows long damp bands and gathers at the foot and under the cornice, with an odd big peeled sheet. It shows a grey older coat, and in the bigger flakes the bare wood at the core (colours are per board and in the manifest's `loss`). It stays 24 mm off the board's edge (the moulding ring) and off every letter, rule and border. On a box sign the loss is the OLD BOARD's ring (8 per cent of that ring), not the lit face. Where the free ground is nearly full (the empty unit, 17 per cent) flakes shrink a little and sit one pixel apart.
2. **Where the rust starts.** Mickey's two: the console fixings at the board's ends, near the top. The ironmonger's two: the board's own nail heads at the left end. The chandler's three: fixings along the top edge. The empty unit's three: three of the six nail holes of the removed lettering (the other three have none). Rain runs hang from the top of any free stretch; gull marks and runs are kept off every letter. Gull marks and flakes are the same colour-world on every board, only the substrate colours change with the ground.
3. **Block shade** is a solid down-right extrusion of the glyph by the target's `d_mm` on each axis (the reference renderer's meaning), at 4x supersampling. Painted letters stand +0.20 mm, the shade +0.15, vinyl +0.08, gilt +0.05, keylines +0.15, loss -0.30 (older coat) and -0.50 (bare wood), the grocer's strips +0.8, joints -0.5, bevels a 2 mm ramp to -0.3, the planted moulding +0.6 mm with a 4 mm chamfer on the outer 24 mm (height map only, no colour).
4. **Mickey's contact shadow** is under the footprint of the letters (offset (-2, -5) mm, blur 6 mm, opacity 0.35), so the texture ALONE shows a soft dark MICKEY'S: it is the shadow the 14 mm stand-off throws, and the letters hide it. `fascias-mickeys-with-letters-v1-...jpg` lays the gilt letters flat over it, for a reviewer, and says PREVIEW ONLY. The check reads the shadow as "the band under the footprint" (the footprint pushed down 0 to 8 mm, less the footprint) against the ring 16 to 24 mm out: -3 to -9 L*.
5. **Ghosts.** Mickey's, the fish shop's and the ironmonger's are the target's exact words, fonts, sizes and colours; the share painted over is drawn on the part of the old lettering still IN VIEW (the ironmonger's ghost sits almost under its own newer name, 190 mm cap against 180: only a few strips are visible) so the number a viewer can measure is the target's 60 per cent. Rita's patch is brush texture only (3 dE, no glyphs); the empty unit's painted-out buff patch has six nail holes (black, 3.5 mm) and no letter.
6. **The grocer's glass** is reverse-painted clear plate glass (the target's method), three slabs with 3 mm mastic joints at 1380 and 4030. The board outside the chrome edge strip is dark mastic (30,30,28): the target does not say what the glass is set in.
7. **The lit boxes' emissive** (review note 5): base level 0.85 of the face colour, +6 per cent in a band round each tube row, 60 mm tube-end shadows 10 per cent dimmer at every joint in both rows, the laundry's dead tube (upper row, x 3300 to 4800, to 60 per cent, lower row full). The lengths beyond the first and last joint are lit by short end tubes at the same level, with no end shadow at the box's own ends.
8. **Numerals and glass rows.** Rows that are only figures are sized by the FIGURES' own height (Josefin Sans' stand at 0.9 of its H, so "11" at the target's 110 mm would otherwise measure 99). z_m of a row is its BASELINE (shop-room.py sets its glass lettering by its baseline), x_street_m the ink's centre. The whitewash and the empty unit's flaked "7" are hand-lettered (the same jitter); the gold rows have none. Gilt rows carry a black shade of 3 per cent of the cap where the target says "black shade" without a size.
9. **Hours plates** are read from `production/specs/hook-cast.json` at build time: `MON-SAT h-h`, a line for any day that differs (Wednesday half-day), `SUN h-h` or `SUN CLOSED`; times on a 12-hour clock (`5.30`, `10`). Every line matches the plates' own rule.
10. **The empty unit's whitewash** is a whole-pane haze of brush sweeps, nothing legible.

## Where the target contradicts itself or its own numbers (reported, not hidden)

1. **Three hanging-sign faces are too small for their words.** At the target's caps LAUNDERETTE is 645 mm on a 620 mm face (524 mm free), KEYS CUT 739 mm on a 550 mm board (442 free), CHANDLERY 997 mm on an 800 mm board (676 free). The manifest has both numbers. Either the town accepts the smaller letters, or the boards grow (and their lowest points are re-run).
2. **The newsagent's glass line is 1.69 m wide** (TOBACCONIST & CONFECTIONER at cap 70) but the shop door's glass is only 0.68 m (door light) to 0.9 m wide. The ink is 1.66 m (the tile 1.686 m, shown at 93 per cent on the preview sheet). It is drawn at the target's cap and centred as the target says; it can only be laid across the door AND the window beside it, or set in two lines, or smaller. Not decided here.
3. **G3 and G4 read against the target's own layouts.** G3 says each string appears once per board, but Rita's 5 and the chandler's 13 stand at both ends (as the target specifies, after P1) and the ironmonger's ghost is the same word as its name. G4 says a ghost word appears only in a ghost block, but IRONMONGER is the ironmonger's name and MICKEY'S is Mickey's. The check reads G3 as "no WORD is painted twice (numerals at the two ends are the layout)" and G4 as "a ghost word only in a ghost block or as the name of its own shop".
4. **The mirror margin of G10 at 3.5 mm.** The target's rule (the flipped re-render must score 0.15 lower) cannot hold at 3.5 mm for a heavy single word: CHANDLERY scores within 0.113 of its own mirror image. The 3.5 mm governs the pass score (F at least 0.90, the review's fix); the mirror margin is read at the smaller of the block's tolerance and 2.5 mm. A string that is within 0.15 of its own mirror image at that tolerance is symmetric to the check's power: the margin is waived for it and the result says so (the `symmetric_strings` mechanism the target already has); its mirrored-ness rests on its position check and the board's others. Five blocks are waived, each with its "reference vs its mirror" (0.856 to 0.91) in the result: the lone digits 1 (Mickey's), 3 (fish), 9 (laundry), and CHANDLERY on both faces of the chandler's hanging board.
5. **"Mickey's texture is not read by a pixel check"** (review note 2): added, no pixel within dE 14 of `brass_gilt` forms a patch (a handful of stray pixels is noise, a letter is thousands).
6. **No 1990 photograph of a fascia was reached** (the target, section 11). Nothing here is a likeness of one: colours, letter heights and wear rest on the Hook sheet, P1 and judgement, as the target says. The pictures are the textures flat; the look is made in Unreal, against the sheet.

## How the checks were made to read pixels, and where the target left a measure open

Every result in `check_v1.json` has `reads` (pixels, pixels+font, manifest, geometry) and a `note` saying the measure used. Where the target's wording left a measure open I chose, and say so:

- **G13 wear** counts connected marks in the `_wear.png` layers (the target says "the wear layers"), 6 pixels or more at 25 per cent, within one; and a second row per board (`.wear.visible`) checks each mark also shows in the BASE colour (dE 2 or more from its surroundings).
- **Paint loss (`.age`)**: pixels nearer a substrate colour than the ground round them, in the free zone, labelled; the fraction within the target's 0.012 absolute tolerance, the median equivalent diameter 4 to 16 mm, the median aspect at least 2. A box sign's face is judged on its own (none), its old-board ring on its own (8 per cent, within 3 points).
- **G2 moulding**: the height map's outer 20 mm ring against the ground of the band 30 to 50 mm in (rules, strips, flakes and marks are left out of the band); the chamfer is the 10-to-90 per cent ramp.
- **G5 / G6** read the board's free ground (flaked paint stays in: it follows the grain); G5 removes each row's mean (a vertical gradient is not a period), takes out everything slower than 60 px, normalises for the overlap and counts a lag only when at least a quarter of the lag-600 pairs are on free ground; G6 is the structure tensor of a 2 to 15 px band-pass of L*.
- **G14**: the ghost is measured by edge contrast against a local reference of the ground round it, on the part not under newer letters; and a whole-board "unlisted ink" row (no letter-sized, high-contrast mark that no block, ghost, line, wear mark, hole or flaked patch explains), tested with a stray word painted on to each board.
- **Border**: samples along every rule, rope, keyline, frame, face and panel edge in its colour, plus the edge rows of the thin bands to 4 mm.
- **Geometry checks (G15 mount, G17)** read the manifest's placement rows against the target's numbers: no mesh exists in this unit, so these nine rows are manifest-level, and the letters' box is re-measured from the font file.
- **Pins and nail holes**: near-black seeds grown to half their depth; on the empty unit the search is in the painted-out buff, because nothing can be told from a dot in its soot-dark flaked timber.
