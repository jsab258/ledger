# The pillar box's target checked against real photographs (11 October 2026)

Jafar's order of 11 October: check the pillar box's target against a real photograph before it is built. The network was open; done by the coordinating session, which neither wrote the target nor builds it.

**Verdict: FAIL on proportion.** Both photographs contradict the target's body width, its foot and its black band; the height and the slot's height agree. The build (unit 3.6) takes the amendments below; the rest of the target stands.

Both photographs are of boxes cast in the 1950s to 1980s and unaltered since; the casting is what a 1990 street had. Neither is dated before 1995, so the paint (the black base) is 2009 and 2012's; no photograph of 1990 was found (Geograph's dated search refuses the session; Wikimedia's API answered 429, rate-limited, on every try after the first two).

## The amendments for the build (3.6)

1. **Body 390 mm across** (F1's 48 inch circumference; with the target's 1500 above the ground the ratio is 3.85, inside P1 and P2's 3.68 to 3.98). The target's 489 came from the earlier research's "49 cm across", which neither photograph supports.
2. **Height 1500 above the footway kept** (F2's 54 to 60 inches above the ground; the earlier research's working figure).
3. **Cap rim 535 across** (1.37 of the body: P1 and P2), its rim band about 70 mm deep, the dome rising about 60 mm above it (P1: about 5 and 4 per cent of the height).
4. **No splayed foot.** The black base is the body's own diameter (at most 8 mm wider, a plain collar at its top) and **300 mm high** (P1 and P2: 17 to 27 per cent; 20 chosen). The wear target's black band (200) follows this when the masks are made (4.5).
5. **The slot 250 mm wide** (0.64 of the 390 body, P2), in its projecting hood under the cap, its middle at 1278 as the target has it.
6. Door, plate, reserved flush areas for the cypher and the lettering, paint and the rest: **as the target says**, scaled to the narrower body (the door's width and the plate's frame by 390 / 489 where they would not otherwise fit). The photographs show the crown and cypher above the lettering on the door; the target keeps both areas blank and flush (canon owes the cypher).

Type: F1 calls this size Type B (the commoner, smaller box); the target named its pattern "Type A". The photographs win on the shape: the build follows them, and the name in the target is left as written (a note, not a fault for the build). Recorded in DECISIONS.md.

## Addendum, 12 October 2026 (the first fresh review of the build's street view, kit/pillar-box/REVIEW-v1-street.md)

Five photographs (the two above, Rhayader 2011 Geograph 2401662, Alresford 2012 Geograph 3114422, Chester 2019 Geograph 6307696; CC BY-SA 2.0) show more than the first check read, and the build takes it: **the cap** is a collar ring just over the slot's hood (z 1350 to 1380), a deep concave flare to the rim's lower edge (1440), a rim band 1440 to 1485 with a beaded lower edge and a low dome rising about 15 (not the target's shelf, neck and 60 dome); **the collection plate's frame is upright**, about 130 x 240, its top about 45 under the slot; **the lock is on the viewer's right** (the target's frame line is mirrored: with +y the front and z up, the viewer's right is -x); **the cypher and crown come below the plate and the lettering below them**, so the two reserved flush areas move to z 620 (cypher) and z 420 (lettering).
