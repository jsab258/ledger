## What the review got wrong or amended

1. **The awning's slope (phone box fault 2).** The review gives the canopy as falling 0.995 m over 1.577 m. Read from the awning model's vertices (awning_02.glb), the canopy is one plane falling 1.003 m over 1.729 m (the mesh has no vertex between), with the valance 1.318 m below the top at its lowest. Over the box's front edge (1.04 m out from the wall) the canopy's top is then about 2.35 m above the crown, not 2.29 m; the shared check still finds the lid 26 mm from the canopy's underside (a 20 mm slab), so the review's "15 to 25 mm apart" and its finding hold; the reasons use the measured numbers (2.35 m, the valance to 1.63 m between z 3.39 and 3.40). The review's strip at head height is 0.53 m; the shared check measures 0.447 m with the box's full 890 depth (the first version's own depth, door to the road); either is under the 0.68 m a walker needs. Evidence: sd_place.py `_awning_slices`, and the control that puts the first version's x 23.5 pose back.
2. **The west wires' height (pole fault 3).** The review asks for about 3.3 m. At 3300 above the pavement a 100 mm anchor stands in the soffit of the drawn roof's 300 mm overhang (the soffit is 3.31 m at 90 mm out), so the anchors are 3200. Evidence: the `roof_west` plane in telegraph-pole/make_target.py and the row that every anchor lies under its eaves.
3. **The pole's bracket (not in the review, found while moving the pole).** The first version's bracket and knobs were on the -y face while its own slot text said the bracket faces the street's axis; fixed (see the pole).
4. **The bicycle's lean (not in the review).** The first version leaned the bicycle away from its wall; the review's "its 1.64 m of clear footway worked out" rested on that. Fixed (see the bicycle); the footway left is 1.22 m.
5. **The cottage window's sill (bicycle narrow point 1).** The review's reading of the drawn row is right on the places (a door near x 13.5, a window near x 15.9), and the front-doors-all and house-windows targets give the exact openings (door x 13.081 to 13.919, window x 15.475 to 16.325); the sill is 0.82 m above the crown there (0.8236), not the review's 0.9 m. The numbers from those targets are used.

Nothing else in the review was found wrong. For the record, two things the review left open were settled by reading: Schedule 1's plate size and stripe breadth (the breadth is measured vertically up the plate, which the texture and the row take), and the period code 0632 in the review's example number (a real code of the time, so the number is kept as a fictional placeholder for the town to replace).
