# Alison Sedman

The journalist. A principal of LEDGER (production/casting/CASTING.md); until 24
September she was Noor, and the old name survives only as an internal id
(noor). Text only: face, clothes and voice samples are the builder's.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Age** | 30 (born 1960) |
| **Origin** | Local; grammar school, the first of her family at college. |
| **Occupation** | A reporter on the town's evening paper (its name is still owed by the brand bible). Canon: a love interest in the day-life ring. |
| **Build** | About 5 ft 5 in, quick. |
| **Face** | Big glasses: by the late 1980s fashionable frames were smaller and rounder, so hers are a few years behind, which suits a provincial reporter. Big earrings. |
| **Hair** | Big permed hair. |
| **Clothes, 1990** | A jumper or blouse and a skirt, a belted mac, flat boots for walking, a shoulder bag with a spiral-top shorthand notebook and a biro, her contacts book never far off. |
| **How she moves** | Fast, always half writing; leans on counters; asks the second question after the answer. |
| **Voice** | Bright and quick, local, a reporter's neutral politeness over it. **No approved voice:** a woman of 30 with a local accent is still to cast, and nothing is cast without Jafar's yes. |

## Three lines

1. "I'm not writing anything yet. I'm just listening. People forget there's a difference."
2. "I like to hear a thing twice before I believe it once."
3. "You'd be surprised what people tell you when you don't ask."
