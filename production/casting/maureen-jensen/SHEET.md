# Maureen Jensen, "the Widow"

Head of the dockside syndicate. A principal of LEDGER
(production/casting/CASTING.md); until 24 September she was Sera Kest, and the
old name survives only as an internal id (sera; the voice file says kest). Text
only: face, clothes and voice samples are the builder's.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Age** | 57 (born 1933) |
| **Origin** | A fishing family of the Hook (Jensen is among the evidence's fishing-family names); a trawler-owner's widow. |
| **Occupation** | Heads the dockside syndicate: muscle and smuggling (canon). |
| **Build** | About 5 ft 3 in, solid, straight-backed. |
| **Face** | A hard face; smoker's lines round the mouth; careful make-up. |
| **Hair** | A lacquered blonde set: big, heavily styled hair carried into the early 1990s. |
| **Clothes, 1990** | A tailored dark suit or wool dress; heavy gold (sovereign rings, a chain, earrings). An older wool coat with a fur collar, bought years ago and worn without caring: by 1990 fur drew abuse in the street, and she has never cared what the street thinks. Court shoes. The fish-factory women she came from wore winter coats, white headscarves and wellingtons; everything she wears says how far she has come from them. |
| **How she moves** | She hardly does: people come to her. Sits facing the door. Smokes steadily and taps the ash as if it were a point being made. |
| **Voice** | Low, smoke-roughened, flat local vowels; says little and means it. Approved in July (as Kest): p244 (a woman, 22, English, Manchester). **Needs an older voice:** 22 against 57, and a Manchester accent against a woman of the Hook. |

## Three lines

1. "I've learned to count. You'd be surprised how few round here have."
2. "I don't like surprises, love. I've had enough of them for one life."
3. "I'm not interested in what you are. I'm interested in what you'll do."
