# DS Carol Ellis

The detective. A principal of LEDGER (production/casting/CASTING.md); until 24
September she was Mara Ellis, and the old name survives only as an internal id
(ellis). Text only: face, clothes and voice samples are the builder's.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Age** | 40 (born 1950) |
| **Origin** | A local woman who came up through the town's force. One of few women detectives then (the share of women in CID in 1990 is unsourced). |
| **Occupation** | Detective Sergeant, CID, in plain clothes. Which force Meridian's police belong to is not decided in canon, so the sheet names none. Canon: police are corruptible as individuals, never as a thesis; nothing on this sheet says which she is. |
| **Build** | About 5 ft 5 in, slim and upright; the average woman of 1993 was 5 ft 3 in. |
| **Face** | Minimal make-up; lines of tiredness rather than age. |
| **Hair** | Short and practical: a feathered bob, mid-brown. |
| **Clothes, 1990** | A navy or grey jacket and skirt, a plain blouse, tights, low court shoes or flat boots for walking, a shoulder bag, a belted raincoat. Skirt suits with modest shoulder pads were what working women wore. |
| **How she moves** | Brisk and economical. Writes in her notebook while she listens and lets silences run. Stands side-on in doorways, where she can see the room and the street. Procedure is her drama (Prime Suspect, 1991, is the reference for the procedure, not for her face). |
| **Voice** | Level and quick, a local accent, short questions with gaps left after them. Approved in July: p231 (a woman, 23, English, southern). **Needs an older voice:** 23 against 40, and southern against a local woman. |

## Three lines

1. "I'm not asking you to remember everything. I'm asking you to remember it the same way twice."
2. "Write it down. Paper doesn't get tired and it doesn't change its mind."
3. "You can talk to me here, or you can talk to me at the station. I'd rather here. So would you."
