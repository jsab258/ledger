# Casting the Hook

Who lives in the game's town, and who the characters are. Drawn from the
casting research (production/research/casting/, SUMMARY.md and DELIVERY.md,
where every number below is sourced) and Jafar's rulings of 24 September
(DECISIONS.md, canon.md). Nothing here is built until its sheet is approved
on an approval page; nothing is multiplied until one complete sample is
approved in the assembled game (CLAUDE.md).

## The town's mix: Hull and Grimsby, the 1991 census

- **Age:** among adults, about 29% in their late teens and twenties, 36% aged
  45 or over. Two in three people over 75 were women.
- **Background:** 98.8% white. The largest minorities were Hong Kong Chinese
  families in Hull and Indian families in Grimsby, a few hundred people each.
  Fewer than 1 in 40 born abroad.
- **Home and work:** in Hull over a third of households rented from the
  council and half had no car; unemployment about 12%, worse for men. Fishing
  was under 1% of jobs by 1990.
- **Looks:** about 30% smoked; half of those over 55 had no natural teeth; one
  adult in seven obese; people slightly shorter than now and looking older for
  their age. Perms and sets on older women, moustaches on many middle-aged
  men, big glasses, faded tattoos only on seafarers and hard men, ears the only
  piercings, nothing groomed.
- **The rules, always:** no children anywhere, every face clearly adult (a
  face a test group guesses under 21 is recast); no real person in any face or
  voice (reverse image search, and "who does this remind you of?"); minorities
  placed as real families, never a lone token; older women among the leads.

**The thirty regulars, as census shares:** 8 in their twenties, 8 in their
thirties and early forties, 6 aged 45-59, 5 aged 60-74 (3 women), 3 over 75
(2 women); 14 men, 16 women. By trade: 4 cab drivers and a dispatcher, 5 fish
market and processing, 6 shop and café, 2 office, 2 dock and shipping, 5
retired, 3 keeping house, 2 out of work. One Hong Kong Chinese family runs the
parade's takeaway (two people), and an Italian-descended café family with
English first names belongs with the white majority.

**The sixteen crowd faces:** women about 23, 33, 41, 49, 57, 65, 72, 80; men
about 24, 32, 40, 48, 56, 63, 70, 77. One East Asian and one South Asian face,
each placed about once in 150. Period traits applied as settings: moustaches
on a third of men 35-60, a perm or set on half of women over 45, glasses on a
third, smokers' marks on 4 in 10 working men. The variety goes on jackets,
hats, glasses, bags and cigarettes: people notice repeated clothes before
repeated faces.

## What a sheet holds, and how it is approved

Each principal's sheet has age, occupation, a front portrait, a profile, a
full-length figure, clothes, hair, and three lines spoken in the chosen voice.
Face, clothes and voice are approved together, on one approval page per
sitting, and the approval file sits beside the sheet (tools/approvals.py).
Only then is the MetaHuman built to the approved portrait, and only then is
the next principal's built.
