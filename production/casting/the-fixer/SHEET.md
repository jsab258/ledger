# Keith Garbutt, the Fixer

The broker between all three rivals. A principal of LEDGER
(production/casting/CASTING.md). His name is Keith Garbutt, the casting
research's proposal (Jafar, 28 September); most call him the Fixer. Text only: face, clothes
and voice samples are the builder's.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Age** | 48 (born 1942) |
| **Origin** | Of the town; nobody can say which side he started on, which is the point of him. |
| **Occupation** | Brokers between the old-money machine, the dockside syndicate and the new crew (canon). |
| **Build** | Medium, about 5 ft 9 in, neat, a little heavy at the middle. |
| **Face** | A neat moustache: the 1970s and 1980s were its heyday and the 1990s preferred a bare lip, so it says he settled his look in the seventies. |
| **Hair** | Dark going grey, short and neat. |
| **Clothes, 1990** | A car coat, between a jacket and a three-quarter coat, in cotton suedette or brushed nylon lined with pile (sheepskin was the football manager's); driving gloves, slip-on shoes, a plain suit or slacks and a knitted shirt. Not the camel coat and trilby of the TV wheeler-dealer, which is the cliché to steer away from. |
| **How he moves** | Calm; sits with his back to a wall; never raises his voice; lights other people's cigarettes before his own. |
| **Voice** | Even and unhurried, local, never raised. **No approved voice:** a man of 48 is still to cast, older than any approved man's voice (the oldest, Ron's p227, is 38). |

## Three lines

1. "I don't take sides. I take a percentage. It's a more honest arrangement."
2. "Everybody wants the same thing. They just want it first."
3. "Say what you need. Whether I can help is another conversation."
