# June

Mickey's estranged daughter, and so Tom's first cousin (canon: Tom is the son of
Mickey's sister). A principal of LEDGER (production/casting/CASTING.md); she
keeps her name. Her surname would be her father's, Suddaby, unless she married
(the casting research's proposal; not yet ruled: see the page). Text only:
face, clothes and voice samples are the builder's.

| **Voice** | Quick and clipped, the local accent worn down by years away. Approved on 24 September: p277 (a woman, 23, English, North-East England). **Needs an older voice:** 23 against 38, by the same rule as the others; the North-East accent is near a local one but not it. |

## Three lines

1. "I didn't come back for the business. I came back because somebody had to."
2. "I'm not stopping long. I've a life to get back to."
3. "Don't make it a speech. Just say it."
