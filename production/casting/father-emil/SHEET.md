# Father Emil

The priest. A principal of LEDGER (production/casting/CASTING.md). His name is
not yet ruled: the casting research offers an Irish-born parish priest, Father
Brendan Walsh, or keeping Emil as the priest of the town's Polish community,
who would have known Tom's father (see the question on the page). Text only:
face, clothes and voice samples are the builder's.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Age** | About 61 (born about 1929) |
| **Origin** | Either Irish-born, as many English Catholic priests of his generation were said to be (unsourced), or of the Polish community that settled after the war. The sheet waits on the name. |
| **Occupation** | The Catholic parish priest. Canon: religion is present as part of life, never mocked, never a mechanic. Where his church stands, canon does not say (the routines of 28 September put it off Quay Street). |
| **Build** | Medium height, thin, a little stooped; about 5 ft 8 in. |
| **Face** | Clean-shaven, lined, attentive; reading glasses in a top pocket. |
| **Hair** | Thinning, grey, combed flat. |
| **Clothes, 1990** | A black suit over a black clerical shirt and collar, which a man trained in the 1950s wears all day; round the parish a black or grey pullover or cardigan over the clerical shirt, black trousers and shoes, a dark raincoat, perhaps a black beret. The cassock had gone from everyday wear in the 1960s. |
| **How he moves** | An unhurried walk round the parish with his hands behind his back; stops for everybody; calls on the old. Listens with his head a little down. |
| **Voice** | Soft and slow; Irish if he is Father Walsh, a faint Polish lilt if he is Emil. Approved in July: p245 (a man, 25, Irish, Dublin). **Needs an older voice** (25 against 61). The Irish accent fits only if he is Father Walsh. |

## Three lines

1. "I don't ask where people have been. I ask whether they're all right."
2. "I'm not here to ask you anything. I'm here in case you want to say something."
3. "Sit a minute. Nobody's going to mind you sitting a minute."
