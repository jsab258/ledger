# Tom Nowak

The player. A principal of LEDGER (production/casting/CASTING.md); until 24
September the name was spelt Novak. Text only: face, clothes and any voice are
the builder's, made to this sheet once it is approved.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Age** | 32 (born 1958) |
| **Origin** | Son of a Polish post-war settler and Mickey's sister. The family moved away from the port when he was small (the casting research's account, 7.3), so he is a stranger to everyone in the Hook, known only as Mickey's nephew by name (canon). Where he grew up and what he did there is left unsaid: nobody in the game asks and he never tells (Jafar, 28 September). |
| **Occupation** | The new owner of Mickey's, the minicab office on Quay Street, with its half-dead outfit and book of uncollectable debts (canon). Arrives with one suitcase and a letter. |
| **Build** | About 5 ft 9 in (175 cm, the average man of 1993), lean; nothing about him says money. |
| **Face** | Clean-shaven (a shave read as trustworthy then), no tattoos, ordinary. |
| **Hair** | Short, dark brown, cut plainly: short back and sides. |
| **Clothes, 1990** | He arrives after the funeral, which he missed; his one good suit, a plain dark two-piece, is for the business of the inheritance. Day to day: jeans, a plain jumper or sweatshirt, leather shoes or trainers, a zip-front nylon or cotton jacket for the rain. One suitcase: he is seen in the same few things again and again, which the town will notice. |
| **How he moves** | A stranger's walk: head up (nobody in 1990 walks looking at a phone), reading shop boards and doorways, stopping where a local would not. The sheet does not make him a smoker (about three men in ten were). |
| **Voice** | The player's lines are typed, so Tom needs no voice. If he is ever to speak aloud he needs casting: of the approved men's voices one is 38 (Ron's) and the rest are 21 to 25, and none is his. |
