1. **Age and build.** A soft, wide lower face and jaw, deep recession at the temples and hollows under the eyes put him in his mid-to-late thirties and not lean. The sheet wants 32, lean, ordinary.
2. **Hair.** Near-black from the front, dark brown only in profile; and the cut reads two ways: a lifted, glossy swept-back top in front, a bristly brush in profile, with a thin, patchy sideburn. The sheet wants plain dark brown, short back and sides.
3. **Brows and eyes.** The brows are still a shade lighter and warmer than the hair; pink lower-lid rims give a slightly sore-eyed look; in this brighter light the skin reads very pale.
