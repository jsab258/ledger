# Danny Cammack

Head of the new crew. A principal of LEDGER (production/casting/CASTING.md);
until 24 September he was Danny Ro, and the old id survives (danny). Text only:
face, clothes and voice samples are the builder's.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Age** | 27 (born 1963) |
| **Origin** | Grimsby-side; Cammack is a Lincolnshire name. |
| **Occupation** | Heads the new crew: flashy and reckless (canon); loud money (CASTING.md). |
| **Build** | About 5 ft 10 in, wiry, restless. |
| **Face** | Clean-shaven, quick-eyed; a smoker. |
| **Hair** | Short on top and long at the back, the cut everywhere in 1980s Britain. **Nobody in the game calls it a mullet:** the word only caught on in 1994. |

| **Voice** | Fast and loud, Lincolnshire, laughing at his own lines. Approved on 24 September: p243 (a man, 22, English, London). The age is near enough; **the accent is not: a London voice for a Grimsby man.** Flagged for the accent check, not for age. |

## Three lines

1. "Old men and their arrangements. It's 1990, mate. Nobody asks permission any more."
2. "Always in a hurry, me. Somebody's got to be."
3. "You want to watch who you stand next to round here. You're stood next to me."
