## 2. The work, in build order

Hours include the piece's own reviewers. Two tries each; a piece passes only in that night's packaged walk.

1. **Baseline.** Every check above on today's frame: Monday's "before". *Ref:* none found. *0.5 h.* *Check:* one table, each number with its tool.
2. **Real values.** Read the unmeasured surfaces (Mickey's paint and gilt, joinery, slates, tiles, kiosk, render); move outliers only. *Ref:* neither game's albedos found (WET-OVERCAST-VALUES §6); MAFIA-WEAR C1, C6. *0.25 h.* *Check:* each in band.
3. **No frozen poses.** Find why idles hold still packaged (walk of 29 October: three stiff by the fish shop); then looping idles, random start, play rate 0.9 to 1.1, neighbours differing. *Ref:* Mafia's 389 looping scenes (studies/01-npc-life.md); production/research/natural-idles/NOTE.md §4. *0.75 h.* *Check:* everyone within 30 m moves a head, hand or chest bone 1 cm in any 4 s (inferred), logged; the tester names no mannequin.
4. **Nobody overlapping.** Standing places become slots, cap one; the same clearance test on every move. *Ref:* Kingdom Come II's capped places, Mafia's slots (studies/01-npc-life.md). *0.5 h.* *Check:* no two bodies within 0.6 m (inferred), none inside Tom, a prop or a door's swing, over a walk's log; Ron and Darren at the rank the wrong copy.
5. **The pale line.** Test other objects on the coat first (DECISIONS 1 November 21:53), then research the coat at a contact. *Ref:* Mafia's puddle decals carry occlusion (WET-GROUND-METHOD-2026-10-29.md §2); none found for contacts. *0.75 h.* *Check:* a 2 cm band at each contact within 5 levels of the ground 15 cm off (inferred); wet_ground_check.py no worse.
6. **Brick that does not repeat.** Per house: whole-brick offset and tone; world-space variation at 2 to 10 m; the chandler's streak extended. *Ref:* Mafia's world noise on 31% of placements (MAFIA-KIT-2026-10-28.md §3); production/research/aaa-street/BRICK-2026-10-04.md, methods 1, 3, 4. *1.0 h.* *Check:* the base-colour repeat peak under a threshold set in step 1; 5 to 15% brightness across a bay (MAFIA-WEAR S3); a reviewer hunting the repeat finds none.
7. **Walk off, never vanish.** A person whose hour moves them walks to their door, the yard mouth or the street's end, removed only out of view; the 12 s vanish goes. *Ref:* Mafia removes civilians 5 m out of view; Kingdom Come II's extras leave by a door (studies/01-npc-life.md). *1.0 h.* *Check:* no removal in view over a walk (each logged); the tester keeps Ron.
8. **Midweek page** (section 4).
9. **A kit with trims.** Mickey's front onto the chandler's timber sheet and painted wood; sills and lintels onto a stone sheet; gutters and pipes onto a metal sheet. The terrace kit trial folds in: its table becomes the corner's kit table. *Ref:* Mafia's trims, 26% of slots (MAFIA-KIT §2). *1.0 h.* *Check:* 512 px a metre within 20%; worn paint 3 to 10%, at edges (MAFIA-WEAR S2); asset_checks.py; no lit pier at night.
10. **Dressing tier 1, the nine planned kinds first.** Ruled out: skip and pallets (off until real, 2 October), the bench (2.0 m footway). Build the pavement board and the milk crates (milk only, by canon); mend the kiosk's see-through side. *Ref:* Mafia's 4.9 props per 10 m (studies/04-street-world.md). *1.0 h.* *Check:* each family's checks and sd_place.py; at least 15 true props in the 30 m.
11. **Dressing tiers 2 and 3.** Ground: litter, gum, leaves, moss, broken flags, densest on the walked line. Walls: wear decals by MAFIA-WEAR's rule; pipes, wires, signs. *Ref:* Mafia's 73 m street: 91 ground pieces, 8 decals, 18 add-ons (studies/04-street-world.md). *0.75 h.* *Check:* ground 12 per 10 m, add-ons 2.5 (Mafia's, first bars); decals by kind per wall (tools/stain_check.py).
12. **A reaction at Rita's window.** Wire the ported reaction rules: a bold witness walks toward it, a timid one shouts; passers-by in the next hour slow and look 2 to 4 s (his 23 September ruling). *Ref:* Kingdom Come II's look, check, watch; Mafia's stop, look, startle (studies/01-npc-life.md). *0.75 h.* *Check:* within 3 s of the smash someone in view turns to the window for 2 s or more (logged); nobody passes within 8 m without a look.
13. **Light set by values.** First confirm or clear the 1 November walk's "the street lamps give no light"; then C2, C3, C4 on the new surfaces; a lamp changed only if one fails. *Ref:* Mafia's lamp, judged by lamp-to-ambient ratio (studies/05-visuals.md, item 3); NIGHT-GAPS-2026-10-29.md. *0.5 h.* *Check:* C2 in band; C4 4 to 20; clip 2% at most (tools/measure_night_pools.py).
14. **Final page** (section 4).

Total about 9.75 hours, the pages' 1.0 included.
