# Review of STEP9-PLAN-2026-11-03.md (3 November 2026, fresh reviewer)

**Verdict: PASS WITH FIXES**

1. **Numbers match (Q1).** uvdens.py and uvdir.py were read and re-run on quay-street.glb (15:21). Medians 1.000 on every mesh; p10 0.935 on frame_painted; sign fascia 0.620; areas 0.3+4.1+4.1=8.5, 115.3, 444.7, 17.0, 20,810.7, 637.7 m2; COLOR_0 constant (1,0,1,1). The scripts measure what is claimed, with two limits. uvdir only reports U on vertical faces (46 to 84% of area), and none runs vertical; "right on fascias, rails, sills, lintels" (plan line 11) is not measured. Both scripts read TEXCOORD_0 only.
2. **Route (Q2).** Followed: material only, no rebuilt mesh, after the office passes packaged (CORNER-PLAN lines 78, 33). But 9a drops "the chandler's painted-wood material" (CORNER-PLAN line 78): M_LedgerKitPaintWood needs UV 2 (make_kit_materials.py line 394), which the street mesh lacks. Say so; the plan implies Mafia's paint-over-wood is reproduced, though chips are not.
3. **Density (Q3).** 2048/4.2 = 487.6 px/m, inside 410 to 614: correct. But the plan cuts the board strip (v 0 to 0.75) square, so the tile is 1536 px over 3.15 m, and covers_m must be 3.15, not 4.2. Not stated.
4. **Stone density error.** Plan line 19: 2k is about 680 px/m, "resampled to 512". Literal 512 px over 3 m is 171 px/m. 683 px/m is 33% over target, so a resample is needed: 512 px/m means 1536 px per 3 m tile. Metal: a 1 m tile needs 512 px (METAL-READING line 48); not stated.
5. **EdgeFromBox (Q4).** Right for the street-wide meshes: one box per material instance (make_kit_materials.py lines 390, 560), and the extents are 83 to 420 m. Weaker for Mickey's own three small meshes (0.9 m extents, separate names); the plan calls meshes "one per material row" (line 13) yet lists Mickey's as separate. Whether EdgeFromBox could serve them (frame parts, not a single box) is untested, not shown impossible. The glb has no materials block, so mesh-to-row mapping is unverified.
6. **Row mapping unsupported.** Spec rows are frame_painted and paint_fascia (ps5-corner.json lines 432, 450); the measured meshes are street_paint_joinery and fascia_slate_blue. The plan does not show which holds Mickey's pilasters, door and fascia (the reading says pilasters come from the shopfront kit, STEP9-READING line 14). "The hero front" is unproven.
7. **Unsupported as fact.** "No lit pier at night ... so not expected" (line 17): RECIPE says the cause was not found (STEP9-READING line 10). The "lead" row is 638 m2 over 397 m, far more than downpipes; the plan checks "stone" parts but not "lead". The rusty_metal_02 colour and flatness come from page text only; no picture seen (METAL-READING line 5). "Black-painted cast iron" is correctly marked unverified.
8. **4 October note (Q5).** No contradiction: the note says concrete scan on timber failed and the joinery waits for a painted-timber surface (ps5-corner.json lines 447, 465). The accepted grain across pilasters is a new risk the note does not cover. Rows need diff, normal and rough maps (lines 350 to 354); wood_trim has masks (roughness in R), so a conversion step is missing from the 2 h.
9. **Mafia source.** Paint Roughness 0.32 and the S2 check are correct (MAFIA-WEAR lines 28, 134). S2 requires worn paint 3 to 10%, which 9c leaves unmet; the plan says so.
