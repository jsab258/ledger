A third fresh reviewer: FAIL on both men.
1. Both: the hem a fat rounded bolster flaring like a bell, ballooning and curling under walking; a melton hem is a
   flat turned edge that hangs straight. In the rest shape, so cloth will not remove it.
2. Both: the sleeves inflated, widest at the forearm and gathered at the cuff like a smock; a donkey jacket's sleeve
   is a straight stiff tube. With 1, it reads as a padded jacket.
3. Ron: a round chest outline on his left front; the shiny back yoke tracing his shoulder blades and spine.
4. Darren: the carry keeps a heavy man's volume: a shelf across the back at the waist, the lower back bagging past
   the side seams; sitting, a belly and chest bulge wrong for a thin man.
Narrow: the buttons stand off in profile; the collar stand open at the throat, its points thin; a pale smear on
Ron's right chest; the lower edge of Darren's right front yoke jagged; small steps at the back hem corners; arms
raised, the body does not rise and a dark line at the underarm (Unreal's correctives); walking the thigh through
the front skirt and sitting the skirt through the seat, acceptable only if Chaos cloth is shown to cover it in
Unreal; the front yoke two angled patches, not one straight band; the pockets faint.
Right: navy with a black glossy yoke front and back, the back yoke full width to armpit level with a straight edge;
four dark buttons evenly spaced; two flapless patch pockets low; a turn-down collar, no lapels; the length right;
no waist shaping or vent; open below the last button; it resizes to Darren (about 10% narrower at the chest);
standing, no tears, nothing floating, no body through.

SET ASIDE (the two-tries rule: three reviews of the game-way jacket, the last after research; review 1 had passed
Ron). What works and stays: the game mesh from the flat pattern (about 21,500 triangles, clean seams, both UV
sets), the bake (yoke, pockets, front edge, seams, roughness), the skinning on the MetaHuman skeleton with the joints
corrected, the carry to a second body of the same mesh, the skirt's SimMaxDistance. What fails is the shape the
cloth drape gives: the rolled hem, the full sleeves and the bodies' forms, the same "padded coat" the morning's
procedural jacket was failed for. Whether to try its shape once more, and how, is Jafar's scope call (CLOTHES.md).
