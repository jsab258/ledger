# The MakeHuman suit jacket on Ron and Darren: blind review 1 (30 September 2026)

Men's Suit 3 by Margaret Toigo (MakeHuman community, CC0; THIRD-PARTY.md), fitted to each man's MetaHuman body by
tools/meshgen/blender/fit_mhclo.py, made game-ready by mh_garment.py (the trousers left out; re-coloured; the body's
hollows bridged; the skirt plumb and its hem levelled), skinned by skin_garment.py. A fresh reviewer judged it
standing (textured) and in four poses (grey) against Tom Nowak's sheet and photographs of British men in dark suits,
1989 to 1991 (John Major at Camp David, 22 December 1990; the G7 in London, 15 July 1991; Clive Hollick, July 1989).
Pictures: F:/LedgerTools/tmp/clothes/suit/gate1.

VERDICT: FAIL on both.
1. The shirt's cuffs through the sleeve ends as torn white shards.
2. The lapels crumpled, ragged and stepped; white flecks of shirt beside the wearer's left lapel.
3. The breast pocket a thick puffy lifted flap, a pouch on the chest.
4. The body's shape through the jacket: Darren's spine, ribs, back muscles and stomach lines; Ron's back rolls; the
   cleft of the seat sitting; a paunch on Darren sitting.
5. Ron: skin through a gap at the back of both armpits.
6. The back vent and hem jagged and stepped in profile.
Excused as the cloth's and Unreal's: the thighs through the front skirt walking and sitting; the underarms pinching.
Narrow: the cut reads 1960s or 2010s (narrow lapels, a short V, three close buttons, a skinny tie) where 1989 to 1991
has wide notch lapels, a deep V to one button and padded shoulders; a wobbling stripe in the cloth; no waist shaping,
a size large on Darren; square front corners; no shoulder structure; the hip flaps unequal.
Right: dark charcoal-navy; single-breasted, notch lapels, flap hip pockets, the breast pocket on the left, a centre
back vent; buttoning a man's way; four sleeve buttons; the length covers the seat; the shirt collar at the back of the
neck; it encloses both builds, nothing floating or torn through the body; the back clean at a distance.

WHAT WAS DONE (the method first): the fit drew the jacket onto his real skin, grooves and muscles included, and moved
its stacked layers along fixed directions; so it is now fitted to a stand-in body whose hollows are filled (the
research's "proxy that fills the body's hollows"), and carried by the base body's own change round each point, so
layers move together (fit_mhclo.py). The shirt's cuffs are left out (the sleeve hides them); every layer at a place
moves out alike when the hollows are bridged, and the lapels' V is left alone; layers that never show are taken out;
the cloth's stripe softened; the body the jacket covers hidden in the pictures, as the game hides it. Taking the
pocket's handkerchief out, and pressing it flat, both tore the lapel's layers beside it: the maker's pocket is kept.
