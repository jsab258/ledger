# Blind review 1 (29 September, evening): FAIL

A fresh reviewer, shown nothing of the making, judged run 16 (the jacket pressed and finished, in the export pose and worn in four test poses: arms down, raised, walking, sitting; the finished render mesh carried on the coarse simulation mesh, as Unreal will carry it) against production/reference/donkey-jacket-1990.md and the V&A's T.561-1993. "From across a room it reads as a donkey jacket. Up close, three things look broken or wrong."

Obvious faults, and what was done:

1. The chest moulds to the body: both pectorals and a crease under them, like a stretch shirt; the V&A's falls flat and straight from the yoke. Done: tailor.span_hollows, the small hollows of the cloth's outline (up to 3 cm, below the armpits) spanned across and down, as stiff melton bridges them, the moves smoothed so no step shows (unbounded at first, it squared the shoulders into a box).
2. Dense small papery crinkles on both forearms in the export pose. Done: the sleeves pressed a second time on their own.
3. The body through the inside of the upper arm in the walk, and the hem through the fingers. Done in the pose test: the cloth held at half within 9 cm of each armpit, so the body's collision can push it clear.

Narrow points: the collar small, close at the throat, on a tall padded band (done: a broader fall, 72 mm, points spread out over the yoke, a lower stand); the front yoke two wedges, not an even band (done: 17 cm under the shoulder seam, so a band shows below the collar); a roll of wool under the back yoke and a rippled yoke edge (done: the back shortened 3 cm above the armhole, tools/meshgen/alter_brian.py, the belly taking the front's length); the back swinging out like a cape with the arms down; the body not lifting with the arms raised, crumples in the armpits; sitting with no chair; buttons small and flat (done: 25 mm, rounder); surfaces slightly soft and inflated.

Right: navy-black body, glossy black yoke front and back, the back yoke armhole to armhole down to about armpit level; the length at the top of the thigh, the back about 88 cm; buttoned to the neck, no lapels; two large flapless patch pockets low; set-in sleeves, fairly slim, to the wrist, not riding up; square front corners, no vent, no waist shaping; no spikes, tears or quilting.
