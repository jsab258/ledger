# Darren's plain black lace-up boots: blind review 1 (1 October 2026), against the bar

Jafar's ruling of 1 October (relayed by the builder; his sheet changed the same day): plain 1990 clothes, no contrast
stitching, no trainers; every visual judged against the bar (production/reference/hook-sheet.png and the KCD2 frames).
Made by tools/meshgen/blender/model_footwear.py --kind boot on F:/LedgerTools/bodies/MH_SamC5/MH_SamC5_FullBody.fbx
(16,500 triangles, a 28 mm sole), tested skinned standing, walking, sitting and on a stair (pictures:
F:/LedgerTools/tmp/clothes/feet/db1). The reviewer compared V&A Dr Martens boots (R. Griggs, about 1982, O38480; a
1980s pair, O351949) and a worn black 1460 on Wikimedia Commons.

VERDICT: FAIL. "Fine in motion ... In the standing views they look like a cartoon boot."
1. The collar's top edge cut like a saw blade all round the ankle.
2. A wavy, frilled join between upper and sole, worst at the heel.
3. The wrong shape: a flat-topped brick toe and a second slab on top (a step in the side outline), where a 1460 rises in
   one curve from a domed toe to the instep.
4. The laces drawn flat on a closed tube: no opening, no tongue edge, no lace ends.
5. A blob heel from behind.
Narrow: the shaft too short and wide (eight eyelets, above the ankle bone, a padded collar); no toe lift, no creases, no
heel loop; floating laces.
Against the bar: "Kingdom Come II's footwear has the right outline before any texture goes on ... Texturing cannot
fix a box toe, a stepped side, a saw-tooth collar or a frilled sole join. Even textured, they would sit well below a
2026 game at that standard; they read as a placeholder."
Right: plain black, no yellow stitch, no trainer details; the sole's thickness and welt shelf; no poke-through in any
pose; the length.

WHAT IT MEANS (the method, not the symptom): footwear lofted from the foot's own skin cannot reach the bar; the
professional route, and the free one, is a modelled shoe: Epic's own MetaHuman boots (in its wardrobe, and the free
MetaHuman footwear on Fab, production/research/clothing-pipeline/TAILORED-ROUTES-2026-09-30.md). Not tried again here;
the builder swaps Epic's plainest black leather lace-up boots for Darren, and compares Epic's with Ron's own boots
(passed on 30 September, before the bar) against the bar.
