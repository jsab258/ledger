# Darren's trainers, blind review 3 (30 September, night): FAIL, set aside

The last try, after research (production/research/clothing-pipeline/SHOE-TOE-AND-LACING-2026-09-30.md): the toe drawn as a last is (its top falling from the ball to a 15 mm nose, the inner edge straight, the outer curving in, the tip on the second toe), an open lace throat with seven pairs, a tongue under it standing 20 mm above the collar, raised eyestays, a padded collar, the toe cap and heel counter as panels a shade apart, the upper meeting the sole on one clean line. A fresh reviewer judged it against his concept and the V&A's 1986 Diadora trainers.

Obvious faults:

1. The toe and sole: the forefoot long, wide and flat, the toe squared like a duck bill; the sole an even slab with no toe spring or heel drop and a lip past the upper round the toe: a slipper, clog or bowling shoe (the references: a rounded, fairly narrow toe lifting off the ground, a deeper sole at the heel).
2. No tongue or lace opening seen: the laces flat on a closed upper like a pressed pattern; the tongue a thin strip barely showing (the references: a padded tongue above the top eyelet, a laced gap between eyelet panels, a bow with loose ends).
3. The heel from behind: the counter lumpy with diagonal creases; the collar a thick rolled tube like a folded sock; the sole bulging at both sides of the heel (a firm smooth counter, a heel tab, a collar dipping at the Achilles).

Narrow points: about 10 per cent long; no panel lines in the shape; no bow to be seen; the sitting pose tests little.

Right: a plain white low-top, no logos; the collar's height; seven pairs of eyelets; a believable cupsole depth; the collar hides the foot; the pair match; clean in every pose, nothing through, torn or left behind.

Set aside under the two-tries rule (three reviews, the last after research). The three reviewers disagreed on points of fact (the second saw the toe spring and cupsole edge as right; this one saw neither), which suggests plain grey renders without leather textures are hard to judge; the best candidate is kept in F:/LedgerTools/tmp/clothes/feet/t30.
