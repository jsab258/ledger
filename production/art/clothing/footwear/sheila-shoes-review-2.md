# Sheila's shoes, blind review 2 (30 September, night): FAIL

A fresh reviewer judged the second attempt (model_footwear.py --kind shoe, rebuilt as the trainers' second try, with a 4 mm rim on the sole) against her concept and the V&A's Rayne brogue of March 1989 (O1385780).

Obvious faults:

1. The toe a swollen dome, widest and tallest at the very front and bulging over the sole: cartoon shoes at a glance (the concept and the Rayne: a neat, low, rounded toe narrowing forward from the ball).
2. The heel counter like crumpled cloth: its lower edge lumpy and wavy with bunched folds; a horizontal slit across the back and a small nick below it (a leather counter is a smooth, firm cup).
3. Parts that look torn: thin slivers of the outer quarter near the heel standing away from the shoe; a sharp diagonal cut from the quarter down into the heel on the outer side.

Narrow points: the lacing small and high by the ankle, three or four pairs bunched, a tiny flat bow, no tongue or open throat, the eyelets as if printed on; the collar a little off the ankle at the back; the cap-toe seam fine though the concept reads plain; the sole's edge a little proud at the toe.

Right: clearly a flat brown leather lace-up of 1990, a low stacked heel and flat sole; the length; the topline following her ankle; in every pose the shoes follow the feet, nothing through, torn, spiked or left behind, no wrong bend; the brown matches.

Next, under the two-tries rule: research, then one last try.
