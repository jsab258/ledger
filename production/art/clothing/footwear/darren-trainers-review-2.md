# Darren's trainers, blind review 2 (30 September, night): FAIL

A fresh reviewer judged the second attempt (model_footwear.py --kind trainer, rebuilt: the topline cut by one plane, the heel closed by narrowing rings, the sole along the upper's own widest line with a cupsole wall, a toe cap and heel counter, a tongue, crossing laces and a bow, the upper evened without shrinking) against his concept and the V&A's 1986 white leather trainers.

Obvious faults:

1. The toe tall, domed and wide, the big toe's knuckle showing as a lump: a clown or bowling shoe from the front (the references: a low, fairly flat toe tapering into the sole).
2. No tongue to be seen; the laces a short flat cluster of crosses bunched under the ankle, the rest of the instep bare: a slip-on with a lace decal (the references: a padded tongue standing above the laces, a raised eyestay with six or seven pairs of eyelets running down about half the shoe, laces with thickness and a bow).

Narrow points: the collar a thin lip with no padding; a V-notch and a crease at the heel's back seam, the counter's base lumpy; the sole about 1.5 cm to the eye, barely lifted at the heel; a flat grey patch at the throat; the sitting pose tests little.

Right: plain white low-cut, no logos; a rounded cupsole edge with toe spring; the length in proportion; the toe cap seam and heel panel line; in every pose the shoes stay on the feet, nothing through, tearing or left behind; at full-body distance they already read as plain white trainers.

Next, under the two-tries rule: research, then one last try.
