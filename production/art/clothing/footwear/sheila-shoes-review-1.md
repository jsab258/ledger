# Sheila's shoes, blind review 1 (30 September, night): FAIL

A fresh reviewer judged the first attempt (model_footwear.py --kind shoe) against her concept and the V&A's Rayne brogue of March 1989 (O1385780).

Obvious faults:

1. The collar torn in every view: sawtooth spikes and flaps round the ankle; a square notch at the back of the heel showing the leg (the concept: a clean smooth edge snug below the ankle bone).
2. The lacing in the wrong place and unfinished: four eyelet bars in a column on the outer side instead of along the top of the instep; no tongue, no throat or facings, no bow (the concept and the Rayne: a tied bow on the centre of the instep).
3. The toe and forefoot too wide and too flat, a "duck" shape; the upper sagging and wrinkling where it meets the sole; no toe box height (the references: a neat rounded toe, a slimmer forefoot, a raised toe box, a clean line at the sole).

Narrow points: no heel (the references: a low heel of about 2 cm); the heel counter boxy, its rim flaring out at the back; on the raised foot the sole a ledge past the heel; the sitting pose testing little ankle bend; the eyelets pale.

Right: flat brown leather lace-up; the sole a separate part of plausible thickness; the length; mirrored; the skinning holds in all three poses, nothing through, no tearing, nothing left behind.
