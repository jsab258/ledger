# The Marvelous suit jacket: a preliminary blind review (2 October 2026): FAIL BROAD

Before the builder's in-game test, a fresh reviewer judged my own pictures of Ron's jacket (textured in Blender's
overcast light, and grey in four skinned poses; F:/LedgerTools/garments/md_suit_jacket/ron/pictures) against the
Hook sheet, the KCD2 frames and dated photographs (John Major, 27 and 28 November 1990; Neil Kinnock, October 1989;
Michael Heseltine, November 1990). The formal verdict comes from the builder's in-game frames.

VERDICT: FAIL BROAD. "Would this read as a 1990 British suit jacket at conversation distance? No."
1. Shape: a sack, not tailored. From the side, the front below the belly balloons forward and the hem flares like a
   smock; no waist; the back boxy with drag folds. Heavy men's 1990 jackets fall straight from chest to hem, the
   fronts opening below the bottom button.
2. His right lapel collapsed, crumpled and folded under, no notch; the lapels do not match; shirt through at the side.
3. In Blender's skinned poses the hems, cuffs and collar edge are ragged; walking, the thigh through the hem; seated,
   the fronts hang between the legs; arms raised, a hole at the armpit (the skirt is cloth only in Unreal).
4. Sleeves gape like bells; 5. shoulders built up and pointed, not soft; 6. the lapel turns above the top button, the
   front hem a shirt-tail point, no cutaway; 7. the notch high; 8. the vent crooked; 9. a gap at the back of the
   collar, pillowy flaps; 10. the shirt collar a box, the tie knotless.
Right: charcoal that reads as wool; two-button single-breasted; the left lapel a proper notch lapel of the period's
width; welted breast pocket, flap pockets, centre vent; the length covers the seat; cuff buttons; the striped tie.

My own tries on the shape after the method research (MD-TAILORED-JACKET-2026-10-01.md), all on Ron, each draped and
measured: the front balanced 9, 11 and 13 cm; the stance lowered 7 cm; 4 cm more length; the fronts soft, then stiff
without Strengthen (the shelf goes, the fronts go limp and the V gapes); the fronts and lapels layered in the
tailor's order (the right lapel still collapses). Darren's own drape failed three ways (bulging, limp, flared).
