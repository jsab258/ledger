# The Marvelous suit jacket in the game, Ron: blind review 1 (2 October 2026): FAIL BROAD, on the cut and the lapel

The proof (DECISIONS.md, 1 October): one 1990 single-breasted charcoal suit jacket made the tailor's way in
Marvelous Designer, judged against the Hook sheet and the KCD2 frames in the builder's in-game test. Frames: the
builder's, in the game's portrait light at 2560 by 1440 (F:/LedgerTools/renders/md-jacket-2026-10-02: standing from
four sides and a portrait, walking, Epic's range of motion, sitting), the jacket from
F:/LedgerTools/garments/md_suit_jacket/ron on MH_RoccoP2 (body checked against its SHA-256), its lower part as cloth
up to 12 cm. The shirt front rendered black and the tie did not show (no material yet; not judged). A fresh reviewer,
who had seen neither the making nor the preliminary review, compared it with the bar and with John Major at Camp
David (22 December 1990), the Maastricht European Council (9 December 1991) and Neil Kinnock (17 December 1990).

VERDICT for Ron: FAIL BROAD. "Would this read as a 1990 British suit jacket at conversation distance? No: an
oversized, crumpled jacket thrown on, with a broken lapel and denim showing through the back."
1. CUT/LAPEL: his right lapel collapsed into a crumpled tube from the notch to the top button; the left one flat and
   wide, its notch high; the two do not match.
2. SKIN/WEIGHTS, COLLISION: the jeans' back pockets and stitching show through the seat and hips standing, walking
   and worst sitting.
3. CUT, WORSE BY WEIGHTS: the front hangs as a sack, drag folds from the buttons, the fronts curling under and
   pinching at the crotch; walking, the fronts twist between the legs; sitting, they crumple into a tube.
4. CUT: boxy, extended shoulders with peaks past the sleeve head, not the soft shoulder asked for.
5. CUT: sleeves far too full, ballooning shoulder to cuff, the right one rotated (cuff buttons forward).
6. CUT: the breast welt nearly at the collarbone.
7. SIM: the back hem ragged walking, a spike of cloth thrown out.
8. Smaller: the chin through the lapel when he bows his head; the top button level with the flaps; the cloth flat
   grey without wool texture. Not tested: no frame raises the arms above the shoulder.
Right: every part there (notch lapels, two buttons, flap pockets, breast welt, three cuff buttons, centre vent); the
length covers the seat; the standing side view reads fairly well; the elbows bend without tearing; no skin through.

THE CAUSE, named: the jacket fails on the cut and the lapel, not on skin or weights (those are second). The lapel:
Marvelous cannot turn and hold a lapel by script (Fold Arrangement, Bond and fold angles have no script calls;
MD-TAILORED-JACKET-2026-10-01.md, MD-LAPEL-FOLD-TURNED-2026-10-02.md); the lapel cut already turned and hinged on its
roll line (my workaround) collapses on the side that lies under the other front. The cut: a pattern drafted from body
measurements (FreeSewing's Jaeger) and corrected by measurement and blind redraping is roomy and boxy, and a fused
front stands off a big belly; a tailor fits it by eye on the live drape, which scripting did not reach in nine drapes
(review-0-preliminary.md). By Jafar's ruling, a fail on the cut or the lapel means the tool is not the cure: a blocked
capability in Needs you, with research in a different direction: professional hands or professional patterns
(production/research/clothing-pipeline/READY-MADE-TAILORING-2026-10-02.md). Darren's frames, through the Outfit
Asset's resizing, are filmed next (review 2).
