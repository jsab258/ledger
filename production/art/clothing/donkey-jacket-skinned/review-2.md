# Ron's remade donkey jacket, blind review 2 (30 September, morning): FAIL

After review 1's fixes (the body's own armpit weights kept, the armpits cleared, the chest spanned, a broader pointed collar, no thigh above the skirt, a plain hem, the pockets higher), a second fresh reviewer judged it standing, walking and (for breakage only) with arms raised; the arms-raised shape is for Unreal, where the rig drives the armpit's corrective joints.

Obvious faults: walking, the leading side's pocket top crumpling into jagged shapes; walking, a white triangle through at the back of the armpit; the collar the wrong kind, a crew neck with a wide flat cape across the shoulders, its tips out at the shoulders, rising at the back like a hood's rim (the reference: a broad stiff pointed turn-down collar, its points at the front of the neck); the hem puffing and rolling under with rounded corners, an anorak's (the reference: hanging straight, square front corners).

Narrow: the front yoke a sloping wedge under the collar (the V&A's lower edge level); the chest and upper arms' muscles showing; the sleeves bulging at the upper arm and elbow from behind; small black spikes where the collar meets the shoulder at the back; zigzag edges down the side seams under the arms; arms raised, cloth bunching under each arm, the back rising into a fin, the yoke's side edges ragged, nothing torn; a pale rim at the cuffs walking; the hem at the crotch, the short end of the range.

Right: navy with a black yoke front and back, the back yoke full width to the armpits with a straight lower edge; four dark round buttons, three below the collar; two large flapless patch pockets low; straight and boxy, no waist, no vent; sleeves to the wrist; standing, clean, no body through; the bulk suits a heavy man.
