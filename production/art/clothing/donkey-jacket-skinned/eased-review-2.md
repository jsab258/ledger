# The eased sewn donkey jacket over Ron's jumper: blind review 2 (30 September 2026)

A fresh reviewer, who had not seen the work, judged the jacket over the jumper, standing, against Ron's casting sheet, the donkey-jacket reference notes and the V&A's jacket T.561-1993 (pictures: F:/LedgerTools/tmp/clothes/donkey-eased/gate3).

VERDICT: FAIL. The throat, fixed after review 1, now passes; the back yoke's edges do not.

Obvious fault:
1. The back yoke's side edges look torn: the navy shows through the black in jagged flecks down both sides of the yoke at the back of the armholes (close-back, close-side; visible in rest-back and rest-side).

Narrow points: a pale ledge under the back yoke's lower edge, its left end drooping; the front yoke's inner ends step beside the collar point; the buttons spaced unevenly (about 12, 14 and 17 cm); the pockets' bottoms 2 to 4 cm above the hem (the references about 8 cm) and a little off-centre; puckers at both front armpits, a lump under the right upper sleeve and a crease across the chest, more than stiff melton makes; a small kink in the front edge between the second and third buttons; the back flaring 5 to 6 cm to the hem; the hem at crotch level (5 to 8 cm short); a pale edge at the cuff, probably the wrist inside a wide sleeve.

Right: navy, straight and boxy, clears his belly; the black yoke front and back at the right depth; buttoned to the neck, the collar clean at the throat; two large flapless patch pockets; sleeves to the wrist, roomy; no body through anywhere else.

WHAT IT MEANS (the method, not the symptom): this is the eased jacket's second failure. The yoke is a separate shell lying millimetres over the wool, and two surfaces that close always fight in a renderer; the whole jacket is also the raw 260,000-point drape. The game pipeline's missing stage is retopology: one clean surface at game density, the yoke a region of it, the drape's details baked onto it. The jacket is remade that way (CLOTHES.md, item 1), after research (production/research/clothing-pipeline/RETOPOLOGY-AND-SKINNING-2026-09-30.md).
