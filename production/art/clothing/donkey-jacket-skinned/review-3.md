# Ron's remade donkey jacket, blind review 3 (30 September, morning): FAIL, set aside

The last try, after research (production/research/clothing-pipeline/FREE-BASES-AND-COLLARS-2026-09-30.md): a turn-down collar laid over the neckline as a stand and a fall with points at the front of the neck; the patch pockets as the panel's own raised geometry with hard outlines, cut only round themselves; the hem's loop smoothed with each point kept at its own distance (smoothing had drawn it in); the skirt's join evened round; the hem back at 0.80 m (at 0.77 the skirt's lowest rows tore); the covered body hidden more widely in the test.

Obvious faults: the hands looking broken off at the cuffs in every pose (my test's error: the wider hidden zone reached the hands); it reads as a quilted puffer or anorak, not stiff melton: the hem a rounded rolled tube with soft corners, a full-width line across the hips at the pocket tops like a quilting seam, the sleeves inflated with flared bell cuffs (the V&A's: a flat hem with square corners, plain straight sleeves, a boxy slab).

Narrow: a small shirt collar on a stand, not the V&A's broad pointed one, and a white gap at the throat like a vicar's collar until the jumper fills it; the pockets barely reading as patches, their tops lumping walking; the chest clinging; jagged creases at the armpits and side seams under the back yoke; a shirt-like strip down the front, small buttons; the hem at the crotch, short for a man of 6 ft 1; the collar dipping at the centre back; arms raised, a wedge of cloth from the armpit, batwing folds, the back yoke's side edges ragged, nothing through.

Right: one black yoke front and back with straight lower edges, the back full width to about armpit level, the front's depth in the NCB range; navy wool, black yoke, four dark buttons to the neck; straight and boxy, no waist, no vent, the bulk of a heavy man; walking, the body and skirt follow the legs with no tearing and no body through.

SET ASIDE under the two-tries rule (three reviews, the last after research).
