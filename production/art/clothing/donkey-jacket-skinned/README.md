# Ron's donkey jacket, remade as a game garment (the clothing session, 30 September 2026)

CLOTHES.md's new list, item 1, the jacket first (Jafar, 30 September: game clothes are skeletal meshes skinned to the MetaHuman skeleton, weights from the body, joints fixed; only loose parts simulated; the first jacket, production/art/clothing/donkey-jacket-sewn, was judged as simulated cloth). The method: production/research/clothing-pipeline/PIPELINE-2026-09-30.md.

## How it is made

- **The shape** (tools/meshgen/blender/model_cardigan.py, the garment modeller, with the settings in F:/LedgerTools/tmp/clothes/donkey2/args2.txt): Ron's torso and arms set out 36 mm and smoothed, hanging straight from 1.52 m, the chest's hollows spanned up to 6 cm (--bridge: melton does not follow the muscles); below 1.00 m a straight skirt over both legs to the hem at 0.80 m, just below the crotch (--tube-from); the underarms evened into one smooth open curve 2 cm off him (--armpit-clear: the research's clearance at the armpit); a black yoke cut on clean planes, front 10 cm under the neck, back to the armpits, ending at the armholes; a broad turn-down collar with pointed ends (85 mm, --collar-point); a 40 mm front band, four 25 mm dark buttons; two flapless patch pockets, 175 by 190 mm, 11 cm over the hem; no hem band (a plain turned hem).
- **The game mesh** (tools/meshgen/blender/pose_skinned.py --decimate 0.45 --uv-smart): about 29,000 triangles (the research: a hero's jacket 15,000 to 30,000), the yoke's edge held, one automatic UV set (a first pass).
- **Bound to his skeleton** (pose_skinned.py, the settings in F:/LedgerTools/tmp/clothes/donkey2/skin-args3.txt): the body's own skin weights copied to every point, the armpit's corrective joints included (upperarm_in, the latissimus, the twist correctives: stripped of them, the jacket's side stood still while his skin came through it); fixed at the joints: below the armpit, points more than 10 cm from the arm's bones carry no arm (the coat's side beside his hanging hands had taken the hand's weights), the cuffs on the forearm's twist bone, the collar onto spine_05 and neck_01, the armpit's weights smoothed; above the skirt no thigh (the front split at the lowest button walking); at most four influences a point.
- **The loose part**: below 0.95 m the skirt hangs from the pelvis, the front keeping 80 per cent of the thighs' pull and the back 25; a vertex colour, SimMaxDistance, rises from 0 there to 1 at the hem, for Unreal's cloth (the research: 5.8's VertexColorToAttribute node reads it).
