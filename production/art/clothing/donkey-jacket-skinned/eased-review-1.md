# Ron's sewn donkey jacket, eased for the game, over his jumper: blind review 1 (30 September): FAIL

The sewn jacket of 29 September (production/art/clothing/donkey-jacket-sewn), which the builder bound panel by panel and filmed walking and raising its arms cleanly in Unreal (production/art/clothing/jacket-bound-test-2026-09-30), eased for his notes (tools/meshgen/blender/ease_garment.py): 10 mm more room at the belly and hips; the chest's crease (the surface folding under itself at 1.40 to 1.45 m) and the roll under the back yoke unfolded; the skirt below 1.00 m marked for cloth (a vertex colour, SimMaxDistance, on the render and the coarse simulation mesh); its "pattern" UV layer kept. Ron's jumper fitted under it (tools/meshgen/blender/fit_under.py: drawn to 6 mm inside the jacket, its parts deep under the jacket removed, as games remove what an outer layer always hides) with a "pattern" UV layer of its own (tools/meshgen/blender/pattern_uv.py). Judged standing.

Obvious: the throat broken, a hole each side of the top button under the collar, the jumper showing through as pale torn scraps and a shard (the concept: the jumper's grey round neck as one clean band); the back yoke's side edges torn and ragged where it meets the sleeves, navy blotches through it (the V&A: clean straight sewn edges).

Narrow: pale jagged slivers inside the cuffs; the front yoke stopping short of the front opening, a navy strip up to the neck; the buttons on a narrow shirt-like strip, with the pointed collar drifting towards a work shirt; soft creasing (a curved sag under the yoke, puckers at the armholes, a lump at an inner elbow, a kink in the front edge); the back bulging over the seat; the hem at the crotch, a little short.

Right: the navy; boxy and straight, suiting a heavy man; the yoke over both shoulders, the back one full width to the armpits with a straight lower edge; dark round buttons well spaced; two large flapless square patch pockets low; a broad pointed collar, no lapels; plain sleeves to the wrist; a straight hem with square corners; nothing floating, no body through the torso.
