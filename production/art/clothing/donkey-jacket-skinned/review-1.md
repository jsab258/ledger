# Ron's remade donkey jacket, blind review 1 (30 September, morning): FAIL

A fresh reviewer judged the jacket remade as a game garment (the shape modelled round him, bound to his skeleton with the body's weights, the armpits fixed by the old rule, the skirt marked for simulation), standing with arms down, walking and with arms raised, against his sheet, the reference notes and the V&A's T.561-1993.

Obvious faults: the underarms crumpled into a jagged frill in the idle, worse walking (a loose flap); the body through at the back yoke's corner and the right underarm walking; the front band stepping sideways at the lowest button walking, the hem's front edge torn; arms raised, the sleeves ballooning into batwing webs.

Narrow: the collar too small, a crew-neck band from the front; the cloth clinging, the chest's muscles showing; the hem a rounded tube, the pockets padded, leaning to a puffer; the pockets' feet 5 cm over the hem (8 in the reference); small dark spikes where the collar meets the back yoke; the concept portrait shows a zip-front jacket, the sheet a donkey jacket (the sheet was built).

Right: the length just below the crotch; boxy and straight, no waist, suiting a heavy man; the black yoke over both shoulders, front about 12 cm with a straight edge, the back to the armpits; four dark round buttons about 16 cm apart; two large square flapless patch pockets set low; sleeves to the wrist; the navy.
