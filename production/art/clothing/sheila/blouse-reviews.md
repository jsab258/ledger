# Sheila's cream blouse, blind reviews (30 September, early morning)

CLOTHES.md item 6, Sheila's sheet: "a cream blouse with a small round collar" (under the cardigan, which is set aside; the blouse had only been made as its collar points in the cardigan's V). Made with the knit modeller (tools/meshgen/blender/model_cardigan.py --crew --flat-collar): a long-sleeved blouse set out from her body, a narrow stand at the base of the neck, a flat round collar laid on the blouse by rays from above (walked over the surface from the neckline, it snapped back to the edge and crumpled into a frill; from a neckline set too high it grew from a tall stand like a polo neck), its ends rounded at the throat; a buttoned placket; its foot tucked under her skirt's band (which sits only about 3.5 mm off her). Judged standing, with her skirt.

Review 1: FAIL. Shrink-wrapped over the whole torso: into the breastbone groove, the creases under the chest, the belly and side bulges above the band, a stretch bodysuit not a woven blouse, not one fold, no fullness at the tuck (a 1990 blouse for a woman of 53 falls from the bust with some ease). Narrow: the hem stopping on the band as a pale ledge instead of going in; the collar broader and shallower than the sheet's "small", spreading like a bib; the neckline standing off the neck at the sides, crumpled bits of the stand either side of the throat, small pinches where the collar's ends meet it; the top button half sunk; open tube sleeve ends standing off the wrist with no buttoned cuff; a grey-putty colour, not warm cream. Right: the right garment, long-sleeved, round-collared, buttoning down the front, modest and of the period; the collar lying flat, rolling cleanly over the back of the neck, its ends meeting neatly at the front, buttoned to the top as in the concept; the button strip straight, the buttons evenly spaced; the sleeves' length and ease; the length; nothing through her.

Then: 22 mm of ease (12 had clung), hung straight from the bust at 1.335 m, smoothed longer; only its lowest 20 mm drawn in under the band, so it blouses 14 mm above it; no hem band on a tucked blouse (it showed as a ledge); the collar 46 mm on a 4 mm stand; the sleeves drawn to the wrist; a warmer cream; the buttons out on the placket's face.

Review 2: PASS on shape. One fault, the colour: it read grey-putty (about 160) against the concept's cream (222), the skirt in the same light matching the concept's; a material value, not the shape. Narrow: the fabric following the stand-in's chest muscles and breastbone a little; the bloused waist an even swollen ring with no vertical folds, a thin lip on the band, small dark marks where the placket ends, a small pinch at the centre back; plain sleeve ends with no buttoned cuff; the collar lower, flatter and wider than the concept's (three quarters of the shoulder against half), a small crimp where its ends meet; a button edge-on standing proud. Right: a true round collar, its ends meeting at the throat, lying down across the back, nothing torn; the button front centred and sensibly spaced; long sleeves to the wrist with modest fullness; neither tight nor boxy; tucked in; nothing floating or through her; a plain 1990 blouse for a neat woman of 53.

The colour: Blender's preview light cannot show a pale colour brighter than about 160 (raising the material to near white moved the chest from 158 to 163), so the base colour is set near-white cream and the builder aims for the concept's cream in the game's light. Handed to the builder 30 September (NOW.md, Handovers; F:/LedgerTools/garments/sheila_blouse).
