# Sheila's cardigan, blind review 2 (29 September, night): FAIL

A second fresh reviewer judged the second attempt (cardigan-review-1.md's fixes) against her approved concept and casting sheet, and six of Tom Wood's Women's Market photographs.

Obvious faults:

1. The blouse collar the wrong kind: a stiff upright band with a gap at the front, clerical or mandarin (the concept: small, soft, round, lying flat over the neckline, two rounded points at the throat).
2. The neckline looks torn: a jagged strip across the base of the throat between the bands' tops; over the shoulders and across the back a wavy, frayed double lip.
3. The neckline the wrong shape: very wide, near a boat neck; each band's top a notched point like a lapel; the back straight across at shoulder level (the concept: the V from the side of the neck, the back band close to the neck).
4. The V too deep and wide, the buttons crowded low (the concept: the V to mid-chest above the bust, seven buttons spread from the upper chest to the welt).
5. The blouse front clings, following the cleavage, no button strip, reading as bare chest; body through it with the arms raised.
6. The button band ragged beside the buttons, a zigzag sitting.
7. Sitting deforms it: the back balloons into a hump, the back hem below any seat; at the front the hem flares like a peplum.
8. The cuffs flare open with a thin ragged rim, a gap at the wrist (the concept: deep ribbed cuffs gripping the wrist, the sleeve gathering above).

Narrow points: a ridge along each shoulder like an epaulette; the upper sleeves puffy arms down; a step where the welt meets the band; the hem ragged on one side walking; flat disc buttons (the concept's small and domed).

Right: the length and the straight hang, to the hip over the skirt's band, loose not tight; single-breasted, seven buttons down to the welt; the deep welt reads as ribbing; the sleeves to the wrist, not riding up arms raised or walking; no spikes or tears at the armpits; the silhouette right for a woman of 53 in 1990.

## What follows

Two failed reviews, so the two-tries rule: research first, then one more attempt; if that fails the cardigan is set aside.
