# Sheila's skirt, blind review standing (30 September, early morning): PASS

The skirt made on 29 September (tools/meshgen/blender/model_skirt.py; this folder's README): its sitting was set aside after seven tries and research, and it was never reviewed on its own shape. Under the recommendation to Jafar (shapes to the gate standing; the builder tests poses in Unreal, where its cloth runs in Chaos), a fresh reviewer judged it standing, against her concept.

No obvious faults: nothing torn or floating, no spikes, her body nowhere through it.

Narrow points: over the front of the belly the plain part above the pleats sinks into a hollow with a light ledge below it where the pleats begin (a small pouch); the pleats read faintly from a distance, like panel lines (the concept's folds softer and fuller, more flare at the hem; cloth may soften them); a small staircase of corners at the hem where the pleat underlays overlap; the brown lighter than the concept's (the pictures were brightened).

Right: the length just below the knee, matching the concept and "knee-length"; a narrow band at her natural waist, right for her age and 1990, with a small overlap for its fastening; knife pleats all one way, about twenty round, stitched flat over the hips about 15 cm below the band, a typical and flattering high-street construction for an older woman then; hanging cleanly off the seat with no cling; an easy straight-to-slight-A line; sober and suitable for a bookkeeper of 53 in 1990.
