7. Gaps at the wrists between cuff and hand.

Right: hip length over the skirt's top; the ribbed welt clear and deep; seven buttons evenly from the V to the welt; the V's depth; full sleeves to the wrist with ribbed cuffs; smooth shoulders, sleeves not riding up; the blouse front in the V with its placket and buttons; a plain, modest hand-knit, right for a woman of 53 in 1990.

## Set aside (the two-tries rule)

Two failed reviews, the research, and a third failure: the cardigan is set aside, with the skirt's sitting (production/art/clothing/sheila/README.md). What it leaves: the body, length, welt, buttons, V and sleeves now read right; every fault left is at an edge (the collar, the neckline, the bands) or in a pose. The edges want the research's route in full (the knit built as rings, its edges drawn as curves, not cut from her skin), which is a rebuild, not a fix.
