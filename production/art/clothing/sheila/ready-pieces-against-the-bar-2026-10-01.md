# Sheila's blouse and skirt, Darren's T-shirt, judged again in poses and against the bar (1 October 2026)

All three were handed over on 30 September judged standing only. The outside audit stopped standing-only acceptance,
and Jafar's ruling of 1 October judges every visual against the bar (production/reference/hook-sheet.png and the KCD2
frames), so a fresh reviewer judged them standing (rendered, the covered body hidden) and in four poses (skinned from
the body), against their sheets, the bar and photographs (Roman Road Market, London, 1990; Church Street, Liverpool,
11 July 1991; Poll Tax protesters, Kennington, 31 March 1990; Diana at Relate, Northampton, 9 October 1989).
Pictures: F:/LedgerTools/tmp/clothes/bar1.

SKIRT: PASS. "A correct 1990 knee-length knife-pleated skirt with a believable silhouette and length ... With cloth and a
wool texture it could reach the bar." Narrow: a step at the front hip where the pleats begin; dark blocks at the hips
(probably the hands' shadows: to confirm); the body's cut edge 1 cm above the waistband (the tucked blouse covers it);
skinned, a stiff drum seated with stepped hips, and the panels splitting walking (the cloth's, to be shown in Unreal);
crisp identical pleats, a flat colour.

BLOUSE: FAIL. A gap at the throat between the collar and the neck; the collar's back edge jagged with the arms raised and
a cuff edge fraying walking; the cloth shrink-wrapping the torso, breastbone and ribs through (on Sheila it reads as a
man's chest). Narrow: near-white, stiff as foam with no drape; a dent on her right breast; a shallow tuck; the
shoulders 28 mm inside the body arms down (Unreal's correctives, unproven). "With the hole it reads as a placeholder."

T-SHIRT: FAIL. Both sleeves stand off the arm as stiff wide tubes (the 1990 photographs' fall onto the arm); a dark
crescent at the throat (the neckband stands off the neck); seated the hem crumples into a ragged frill. Narrow: flat
white with no folds or rib band; short and boxy; clinging to the stomach seated. Holes it also named in the upper arms
were the picture tool's (it hid skin by distance alone; tools/meshgen/blender/look_garment.py now hides only skin the
garment lies over, away from its openings, and the holes are gone).

WHAT FOLLOWS: the skirt stays ready to fit (F:/LedgerTools/garments/sheila_skirt). The blouse and T-shirt are withdrawn:
the builder gives Sheila and Darren Epic's plainest cream blouse and plain white T-shirt, judged against the bar, as he
does their other pieces (NOW.md, Handovers).
