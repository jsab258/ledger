## What the drape is (measured, read-only)

- The OBJ: 13 garment groups and the body's 4 LODs; millimetres, Y up, the figure facing +Z.
- Each piece one connected sheet with one UV island; total pattern area 1.205 m².
- Marvelous's UVs are each piece centred on (0,0) in fabric-tile units (twill 89.83 x 57.74 mm, rib 156.89 mm square, canvas 42.42 x 21.08 mm), so the islands overlap; `uv x tile + the plan's piece centre` gives every boundary on the plan outline within 0.01 mm.
- All 29 seams: every seam vertex has its partner at the same point, equal counts both sides.

## Steps, each with its check

1. **Importer** (tools/meshgen/blender/import_md_drape.py OBJ PLAN OUT.blend; 2 h): the OBJ at 0.001 facing -Y, LODs 1 to 3 dropped; the MetaHuman FBX for the armature and body (its bounds within 2 mm of LOD0, then LOD0 dropped); the 13 groups joined with a face attribute "piece" and materials Shell, Rib, Tape from the plan; "pattern" rewritten in metres with a layout offset per piece (import.json); welded at 0.01 mm. Check: tiles as measured, boundaries within 0.05 mm of the plan, no non-manifold edges, 4 openings, one connected part, body within 2 mm.
2. **Pieces and runs from data** (1.5 h): zip_jacket.py writes a "retopo" block (each piece's four patch corners, the rounded side, the sleeve's elbow warp); runs matched to plan lines by their ends. Check: 29 of 29 seams found with partners, every run end within 0.1 mm of a plan corner.
3. **Counts** (2 h): from 3D length over spacing (about 0.0115 m for about 20k triangles), each patch solved for its one unknown. Check: equal counts both sides of every seam; 18k to 22k triangles predicted.
4. **Grid, lift and weld** (2 h), the donkey jacket's branches switched off (collar solid, buttons, yoke, strips): fronts, back, sleeves as now; bands about 4 rows, bottom open; cuffs a tube; the collar single-layer, its top thickened by the lip; the tapes their own strip, 2 columns each, rows matching the fronts'. Check: triangles in range; lift misses under 0.5%; pictures front, back, side, zip; tape width 24 +/- 2 mm at 10 heights (smoothing can narrow it).
5. **UVs** (1.5 h): the tapes laid side by side so the teeth line has no UV seam, scaled x2 before packing. Check: cloth 1.5 px/mm or more, tape 3 or more, no overlaps; 8 px or more per tooth, else a tiling teeth normal in the material.
6. **Floaters** (4 h): one interleaved row of teeth along the tapes' shared seam, 0.3 mm proud; the stops; each welt a 150 x 15 mm rectangle from the plan's place and lean, lifted through its front's pattern, two 7 mm lips 1.5 mm proud with a 1 mm slit and bar tacks. Check: teeth = length / pitch; floater gap 0.3 to 2.5 mm; welt 120 +/- 10 mm above the band seam at 20 +/- 3 degrees.
7. **Bake and packing** (3 h): normal from the drape plus floaters; occlusion with the body hidden, in the colour map's alpha; rib ridges drawn from the pattern; the ID mask (cloth, rib, tape, teeth, welt) in the packed map's G at 0, .25, .5, .75, 1, unblurred, beside roughness (R) and metal (B). Check: hits 97% or more; the ID's five values with areas within 1% of the raster; pictures at the game camera; G point-sampled in Unreal.
8. **Gate** (1.5 h): pictures and a fresh reviewer against the 1990 references.
