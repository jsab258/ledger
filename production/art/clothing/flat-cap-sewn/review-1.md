# Blind review 1 (29 September, night): FAIL

A fresh reviewer judged the first attempt against production/reference/work-trousers-and-flat-cap-1990.md and Peter Fryer's photographs 149-012, 149-005 and 149-009, which it opened.

Obvious faults:

1. Far too high: the lower edge 4 to 5 cm above the brow, the forehead bare, the side edge clear of the ear tops, the back high on the skull (the photographs: down on the brow, the band at the top of the ear).
2. The peak a separate flat board, square-cut, wider than the crown and past it on both sides, a strip of background between two layers; in profile past the nose.
3. A crumpled, ragged ruffle across the front on top of the peak (the photographs: the crown pulled smoothly forward, the front nearly level).
4. The crown domed and tall: from the front a dome, near a baker boy's; in profile a jockey or baseball cap.
5. The back and sides ragged: a tab at the rear, the back hem wavy, bits jutting at the sides.

Narrow points: a little bulky for its width; the peak's edge should be rounded and thinner; no edge binding readable.

Right: one-piece top, no button, no eight-panel seams; the top leaning forward; the crown's cloth soft, like tweed; the ears uncovered; the colour.

## What followed (the second attempt)

- The hat line lower (the front just above the brow, the back lower on the skull, 7 degrees) and taken over the ear tops, not round them; the cap redrafted for that line's girth (Florent for a 636 mm head).
- The peak 80% as wide and 85% as deep, 3.5 mm thick with a rounded edge, every face turned up before thickening (its rim had come out speckled).
- The crown's front edge sewn to the peak 3 mm from its edge for 135 mm each way; the cloth resting on the peak laid on it; the seam between crown and band smoothed along itself; the opening's edge smoothed; nothing left inside the head.
