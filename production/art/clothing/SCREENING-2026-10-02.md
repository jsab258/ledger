# Ready-made rigged suits, screened against the rubric (2 October 2026, night)

Jafar's order of 2 October: screen ready-made, rigged suits against the fixed rubric (RUBRIC.md beside this file),
from their pictures: anything free and ready-rigged the search from this PC found first, then Husky's Business Suit 55
on Fab, then others. Reject any whose shoulders, button stance and length would all need rebuilding; reject any with
Epic's NoAI tag. The best one, free or under $40, to him on one page, or a plain report that none qualifies. Seen in
the browser on this PC; nothing signed into, downloaded or bought.

## The answer

**None qualifies outright.** Every suit rigged to the MetaHuman skeleton at $40 or under that I could read is
NoAI-tagged, Husky's included. One candidate could not be read: Nice Pictures' "Business Jacket", rigged to the
MetaHuman skeleton, $39.99, whose Fab listing is hidden from a signed-out browser as mature content, so its NoAI field
is unseen (UNREACHED; nothing concluded). It goes to Jafar's page to check signed in.
