# Darren's shell-suit jacket, blind review 1 (30 September, night): FAIL

A fresh reviewer judged the first attempt (tools/meshgen/blender/model_cardigan.py --zip: his torso and arms set out 35 mm, hanging straight from the chest; teal raglan panels cut clean by planes; the zip open to mid-chest over a white T-shirt front; a stand collar round the back and sides; ribbed waistband and cuffs; skinned with zone weights) against his concept (production/casting/darren-milner/full.jpg), no dated museum shell suit found.

Obvious faults:

1. The collar: its front ends jagged with teal shards; at the back and sides low and flat like a shirt collar (the concept: a clean purple stand-up collar round the neck, the zip running up into it).
2. No ballooning over the waistband: a straight tube, the band as wide as the body (the concept: loose, puffing over a band that pulls in tight; the thing that makes it a shell suit).
3. The hem ragged: frayed corners, flaps and notches; sitting, the band a spiky frill.
4. Dark specks like holes on the sleeves where teal meets purple.
5. Arms raised, bulbous sacks under each arm; a light spot under the left.

Narrow points: the teal stopping above the elbow all round (the concept: down the outside to the lower forearm, purple beneath); both colours darker and duller than the concept's violet and turquoise; the T-shirt a flat plate with no round neckband, white lines along the front edge; the shoulders and upper sleeves bulging like muscles; no slanted front pockets, no zip pull seen; the zip's sides ending at different heights; a dent below the collar at the back.

Right: the length, to the top of the hips; the colour layout (purple body, teal panels from the neck over the shoulders on the diagonal); the zip down the centre open to mid-chest, white in the opening; a ribbed waistband and gathered cuffs; sleeves staying at the wrist in every pose; no gross tearing walking or sitting.
