Narrow: the sleeve hems rolling away from the arm, a nick and a spike at the cuffs; the sleeves a little short and snug for 1990 (roomier, to mid-bicep or lower); a gap under the arm walking; faint rumpling along the side seam, a dimple high on the back. Right: the overall shape for a thin young man, straight and slim, neither baggy nor skin-tight; the length, reaching the hip; the shoulders; a plain crew-neck short-sleeved shirt, true to the period; nothing through him standing.

Then: the neck rib thin and flat (15 mm, lying at 0.6 of a stand); the hem band laid on the shirt with no row below its edge (below, it met the body and frilled) and its misses filled from their neighbours (dropped, they left gaps at the side seam that read as tears); the sleeve hems grown from the sleeves' own edges (as the shell-suit jacket's trims); the sleeves roomier and lower (to 60 per cent of the upper arm).

Review 2: PASS, judged standing (the rest pose and arms down). No obvious faults: nothing torn, spiky, floating or through him. Narrow: the neckline dipping to a slight point at the front, the band about 1 cm (1990's ribs about 2 cm); tucked or loose (the portrait suggests tucked; the builder to decide); the sleeves hanging as stiff tubes off the arm, no folds at the armpit; the hems rounded tubes, not flat turned hems, a dark ring under each sleeve hem; a small lump high on the back, rippling down one side seam. Right: a plain straight cut for the period, loose but not baggy on his thin frame, like the V&A's dated T-shirt of about 1990; the shoulder seams at the shoulder; the sleeves to mid-upper arm; the collar round the neck with no gap; an ordinary length; hanging cleanly in both poses.

Then: the neck rib 20 mm, the front neckline rounder (a smaller dip).
