# Sheila's spectacles and chain, blind reviews (30 September, night)

Made by tools/meshgen/blender/model_accessory.py --kind spectacles: large square frames in brown (58 by 50 mm lenses, an 18 mm bridge), a light brown tint darker at the top, fitted to her own head 12 mm in front of her eyes with the bridge 2 mm off her nose, the arms along her head to her ears and hooked behind them; a thin gilt chain from the arms' tips down round her nape, weighted from the skin of her neck.

Review 1: FAIL, on fit alone: they sat 10 to 15 mm too high, her eyes three quarters of the way down the lenses, the top rim far up her forehead (the period's frames sit with the pupils at or just above the middle, the top rim near the brow, the bottom on the upper cheek). Narrow: nothing rests on the nose (a thin rod bridge along the very top and hinges at the top corners read modern; the museum's 1980 frame has a moulded bridge and hinges a third of the way down); the frames a little wider than her head from behind; the chain very thin (it may flicker at game distance) with a gap at the arms' tips; the chain on bare skin at the nape (in the game it must lie over her collar); the tint a hard two-tone line (the material grades it). Right: the frame's size, shape and colour for the sheet and her age; level, symmetrical, a sensible distance from the eyes; the arms hooked neatly behind the ears; the chain hanging from the tips behind the neck as a worn chain does, clean in every pose.

Then: the frame lowered 12 mm (its height set from the top of the nose's bridge; a search of the eye sockets, tried first, found the hollow under the eye and set it too low); a moulded bridge at the lenses' upper third; the hinges a third of the way down; the arms found against the side of her face from outside in front of the ear (from inside they had cut her cheekbone) and meeting the ear where its rim joins the head; the chain 2 mm thick and joined into the arms' tips.

Review 2: PASS. No obvious faults: nothing through her, level and on in every pose. Narrow: still a little high (her eyes just below the lenses' middle; about 4 mm lower); wider and heavier than the portrait's thin rims (as wide as her head; the sheet says only "large"); the bridge a thin bar with blocks, a metal frame's build (a plastic frame's is moulded with the front); the chain behind her neck with arms raised not shown close. Right: large square tinted lenses about 130 to 145 mm across, close to the museum's 1980 frame; brown plausible for her age; the front tilted slightly back at the foot; a sensible distance from the eyes; the arms level from the hinges, bending at the ear's top and curling behind; the chain thin, joined to the arms' ends and hanging behind the neck.

Then fixed, as small changes: 4 mm lower, 5 mm narrower, a deeper moulded bridge; the arms' ear point held where the ear's rim joins the head (lowered with the frame, the hook ran over the rim), the chain's ends set at the arms' tips (2 mm forward, a link came through the ear), the hook 4 mm shorter; the close pose views with arms raised made.
