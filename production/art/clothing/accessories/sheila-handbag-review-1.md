# Sheila's handbag, blind review 1 (30 September, night): PASS

A fresh reviewer judged the first attempt (tools/meshgen/blender/model_accessory.py --kind handbag: a structured frame bag 27 by 19 by 8 cm, narrowing to its top, a gilt frame and clasp, two handles meeting in her right hand, placed with her arm down and skinned to the hand) against her concept and the V&A's handbag used as Dot Cotton's, about 1990.

No obvious faults.

Narrow points: the clasp faced her thigh; the brown too dark (the concept a warm chestnut matching her shoes); a little boxy (the references narrow to the frame and widen at the base, with softer shoulders); the handles short (about 8 cm above the frame; 11 to 13 better); no metal loops or tabs where the handles meet the frame; the frame a flat board a little proud at its ends; the end panel's shoulders slightly faceted; the clasp flat; her thigh a hair from the inner face, so a swing may clip it.

Right: the size (close to Dot's bag, hanging just above her knee, in scale with her); carried correctly, the broad faces parallel to her leg; clearly a woman's structured handbag of the period; clean walking.

Then fixed, as one-line changes: the clasp on the outer face, a chestnut leather, the handles 11.5 cm. Handed to the builder 30 September (NOW.md, Handovers; F:/LedgerTools/garments/sheila_handbag).
