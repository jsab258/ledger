# Blind review 3 (29 September, night): FAIL. Set aside.

A third fresh reviewer judged the third attempt, made after the research (production/research/clothing-pipeline/SKINNING-TROUSERS-2026-09-29.md): zone weights on the main bones instead of the nearest skin's (the belt, band and loops one ring on the pelvis and the spine at the back; the pelvis giving way to each side's thigh down the front and seat; the knee blended over 15 cm; below it the calf alone), the body the trousers always cover hidden as the game hides it, the seat bridged deeper, a knee bag of 13 mm, gathers under the belt.

Obvious faults, all in the skinned poses:

1. Walking, a bright rip just under the belt on the left front by the pocket.
2. Sitting broken: from the front the belt, buckle and fly vanish, the belly straight on the thighs; the crotch crumpled with jagged bits; the seat's underside a sawtooth edge; needle spikes below the hip; a lighter panel below the belt at the side, like the inside showing.
3. The stair: slivers lifting off the hip; the seat's back edge ragged.
4. The hems still gather into a crumpled cuff when a leg bends (the stair, the walk's back leg).

Narrow points: the seat still follows the cleft with a pucker; neat slacks rather than heavy baggy cloth; the belt's end not through the buckle, no prong, loops like handles; the fly a plain line ending in a knob; small ragged folds at the hem on the foot, a flap at the seat, a ledge under the belt.

Right: dark grey, flat front, belt loops and a plain belt, no turn-ups, slanted front pockets and slit back pockets; the leg straight and a little wide at the hem; the length, a half break over the instep; the waist under the belly, lower at the front than the back; standing, walking and the stair keep their shape on the legs with no body through; the waist level walking and on the stair apart from the rip.

## Set aside (29 September, the two-tries rule)

Two failed reviews, the research, and a third failure: the trousers are set aside and the list moves on (CLOTHES.md). At rest they pass most points; what fails is how they deform when skinned to the body, which in the game is the builder's MetaHuman fitting, not this test: the research found Epic's route transfers the weights from the body again (its Inpaint Weights method with a mask over the crotch, seat and lower legs is its recommended fix) and that folds that come and go belong in wrinkle maps driven by the joints. If they are taken up again: that route, in Unreal, with a short sit and stair test there.
