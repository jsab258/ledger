A second fresh reviewer judged the second attempt (review-1.md's fixes: a crew neck at the neck's base dipping at the front, the band lying on the slope and leaning in, snugger cuffs, smoother armpit weights, a shorter hem, the test hiding only body well inside the jumper).

Obvious faults:

1. Sitting breaks the hem: at the back and sides the welt a stiff shelf jutting out behind the hips, the knit above torn into square notches with dark slits, see-through gaps at the hip, the lap's hem ragged (149-006: soft folds across the lap).
2. The cuffs do not meet the hands: a see-through ring between cuff and hand in several poses, the wrist edge jagged walking; at rest the cuff stands off the wrist.
3. The neckband ragged: sawtooth tears and dark slivers round its inner edge, a dark slit across the back below it; lying flat on the shoulders like a flat collar (front.jpg: a thick raised ribbed crew neck snug to his thick neck).
4. Arms raised: triangular fins out from both sides below the armpits, a pinched notch where the sleeve joins, ripples under the raised sleeve.
5. The hidden body reaching below the hem: a strip under the welt with a blocky edge, holes walking; a pale streak near the hem walking.

Narrow points: the hem not rising with the arms; too smooth and tight (149-011: bloused over the belly, bunched at elbows and forearms), the sleeves ballooning just above the cuff; the welt straight with no draw-in, a light line at its end; specks near the cuff; nipple bumps; boxy from the side against his belly.

Right: the type (a plain grey crew-neck pullover, nothing wrong for 1990); the length, at the top of the hips; the welt and cuffs' depth, sleeves to the wrist; roomy on a heavy frame, shoulders and back hanging believably at rest; the rest pose clean apart from the neckband; no poke-through arms down or walking.
