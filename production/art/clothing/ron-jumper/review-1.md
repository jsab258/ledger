# Ron's jumper, blind review 1 (30 September, early): FAIL

A fresh reviewer judged the first attempt (tools/meshgen/blender/model_cardigan.py --crew: his torso, arms, neck base and hips set out 26 mm, smoothed, spanning his hollows, hanging straight from the chest; a level hem at the top of the hip with a ribbed welt, ribbed cuff tubes, a ribbed neckband anchored to the knit's own neck edge) against his concept (production/casting/ron-kirby/full.jpg, front.jpg) and Peter Fryer's Smith's Dock photographs (149-011 the useful one).

Obvious faults:

1. The neck: a tall stiff tube about 4 cm, the same height front and back, a mock turtleneck or clerical collar; a split at centre back, a wavy top rim, a dent at front right (front.jpg: a narrow soft ribbed band close round the base of a thick neck, dipping at the front towards the collarbones).
2. See-through at the nape in the poses, dark spikes at the sides of the neck.
3. Gaps between cuff and hand.
4. Sitting: the back hem jagged, the welt out behind like a box, holes under the seat; the front hem crumpled.
5. Arms raised: a sawtooth tear where sleeve meets body, slits in both armpits.
6. Walking: see-through below the hem at the hip.

Narrow points: a pale spike above the hem walking; the cuffs loose rings standing off the wrist; a straight tube, flatter than his belly (full.jpg: blousing a little over a snug welt, stretched over the belly); the hem a touch long; the hem not lifting with the arms raised (acceptable skinned).

Right: the mass (a roomy, plain, heavy man's pullover, broad through chest and shoulders, no logos or zips); the welt's depth; the sleeves to the wrist, bunching softly at the elbow; the torso keeping its shape standing and walking; right under the donkey jacket.

## What followed (the second attempt)

Faults 2, 3 and 6 were the test's: the body was hidden near the garment's edges, which the skinned jumper leaves as it moves; the test now hides only the body well inside it. The rest: the neck a crew neck (the knit cut lower at the front, the band 20 mm, lying close, one closed ring with a smoothed rim); snug cuffs; the weights smoothed further at the armpits; the hem 2 cm shorter; the knit allowed over his belly.
