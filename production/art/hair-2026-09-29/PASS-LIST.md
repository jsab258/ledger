RULINGS.md keeps the rule (no hair skill until one character's hair passes the full gate, his look included; 9 Nov: strands stay). This file is the home of the pass list.

Hair passes, per character:

- from his camera behind (the crown), in a talk close-up, sitting and walking side-on;
- at about 1, 3.1, 10 and 25 m with no colour change or popping between detail levels;
- by day, overcast, by a window, indoors under lamps at night, on the night street and wet;
- without shimmer walking, turning or sitting;
- no hard or grainy shadow on walls, collar or neck;
- no pale scalp;
- its frame cost measured for one person and the corner's crowd;
- judged against period photographs and the approved face, then the packaged walk, then his look.
