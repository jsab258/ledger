# Street clutter, 1990: ten pieces (the builder's list, item 10), 29 September

Ten pieces of the street's everyday furniture, each made against the research
(production/research/street-clutter-1990: the ten, their sizes, colours and
parts, and photographs of the real things) and through the quality gate: my
check against the photographs, then a reviewer who had not seen them made.
The pictures here are the gate's (tools/meshgen/blender/clutter_sheet.py:
front, three-quarter and side, plain overcast light, a 1.80 m figure for
scale, true colours). The models and their .blend files are on drive F
(F:/LedgerTools/tmp/clutter/<piece>/); each is made by its script under
tools/meshgen/blender/clutter/.

## Live or scripts: the route for clutter

The item asked for the pieces live in Blender, timed against the script
route. The Blender connection (production/research/blender-mcp) loads only
when a Claude Code session starts, so it was used through a Claude Code helper
started from a script on Jafar's subscription (tools/blender-live/live_piece.py;
its record says no API key). Two pieces were made both ways from the same
brief:

| piece | live (helper in Blender) | script |
|---|---|---|
| pillar box | 13.6 min, 41 turns, 4,140 triangles | about 4 min over two attempts, 3,972 triangles |
| telephone kiosk | 6.8 min, 37 turns, 3,388 triangles | about 1.6 min first attempt, 7,548 triangles |

Both routes reached the same quality against the photographs; scripts were
three to eight times faster, so under the item's rule scripts are the route
for clutter, and the other pieces were made by script only. (Times start after
the brief; reading the photographs is the same work for both.)
