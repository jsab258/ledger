# BRIEF: crime and combat coverage audit

STATUS: BRIEF. Written 2026-09-21, before any research began, per the standing
instruction that a topic's brief is a file in the topic's folder, in Jafar's
words.

## Why this topic exists

> The earlier coverage audit worked backwards from KCD2, Hitman, RDR2, Disco
> Elysium and Shadows of Doubt. None of them is a crime-empire game, so the half
> of LEDGER where the player is a boss rather than a witness has far fewer
> designed verbs than the half where the town watches him. That gap was found in
> conversation, when I asked whether I could order a crew member to kill a rival
> and nothing had been designed.

## Part one: derive the verbs from the game's own structure

> Every crime has a life: planning it, doing it, being seen, being recognised,
> being remembered, being talked about, being acted on by police, rivals, crew,
> the public and the press, and living with it afterwards. Cross those stages
> with who is involved, the player, his crew, rivals, police, ordinary people
> and the press, and at each point ask what the player can do to use it, block
> it or bend it. Mark each cell as built in Core, designed but not built, or
> missing, with the file that evidences it.

> Core already has operation planning, negotiation, informing, debts, a rival
> who rings you, the press, phones, rackets, loose ends and the aftermath of a
> body, so check before calling anything missing.

## Part two: the reference games the earlier audit did not use

> Mafia, the Godfather games, Sleeping Dogs, Yakuza, GTA, and Crusader Kings for
> scheming and intrigue. Enumerate their crime, empire and combat verbs and map
> each against the grid.

## Combat as its own layer

> Treat combat as its own layer and give it real depth, since it matters as much
> as the information verbs: fists, improvised and scarce weapons, injury, being
> outnumbered, running, fighting dirty, police response, and what witnesses do
> during and after a fight. D4 and D18 set the frame: melee first, firearms
> rare, killing possible, rare, permanent and remembered forever, blood and
> light gore allowed, no torture or cruelty as spectacle.

## The content rules

> Respect the content rules: drugs exist only as an off-screen economy and are
> never a player verb, no alcohol or gambling shown, no children anywhere. Where
> a verb touches those, say so and mark it out.

## How to recommend

> For each missing verb, recommend in, out or ask, with a two-sentence argument
> against the pillars. The best verbs will be the ones only this game can have,
> where a crime's consequence runs through who saw, who knows and who talks, so
> rank those highest.

## Deliverables

> SUMMARY.md for me, one page, plain English: the grid's biggest holes, the ten
> verbs you would add first, and the ending question, since how the game ends is
> undecided and the red team found no route back once the town turns on you.
> DELIVERY.md for the studio with the full grid and its evidence.
