# The crime half is one verb short, and it is the one you asked about

Research topic: crime and combat coverage. The long version with the full grid
and its evidence is [DELIVERY.md](./DELIVERY.md) beside this file.

## First, the premise was wrong in our favour

I expected to find a thin boss half and found a thick one. Core has operation
planning with three approaches and a crew you bring, a weapons model with seven
ways an attack goes wrong, injuries that reduce what a man can do and leave
marks that identify him, blood you can wash if you have water and privacy,
weapons with a provenance and a disposal risk, feuds between people who then
refuse to work together, a police inquiry that escalates through four stages,
rivals who summon you to a table, crew whose loyalty has a ceiling set by their
nerve, and a rule that a man past that ceiling will talk to the police.

All of it read out of the source this session. The brief told me to check before
calling anything missing and that was the right instruction.

## The hole, and it is exactly where you put your finger

**There is no verb between deciding a man should be hurt and the book recording
that he was.**

You can fight him yourself. You can plan a job on a building and bring three
men. You can recruit, pay and cut in a crew. You cannot tell one of them to go
and do something to somebody. I searched every file in Core for any ordering,
commanding, dispatching or instructing verb and the only matches were database
sorting. And a job's target is a place, not a person: it has a payout and an
exposure and a building, and no field for a man.

So the boss half is not underbuilt. It is missing its one defining act, and the
seven other holes below are all downstream of the same absence.

## The ten verbs I would add first

Ranked by how much the consequence runs through who saw, who knows and who
talks, because that is the only thing this game has that others do not.

1. **Order a named man hurt, through a named crew member.** IN. The man you send
   becomes a second person who knows, with a nerve, a loyalty ceiling and a
   rule that says he talks when it breaks, all of which already exist. No other
   game makes the accomplice the risk rather than the alibi.
2. **Point a plan at a person, not a building.** IN. One field on a target and a
   resolver; everything downstream, from witnesses to the inquiry, already runs.
3. **Frame someone.** IN. The police already point at a suspect, and a verb that
   steers who they point at is pure moat: it works entirely through what the
   town believes rather than through any stat.
4. **Lean on a witness to change what they say.** IN. We can already pay a man
   to tell us something, so the machinery for paying him to say something else
   is half built, and a bought retraction that the street then learns about is
   the best possible use of the gossip mill.
5. **Move or hide a body.** IN. The record already stores where a killing
   happened, and the gap between doing it and it being found is where the
   tension of a crime game lives.
6. **Sanction your own crew.** IN. A man you dock, expel or beat is a man who
   knows everything and now hates you, which the feud system and the talks-to-
   police rule would carry without new machinery.
7. **Send men at a rival's property rather than his person.** IN. This is the
   empire game the rival arms and rackets are already built for, and it lets the
   player escalate without spending a life.
8. **Have someone vouch for you.** IN. Contradictions in what you claim are
   already detected, so an alibi that a second person has to sustain is a lie
   with a dependency, which is far better than a lie with a dice roll.
9. **Feed or kill a story in the paper.** ASK. The press already prints on its
   own from how loud things got, and a verb that touches it risks turning a
   living town into a meter the player manages. Worth your call rather than
   mine.
10. **Turn yourself in, or turn on someone.** ASK. Informing exists as a thing
    done to you and the machinery is symmetrical, but it interacts with the
    endings in ways section 7 of the delivery says nobody has traced.

Two things are OUT by the content rule rather than by judgement: anything that
makes drugs a player verb, and anything that leverages a child. Ordering a
beating is in scope; depicting one at length is not.

## Combat, briefly

Fists, improvised weapons, carrying a weapon that can be spotted or frisked,
injuries that linger and show, and witnesses who saw it: all built. Three things
are not. **Being outnumbered**, because a fight resolves one against one.
**Running**, because backing off is a move inside a fight rather than a way out
of one. **Police arriving while it is still happening**, because the law only
engages afterwards, through the body.

The first of those three is the one that would change how a fight feels, since
being outnumbered is what makes a crew worth having.

## The ending question

**The ending is not undecided.** There are five, they are written, and they are
wired: keep both, keep the kingdom, keep the straight life, lose both, or hand
it to somebody and walk.

The red team's finding is real but narrower than it sounded. Handing it over is
barred outright if you are being hunted, and the file says why in its own words:
signing over a licence settles who owns a pub, it does not settle a body, and a
successor cannot inherit a homicide. Keeping both is barred once the information
landscape is out of hand. What is left is keeping everything with nobody around
who knew you before, or giving up the business to keep the people. Losing both
is what happens if you do nothing, and the file is explicit that this is
correct.

So there is a route back. There is no route back that costs less than half of
what you have.

**The question for you is whether that is the game or a fault.** I think it is
the game, and I would not add a redemption path. But one thing genuinely needs
checking before anyone decides: I read the first gate of the eligibility rule
and not all of it, so whether those last two endings are actually reachable from
a hunted state is unconfirmed, and it is one method away from being certain.
