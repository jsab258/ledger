# BRIEF: casting the town, starting from who actually lived there

STATUS: BRIEF. Written 2026-09-24, before any research began, in Jafar's
words. Branch `research/casting`; this topic writes only under
`production/research/casting/`, and is brought onto main when done.

## The problem, in his words

> The characters in this game were never cast. Lena, Sam and Rocco are faces
> taken from Epic's MetaHuman presets as stand-ins, so Lena is a middle-aged
> woman whose face nobody chose and whose cast voice does not match her. From
> now on everyone will be cast deliberately, with face, body and voice chosen
> together, starting from who actually lived in a British port town in 1990.
> This topic provides the evidence for that.

## What to establish, in his words

> With dated sources, and name what could not be sourced:
>
> 1. Who lived in a northern English port town around 1990. Use the 1991
>    census, the first to ask about ethnic group, for towns like Hull and
>    Grimsby: age structure, sex, ethnic groups and where people were born,
>    household types, and occupations, especially docks, fishing, markets,
>    offices and industry. Where the census is hard to reach, say what else is
>    sound. Give proportions the casting plan can use directly.
> 2. How working people in such a town looked then: hair and hairstyles by age
>    and sex, facial hair, glasses, skin and how age and outdoor work showed on
>    faces, weight and build, and grooming. Use period photographs the research
>    already cites and others. Note what separates 1990 from today at a glance.
> 3. How to build a MetaHuman to match an approved portrait rather than
>    nudging presets by eye: what the current MetaHuman tools on Unreal 5.8
>    can take as input, a photograph, a generated image, a scan or a mesh, and
>    how close a match they get. Include what our own image lane could produce
>    to feed them.
> 4. How games make a crowd of hundreds from a small set of base faces and
>    bodies without it looking like clones: variation in age, hair, build,
>    clothing and movement, and what it costs.
> 5. The rules we already have that bear on this: no children anywhere, no
>    recognisable real people, and avoiding stereotypes. Say how a casting plan
>    should respect each.

## What it ends with, in his words

> SUMMARY.md for me, one page in plain words, and DELIVERY.md with the
> sources, ending with a proposed casting plan: the town's mix, how many
> people in each tier, and what the named cast should look like, grounded in
> the evidence.
