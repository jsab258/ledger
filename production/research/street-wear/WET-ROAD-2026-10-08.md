# The wet road under the true sky (research, 8 October 2026)

The sky light equals the dome; the wet road mirrors it near-white. D = read at source today (1-6, 8-10, 14 by a helper agent); S = search summary only; I = inference or own calculation.

## 1. What a wet road is

- **Mostly not a mirror.** UK chipped asphalt has 1.0-1.8 mm of texture [1 D, 2 D]; in ordinary rain the water stays below the stone tops [3 D; I]. Mirrors only in ruts, gutters, dips (I).
- **Wet stone tops:** darker (asphalt 0.12 to 0.08, ×0.68) [4 D, 5 D], shinier but no mirror (specular ratio up 3-13×) [6 D].
- **Four zones** [5 D, 7 D]: dry; wet (darker, glossier, relief kept); drenched (relief smoothed); puddle (flat mirror). Convex parts dry first.
- **Fresnel:** water reflects 2% straight down [8 D]; 21% at 15° above the road, 35% at 10°, 58% at 5° (I, [8]'s formula).
- **Overcast sky:** horizon a third of the zenith [9 D, 10 D]. A mirror at 10° returns about 0.16 of zenith brightness, wet asphalt's diffuse 0.06, so the near road reads mid-grey (I).

## 2. How games and Epic build it

- **Remember Me** [7 D; Anthem alike, 11 S]: two flood levels: a height map fills cracks, vertex alpha places puddles. Puddles: gloss 1, specular 0.02, flat normal; the wet zone keeps its normal.
- **Epic** [12 D, 13 D]: Lumen covers every roughness; Single Layer Water is forced to mirror; small-scale shadowing goes in a cavity map (Specular = Cavity × 0.5), never Specular 0.
- **Stretched highlights** come from roughness [14 D].

## 3. What UE 5.8.2 does with this road (source read, D)

- **Our material:** roughness lerps toward 0.08 (about 0.1 at wetness 0.85, I); a flat normal on the whole road from wetness 0.5 (VignetteShot.cpp ~7925). So Lagarde's puddle zone covers the street.
- **Ray tracing:** dedicated rays below roughness 0.4, fading over 0.1 (Scene.cpp:658; LumenReflections.cpp:129; LumenReflectionsCombine.ush:14). A miss returns the dome's capture, mirror-sharp (LumenTracingCommon.ush:32-47).
- **Reflection** × EnvBRDF(F0, roughness, angle) (DiffuseIndirectComposite.usf:640). Screen occlusion touches only the rough part (:650); material AO only diffuse (LumenScreenProbeGather.usf:1352, 1446). F0 below 2% counts as shadowing and kills the grazing rise (BRDF.ush:409, 598).
- **Reflectance from the engine's own integral** (SystemTextures.cpp:567-611; computed, I), at 5°/10°/16° above the road: roughness 0.08 gives 0.60/0.40/0.23; roughness 0.45 gives 0.17/0.13/0.10.
- **Asphalt gain 0.13:** the road is nearly all reflection (I).
- **Our dome** (belfast_open_field_2k, measured today): at 16°, 0.68 of its zenith on average, 0.46-1.39 by direction (CIE 0.52), repeated below 16°. The film mirrors 1.3-2.7× a standard overcast horizon (I).

## 5. Cheats to avoid

- **Dimming reflections:** sky light below the dome, r.Lumen.Reflections.SpecularScale or .Contrast (DiffuseIndirectComposite.usf:220-224): they dim glass and puddles too.
- **Specular under 0.25** road-wide: matte, not wet. Cavity map only.
- **Faking it in colour:** darker colour, road-only grading or exposure, dark decals.
- **One middle roughness:** seven tries read grainy or matte.
- **Two skies:** a dome darkened for the capture only.
