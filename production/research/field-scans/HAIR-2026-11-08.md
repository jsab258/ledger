- Indiana Jones and the Great Circle (MachineGames, SIGGRAPH 2025 Advances, "Strand Hair in IJGC"): reported as strands for characters and facial hair, a couple of corpses on cards, one hair version only, fur on animals less successful. Unverified: the slide PDF (87 pages) could not be read as text; this comes from a search summary.

## 2. Epic's route (UE 5.8 docs, read 8 Nov)
- Strands run on Windows (DX11/DX12/Vulkan), PS5 and Xbox Series; cards run everywhere. Skinned binding and RBF cost more; Rigid binding without simulation skips interpolation. Epic's one benchmark: 30 Hz or higher, human groom, 1080p, on a card the page names "RTX-2090Ti" (probably a typo). No AMD notes. [Groom platform support](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/groom-platform-support-in-unreal-engine)
- Scalability page: cost drivers are curve and point counts, interpolation, voxelisation and shadows, sky lighting and sky AO, ray tracing. Levers: Auto LOD bias, curve decimation, `r.HairStrands.SkyAO 0`, voxel size and stepping scale, MSAA 1 to 8 samples (default 4); PPLL only for cinematics. A groom LOD may mix strands with cards, chosen by platform or scalability. No millisecond figures. [Scalability](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/groom-scalability-and-performance-with-unreal-engine)
- Hair Card Generator (5.8): Dataflow-graph plugin, marked Experimental, per-LOD settings (clumps, flyaways, triangles, textures); input is an Alembic with card groups from a DCC. [Generator](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/hair-card-generator-for-grooms-in-unreal-engine)
- MetaHuman default (via search summary, unverified in full): strands at LOD 0, cards at other LODs; forcing cards gave 20 to 30% in "typical scenarios" (Fortnite-oriented page; may not apply). `r.HairStrands.UseCardsInsteadOfStrands 1` forces cards. Forum reports for 5.6 and 5.7 (strands not visible, thick or clumped strands at close range, a 5.7 shader error said fixed in 5.8, groom LOD data resident in VRAM at all distances, high render-thread cost of bindings even on Rigid) are single user posts, unverified.

## 3. AI-native and open source
- HairDeck (image to animatable hair cards): code and weights Apache-2.0, third-party parts (SAM 3, DINOv3, gated) keep their own licences. Outputs GLB, VRM, JSON; README names Blender 4.2, not Unreal. Needs CUDA (Python 3.12, PyTorch 2.8); VRAM not stated. Anime-leaning ("stylized") by its title; fit to realistic 1990 faces unverified. [Repo](https://github.com/hyz317/HairDeck)
- Strands2Cards (SIGGRAPH Asia 2025): strands to cards by clustering and differentiable fitting; code on GitHub, no licence stated (so not usable under our allowlist until one is found); needs CUDA 12.4 and the CT2Hair-derived dataset; FBX export loads in Unreal per the README. [Page](https://kenji-tojo.github.io/publications/strns2cards), [repo](https://github.com/kenji-tojo/strns2cards)
- DiffHairCard (arXiv 2505.18805): paper only read in search listing; code and licence unverified.
- Either tool needs an NVIDIA card; the PC has an AMD card. Not confirmed whether CPU runs are possible.
