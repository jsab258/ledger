## Findings
1. **Nanite vs classic LODs (Epic, [Nanite overview](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/nanite-virtualized-geometry-in-unreal-engine), read 9 Nov).** Nanite static meshes use no LODs; Epic says to enable it wherever the platform supports it; good candidates are many-instance, high-triangle, occluding and shadow-casting meshes. Unsupported: Minimum Screen Radius and distance culling, forward rendering, MSAA, morph targets; masked and WPO work but WPO splits clusters. The page gives no cost figure for small meshes and says to measure per content and hardware. Epic's Technical Details page was not reached. No source found on Nanite's cost per kit piece on a 6 GB card: unverified, a local measurement is the only evidence.
2. **HLOD (Epic, [World Partition HLOD](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/world-partition---hierarchical-level-of-detail-in-unreal-engine), read 9 Nov).** Three layer types: Instancing (lowest LOD as instances; Epic says it suits trees and foliage), Merged Mesh (no simplification), Simplified Mesh (Screen Size, Merge Distance to close doors and windows; more reduction, more tuning). Actors must be Static and assigned a layer; built by Build > Build HLODs. Small sampling on large geometry costs much memory; disabling distance fields on far merged meshes saves memory. No small-district guidance on the page. The older [HLOD intro](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/hierarchical-level-of-detail-in-unreal-engine) (read 9 Nov) only says proxies cut draw calls "especially in large open worlds".
3. **Impostors and trees.** Epic's UE 5.7 Nanite Foliage page ([link](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/nanite-foliage-in-unreal-engine)) returned only a table of contents (sections: Status, Voxel distant representation, Requirements, Limits, Performance claims): nothing confirmed. A search result (not read in full, unverified) calls it experimental in 5.7 with a voxel far form and forum reports of voxel plus WPO and ray-traced shadow faults ([80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/get-a-glimpse-of-nanite-foliage-with-voxel-representation-in-ue5-7), forum threads). Octahedral impostors are the older route (forum thread only, unverified). Whether 5.8 changed this: not checked.
4. **Texture streaming.** Epic's [overview](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/texture-streaming-in-unreal-engine) (read 9 Nov) only says the streamer raises and lowers each texture's resolution; pool and over-budget pages were not read (the project's own notes cite them as unreached). Project fact: never-streamed textures escape the pool cap.
5. **Mafia: The Old Country.** Epic's developer interview with the game director ([link](https://www.unrealengine.com/developer-interviews/mafia-the-old-country-making-the-old-feel-new-with-unreal-engine-5)) returned 403: unreached, not used. Search results only: a German tech test says UE 5.4.4 with Nanite replacing tessellation; Wikipedia mirror says Nanite, software Lumen, MetaHuman; a Guru3D claim of updated streaming threads has no primary source (unverified). No public statement of cell sizes or ranges found.
6. **Kingdom Come II.** [GamePro](https://www.gamepro.de/artikel/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-clevere-grafiktechnik,3427400.html) (read 9 Nov): CryEngine 5, SVOGI; PS5 30 fps at 1440p or 60 fps at 1080p; stutters blamed on memory or CPU limits; nothing on LOD or draw distance. No Warhorse talk on LOD or streaming was found.
7. **Studios, open source, solo developers, AI research.** Not reached in this scan (no GDC, Digital Foundry, Epic Fest or open-source impostor tool read). Absence is not evidence.
