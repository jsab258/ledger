## 1. Mafia's kit and its grid

**Two layers.**
- **Shells.** Each house type is a whole-building base shell baked by a Houdini generator (Study 4, R), with none to three floor shells stacked on it. Where there are three, the middle type repeats: ArchD House 07 stacks base, Floor_a2, Floor_a2, Floor_b (15 times) (M, S/extra_measures.json).
  - Grouping stacked shells by position gives 96 buildings: 27 with no floor shell, 11 with one, 31 with two, 27 with three (M).
  - Example, ArchB House 03: base 8.3 × 12.4 m, 14.8 m high; floor shells 4.5 and 5.5 m high (M).
  - Floor shells run 3.9 to 5.9 m high; house widths 7.8 to 17.2 m (M).
- **Instanced elements.** 29,900 placements of windows, doors, ledges, corbels, balconies and details sit on the shells (M).
- **Hidden pieces.** 1,347 placements never render: a "cut volume" mesh (443, hidden; a generator helper whose use is U), collision shells and distant proxies (M). 32,636 render.

## 2. Shared materials, strips and texel density

**One material carries the style.**
- One vertex-blend plaster (two layers and a tint) and its 13 colour variants cover 45 meshes and 8,852 slot placements (M, S/texel_main.json).
- 141 material instances are named as overrides ("ovrd") of base materials (M).
- Shells take one to four slots from the shared wall library (three most often, 14 of 23 read), one to nine slots in all (M).

**Variety by swapping, not by new meshes** (M, S/extra_measures.json; placements, not slots):
- 274 of 726 instance groups (12,010 placements, 35%) carry a material override list; many restate the mesh's own material.
- **Real swaps: 227 groups, 7,429 placements (22%)** draw a different material from the mesh's own.
- **To a sibling variant: 131 groups, 5,054 placements (15%)**; the rest go to unrelated materials.
- The reviewer's count: 229 and 136 groups, the same 22% and 15%. (8,591 is the override count in slot placements, a different unit.)

**Trim sheets** (M):
- The game has 35 trim sets in one library (index). Most are 2048 × 2048 authored; others are 1024 square or rectangular, one 2048 × 4096 (texture properties).
- On the street the library covers 3,792 of 35,423 slot placements (11%, 29 meshes): the wood-elements sheet on 21 meshes (windows, shutters, doors; 3,513), blinds (171), paving (108).
- A separate **ironwork trim** (2048 authored) covers 5,321 more (15%, 26 meshes), mainly balcony railings (2,886 on one 60 cm railing), wall ties and gratings. Trims in all: 26%.

**Texel density** (pixels per metre = texture size × tiling × 100 ÷ Unreal's per-slot UV density in cm per UV unit; M, S/texel_main.json):
- **The wood sheet** (tiling 1): window leaves and large shutters, 82% of its placements, sit at 205 to 209 cm per UV unit: about 1,000 px/m authored, 490 to 500 cooked. Door leaves, small shutters and one window-leaf type are mapped up to twice as dense (101 to 153).
- **The whole street** (34,291 slot placements): authored median 1,131 px/m (p10 965, p90 3,569); cooked median 575 (487 to 1,785).
- **By kind, cooked median:** shells 552, windows and shutters 507, style elements 641, wear patches 690.
- **Cooked is what ships** (R): the device profile ships colour and normal one mip down (WEAR), and the index holds one optional texture file in the whole game. **The in-game median is about 575 px/m.**

**How UVs map** (I, from parameters and densities): walls are tiling materials at world scale (the shared plaster repeats about once a metre; vertex colour picks the layer); joinery and ironwork sample fixed strips of a sheet at one scale. The strips' own layout was **not read** (see Not read).

## 3. Grime and decals per front

**Generated streets carry almost no decals.**
- 8 decals sit on the main street's building fronts, all on one building and within a window's height; the back street has none (M, S/decals_*.json).
- 15 of Palermo's 2,461 decals lie within 3 m of any main-street kit piece (M; script not kept, its input since deleted).

**Palermo's decals, from the reviewed wear study** (WEAR, M):
- The densest cell (878) is underground. Street walls outside it carry 754: leak stains 38%, plaster grunge 22%.
- Per wall: median 1 decal, p90 6.
- Bottom edge of the decal box (595 with a street level): at or below 30 cm 30%, 0.3 to 1.5 m 18%, 1.5 to 4 m 21%, above 4 m 32%. Boxes at the base are mostly full-height leak stains, not foot bands.
- Sizes: leak stain 5.4 × 5.1 m; projection depth median 15 cm; fade-out screen size median 0.044.
- Decal sets per house type exist by name: 14 level instances carry "Decals_HLODLayer", three of them ArchB types (R index). One stands on the measured street (M).

**What makes generated fronts look worn** (M unless marked):
1. **Vertex colour:** 90% of placements read are meshes with vertex colours; 55% use a vertex-blend master.
2. **World-position noise:** on for 31% of placements through the plaster and 5% through other materials (stone, door frames). Each placement then differs with no new data (I).
3. **Per-house material swaps:** 22% of placements (section 2).
4. **Per-placement colour:** shutters and frames carry four custom numbers, a paint colour: 186 shutter leaves brown, 46 pieces dark grey, 36 unset.
5. **Wear as geometry:** plaster patches on 28% of storey-band fronts; overlays (patches, pipes, ties, blinds) median 1 a band, p90 9.

**Mafia's rule (I):** grime lives in the material, varied per house and per placement; patches are geometry the house type asks for; decals are spent where a scene is authored.

## 4. Epic's procedural city method in Unreal 5.8

P5 covers PCG's status, partitioning, seeds and costs; this is the facade method only.

**Documented and in core PCG (version 1.0):**
- Grammar nodes: Spline To Segment, Subdivide Segment, Subdivide Spline, Duplicate Cross-Section, Select Grammar, Print Grammar (R [1]). Primitive Cross-Section is "currently Experimental" and needs the Geometry Script interop (R [1]).
- A module is a symbol, a size and a "scalable" flag (R [2]).
- Grammar strings (R [1][2]):
  - `[A,B]` a sequence; `[A,B]3` three times;
  - `*` as many as fit; `+` at least once;
  - `{A:2,B:1}` weighted random;
  - `<A,B,C>` the first that fits.
- "Accept Incomplete Subdivision" makes a partial fill valid; subdivision takes a seed attribute (R [1][2]).
- **Core building sample** (R [5]): a map, a building graph and four modules (wall, window, window hole, column); its floor grammar reads "[MainFloor][Intermediate]*". Duplicate Cross-Section stacks floors.

**Shipped as Experimental, PCG Primitives' shape grammar** (R [3][4], skill text and demo assets):
- A definition is a data asset of **slots** (floors stacked, or lanes side by side): symbol, size, scalable, mirror, offset, "is corner".
- A **main grammar** orders the slots: the demo building reads "L1, L2*, L3", a "vertical building with 3 floor types".
- Each slot's **rule** grammar places modules: "C,W,Win*, D, Win*, W, C-". The advanced template groups four wall and window widths, XL to S, in `[ ]`; how the code applies them was not read (U).

**What fits Mafia's kit:**
- **Fits.** Mafia's stacks (base, middle repeated, top) are exactly "L1, L2*, L3" or "[MainFloor][Intermediate]*". Fixed openings with fitted trims are modules with and without "scalable". House variety is a seed and attributes; per-instance data carries a colour or variant (P5).
- **Does not fit.** PCG makes no shells: wall between openings must be modules (as Epic's wall and column), or come through the Experimental Geometry Script route. PCG does not pick materials per house; attributes do.
- **Licence.** The method is free to take. Copying Epic's sample or Primitives graphs or assets needs his answer first (P5).

## 5. The method for LEDGER: a proposal for phase 2's design

His ruling of 25 October: phase 5's design is written during phase 2, and until then only its research. This section is therefore **a proposal**, to be adopted, changed or dropped when phase 2 writes the design. Only 5.6, a research trial outside the game, can run now.

### 5.1 Kit grid and families

- **Grid in bricks.** 75 mm vertically (one course), 225 mm across (one brick and joint) (R: vignette-scene.json, brick_course_m 0.075 and brick_length_m 0.215; the 10 mm joint is I).
  - Every piece's size is a whole number of these; names carry the opening size (SM_SashWindow_900x1650_01), as Mafia's do.
- **Fixed and fitted.** Openings, doors and corner piers keep their size; wall infill, string courses, cornice and gutter runs are "scalable", within 10% either way, else another module is chosen (I; Mafia keeps 88% of windows and doors within 10%, M).
- **Pivots.** Bottom face; linear pieces at their left end; openings centred at the sill; leaves at the hinge (Mafia's pattern, M).
- **One house family:** a ground, a repeatable upper and a top floor type, with end, party-wall and chimney pieces (Mafia's stacks, Epic's samples).
  - **Size target (I):** 30 to 45 distinct meshes a family, about half Mafia's 62 to 94 for plainer houses; each whole front shows at least 15 (Mafia's storey-band median; its whole-front median is 26).
- **Spec values for the period check** (I; 8 October rule: a fresh helper writes targets from sources, photographs win): storeys 3.4 → 3.375 m and 2.8 → 2.775 m; window 0.85 → 0.9 m; plot widths 5.4, 5.85 or 6.075 m. Quay Street as built is untouched.

### 5.2 The strip material

- **Walls:** kit-materials.json's existing "brick" kind, plus one new "render" kind, each with a vertex-colour wear layer and world-position noise. No material in tools/ue reads vertex colour yet (WEAR, R).
- **Trim:** three 4096 × 2048 sheets at 512 px/m, as 4-BUDGETS.md sets: timber (sash, door, fascia), stone and stucco (sills, lintels, string course), metal (railings, gutters, pipes; Mafia's ironwork trim covers 15%).
- **One scale per sheet:** every piece within 5% of its sheet's UV density, close-up pieces allowed denser by name (Mafia's common window pieces sit within 2%, M).
- **Per-house variety without new meshes:** street_wear.py's seed, tint and wear per bay (street-wear.json, R) as per-placement custom data, plus a wall or paint variant per house. Target (I, after Mafia's 22%): each house differs from both neighbours in at least one of wall variant and joinery paint.

### 5.3 The decal rule per front

- Base grime in the material (D53; street-wear.json), as Mafia does.
- Decals by WEAR's rule, its one home (method step 2): median 1 a wall, at most 6 where the player looks, leak stains about two in five, each tied to a source (sill, gutter, pipe).
- Expected: about 1 decal on a typical 6 m front, up to 6 on the walked path. No foot band is taken from Mafia.
- Fade-out screen size starts at Mafia's 0.044, checked set on every decal.

### 5.4 The generator

- **Core PCG only**, to meet "kits plus Unreal's procedural tools" (25 October):
  - one spline per front;
  - Duplicate Cross-Section stacks floor types by a grammar such as "[Ground][Upper]*[Top]";
  - Subdivide Segment places modules per floor;
  - the module table, kept in the kit's JSON home, imported as a data table the graph loads (core node [2]);
  - each plot's seed from its identity;
  - output exported as JSON (Export Selected Attributes, editor only) to a placement spec, its one home.
- **Structure from Epic's samples, not their assets** (licence above).
- **Where the result lives is open** (P5 step 5): a new saved level, static and streamed by cell, or spawned at load from the spec as the street is today. A design choice for phase 2, and a scope question for him.
- **Fallback:** if PCG fails twice, a script reading the same grammar (I); that changes how his ruling is met, so he is asked first.
- **Hand edits:** tuned plots carry our own tag; a check fails when one changes (P5).

### 5.5 Sampled checks, on the placement spec

1. **Grid:** openings and piers on the 75/225 mm grid within 5 mm; fitting within 10%.
2. **Grammar:** doors on the ground floor only; at least one brick of pier between openings; every corner resolved.
3. **Variety:** each whole front at least 15 distinct meshes; adjacent plots never share a module string (P5); the neighbour rule in 5.2.
4. **Placement (I tolerances):** no overlap over 1 cm; nothing floating or buried over 2 cm; 0.9 m clear before every door; front-to-front width 9 to 15 m (Study 4).
5. **Budgets** (4-BUDGETS.md, through tools/ue/asset_checks.py and asset-budgets.json, reuse): a module at most 8,000 triangles and 4 materials; a whole frontage at most 150,000 triangles and 12 materials.
6. **Materials:** every sheet piece within 5% of its sheet's density; wall materials within 20% of 512 px/m (WEAR's C6); every slot named in kit-materials.json.
7. **Decals:** per wall by kind against 5.3; each within 30 cm of its source; fade size set.
8. **Frame time** (when it enters the game): packaged walk median at most 16.7 ms, card at most 6 GB (PLAN, P5).
9. **Repeatable:** generate twice and the specs match; counts against the last run, with a link per failure (P5).
10. **People look at:** fixed views, seeded random points and every flagged item, one fresh reviewer per view.
