# Phase 5 by generation: Unreal 5.8's PCG and checking at scale (28 October 2026)

Builds on aaa-street (1 October) and Studies 4 and 12. Helpers read the web; the writer re-read key pages and the installed engine. (h): read by a helper only. (I): inference.

## 1. What PCG in 5.8 gives

- **Status.** The core plugin is version 1.0, not Beta, on by default [1]; Production-Ready since 5.7 (h) [9].
  - **Beta:** the Geometry Script interop (holding the Primitive Cross-Section node), External Data and Python interops [1]; GPU processing, whose mesh spawner is Experimental [7].
  - **Experimental plugins:** PCG Primitives (Epic's example library), the PCG Toolset (assistant tools with eight skills, one "Shape Grammar Definition") and seven interops [1][3].
  - **Experimental in core code:** the Manual Edit and Draw Spline tools, among others [2].
- **Facades.** Grammar nodes split splines and segments into kit modules [2]. City Sample PCG keeps each grammar in a data asset: slots, each with a symbol, size and rule [4]; ground, middle and top floors get separate grammars (h) [14].
- **Streets and scatter.** Spline sampling, offsets, spline meshes, polygon clipping; Self Pruning removes overlaps; Distance and World Raycast queries; the spawner picks meshes by weight, attribute or category and packs per-instance data [2].
- **Partition and hierarchy.**
  - Hierarchical generation runs nodes on grids of 4 to 2,048 m (default 256 m), scalable by a multiplier; data flows only from larger grids to smaller [2][5].
  - Output joins its source's switchable layer (Data Layer) and distant-version layer (HLOD) [8].
  - A builder generates whole maps by script, but only in a World Partition (streamed, cell-based) level [2].
- **Editor or runtime.** Generate on load, on demand or at runtime [2]. The installed engine lets generation take 5 ms a frame in play, 15 in the editor (these apply; Epic's page gives 16.667 and 50) [2][5].
- **Seeds.** A component seed (default 42) and per-point seeds; per-node determinism tests and a "Run Graph Determinism Test" button [2]. Attributes export to JSON [2].
- **Hand edits are fragile.** Edit Points and Manual Edit (Experimental) overrides are matched by location: "A significant change in location or bounds will orphan this override"; orphans are only badged in the editor [2][6]. A scripted build loses them silently.
- **GPU.** Eight node types can switch to the GPU, plus a custom-shader node [2]. GPU instances are not saved and have no collision or navigation [7].

## 2. City Sample and Electric Dreams, as methods

- **City Sample (5.0).** Houdini made layout, roads, lots, buildings, then furniture, as points instanced in Unreal. Each building is a volume dressed by its style's grammar, "hundreds of instances", with storeys of Corner, Wall and Entrance modules (h) [10][11].
- **City Sample PCG (5.8).** No Houdini: 18 graphs run in order, terrain to an outer forest [4]. Buildings are footprints extruded to a height in one of 27 styles [4]. Populated, it is about 2,600 actors; all must be loaded to regenerate, in "a few minutes" [4]. It uses PCG Primitives "extensively" [4], and that plugin is Experimental [1].
- **Electric Dreams.** A hand-built assembly is saved by "Level to PCG" as points with mesh, material, tags and parent; graphs respawn it and tags filter pieces (h) [12][13].
- **Licence.** The method is free to take. A graph copied from City Sample PCG or PCG Primitives is Epic content with no allowlist line [24]: his question first.

## 3. How big games check generated content

No source gave a sampling rate. At scale, studios made generation repeatable, validated everything automatically on a schedule, and had people look only at flagged items, each linked.
- **Assassin's Creed Origins** (120 km², Syndicate 12) [16]:
  - Daily C# validators: placement metrics on 22 object types, AI network tests, loot per location; each failure has a reason ("Rope too steep") and a link.
  - One build machine, 3 hours, 12 reports: 520 locations, 155,932 paths, 210,689 gameplay objects, 1,000,000 AI network nodes and edges, 5,000,000 procedural objects.
  - Nightly tables give totals, invalid counts, the change since the last run, and a link; one showed 746 of 989 ladders invalid. By eye, 228 ziplines at 2 minutes is 8 hours.
  - Fixed QC passes became daily tests. Lessons: start earlier; false positives; too many emails.
- **Spider-Man** (about 3,000 vignettes, 3,000 crimes) [17]: numeric rules (150 m apart, over 9 m from a crosswalk). Tuned items are tagged; on regeneration they stay if they still fit, else are removed or flagged with camera coordinates and in-editor notes. A world scan fixes, flags or removes assets in walls or floating. Buildings are blocked out by hand, then modularised from kit.
- **Ghost Recon Wildlands** (h) [19]: unlocked results lose "all you manual tweaks at the next update"; its terrain generator was "still not 100% deterministic".
- **Horizon Zero Dawn** (h) [21]: deterministic placement, 0.5 m exclusion maps, about 250 µs of GPU.

## 4. Studies 4 and 12 as rules and checks

"Module string": the sequence of kit pieces along one front.

| Target | PCG rule | Check (on exported JSON) |
|---|---|---|
| ≥5 props per 10 m | Pavement spline; props by kind from the fixed table; none at doors or crossings (I) | Per 10 m, both sides, by kind: street mean ≥5; no empty 20 m on the walked path (I) |
| Kit reuse | One family per terrace, stacked floor types; hero plots hand-made | Distinct facade meshes, uses per mesh; adjacent plots never share a module string (I) |
| Width 9–15 m | Fronts offset from the street spline | Front to front every 5 m (I) |
| Views end on a building or a turn | Turns and closing buildings in the plan | From fixed cameras and seeded walk points, an eye-height ray hits a building, or the view is marked a turn (I) |
| Budgets [23] | Static pieces, Nanite; plain instanced meshes, which with Nanite "is always the choice" [22]; small props shadow only near | Per 128 m cell (I): instances, meshes, shadow lights; packaged walk 16.7 ms median, card 6 GB |
| Placement | Self Pruning; ray down to ground | Overlaps, floating or buried pieces, blocked doors, navigation breaks |

## 5. Rooms behind windows

- Neither PCG nor Epic's samples generate rooms; no primary source explains City Sample's window rooms (h) [4][10].
- Study 4's games use rooms sparingly: Mafia 24 fake rooms in about 8,100 window pieces; Kingdom Come II furnishes 88 of 166 houses [23].
- For us (I): a rendered room per shop only where the walk passes; elsewhere each window gets an index into a small atlas of scripted rooms, passed to the window material as per-instance data [2], or blinds and dark rooms.

## 6. Costs on this PC

- **Instances are cheap.** Nanite's cap is 16,777,216 instances [2]; an instance takes 64 bytes on the card, a separate object 672 [22]. Twenty streets like Mafia's measured one (about 2,350 pieces [23]) make about 47,000 instances, 3 MB (I).
- **What likely binds.** The render thread: median 15.17 ms in a 15.19 ms frame, "a lead", since what the engine's render-thread figure means was not checked. The street's meshes are movable, so the shadow cache never applies. The game alone peaks at 5.9 to 6.8 GB of card memory [23].
- **So:** generated pieces static, streamed by cell, nothing generated at runtime; GPU scatter only for collision-free litter and leaves [7].
- **5.8 notes:** faster GPU scatter, instance reuse and scheduling; no timings [6].

## For LEDGER: a method for phase 5

1. **Kit as data.** One table, the kit's home: symbol, grid size, family, kind, approval; graphs read it (CLAUDE.md).
2. **Small hand inputs.** Street splines, plots, a style per plot, "hand" flags for hero plots; everything else derived (Spider-Man's hand blockout [17]).
3. **Stages in order.** Streets and kerbs, plots, facades, roofs, furniture, dressing, windows; each passes its no-go areas on (City Sample PCG's order [4]).
4. **Repeatable.** Each plot's seed from its identity (I). Generate twice; exports must match [2]. Keep the export as a golden; compare after each engine update and between this PC and the build machine (I). Tuned plots carry LEDGER's own tag; a check fails when one changes or no longer fits (Spider-Man [17]), since PCG's overrides orphan silently [2].
5. **Built by script; saved static, streamed by cell.** No saved level exists: the street is spawned at run time, movable, into the engine's empty Entry map [24]. A new saved, partitioned level is a scope item for phase 5's design. Nothing solid generated at runtime or on the GPU.
6. **Validators after each generation (I),** on exported JSON in the build checks, with counts against the last run and a link per failure, as Origins' tables [16].
7. **People see** one complete sample block first (CLAUDE.md); then per batch the fixed views, seeded random walk points and every flagged item, one fresh reviewer per view. The sample size his 25 October ruling asks for is open [24].
8. **Measured per batch** in the packaged build, with 0, 5, 10 and 20 visible people (PLAN [24]); each stage's cost and failures logged.

Experimental parts (grammar skill, Primitives graphs, Manual Edit) are optional; no step needs them.
