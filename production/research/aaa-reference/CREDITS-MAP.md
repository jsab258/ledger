## In plain words

Kingdom Come II's full credit list could be read: 1,826 credits, 1,490 different names, 445 credits under Warhorse's own name. Mafia: The Old Country's could not (MobyGames and IMDb refuse robots, no fan wiki has it), so it has no headcount, only 9 lead credits. Seven jobs have nobody in LEDGER; the three that matter most for the thirty-minute build are motion capture beyond library clips, authored cutscenes, and music. Jafar can close the Mafia gap by pasting MobyGames' credits page from his own browser.

## 2. Kingdom Come: Deliverance II, counted

**How the counts were made.**
- One entry is one name (or one organisation) under one role title, exactly as the roll shows it. A person credited in three roles is three entries. The "people" column counts each name once inside its own row-set (a nickname or degree in brackets after a name is dropped; accents kept; with accents folded the whole roll has 1,487 names, three fewer).
- The fan page states its own tally: 1,520 people across 1,846 entries (S1, wiki). My count from its table is 1,826 entries and 1,490 names (derived, my script). The page's figures are 1.1 % and 2.0 % higher; I could not tell why. Mine leave out the software and copyright notices, and treat 10 organisations (two translation agencies, two recording venues, one recording studio, one horse-supply company, four scanning or photography companies) as organisations, not people.

## 5. The gaps: every job with nobody

**Gap 1. Motion capture, stunts and combat choreography** (map row 20).
- In the AAA game: KCD2 credits 158 entries for capture crew and actors (89 of them cutscene capture actors), 12 for stunts and fencers, and 18 more at an outside capture-editing studio.
- What it would take (options): (a) do without, as now: library clips only (Mixamo and Epic's movement clips are on the allowlist, items 3 and 8) and combat stays deferred (PLAN.md, P17). (b) Markerless capture from video of a person who is not hired (Jafar or a friend) through an Apache 2.0 pose estimator (MediaPipe or MMPose, named in production/research/markerless-mocap/RECHECK.md), cleaned and retargeted by script in Blender; neither tool is on the allowlist, so each needs a decision record naming its weights licence first.
