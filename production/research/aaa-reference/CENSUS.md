# CENSUS: what Kingdom Come: Deliverance II and Mafia: The Old Country shipped, set beside LEDGER's plan

Cloud week 42, unit 7b, part CENSUS. Written 12 to 13 October 2026 (Swiss time) from public pages only. Nothing in this file comes from either game's files, text, pictures or audio. Every number is marked with a key such as [K1] or [M2]. The key opens the page it came from, and the Sources section at the end gives each page's address, the date it was read and what kind of source it is.

## In plain words

KCD2 is about 32 square kilometres, with nearly 2,400 simulated people, about 630 named ones and 32 main quests. Mafia: The Old Country has 15 chapters, about 52 named people and a story of 10 to 15 hours.
LEDGER's planned town is 0.75 square kilometres, with 44 named people planned and five story threads in Act I.
In total size LEDGER is 2 to 7 percent of KCD2. Per square kilometre and per hour of play it is close or ahead on people and story threads, and far behind on lines, items and length.
Neither studio published buildings, enterable interiors or animation counts, so no ratio was made for them.
Jafar can use this as a yardstick for scope, not as a target. Nothing here recommends a purchase.

## How to read this file

- Kinds: **official** (the studio or publisher), **developer interview** or **developer talk**, **wiki** (a fan wiki's own counts), **community count**, **press**, **derived** (my own arithmetic, with the formula shown).
- "Not published" means the studio did not say, after the searches listed in "Not reached". No figure was guessed. A figure marked unverified is never used in a ratio.
- Plan and built: every LEDGER figure is marked **PLAN** (written down, not built) or **BUILT** (exists in the game or in the simulation today). Cloud-week pieces that exist as files but are not yet in the game are marked **MADE, NOT IN GAME**.
- Hours: HowLongToBeat, the usual long-play tracker, was not used. Its robots.txt forbids automated reading and names Claude's agents, so no tracker figure and no tracker sample size is shown. The hours below are the developers' own words where they gave any, then press estimates. Press estimates show no sample size.
- Two wikis supplied the character, location and item counts: the Kingdom Come wiki on Fandom (read through its public interface, because its pages refuse automated reading) and the Mafia wiki on Fandom. A wiki count is a floor, not a total: nobody checks that a wiki is complete. The wiki's voice-cast page says its list was extracted from the game files by the wiki's own editors. I read only the published pages and opened no game file.

## 6. What the gap means

**Where LEDGER is far from AAA density**
- Total size. The planned town is 2.3% of KCD2's area. The simulated population is under 2% of KCD2's. Named people are 7% of KCD2's wiki count. Story threads are 3% to 4% of KCD2's quests. The thirty-minute build is about 1% of KCD2's main story and 3% to 5% of Mafia's.
- Written words. KCD2's script is 2.2 million words; LEDGER's fixed lines are 55 authored plus enumerated street lines. The comparison is not like for like, because LEDGER's talk is made live. But half of KCD2's recorded lines are reactions to what the player did, which is the kind of line LEDGER's design leans on. LEDGER has no hand-written bank of such lines of that size.
- Things. About 160 planned asset kinds against about 1,000 item pages for KCD2. The plan lists no playable inventory.
- Interiors. One walk-in room is built. The plan says 6 to 10 in all. Neither game published its number, so the distance cannot be measured.

**Where LEDGER is close**
- People per square kilometre. LEDGER's planned 59 named people per km2 is about three times KCD2's 19.7 named per km2. The 41 simulated residents give 55 per km2, which is three quarters of KCD2's 75 simulated per km2.
- People and threads per hour. The first thirty minutes plan to meet 5 named people (10 per hour), against 6 to 9 per hour in KCD2 and 3 to 5 in Mafia. The first week plans 3.5 story threads an hour, against 1.2 to 2.4 quests an hour in KCD2 and 1.0 to 1.5 chapters an hour in Mafia.
- Named against simulated. KCD2 simulates nearly 2,400 people and its wiki names about 630, so roughly one in four is named (derived, a rough guide because the two numbers come from different kinds of source). LEDGER's 41 simulated people are all named, and CHARTER.md says every visible person takes part in perception and memory. Mafia's streets were described by a reviewer as full of people who say nothing.

**Two cautions**
- The close ratios are flattered by the small area and the short play time. 59 per km2 across 0.746 km2 is 44 people; KCD2's 20 per km2 across 32 km2 is 629.
- The most important numbers are the ones neither studio published: buildings, enterable interiors, animations and lines of dialogue. The sure way to fill them is the study of the game files on the PC that RULINGS.md allows (11 October). It is outside this file.

Wikipedia reports a critic praising the choice to play as a gambler [K18]. LEDGER's rule of no alcohol and no gambling rules out the tavern and dice features entirely.
- Mafia TOC: Steam notes suggestive themes [M18]. The plot summary has a vineyard and wine business, a cover story of a drunken car accident, and (in Man of Honor) a robbery of a gambling den [M1]. The wiki lists a wine business and a cafe-bar among its business and building pages [M17].
