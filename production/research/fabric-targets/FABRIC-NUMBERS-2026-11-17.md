# Fabric numbers for Kiln presets: research notes, read 2026-11-17

Method: pages fetched through a text-extracting reader. Only HTML pages and the few PDFs the reader could decode gave readable text. Every table below was copied from a page actually read on 2026-11-17 (the "date read" for every row). "Verified" means the number was read in the primary source page; it does not mean the source is a standard reference. Values taken through the reader's summary of the page, not by eye on the original, are flagged "reader-extracted". No synthesis is made here.

Unit notes (from the NCSU KES page, https://textiles.ncsu.edu/tpacc/comfort-performance/kawabata-evaluation-system, read 2026-11-17): B in gf cm2/cm (FB2); G in gf/(cm deg) (FB1); EMT in % (extension at 500 gf/cm max load); WT in gf cm/cm2; LT dimensionless. 1 oz/yd2 = 33.9 g/m2 (arithmetic; so 12 oz = about 407, 14 oz = about 475 g/m2; unverified as a source figure, search snippet only).

## Coverage at a glance

Real KES-style measured values were obtained for only a few mixed woven fabrics (table Q2/3/4 A) plus two denim figures (Q3 B, snippet only). No source read gave full KES numbers (EMT/LT/WT, B, G) for melton/coating, drill, taslan/ripstop, jersey, rib or skirt wool. Gaps are listed at the end.
