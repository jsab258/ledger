## Period checks (photographs win over books)

- Kames, 24 May 1995 (Geograph 27024): an unlet shop's glass reads grey-blue and blank, paint gone, **no whitewash**. The ruling outranks it (shop-windows D10); the photograph governs only the tone.
- East London, 1986 to 1990: "innumerable empty shops many of them faced with graffitied corrugated iron" (Flashbak, Marshall, re-read 17 Nov). Some units stood empty more than five years (Dein, 1988; NEXT-PIECES note). Both are London, not the north.
- No dated 1988 to 1992 photograph of a whitewashed empty shop was found, in the north or anywhere (NEXT-PIECES note; Picture Sheffield's "empty shop" and "to let" results, searched 17 Nov, date from 1961 to 1967 or after 2000).
