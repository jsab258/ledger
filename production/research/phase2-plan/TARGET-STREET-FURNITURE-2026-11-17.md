# Target: street furniture and lamps, Quay Street (17 Nov 2026)

What the pieces must look like and measure; not the build. Written by a helper who did not build them (RULINGS 8 Oct). Most pieces already have a reviewed cloud-week target (9 to 13 Oct). This target gathers them, says which built piece is reused and adds what is still owed. "Read 17 Nov" means re-read today; project files are cited with their own dates. "Judgement" means no source sets the number. Street frame: x 0 to 48 m, kerb faces at ±3.0 m, footways 2.0 m (vignette-scene.json).

**Questions answered.**
1. Which furniture a northern port street of 1988 to 1992 plausibly has, and which pieces exist already.
2. Each part's size, colour, material, mounting height and place along the 48 m.
3. The lamps' light by night, and each part's wear in the street-weathering skill's terms.
4. Which photographs a reviewer judges each part against.

**Reference first.** Mafia: The Old Country's measured Palermo street carries 4.9 true props per 10 m, of which lamps 0.5 and signs with post boxes 0.5 (aaa-reference/studies/04-street-world.md, 25 Oct). Its lamp is two lights: a key spot for the pool and a bulb point reaching about 1 m (studies/05-visuals.md). Both methods are copied. The density bar of 5 per 10 m includes pots, boxes and laundry, which this street does not carry, so only the lamp and sign rates are taken (Judgement).
