# PS5 gap list, per element (n09-4; fresh reviewer, 8 Nov 2026)

Judged from seven stills at full size: the corner as the packaged game draws it on 7 Nov (hook and reverse views, day and night) and Ron and Darren dressed on 8 Nov (two close, Darren whole). The bar is the Hook sheet (production/reference/hook-sheet.png) and two KCD2 town frames (arcades, fountain; kept on F:, not in the repository). The 1 Oct study (production/research/aaa-street/SUMMARY.md) found the gap was art, not engine features; nothing here contradicts it. Re-judge at each phase's end on the same views.
