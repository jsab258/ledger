## 1. The reference method, and which numbers fit

- **Mafia** builds its streets from kit. A generator bakes facade and roof meshes; windows, doors and balconies are placed on top; the measured street holds about 2,350 placements of about 150 meshes, about 15 uses a mesh, 18 to 19 facade pieces from 2 families. Its hero town San Celeste has about one shape mesh per building, so hero buildings are one-offs inside the same system (R, studies/04-street-world.md, "Unique buildings against kit", read 17 Nov; study measured 25 Oct). Its variation comes from layers over shared pieces, not from new pieces: world-position noise on 31% of plaster placements, material swaps per house on 22%, wear as geometry on 28% of storey bands, trims on 26% of kit slots (R, as cited in N16-4-TARGET-2026-11-16.md section 2 and the shopfront skill, from MAFIA-KIT-2026-10-28.md; that file was not re-read for this plan: U at second hand). Decals: median 1 a wall, at most 6 where the player looks (R, street-weathering skill section 4, from MAFIA-WEAR-2026-10-28.md).
- **Kingdom Come II** builds each house as one whole mesh with placed windows and doors; about 3 uses a building mesh, the most reused house 7 times (R, studies/04). No shopfront or roof method was found in its studies (R, shopfront and slate-roofs skills).
- **Study 13**: tuning and rules live in data homes, one rule per system, never hand-placed values (R, studies/13-pipeline.md "How tuning lives in data", read 17 Nov).
- **What fits.** Quay Street's shopfronts are one-offs, as San Celeste's hero blocks are (studies/04, target 4). So "copying the corner" is not copying meshes: it is laying the corner's proven layers (shared material rows, wear placed by rule from the street's data, per-house seeds and variation, the street's own glass and night light) over each one-off front, Mafia's way, each change in its home.
- **Numbers taken:** decals median 1 a wall, at most 6 in view; brick 5 to 15% brightness across a bay; worn paint 3 to 10% within 5 cm of an edge; texel density 512 px a metre within 20% (street-weathering skill sections 2 to 4; shopfront skill section 5).
- **Numbers not taken:** Mafia's 15 uses a mesh and 88% kit share (they describe kit terraces; the shops are one-offs, which n16-4's kit-reuse check counts apart, N16-4-TARGET 3b). Per-front cost in either game: none found; this item's own scoreboard is the measure.
