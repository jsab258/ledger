## 1. What the 5.7 update adds (all secondary, unverified)

- About 400 new animations, a new character and a locomotion dataset "with less data" (readray.ai summary of Epic's post, https://readray.ai/en/notes/unreal-engine-5-20260221/ue5-game-animation-update, 9 Nov).
- A Smart Object level: seats that non-player characters approach "from different angles" to sit, plus improved warping (same summary; a search summary says the same, 9 Nov).
- Also Mover plugin (experimental), slide mechanic, an animation widget, a procedural tank.
- NOT confirmed anywhere reached: bench versus chair, the number of angles, sit-down and stand-up transition clips, weight-shift idles, new look-at, foot-placement tools. The advisor's wording "bench sitting from several angles, sitting and foot-placement tools" matches the summary only for "seats, several angles"; the rest is unverified.
- Earlier reading (LOCOMOTION-2026-10-06.md): The Rookies, 24 Aug 2026, said the 5.8 update sits people on benches by smart objects and motion matching (that file marks it observed; Epic's post 403). Search text for 5.8 (9 Nov): "Enhanced Smart Objects ... not a fully fledged interaction system ... demonstrates how motion matching can be utilized for seamless interactions with objects in the world", dated 12 Aug 2026 (unverified, search text). 5.8 also reportedly adds an experimental Look-At point-of-interest solver in Control Rig (ALIVE-READING, unverified).

- Reached (docs page, 9 Nov): motion matching picks databases through a Chooser table, CHT_PoseSearchDatabases; Leg IK pins feet to IK foot bones; steering is blocked during idle clips. Sitting itself is not covered by the docs page.
- Smart objects driving the sit: from secondary text only. Mechanism, assets and whether the sit is a motion-matching database, a montage or warped clips: unverified.
- What would carry (inference, not confirmed): GASP clips are on Epic's UEFN mannequin and reach our MetaHuman through the retargeter RTG_UEFN_to_Metahuman_nrw already used for the walk (TURN-FOOT-DIAGNOSIS-2026-11-08.md). Sit and stand clips would retarget the same way; smart-object and chooser logic is Unreal assets, not skeleton-bound, but our people are driven by C++ (SliceCharacter), so adopting it means rebuilding it there.
