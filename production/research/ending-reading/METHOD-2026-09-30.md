# Making hidden ending conditions readable without meters, end to end (research note, 30 September 2026)

Asked by Jafar's rule of 30 September (research the whole method first) for
item i, reopened as U4. A research helper was given the work and the two failed
attempts, not a theory; about thirty minutes, web only.

The work: in LEDGER, hidden simulation state decides how Tom's week ends:
- the books;
- how far the police have got;
- the detective's case;
- the successor;
- a friend's loyalty;
- the street's talk;
- the witnesses' nerve.

The first attempt put the numbers into words, a meter by another name. The
second built Tom's reading from signs that do not change where the thresholds
do (production/research/ending-reading/NOTE-2026-09-28.md).

## 1. Find what decides the ending, and where it flips

1. Simulation the player cannot perceive is wasted: only the model in the
   player's head counts. Sylvester, "The Simulation Dream", June 2013,
   https://tynansylvester.com/2013/06/the-simulation-dream/
2. Plot the stats against the outcomes to find the abrupt flips, and there widen
   the buffer or add a warning event. Short, "Narrative States", 23 Nov 2019,
   https://emshort.blog/2019/11/23/narrative-states/
3. "Complex deterministic outcomes can be just as obscure as a stat roll"
   unless the player is shown how outcomes are decided. Short, 9 Apr 2022,
   https://emshort.blog/2022/04/09/what-does-your-narrative-system-need-to-do/

## 2. A few named states per variable, and every sign tied to the state

4. **Dishonored 2:** three chaos states. The same state sets the bloodfly nests,
   the guards and some lines, and each part of the ending has a version per
   state. Harvey Smith via Wccftech, 30 May 2016,
   https://wccftech.com/dishonored-2s-chaos-system-implications-explained-game-director/
5. **Undertale:** once an area's kill quota is met, fights come up empty and the
   music changes. The sign and the route's gate read one counter.
   https://undertale.wiki/w/Genocide_Route (accessed 30 Sep 2026)
6. **Disco Elysium:** at four hidden copotype points a thought offers the
   identity, so the notice fires on the threshold itself.
   https://discoelysium.wiki.gg/wiki/Copotypes (accessed 30 Sep 2026)
7. **Hitman: Blood Money (2006):** notoriety shown only through the paper's
   police sketch, which grows more accurate as it rises.
   https://hitman.fandom.com/wiki/Newspaper (accessed 30 Sep 2026)
8. **Kingdom Come: Deliverance II:** reputation shown as one of three faces,
   never a number. **Red Dead Redemption 2:** honour in bands.
   - https://kingdomcomedeliverance2.wiki.fextralife.com/Reputation
   - https://reddead.fandom.com/wiki/Honor
9. **Shared state.**
   - Valve's lines query one shared store of world facts, the same state the
     rules use. Ruskin, GDC, March 2012,
     https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1015528/AI-driven-Dynamic-Dialog-through
   - Shadows of Doubt derives its clues from what it simulated. 18 Aug 2018,
     https://colepowered.com/shadows-of-doubt-devblog-7-theres-been-a-procedurally-generated-murder/

## 3. A visible stage between crossing the line and the ending

10. **Frostpunk:** crossing discontent or hope starts an ultimatum, not an
    ending: about two days to recover.
    https://www.gamepressure.com/frostpunk/what-happens-when-discontent-or-hope-reach-critical-values/zfad7c
    Players cheat its single check:
    https://steamcommunity.com/app/323190/discussions/0/689746131737958368/
11. **Papers, Please:** names each relative's state every night before anyone
    dies. Game Developer, 3 Sep 2013,
    https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/designing-the-bleak-genius-of-i-papers-please-i-
12. **Pentiment:** no hidden right answer; the consequence is people remembering
    and questioning. Sawyer via Shacknews, 8 Oct 2025,
    https://www.shacknews.com/article/146276/pentiment-josh-sawyer-no-correct-answer

## 4. Check that the sign and the rule agree

13. **Valve:** a design is a hypothesis and a playtest an experiment: watch,
    have players think aloud, log stats. Ambinder, GDC, March 2009,
    https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1566/Valve-s-Approach-to-Playtesting
14. **Readability has two halves:** the warning before, and the expectation
    after. Kubodera, 19 Apr 2022,
    https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/designing-for-difficulty-readability-in-arpgs
15. **Where players read a sign as meaningful, the rule was moved to match it.**
    - Dishonored 2 made the Heart's judgements count. 17 Aug 2016,
      https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/how-arkane-studios-is-mixing-up-the-morality-of-i-dishonored-2-i-
    - Meier's keynote put what players perceive ahead of the simulation. 31 Mar 2010,
      https://www.museumofplay.org/blog/gdc-2010-game-psychology-101/

## 5. What goes wrong most often

16. **Hidden choices revealed only at the end:** The Witcher 3's Ciri endings
    felt arbitrary. Shamus Young, 6 Oct 2018,
    https://www.shamusyoung.com/twentysidedtale/?p=44246
17. **A visible number with hidden thresholds:** Mass Effect 3's readiness; the
    2021 remaster removed that factor.
    https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mass_Effect_3
18. **Late flips feel like punishment:** Dishonored's last levels could overturn
    the whole game's chaos.
    - https://dishonored.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos
    - Unwinnable, 12 Nov 2021,
      https://unwinnable.com/2021/11/12/dishonoreds-chaos-system-was-never-punishing-you/

## What it says for LEDGER's third attempt (inference)

- **One source of truth.** Professionals mostly neither move the sign nor the
  threshold alone. Each ending factor becomes a few named states, and the
  ending's rule and every sign read that one state. Where players already trust
  a sign, the rule is moved onto the sign.
- **Police:** named states (nothing, asking round, investigating, manhunt), and
  a killing with a sure witness passes through a visible "investigating" before
  a manhunt.
- **The friend:** "calls him by name" belongs to a warmer-but-not-loyal state.
  Either give the loyal state its own sign (an unasked act of help), or move the
  ending's threshold onto the name.
- **A warning window** before an ending shuts; judge on state that has held,
  not a snapshot.
- **Tom's reading** says "quiet" only when every factor's state is quiet, and
  its reasons are the signs those states gave him.
- **Checks:**
  - a soak test that fails if any change of state emits no sign;
  - the AI tester asked at checkpoints "how does this week end, and why?",
    compared with the ending the state predicts.
