## Finding

- The largest cause by far: every handover today carried the hourly row's time as "**Next due:**". The gate (advisor 160, 14 Nov) reads that line as a stated quiet spell and holds any start after a clean stop until that time, up to 55 minutes. Six day gaps, about 196 day minutes of 240, came from it, and the 19:51 stop's 25 minutes too. The gate fix of 12 Nov (18 s quiet after a Stopped line) worked: no start was held by the quiet seconds today.
- Second: two sessions ended their turn without a Stopped line (07:52, 11:54), and the gate rightly waits 30 quiet minutes before taking them as stopped: 62 day minutes.
- A helper still running after Stopped did not hold a start today on its own (the helper's turns would hold for 18 s only after its last one; the 20:06 hold was the quiet spell).
- Night: 150 night minutes were stated quiet spells ("work next due 07:00"), against the round-the-clock ruling (9 Nov) in a doubled week.

## Fix (16 Nov 20:28)

- tools/fresh_gate.py: a Next due line holds only when it says "quiet" on the line (a spell the session meant); any other Next due, or a "**Row due:**" line, never holds. Self-tests 70 pass, two new cases. Checked on today's real handover (git 7e2198b04) at 20:06: the old answer WAIT, the new one GO.
- The handover's row time now lives on "**Row due:**". A quiet spell is written only when nothing in the order or its waiting list can be worked, which in the doubled week is never.
- Not changed: the 30-minute rule for a session ending without Stopped (shorter risks two sessions at once while one thinks); the habit stays "Stopped is the last thing written".

Expected: day idle from 240 to under 70 minutes on a day like this one; measured on 17 and 18 Nov with tools/idle_gaps.py.
