# BRIEF: which conversation model, doing exactly what job

STATUS: BRIEF. Written 2026-09-19, before any research began, per the standing
instruction that a topic's brief is a file in the topic's folder, in Jafar's
words.

This is gap 3 of `production/research/brief-coverage/SUMMARY.md`.

## Why this topic exists

From the commission:

> The pillar says the model classifies and picks from closed sets and never
> decides an outcome, so it can be small. The only measured evidence this lane
> has found points the other way: the RECHECK on arXiv 2511.10277 found a 1.1B
> model at 55 percent factual accuracy and a 130MB model its own authors do not
> recommend for dialogue.

## The question, in his words

> The question is capability, not size or price, which are covered.

> - What is the smallest model that reliably does closed-set classification
>   while holding a fixed persona, on measured evidence rather than vendor
>   claims.
> - What benchmarks bear on that specifically, since general benchmarks do not.
> - Has anyone shipped a game with a local model doing this job, and what did
>   they find.
> - And what does reliability mean here as a number a build could be held to.

## The decision that waits on it

> A decision waits on this: the hardware floor cannot be written until the
> model's size is known, and its size cannot be chosen until its job is proven.
