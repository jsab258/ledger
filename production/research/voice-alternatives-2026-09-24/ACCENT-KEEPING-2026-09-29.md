# Keeping a British regional accent in free voice cloning: research note, 29 September 2026

**The problem.** The game speaks live replies by cloning each character's voice from a 10 to 12 s clip, on this PC beside the game (processor preferred; the RX 6700 draws the game), with free, commercially usable licences only (Jafar, 29 September: no paid voice service). The game's engine, Chatterbox Nano, holds Ron's northern English and Darren's Scottish but takes about 4 s to the first sound; Sheila's northern English leans American. Pocket drifted American; VoxCPM2 lost Darren's Scottish. Sopro V2 Turbo (first sound 0.8 to 1.4 s on the processor), after two attempts, holds Ron's accent on 15 of 16 lines (temperature 0.5, top-k 15, his approved in-game line added to the reference) but Darren's Scottish on 1 of 4 and Sheila's English on 2 of 4. Two failed attempts, so researched (the two-tries rule) by a separate helper given the problem, about 30 minutes; saved by the builder. "Inference" marks the helper's own reasoning. Sources accessed 29 September 2026 unless dated.

## Findings

- **No free, commercially usable model has published evidence of holding a northern English or Scottish accent from a short reference.** The only method with measured accent gains is fine-tuning.
- **Sopro V2 Turbo** (Apache-2.0, code and weights): trained on Emilia-YODAS, LibriTTS-R and FalAR; conditions on the reference's semantic and style tokens, no transcript; the authors do not plan to release training code, so it cannot be fine-tuned. Inference: training data heavy in American speech explains the pull. [github.com/samuel-vitorino/sopro; huggingface.co/samuel-vitorino/sopro-v2-turbo; research.haloneuro.ai/posts/sopro-v2, 27 Aug 2026]
- **NeuTTS Air** (Apache-2.0, 748M, GGUF for the processor): clones from 3 to 15 s plus its transcript; has a fine-tuning script; about 119 tokens/s on a Ryzen 9 (third party). No accent evidence; depends on the espeak phonemiser (GPL), British or American phonemes unverified. NeuTTS-Nano's own licence not checked. [github.com/neuphonic/neutts; marktechpost, 2 Oct 2025]
- **Chatterbox** (MIT): a Singapore-English study found zero-shot cloning keeps timbre but flattens accent; fine-tuning its T3 module raised accent similarity 0.13 on trained speakers and 0.09 on unseen ones, using 55 h and 100 epochs, word error 11.5% to 15.4%. [arXiv 2607.23027, 25 Jul 2026]
- **Qwen3-TTS** (Apache-2.0): a user report says a cloned British accent reverted to American and accent prompts are ignored; slow on a processor. [ocdevel.com, 2 Mar 2026]
- **Piper's en_GB voices** (alba, northern_english_male, vctk): fast, but fine-tuned from "lessac", whose Blizzard licence is research-only: not commercially clean. [huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices; github.com/rhasspy/piper/discussions/271, Nov 2023]
- Excluded on licence: Voxtral TTS (CC BY-NC 4.0, 26 Mar 2026), DEFINE (CC BY-NC-ND; arXiv 2609.32777, 26 Sep 2026; accent accuracy 0.065 to 0.196 over zero-shot F5-TTS: the method works, the licence forbids it).
- **Training here:** AMD's Windows PyTorch supports RX 7000/9000, not the RX 6700 (rocm.docs.amd.com Windows matrix). Inference: days on the processor, Linux with an override, or a cloud GPU; each a scope decision for Jafar. A Chatterbox fine-tuning kit (Apache-2.0) asks for at least 30 minutes of audio, LoRA below 10 h. [github.com/gokhaneraslan/chatterbox-finetuning]
- **After-the-fact conversion:** no commercially licensed accent converter found; timbre converters (OpenVoice v2, MIT since Apr 2024) keep the source's accent. kNN-VC swaps in the target's own sound frames and might restore some vowels (unproven).
- **The check itself:** calibrated on northern English women (it still flags 3 of 50 genuine clips, ACCENT-WINDOWS-2026-09-29.md) but never on genuine Scottish speech, so Darren's 1 of 4 is too small and unvalidated a sample to judge.
- **Datasets:** VCTK (CC BY 4.0: 14 Scottish men, 5 Scottish women, 7 northern English women, about 20 to 25 minutes each, estimated); OpenSLR 83 (CC BY-SA 4.0: Scottish male about 1.8 h, northern female about 0.9 h; ShareAlike may carry to fine-tuned weights, a licence question); GLOBE (CC0, from Common Voice, accent-labelled, 535 h); EdAcc (CC BY-SA 4.0, 40 h); CMU ARCTIC awb (a named researcher: training data at most, never a character voice).
