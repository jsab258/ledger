## 2. The first-use stall (measured, named)

- In every walk, about 5.4 s in, the game switches Mickey's office's lamp and fittings shadows on as the camera comes within 7 m (VignetteShot.cpp TickRoomShadows). By day no other local light casts shadows on the street, so that is the first local-light shadow of the walk.
- With the driver's cache cleared (-clearPSODriverCache, as a player's first run), the walk logs 15 pipelines precaching never requested (PSOPrecacheState: Missed): 2 at the capture's first frames (every run, the same two), 1 at frame 396 and 12 at frame 397; frame 398 takes **379 ms**. With a warm driver cache the same moment took 66 to 87 ms.
- -logpsodetails describes them: the one at 396 draws to the shadow mask's colour format with no depth test and a full-screen vertex layout; nine of the twelve share one full-screen vertex shader. They are the engine's global passes that run once a local light's shadow appears, which precaching covers only for a few kinds (Slate, deferred lights, Cascade, volumetric fog). Epic's remedy is a bundled pipeline cache, compiled before play.
- The synchronous load just before (FlushAsyncLoading, 1 package) costs 0.3 ms: not the stall.
- Three other slow frames (44, 37 and 43 ms) in the cold walk have no pipeline miss: not yet explained.
- One night capture (run b) had 36 slow frames spread through it with none of the game's threads, nor the card, accounting for them (88.6 ms frames with the card at 11.6 ms): the game was kept waiting from outside. Not reproduced in run a.

## The bundled cache, first half (17:00 to 17:50)
- Package 1 (a212715, stable keys on) landed at 17:01; the build machine kept four stable-key files (the project's and the engine's, SM5 and SM6).
- Recorded with -logPSO on that package: day standing, night standing, day walking, night walking; 189 to 213 pipelines each, 238 in all.
- Expanded against the keys (ShaderPipelineCacheTools expand): 8,477 stable entries, 208 KB, F:\LedgerTools\game-inputs\pipeline-caches\Ledger_LedgerProbe_PCD3D_SM6.spc; 0 errors.
- The measure's no-voice option first failed: PowerShell's names ignore case, so the voice process's variable overwrote the -Voice option (also the cause of the old "null Id" errors); renamed.

- Package 2 (15a0b0c) failed in its cook (18:11): the cook's cache build (ShaderPipelineCacheTools build, run by the cook with this platform's SM6 stable keys) stopped on the engine's check that every key in the bundled cache is for the cook's own platform (PipelineCacheUtilities.cpp line 756): the cache had been expanded against the SM5 keys too. Expanded again against the SM6 keys alone (19:57): the same 238 pipelines, 8,477 entries, 146 KB. The waiting loop sat out its full two hours on a stamp that never came: lost about 1.5 hours.

## The bundled cache, measured (package 3, ed3244b, 20:40; 21:00 to 21:10)
- The cook bundled 238 pipelines (234 graphics, 4 compute). In every capture after it: **no pipeline missed**.
- Walking with the driver's cache cleared, the voice on the processor: worst frames 38.2, 30.5 and 27.0 ms (were 87 warm, 380 cold); the capture waited 72 to 78 s for the cache to compile first (a player waits at the title on a first launch). The 38.2 ms frame had every game thread short (card 15.9, game 3.7, render 9.9 ms): an outside wait. Walking with no voice beside the game: worst 24.4 ms. So the remaining over-budget frame comes with the voice's processor load.
- Standing: by day worst 26.0, at night 25.8 ms.
- **But the night's spare time fell to 0.10 ms** on this package (today's shops are in it): shadow drawing 4.98 ms against 3.27 that morning. Test on the packaged copy's own data, the shop window lamps' shadows off: 0.70 ms, spare 2.61. The window lamps cast shadows from every caster within 9 m of them.
- **Fix:** the window lamps cast from the shop's own pieces only, as the rooms' lights already do: the displays, the fronts and the people marked "cinematic" casters (VignetteShot.cpp SpawnShopSpill). In the editor, the same build with the old rule (-RoomShadowsAll) beside the new: the 13 night views differ by under one level on average; what differs is passers-by, wet glints, and a dark band at the chandler's stallriser foot that appears in one run and not the next with either rule (an instability of its own, listed).
- Card memory on this package rose: the game alone a median of 4.9 to 5.1 GB, peaks 6.3 to 6.8 GB walking; the card in all peaks 7.4 to 8.7 GB, with the game's own cast voice server holding about 2.3 GB of the card in the walk with no outside voice (8.26 in all against the game's 5.91).
