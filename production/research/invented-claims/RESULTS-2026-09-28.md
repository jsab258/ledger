# Invented details in live talk: what was measured, 28 September

Town list item 3. The research note beside this one (SUMMARY-2026-09-28.md) says what the literature offered; this says what was built and what it did, on our own test conversations.

## The test set (ledger/ClaimBench, data in bench/)

- 3 characters (Ron, Sheila, Sam) × 8 memory sets (from nothing seen to a clear sighting) × 14 player lines and 2 three-turn chains that push for details: 480 replies from the live conversation model (Sonnet 5) with the live prompt.
- Each reply labelled detail by detail by two models (Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5) under one rule: a detail is invented unless a memory or the card states or directly implies it; the player's words are never evidence. Where they disagreed, a third reading settled it. Gold: 227 of 480 replies invented something.
- Half the memory sets were used to tune the check (tune), half held back to measure it (held: 108 invented replies, 132 clean). End to end, the held-back half's 240 turns were replayed through the whole turn as the game runs it, and what the player would hear was labelled again.

## The check on its own (held-back half)

| check | invented replies caught | honest replies flagged | median, slowest tenth |
|---|---|---|---|
| before: one call asking what is invented (v2) | 36/108 (33%) | 4/132 (3%) | 0.65 s |
| every specific listed with the item that supports it, then a second look at what was flagged (v3v), first version | 93/108 (86%) | 41/132 (31%) | 2.3 s |
| the same after the independent check's fixes, second look shown the line | 75/108 (69%) | 27/132 (20%) | 2.4 s, 3.5 s |
| one second look per detail, shown the line | 73/108 (68%) | 27/132 (20%) | 2.3 s, 3.2 s |
| one second look per detail, not shown the line | 94/108 (87%, 95% 79-92%) | 36/132 (27%) | 2.2 s, 3.3 s |
| **the same after the third pass (live)** | **97/108 (90%, 95% 83-94%)** | **40/132 (30%)** | 2.2 s, 3.3 s |

Shown the line, the second look cited the line itself as its source ("source": "LINE") and cleared a whole invented description; it now has to name a real item, and it is not shown the line.

## End to end: what the player hears (held-back half, 240 turns)

| run | turns with an invented detail | fallback line | whole turn, median / slowest tenth | first sentence heard, median / slowest tenth |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| before (old prompt, old check) | 73/240, 30% (25-37%) | 17 | 2.2 s | not measured |
| new prompt and cards, old check | 64/240, 27% (21-33%) | 3 | 2.1 s / 4.0 s | |
| new prompt, the list alone | 20/240, 8% (5-13%) | 50 | 4.2 s / 9.9 s | |
| new prompt, first v3v | 27/240, 11% (8-16%) | 35 | 3.7 s / 9.3 s | |
| new prompt, old check, first sentence spoken early | 57/240, 24% (19-30%) | 2 | 2.5 s / 4.9 s | 1.7 s / 4.1 s (8 of 240 held back) |
| fixed v3v, first sentence spoken early | 13/240, 5% (3-9%) | 17 | 4.1 s / 8.3 s | 1.9 s / 7.9 s (34 of 240 held back) |
| **live: the same, a failed first sentence stopping its draft and the second draft streamed at once (town list 6a)** | **16/240, 7% (4-11%)** | **4** | 4.2 s / 6.7 s | **1.9 s / 4.8 s** (9 of 240 held back) |

- The prompt and card changes alone did little (30% to 27%); the check does the work.
- The first sentence is checked on its own and spoken as soon as it passes. Against the old check, the new one costs 0.2 s typically (1.9 s against 1.7 s) and about 4 s in the slowest tenth (7.9 s against 4.1 s): when the first sentence fails (34 of 240, against 8), the player waits for the whole turn, and those turns reach the helper's 8-second patience and end in a brush-off. That delay was town list item 6a: now a first sentence that fails stops its draft there, and the second draft is asked for at once and streamed the same way, its own first sentence spoken as soon as it passes. The slowest tenth then hear their first word at 4.8 s, the whole turn ends inside the helper's 8 s at 6.7 s, and the fallback line ends 4 turns in 240 instead of 17. The invented rate, 7% against 5%, is within the noise of 240 turns.
- The live run was made just before the independent check's third pass, whose changes are small (which loose kinds count as claims; waiting for every second look on a cancelled turn) and before the fallback line got its other wordings.

## Three rounds of the independent check

1. Pass with fixes, ten faults: the worst, a flagged line said word for word when the second look failed. All fixed with a regression each.
2. Pass with fixes, five more (a cut-off answer read as clean; names under loose kinds; the scene as proof; numbering; two answers in one). Fixed; the bench then showed the second look too lenient when shown the line, and the Core now decides what counts as its source.
3. Pass with fixes: sentence-case words taken for names; now and weather letting names through; unwatched second looks on cancellation. Fixed.

## Known limits (in FINDINGS)

- A detail about another event can be cleared by an item about a different one (a fish van this morning for a van last night), since the second look is not shown the line.
- A denial or a guess is never read further ("Dennis never came back after he put the window in"), and the name test is a word list: a name in lower case, or "the fuzz", gets through.
- A detail sourced only to the card or the time now is cleared by the second look's instructions alone.
- 30% of honest replies are flagged: most are redrafted into something close, and the fallback line ends 17 of 240 turns (7%), as before.

## Cost

About $40 of model calls for the whole bench (generating, two labellers, every check and run). The live check costs one Haiku call per line, plus one per flagged detail.

## Tried later the same evening, not kept (town list 6m)

Loosening the check for habits, a new "general" kind (how the town usually goes, and numbers that are only a way of speaking) and clearer denials: on the held-back half, honest replies flagged fell from 30% to 21%, but invented replies caught fell from 90% to 85%. Catching inventions is the item's point, so it was reverted. Tuning precision further first needs the test replies written again with the current cards: some of the tuning half's false alarms are details from the three cards' old sample lines (the crate by the yard door, checked), which the cards no longer carry. The final check on the tuning half: 115 of 119 invented replies caught (97%), 39 of 121 honest ones flagged (32%).
