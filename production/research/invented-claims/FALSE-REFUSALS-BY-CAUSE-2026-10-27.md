## What a change could fix, and what it would risk

- **f and h (19), the list's wording, low risk:** write each detail whole, naming who and what, never "it", "he" or "the speaker" for the person addressed; "there is June" among family is not "June is here". The look judges the same claim, put correctly.
- **e (27), the name-and-number rule, some risk:** a place name or the speaker's own price should not make an opinion, denial, weather or manner a claim; keep the rule for anything telling of another time (6bf; TellsOfThen). Measure on the invented half first.
- **b (23), the look's examples, real risk:** "at the office" gives "in the office". The 27 October switch let "his flat over the office" through by such loosening; an example must say another room is no paraphrase.
- **i (5), a code rule:** let the look clear a timeless "who somebody is" from a people line. Risk: "Ron minding the door" said of that night (6ad).
- **c (7) and d (3), leave:** forbidden on purpose; loosening lets inventions in.
- **Labels' fault:** none sure; five unsure lean a. At most 5, plus the 10 c and d where the labels' standard is looser than the check's.

Not measured: with f, h, e and i fixed and half of b, about 62 of 96 would pass (31% refused to about 11%).
