# The first sentence's check: a time-boxed research step (proposal, 7 October 2026)

Jafar, 7 October, on the voice page: "Report it failed, as a blocked core capability. The measurements show the check on the first sentence, not the voice, sets the floor. Propose a time-boxed research step on that: starting to speak while the check runs, or a lighter check, or choosing the facts before writing so less needs checking. No third attempt at the voice itself."

## What is known (measured 7 October on the real path, CLOUD-VOICE-2026-10-07.md)

- The first sentence is written 0.75 s (faster model) or 1.35 s (one model) after Enter; its check clears it 1.0 to 1.9 s later. With a voice taking about 0.2 s, the first sound would come at about 2.4 s, the check setting it.
- The check stopped a first sentence in 1 of 5 replies today and 4 of 23 on the 29 September bench (RESULTS-2026-09-29-firsts.md); each stop costs a second draft, 5 to 8 s.
- **The limit, plainly:** today's free voice takes 1.6 to 4.4 s to make the first sound. Even an instant check leaves that, so with no third attempt at the voice, no outcome of this step brings speech under two seconds in the game as it is. What the step would show is what the check can give up, for a faster voice later.

## The step

- **Box:** one working day and at most $1 of measuring (the dollar a day), then stop and report, whatever the numbers.
- **The same lines each way:** 10 newcomer questions to Sheila, Ron and Darren from the 29 September bench, through the real engine and check (ClaimBench), each direction against today's way.
- **Measured per direction:** time from the first sentence written to "cleared to speak"; how often something the check would stop is heard, and how much of it; the whole reply's quality, read blind by a fresh reviewer.

| direction | what changes | what it could give | what it risks |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Speak while the check runs | the first sentence plays as soon as written; a failed check cuts it at the next word and the turn redrafts | the whole check, 1.0 to 1.9 s | the player hears the start of an invented claim, cut off (1 in 5 today) |
| 2. A lighter check | the first sentence checked against only the facts it names, in one short call | about half the check (estimate) | more invented details let through |
| 3. The facts chosen before writing | the plan-first mode (built 30 September, off, never measured on the real path): the writer names its facts, the check confirms only those | unknown; the plan may slow the first sentence | quality; the 30 September bench did not finish measuring it |

**Out:** one screen: each direction's time to "cleared to speak", its safety count and its quality, against today's.
