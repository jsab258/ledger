His order of 21 October, item 6: "Apply research note 1d (production/cloud-week/research/1d-shop-glass-without-frame-cost.md): the packaged build's worst frame at or under 33.3 ms on the walk, measured; then P1 closes with a fresh review." P1 was measured packaged on 7 October (P1-PACKAGED-2026-10-07.md) and regressed on 8 October when the shop glass began catching the street with Lumen at 1024 (p99 46.8 ms in the build machine's slice).

## How (as 7 October)

The played copy, started through its launcher (tools/p1-packaged.ps1): the hook camera, 1800 frames each standing by day, standing at night and walking up the street at 1.4 m/s, 3440 x 1440 drawn at 55%, uncapped, off screen; the cast voice on the card speaking throughout (tools/voice-live/voice_load.py); Windows' own memory counters every two seconds. RX 6700, Ryzen 5 5600X, 32 GB. Read by tools/perf-hook.py, which now also reports the worst frame and the frames over 33.3 ms. Every line is in p1-packaged/runs.jsonl; the frames in F:\LedgerTools\tmp\p1-packaged.

## Three causes, each measured to its source

| | what the slow frame was | how it was found | the fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | the shop windows re-photographing the street with Lumen, at load and at each change of light | note 1d; the build machine's slice: worst 211.8 ms, 13 frames over | each window's street baked once by day and once at night (tools/glass_bake.py), imported as compressed cubes with a stamp (tools/ue/import_glass_bake.py), looked up and swapped in play (VignetteShot.cpp, route A of note 1d) |
| 2 | the hour's autosave at 10:00: 51 ms of game thread, 49.9 of it its files' writes flushed through to disk | the game's own CSV timers (Ledger/Autosave, Ledger/SaveFileWrites); the log's clock line at the same second | the hour's save writes its files on one background task; its text is still made at one moment on the game thread; the next save, a load and the exit wait for it (CrimeProbe.cpp) |
| 3 | two frames of 38 to 45 ms, always where the walk comes within 7 m of Mickey's office | an Unreal Insights trace of the packaged walk, exported headless: the renderer waiting 30 ms for the card's occlusion query results (GPUBound_WaitingForGPUForOcclusionQueries) | r.NumBufferedOcclusionQueries=3 (DefaultEngine.ini): a thing coming into view can appear up to two frames late |

Rejected on the way: the office room's shadow switching at 7 m (-RoomShadowsAll gave the same spikes).

## Added by the fresh review (21 October, 22:30: CLOSE)

- The voice throughout, 00f81b1: 169 lines, 689 s of speech made in 661 s (0.96 of real time), on the card.
- The margin is thin: with 3 buffered queries the worst walk was 32.75 ms, 0.55 under the bar; with 2, one walk reached 35.7. New street content can break it.
- The 10:00 hour still makes a slow frame standing: 34.9 ms by day, and at night it is the run's worst frame, 32.98. The game thread takes 16 to 21 ms on the frame after the save, and at night the game's own timers name only about 1.5 ms of it (save 0.97, consequence 0.51): most of the hour's cost has no named cause yet.
- The occlusion setting costs card memory: on b414e8c's walk the game's median went 4274, about 4440, about 4568 MB for 1, 2, 3 buffered queries; the walk's peak with the voice 9056, 9150, 9327 (9422 on 00f81b1), about 80 MB under note 1d's 9.5 GB line.
- Parts of note 1d not done: box projection and mips by roughness (the glass material is untouched, one ReflectCube); the camera panes not supersampled (the bake ran at 1280 x 720 in the editor's game; the stair-steps in the glass remain); no room-side cube for Mickey's office; the forced change of light (step 2) not measured; no same-build comparison with -GlassCatchLive or with the cubes off.
- A stale bake does not fail the build: the game quietly catches live again, and the build machine still wrote MEETS on 8a0c2b2 (worst 211.8 ms, 13 frames over). Owed: any glass bake status but OK fails the build, and the slice's verdict counts the worst frame and the frames over 33.3. The stamp covers four JSON files; a kit front, display or held prop changed on F: alone would not mark the bake stale.
- "Slowest 1%" here is tools/perf-hook.py's mean of the slowest 18 frames, not a percentile; the true 99th percentiles are 23.4 to 24.0 ms, under the plan's 25.
- Measured off screen on the Development package with the frame profiler on, as on 7 October; not on screen, not Shipping. No night walk was measured. The final package has not yet been walked by the AI tester (the two-frame delay could show as things appearing late at Mickey's door).
- The walk's median rose 0.4 to 0.5 ms from the live-catch build (13.99) to the baked ones (14.38 to 14.76), the GPU's by 0.1; the note's "within 0.3 ms of the baseline" was not checked.
