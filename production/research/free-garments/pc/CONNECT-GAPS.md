# CONNECT gaps: the free items earlier searches named but never opened (2 October 2026)

Done in the browser on Jafar's PC on 2 October 2026. It took about 65 minutes; the cap was 50. Everything below was seen that day. Nothing was downloaded, added to a cart, bought or signed into. No terms or cookie banner were accepted.

## How it was done

- Each item was found with the store's own search box, with the Free filter on (the address gets `pricingOptions=1`). No search was scripted.
- Every item page was opened. Its text was read: maker, dates, formats, triangles, licence lines.
- Its pictures were the page's own gallery pictures, laid side by side in my own tab. The 3D viewer was not used.
- CONNECT Official items have two pictures: a large front view, and a strip of four small views (front, three-quarter, side, back). So their backs and sides were seen small.
- The bar was read first: the Hook sheet and the two KCD2 frames. For a white block pattern the grade is for the drafting. The cloth, wear and weight are ours in every case.

## Does anything beat the best so far?

- **M5, the car coat: the MV2 Mac Coat (Nov 2024) may beat the MV2 Car Coat.** It has no chest yoke and a set-in sleeve, the two things the Car Coat's note said to remove. Keep the Car Coat as the donkey-jacket base, where its yoke helps. Check the Mac Coat on the body first.
- **M5, a third coat: the MV2 Classic Raincoat.** It is the man's mac, which neither the Chesterfield nor the Car Coat covers. It adds; it does not replace.
- **M3: page.fang's "business suit" ties with the MV2 Easy Tailored Jacket on cut.** It is finished in cloth and drapes well. But it is heavy (359k triangles) and its pattern pieces were not seen. A second reference, not a replacement.
- **W1: PLAN.B's "Oversize Wool Coat" is the best-looking finished wool coat seen.** But it is a belted wrap, not double-breasted, and heavy (928k, with a dress). A rival for a second coat, not a replacement for the FV2 Double-Breasted Coat.
- **Nothing beats the best for M1, M2, M4, M6, W2, W3 or W4.**
