# Free garments for the four women's silhouettes, checked from Jafar's PC (2 October 2026)

W1 wool coat. W2 belted mac. W3 quilted country jacket. W4 skirt-suit jacket of 1990.

Done in the browser pane on Jafar's PC, about 60 minutes, on 2 October 2026. Nothing was signed in to, downloaded, bought or accepted. Optional cookies were declined on CONNECT.

How I judged: against the Hook sheet (ordinary 1990 people in a wet street, dark wool coats and jackets) and the two KCD2 frames (clothes with real weight, fabric, seams and wear). No free item below reaches that bar as it stands. The best ones are professional patterns: plain white blocks with no fabric, no wear and no finishing. Their cut and collars are right. The fabric, the 1990 shoulder pads and the finishing would be ours.

A limit on what I saw: the browser pane was hidden much of the time, so the 3D viewers never drew. I judged every CONNECT item from CONNECT's own rendered pictures (front, three-quarter, side and back), and every Sketchfab and Fab item from its gallery pictures. CONNECT shows no pattern pieces before download, so I saw none.

## The answer in short

- **CLO's CONNECT has a professional free pattern for every one of the four.** The best are CONNECT Official's own blocks:
  - W1: the FV2 Double-Breasted Coat;
  - W2: the FV2 Trench Coat;
  - W3: the FV2 Quilted Jacket (February 2024);
  - W4: the FV2 Power Shoulder Jacket.
- **Their licence is on the allowlist.** It is CLO's free CONNECT licence. I read its terms in full (below): commercial use inside a sold game is allowed, and it is revocable only for misuse.
- **Everything else is weaker.** Sketchfab and Fab have free CC-BY or Fab Standard meshes, but they are modern cuts. CGTrader's free item carries the no-AI licence, which is not allowed.
- **Downloads need an account everywhere.** CONNECT needs a CLO-SET account, Sketchfab an account (seen), and Fab an Epic account. None was tested.

## Best per garment, ranked

**W1 Wool coat**
1. **FV2 Double-Breasted Coat (Feb 2024).** The six-button double-breasted wool coat of a 1990 street, at the right length. The notch lapel and pockets are drawn by a pattern-maker. We add shoulder pads, wool and wear.
2. **FV2 Single-Breasted Coat (Nov 2024).** The same family, single-breasted, for a second woman.
3. **FV2 Balmacaan Coat.** The raglan alternative. It also serves W2.

**W2 Belted mac**
1. **FV2 Trench Coat (Nov 2024).** Every trench detail is there: gun flap, epaulettes, belt, cuff straps, back yoke. It needs lengthening and gabardine, and nothing else structural.
2. **mianmian's Women's trench coat (Fab, Standard License).** Already textured and belted at mid-calf. But it is a mesh with no pattern, at 380,000 triangles, and its rounded 2020s shoulders are wrong for 1990.
3. **FV2 Balmacaan Coat with a belt added.** The plain 1990 raincoat.

**W3 Quilted jacket**
1. **FV2 Quilted Jacket (Feb 2024).** It is the country quilted jacket already: diamond quilting, turn-down collar, snaps, patch pockets, bound edges. We add a cord collar, the puff of the quilting, and olive or navy nylon. Nothing else found comes close.
2. **FV2 Liner Jacket.** A quilted base only.
3. None other is worth naming.

**W4 Skirt-suit jacket**
1. **FV2 Power Shoulder Jacket.** The only item whose shoulder is already the 1990 power shoulder. A professional notch lapel and princess seams.
2. **FV2 Double-Breasted Jacket (Nov 2024).** The double-breasted 1990 suit jacket, broad-shouldered.
3. **FV2 Peaked Lapel Tailored Jacket.** A peak-lapel variant for an office woman.

Against the bar: all of these are plain white blocks fitted to CLO's FV2 woman. They will not look like KCD2 until they have real cloth, interfacing, weight and wear. That is the finishing step that has failed before. What they settle is the cut and the lapel: both are professionally drafted, and both can be judged in the Marvelous trial before anything ships. Every KOFOTI item auto-grades to other avatars ("AUTO GRADING O"). That they re-drape cleanly on our MetaHuman bodies is untested.
