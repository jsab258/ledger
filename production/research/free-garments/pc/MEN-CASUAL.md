# Free garments for M1, M2 and M6, checked from Jafar's PC (2 October 2026)

**The question.** Are there free garments for the donkey jacket (M1), the anorak (M2) and the shell-suit top or Harrington blouson (M6)? We would re-drape them in Marvelous Designer on our own bodies, cut them to 1990, then recolour and finish them.

**How I looked.** I used one browser tab for about an hour on 2 October 2026. I read every licence on the item's own page or in its own listing data. I judged the pictures against the Hook sheet and the two KCD2 frames.

The Hook sheet shows ordinary people of 1990 in dark wool coats and jackets, worn and heavy, seen at street distance. The KCD2 frames set the finish: cloth with weight, wear and believable folds.

**Marks:**
- **Closeness:** 3 = the silhouette itself; 2 = the right garment; 1 = a base only.
- **Quality:** A = professional; B = decent; C = crude.

## The answer in short

1. **CLO's CONNECT store has the answer for all three garments, free and on the allowlist.** CONNECT Official's free men's patterns (the "MV2" set, many made with KOFOTI) include:
   - a real pull-over cagoule (MV2 Anorak);
   - a Harrington-type blouson with the two-button stand collar (MV2 Split-Sleeve Blouson);
   - shell-suit-type track tops (MV2 Track Jacket, MV2 Oversized Zip-Up Jacket);
   - a car coat whose front yoke, collar and five-button front make the nearest donkey-jacket base (MV2 Car Coat).
2. **They are blocks, not finished garments.** Each is a complete garment with its collar, pockets, cuffs and hems. But it is shown in plain white cloth with no wear and no stitching, and most are cut in today's oversized fit.
   - As patterns they are professional work.
   - As they stand they are not near the KCD2 bar. The cloth, the weight, the wear and the 1990 fit are ours to add.
3. **Every page carries the same licence statement.** The KOFOTI items say: "This pattern collection is provided under a free commercial license by the Korea Federation of Textile Industries (KOFOTI)". They add: "We recommend you to give attribution with hashtags below: #KOFOTI #Connectofficial".
   - Over this sits CONNECT's "Free License" (Additional Terms, last updated 7 August 2025), quoted below. It is the licence on the allowlist ("free for commercial use", revocable).
   - The terms allow the item in a game as part of the project. Players must not be able to pull it out as a separate file.
4. **Downloading needs a CLO-SET account.** Each page shows "Add to cart", and the site shows "Sign In". The terms speak of downloading "off your account page". I did not sign in or add anything to a cart.
5. **The other sites add little.**
   - Sketchfab's free garments for these three are student or hobby pieces, grade C.
   - Fab has one allowed, MetaHuman-resizing jacket that could be a donkey-jacket base (polycornStudio's "Survivor Outfit"), and a chrome "shell suit" fashion piece under a licence that needs a ruling.
   - BlenderKit has one track suit under its own "royalty_free" licence, which needs a ruling.
   - CGTrader's and ArtStation's searches did not work from here (see Unreached).

## Against the bar

None of these reaches the KCD2 finish as downloaded. The CONNECT items are clean professional blocks: real collars, cuffs, plackets and pockets, auto-graded to CLO's MV2 avatar, with thin meshes of about 16,000 to 37,000 triangles. But they are white, unworn, unstitched, and mostly cut oversized in today's fashion.

What they give us is the hard part we have failed at: a correct pattern. Our work on top is:
- the 1990 fit, which is narrower and shorter;
- the cloth: melton, proofed nylon, shiny nylon, cotton-poly;
- the donkey jacket's yoke panel and the Harrington's lining;
- wear and colour.

Nothing on Sketchfab or Fab for these three is better than grade C or B, and none is a pattern.
