# Free garments in the other stores, checked from Jafar's PC (2 October 2026)

**The question.** Do ArtStation, CGTrader, Gumroad, itch.io, BlenderKit or archive.org have free garments that beat the free CLO patterns from CONNECT? Those are the bests so far: grade B, plain white blocks. We need ten silhouettes: M1 donkey jacket, M2 anorak, M3 suit jacket of 1990, M4 sports jacket, M5 overcoat or car coat, M6 shell-suit top or Harrington, W1 wool coat, W2 belted mac, W3 quilted country jacket, W4 skirt-suit jacket with shoulder pads.

**How I looked.** I worked for about 35 minutes on 2 October 2026, in two browser tabs of my own. I did not sign in, download, accept anything or buy anything. I left the cookie banners alone and agreed to nothing.

The browser pane was hidden, so screenshots failed. To judge pictures, I fetched each store's own preview images and looked at them. **I opened no 3D viewers.** I read each licence on the item's own page.

I judged every picture against the Hook sheet and the two KCD2 frames:
- The Hook sheet shows heavy, dark, worn wool coats seen at street distance.
- The KCD2 frames set the finish: cloth with weight, wear and believable folds.

**Marks:**
- **Closeness:** 3 = the 1990 silhouette itself; 2 = the right garment; 1 = a base only; – = none of the ten.
- **Quality:** A = professional; B = decent; C = crude.

## The answer in short

1. **Nothing I found beats the CONNECT patterns.** No free item here is both on the allowlist and closer to a 1990 silhouette.
2. **Three items could rival CONNECT. All three need a ruling first.**
   - **ArtStation "women's coat" (W1).** A houndstooth, single-breasted coat that comes below the knee, made in CLO. It is the best shape and finish I saw. But the free version lists only unnamed files ("17.zprj, 2.fbx, 2.obj"), so it is unproven that the free part is the coat.
   - **BlenderKit "Raincoat" (M2 family).** A yellow, hooded fisherman's raincoat with snaps.
   - **BlenderKit "Sportswear Track Suit (Male)" (M6 family).** A white and blue pullover top made in Marvelous Designer. It carries a Nike logo, which would have to go.
3. **Every free CGTrader garment I opened says "Royalty Free License (no AI)". That is NOT ALLOWED.**
   - I opened 48 item pages, and all 48 said this.
   - CGTrader's own licence filter found 0 "editorial" items among its 650 free jacket items.
4. **ArtStation's free items use ArtStation's own licences. Both need a ruling.**
   - The "Standard License" says: "For personal use and one commercial project (up to 2,000 sales or 20,000 views)". That cap is a real limit for a game.
   - The "Extended Commercial License" says: "For use on an unlimited number of commercial projects with no limits on sales or views". A few items are free under it.
   - Many "free" listings give only a sample, such as a T-shirt or a skirt, while the coats cost money.
5. **Gumroad, itch.io and BlenderKit add little.**
   - Gumroad has Jeff Studio's free "155 Clothing Archive" of white Marvelous Designer blocks, which includes long coats. It has no licence text.
   - Virtual Filmer's free MetaHuman outfit is a shirt and jeans, not a coat. Its page has no licence text.
   - itch.io had nothing relevant.
6. **archive.org has good, readable, public-domain drafting books.** Examples are W.D.F. Vincent's "Cutter's Practical Guide" volumes (1893 to 1905), Rohr (1961) and Smith (1968). The 1960s to 1990s pattern-cutting books are there too, but they can only be borrowed, which needs an account. Those were not read.
