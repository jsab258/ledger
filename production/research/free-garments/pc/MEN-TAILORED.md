# Free men's tailoring: M3 suit jacket, M4 sports jacket, M5 overcoat or car coat (from Jafar's PC, 2 October 2026)

Done in the browser on Jafar's PC, about 75 minutes, 2 October 2026. Everything below was seen on that date. Nothing was downloaded, bought or signed into.

## In short

- **CLO's own free men's patterns on CONNECT are the answer for all three garments.** CONNECT Official gives away a set of men's (MV2) patterns made with KOFOTI, the Korea Federation of Textile Industries. They include an "MV2 Easy Tailored Jacket", an "MV2 Single-Breasted Tailored Blazer", an "MV2 Sports Coat", an "MV2 Chesterfield Coat" and an "MV2 Car Coat". Each is a .zprj pattern, plus FBX and glTF meshes.
- **The cloud session never found these.** They are not in the "KOFOTI_Male_Patterns" collection it named (that holds 36 other items). They turn up only in the store's own search, filtered to free.
- **Their licence is on the allowlist.** Each page reads: "This pattern collection is provided under a free commercial license by the Korea Federation of Textile Industries (KOFOTI)". It also says: "We recommend you to give attribution with hashtags below: #KOFOTI #Connectofficial". CONNECT's own terms add that the licence is "revocable".
- **What I saw is a professional block pattern, not a finished garment.** Each picture shows a white garment on CLO's MV2 man, with its seams drawn in black. The cut, lapels, pockets and buttons are drafted. The cloth, colour, wear and trims are ours to add.
- **Sketchfab's best free mesh is Style3D CG's "Man Black Business Suit".** It has a real lapel roll and a proper drape, under CC BY 4.0. But it is 663,736 triangles, a mesh with no pattern. It is a fallback only.
- **CGTrader's free blazers and overcoats are all "Royalty Free License (no AI)", so they are not allowed.** Fab, ArtStation, Gumroad and BlenderKit had nothing free that fits.

## Marks

- **Licence class:** ALLOWED (on Jafar's allowlist), NOT ALLOWED, or RULING (needs a ruling).
- **Closeness to the 1990 garment:** 3 is the silhouette itself, 2 is the right garment, 1 is a base.
- **Quality against the bar** (the Hook sheet and the two KCD2 frames, read first): A professional, B decent, C crude.
  - For a pattern, the grade is for the drafting I could see. The finish against KCD2 (cloth, wear, weight) is ours in every case.

## How a download works on each site

- **Sketchfab: a download needs sign-in.**
  - I tested it on TopNotch Assets' "Black Suit". With no one signed in, "Download 3D Model" opened "Log in to Sketchfab". Its options were Epic Games, Facebook, Google, Twitter, Apple, company SSO or email.
  - Sketchfab's footer now reads "© 2026, KitBash SF Operations LLC".
- **CONNECT: a free item goes through "Add to cart" on a site with "Sign In" at the top. So it needs a free CLO-SET account.**
  - The terms speak of downloading the item "off your account page" (CONNECT Terms 2.3.5).
  - I did not click "Add to cart".
- **CGTrader, Fab and ArtStation:** the pages show "Free Download", "Download" or "Add to My Library", or "Add to Library". I did not test whether they need sign-in.

## CONNECT's free licence, as read

I read the CLO-SET CONNECT Terms at legal.clo-set.com/modal-tou-connect. They are "Last updated as of Aug 7, 2025".

- **2.3.3:** a Free Item comes with a "non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable, limited, revocable, and worldwide license to download, use, reproduce, display, perform, modify, and create derivative work".
- **2.3.6:** the licence is "revocable by CLO if we have reason to believe" the item was acquired or used fraudulently, against the law, against others' rights, or against the terms.
- **2.3.2:** free items may be used by the buyer, a company's employees and its contractors, "provided that ... the Item must be used for the Internal Business Needs of the User".
- **2.4.5:** a Project may be distributed "as an included dependency to end users". End users must be restricted "from extracting or otherwise using Item outside of the Project".
- **2.4.6.2 and 2.4.6.3:** items acquired under the "Free License" may be distributed in a "Digital Space". The terms define that as including an "open-world game". It must be done in no way that lets a third party extract the item "as a stand-alone file".
- **2.4.1:** the Project must show "substantial added value", and the Item must be "merely ... a component".
- **Class: ALLOWED** under Jafar's ruling on CONNECT's free licence, with the revocable clause noted.

### Best for M3, ranked

1. **MV2 Easy Tailored Jacket (CONNECT, KOFOTI).** It is the only free pattern I saw with the 1990 balance. It has two buttons, wide notch lapels with a long roll, flap pockets and an easy body. It is a professional draft, allowed, and comes as a .zprj that Marvelous opens.
2. **MV2 Single-Breasted Tailored Blazer (CONNECT, KOFOTI).** It has the same features with lapels of medium width and a trimmer body. It suits the slim build, while the Easy jacket suits the average and heavy builds.
3. **Style3D CG's "Man Black Business Suit" (Sketchfab, CC BY).** This is only a fallback reference for drape and lapel roll. It is a mesh with no pattern, at 663,736 triangles.
