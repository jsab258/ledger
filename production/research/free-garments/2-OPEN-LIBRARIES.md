# Free garment meshes in open libraries (helper 2, 2 October 2026)

Area: downloadable garment meshes under CC0, CC-BY and similar licences. Covers Sketchfab, Rocketbox, MakeHuman, BlenderKit, museum scans, Mixamo and research datasets.

Marks: **[READ]** read at the source. **[SS]** search summary only. **[I]** my inference.

The closeness order is always: M1 donkey jacket, M2 anorak, M3 suit jacket, M4 cord/sports jacket, M5 overcoat/car coat, M6 shell suit/blouson, W1 wool coat, W2 belted mac, W3 quilted anorak, W4 skirt-suit jacket.

## 1. In short

- **Nothing in this area reaches grade A for any of the ten.** No free donkey jacket, anorak, Crombie or car coat, power-dressing jacket or proper belted mac turned up.
- **Best lead, needing a ruling:** Style3D CG's "Man Black Business Suit" on Sketchfab [8].
  - Licence: CC Attribution. It was made in Style3D's own pattern software, so the cut is likely professional [SS][I].
  - The mesh is a 663,700-triangle simulation mesh, so it needs retopology. The cut is modern and slim. At best it scores M3 2.
  - Style3D has more free CC-BY coats on Sketchfab: black, brown and plain "jacket coat", a down coat and a sheepskin coat set. They might serve W1, W3 or M5, but I could not see them [9].
- **Best allowed (MIT):** the Microsoft Rocketbox avatars [1].
  - They include four men's business suits, four women's suits (one a skirt suit), a tweed sports jacket, a track top and work jackets.
  - All are grade C [READ]. Each is one fused mesh of about 7,200 to 8,600 triangles for the whole person, with flat lapels. They are shape references only.
- **Already in our repository:** our own Mixamo bodies Joe and Martha wear suit jackets as separate meshes of about 10,000 triangles [3] [READ]. They are also grade C: thin, flat lapels with no roll, and a modern cut.
- **For W3 and M6, needing a ruling (CC-BY, not seen):**
  - a Marvelous-made "Puffer Jacket" by sivayuce [15];
  - four CC-BY bomber jackets [13];
  - a Marvelous-made varsity jacket [14].
- **Not available:** a scanned "1990's Tracksuit Top" exists (FTI_Capture, 349,200 triangles), but it is not offered for download [18] [SS][I].
- **Research clothing datasets** are non-commercial or unclear: CLOTH3D, BEDLAM and 4D-DRESS [I]. GarmentCode is MIT [READ], but its only lapel is a "SimpleLapel" component, not a tailored notch lapel [READ].
- **Search was cut short.** This session's shared web-search allowance (200 calls) ran out partway through. Harrington, quilted jacket, kagoul, 1980s blazer and women's coat searches on Sketchfab were not finished. See section 4.

## 3. Per silhouette (best free candidate in this area)

- **M1 Donkey jacket:** nothing free and good enough. Rocketbox's work jackets (MIT, grade C) give a hip-length shape only. They have no yoke and no melton weight.
- **M2 Anorak:** nothing free and good enough. Rocketbox's hooded zip tops are grade C, and GarmentCode's hood is code.
- **M3 Suit jacket:** Style3D CG "Man Black Business Suit". It is CC-BY, so it needs a ruling. The cut was made in pattern software but is modern and slim, the mesh needs retopology, and I have not seen it: M3 2 at best. Allowed fallbacks are Mixamo Joe and the Rocketbox suits, both grade C.
- **M4 Cord or sports jacket:** nothing good. Rocketbox Male_Adult_03 (tweed check) is the nearest allowed, at grade C. ExposedLeaf's "Suit Jacket" (CC-BY, 8,800 triangles) is unseen.
- **M5 Overcoat or car coat:** nothing free and good enough. Leads to look at are Style3D's "jacket coat" series and its sheepskin coat set (CC-BY, unseen).
- **M6 Shell suit or blouson:**
  - Nothing confirmed good.
  - Leads: the CC-BY bomber jackets and Sev's varsity jacket, as blouson bases. Rocketbox Male_Adult_10 is a track top at grade C.
  - The only real 1990 scan, FTI_Capture's "1990's Tracksuit Top", is not downloadable.
- **W1 Wool coat:** nothing confirmed. Leads: Style3D "Black jacket coat" and "Brown jacket coat" (CC-BY, unseen). The 3DECraft wool coat scan is a branded product, so it is out.
- **W2 Belted mac:** nothing free and good enough. Elvaerwyn's trench coat is male and grade C. kampeao's trench coat is unseen. The Santa Cruz museum holds a scan of a 1940s Women's Army Corps trench coat, but its licence and download status were not seen [23].
- **W3 Quilted anorak:** nothing for the diamond-quilted, cord-collar version. For the puffer variant, sivayuce's "Puffer Jacket" or Style3D's "Purple Down Coat" (both CC-BY, unseen).
- **W4 Skirt-suit jacket:** nothing free and good enough. Rocketbox Business_Female_03 (a skirt suit) and Mixamo Martha are grade C, with no 1990 shoulders.

## 4. What I could not reach or verify

**Blocked by the network (WebFetch or curl):**
- sketchfab.com, api.sketchfab.com, blenderkit.com, api.polyhaven.com;
- www. and static.makehumancommunity.org (assets.json now returns 403);
- frontiersin.org, research-collection.ethz.ch (GarmentCodeData's licence), bedlam.is.tue.mpg.de (BEDLAM's licence);
- mixamo.com and community.adobe.com;
- huggingface.co (Objaverse metadata), opengameart.org, the si.edu APIs.

So every Sketchfab item above is [SS]. Nothing on Sketchfab was seen in pictures, and its licence fields were not read.

**What did work:**
- github.com by git clone, and raw.githubusercontent.com.
- api.github.com refused (this session has no access to that repository).

**WebSearch hit the session's cap of 200 calls** partway through, so these were never searched:
- Harrington jackets (beyond bombers), quilted or Barbour-style jackets, kagouls;
- 1980s blazers and shoulder pads, women's coats and women's blazers;
- donkey jackets beyond one search;
- Sketchfab's CC0 tag filtered to clothing.

**Licences known only from memory [I]:**
- CLOTH3D: the README only says to register at ChaLearn. I believe it is academic, non-commercial.
- 4D-DRESS: no licence in the README. I believe it is non-commercial research.
- BEDLAM: the README points to a licence page I could not open. I believe it is non-commercial scientific research.
- Treat all three as **NO** until their terms are read.

**Objaverse:**
- The dataset as a whole is ODC-By 1.0, and each object keeps its own licence [31] [READ].
- Objaverse 1.0 is drawn from Sketchfab, so it adds no new garments beyond Sketchfab's own [I].

**Mixamo:** I could not list Mixamo's online character library. Only our 18 downloaded bodies were checked. Of those, only Joe and Martha wear jackets; "The Boss" wears a stylised 1,803-triangle jacket.

**Sketchfab's "Free Standard" licence:** whether it is the same as the Fab Standard License is unverified [I]. Treat it as RULING.

**Rocketbox's Children folder** (four avatars): it must never be imported, under the content rule. Its police and military avatars are American or German, not British.

**Earlier work reused:** the MakeHuman CC0 and CC-BY counts (558 and 804) come from the 30 September note [5]. I could not re-read the index today.
