# Free Marvelous Designer and CLO project files (helper 1, 2 October 2026)

Marks: [READ] read at the source; [SS] search summary only; [I] my inference. Almost every host was blocked, so nearly everything here is [SS]. I saw no pictures, so every grade comes from a description.

## 1. In short

- **Best find: CLO's own free block patterns on CONNECT.** "CONNECT Official" gives away hundreds of .zprj block patterns. Some it made itself (the "Block_FV2" set). Others it made with KOFOTI, the Korea Federation of Textile Industries (the "300 free patterns" / "500 block patterns" campaign). [SS] They come closest to W1 (double-breasted, princess-line and Balmacaan coats), W3 (quilted jacket, liner jacket), W4 (a "Power Shoulder Jacket") and M2 (a shell parka and a hooded anorak). [SS]
- **Their licence is the catch.** The campaign post says the patterns are "completely FREE and available for commercial use" [SS]. They are still covered by CONNECT's own "Free License", whose rules for use and redistribution I could not read. Class: **RULING**.
- **Men's suit jacket:** the only free men's blazer that comes with its pattern is fulyaugurr's "Man Basic Blazer" on CGTrader (CLO 6.2, ZPRJ and ZPAC; "Royalty Free License (no AI)") [SS]. It is unseen and probably a modern cut [I]. Class: **RULING**.
- **Marvelous Designer's own library and Modular Configurator** include a jacket module and a trench-coat module, and we already have them in the trial [SS]. I found no licence that lets us ship them, so they count as **NO** until CLO confirms it in writing.
- **Nothing free in this area is ALLOWED outright.** Every candidate is RULING or NO.
- **Nothing free covers the donkey jacket, car coat, Crombie, men's Harrington or shell-suit top** in this area.
- **Avoid:** ArtStation's free items carry its "Standard Use License", capped at one commercial project and 2,000 sales [SS]. FlippedNormals' free bomber is personal use only [SS]. Several "free" packs on mirror sites are pirated copies of paid items.

## 3. Per silhouette

- **M1 Donkey jacket:** nothing free and good enough. Only a base: KOFOTI MV2 Four-Pocket Sports Coat or Oversized Mac Coat (1).
- **M2 Anorak:** KOFOTI MV2 Oversized Shell Parka or Oversized Hoodie Anorak (2). Unseen; RULING.
- **M3 Suit jacket:** fulyaugurr's Man Basic Blazer (2, only if its lapel holds). Unseen; RULING. No professional men's notch-lapel jacket was confirmed.
- **M4 Cord or sports jacket:** the same blazer, or the KOFOTI MV2 Four-Pocket Sports Coat (1, perhaps 2).
- **M5 Overcoat or car coat:** nothing free and good enough. Only a base: KOFOTI MV2 Oversized Mac Coat (1).
- **M6 Shell suit or blouson:** nothing free and good enough. The only Harrington is a women's block (KOFOTI or FV2 Harrington Jacket, 1). There is no shell-suit top.
- **W1 Wool coat:** CONNECT Official FV2 Double-Breasted Coat, Princess Line Coat or Balmacaan Coat (2). RULING.
- **W2 Belted mac:** the Modular Configurator trench coat (2, but NO until CLO states its terms). Otherwise the FV2 Balmacaan Coat (1).
- **W3 Quilted anorak:** CONNECT Official FV2 Quilted Jacket (Feb 2024) or FV2 Liner Jacket (2). RULING.
- **W4 Skirt-suit jacket:** CONNECT Official FV2 Power Shoulder Jacket (2). RULING.

## 4. What I could not reach or verify

- **Blocked hosts.** WebFetch was refused by legal.clo-set.com, connect.clo-set.com, support.clo3d.com, cgtrader.com, fab.com, marvelousdesigner.com, virtualfilmer.com, fashiontoolbox.co.uk and learn3dfashion.com. GitHub's general search API is closed to this session. The GitHub tool's repository search worked [READ], but found no garment files, only fake "repack" (cracked software) repositories. The session's web-search budget (200, shared) ran out, so I stopped there.
- **The scope of CONNECT's "Free License" was not read.** Two things were seen only in summaries: who may use a free item (the person, their company's staff and its contractors), and that free items need no approval. The clauses that decide our case were not seen: modification, redistribution inside a sold game, and credit. **This is the decision Jafar would need, and a person with a browser can read it in five minutes.**
- **I saw no pictures.** These are unchecked: whether the FV2 and MV2 blocks have real lapel roll and finished construction or are bare blocks; their sizing; whether the "Four-Pocket Sports Coat" is a tailored jacket; the Man Basic Blazer's lapel; and what kind of jacket Solati's "Men's Jacket 2" is.
- **Unclear membership.** The summaries mixed up which items sit in the Block_FV2 collection and which in KOFOTI_Female_Patterns. One summary gave the female collection 2,051 items, which is probably the whole Official portfolio [I].
- **Untested:** whether a free CLO-SET account can download these files into our Marvelous Designer trial.
- **Not checked closely:** Reddit (no useful hits); Etsy (it has no free listings [I]); Patreon (one paid tutorial only); YouTube. Marvelous Designer's own "Suit: Jacket" tutorial (version 9.5) may come with a project file, but tutorial assets look educational-only [20].
- **Style3D's in-app library** (4,000+ assets) is non-commercial on the free plan [SS]. Its paid-plan terms were not read.
