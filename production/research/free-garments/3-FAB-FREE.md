# Free garments on Fab, Epic samples and Fab's free rotations (helper 3, 2 October 2026)

Tags: **[READ]** read at the source. **[SS]** search summary only. **[I]** my inference. **[note]** taken from an earlier project note and not re-checked today.

No picture was seen. Every grade comes from descriptions only.

## 1. In short

- **Fab has nothing free that is good enough for any of the ten silhouettes.** Epic has no free jacket, blazer, suit, overcoat, mac or women's coat up to 2 October 2026 [SS][note]. MetaHuman 5.8 added no garments [READ].
- **The best of a weak lot is Epic's free MetaHuman Techwear Outfit.** It is a professional jacket that resizes to fit MetaHuman bodies. But it is modern techwear, and one asset with the trousers and shoes. At most it is a base for the anorak, the blouson or the quilted anorak (closeness 1). Fab Standard License: ALLOWED.
- **Second: Epic's free Clothing Construction Presets** (sets of four and two source bodies). This is a tool, not a garment. It turns one good pattern into a garment that resizes across our bodies [SS].
- **Free now, until 6 October 2026 at 13:59 UTC: Polyphoria's Paladin RPG Set** [SS]. It is a resizing MetaHuman outfit, but it is fantasy armour, so it is no use for 1990 (closeness 0). Claim it only as a free working example of a professional resizing garment. Claiming needs Jafar's sign-in.
- **Limited-Time Free** has run every two weeks since November 2024 [SS]. The windows I could see had no realistic jacket, coat or suit, and nothing relevant is announced. Items claimed during a window stay usable under the Fab Standard License for good [SS]. Epic's own documentation says the same about the 2024 Megascans window [READ].
- **A practical find.** Fab's documentation says free products can be downloaded without signing in, and only adding to the library needs an account [READ]. That may unblock Epic's free Oxfords, Loafers and Chelsea Boots without Jafar's sign-in [I]. I have not tried it, and I have not checked that MetaHuman items offer that download.
- **City Sample Crowds and Paragon are no help for tailoring.** City Sample Crowds is UE-Only (needs his RULING) and modern American casual. Paragon is UE-only fantasy.
- **Megascans has no garments.** Its scanned fabric surfaces would help finish ours: plain wool for melton, herringbone for tweed, leather or tarp for a donkey-jacket yoke [SS][I]. I could not check whether they are in the free starter set.
- **Nothing in this area is a PATTERN.** Everything is MESH.

## 3. Per silhouette

- **M1 Donkey jacket:** nothing free and good enough in this area. Megascans wool, leather or tarp surfaces could finish one that we cut [I].
- **M2 Anorak:** nothing free and good enough. The weak base is Epic's Techwear jacket (closeness 1). It would have to be split from the trousers in Blender, and the jacket would lose its resizing until rebuilt with the Construction Presets [I].
- **M3 Suit jacket:** nothing free and good enough in this area.
- **M4 Cord or sports jacket:** nothing free and good enough. Megascans herringbone could texture a tweed [I].
- **M5 Overcoat or car coat:** nothing free and good enough in this area.
- **M6 Shell-suit jacket or blouson:** nothing free and good enough. The weak base is the Techwear jacket (closeness 1).
- **W1 Wool coat:** nothing free and good enough in this area.
- **W2 Belted mac:** nothing free and good enough in this area.
- **W3 Quilted anorak:** nothing free and good enough. The Techwear jacket is a weak base (closeness 1); the quilting would be ours.
- **W4 Skirt-suit jacket:** nothing free and good enough in this area.

**Paid near-misses, seen in passing (not free; prices not seen).** These are for the separate paid note, and all are under the Fab Standard License:
- outfit-maker's "Coat for MetaHuman 53", a brown wool overcoat with notch lapels, a button front and welt pockets (M5).
- "Coat ... 113", a belted trench (W2).
- "Jacket 197", a tan field jacket.
- "Modular Formal Clothing", with eight men's and five women's jackets.
- A 1920s "Mafia Gangster Suit".

All are listed in [28] [SS].

**Limited-Time Free: what was offered, and the terms.**
- **How it runs:** every two weeks since November 2024 [SS 17]. Three or so items per window [SS].
- **Windows I could see:**
  - 23 September to 7 October 2025: included a stylised Viking character bundle [SS 16].
  - 8 to 22 September 2026: Normandy Village, Industrial Infrastructure and RPG crafting effects [SS 15].
  - 22 September to 6 October 2026: Asian Canal, the Paladin RPG Set and Global Search Pro [SS 14].
  - Of about ten items in these windows, one was MetaHuman clothing (fantasy armour). None was a realistic jacket, coat or suit.
  - The full history, including the old Unreal Marketplace "Free for the Month" (2014 to 2024), could not be checked; see section 4. From memory, I recall no realistic tailoring in either [I, unverified].
- **Next window:** nothing announced that I could see.
- **Terms:**
  - An item claimed in its window stays usable under the Fab Standard License after the window ends [SS 18, 19].
  - The Personal and Professional tiers carry the same rights. The tier depends on the buyer's revenue when claiming (Personal: under US$100,000 in the last 12 months) [READ 2 for the tiers; SS 18 for "same rights"].
  - Epic's own documentation says the same for Megascans added during the 2024 window: "entitled to use them under the Fab Standard License terms forever" [READ 1].
  - Keeping an item needs it added to the library while signed in. A plain download needs no sign-in [READ 1].
  - Check each listing's own licence at claim time, because some Epic content is UE-Only [I].
