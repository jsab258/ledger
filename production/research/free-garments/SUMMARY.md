# Free garments for the ten tailored silhouettes (research summary, 2 October 2026)

**The question.** The first street needs about 40 base garments. Ten are tailored outerwear, and our sessions cannot author them: every attempt failed blind review on the cut and the lapel. We have Marvelous Designer on trial, and we can re-drape, adapt, re-colour and finish anything that has a good pattern or a good mesh. Before Jafar spends anything, what already exists for free?

**The ten silhouettes:**

| | Men | | Women |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | Donkey jacket | W1 | Wool coat |
| M2 | Anorak | W2 | Belted mac |
| M3 | Suit jacket | W3 | Quilted anorak |
| M4 | Cord or sports jacket | W4 | Skirt-suit jacket |
| M5 | Overcoat or car coat | | |
| M6 | Shell-suit jacket or blouson | | |

**How the search was done.** Four separate helpers each took one source. Each was given the problem, not a theory, for about thirty minutes, read-only, with dated sources:
1. `1-MD-CLO-PROJECT-FILES.md`: Marvelous and CLO project files shared free by artists, CLO's own CONNECT store, and Marvelous's built-in library.
2. `2-OPEN-LIBRARIES.md`: CC0 and CC-BY meshes on Sketchfab, plus Rocketbox, MakeHuman, BlenderKit, museum scans, Mixamo and the research datasets.
3. `3-FAB-FREE.md`: Fab's free garments, Epic's samples, and Fab's free fortnightly items.
4. `4-SEWING-PATTERNS.md`: free professional sewing patterns, open pattern files and public-domain tailoring books.

This session then checked the deciding facts itself (see "Checked here").

**Marks used:**
- **[READ]** read at the source.
- **[SS]** search summary only.
- **[I]** inference.

**One limit governs everything below.** This cloud session's network refuses Sketchfab, Fab, CLO's CONNECT, CGTrader, ArtStation, Gumroad, FreeSewing's site, archive.org and most other hosts. GitHub, GitLab, npm, Epic's developer documentation and Wikipedia were reachable. So most items were never seen in pictures, and most licence pages were never read. Every helper's web searches ran out before the end. Each note lists what it could not reach.

## The answer in short

1. **Nothing free is both allowed outright and good enough for any of the ten.** The items that pass the allowlist are of two kinds:
   - patterns we must still finish ourselves: FreeSewing's coats and bomber (MIT) and Vincent's 1900 tailoring system (public domain);
   - crude meshes: Rocketbox (MIT), our own Mixamo suits, and MakeHuman's CC0 suits, which have already failed.
2. **The best professional source is CLO's own free patterns on its CONNECT store.**
   - What: CONNECT Official's "Block_FV2" set, and its "300 free patterns" made with KOFOTI, the Korea Federation of Textile Industries. Among them are a Power Shoulder Jacket, a Double-Breasted Coat, a Balmacaan coat, a Quilted Jacket, a Liner Jacket, a men's Shell Parka, a Hooded Anorak, a Four-Pocket Sports Coat and a Mac Coat.
   - Format: .zprj Marvelous/CLO pattern files [SS].
   - Licence: CLO's post says they are "completely FREE and available for commercial use" [SS]. They come under CONNECT's "Free License", which by summary is "revocable" [SS]. So they need Jafar's ruling. Nobody here has seen them, and "block" may mean a plain base pattern rather than a finished garment [I].
   - Together they could cover four silhouettes (W1, W3, W4 and M2) and give weak starts for three more (M4, M5 and M6).
3. **The best-built free suit jacket draft is "Gent jacket (with tummy)",** in the sample collections of Seamly2D and Valentina, the free pattern programs.
   - It is a Finnish bespoke draft of 36 pieces, cut for a heavy man with a belly (180 cm, chest 122, waist 130), which is Ron's build.
   - I drew its pieces here (not committed: the picture would be an unattributed copy of a GPL file). It has a real tailor's front with a waist dart and pocket, a facing that carries the lapel, separate upper and under collars, chest canvas, a side body and a two-piece sleeve [READ].
   - The file states no licence and sits inside GPL-3.0 repositories. That needs a ruling; I lean no, unless the author agrees.
4. **Nothing free and good enough exists for:**
   - the donkey jacket (M1);
   - the car coat (half of M5);
   - the shell-suit top (half of M6);
   - the belted mac (W2).

   The one possible exception is Marvelous's own built-in trench-coat module, whose licence to ship is unknown.
5. **Fab has nothing free that helps.** Epic's free outfits are modern, and no free fortnightly item has been realistic tailoring. Fab's realistic coats and suits are all paid.
6. **"Free" still needs one purchase to ship.** Anything draped in the Marvelous trial is for evaluation only, by its own terms (DECISIONS.md, 1 October). So a free pattern still needs the paid month, or the work redone in it, before it ships. Testing it in the trial is allowed.

## Licences, as they apply here

| Class | Licences found | What it means for a sold game |
|---|---|---|
| **Allowed** | MIT (FreeSewing, Rocketbox, GarmentCode's code); public domain (Vincent, 1860–1926); CC0 (MakeHuman's CC0 items, museum scans); Fab Standard License (Epic's free MetaHuman items); Mixamo (allowlisted) | Use and modify in a sold game. MIT asks for its notice to ship with any copy of the code or files, and nothing for a garment drafted from it [I]. The others ask for no credit |
| **Needs his ruling** | CONNECT "Free License" (CLO); CC-BY (Style3D CG's coats, Sketchfab jackets, MakeHuman's CC-BY coats); CGTrader "Royalty Free License (no AI)"; GPL-3.0 (the Gent jacket file); Epic "UE-Only" content (City Sample Crowds) | **CONNECT:** modification and derivatives are allowed, but the licence is revocable and its wording on games was not read [SS]. **CC-BY:** allowed with credit (author, title, link, licence, changes). **CGTrader:** allowed inside a product, not as a separate file [SS]. **GPL:** copyleft, so a garment made from the pattern may count as a derivative [I] |
| **No** | Personal-use pattern companies (Mood Fabrics, Peppermint, Fibre Mood, Wardrobe By Me, Style Arc); ArtStation's free "Standard Use" (one project, 2,000 sales); FlippedNormals' free personal licence; CC BY-NC-ND items; scans of branded coats; pirated "free" mirrors of paid packs | Not usable |

## What this means (I)

- **With no ruling at all,** the free and allowed route is FreeSewing's Carlton, Carlita and Jett for M5, W1 and M6. Vincent's book is the method for anything else. Every one needs our own finishing, the part that has failed so far. They are worth one trial each, but they do not answer "the cut and the lapel need a professional pattern".
- **One ruling, on CONNECT's Free License,** would open CLO's own patterns for W1, W3, W4 and M2. Those are the four silhouettes the free world covers best. Testing them in the trial is allowed, so they can be judged before any ruling.
  - The licence page needs a person with an open browser. Jafar's PC can read it; this session cannot.
  - So can the question "are these finished garments, or plain blocks?"
- **The men's tailoring (M3, M4) has no free professional pattern under a licence we already accept.** The Gent jacket is the nearest. It is free to try in the trial; it ships only if its author agrees or Jafar rules GPL acceptable.
- **The donkey jacket, car coat, shell-suit top and belted mac** have nothing free. They stay with paid patterns or a commissioned artist (production/research/clothing-pipeline/READY-MADE-TAILORING-2026-10-02.md), or with Marvelous's own trench module if its licence allows shipping.

## Checked here (this session, 2 October 2026)

- **FreeSewing's outerwear on npm** [READ]: Jaeger ("a sport coat style jacket"), Carlton and Carlita ("a nice long coat"), Jett ("a bomber or letterman jacket"), Devon ("a denim jacket, based on the Bent block") and Bent ("a menswear body block with a two-part sleeve").
  - All at version 4.10.2, published 13 September 2026, licence MIT.
  - The repository's licence reads "MIT License, Copyright (c) 2022 Joost De Cock".
- **The Gent jacket file** [READ], read from Seamly2D's repository (src/test/CollectionTest/share/gent_jacket_with_tummy.sm2d) and Valentina's (src/app/share/collection/Gent_Jacket_with_tummy.val).
  - The file: pattern name "Gent jacket(with tummy)", number Jacket_20160914, company "StindeDesign", size "Bespoke", 36 pieces.
  - The pieces include Etukappale (front), Laitavaate (facing), Yläkaulus and Alakaulus (upper and under collar), Rintaliina (chest canvas), Sivupala (side body) and Hihayläosa and Hiha-alaosa (top and under sleeve).
  - Its increments set the collar stand at 2.5 cm and the fall at 4 cm, the break point, the pocket heights and the chest ease.
  - No licence line is in the file. Seamly2D's LICENSE is GPL version 3.
  - **Drawn here:** installed Valentina 0.7.52 from Ubuntu, exported every piece headless, and rendered the front, facing and collars. Anyone can repeat it: open the file in the free Valentina or Seamly2D, which also export it as DXF-AAMA for Marvelous. The pieces are a tailor's, not a block's. Its lapel roll on a body was not seen: that needs a drape.
  - Valentina's whole sample collection (41 files) holds no coat [READ].
- **Microsoft Rocketbox's licence** is MIT ("Copyright (c) 2020 Microsoft") [READ]. The helper rendered its avatars: grade C.
- **CLO's free-pattern campaign:** CLO's own community post says the KOFOTI patterns are "completely FREE and available for commercial use" [SS].
  - CONNECT's terms say a free item comes with a "non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable, limited, **revocable**, and worldwide license to download, use, reproduce, display, perform, modify, and create derivative work" [SS].
  - The "FV2 Power Shoulder Jacket" is listed free in the Block_FV2 collection [SS]. Nothing on CONNECT could be opened.
- **W.D.F. Vincent** lived from 18 June 1860 to 1926 and edited *The Tailor and Cutter* from 1895 [SS]. So his books are out of copyright in Britain (life plus 70 years) and in the United States (published before 1931) [I].
- **Virtual Filmer's free "casual MetaHuman resizable clothing" (UE 5.6)** on Gumroad exists [SS]. Its garments and licence were not read, so it is a lead only.
