# What Epic and others give away for Unreal (October 2026), and what fits a 1990 British port street

**Access caveat first.** The network blocked fab.com, support.fab.com, unrealengine.com, quixel.com, polyhaven.com, ambientcg.com, cgchannel, 80.lv and every news site. I could read three kinds of page in full: Epic's developer documentation (dev.epicgames.com), one Unreal EULA PDF, and Wikipedia. Every Fab listing and every licence label below therefore comes from search-engine summaries. Each source is marked as read in full or summary only. After about 40 searches the session's web-search budget (200, shared across the session) ran out, so some leads could not be checked. They are listed at the end.

## 1. Licences that matter

- **Fab Standard Licence.** Allows commercial use, changes, and distribution inside your own project, in any engine (D:2,3). It has two price tiers, Personal (buyer under US$100k gross revenue in the last 12 months) and Professional (over that). The usage rights are the same in both. The tier depends on the buyer's status when they buy, and nothing needs upgrading if revenue grows later (D:2,24). This licence is on our allowlist.
- **UE-Only Content.** The EULA text, read in full: rights apply "only if and to the extent that the UE-Only Content is utilized in a Product that requires the Engine Code to operate" or in rendered linear media (D:1, §1(B)(i)). **So in law, a commercial Unreal game may ship it** (D:1). The project's own allowlist entry, SHIP-SAFE 8, covers only Epic's animation packs, so anything else needs Jafar's ruling (project rule, DECISIONS 2026-09-30).
- **CC-BY** is also offered on Fab (D:2). It is not on our allowlist.
- **Old Megascans from the Unreal plan or Bridge** were UE-Only. They stay UE-Only unless the same items are claimed or re-downloaded through Fab under the Standard licence (D:9,10).

## 2. Megascans after the move to Fab

- **Free to everyone until 31 Dec 2024.** About 17,000 assets could be claimed in one click under the Fab Standard Licence, for any engine. **Anything claimed then stays free to use forever** (D:4,5,7).
- **Paid since 1 Jan 2025.** Single 2D/3D assets from US$0.99, procedural kits from US$4.99, packs from US$24.99 (D:5, quoting Epic's blog).
- **Still free:** a starter set of "1,500+ assets", plus 5 tree packs and 11 scenes that are marked Unreal-only (D:4,6). If his account claimed everything in 2024, the starter set is already included (D:6). The Megaplants vegetation is described as free (D:6, summary).
- Epic's own UE 5.8 Bridge page is marked **Deprecated**. It still says "Megascans is completely free for all users of Unreal Engine… as UE-Only Content" (D:9). That sentence contradicts the 2025 change and looks out of date (I). Not every old Megascans asset was moved to Fab (D:10).
- **New in 2026:** Urban Ruin, Neo Renaissance Building, Abandoned Factory, Pakistan Street and Urban Megadrop (600+ assets), all paid (D:8, summary). There is no British or Victorian pack (D:8).
- **The free 1,500, item by item, could not be listed** (Fab blocked). A "City Street" collection exists (210+ items: asphalt, concrete, bricks, granite kerbs, drain trims, round manhole covers, litter) (D:6, summary), but I could not confirm which of those are in the free set.

**What would suit a 1990 port town (all I):**
- Granite kerbs, drain gullies, cast-iron covers, wet asphalt, concrete, cracks, stains, leaks and puddle decals: **yes**. These are period-neutral, and scans are the 2026 bar.
- Cobbles: **partly**. Right for the quay, wrong for the main street.
- Brick: **partly**. Scans are mostly continental; check the bond and the brick size against the UK reference photographs.
- Slate: **yes**, if a scanned slate exists. Not verified.
- Litter: **partly**. Remove modern packaging and anything branded.
- Trees for the hillside (Megaplants): **partly**.

**The single biggest lever:** find out whether Jafar's Epic account claimed the library in 2024. If it did, the whole library is ours under the Standard licence at no cost (I). Fab downloads need him signed in (previous note). If it did not, a targeted buy of about 20 surfaces at US$0.99 each costs about US$20. That is a one-off and his decision (I).

## Bottom line (I)

No free Epic city content fits a 1990 northern English street. The free 1,500 Megascans, any library claimed in 2024, and the CC0 libraries are the realistic route, for **surfaces, kerbs, drains and decals**. The buildings stay ours. **For Jafar:** (1) Did his account claim Megascans in 2024? He can check by signing in; it costs nothing. (2) Any UE-only non-animation content, such as City Sample's Mass setup, needs his ruling. (3) A targeted Megascans buy of about US$20 is money, so it is his decision.
