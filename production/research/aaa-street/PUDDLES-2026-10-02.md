# Puddles that read as mirrors (research, 2 October 2026)

O = opened; S = search summary only; I = my inference.

## 1. How it is done professionally

- **Wetness lives in the ground's own material.** Games shift the dry material's colour, roughness and normal by a wetness level [6 O, 8 O]. Puddles go where vertex paint or a height map says water collects [7 O]. UE5 artists use vertex paint [14 O] or world-space noise in the road material [15 O].
- **Lagarde's zones** [6 O]:
  - wet: darker, a little glossier;
  - drenched: normal slightly smoothed;
  - puddle: normal fully flattened.
- **The flat normal makes the mirror (I).** The road keeps its stone normal, which breaks its own reflection up.
- **Darkening.** Asphalt goes from 0.12 to 0.08 albedo when wet (×0.68) [6 O].
- **Gloss.** His boost is deliberately damped [6 O, 7 O]. Asphalt that is wet but not flooded stays far rougher than water, around 0.5 roughness (I).
- **Water.** F0 is 0.02 [6 O], which is Specular 0.25 in Unreal (F0 = 0.08 × Specular [5 O]).
- **Without ray tracing.** Far Cry 6 gave puddles and chrome alone "high-precision" reflection traces [9 O].
- **KCD2, TLOU2, Cyberpunk:** no technical source found.

## 2. Can a DBuffer decal drive Lumen's reflection?

Yes. In UE 5.8 the base pass mixes the decal's roughness, specular and normal into the road's pixel before the GBuffer is written. Lumen reads roughness from the GBuffer [5 O]. Limits:

- **Opacity mixes everything** [5 O]: roughness = road × (1 − opacity) + decal. With a soft mask, only an opaque centre reaches 0.05. The flat normal is mixed the same way, so the asphalt's normal survives over much of the puddle.
- **The road must accept it:** its Decal Response must include normal and roughness [4 O].
- **Unanswered report:** decal roughness can fail over constant roughness [13 O].

## 3. Why ours probably vanish (I)

- **Under an even overcast sky, roughness changes only sharpness, not brightness.** From a 2 m camera, road and puddle share the same Fresnel. So 0.15 and 0.05 differ only in sharpness, which software Lumen loses:
  - **High quality:** UE 5.8 traces reflections at quarter resolution with fewer reconstruction samples; Epic quality traces at full resolution [3 O, 5 O].
  - **The denoiser** mixes over an 8-pixel kernel [5 O].
  - **Off screen** (e.g. the sky above the frame), reflections come from distance fields and the surface cache and look "splotchy"; only on-screen things are sharp [2 O, 11 O].
  - **Hardware ray tracing** is what mirrors need [16 S, 11 O].
- **Lumen is weakest at roughness 0.2 to 0.39.** It handles about 0.1, and 0.4 upwards, well [10 O].
- **A puddle reads only where it mirrors contrast:** a dark facade against sky, a post, a person.

## 4. Settings (UE 5.8 engine files [5 O] unless marked)

- **Max Roughness To Trace** defaults to 0.4; above it there is no traced reflection [3 O].
- **r.Lumen.Reflections.DownsampleFactor:** 1 = full resolution, 2 = quarter [3 O].
- **r.Lumen.Reflections.SmoothBias:** smooths everything below the value, for reflections only; global [10 O].
- **Single Layer Water:** Lumen forces its reflections to mirror [1 O].
- **Front-layer translucent reflections** are switched off at High [1 O].
- **Planar reflections** are reported not to work with Lumen; no Epic statement [12 O].
- **Puddle sizes:** no source; photographs suggest kerb channel, ruts, gullies, dips (I).

## What to try, in order

1. **Compare quality levels:** one frame at Epic beside High, same camera.
2. **Retune road and puddles.**
   - Road: ×0.6 to 0.7 darker, roughness 0.4 to 0.5, full stone normal, water in cavities at 0.1 or below.
   - Puddles: roughness 0 to 0.02, Specular 0.25, flat normal.
3. **Fix the mask.** Give the decal an opaque core with a soft edge a few centimetres wide, or put puddles in the road material as a painted mask.
4. **Place puddles where they mirror contrast:** the kerb channel and the dips.
5. **Test r.Lumen.Reflections.SmoothBias at 0.1 to 0.2.** Watch glass and cars.
6. **Hero puddles:** a thin Single Layer Water mesh.
7. **Last, hardware ray tracing.** The project's config notes about 3.6 ms at 1280×720 for its ray-tracing scene alone.
