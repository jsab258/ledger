# Marvelous Designer's price, read 23 October 2026

For his page of 22 October (decide-marvelous-2026-10-22: "one month for one sample outfit (Tom's), the price shown first"). Nothing is bought or signed up for here. An account and a payment are his to make; this session never makes them.

## Questions

1. What does one month cost, and is there a monthly plan for one person?
2. Is there a free trial, and how long?
3. Does the plan allow commercial use of what is made, and export to a game (FBX/OBJ/glTF)?

## Read

- https://www.marvelousdesigner.com/pricing, read 23 October 2026, about 07:20 Swiss time:
  - Individual: "23.3 USD / Month", "Billed annually", "Save 188 USD with an annual subscription". 23.3 × 12 = 280 USD a year. So the monthly price is (280 + 188) / 12 = 39 USD; that is derived, and the monthly price is not shown on the page itself.
  - Free Trial: "2-week free trial for new users", "Full access to everything in Individual", button "Start for free".
  - Student 8.25 USD a month (not his case). Enterprise plans 199 USD a seat a month and up (not needed).
- A web search's summary of 2026 sources (Digital Production, 15 April 2026; CG Channel, 20 April 2026) gives the same 39 USD a month and 280 USD a year. Read only as snippets, so it is not evidence; it agrees with the page.

## Answers

1. **39 USD a month** (derived from the official page), or **280 USD for a year**. Taxes are not stated (the FAQ's answer did not load).
2. **Two weeks free** for new users, the full Individual plan.
3. **Unverified.** The pricing page says nothing on commercial use or export formats. The licence must be read before anything made in it enters the game (the licence allowlist is law).
