## 1. Epic's MetaHuman clothing

- **Two kinds** [E1]: a Chaos Outfit that "resizes dynamically", or a fixed skeletal mesh, which "does not resize" but can be made "for any body shape" [E2]. Needs the MetaHuman Creator and Chaos Cloth Asset plugins and Creator Core Data; outfits in /Game/Outfits [E3].
- **Source bodies** [E4]: at least two; Export tool ▶ Geometry Export ▶ Full Body Skeletal Mesh (head and body, `*_CombinedSkelMesh`), FBX to "the DCC of your choice"; keep armpit and thigh-gap clearance. 5.8's Export tool also gives DNA Export and DCC Export [E5][E12].
- **Import** [E6]: FBX (render mesh only) or USD from Marvelous or CLO (sim and render mesh). FBX [E7]: import as a static mesh ("importing a rigged or skinned FBX as a skeletal mesh is not recommended"); in a Cloth Asset, StaticMeshImport (Import Sim Mesh off) to TransferSkinWeights (Render Mesh, from the combined body, Closest Point on Surface); levels of detail by Remesh at **50, 30, 10%**, "MetaHuman expects four body LODs: 0, 1, 2, and 3"; tearing "in the armpits and upper thighs" mended with painted Relax, Prune, Hammer, Clamp and Normalize maps (SetSkinningSelection). USD is the same graph with USDImport [E8].
- **Outfit Asset** [E9]: Resizable Outfit template; one Sized Outfit Source per cloth asset, paired with its body; **Num Resizing Interpolation Points 1,500** (1,000 to 2,500 recommended). Bodies far apart "can cause warping"; Epic advises "multiple outfits for multiple source bodies" [E2].
- **Wearing it** [E10]: Hair & Clothing ▶ Outfit Clothing ▶ Wear makes a WI_ item; a **Body Hidden Face Map** on the body's UVs removes covered skin at assembly. The page: "Faces on the head mesh cannot currently be hidden."
- **The 5.8.2 code (Source):**
  - An outfit's weights are transferred again from the merged head and body at every build (`bTransferSkinWeights = true`, MetaHumanDefaultEditorPipelineBase.cpp 498, 1604 to 1609; MetaHumanOutfitEditorPipeline.cpp 71 to 74). A skeletal-mesh item passes untouched (WARDROBE-ITEM-ROUTE-2026-10-07.md).
  - **Both item pipelines carry `HeadHiddenFaceMapTexture`**: "If this clothing covers any part of the head mesh, you may want to provide a hidden face map to remove or shrink the head geometry out of the way of the clothing" (MetaHumanSkeletalMeshEditorPipeline.h 25 to 41), applied to the face mesh's skin slots on every level of detail (EditorPipelineBase.cpp 762).
  - Map values under MaxCullValue 0.1 remove, over MinKeepValue 0.9 keep; between, vertices move "in the opposite direction of their vertex normal" up to **MaxShrinkDistance (default 0)**, unit UNVERIFIED (Unreal's is the centimetre, inference) (MetaHumanGeometryRemovalTypes.h 22 to 50).
  - So Epic's page lags its code (inference). LEDGER's 30 October study saw the head map and did not use it (HIDDEN-FACES-2026-10-30.md).
- **Releases:** 5.7's Python API "supports sculpting, conforming, wardrobe, rigging, and textures" [E11]; the 5.8 notes add no clothing feature [E12]. Marvelous 2025.2 suits UE 5.6 and later; 5.5 not ("technical issues regarding simulation data") [M2].

## 2. Marvelous's MetaHuman import

- **DNA importer** (2025.2) [M1]: File ▶ Import (Add) ▶ DNA, Body and Head .dna "exported from the MetaHuman Editor"; options Unit and scale, Auto Add Arrangement Points and Auto Create Fitting Suit ("for humanoid avatars only"), Axis Conversion; one avatar, posed by its joints; no .mtn motions. Its pose, triangle count and saving as .avt are not stated (UNVERIFIED); no API call (MD-AVATAR-2026-10-26.md). CLO took the DNA from Assembly ▶ DCC Export in 5.6 [C1]; in 5.8 it is the Export tool [E5].
- **Locked:** Marvelous's 18 MetaHuman bodies are encrypted .avte that "cannot be exported" [M3]; LiveSync MetaHumans are "experimental", saved only as ZPRJ [C2].
- **FBX:** Epic's sweater imports with points and fitting suit on at **cm (DAZ Studio) 100%**, Y up [E13]. Tom's full body at scale 10 already has **109 points** and his neck (MH_TomL1.avt; production/art/clothing/tom-outfit/SPEC.md).
- **To Unreal:** USD export welds or not, thin or thick, Unified UV 100 to 10,000 px, seam fill 0 to 15 mm [M4]. CLO's Unreal guide [M5]: close-fitting pieces skinned, only loose parts simulated; EveryWear at most **8 joints** a point; collision thickness **2.5 to 5 mm** to part layers.
- **Shoes** [M6]: an accessory (.zacs, millimetres, shoes_r, shoes_l); laces and heels: "wrap them in a transparent, flat dummy mesh", spaced "tighter and more even" than the shoe. CLO's form of LEDGER's smoothed boot proxy (30 October).
- 2026.0 and 2026.1 add nothing for MetaHumans [M7][M8].

## 3. Artists' and studios' breakdowns

- **Epic's sweater** [E13]: the collar a rectangle the neckline's length, M:N sewn, its own fabric (Trim_Fusible_Rigid), its seam's 3D Seamline Intensity and Thickness 0; bands by Offset as Internal Line, Cut and Sew; USD Thick, Unified UV, 2,048 px, 5 px seam fill.
- **CLO's checklist** [C1]: necklines, cuffs, waistbands up close; heaviest and thinnest bodies; "no collar digging into the neck".
- **Mafia** (studies/06-characters-wardrobe.md): 53 hiding groups and 35 morphs reshape what is underneath ("a hood extends over the collar"): the body under a garment changes too (inference).
- **Don't Nod, Banishers** (Victorien Berry, 24 June 2024) [X1]: four body sizes per sex sharing topology, UVs, landmarks; contact "faked with elastic bands"; folds off the topology triangulated over "square topology" for riggers; garments deformed from one character to others, hidden polygons cut.
- **EA Criterion** (Pedram Karimfazli, 14 August 2019) [X2]: "aligned topology on the outside and on the inside so … the two sides don't intersect"; static folds removed where cloth moves.
- **Dmitry Bezrodny** (7 April 2020) [X3]: retopology on a decimated model in pieces under **2 to 3K triangles**; Marmoset bake, pieces spaced apart.
- **Shaafi Ahmad** (5 August 2020) [X4]: thickness off before retopology ("otherwise, I was getting spirals"), back after; density at the collar's break line.
- **Epic, Jared Monsen** (25 June 2024) [X5]: USD carries sim and render meshes; auto-skinning and auto-LOD nodes.
- **Not found:** artists' numbers for hems over footwear or collar weights.

## What changes in the skill

- Step 2: "Footwear under a hem: laces and heels wrapped in a flat dummy mesh, spaced tighter and more evenly than the shoe, as Marvelous's shoe accessory asks" [M6].
- Step 9, if trial 1 passes: "Anything covering the head's neck carries a head hidden-face map: grey (shrink) under the edge, never black where the edge can open, the distance from the probe" (Source, 5.8.2).
- Step 9: "A Chaos Outfit's weights are re-transferred at every build; a garment with hand-fixed weights ships as a skeletal-mesh item" (Source, 5.8.2).
- Refit: "Epic's resizable Outfit Asset (a source per body, 1,500 points) is the comparison for a close-fitting refit; untried" [E9].
- Step 2, after trial 2: "The avatar is the full body at scale 10; Marvelous's DNA importer is the official alternative (one window step, no API call)" [M1].
- Step 4: "A collar has its own, stiffer fabric (Epic: Trim_Fusible_Rigid), its seam's 3D seamline 0, its length the neckline's" [E13].
- Step 7: "Thickness off before retopology, back after; density at a collar's fold; inner and outer loops aligned" [X4][X2].
- Refit check: "The heaviest and thinnest bodies; no collar digging into the neck" [C1].
