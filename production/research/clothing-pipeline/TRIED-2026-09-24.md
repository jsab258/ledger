# The donkey jacket, tried end to end: 24 September

Jafar's item: one garment end to end, a 1990 donkey jacket on a MetaHuman,
rendered in the street, trying the routes cheapest first, and recording what
worked, what it cost, and whether the route could dress a street. If none
works, that is the finding.

**The finding: no route put a donkey jacket on a MetaHuman today.** Each route
stopped at a named step, below. Time is the only cost this session can
measure; the usage meter cannot be read from here.

## First, the two faults the research named (fixed, about 25 minutes)

- The clean-up script ruined coats: it dropped the armature, re-pivoted and
  re-scaled the mesh off the body, and applied the modifier that binds it.
  It now has a garment mode that keeps the coat where it was.
- The checker could not tell a working coat from a ruined one. A new garment
  check compares the cleaned coat with the uncleaned one and says "moved off
  the body", "skin dropped" or fine.

Both were proven on The Boss's jacket from our own character files. The
garment mode passed with its skin kept (68 joints); the old mode was named
RUINED. Commit a9442a30.

## The routes, in order

1. **Free MetaHuman-compatible clothing on Fab (about 10 minutes).**
   - There is no free donkey jacket.
   - The nearest free item is a "Worker Jacket" by Adam Arifin (glb and
     Blender). It is described as a real brand's design, so any branding would
     have to come off.
   - Epic's own free MetaHuman garments (T-shirt, slim jeans, hoodie, techwear)
     would dress the cast plainly.
   - Downloading needs Jafar's yes, so nothing was downloaded.
   - Could it dress a street? Only as far as garments exist, and none is 1990
     British workwear.
2. **MetaHuman's own fitting tools, the route that needs no skinning (about 15
   minutes).**
   - The outfit is built by Epic's graph tool (Dataflow), from the templates
     the engine ships.
   - In 5.8 the graph's inputs are protected from Python, so a script cannot
     drive it. It has to be done by hand in the graph editor, or with C++
     editor code.
   - The plugin's one garment shows the route works: the cast wear it.
   - Could it dress a street? It is the one route designed to fit one garment
     to every body, so yes, once per garment and by hand.
3. **Blender from a flat pattern (about 20 minutes).**
   - This route stopped before any cloth, at getting a body to fit to.
   - The engine's FBX export of a MetaHuman body crashes.
   - Read by other means, the built body has no torso, even when no garment
     was added (MH_RoccoBare). The likely cause, NOT VERIFIED, is that the
     shipped preset already wears the plugin's garment and a build removes the
     skin under clothes.
   - The plugin's own full source body is a different shape: Rocco's arms sit
     8 to 10 cm wider and higher. That is the warping Epic warns about.
   - Next step if taken: strip the preset's garment, rebuild, fit in Blender,
     then copy the weights with the engine's geometry scripting. Estimated at
     an hour or more, with unknowns at each step.
4. **Generating it from an image (5 minutes).**
   - This is the generation station's first real test on this card, and it
     fails at the first requirement.
   - The station's model (TRELLIS) needs an NVIDIA card with 16 GB and CUDA.
     This PC has an AMD Radeon RX 6700 with 10 GB (checked today).
   - Generation of 3D shapes does not work here as the station is built.

## What would put a jacket on him soonest

Epic's outfit route by hand, once. Someone (Jafar, or a C++ editor tool
written for it) takes a jacket model through the outfit graph against a
MetaHuman body. After that the plugin fits it to every cast member without
skinning. The jacket model itself still has to come from somewhere: a paid
or free download, or Blender against a garment-free body.

## Overnight, 24-25 September: what changed, and what now stands in the way

- **The outfit route runs end to end by script.** Epic's free MetaHuman
  garments (Fab, .mhpkg) import by an ordinary automated import task, go on a
  character by `set_single_slot_selection` / `try_add_slot_selection` on the
  "Outfits" slot, build with the character, and fit three different bodies
  (Sheila, Ron, Darren) with no skinning of ours. Their colours are set on the
  built materials. `tools/ue/import_fab_clothes.py`, `make_cast_metahumans.py`
  (dress, recolour), `fab_closure.py`. So a donkey jacket that exists as an
  outfit asset (OA_ + WI_) would be one line in `OUTFITS`.
- **What is missing is that outfit asset.** It is made from a garment mesh in
  Epic's outfit graph (Dataflow), whose inputs Python cannot set in 5.8; by
  hand in the graph editor, or with C++ editor code.
- **Image-to-3D for the garment mesh, tried on this card:**
  - Hunyuan3D (a DirectML port): its outputs are banned by the licence
    allowlist (territory-excluded licence). Not tried.
  - TRELLIS.2 through trellis.cpp (MIT, prebuilt Windows Vulkan, res 512 fits
    about 6 GB): the code and the TRELLIS weights are MIT, but it needs Meta's
    DINOv3 image model, under Meta's own licence (commercial use allowed;
    weapons and military uses banned; indemnity; terms changeable). Not on the
    allowlist, so stopped before any download: his ruling.
  - Even then it makes a closed shell of the man wearing the jacket, to be cut
    out, fitted and skinned (the engine's geometry scripting can transfer
    skin weights from a body), before the outfit graph.
- **The Blender route's crash** was not re-diagnosed tonight.

What would make the jacket soonest, in order of cost: a ruling allowing
DINOv3 as a tool never shipped; or a paid generator already on the allowlist
(Meshy or Tripo, a money question); then the outfit graph once, by hand or by
a C++ editor tool.

## 25 September, after midnight: the Blender route opened, and a first jacket

- **The export crash, diagnosed.** Both crash reports say "Assertion failed:
  MeshObject" in the skinned mesh component, from the export task, in the
  command-line editor. That editor has no renderer, and exporting a skinned
  mesh goes through a component whose render object needs one. In the full
  editor the same export works (`tools/ue/export_body_fbx.py`).
- **Which bodies export.** The bare built body (MH_RoccoBare) exports, but has
  legs and no torso: the build removes the skin under the preset's own
  T-shirt and shorts, as suspected. The plugin's identity template
  (`/MetaHumanCharacter/Body/IdentityTemplate/SKM_Body`) exports whole. The
  default garment's source bodies (archetype, the short thin male) and its
  garment meshes crash the exporter ("Array index out of bounds").
- **A jacket from dimensions** (`tools/meshgen/blender/donkey_jacket.py`): the
  template body's torso and arms cut at hips, neck and wrists, pushed out 3 cm
  and smoothed, the front hung as one flat panel at the chest's depth (the
  template is a woman's body; the jackets are for men), a hem to the top of
  the thigh, loose cuffs, a turned collar, a black yoke, buttons. It keeps
  the body's own skin weights, because it was cut from the body. Two
  attempts, as the rule allows: the first showed the bust, jagged collar
  teeth and side ridges; the second is a plain navy boxy jacket.
- **In the game** it is a skeletal mesh that follows a cast member's body by
  leader pose (`ue-probe/Source/LedgerProbe/Public/LedgerJacket.h`), so it
  takes their bones and moves with their idle. No outfit graph, no paid tool,
  no new licence. `-Jacket=rocco,sam` chooses who wears it; Ron by default.
- **What it is not:** cloth. It does not drape or swing; a heavy build could
  push through its front, since leader pose carries bones, not girth. The
  outfit graph remains the route that fits a garment to each body's shape.
