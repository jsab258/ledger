# Sheila's cardigan: the band missing on her left in the packaged film (diagnosis, 11 November 2026)

Fault: in the close talk film (MouthFilm-close-1111, BP_MH_LenaS4, f_035) the front opening on her left shows the knit's raw stepped cut from the V down past the top button; on her right the rib band covers it. Diagnosed only, nothing changed. Game file checked: F:/LedgerTools/garments/sheila/cardigan-s4-2026-11-11/chain2/skinned/sheila_cardigan_s4_skinned.blend (the same chain that wrote the .fbx garments.json names). Her left is +X (upperarm_l head at x +0.142).

## The measure first
The garment's own pictures could not show this fault. render_views.py sets no back-face culling, and Blender's workbench draws back faces by default, so a band facing the wrong way still draws there. Unreal does not draw it (below).

## Most likely cause (high confidence): the left band's faces point inward, so Unreal culls them
- build_cardigan.py line 120 makes every band quad in the same order: along the path, then outward (I, flipped per side at line 111). For s=+1 the normal is up x (+X) = +Y (into the body). For s=-1 it is up x (-X) = -Y (out of the front). The two bands are wound opposite ways. Line 164 (normal_update) only recomputes normals from that winding; nothing later reorients faces (clear_layers.py, follow_cloth.py and skin_garment.py move or weight points only).
- Measured in the skinned file (face normal from winding, dotted with the normal of the knit face under it): left band 221 of 234 faces opposite, median dot -0.999; of its 167 front faces below z 1.40, 1 faces forward. Right band: 217 of 234 agree, median +0.999, 165 of 166 front faces forward. All 663 knit front faces face forward.
- Unreal: FMeshPassProcessor::ComputeMeshCullMode (Engine/Source/Runtime/Renderer/Private/MeshPassProcessor.cpp, lines 1860-1867) returns CM_None only for a two-sided material, otherwise CM_CW. The SheilaCardigan row in production/specs/garments.json has no "two_sided", so import_garments.py lines 324-326 set the instance's two-sided override to false. The right band's faces wind like the knit and draw. The left band's faces point back toward the body, are culled, and the cut edge under them shows. This matches the frame: rib on her right, bare stepped knit on her left.
- Confirm: draw the skinned .blend with back-face culling on (the left band disappears), or set the cardigan's Rib instance two-sided in the editor for one film (the left band comes back). Neither was run here.

## Others, ruled out by measurement
1. Band height above the knit (signed, along the knit's outward normal, z < 1.24). Left: row 0 (centre, |x| < 6 mm) median 1.35 mm (min -0.73, max 3.31); row 1 median 1.49 (0.94 to 1.62); row 2 median 1.50 (1.33 to 1.73). Right: row 0 the same points (remove_doubles merged both sides' centre rows); row 1 median 1.50 (1.12 to 1.59); row 2 median 1.49 (1.39 to 1.65). On each side 9 points are under 0.5 mm, all on the shared centre row. clear_layers and follow_cloth left the 1.5 mm lift intact on both sides. Ruled out.
2. Skin weights. Front band points below z 1.40 (251 a side) against the knit weights under them, interpolated: L1 difference median 0.051 on both sides, 95th percentile 0.288 left and 0.301 right. Same main bone for 249 of 251 points on the left and 248 of 251 on the right. All are spine_02-04 or pelvis; no clavicle or upper arm. Posed with both upper arms turned 60 degrees about the front-back axis, rows 1 and 2 stayed 0.94 to 1.73 mm proud on both sides. The rest pose is already 53 degrees below level, so the posed arm went 23 degrees past vertical; the trunk band carries no arm weight, so the angle does not change the result. Ruled out.
3. Cut edge wider than the band. Open knit edges on the front opening, z 0.98 to 1.40: left 54 points, right 56. At rest every one has the band in front of it within 12 mm (cast along the outward normal); distance to the band is median 1.41 mm (max 2.05) on the left and 1.46 (2.56) on the right. The widest open point (57.8 mm left, 62.2 mm right) is at the V's corner, which the band follows. Posed, 3 left and 2 right points at z 1.39-1.40 (the V's top corner, both sides) come out from under the band; that is not the fault's place. Ruled out.
4. Mirroring: the bands are built per side (lines 99-133), not mirrored, and are the same size (121 samples each, 0.722 and 0.725 m). Side-specific bugs other than the winding: none found. A minority of faces (13 left, 17 right) have the other side's winding; where they lie was not mapped.

## Smallest fix it points to (not made)
In build_cardigan.py, give the s=+1 band the reverse quad order at line 120, or after line 134 flip any band face whose normal opposes the knit beneath it. Then rerun the chain, and add a build check that every band face's normal agrees with the knit under it. Making the instance two-sided would hide the fault but not fix it.
