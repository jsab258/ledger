# Ready-made tailoring: buy, adapt or commission (2 October 2026)

D = documented. I = my inference. [n] = source. Fab prices in CHF: Personal tier to Professional tier.

## The problem

We need a 1990 charcoal suit jacket and about ten more tailored coats, on MetaHumans in UE 5.8. They must fit a heavy 194 cm man and a slim 188 cm man, to the KCD2 bar. We have no clothing artist. Our scripted cuts failed review: sack-like, flaring over the belly, lapel collapsing.

## 1. Epic's own clothing

- Creator ships with grooms and one outfit [1] (D). In June 2025, users found no clothing presets in 5.6 [4] (D).
- Epic's free Fab outfits are a Techwear set (one piece, colours editable) [5], T-shirts, crop tops and body presets [6] (D). There is no Epic jacket, blazer, suit or overcoat [6] (D).
- Resizing stretches clothes from a "source body". Epic warns that very different bodies "can cause warping", especially in realistic clothing, and advises one source per body type [2] (D). A size override makes the fit tighter or looser [3] (D). Lapel, length and cut are not settings (I).
- Skeletal-mesh clothing does not resize, but it can be made for any body [2] (D).
- 5.7 (12 November 2025) added scripting of the wardrobe [7]. 5.8 added fixes only, and no garments [8] (D).
- Licence: the Fab Standard licence, which is on our allowlist. Both tiers give the same rights; the tier depends on the buyer's revenue [9] (D).

## 3. How small teams get tailored clothes

- **Commission:**
  - Upwork lists Marvelous Designer artists at $10–45 an hour [21] (D).
  - Cad Crowd lists $5–100 an hour [22] (D).
  - Fiverr has 626 such services [23] (D).
  - Husky and Nice Pictures already make MetaHuman jackets and take paid changes [12][13] (D).
  - I found no dated figure for the time a garment takes. My guess for one game-ready jacket on two bodies is 30–60 hours (I).
- **Buy a pattern file and re-drape it:** the CONNECT suit's pattern, lapel roll and drape on a MetaHuman are done already [17] (D). Moving it to our bodies by hand is quick; by script it is unproven. The belly still needs the pattern changes we failed at (I).
- **Scan:** the libraries hold trousers, shirts and hoodies [24] (D). A scan is one body in one pose, so it is no route to two bodies (I).
- **Kitbash:** alter a bought jacket in Blender. Its cut stays modern (I).

## 4. One jacket to the KCD2 bar on both bodies in a week

- **A. Buy two or three Fab outfits** (CHF 60–150, one day to test). They might pass on the slim man; on the heavy man, warping is likely [2] (I).
- **B. Re-drape the CONNECT pattern ourselves** (CHF 18–54, 3–5 days). The lapel is professional, but the belly fit is where we fail (I).
- **C. Commission an artist to adapt pattern B** to the 1990 specification on both bodies, returning the pattern and the game mesh ($500–1,500, 4–7 days) (I). This is the likeliest to pass within a week. It needs a money decision.
- **D. Commission from scratch** ($1,000–3,000, 1–2 weeks) (I). This is the surest route for the ten later coats.
