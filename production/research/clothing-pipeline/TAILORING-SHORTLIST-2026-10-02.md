# Tailoring shortlist: artists, patterns and a brief (2 October 2026)

D = documented. I = my inference. [n] = source. Nobody was contacted; nothing was bought. This goes further than READY-MADE-TAILORING-2026-10-02.md.

## The problem

One 1990 charcoal suit jacket, game-ready and skinned, on a heavy 194 cm MetaHuman and a slim 188 cm one, to the KCD2 bar; then about ten more tailored coats. We have no clothing artist.

## 1. Three artists

Searched: ArtStation, Fab, Fiverr. Nobody shows a lapelled jacket both well cut and game-ready at the KCD2 level (I); each covers part of the job.

**A. Chadi Belbaz** (ArtStation chadi_belbaz; Casablanca). Senior character artist, open for opportunities [3] (D).
- Tailored work: Sherlock Holmes waistcoat, shirt and trousers for UE5, 25 Jun 2025. Marvelous Designer, Maya topology, Substance, UE5; wireframe posted [4] (D). Led a realistic outfit for MixEnix, 28 Jul 2024 [5] (D).
- Game-ready: yes, in UE5. Skinning not shown (D). Rate: none public (D).
- Judgement: nearest to KCD2: the wool reads as wool, the folds sit naturally (I). No lapelled jacket shown; the hard part is unproven (I).

**B. Tara Mostaghimi** (ArtStation taramostaghimi; Brighton). Digital fashion; lists bespoke tailoring [6] (D).
- Tailored work: three suits from her MA, all 6 Jun 2025: a Bond three-piece, a 1950s oversized jacket, a fashion suit. Pattern blocks drafted by hand from MetaHuman measurements, made in Marvelous Designer and CLO, rendered in UE [7] (D).
- Game-ready: a UV sheet only; no retopology, skinning or LODs (D). Rate: none public (D).
- Judgement: the best pattern-cutting I saw; crisp lapels that roll cleanly (I). But all on a female mannequin, fashion cuts, by a recent graduate (I).

**C. Jakub "Vanya3D" Karwacki** (ArtStation vanya3d; Łódź). Lead character artist at nopixel Studios; marked open to freelance [2] (D).
- Tailored work: "Game ready suit and vest sets", 30 Jul 2023. Jacket 3,829 triangles buttoned; Marvelous Designer 12, retopology in Blender, Substance. The base pattern was another artist's [1] (D).
- Game-ready: yes, the whole chain, for a GTA role-play server; not Unreal or MetaHuman (D). Rate: none public (D).
- Judgement: the only lapelled jacket I found retopologised. The lapel lies flat; the front hangs straight (I). But tight, short, modern, at GTA detail, well below KCD2 (I).

Passed over: najdmie (best period patterns) has closed commissions [8] (D). A Fiverr MetaHuman clothing gig costs CHF 43.55 to 304.83 and shows no tailoring [9] (D): a price floor, not the bar (I).

## 2. Two ready patterns

**P1. najdmie, "Classic Suit Jacket"**, ArtStation Marketplace [10].
- Price: USD 2 Standard, USD 4 Extended Commercial (D).
- Included: CLO .zprj, OBJ, FBX, glTF, UVs, textures. High-poly, not retopologised (D).
- Cut: two buttons, the lapel rolling to the top one; wide notch lapels; low stance; flap pockets; breast welt; roomy, straight, long body (I, from previews). The closest to 1990 I found; the shoulder looks slightly built up (I).
- Licence: ArtStation's marketplace agreement. Standard: one commercial work, up to 2,000 sales or 20,000 monthly views. Extended: any number of commercial works. Sharing with a collaborator on the work is allowed [11] (D). Not on the allowlist: Jafar's decision.
- Against the bar: a plain render on CLO's default avatar, flat black. The shape is right; the surface is untested (I).

**P2. CGMT-STUDIO, "Men's suit Marvelous Designer / CLO Project file"**, Fab, 17 May 2026 [12].
- Price: CHF 4.14 Personal, CHF 12.45 Professional (D).
- Included: .zprj, OBJ, FBX, on CLO's m_med_nrw avatar. Not retopologised (D).
- Cut: two buttons, shown open; medium lapels; welted breast pocket; flap pockets; slim and short (I). Further from 1990 than P1 (I).
- Licence: Fab Standard, on the allowlist [13] (D).
- Against the bar: clean drape and believable wool, on a faceless studio mannequin (I).

The earlier survey's CONNECT three-piece remains the only pattern already on a MetaHuman (D).

## 3. The brief (written, not sent)

> We make a third-person PC game in Unreal Engine 5.8, set in Britain in 1990, with MetaHuman characters.
> **Make:** a single-breasted charcoal wool suit jacket of 1990: notch lapels of the period's width rolling to the top of two buttons, low button stance, flap pockets, welted breast pocket, centre vent, soft natural shoulders.
> **Bodies:** two MetaHuman bodies we supply: a heavy man of about 194 cm with a belly, and a slim man of about 188 cm. Each gets its own fit. On the heavy man it must close over the belly and hang straight.
> **Hand back:** the .zprj for each body; a retopologised mesh with UVs and LODs, skinned to the MetaHuman skeleton; PBR textures; a turntable in Unreal.
> **The bar:** the clothing in Kingdom Come: Deliverance II (frames attached).
> **Start from:** [P1 or P2], or your own pattern.
> **Rights:** we own the work outright, or hold it under CC0, CC-BY or Fab's Standard licence; any bought pattern in it must allow that.
> **First:** a paid test of the slim fit, at a fixed price and date. Quote your hours and rate.

Adapt: for A, "treat the test as proof of a lapelled jacket"; for B, "name who does retopology and skinning"; for C, "more detail, and a looser, longer cut than your GTA set".

## 4. Recommendation

- **Artist:** A (Belbaz), after a paid test (I). His surfaces are nearest the bar; the test shows whether he can cut a lapel. If it fails, B cuts the pattern and A or C makes the game mesh (I).
- **Pattern:** P1 if Jafar accepts ArtStation's Extended licence (USD 4); otherwise P2, which needs more recutting (I).
- **Cost and time:** no rate is public. At the earlier survey's USD 10 to 45 an hour, 30 to 50 hours is about USD 300 to 2,250, over one to two weeks (I). Later coats can reuse the pattern (I).
- **Risks:** nobody has shown a lapelled jacket at the KCD2 bar on a MetaHuman (I). The belly fit beat our cuts and may beat theirs (I). Ownership must be assigned in writing (I). P1 is off the allowlist (D). Availability marks may be stale (I). Paying anyone is Jafar's decision (D).
