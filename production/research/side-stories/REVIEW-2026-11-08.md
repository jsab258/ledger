# Review of the 32 side stories (draft of 8 November 2026)

One fresh reviewer, one round, against canon, REGULARS.md, the outline, studies 16 and 17, RULINGS.

## Verdict: pass with fixes

No breach of the content rule, the era, a name, an age or a trade (text searched for drink, gambling, children, slurs). Tom never drives. Regulars match their sheets. The Spread counts are right: tone 15/14/3; acts 21 open in Act I, 9 in Act II, 2 any; size 22 micro, 10 small; 24 givers plus Kevin, Terry, Peter, Dennis, with Lee and Wayne unused.

## Faults

1. Story 16, real name. "The board off the Morning Star": Morning Star is a real British newspaper. Breaks "No real names, brands" and RULINGS 29 Sep. Rename the title.
2. Story 20 reaches a main beat. "someone from a solicitor's in the Exchange has asked for copies of Mickey's licence file"; "Agar's paper arrives in Act II". This is the outline's Act II licence and solicitor attack, and the draft's own "Not here" list. A harbour clerk holding a cab licence file is also odd.
3. Stories 1 and 19 reach Act III. "which Act III will read"; "which Act III's inspection may hear". Reword so neither names the inspection.
4. Stories 11, 23, 31 pre-spend Act III's Ellis trade. "hand him to Ellis (the street learns who talks)" is the outline's Act III price. Use another consequence.
5. Stories 18 and 24 keep nothing. "The town keeps: nothing, unless Gordon talks"; "nothing at all". Breaks D24 and the Spread's "every ending changes what somebody knows".
6. Offered on a state. Only 1, 4, 29 and 32 name a state. Story 26 ("Come on a Monday") and 13 ("any afternoon") rest on the clock alone. No story names its patience window.
7. Story 18, logic. "a parcel addressed to Mickey... letters in Mickey's hand to June, never sent": Mickey cannot be both sender and addressee. Fix.
8. Story 24, law. One stranger to "witness her will": a 1990 will needs two witnesses together (Wills Act 1837). Make Tom the second.
9. Story 17, boats. Paying for, selling or losing Mickey's launch makes a boat an item (RULINGS 23 Sep: boats scenery). It also invents a property of Mickey, and is missing from the "His" list. Gary is at the yard; his sheet puts breakfast at the caff.
10. Size tags. "Small" is 6 to 10 steps, but 1, 9, 11, 17, 25, 31 list 3 or 4, and 29 lists none.
11. Story 30 owes Keith Garbutt (an Act II principal) a "small debt". Check it does not pre-empt his Act II role.
12. Spread wording. "Five principals beside them" omits Ron, Sheila, Garbutt, the Widow, Danny and Alison, who all appear.

## Applied (8 Nov 10:33, the session)
All twelve made: 16 retitled "The name board"; 20 now a stranger asking who holds the yard's lease; 1 and 19 no longer name Act III; 11, 23, 31 give the Ellis endings their own costs, not the outline's "who talks"; 18 and 24 end in something the town keeps; a table gives every story its state and its patience; 18's parcel is Mickey's to June, returned "not known"; 24 makes Tom the second witness beside the newsagent; 17 is a mooring, not a boat, and named as invented; all sizes now micro; 30's favour is a works man's, not Garbutt's; the spread names every principal who appears. Gary's breakfast moved to the caff.
