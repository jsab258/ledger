# Dressing the cab office's front room for a 2 m look

4 October 2026, about thirty minutes; extends NOTE.md, CLOSE-RANGE and GOODS. O = opened, S = search summary only, I = my inference.

## Method, ranked by how much it lifts a 2 m view (ranking I)

1. **Print is texture, not geometry.**
   - Text and stamps are decals or textures, with small flaws such as ink bleed (Meisel, 80.lv, 19 Feb 2025, O).
   - Some "blank bars" are real: the recipe draws fare-book entries and calendar rules as boxes (I).
   - Size (I): on the hook camera (fovV 39°) at 1440p, 1 mm at 2 m is about 1 px. Headings need a 10 to 15 mm cap height; body text needs real glyphs, not bars.
   - Print at 2048 px/m into one atlas (the hero-prop norm is 1024 to 2048 px/m, S). Face the important notices toward the glass.
2. **Articulated props, not boxes.** Bevels, knobs, handles, a drawer ajar, cords. Meisel hand-placed 90% of props (O); Teichmann's shop had about 4,000 actors, small items clustered by large (80.lv, 24 Aug 2023, O).
3. **Glass and tubes** (below).
4. **Shopfront hero details** (below).
5. **Wear.** Baked curvature, AO and edge masks drive edge wear and dirt through shared materials (Honey, 80.lv, 27 Mar 2025, O; Meisel, O); dirt along edges grounds objects (Teichmann, O). Mesh decals are cheaper than projected ones (Epic 5.8, O).

## Tubes

- A real fluorescent tube is about 12 kcd/m² (Wikipedia, O).
- An unlit emissive mesh shows the light's surface (Epic 5.8, O).
- Turn Affect Indirect Lighting off to stop noise; the rect light does the lighting (Goryachev, 80.lv, 1 Oct 2026, O).
- Small or very bright emissives make noise (Epic 5.8, O).
- Check that the glTF import kept an emissive value above 1 (I).

## Glass

- One translucent layer, Surface ForwardShading, with High Quality Translucency Reflections. These are front-layer only and "increase GPU cost" (Epic 5.8, O).
- Specular 0.5, Epic's measured value for glass (O). Roughness about 0.02.
- Lumen traces reflection rays only below roughness 0.4 by default (S).
- Grime: smear and fingerprint masks raise roughness in patches, with a gradient over the bottom 15 cm and the corners.
- Tubes on by day; Fresnel sets the balance (I).

## Shopfront

- **Gilt letters:** bevelled geometry, metallic 1, base colour (1.000, 0.766, 0.336) (Epic 5.8, O), roughness 0.15 to 0.3, with a black shade.
- **Console brackets:** "typically carved from wood with a curved outline. Usually of greater height than projection" (Durham SPD, Dec 2024, O). Extrude a scroll with volutes and add an acanthus leaf as a normal map (I).
- **Stall riser:** the cill above it is timber, granite or bronze, and tiles were a traditional facing (Durham, O). The riser sits flush under the cill, never jutting. Model the tiles bevelled, with grout recessed about 2 mm; glaze roughness 0.05 to 0.15, grout 0.9 (I).
- **Fanlight:** the number reverse-gilded on real glass, "shaded and outlined" (Garrett, O).
- **Side door:** panels "raised and fielded" (Taylor, 1994, O), with a letterbox and a kick plate.

## Frame cost (I)

Text atlas (4K BC7) about 21 MB and 0 ms; Nanite props, tiles and letters about 0.1 ms; mesh decals about 0. The largest: glass reflections and the shadowed rect light. Measure both.
