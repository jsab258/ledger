## Facts

- **Price.** ONS average retail price of cod fillets (series CZOL), 1990: 574p/kg, about £2.60/lb (January £2.42, December £2.85; 1988 £2.08). https://www.ons.gov.uk/economy/inflationandpriceindices/timeseries/czol/mm23 (OPENED, data file). No 1990 figure was found for other fish.
- **Per lb was legal** for loose goods until 1 January 2000. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metrication_in_the_United_Kingdom (OPENED).
- **The window.** Butchers, fishmongers and greengrocers favoured large sash windows over white marble slabs, tilted towards the passer-by, with drains; tiles were made for these trades. Building Our Past, 28 November 2016: https://buildingourpast.com/2016/11/28/a-spotters-guide-to-historic-butchers-shops/ (OPENED; historic fittings, not dated 1990).
- **A London shop.** Steve Hatt, Islington: the wet fish counter "at the very front"; tiles "put in during the early 1990s"; it smoked its own haddock, mackerel, cod and trout. Islington Faces, 18 September 2013: https://www.islingtonfacesblog.com/2013/09/18/steve-hatt/ (OPENED).
- **Slab and ice.** Fish goes on marble or impervious tiles, cooled with ice. Seafish guide (modern): https://www.seafish.org/media/xlpfbfah/guide-to-food-safety-management-for-fishmongers-full-pack.pdf (SNIPPET).
- **Plastic grass** is "the classic UK butcher counter look" per an undated supplier blog: https://msbutcherequipment.co.uk/blogs/blog/separation-in-transparent-plexi-for-showcase (SNIPPET). Nothing ties it to 1990 fish shops.
- **No source established** the scales, till, floor, lighting, chiller, counter material or shopfront paint.

## What the room and the window must have

- Glazed window (a sash raised in trading hours, or fixed glass); behind it, a tilted, drained white slab (marble or tiles) under crushed ice (sourced).
- Fish in rows, each ticketed per lb; cod fillet about £2.60/lb (price sourced; tickets mine: hand-lettered card or plastic stick-in).
- Cod, haddock, plaice, smoked haddock, kippers, mackerel; crabs and prawns in trays (mine; smoked fish from Hatt).
- White glazed wall tiles (sourced). Plastic grass between trays: possible, weakly sourced.
- Mine, to check against photographs 1 to 3: a white enamel or steel counter, a dial or electronic price-computing scale, a wooden chopping block, bare fluorescent tubes, a wet quarry-tile or vinyl floor, a cash register, a chiller at the back, a sign-written fascia.
- No alcohol and no children in any sign or picture.
