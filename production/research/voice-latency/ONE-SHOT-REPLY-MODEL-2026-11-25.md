## Cost per evening (estimate)
Assumptions: 3 hours an evening (no record sets it); 15 to 60 replies an hour (N23-3-QUESTIONS, Cost); about 8,000 tokens in a reply (IN-GAME, run B), at most 300 out; Sonnet 5.5 US$2/US$10 per million, read 24 Nov (PRICES, table).
- Per line with checks: bench floor US$0.0057 (N23-3-QUESTIONS, Cost); in-game ceiling about US$0.021 (IN-GAME, run E review: about US$0.31 an hour at 15).
- Every line on Sonnet 5.5: 45 lines US$0.26-0.95; 180 lines US$1.03-3.78. Fits US$5; at the cap the game brushes off (friends-build/MAP.md).
