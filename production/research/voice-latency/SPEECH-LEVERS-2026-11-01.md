# Speech: every lever, measured or ruled (1 November 2026)

His order of 2 November, 3.1: "research every lever: the fact check is the bottleneck; checking while the reply is written; prepared openers that need no check; the fast checker's reach; study A. Two tries. The bar is 2.0 s." The bar, as phase 1's exit writes it (PLAN.md): the **median** first meaningful speech two seconds or less, no prepared openings (his ruling of 7 October).

## Where the time goes now (REMEASURE-2026-11-01.md: 60 lines beside the packaged game, the cast's Pocket voices)

- Median first sound about **1.17 s**; about two thirds of lines under 2.0 s. **The bar is met on this measure**; the tester's real-talk run inside the packaged game is the check that counts (next morning's build).
- Two groups: the fast checker clears the first sentence (about 55% tonight) and it is heard at about 0.9 to 1.2 s; or Haiku must judge it, about 1.1 to 1.25 s after it is written, and it is heard at about 2.0 s. A few redrafts reach 3 to 8 s.
- The voice is no longer in the way: Pocket on the processor makes the first sound 0.1 to 0.2 s after the sentence (POCKET-SWITCH-2026-10-26.md).

## What follows

- The median meets the bar; the share under 2.0 s (about two thirds) is the shortfall to name at the exit.
- The second try, if spent, goes at Haiku's own time (a shorter answer, the prompt cached), measured the same way: old against new, alternating, 40 lines or more an arm.
