## The cause (a free run with the stand-in, LEDGER_TALK_SIZES; F:/LedgerTools/latency/sizes-2026-11-02/)

- The requests are the same size beside the game and in it: the reply about 23,200 characters, the check about 13,050.
- **The model differs.** Beside the game the reply went to Haiku 4.5 (small talk); in the game every reply went to Sonnet 5 (real conversation). The talk program makes a moment real conversation when "a deed or evidence comes with the line" (TalkMoment.Of, his ruling D48: the model follows the kind of moment). The game sends the window's evidence with every line, `"held":false` and nothing near when the person holds nothing of it (CrimeProbe.cpp EvidenceFor), and the talk program counted any evidence record (Program.cs, `evidenceSent`). latency.py sends none.
- **So 1 November's 1.17 s was the small-talk model's**, not what the game does: wrong, and corrected here.
