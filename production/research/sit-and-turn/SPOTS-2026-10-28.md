## 1. The two games

### Mafia (Unreal 5.4)

**A seat is a Smart Object actor with slot settings** (R).
- Source: mafia/exports/.../SharedWIs/UniversalWIs/BP_WISOSitChair.json.
- The component points at a shared definition (DA_WILoopingSoloSODef): one slot at the origin, behaviour an empty Gameplay Interactions state tree.
- A chair mesh is a child actor, and a spawner names who sits.
- Per slot: name, animation data, timeout (−1), cooldown, "return after interruption", "occupied once", docking mode, transforms.

**Entry point** (M, mafia_slots.py over all 478 slots).
- The chair's *character start* is 47.1 cm in front of the slot, facing as the seated man faces. Its root-motion end is −47.1 cm, and its docking is "slot as animation end".
- So the sitter arrives 47 cm out, already facing away, and the stand-to-sit clip backs him on.
- Other seats: 40 cm, 60 cm, and 88 cm to the side, turned 85 degrees.
- 96 of 126 recorded starts are zero. Study 1 says 374 scenes carry their own person, who appears when the scene streams in; that these seats are among them is I.
- Docking: none 352, slot as animation end 98, slot to a dock bone 15, slot as start 11, other 2.
- Fly mode on 52; **leg IK kept on in 477 of 478**; "ignore root bone transform" on 476 (meaning not read).
- Mafia's own warp windows on these clips, and how a walker reaches the start, were not read (U).

**Clips** (R, DA_AD_SitChairM → DA_AM_SItChairM).
- An Entry, loop sections picked at random by weight (two idles at 3, two fidgets and a "clean" at 2), an Exit and a FastExit.
- 16 of 386 sets are "NoEntry" twins.
- Users: an empty filter; the only precondition is "unless inactive". Reservation is Epic's claim.

**Doors** (M).
- For walkers, a navigation link from +70 to −70 cm.
- The hero's 28 door-opening clips (push or pull × near or far handle, by stance and mood) are all left-hand; mirroring for the right is I. The lock-pick clips come in both hands.
- The door actor's interface was not exported.

### Kingdom Come II (CryEngine)

**A seat is a class of helpers** (R, Libs/SmartObjects.xml, 279 classes).
- "Place0" is the seat: category, links to entry helpers (front, back, left, right) and the seated idle.
- Each entry lists animations with tags, users (NPC-only or player-only) and options: skip the navigation test ("OmmitNav" 95 times, "OmitNav" 22), skip the physics test ("OmmitPhys" 157), strict navmesh tolerance, project the end onto the navmesh, "requires all fragments valid", "alternatives".
- Reservation is in the schedule links: 2,254 cap users (1,694 at one), and 829 may push the user off (Study 1).

**Each approach is precomputed from its clip** (M, Kuttenberg's smartobjecthelpersetanimations.xml, kcd_helpers.py).
- Each record holds the start transform relative to the seat, the clip's root movement, an "aligned" flag and the navmesh tolerance.
- **The start is the clip's own movement reversed.** Example: front sit-down starts 1.26 m out and moves 1.28 m, turning 179 degrees inside the clip.
- The low bench has 8 approach directions, starting 0.23 to 2.54 m out. The data does not give sector widths: the start headings are uneven (I).
- A 0.25 m variant starts facing away and only backs down.
- Stand-ups are never aligned. They start on the seat, or 0.04 to 0.17 m off it, and move:
  - 0.33 to 0.99 m forward;
  - 0.67 to 1.25 m back over the bench;
  - 1.04 to 2.39 m to the side or diagonally.

**Placed spots are tested in their level, offline** (M, tables/ai/smartobjectanimations.xml).
- For 19,758 placed objects, per clip and layer, a drop reason is kept: "Nav" (start or end off the navmesh) or "Physics".
- Base layer: 32,898 navigation drops, 5,926 physics drops, 729 explicit passes. That a missing record means a pass is I.
- **The test runs unless the entry opts out, and every door stage skips the physics test.**
- What "Physics" sweeps (the clip's path, or only its ends) is not in the data (I). That a bench by a wall thereby loses its wall-crossing approaches is I; no placed bench was checked.

**Timing** (M, kcd_male_database.adb, anim_fragment.xml, kcd_adb.py).
- Sit-down:
  - 98 options, 96 aligned, median 4.35 s;
  - blend in median 0.2 s (0.2 to 1.0), out 0.2 s;
  - the alignment layer starts at 0 s, and its blend field is median 1.0 s (0.2 to 2.6). That this field is the window's length is I; that the correction ends before the hips drop is U;
  - ground alignment off in 65 of 71;
  - collider "pushes players only" in 62 of 68.
- Stand-up: 205 options, none aligned, median 2.9 s, blends 0.2 s.

**Doors** (M).
- Helpers sit 0.7 m either side of the doorway.
- There are three stages per side (open in, close out, open out), approached straight, from the left or from the right.
- 96 door records start 0.66 to 2.44 m out. All are aligned on strict tolerance, and all skip the physics test.
- The leaf's own database (kcd_slaveDoorLeft_male.adb) holds only a locked-door shake (two clips). How the leaf moves when opened was not found (U).

**What the two agree on** (M): a spot is an anchor plus entry points, and each entry point is the clip's own start. Kingdom Come II adds an early alignment (I), 0.2 s sit blends, and an offline test with opt-outs. Mafia's warp is unread (U), and its often-quoted 0.2 s is its walking state machine's fade, not a sit blend.

## 2. Unreal 5.8 on this PC

The project enables SmartObjects, GameplayInteractions (which brings ContextualAnimation, StateTree and NavCorridor), MotionWarping, PoseSearch and Chooser (LedgerProbe.uproject). Build.cs links MotionWarping and PoseSearch.

**Smart Objects** (no Beta or Experimental flag).
- A definition holds slots: offset, rotation, user tag filter, activity tags, preconditions, behaviours and annotations.
- **A definition can be built at runtime** (DebugAddSlot, GetMutableSlot, Validate), but Epic marks the adding for testing. An editor asset is the supported way (SmartObjectDefinition.h).
- The subsystem finds, claims, finds an entrance, occupies (returning the behaviour) and frees a slot, with a callback if the slot is lost. Epic's overview page gives this flow and says agents release their own slots.
- **Entrance annotations** are Unreal's entry points: offset, rotation, tags, entry and exit flags, priority, a ground trace, and an optional straight test, a 10 cm capsule from 80 cm up at the entrance to 40 cm up at the slot.
- FindEntranceLocationForSlot also projects onto the navmesh (within 5 × 5 × 40 cm) and checks a user capsule of 35 × 180 cm (SmartObjectTypes.h).
- A straight test cannot see a curved clip path (I).

**Gameplay Interactions** (Experimental) runs a slot's behaviour as a state tree (find slot, find entrance, play montage or contextual anim).

**Motion Warping** (Beta; Epic's page motion-warping-in-unreal-engine).
- Named targets live on a component; a clip's window bends its root motion toward its target.
- Modifiers: Skew Warp (codeable with AddRootMotionModifierSkewWarp), Scale, and the Experimental Precomputed Warp ("only suitable for stationary targets").
- **With a warp point taken from a bone, the target is where that bone lands, not the root.** The root is placed at its animated offset from the bone (RootMotionModifier.cpp, `CachedOffsetFromWarpPoint * WarpPointTransformGame`).
- "Subtract remaining root motion" is read only by Skew Warp (RootMotionModifier_SkewWarp.cpp), not by Precomputed Warp.
- Characters have an adapter built in; plain actors, which our townspeople are, need one.

**Contextual Animation** (Experimental; already enabled through Gameplay Interactions; scene asset headers; vorixo.github.io/devtricks/contextual-anim, updated 1 March 2024).
- Roles and sections (enter, loop, exit) hold anim sets, one clip per role, chosen by polygon, cone (200 cm, 45 degrees) or distance.
- Alignment tracks are generated in the editor. At runtime, TryCreateBindings then StartContextualAnimScene, with Motion Warping inside.
- For one person on a still bench it adds a module dependency and editor assets, no new capability (I).

**Pose Search and the chooser.**
- UPoseSearchLibrary::MotionMatch (Experimental) returns clip, start time and cost.
- **But the sample's distance term reads its chooser's context**: the Blueprint struct SmartObjectSelectionInputs (TargetDistance, TargetAngle; DistanceToSmartObject's names). MotionMatch alone leaves the term empty.
- Evaluating the chooser from C++ needs an FChooserEvaluationContext carrying that struct, built by reflection (ChooserFunctionLibrary.h). This is untried (U).

**Minimal bench with Smart Objects, in C++:** a definition made by an editor script from the seat file (the editor asset); SmartObjectsModule in Build.cs; a C++ actor with the component (SetDefinition before registering); then find, claim, entrance, walk, occupy, warp target, In/Loop/Out, free.

## 3. What makes a sit look right

**Epic's Game Animation Sample**, read from the names in its assets (F:/LedgerTools/GameAnimationSample, Blueprints/SmartObjects, ustrings.py; values need the editor).
- The bench has two slots with entrances, users NPC or Player.
- Its chooser picks among ten approach montages (front, left 90, right 90, behind-left and behind-right, each foot), by target angle and then a **pose search**. The search scores the feet's position and velocity, and the distance to the clip's **"attach" bone** (the bench marker carried in the clip).
- STT_PlayAnimFromBestCost runs each tick while walking and plays only below a cost threshold, otherwise it "will try again next frame". It reads the navigation path length.
- **So the start falls where feet and distance both match, often mid-clip.**
- Each montage's precomputed warp (ease in-out) takes its warp point from the "attach" bone, **so the bench marker lands on the slot**.
- From our log (sit9.log): the front left-foot clip is 10.87 s long, warps from 0 to 3.10 s, and its root then travels 23.4 cm.

**Two shipped ways:** the *walk-in*, where approach, turn and sit are one clip (Kingdom Come II, the sample), and *stop, face away, back down* from 25 to 60 cm (Mafia, Kingdom Come II's 0.25 m variant). Arrival tolerances and the sample's cost threshold were not found (U).

## 5. The method for LEDGER

**Choices, to record as decisions.**
- Spots live in production/specs/seats.json, which stays their one home.
- Reservation stays with the simulation (CastDay).
- **Smart Objects are not used for Tom**: the simulation already owns reservation, and the sample's entry needs a player state tree the puppet lacks (WALK-2026-10-25.md). But Mafia runs all 478 slots on them, and FindEntranceLocationForSlot already does part of step 4, so they are the likely route for the townspeople.
- Contextual Animation waits for a two-actor act.
- Motion Warping and Pose Search's clips are used.

**Steps and checks** (thresholds marked "ours" are LEDGER's own, I):

1. **Spot data.** Each spot holds:
   - an anchor (floor point under the seat bone, facing, seat height);
   - slots with a cap of 1, and who may use them;
   - per slot: In clips by side and foot, weighted Loops, Outs and the exit.

   *Check (spec test):* each slot has at least one In and one Out; each anchor lies inside its furniture's floor box (mickeys-office.json) and at least 20 cm from its ends; anchors are at least 50 cm apart (ours).

2. **Clip sheet**, measured once by editor script. Per clip:
   - root travel and turn;
   - the warp window, its warp bone, and that bone's offset from the root at the window's end;
   - the pelvis's offset from the warp bone when seated;
   - per frame, the planted foot and stance phase (contact curves, or ball within 1.5 cm of its lowest for 4 frames, as clip_feet_all.py);
   - lowest head and greatest neck bend.

   *Check:* seated pelvis within ±3 cm of seat height.

3. **Entries derived.** The warp target is the slot, where the warp bone lands. The entry is the root's place at the chosen start, worked back through the bone offset and the root travel.

   *Check (not circular):* play the In clip from the derived entry with the warp off. Hips within 5 cm of the seat point and ±3 cm in height.

4. **Room test of what will be played.** Play each entry from its derived start and from the four corners of the start envelope that step 6 may use: ±15 cm along, ±10 cm across, ±20 degrees (ours). Do the same for each Out to its exit. Sample feet, shins, knees, hips and hands every 1/60 s against collision. Starts must lie within 5 cm of the navmesh, with a 35 × 180 cm capsule clear.

   *Check:* no contact deeper than 1 cm on any kept entry; failures are dropped and listed.

5. **Choose.** The simulation grants the slot. Take the kept entry whose direction is nearest Tom's approach heading.

   *Check:* heading difference no more than 20 degrees; dropped entries used: 0.

6. **Approach and start.**
   - The approach path: E within reach (160 cm, as now) hands the mover to the spot. It is steered along a navigation path toward the entry. When the path's remaining length falls inside the clip's travel, a start is sought each frame.
   - The start is chosen Kingdom Come II's way, as the project's chosen stops already are (-TomStopChosen, SliceCharacter.cpp). From a table of moments in the side's clips, take one with the same planted foot, stance phase within ±10 points, and remaining travel equal to the path's within ±10 cm.
   - The sample's chooser, fed its context from C++, is the alternative (U).
   - Blend 0.2 to 0.25 s.

   *Check:* path found, its last metre within 20 degrees of the entry's heading; the start inside step 4's envelope, or keep walking a frame. With no start by the entry point, fall back to stand-still: turn on the spot, then Ron's backward sit-down.

7. **Warp the approach only.**
   - The sample's clips: land the attach bone on the slot.
   - Skew-warped root clips: subtract the remainder.
   - Ground alignment off while fitted (KCD2). Foot lock off is I.
   - The capsule ignores only the bench.

   *Check:*
   - the warp stays inside the tested envelope;
   - the bone within 2 cm and 3 degrees at the window's end;
   - hips within 5 cm, ±3 cm in height;
   - planted feet slide no more than 3 cm.

8. **Loop** by weight. *Check:* pelvis steady within 1 cm.

9. **Out** unwarped, along a tested exit (stand-ups travel up to 2.4 m); release the slot. *Check:* no contact; slide no more than 3 cm.

10. **Judge.** Numbers; pictures at the game camera every 1/60 s round each contact, beside the sample's mannequin in **its own level**, with a fresh reviewer per view; then the packaged build, the AI tester and RECIPE.md.

**Later uses.** Townspeople take spots through their schedule (with a warping adapter or a Character body). Doors are spots with two sides at 70 cm; how the leaf opens is still to be found.
