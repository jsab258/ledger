# Walking, turning and sitting a MetaHuman in UE 5.8: the method

Research for item 1.2, 6 October 2026. Marks: OPENED = page read today. DISK = a file on this PC read today. SNIPPET = search summary only. UNREACHED = could not be opened, so not evidence. "Mine" = my inference, untested. Nothing was built or run in Unreal.

## 1. What studios do

1. **List the moves first**: walk loop, start, stop, turn in place, sit down, sit idle, sit talking, stand up. Each is carried onto the body and judged in the game camera (the 30 September townspeople research; Epic's Lyra page, OPENED).
2. **Retarget offline, once per body**, with Epic's IK Retargeter. Its 5.8 op stack has Pelvis Motion, Root Motion (source "Copy From Source Root" or "Generate From Target Pelvis"), Floor Constraint, Speed Plant and Stride Warp, and 5.8 adds a Foot Definition (OPENED: op stack page, release notes, "Fix Foot Sliding" page).
3. **Walking without sliding** is done one of three ways. (a) Root motion moves the body, so feet match by construction. (b) Code moves the body and the clip is matched to it: play rate to speed, Stride Warping, Orientation Warping, and Distance Matching for starts, stops and pivots. Lyra does distance matching into the start, then stride warping as the jog arrives (OPENED). (c) Motion matching, which is heavy, and whose Epic sample is NoAI. Stride and Orientation Warping need an IK Rig and IK foot bones (OPENED, DISK).
4. **Turn in place**: in Lyra the actor follows the controller yaw while the root is counter-rotated ("Root Yaw Offset") so planted feet stay still; a turn clip is picked by angle and by how long the player waited; the clips carry root motion and a baked turn-yaw curve (OPENED).
5. **Sitting**: the chair carries a reference point. The actor walks to an entry point; a sit-down clip with root motion plays while Motion Warping bends its translation and rotation so the pelvis lands on the seat point; the seated loop is held there; standing up is warped to an exit point. Smart Objects supply "claim a slot, play a montage" (OPENED). Epic's Contextual Animation plugin does the same with Motion Warping inside (a March 2024 third-party article, OPENED). EA's 2021 GDC talk uses locators as shared reference points (abstract only, OPENED).
6. **Check by number, then by eye**: stance-foot speed over the ground, seat contact.

## 2. What UE 5.8 on this PC gives (all DISK)

- **Motion Warping** is Beta. On a Character it works out of the box (an adapter hooks the movement component). Any other actor needs a hand-written adapter (the base class is abstract). A static call, `AddRootMotionModifierSkewWarp`, warps translation and rotation toward a named target from code, with no editor notify; the warp point may come from a bone.
- **Animation Warping** (Stride, Orientation, Foot Placement) is stable. **Animation Locomotion Library** (Distance Matching) is Beta, and its calls take Blueprint node references, so a native instance cannot call them as they stand (mine). **Contextual Animation** and **Gameplay Interactions** are Experimental. Smart Objects carries no flag.
- **Root motion** is a flag on each sequence; a montage takes it from its sequences. A native anim instance defaults to "root motion from montages only" (AnimInstance.cpp). For a Character the movement component consumes it. For any other actor the code must call the mesh's `ConsumeRootMotion` and move the actor (mine, from the headers).
- A slot node registers itself with the instance when it starts (AnimNode_Slot.cpp). A montage can be made from any sequence in code (`PlaySlotAnimationAsDynamicMontage`). A single-node instance plays one asset and cannot blend or layer: fine for a held pose, useless for walking.
- **The MetaHuman plugin's clips** (UEFNAnimPreset/Locomotion): walk and run loops, starts and stops, one stand idle, on the cast's own skeleton. The loops carry sync markers (probably the feet's; step 1 reads them); starts and stops are named for the foot (Start_F_Rfoot, Stop_F_Lfoot). There is no turn in place and no sitting. All carry the root-motion property; its value needs the editor (mine: probably on).
- **The cast skeleton** has `root` and `pelvis` and no `ik_foot` bones (its name table). A May 2025 forum answer says the same; the workaround is virtual bones.

## 3. How this project drives animation

No Animation Blueprint exists. `ULedgerPersonAnim` and `ULedgerLocomotionAnim` are native instances whose proxy supplies its own node chain (sequence players, two-way blends, Look At, curve modifiers); the decisions are C++. That is sound and stays. To add montages, put one `FAnimNode_Slot` at the end of the chain (mine, untried; the 1 October note also left it unverified). Keep the state logic in C++; I found no documented way to build a state machine node by hand (mine).

**The cast** are plain Actors (BP_MH_*), not Characters. Each part on the idle's skeleton runs its own instance; garments follow the body by leader pose; the visual is snapped to a hidden simulation body every frame (`SyncVisual`). **Tom** is a Character with a Mixamo body, idle, walk and run blended by speed.

**What it lacks:**
- No slot, montage, root-motion or inertialisation code anywhere.
- No walk, start, stop, turn or sit on any MetaHuman; the plugin's walk is used only to find its skeleton.
- `SyncVisual` would overwrite any scripted move.
- No seat data: the marks list holds "the radio's chair" and Sheila's desk position but no seat height or facing.
- MotionWarping and AnimationWarping are not in the uproject or Build.cs.
- The cook list takes only the plugin's Technical_Loops/Idle folder, so the locomotion clips would be missing from a package (mine; the stand idle already is).
- The person export drops every hip position key, so a clip taken that way loses a sit-down's drop (person-export.py). Sit and turn clips must come from the original FBX files.
- The walkers move at a fixed 1.2 m/s whatever the clip travels.

## 4. Mixamo on a MetaHuman: root and feet

- A Mixamo rig has no root bone; the hips are the top (the repo's clip tool; a forum thread; a vendor page). The MetaHuman has `root`. The Root Motion op's "Generate From Target Pelvis" may make root motion from the pelvis, so a normal download needs no Blender root fix (mine; untested for sit-downs).
- **"In Place" removes the travel.** Right for loops the code moves; wrong for sit down, stand up and turns, where the body's travel is the point (the vendor page needs both versions to make root motion; the forum says keep root motion). Jafar should download Stand To Sit and Sit To Stand with In Place off (mine).
- **Read today from the project's FBX files** (`tools/clip-motion.py`, hips): Walking 1.56 m in 0.97 s (1.61 m/s; the player's 1.6 matches). Female Walk 1.22 m in 1.20 s (1.02 m/s). Running covers 1.52 m/s, 2.28 at its 1.5 rate, while he runs at 4.2 m/s: the feet cover about half the ground (mine). Three readings look wrong for their names and need an eye on a body before use: Sitting Idle's hips stay at 92 to 101 cm (standing; Sitting Talking's are 55 to 56), Standing Turn Right 90 moves 2.05 m in a second and turns 14 degrees (the Left turns 89 degrees and moves 26 cm), and Female Stop Walking moves 2.37 m in 0.8 s.
- At retarget, plant feet with Speed Plant from a Motion Extractor curve on the ball bones, plus Foot Definition and Floor Constraint (OPENED). One retargeter per body build. `tools/ue/retarget_metahuman_idle.py` already runs the batch (commit 5a9a4556); it imports a GLB, so it needs an FBX source for these clips.

## 5. Recommended method for this project

1. **Walk**: the plugin's loops, starts and stops, no retarget. A steady walk is moved by code at each loop's measured speed (route speeds set from the clip, play rate 0.9 to 1.1). Starts and stops play as root-motion clips; the stop is the one named for the foot the loop's markers say lands next.
2. **Turn**: the head first (the existing Look At, 60 degree clamp); past about 60 degrees held half a second (mine; Lyra uses angle plus wait), a Mixamo 90 plays as a root-motion clip that rotates the actor, warped to the exact angle; two for 180.
3. **Sit**: seat and exit marks as data beside the office marks. Walk to the entry mark, turn to the chair's axis, play Stand To Sit warped so the pelvis lands on the seat point, hold Sitting Idle or Sitting Talking at that anchor (talking while the voice's speaking flag is set), then Sit To Stand warped to the exit mark.
4. **One native instance with a slot, and one small driver** that consumes the mesh's root motion, warps it and moves the actor. Own warp (about forty lines, testable as plain maths) in preference to enabling a Beta plugin and writing an adapter; Epic's component becomes the better choice if the cast become Characters (mine).
5. **Gate**: a script measures stance-foot slip and seat contact in a packaged run; the tester walks it; a fresh reviewer judges in the game camera.

**Risks**: Tom's controls gain a start delay (his feel, to be read in step 1); hands and forearms may pass through the desk or chair arms; seat height 0.423 m may not match the clips' seat (the pelvis offset fixes the hips; feet may need Two Bone IK, whose node is already in AnimGraphRuntime).

| 4 | Tom: his MetaHuman body in the player character on the same instance (after his face and body exist) | 4 to 6 |
| 5 | Turn: the two Mixamo turns retargeted from FBX, threshold state, warp to angle | 6 to 8 |

About 36 to 50 hours. Step 2 is the one unknown; if root motion will not run through the native chain in two tries, the fallback is scripted moves with in-place clips and a code-driven hip drop (the Blender sit route), reported as such.

## Step 2, measured (6 October, evening)

The move slot (PersonAnim.h, PlayMove) and the driver ran in the game (-MoveProof): Sheila played Epic's AS_MH_Neutral_Walk_Start_F_Rfoot through the slot as a dynamic montage, its whole 3.2 s, root motion read from her body each frame. The clip itself carries no travel: its root bone moves 0.0 cm over its length (root-motion flag off as shipped; switched on, still 0). So the MetaHuman plugin's locomotion clips are authored in place, the travel in the pelvis, and section 2's "probably on" is wrong. The slot is proved; feet-true starts, stops, turns and sits need clips with root motion made where the method says, at retargeting (the IK Retargeter's Root Motion op, "Generate From Target Pelvis"), from the plugin's clips and the Mixamo FBX files alike. Step 3 starts there.
