## Part 1: the first five spans

  - Probably not seen from the game camera.
  - The measure's 15.5 cm is somewhat high; the rest likely comes from the earlier stop.
- **15.3–16.3 (180° pivot while walking):**

- **20.4–21.2 (the 45° turn back):** the stances hold within 0.4 to 0.9 cm. The listed 21.12 step (10.4 cm) is a swinging foot whose toe passes within about 1 cm of the floor. Nothing to see.

**Does the measure count things a player would not call sliding? Yes, in three ways:**
