# Review: TOOLCHAIN-CORE-DESIGN-2026-11-13 (fresh reviewer, 13 Nov 2026)

Settled by the builder, 13 Nov: every finding applied in the note (port list with garment/ and the right files, the fuller cloth trait, 79 commands, 0.8 s, game items and hours per milestone, the 15% cap, a risks section, the Drape Studio interface as his licence question).

**Verdict: pass with fixes.** The base, the judge outside the tool and the slots are sound and most Seamshift claims hold. But the cloth slot leaves out ClothCraft's pattern-to-mesh path, points at the wrong files for easing and body inspection, and cannot carry the solver's real inputs. Two counts are wrong, and the ruling's per-module hours and 15% cap are missing.

1. **The pattern-to-mesh path is missing from the port list.** VbdSolver::new takes `Arc<CompiledGarment>`, `Option<&SurfaceMesh>` and `State` (ClothCraft src/vbd.rs:181-184). CompiledGarment comes from `GarmentTemplate::compile` (src/garment/template.rs:154, 244). That step uses garment/meshing.rs, seam.rs and embedding.rs, and the solver also needs model.rs (State), energy.rs and math.rs (Jet). Seamshift refits existing meshes and has no pattern mesher. Fix: add garment/{template,meshing,seam,embedding,polygon}.rs, model.rs, energy.rs and math.rs to item 5.
2. **Two files are misnamed.** Easing lives in garment/seam.rs (`allow_ease`, lines 51 and 355). vbd/seam_blocks.rs is "Experimental coupled sewing-component refinement" (line 1). Body inspection is avatar/diagnostics.rs (line 1). avatar.rs is the "Static OBJ avatar" collider (line 1). The start check is OgcWorld::validate_start (ogc/world.rs:392), so that one is right. Fix: correct those names.
3. **The trait cannot carry the solver or the serve commands.** `load/step/positions/diagnostics` has no settings (VbdSettings: dt, iterations, damping, gravity, contact; vbd.rs:24-30). It has no Result (`step` returns `Result<VbdMetrics,String>`, vbd.rs:345-350), and no pins or holds, freeze, particle distance, shrinkage, elastic, extra thickness or pose. The jumper job alone calls SetParticleDistance and SetSimulationQuality (garments/tom/jumper/job_j3.py). The body is a static `SurfaceMesh` with no bones or pose (avatar.rs:10-13). Trims are "annotations only; they do not create physical fasteners" (template.rs:165), so `set_zipper` and `fasten` for the zip jacket have no engine behind them. Fix: `load(body, garment: GarmentSpec, settings) -> Result`, `step(n) -> Result<Metrics>`, with pins, freeze and per-piece mesh size in GarmentSpec. List the commands the three jobs call, map each to the spec, and write zipper and fastener as a named gap (M3 risk).
4. **The serve count is 79, not 85.** `Session::handle` matches 79 distinct command names (DrapeStudio src/serve.rs:131-1110, `other =>` at 1121). Fix: say 79, or drop the number.
5. **The ruling's terms are missing.** RULINGS.md:37 asks every piece to name its game item and the hours it saves, with "at most 15% of weekly usage". Items 1-5 and the front end name no game item or hours, milestones M1-M6 carry no hours, and the 15% cap is not stated. Fix: one clause per part and per milestone, and the cap stated under "Why".
6. **Times cited wrongly.** Seamshift round 1 was 0.8 s per garment per body (SEAMSHIFT-ROUND1:7, 26); "2 s" is the per-job ceiling (line 43). The 3.2 h is the "whole chain" (line 26), not stated as per garment per body. Fix: "0.8 s (under 2 s a job)", "3.2 h whole chain".
7. **The CPU speed risk is not measured.** The solver is f64 with exact local Hessians, dt 1/240 and 16 iterations by default (vbd.rs:1, 33-35). ClothCraft's only production timings are GPU (17-19 ms/step for a T-shirt on an RTX 3090, docs/adaptive-initialization.md:42). Its CPU figures are 16-vertex samples (docs/bounded-contact-search.md:114-116). M2's 186 s bar on a 14,447-point jumper is unproven. Fix: before porting, time 100 steps of a jumper-sized mesh on this CPU; if the projection exceeds 186 s, state GPU or a lower step count up front.
8. **The start check against layering and refits.** validate_start refuses any initial intersection or touching (ogc/world.rs:439, 453). "Solved together" needs an arrangement that starts the jacket clear of the jumper, and refitted inputs must be free of overlap. Fix: name this in item 5 and give M4 a starting-clearance step.
9. **Licence of the front end.** Drape Studio's MECHANICS.md records Ghidra decompilation of Marvelous's API ("Decompiled, round 2", line 72 on), and compat.py mirrors that API. "It never ships" does not answer whether the copy is allowed: Marvelous's terms are on the allowlist (item 9) only for making garments. Fix: put this to him as a licence question before compat.py is copied, with option B as a clean job format of our own.
10. **Determinism and fabrics.** Rayon runs in the solver (vbd.rs:466; local_step.rs:157; ogc/world.rs:566). The byte-identical claim needs a test at 1 and N threads. fabrics.rs has nine presets, "authored approximations, not calibrated" (lines 1-2, 42-133), with no rib knit, although the jumper uses Rib Knit (DRAPESTUDIO-TEST:35). Fix: add both.
11. **Small points.** The judge folder (F:/LedgerTools/tool-dev/judge) does not exist yet; write it as "to be made". Per-piece worst stretch and hem shape already exist in Seamshift (checks.rs:624, 632-667); keep those as Drape Studio's additions only. "59 preset values" could not be checked in the sources given; mark it unverified. Length: 1,040 words, within two pages.
