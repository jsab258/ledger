Brief 2 of 2 for the AI-video motion experiment (advisor helper's web research, 9 Nov; save beside brief 1). EPIC'S MARKERLESS PLUGIN. Footage rules (https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/metahuman/metahuman-animator-video-capture-guidelines-in-unreal-engine): HD or 4K at 60 fps, H.264 CRF 18 or better; whole body head to feet with margin; camera straight on, neutral height, never moving; even diffused light, no motion blur; performer distinct from background, clear floor line, matte floor; fitted clothes, shoes visible; one performer; 'Nothing should block any part of the performer's body during the take, including props, furniture'. So shape the three shots to that: no furniture hiding the legs (a side-on stool or open chair, a wall or rail instead of a solid counter), small props only, and expect video models to drop small objects (Bugliarello, CVPR 2026). Our clips are 16 to 24 fps at 480p: test the solve on the native clip and on a copy interpolated to 60 fps and upscaled to HD with an open, commercially licensed interpolator, and record which the solver handles better. Set the same frame rate on the capture data and the image source (users report 0 or 1 fps errors otherwise); split long clips. Hardware: Epic's baseline is 16 cores, 32 GB and an RTX 3070 or RX 6800 XT (https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/metahuman/metahuman-hardware-requirements-in-unreal-engine); an RX 6750 XT owner processes about 3 seconds of animation in about 2 minutes, but only after rolling back to AMD driver 24.6.1, newer RX 6000 drivers crash with 'D3D device removed', an RX 6700 XT owner hit the same (forum thread pages 1 to 3: https://forums.unrealengine.com/t/epic-games-metahuman-animator-markerless-motion-capture-plugin/2729293). A driver rollback changes the whole PC, the game's own frame and memory measures included: do NOT roll back; try the current driver first with short body-only clips and the preview character off (it costs time: https://forums.unrealengine.com/t/ue-5-8-markerless-mocap-processing-benchmark-visualization-object-causes-major-slowdown/2731703); if it crashes, research other fixes once, and only then put the rollback to him as a page question with its effects. Other faults: jumps between processing chunks (a user fix in the thread), no body smoothing by default, Smart App Control blocking the unsigned plugin files (his click if Windows asks).
