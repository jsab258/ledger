ROUTE: ComfyUI portable on AMD's community TheRock nightly PyTorch wheels for gfx1031 (not official: AMD's Windows ROCm list covers RX 7000 and 9000 only). Outline: ComfyUI portable, remove its PyTorch, install the gfx1031 torch, torchvision, torchaudio and rocm-sdk-devel from AMD's nightly multi-arch index (rocm.nightlies.amd.com), run rocm-sdk init, start with the AMD settings of the CS1o guide (https://github.com/CS1o/Stable-Diffusion-Info/wiki/Webui-Installation-Guides ; fork https://github.com/patientx-cfz/comfyui-rocm ; RDNA2 build reports https://habr.com/en/articles/967998 and https://forum.level1techs.com/t/native-vllm-rocm-7-15-runtime-for-rx-6000-rdna2-on-windows-11-26-tflops-fp16-62-tok-s-one-click-install-no-wsl2-rx-6750-xt-gfx1031-verified/254042). Prove it with a 33-frame 480p test first. MODELS: Wan 2.2, Apache 2.0, 'We claim no rights over the your generated contents' (read from a mirror, https://ai.gitcode.com/hf_mirrors/Wan-AI/Wan2.2-TI2V-5B ; confirm on the official page). Either TI2V 5B (smaller, faster, 24 fps) or I2V A14B as Q4 GGUF high- and low-noise files with the 4-step lightx2v LoRAs (their licence unverified: check before use) plus the Apache slow-mo fix LoRA (https://huggingface.co/rzgar/Wan2.2_I2V_LightX2V_Slow-Mo_Fix), offloading to the 32 GB RAM. Mind F's floor before any download; with the time to 13:00, 5B first is the advisor's suggestion, 14B when room and time allow. Stay at 480p (Radeon cards failed at 720p in https://chimolog.co/bto-gpu-wan22-specs/). Estimated 20 to 45 minutes per 5-second 480p clip for 14B, unmeasured. FALLBACKS: stable-diffusion.cpp Vulkan build (Wan 2.2 I2V, first-and-last frame, VACE, LoRAs; Windows Vulkan decode ran out of memory at 480x832, use its low-memory decode, the small decoder or 288x512; https://github.com/leejet/stable-diffusion.cpp/issues/942 , /discussions/868 , /issues/872); then Wan2GP-AMD for Pinokio (says tested on RDNA2). Skip ZLUDA, DirectML, Amuse. Excluded on licence: LTX-2.3 (revenue cap), HunyuanVideo, MiniMax H3. TECHNIQUE (Civitai and Reddit): image to video from a start frame; make the start frame a render of Ron or Sheila from our own Unreal scene in the action's first pose, whole body, feet visible, plain background, portrait 480x832, so the capture sees our proportions; prompt 'static camera, fixed tripod shot', one subject, the motion step by step; first-and-last frame to pin start and end poses; check timing against a real reference (4-step LoRAs cause slow motion, worse at 832x480); pose or depth control needs a real driving video, so not for us; no upscaling; frame interpolation unproven for capture. EVIDENCE: GenMimic (https://arxiv.org/html/2512.05094v2): Wan VACE clips turned into 3D motion were too noisy to use directly; in-place actions worked, steps and turns about 40%, a raised leg 0%. HumanScore (https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2604.20157): best generators 91.1 against 94.3 for real video on body mechanics; foot contact not reliably measurable. So expect the counter lean and the cigarette (in place) to do best, the chair-to-door walk to need foot cleanup; that contrast is part of the result.
