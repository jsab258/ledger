# Review: m3 solve film beside its source (n09-8), 10 Nov 2026

A fresh reviewer read composed frames 1 and 10 to 80 (every 10th), the still sheet, full-size front and side views at frame 40, and enlarged heads, hands and feet at eight frames. Head, nose, hands, toes and heels were tracked on all 81 solve frames, the source's face and hands on every 4th frame. Scale: about 2.9 mm a pixel (1.60 m over 560 px, side view).

The source barely moves: a woman in heels stands straight with both hands cupped at her mouth throughout; the hands rise about 2 cm between frames 25 and 57. The solve is equally still (solver's numbers: every bone under 2.3 degrees, pelvis 1.1 cm).

## Findings

1. **Foot slide: none.** Toes and heels drift at most 4 px (about 1 cm) over 81 frames; front-view feet within 2.4 px. Both feet point toes-down about 25 degrees (side, all frames), which roughly fits the source's heeled shoes but would be wrong in flat shoes. Her right foot (front view, viewer's left) sits 5 px higher and kinks outward at the ankle; the source's feet point straight ahead.
2. **Jitter: very low.** Mean second difference under 1 px for every tracked point; the only flicker is the lowest toe pixel jumping 3 px at frames 11 and 14 (mesh edge).
3. **Legs and posture: wrong.** The source is straight-legged and upright. The solve bends both knees about 20 to 25 degrees, knees forward, hips back, trunk leaning back about 6 degrees (side, all frames). The head centre is about 18 cm behind the middle of the foot and 6 cm behind the heel: a real body would fall backward. Front view: her right shin angles inward. A fixed offset, not drift.
4. **Arms and hands: half right.** Both hands come up together and stay up all clip, as in the source, and the fingertips rise about 5 cm over frames 24 to 56, matching the source's timing. But they never reach the face: side view, fingertips about 18 cm and palms about 12 cm in front of the nose tip, at chin height (all frames); the front view hides the gap. Elbows held out from the body (wider on her left); the source's are tucked in. Fingers: separate, claw-curled and spread, not cupped round a lighter; joint bends plausible, shape wrong.
5. **Head: still and facing roughly forward, as the source.** Head centre and nose tip move under 2.3 px; no turn or nod, and the source shows none. Side-view nose level. Front view: the nose sits about 5 px left of the head's centre, possibly a 10-degree turn to her right; low confidence, as the front camera's alignment is not known.
6. **Shoulders and spine:** static, leaning back with the trunk; no slight hunch toward the hands as in the source.
7. **Minor:** the solver check counts 79 frames, the film info 81, the source 80; settle this before trusting timing.

## Usable after clean-up

Usable: the arm timing and hands-up hold, once the hands are moved 12 to 18 cm back onto the mouth (IK to a face target) and the fingers re-posed. Head: only as "no motion". Not usable: legs, feet, hips and spine; replace with a clean standing base.

**Verdict: NOT USABLE AS IS; after clean-up only the arm timing survives, with the hands moved onto the face by hand. Legs, posture and fingers are wrong; no foot slide and almost no jitter.**
