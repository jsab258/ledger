## Numbers

- Foot slide: under 2 cm on both solved motions; toes down 25 to 45 degrees on both (wrong for flat shoes).
- Jitter: mean frame-to-frame second difference under 1 px on every tracked point.
- Drift: motion 2's head 5 cm back over 81 frames; motion 3's pelvis 1.1 cm.
- Timing: right on both (head turn frames 2 to 75; hands rise frames 24 to 56, as in each clip).
- Cost: one 81-frame clip (3.4 s at 24 fps) takes about 100 minutes of the card at 832x480 (sampling about 95, decode about 5); the solve about 35 to 41 minutes of the processor, about 10 minutes a second of motion. Session time per motion, filming and review included: about 1 hour. No money, no outside service.

## Verdict

The route gives smooth, well-timed upper-body motion (head turns, hand gestures) that needs a clean standing base under it and hand fixes to contact points; it does not give legs, feet, posture or large pose changes. One front camera and a small video model are the limits. Worth keeping for small gestures in talk and idles on top of Epic's and Mixamo's bodies; not worth more card time for whole-body moves. A start frame (image-to-video from a game frame) is the one untried method for large moves, a new try needing research first.
