## Why not the brief's first route
AMD's nightly index (https://rocm.nightlies.amd.com/v2/, read 9 Nov 2026) carries PyTorch wheels for gfx110X (RX 7000) but none for gfx103X (this PC's RX 6700): its gfx103X-all and gfx103X-dgpu folders hold only the ROCm SDK packages. So ComfyUI on ROCm is not open on this card; the brief's first fallback, stable-diffusion.cpp on Vulkan, is taken (its Wan guide: docs/wan.md, read 9 Nov: 33 frames, 480x832, cfg 6, euler, flow shift 3, --offload-to-cpu; temporal and VAE tiling for low memory).
