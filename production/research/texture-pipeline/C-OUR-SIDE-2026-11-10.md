## 12. What we do today

- **Painted timber, trims** (shopfront skill, steps 5 and 6): scan rows (production/specs/ps5-corner.json) tiled on metric UVs at about 512 px a metre; the paint-wood master by Mafia's chipped-paint medians. Edge chips on shared meshes unsolved (no edge mask; two tries each way spent, a known shortfall). Worn paint 2 of 2 tries spent.
- **Unique stained pieces** (kit_stain.py): own unwrap, Cycles bake of position and facing, seeded runs drawn from the design rows. This is a texturing tool's job done by our own script.
- **Garments** (game-garment skill, step 8): Blender bake of normal, occlusion, ID (zip_bake.py); an ID-tinted garment master in Unreal (tint, tape, teeth, grime, wet); knit or weave surface as a shared tiling detail normal (CC0 tile, Poly Haven's knitted_fleece), rib as a second tile by part ID; crowd variants as material values, no new bake.
- **Brick**: the shared brick material with Mafia's world-noise streak tint; Flemish bond is on the waiting list (not built).

## 13. Card memory
- The ceiling (fault D): the walk peaks at 6,041 to 6,192 MB against the 6,000 line (MEMORY-WHERE-2026-11-08 §0). Textures outside streaming hold 2,459 MB at the peak, most of it the game's own runtime-decoded uncompressed pictures (inferred, MEMORY-WHERE §2); streamed textures need only 187 MB of an 800 MB pool. So the streamed pool has room; the non-streamed pictures are the pressure (n09-3's cooking step addresses them).
- Budgets (pre-production/4-BUDGETS.md §4.7): a 2048² colour (BC7), normal (BC5) and packed mask (BC1) set with mips is about 14 MB; 1024² about 3.5 MB. Medium prop 1024² or a shared trim, ≤ 4 MB; large prop ≤ 14 MB; principal's outfit 2048² tiling cloth plus 1024² masks, ≤ 15 MB; building modules carry no textures of their own (shared 4096×2048 trims, 2048² tiles at 512 px a metre); decals on ≤ 4 shared 2048² pages.
- So: a unique 4K set per asset (about 56 MB with mips, BC7/BC5/BC1, inferred from the 14 MB per 2K) breaks every per-asset budget; a unique 2K set fits only large props and outfits; a shared tile with a 1K mask costs the mask alone (about 1 MB at BC1 1K with mips, inferred) per asset.

## 14. Reference first: Mafia and Kingdom Come II
- **Mafia**: a shared library of masters (253 masters, 1,189 instances in Palermo; main master 307 instances with three textures: colour, packed height-roughness-occlusion, normal, plus a tint); walls on a two-layer vertex-painted master; 2,461 decals of 180 materials in Palermo; textures 4096 21%, 2048 36%, 1024 38%; detail normals almost never (studies/05-visuals.md lines 33 to 42; MAFIA-WEAR). Clothes: 1,213 of 1,416 garment materials children of one layered master; colour from hue, saturation, brightness, grime and scratch values over an ID mask (studies/06 lines 60 to 63).
- **Kingdom Come II**: a 256 px decal atlas in 12 slices, 212 decal materials; garments colour 1024, normal 2048 and an ID mask colouring each part; 25 or more colourways from pattern masks (studies/05 line 34; 06 lines 46 to 47).
- What follows for the test: both games put the look in shared masters with masks and per-instance values, and use decals for particular marks; unique per-asset painting is the exception (hero pieces). A texturing tool's per-asset output fits our budget only when it is reduced to a mask (or a 1K set) over a shared tile, which is how each chain should hand into Unreal.
