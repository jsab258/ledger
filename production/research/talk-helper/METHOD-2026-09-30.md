# How live, knowledge-grounded character talk is built, end to end (research note, 30 September 2026)

Asked by Jafar's rule of 30 September: research the whole method before the
problem. A research helper was given the work and the measurement, not a
theory, and capped at about thirty minutes, web only.

The work: in LEDGER a player types a line and a 1990 street character answers
aloud within seconds. Claude writes the answer live (Haiku 4.5 for most
characters), a second model call checks each specific in it, and then a local
voice speaks it. Measured on 30 September with LEDGER's key over 24 turns:
- first words at a median of 2.0 s;
- the whole reply at 5.8 s;
- 2 of 24 turns turned into brush-offs;
- half of a newcomer's first questions ended in "that's all I know" (29 September).

## The pipeline as professionals run it

**1. What the character knows is chosen before writing.**
- Inworld's graph has a Knowledge step and a Knowledge Filter step before the
  reply, and a Safety classifier after it; the runtime runs retrieval in
  parallel ("sub-500 millisecond latency" in one case study).
  - https://docs.inworld.ai/docs/unreal-engine/runtime/templates/character (read 30 Sep 2026)
  - https://inworld.ai/blog/three-challenges-of-realtime-conversational-ai (11 Apr 2025)
- Convai calls its per-turn knowledge retrieval "a guardrailing mechanism".
  https://convai.com/blog/safety-guardrails-with-convai-ai-characters (21 May 2024)
- CHIM, the successor to the Herika Skyrim mod, gates lore by each NPC's
  knowledge classes; with no entry, the NPC "defaults to ignorance".
  https://dwemerdynamics.hostwiki.io/en/Oghma-Infinium-(RAG) (undated)
- Research:
  - Generative agents embellished 1.3% of relationship answers.
    https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03442 (Apr 2023)
  - Tagging each fact with who may see it raised knowledge-boundary fidelity
    by 34.6 points. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25632 (24 Jun 2026)
  - A detective game picks the one fact the character may reveal, then writes,
    then checks only that fact: invented turns fell from 17.8% to 6.3%.
    https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23043 (19 Sep 2026)

**2. The line is written short.**
- Digital Einstein caps a reply at 60 tokens: the model takes 1.16 s, and a
  rewriting call adds 1.03 s. https://arxiv.org/html/2601.01027 (3 Jan 2026)
- Ubisoft's Teammates grounds characters in tagged world objects; its replies
  lag "an extra second or two".
  https://www.aiandgames.com/p/ubisofts-teammates-demo-and-their (26 Nov 2025)
- NVIDIA ACE turns game state into text for small on-device models.
  https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/geforce/news/nvidia-ace-naraka-bladepoint-inzoi-launch-this-month/ (13 Mar 2025)

**3. The check runs alongside, not in series.**
- None of the products found documents a second model fact-checking every
  reply before it is spoken; they check safety after writing.
- NeMo Guardrails checks streamed output in chunks while the text goes out;
  parallel checks made the first chunk 38% to 73% faster.
  https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/stream-smarter-and-safer-learn-how-nvidia-nemo-guardrails-enhance-llm-output-streaming/ (23 May 2025)
- OpenAI's agent guardrails run alongside generation by default.
  https://openai.github.io/openai-agents-python/guardrails/ (read 30 Sep 2026)
- Small checkers exist:
  - MiniCheck, 770M parameters, at GPT-4 level. https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.10774 (Apr 2024)
  - LettuceDetect, 150M to 396M parameters, MIT licence. https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.17125 (Feb 2025)
- Anthropic's advice: allow "I don't know", and remove unsupported claims
  rather than redrafting.
  https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/test-and-evaluate/strengthen-guardrails/reduce-hallucinations (read 30 Sep 2026)

**4. Speech, and the latency budget.**
- A voice-agent primer budgets about 1.2 s voice to voice, with 650 ms to the
  model's first token. https://voiceaiandvoiceagents.com/ (updated Jun 2026)
- Haiku 4.5's first token comes at a median 0.65 s.
  https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/claude-4-5-haiku/providers (read 30 Sep 2026)
- Tail delay is cut by a duplicate "hedged" request.
  https://cacm.acm.org/research/the-tail-at-scale/ (Feb 2013)

**5. When the character cannot answer.**
- Charisma catches unmatched lines with "wildcard" nodes.
  https://docs.charisma.ai/nodes/wildcard-nodes
- Convai answers with a scripted line that redirects.
- CHIM's NPCs are simply ignorant.
- No studio publishes how often its characters fall back.
- Mantella's users report 10 to 15 s before a conversation starts.
  https://github.com/art-from-the-machine/Mantella/releases

## What it says about LEDGER's method (inference)

- **The departure:** in practice the grounding happens before writing. LEDGER
  lets the writer see everything, then vetoes afterwards with a checker whose
  standard is stricter than the field's ("list every specific").
- **The check sits in series:** it costs about a second, and twice on a
  redraft; that fits the 8-second tails.
- **Check alongside:** check sentence one while the voice prepares it; cover
  the wait with an in-character beat.
- **Replace the fixed line** with each character's own "don't know, but…"
  lines that point to something they do know.
- **Money, for Jafar:** a hedged second draft sent at once (more tokens); a
  small local checker (the card, a licence).
