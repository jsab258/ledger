## Candidates, most important first

1. **A15.18 "A usable return to ordinary play after punishment or escape"** (line 704; with A20.15 "A way to recover from an unwinnable checkpoint or stuck state", line 719, and A14.23 "No permanent lock into combat after every threat is gone", line 686). About 5 h.
   Why: a friend seen at the window by somebody he has met is asked about it on day 2. What he types next is "yeah, it was me" or "keep it to yourself". Today neither counts: the person asks again in every reply for the rest of the game, and if the character says "not a word", the town's gossip passes it on anyway, which is a promise the world does not keep.
   Not done: at Suspicious and above the prompt ends every reply with "whatever they said, ask them straight out" (ConversationEngine.cs:296-297), with no memory of having asked; the answers the helper reads (town list ac) are only where he says he was, so an admission or a request changes nothing; `Suspecting.Derive` has no way down for somebody who saw him, so they stay Confronting for good. The Core already has the design's own silence: `Gossiper.Suppressed` ("they still remember, but they won't pass it on"), `GossipMill`'s Contain, and the Bribe, Intimidate and UseHook outcomes (Contained, Backfired; Gossip.cs:918-1140), reached only by the retired Unity layer; the port keeps Suppressed too (Gossip.h:213). The helper returns nothing that suppresses (no "Suppress" in TalkHelper), `Promises.cs`'s shapes and the prompt's promise rule (ConversationEngine.cs:234) leave keeping quiet out, and RouterExamples has no such verb. The work: read a plain admission and a plain ask to keep it quiet (narrow shapes, as `Claims.WhereHeSays` reads a place); the Core decides from what the person already carries (loyalty, nerve, how they know him, the band) whether they agree, and the model is told the outcome; the reply returns "keepsQuiet" with the topic for the game's gossip to contain; once asked and answered, or owned up to, they stop asking and keep the manner. Whether suspicion should also ease over days of good behaviour is canon's ("remediation is behavioral"), for Jafar only if the Core's existing shapes do not settle it.

2. **A15.10 "A chance to respond before an alert spreads, where promised by the design"** (line 696), its second half: what he answered has to be able to catch up with him. About 4 h.
   Why: condition 2 is being "confronted with something they did earlier", and the sharpest case this town can make is a lie found out. Day 2: he tells Ron he was at the chapel; that evening Ron hears from somebody who saw him at Rita's. Today Ron never connects the two, and nobody else ever hears what Tom claimed.
   Not done: the helper judges his answer once, when he gives it, against where that person saw him at that moment (TalkHelper Program.cs:488-518). An answer left "I can't say otherwise" is never judged again: `HeardAnswer` returns nothing unless the answer changes and `ApplyAnswers` reads the stored result, so a later sighting, or hearsay that he was near it (`Suspecting.Nearness.HeardHeWasNear`), does not catch it. The claim never enters the town's talk either: the mill has `PlayerClaims` and the old "Consequence 1", a rumour colliding with what he told the listener (Gossip.cs:414-427, 511), in both the Core and the port, but the helper's "claim" reply goes nowhere. NOTE-2 planned "the carrying goes to the builder"; the 6ac handover line asks the game only to accept "areas". And deeds are filed as player.<deed> = seen, never as where he was at an hour, so a claim about a place could not collide with them anyway. The work: file his answer as a place at the deed's hour, re-judge a held answer whenever that person's evidence changes, carry "he told me he was at the chapel" through the gossip as ordinary talk, with tests and a TownReach count of how often a lie is caught within a game day; the game's side handed over.

3. **A13.21 "People who do not immediately resume cheerful chatter beside an ongoing emergency"** (line 663; with A13.20 "A way for panic to resolve once danger passes", line 662). About 3 h.
   Why: the crime is the loop's centre and the player is standing right there. Glass goes, the street hushes for a second or two, and the next words he can make out are two neighbours on the price of bread or a roof that held.
   Not done: `StreetVoice.Ambient` (StreetVoice.cs:895-1169) chooses between feud, injured, prices, slump, night and ordinary, and nothing in it knows that something just happened nearby. The hush is volume only and recovers at 0.45 a second (`Notice.HushRecoverPerSecond`, Notice.cs:177); the next clear words come `StreetVoice.ClearWordsEverySeconds` (45 s) later. The new game has no Economy or Harm, and TownReach passes fixed prosperity and prices, so in practice only "ordinary" and "night" are ever drawn. The story itself is told (`Exchange`) only when somebody who holds it meets somebody in a gossip round. The work: a "just now" state for people in earshot of a deed within a few game minutes, a small bank of lines that react without naming anybody they did not see ("What was that?", "Glass, that was. Somebody's window."), the everyday banks held back until it passes, measured in TownReach; the choice of which pair speaks handed over.

The work: a prompt line in the rule's own shape ("say a make or a brand the way people do without naming it"), the two existing lists read in the validator with a second draft rather than the stand-in, and a dozen small-talk probes through the real engine (cents).

## Faults found on the way (not missing features)

- A character's reply can open with a stage direction in the first person, and nothing stops it. `ResponseValidator.ReadsAsNarration` catches only the speaker's own name followed by a verb, and `Validate` catches only an opening bracket or asterisk. In the bench's final replies 15 of 240 (12 of Ron's 80) open like "I look at you steady." or "I look up from the rank, squinting at you through the drizzle.", and some are nothing else ("I turn from the phone box."). The voice reads them out, and with the early first sentence it is the first thing heard. The bench predates the cards' change of 28 September evening, so today's rate is unmeasured, but Ron's card still sends his eye to the pavement and the prompt tells everybody to "start where you were already looking", the likely cause.
- The prompt can contradict itself. After an answer that fits what they saw, it says "You need not ask him again" (ConversationEngine.cs:168-169), yet a person still at Suspicious or above (a fitting answer lowers them by only 0.03) also gets "whatever they said, ask them straight out" (296-297). A caught lie, or a witness sure it was him, asks in every reply for good. No test covers either.
- The plan to carry his claim through the town's gossip (NOTE-2, item 3: "the carrying goes to the builder") never became a handover: the 6ac line in NOW.md asks the game only to accept the cast file's "areas".
