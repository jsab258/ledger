# The first-week rule table, measured and switched on (30 September 2026)

Jafar's list of 30 September evening, item 1: measure the first-week rule table
on the sixty, on a new sixty nobody tuned on, and on thirty that nobody in the
town can answer; switch it on only if it cuts the empty answers without adding
inventions. The table is the research note grounded-dialogue-selection's step 3
(TalkRules), with the plain line (step 1) said only where a rule chose the facts.

## How

- Three runs of each set with today's version (A) and three with the table and
  the plain line (R), the two alternating run by run so that whatever drifts
  through the day falls on both (ClaimBench `firsts` and `firsts --rules --plain`).
- Each reply labelled apart by two labellers for invented details (`firsts-label`),
  and for whether it answers what he said: answers, partly, declines, or beside
  the point, the lower reading standing where they differ (`plainrel --all`).
- The fixed answerable labels of each set as before (two labellers and a third).
- The table's twenty kinds of question were written from the sixty and the
  note's list, never from the new sixty or the thirty.

Inventions (both labellers): A 12, R 12 in 450 replies each. The four R made on
the thirty came from lines of no kind (today's path); of those the table touched,
one was a real invention it led to: Darren, asked "Where do I sleep?", answered
about himself (a room at the boarding house).

## What it says

- **Where its kinds of question reach, it works:** on the sixty, empty answers
  fall from about 28 to 6 a run, and useful replies (answers or partly) from 77
  to 141 of 180, beside-the-point halved.
- **On questions it was not written for, it barely reaches:** on the new sixty,
  empty answers are level (25 against 24) and useful replies 58 against 61; most
  of those lines are of no kind the table knows. Its gain for a new player is
  as wide as its kinds of question.
- **It adds no inventions and costs nothing where it does not reach;** where
  nobody could answer, both decline honestly 64 times in 66.
- **So, by the rule, it is switched on** in the talk program (UseRules and
  PlainFallback at LedgerTalk's start; the plain lines are marked as not written
  by the model). The next talk build ships it; the game wires nothing.
- **Widening it** is more kinds of question from what newcomers actually ask,
  measured on yet another fresh set.
