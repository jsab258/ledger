## Questions, written before any viewing (CLAUDE.md research method)
Footage wanted: amateur or broadcast street footage filmed 1988-1992 in British port towns (Hull, Grimsby, Liverpool, Southampton, Portsmouth, Plymouth, Dover, Sunderland, Newcastle's quays, Glasgow's docks, Cardiff, Swansea, Belfast), uncut or long takes, each clip with its town, year as the uploader states it (unverified unless the footage shows a date, a dated poster or a car registration letter), and its source. At least four clips from at least three towns; daytime and evening if footage allows.
- Q1 Crowds: people in frame on a shopping street and on a quiet back street, counted on frames every 10 s; walking speed by eye (fast, even, slow); groups versus people alone; share standing still (talking, waiting, window-looking).
- Q2 Traffic: vehicles passing per minute on a main street and a side street; share of vans and lorries, buses, taxis, bicycles; parked cars per 20 m of kerb; how close parked cars sit and whether they sit on the pavement; registration letters seen (F, G, H fit 1988-90).
- Q3 Clothes: per sampled adult, outer layer (anorak, shell suit, wax or quilted jacket, donkey jacket, overcoat, mac, denim, leather, knit), trouser kind, shoes (trainers, boots, shoes), headwear, bags; the colours by family; men versus women; workwear near docks.
- Q4 Shopfronts: per frontage, kind of shop, fascia material and lettering (painted, plastic box sign, cut letters), shutters (roller, grille, none), condition (fresh, worn, boarded, to let), window dressing density; the share of empty or boarded units on each street.
- Q5 Stance and movement: how people stand when waiting (hands in pockets, arms folded, leaning on walls or railings), how they carry bags, how close strangers pass, how often people turn to look at the camera.
- Q6 Smoking: share of sampled adults with a cigarette in hand or mouth, men and women, walking or standing; where (doorways, bus stops, work breaks).
- Q7 The ground and the light: wet or dry, litter density per frame (none, some, much), street furniture per 20 m (bins, bollards, phone kiosks, lamp posts, railings), the colour of the light on overcast days.
Counts per clip, each number with its clip, its time mark and the date read. What could not be read or reached is listed below.

## Clips

Method used (budget at the weekly ceiling): the video paused and stepped by script in the built-in browser, one cropped frame per step, read by eye; steps far wider than the 10 s asked for, so counts below are presence notes, not rates. Frames at 30-60 s or 3-5 min as stated.

### Clip 1: David Dixon, "Sunderland in 1989 shot on video 8", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9D2nhHQwV7I (47:02; read 21 Nov 2026 14:30-14:33 Swiss)
A family holiday tape, the camcorder's own date stamp "OCT 10 1989" burned into frames at 8:00 and 14:00 (date confirmed by the footage). 15 frames at 2:00, then every 3-5 min.
- Mostly seafront, beach, cliff paths, ships offshore and a house visit; no shopping street in any frame read. Low yield for Q1-Q6.
- Q7: seafront road at night (2:00, 5:00): illuminations strung in arcs across the road, sodium-orange street lamps on tall posts, wet-looking black tarmac with car lights in long streaks; few cars (2-4 in frame), no people on foot.
- Q3: one older woman on the cliff path (14:00) in a long camel wool coat to below the knee; an indoor display (36:00) with men in white shirts and dark trousers, one in a pale jacket, all bare-headed.
- Q7 light by day (8:00, 21:00): flat grey-white sky, sea and promenade concrete near-colourless; the overcast reads almost monochrome.
Verdict: kept for the date-stamped night lamps and the overcast; not a street source.

### Clip 3: Keith Parker, "Christmas 1990 in Hull City Centre", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bkgGcSuWmEE (5:48; read 21 Nov 2026 14:37-14:40 Swiss)
A hand-held camcorder at dusk and night, December 1990 as titled (unverified by the frames; the decorations agree with the season). 14 frames, 0:10 then every 25-30 s.
- Q7 night light (0:10-0:35, 2:45-3:15, 5:40): a dusk sky still blue-violet behind the buildings at 0:10; street decorations strung across the street and on lamp columns; tall white mercury-looking lamps over a square beside orange sodium heads; shop windows the brightest surfaces in every night frame, throwing white light onto the pavement for about 2-3 m; beyond that the pavement falls to near-black. The upper floors of the stone and brick buildings stay mostly dark above the fascias.
- Q1 night crowd (1:00-2:00, a pipe band marching through, then 3:45-5:15 the crowd at a stage and shopfronts): 15-30 people in frame along the route, standing two to four deep against crash barriers; on the shopping street (4:45) about 20 walkers in frame, mostly in pairs and small groups, heading both ways at an even pace, nobody hurrying.
- Q3 clothes at night: nearly all adults in dark long or mid-length coats and jackets (black, navy, brown); a few pale anoraks or light raincoats, one man in a white shirt-sleeved jacket at 3:45; heads mostly bare, a few flat caps and knitted hats. Colours read as dark masses under the lamps; only the pale coats pick up shop light.
- Q4 (4:45): a department store's long glazed frontage, a green fascia with large cut white letters, lit from inside; the shop next door shuttered dark.
- Q5: the standing crowd hands in pockets or arms folded, close shoulder to shoulder at the barriers; nobody looks at the camera in the frames read.
- Q6: no cigarette made out at this resolution at night (unreadable, not counted as none).

### Clip 4: Keith Parker, "Video of Marina area from 1990", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=op8F3Ivm-9o (8:43; read 21 Nov 2026 14:41-14:44 Swiss)
Hull's marina, lock and old-town edge by day and dusk, 1990 as titled (unverified). The nearest thing to LEDGER's docks of any clip so far. 11 frames, 0:20 then every 50 s.
- Q7 the dock edge (1:10, 4:30-7:00): a three-storey dark brick dock office with white-framed sash windows and a row of flagpoles; tubular steel railings painted white along every quay; a steel lock gantry overhead; lock walls of grey concrete and dark wet stone, timber-faced gates black with water and green slime to the tide line; a small grey steel tug-like workboat in the lock.
- Q4 (2:00, 7:50): a single-storey brick public convenience with stone bands and roundels; a two-storey brick warehouse with the firm's name painted in white capitals straight on the brick above the first floor, a blue roller shutter beside a dark doorway; a white rendered unit beside it.
- Q2 (8:35): a narrow old-town street between tall brick warehouses, three to five storeys, no pavement worth the name: a box trailer parked tight to one wall, a white forklift working in the road, no cars moving; (7:50) one orange car parked at the kerb before the warehouse.
- Q1: nearly empty: 2 figures at the waterside in silhouette (0:20), 1 on the lock walkway (7:00); no crowd anywhere on the dock frames.
- Q7 light: overcast flat white sky in all day frames; the dusk frame (3:40) a pale blue sky with the warehouses dark and the quay lamps just lit.

### Clip 5: Steve Peak, "1989 Hastings Town Centre", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9YlEjhZ3Hcs (52:20; read 21 Nov 2026 14:45-14:49 Swiss)
A local historian's record of the town centre filmed in 1989 (his statement; captions added later name what was demolished in 1990-91, which agrees). Hastings is a fishing-harbour town, not a docks port: used for the shopfront and street questions only. 11 frames, 1:00 then every 4-6 min.
- Q4 shopfronts (5:00, 32:00, 38:00, 44:00): a run of shops on a one-way street under three- and four-storey stuccoed buildings: dark fascias with white cut or painted lettering, a newsagent's window papered with posters; one empty shop unit with a lime-green "moved to" card in the bare window, a pale blue side door with a cracked painted surround, the window full of loose cardboard and a fallen blind; a 1960s concrete-and-glass supermarket block now a discount shop, a long flat fascia, a bus shelter and yellow litter bins in front; a "SOLD / AUCTION" board fixed high on a soot-dark stone frontage (26:00).
- Q2 (5:00, 13:00, 21:00, 44:00): white rental Ford-type box vans in a row on a car park; a red hatchback parked against a cleared site's chain-link fence beside a 1960s slab block; a small cream-and-red minibus-sized bus; a white van parked tight at the kerb; pedestrian guard railings along the kerb at a junction (5:00), a no-entry sign on its post.
- Q1 and Q3 (38:00): one woman crossing in a white short-sleeved blouse and pale skirt, a bag on the shoulder; 2 more figures at the bus shelter; the streets otherwise near-empty in the frames read (filmed for the buildings, not people).
- Q7 (all day frames): bright hazy summer light in most frames; stucco and painted render cream and grey, streaked dark under sills; soot-black stone on the older civic buildings.
