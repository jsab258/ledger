## Q1. Published mechanics

### RDR2

Source A: RDR2.org, "Wanted System" (author Matt Gibbs, page dated 9 Jan 2023), https://www.rdr2.org/wiki/wanted-system/ , read 24 Nov 2026. Fan site; no developer source cited on the page as returned. How known: fan wiki, not a developer statement.
- Steps, in the page's own order: Witness (an NPC saw the crime and is about to report) -> Crime Reported (the witness has contacted the authorities) -> Investigating (lawmen know a crime occurred, not who did it; they can also arrive without any witness) -> Unknown Suspect (shown while the player remains at the scene unidentified; skipped if the player committed an earlier crime in the same outfit and mask or bandana) -> Wanted: Arthur Morgan (lawmen identify the player, by finding him or through a witness; a bounty applies at once).
- White-marked witnesses may not recognise the player; red-marked witnesses can identify him.
- A player can threaten a witness (Triangle on PS4, Y on Xbox One, while interacting) for "a chance" to stop the report; killing one in front of a crowd makes more witnesses.
- Leaving the red area unseen: lawmen lose sight, the wanted meter fades red to grey, lawmen search until it empties. Surrender gives jail and takes a percentage off the bounty; paying at any post office clears that state's bounty only.
- Unpaid bounty: bounty hunters spawn in the crime area "after 12 in-game hours". Lockdown: 12 offences within 60 minutes, closing shops and houses for "about 6 in-game hours".
- The page itself states no distances, no meter-drain time and no expiry for an unpaid bounty (the fetch tool's note on its own reading; the page was not read raw).

Source B: RDR2.org, "Red Dead Redemption 2 Wanted System Explained" (author Aron Gerencser, updated 9 Jan 2023), https://www.rdr2.org/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-wanted-system-guide/ , read 24 Nov 2026. Fan site.
- No witnesses, or only indirect ones who cannot identify the player: "in the clear". An indirect witness saw the player at the scene afterwards but not the crime.
- "Only when a witness who can identify you manages to report the crime will there be a wanted situation." Lawmen who see the crime themselves also trigger it.
- Active pursuit starts when lawmen spot the player at the scene; the mini-map blip turns red while in sight; if sight breaks the red blip stays where lawmen last saw him and re-centres only when he is spotted again. The phase ends after "a certain time" (not stated) or on crossing a state border.
- 12 high-severity crimes within 60 real-world minutes can put some towns in lockdown for 350 in-game minutes (this differs from Source A's "about 6 in-game hours" = 360 minutes; both are fan-site figures).
- No report time, bounty amounts or distances.

Not reached in full: GamesRadar's RDR2 bounty guide (page returned the title only); Shacknews, GamePressure, Twinfinite and others appeared in a search (24 Nov) and were not opened. Search-snippet claim, unverified: a YouTube explainer says dark-red witness icon = saw the crime but does not know who, bright red = knows, white = suspicious and investigating; this conflicts with Source A (white = may not recognise, red = can identify) and is one creator's claim.

### KCD2

No page reached gives a witness-to-report time, a run distance or a bounty duration in the developer's words. What was reached:

Source C: Lootlore, "KCD2 Crime and Trespassing" guide (credited Z. Li, "last verified May 23, 2026"), https://lootlore-d2ce2.web.app/en/kingdom-come-deliverance-2/mechanics/kcd2-trespassing-and-crime-guide , read 24 Nov 2026. A small web app; the page lists Warhorse's site, the KCD2 Fandom wiki and a Reddit thread as sources but does not tie any figure to them. How known: unattributed guide; the page contradicts itself in places (Kuttenberg lockout 200 g vs "never exceed 500 g"; murder values single numbers vs ranges). All figures unverified.
- "Crimes committed unseen don't add bounty (no witness, no crime)." Stolen goods stay flagged and selling to a non-fence vendor can trigger a bounty later.
- Bounty tiers: under 100 g guards threaten arrest (bribe, no arrest unless resisting); 100-500 g arrest on sight, bribe at 50% of face value; 500-2,000 g hostile guards region-wide, jail at 1 in-game day per 250 g; 2,000 g and over all regional guards attack on sight and major settlements close; resisting arrest adds 200 g.
- Per-incident ranges: trespass 5-50 g, petty theft 10-50 g, assault 100-500 g, horse theft 300-800 g, peasant murder 500-1,000 g, noble murder 1,000-2,000 g.
- Trespass: penalty per witness (5 g peasant home, 15 g merchant shop, 30 g noble room, 50 g barracks); a "1-min" escape window in its crime table (meaning not explained). A red eye icon in the screen corner shows in a restricted zone.
- Bounties persist; no decay mentioned.

Source D: Kingdom Come Deliverance wiki.gg "Reputation" page (oldid 44560, last edited 26 Jul 2024), https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Reputation , read 24 Nov 2026. This is the first game's page, not KCD2's, and says nothing on witnesses, timers or icons. Listed only so it is not used again.

Already in our files (not re-read): study 15 gives KCD2's witness counter (starts at 1 to 3, a warning each tick, last at 12, 20 s of grace) and crime duration (theft, pickpocketing, lockpicking 4 days; trespass 2; murder 7), marked R (read from the game's data, 28 Oct). None of the pages above confirms or contradicts those; they give a different kind of figure (gold).

## Q2. What the screen shows

RDR2 (Sources A and B, fan sites): witness shown on the mini-map by a small eye icon, white or red; status words "Crime Reported", "Investigating", "Unknown Suspect", "Wanted: Arthur Morgan" as states; the bounty amount below the WANTED meter in the top right; a red area on the mini-map to leave; the meter fades red to grey while lawmen search; the player's blip red while seen and left at the last-seen spot when sight breaks. Whether each state's words appear as screen text, and for how long, is not stated by the pages: unverified until seen in footage.

KCD2 (Source C only, unverified): a red eye icon in the corner in restricted zones; the pause menu's Reputation screen lists each region's bounty; stolen items flagged in the inventory; guards shout. No witness icon or "wanted" marker for a witnessed crime was described by any page reached.
