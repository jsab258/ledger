# Street photo counts, 1988-1992 terraced streets

Pages read 24 Nov 2026 (all links below). Short answer: this search did not reach enough photographs. Only two strictly dated photographs, both Hull, are countable, and neither is a typical terraced side street. No median can stand on two photos. The spacing question (Q3) is not answered.

## Q1. Where dated photographs can be viewed

- Geograph (geograph.org.uk): pages show "Date taken" and open fully. Hull has a run dated 1990 and 1992 (one contributor, scanned slides). Commons category pages for Hull 1990, 1991 and 1992 (commons.wikimedia.org) list these files. Other northern towns found no Geograph photo dated in the window.
- Newcastle Libraries via Co-Curate (co-curate.ncl.ac.uk): public domain, images open. A few items titled 1990 or 1992, but "Picture Taken" reads Unknown, so the date is loose. Newcastle is not a port in the brief's list (northern terraced town, marked).
- South Tyneside History (southtynesidehistory.co.uk): pages dated 1990 or c.1990 (St Mary's Terrace, Horsley Hill Road, South Shields) give caption only; the image link opened a picture that was a different place (a stone terrace with a "Council" watermark, not Shields). Image not trusted.
- Hull History Centre catalogue: King Edward Street, c.1990 (Lp/9.7 KIN/1): record only, no image reachable.

## Q3. Lamp spacing

No usable measurement. Photo 1 shows one column. Photo 2 shows replica columns in a redeveloped square whose spacing is a design choice, so it does not test the 34 m figure. Unverified.

## Q4. What makes counts unsure

- Only one town, one photographer, and slides taken on a single July Sunday (photo 1 shares its day with six other Hull pictures).
- Photo 1 is a main-road corner: the column belongs to the main road, not a side street.
- Photo 2 is a conservation scheme with replica lamps; the stretch length rests on one car length and heavy foreshortening.
- Frontage width of 5.0 m is an assumption (the brief's range 4.5 to 5.5 m would move the stretch by about 10 per cent).
- Pavement-level hidden items (bins behind walls, kerbside poles out of frame) cannot be ruled out in either picture.
- Dates in the Newcastle and South Tyneside records are loose ("c.1990", "Unknown").

## Unreached or unproductive

- Historic England archive: every item page returned 403 (not bypassed). Photos dated 1990 found only by search listing (for example Edward Street, Shipley; Park Avenue, Hull) were not read.
- Hull History Centre: records give no image. The history site hhtandn.org (Hartlepool) returned 500.
- Commons: no category for Sunderland, Middlesbrough or Liverpool 1990 files; the Hartlepool 1990 category holds a railway photograph only.
- Leodis (Leeds), Flickr groups, newspaper archives, Sunderland, Grimsby, Barrow, Fleetwood, Whitby and Liverpool dated photos: none found by search; not read directly. Flickr itself was not searched.
- Sources not opened: Co-Curate 1988 to 1992 pages (listed, no terraced side street found).

Suggested next step for the synthesis, not a conclusion: the real gap is an archive with scanned 1988-92 street views and open images; a local-studies request or Hull History Centre's image view would be needed.
