# The deciding rows rechecked against the sourcing rule (24 Nov 2026)

The rule (the owner's): real things (sizes, distances, counts, spacing, how long real things take) come from 1990 Britain: photographs, records, standards. Feel and rule numbers (noticing, give-up rules, control feel, how a game system behaves) come from the reference games. Story and people come from canon and his rulings. Anything else comes from the project's own play loops, marked judgement until tested. A row decides only if its game measurement settles a number the blueprint needs and no other source could give it.
