## What it gives the row

- Length: about 96 s of play (from about 107 to about 203 s), a single guided run (follow a fleeing companion) through about six spaces (alley, market, dock walkway, yards, a window, a flat).
- How it ends: scripted capture; the run cannot be escaped (the story needs the arrest). It says nothing about how an escape ends a chase; it is a length and pacing sample for a scripted tutorial chase only, not a systemic one.
- Pacing: a new obstacle or space about every 15-20 s (sprint prompt, walkway, vault, window, wall climb, flat).
- Not read: distance covered, pursuer gap, any cooldown (none exists in a scripted run).

## Limits

- One player's run; the guided route fixes length more than the player does (unverified how much a slower player stretches it).
- 1 s readings around the start and end; the exact hand-over frame between 104 and 111 not pinned (107 may still be the camera move).
