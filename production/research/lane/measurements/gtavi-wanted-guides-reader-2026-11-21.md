# GTA VI wanted system, written guides read 21 Nov 2026

Notes only; no synthesis. Articles dated before 19 Nov 2026 are pre-release, not used. Read via the fetch tool's page summary (no cookies or logins); each answer carries source and read date 21 Nov 2026. Anything from a summary-extracted page is unverified against the raw text.

## Questions
Q1 search area; Q2 duration and heat; Q3 heat icons and masks; Q4 Defuse/provoke; Q5 witnesses; Q6 Pay 'n' Spray; Q7 six stars.

## Q1 Search area
- Keengamer (19 Nov): the area to leave is marked as a "Hot Area"; no size. At six stars the zone grows and can cover the whole map. Star colours: hollow = crime reported, suspect unidentified; solid white = police see you; flashing = sight lost, still searching; red = chase over, attention remains in the area.
- Gtabase (updated 19 Nov): "search radius" and a heat area around the last known location; no size.
- Altchar (19 Nov): wanted bar and minimap turn blue in the chase, red once police are lost; a gap then appears, and keeping it centred makes it expand until the bar goes. This describes the minimap behaviour; it gives no size.
- Pause map: not stated anywhere. Size in blocks/metres: not stated. Shrink or move: not stated beyond the gap/centre behaviour above.

## Q2 Duration, heat, what returns the wanted level
- No seconds or minutes stated in any usable source. Altchar: once icons are cleared, remaining heat fades soon. Gtabase: stay hidden "long enough", no time. Keengamer: wait for attention to fade, no timer.
- Return: Keengamer says police can re-identify you if you stay in the area with the same look or vehicle (via search summary of related guides, unverified). Altchar: careless behaviour (driving badly, weapon out, crimes) restarts the chase.

## Q3 Heat icons and masks
- Gtabase (19 Nov): appearance clears by changing accessories (L1/LB), a barber hairstyle, or a mask; vehicle by respray or a new vehicle; outfit by changing clothes (spare in trunk, or steal clothing); visible weapon by holstering (L1/LB), long guns cannot be concealed so drop or stow in a trunk; partner by splitting up; CCTV: ordinary footage is stored on site and can be destroyed; cloud CCTV cannot be destroyed, an alarm eventually goes off, so clear the other icons and leave. Blood stains and burst dye packs also affect recognition; shower at a safehouse to clean blood.
- Keengamer (19 Nov): same set; appearance by hairstyle, hat or face covering; vehicle by abandoning or Pay 'n' Spray; weapon by stashing; couple by separating; CCTV by leaving and removing evidence where allowed. Per-icon timing: not stated.
- Altchar (19 Nov): vehicle respray or swap; couple split (hold Right on d-pad, or tell partner to take the car); appearance by adding or removing hat or sunglasses; outfit by changing clothes; Loadout > Items from the trunk. Timing not stated.
- Masks: Gtabase says wear before the crime and remove once out of sight; no delay or rule stated. Keengamer: wear a mask before a robbery "when possible"; effect on identification not stated. Whether a mask put on before a crime stops "appearance recognized": implied by Gtabase (appearance clears by mask), no explicit rule or delay stated.
- Immediate vs delayed vs only-out-of-sight: not stated in any source.

## Q4 Defuse / provoke
- Kotaku (18 Nov, pre-release, not used): at one star, hold left trigger on an officer with no gun drawn, then pick defuse or provoke.
- Post-release sources: Gtabase "Defuse: not stated"; Provoke: killing cops raises police aggression and pushes the Criminal Profile toward Violent (that is not the talk option). Keengamer, Altchar: not mentioned. Exact conditions: not stated in a usable source.

## Q5 Witnesses
- Gtabase: witnesses call police; no times. Keengamer: crimes generally need to be witnessed, reported or alarmed before police respond; no delays; leave before officers identify you. Whether stopping a witness prevents the report: not stated.

## Q6 Pay 'n' Spray
- Gtabase: some vehicles may be refused depending on the tracker fitted. Keengamer: repaints the vehicle, clearing the vehicle icon; whether it fully clears heat: not stated. What it does not clear: not stated.

## Q7 Six stars
- Gtabase: scale has six stars. Keengamer: six-star zone can cover the whole map; police response more dangerous; no thresholds. Dexerto (13 Nov, pre-release) and Allthings.how (updated 6 Sep) not used.
