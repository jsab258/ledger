## Rows (K for car, so they never clash with the melee or foot-chase numbers)
- K1 Start: what starts it (a crime seen, a stolen car spotted, a crash, a wanted level carried), and the time from trigger to the first police car moving after the player.
- K2 Length: from the first police car after him to lost (the search state begins) and to cleared; or to caught or wrecked.
- K3 Pressure: police cars in view, counted every 10 s; the most at once; the wanted level at its height.
- K4 Tactics: rams or spin manoeuvres, roadblocks, spikes, a helicopter, shots, each with its time; police crashes on their own.
- K5 Road and traffic: the kind of road by minute; the player's crashes per minute (anything that drops his speed sharply).
- K6 Ending: how it ends (outrun out of sight, hidden, a respray or car change, caught, wrecked, died), and the time from the decisive moment to the HUD clearing or control back.
- K7 Search: after sight is lost, how long until clear, and whether police seen again restart the chase.
- K8 Camera: any cut, cinematic or slow motion, the chase camera's change with speed, whether a marker shows the police out of sight.
- K9 Bystanders: what drivers and walkers do (pull over, swerve, flee, crash).
