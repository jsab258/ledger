## Questions, written before any viewing
- C1 Kind: which conversations: the free talk the player starts in the world (RDR2's greet and antagonize; KCD2's dialogue mode), against story scenes. Measure free talk first; story scenes only as the comparison.
- C2 Shot sizes: share of shots in each size: extreme close-up (eyes to chin), close-up (head), close shot (head and shoulders), medium (waist up), medium-long (knees up), long (whole body), two-shot; and whether over-the-shoulder.
- C3 Cuts a minute: shots per minute over each conversation, from the first line to the last; mean shot length in seconds.
- C4 Distance: the camera's apparent distance to the face, from the face's height in the frame (face height share), and the two speakers' distance apart (from body widths, estimate).
- C5 Who is framed: shots of the speaker against the listener, and the player character's share.
- C6 Entry and exit: how the camera gets into and out of the conversation (cut, blend, no change), in seconds.
Each number with its clip, minute mark and the date read; frames stepped at 0.5 s through each conversation; what could not be read listed.

## Clips
Method: frames drawn into a page canvas at 1 s (2 s for a first look), five across at 158 px, each shot's size judged from the face's share of the frame height; a cut is a change of framing between two frames, so lengths are to about 1 s.

## Reading (four conversations; thin, a first look)
- Free talk in the world: RDR2 keeps the play camera, no cuts, the other person whole-body at a few metres (1 sample, many interactions). KCD2's free talk enters its dialogue camera (the 3 Oct study; not re-measured here).
- Dialogue mode one-to-one (KCD2): close shot and reverse, over-the-shoulder mediums between, a close-up for emphasis; about 10 cuts a minute, shots 2-14 s, mean about 6 s here against 3.3 s in the 3 Oct study (two samples disagree; more needed).
- Story scenes with several people: both games cut faster (about 14-20 a minute, mean 3-4 s) and go wider (medium, medium-long, long group shots).
- For LEDGER (a reading, not a target): street talk can stay in the play camera as RDR2 does, cheapest and never jarring; a sit-down talk can use KCD2's close shot and reverse at about 6-10 cuts a minute.
