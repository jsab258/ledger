## Questions (Britain, 1975-1995)

1. Pedestrian counts per hour (or per 5/15 min) on shopping streets, residential streets, elsewhere: place, street type, year, time of day, weekday/Saturday, count, method.
2. How counts vary by hour of day and day of week (profile, peak to off-peak ratios, evening, night).
3. Walking speeds measured in Britain: fieldwork date, sample size, group.

## Q1 and Q2: counts, WP242 (main source)

Method (WP242 sections 4.2, 4.3): tripod-mounted Panasonic F2 CCD video camera at a first-floor vantage point, filming 0900-1700 on two survey days per site (about 16 h per site); counts collated in 5-minute intervals; one pavement, both directions, at a screen line. Manual counts of 20 minutes at 0840, 1000, 1200, 1500 on three days. Video counts re-counted on validation sites varied on average 2.18% (Table 11). Flows above 80 pedestrians/min were hard to record from video (section 4.2).

Fieldwork: Manchester pilot 14 and 15 May 1986 (both weekdays); main study 18 Oct to 28 Nov 1986; some repeats Feb, March, April 1987 (Table 4, partly garbled; dates below from the figure captions and Annex 1, 1986 unless stated).

Streets: video street at each centre was a main shopping street carrying traffic (pedestrian-only streets were excluded, WP242 5.1). Street details in WP242 Annex 1: Chesterfield Knifesmithgate (road 7 m, pavement 3 m); Sheffield Haymarket (15 m, 6 m); Lanark High Street (15 m, 3 m); Hebden Bridge Market Street (9 m, 3 m); Aberdeen Union Street (15 m, 4 m); Lewisham High Street (15 m, 4 m); Epsom Market Place (2 m pavement); Winchester St George's Street (10 m, 3 m); Guildford Lower North Street (11 m, 4 m); Twickenham York Road (2 m pavement); Bristol The Horsefair (11 m, 5 m); Manchester Cross Street (3 m pavement); Coventry Corporation Street (15 m, 4 m); Hazel Grove London Road (10 m, 2 m). Kilmarnock King Street: pavement width 3 m (Table 33 only; Annex page for Kilmarnock was lost in extraction).
