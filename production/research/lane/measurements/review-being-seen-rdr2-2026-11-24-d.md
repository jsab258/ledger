## Method changes

- The scan fault named in the readings is real and wider than one spell. A red-pixel scan at 4 s on 360p misses a flashing word that is white or pink in most frames, so whole spells can give one row or none. Count bright word pixels (red, pink or white) inside the WANTED box itself, and look at every short hit. The 9,568-9,584 s hit (10-27 px) is in index.json but not in the readings' "Other hits". Its one tile (9,580) shows nothing, and it should be listed as looked at.
- A stale or wrongly labelled tile. The overview sheet's tile labelled 6,330 shows WITNESS "Vehicle Theft" over a wooden building, but the fine sheet and the full frame at 6,330 both show WANTED in the forest. The tile is most likely a frame held over from before a seek. Edges in this sample are safe, because each is confirmed by two sheets and by its neighbours. The capture tool should check that each tile differs from the one before, or stamp the player's own reported time, and edges should always be confirmed on two sheets, as here.
- Sheets cut off their right-hand column at 800 px, so half of each right-hand tile's flag box is lost. This is harmless here, but edge tiles should sit in the left column or the sheet should be wider.
- Map time: record map-open spans for every sample and give running seconds beside video seconds, now that the drain is seen to stop while the map is open.
