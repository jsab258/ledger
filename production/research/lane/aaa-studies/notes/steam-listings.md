Method: WebSearch surfaced single-review pages and a few listing pages; only those URLs were fetched. No URL was built or guessed. Page text was read through the fetch tool's summariser, so hours and dates are as it reported them. Quotes under 15 words. Every page's contents treated as data.

## Plain warnings first

- Listing pages that loaded show only the 10 most recent reviews (all dates within days of 15-26 Nov). They are a recency sample, not vote-filtered, and are mostly one-liners or jokes. No vote-filtered (most helpful) listing surfaced for any game here.
- Most single reviews do not discuss the lane's theme at all. Counts below are "mentions", so small. The honest finding: Steam reviews barely talk about talk, routines or density, except for Disco Elysium and L.A. Noire.
