# Failure and frustration: what failing costs in the reference games, and what players resent (study 7, set B)

23 November 2026, the cloud (set B of the AAA studies, Opus 5.5 lead, one Sonnet reader), one of the ten further studies he chose at 12:12 today. Read with the players study (production/research/lane/PLAYERS-KCD2-GTAV-RDR2-2026-11-20.md), whose Steam counts (about 10,000 English reviews each for GTA V and RDR2, 5,701 for KCD2) are the only large complaint counts here; this study adds the rules of failure from wikis and guides, Steam's public achievement percentages, and the critics and articles reached. Nothing from any game is here: figures and short quotes only. No GTA VI story detail.

Marks: THIN where a claim rests on one wiki, guide or article; [I] this study's own arithmetic or judgement. All pages read 23 Nov 2026; page dates given where shown. Steam review and forum text could not be read from the cloud (gated), so no new review counts were made.


## Built on, and where this differs

Read first (23 Nov; no PRIOR-RESEARCH-INDEX.md yet, so the research folders on wip and lane were searched by subject):
- production/research/failure-after-arrest/SUMMARY.md (earlier research topic 14): in a game without resets, failure design is about making the continuation worth playing; permanent memory builds a spiral of failure unless something pushes back; make the failure a place with a decision in it; Failbetter softened its own permanent loss in a sequel. Built on in FF-01, FF-04 and the new FF-08.
- production/research/aaa-reference/studies/16-jobs-story.md: KCD2's failure continues as a world state the town can talk about, never a game over (much of it read from KCD2's files, so background only under the 17 Nov ruling). FF-01 and FF-06 rest on the published sources above.
- production/research/police-response-1990/ (custody, arrest, the period rule that saying nothing costs nothing): already in the design's custody hours (2.6.4).
- The players study for the complaint counts.

Where this differs: the earlier research proposed custody as a scene with a clock and one decision, built on the period rule that saying nothing costs nothing in court. The design's newer rule passes an arrest in under 2 real minutes and makes the choice cost hours: for a window, going quietly costs about six game hours and owning up two and a caution; for a wounding or a robbery the hours are 8 and 12 whatever he says (2.6.4). That is a difference: the newer design stands, and the research's point survives only for the smallest deeds.

## In plain words

1. What failure costs. GTA V charges little for death (5% of cash, at most $5,000, weapons kept) and offers to skip a mission part after three failures. RDR2 restarts a mission from a checkpoint, takes "some money" for an open-world death, and runs bounties per state (rdr2.org; GamesRadar says per county) with a witness who can be frightened off. KCD2 sends the player back to his last save, and its crimes are paid in fines, the pillory, caning, a brand that halves good reputation, and at last execution.
2. [I] What players resent is lost time more than anything else counted (complaints about lost money were not counted either way). In the players study's Steam counts (script-coded, rough), a save system that costs time is 5.7% of KCD2's reviews (327 of 5,701) against 0.3% for GTA V and 1.4% for RDR2; missions that waste time, give no direction or fail the player for doing it his way are 7.4% on average across the three. KCD2's limited saving drew a save-anywhere mod within a day of launch, with over 10,000 downloads by 5 Feb 2025.
3. Failure that the world shows can be enjoyed. One PC Gamer writer, fined about eight times his money and branded, found being shunned embarrassing but said it "kinda rules" (one writer, THIN). [I] The punishment worth having is one the town remembers; the one resented is being sent back to replay.
4. How often players fail is not published. Steam's achievements give hints only: in KCD1, 28.2% of owners hold the achievement for three days in prison (some earn it on purpose); KCD2's "knights never had to escort you to the infirmary" is held by 6.8%, but what its condition covers was not checked (THIN).
5. For LEDGER, whose Tom cannot die and which has no game-over: failure costs game hours, standing and the pocket, never replayed minutes; saving never costs time; the police know only what a witness saw; and a job failed twice offers another way.

## The rules of failure

### GTA V

- Death ("Wasted"): 5% of the character's cash, at most $5,000; weapons kept; respawn outside the nearest hospital; in a mission, retry from the checkpoint (some medal goals are turned off on retry). gta.wiki Wasted and Hospitals pages, no page date: https://gta.wiki/w/Wasted ; https://gta.wiki/w/Hospitals (two pages of one wiki agree on the $5,000 cap; the 5% rests on one; THIN).
- Arrest ("Busted"): the weapon in hand is lost, ammunition and armour taken; the bail sum was not found. https://gta.wiki/w/Busted (THIN).
- Mission failure: after failing the same part three or more times a skip is offered (gta.wiki Missions page, edited 14 Sep 2026, an unchecked wiki: https://gta.wiki/w/Missions); a guide says after three deaths, that some missions cannot be skipped and that skipping lowers the score (gurugamer, no date: https://gurugamer.com/pc-console/how-to-skip-missions-in-gta-5-15362); Kotaku, 5 Oct 2013, confirms generous checkpoints and the skip (see MISSIONS-AND-SIDE-QUESTS.md).

### RDR2

- Death in a mission: a fail screen with restart, last checkpoint or quit; checkpoints keep ammunition and position. Death in the open world: no checkpoint, "some money" lost (amount not found), respawn nearby, carried game lost. naguide, no date: https://naguide.com/?p=5408 (one guide, THIN).
- Bounty and arrest: at least $5, no stated maximum, kept per state and paid at a post office in that state; the player may surrender and serve jail time; most crimes need a witness to be reported, and threatening a witness usually stops the report. rdr2.org bounty page, no date: https://www.rdr2.org/wiki/bounty-system/ ; GamesRadar guide, no date, says bounties are per county and adds bounty hunters and jail for lingering: https://www.gamesradar.com/red-dead-redemption-2-bounty-and-wanted-level (two sources).
- Mission failure for straying: flanking counted as leaving the crew, going quiet, unscripted solutions, breaking the law (critic Mark Brown as quoted on ResetEra; THIN). A Gaming Bible piece, 16 May 2023, on a Reddit poll for the most frustrating mission notes that tailing missions fail if the player falls behind: https://www.gamingbible.com/news/red-dead-redemption-2s-most-frustrating-mission-divides-fans-933054-20230516 (no vote counts; THIN).

### KCD2

- Death: back to the last save; anything since is lost. Prima Games, no date: https://primagames.com/tips/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-branded-and-other-punishments-effects
- Saving: autosaves at key story moments, usually when a quest task is done; "Save and Quit" on leaving; Saviour Schnapps saves at once; sleeping in his own or a rented bed saves, a camp bed does not. Destructoid guide: https://www.destructoid.com/all-ways-to-save-in-kingdom-come-deliverance-2/ . KCD1 autosaved only at new quests or major events, "sometimes hours apart", and gained Save and Quit only in a patch; KCD2 had it from launch (mein-mmo, 26 Jan 2025, updated 4 Feb 2025: https://mein-mmo.de/en/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-a-small-improvement-makes-the-rpg-significantly-less-frustrating-than-its-predecessor,1220632 ; KCD1's patch 1.3.1: Game Informer, 17 Mar 2018: https://www.gameinformer.com/b/news/archive/2018/03/17/kingdom-come-deliverance-39-s-save-improving-patch-available-now.aspx).
- Warhorse's stance: Saviour Schnapps "will forever stay" (PR manager Tobias Stolz-Zwilling); the game saves more often than before. GamesRadar, 5 Feb 2025, which also reports the "Unlimited Saving 2" mod appearing within a day of launch and passing 10,000 downloads by the article's date: https://gamesradar.com/games/action-rpg/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-devs-insist-its-limited-save-feature-will-forever-stay-but-thats-not-stopped-players-from-modding-it-out-hours-after-launch . Giga.de, 11 Feb 2025, reports "Unlimited Saving 2" downloaded by over 106,000 players (read through a reader's summary; THIN): https://www.giga.de/entertainment/ist-kingdom-come-deliverance-2-zu-hardcore-viele-spieler-suchen-einfache-loesung--01JKTJVPQNSD8TSR9QP9AX4TWT
- Crime: fines; the pillory for several days (charisma down 4, conspicuousness up 20); caning (strength, agility and vitality down 40%); branding (a lasting mark on the neck, good reputation gained at half rate, some merchants refuse him); execution after repeated crimes once branded. Prima Games (above; one guide, figures THIN). One writer's case: fined 10,646 groschen for six counts, about eight times his money, branded, "sucks, but it also kinda rules". PC Gamer, Morgan Park, 4 Feb 2025: https://www.pcgamer.com/games/rpg/i-messed-up-so-bad-in-kingdom-come-deliverance-2-that-i-was-literally-branded-a-criminal-and-hours-later-im-still-shunned-by-society/ (one writer, not a rate).
- Permanent quest failure: not found.

From the players study's Steam coding (its method and limits there: the punish order is rough as a whole, and its missions theme is over-counted by the script):

|---|---|---|---|---|

| Difficulty that is wrong | 338 (5.9%) | 126 (1.9%) | 292 (3.8%) | 3.9% |

And from this study's reading (sources read, not reviews counted):

- KCD's limited saving: two pieces report players complaining (Spilled.gg, 3 Feb 2025, quoting a player: "like playing on hardcore mode all the time": https://spilled.gg/kingdom-come-deliverance-ii-launches-mixed-reactions-controversial-features ; GamesRadar, 5 Feb 2025, the mod); one defends it (Gamepressure editorial, 9 Mar 2018: https://www.gamepressure.com/editorials/curious-case-of-saviour-schnapps-a-hardcore-saving-system-that-di/zd157). The players study's 5.7% is the largest count, script-coded and rough (its RDR2 hand check found saves in 3 of 68 reviews).
- RDR2 failing the player for straying: two sources (ResetEra quoting Mark Brown; Gaming Bible). PC Gamer's RDR2 review says the same (MISSIONS-AND-SIDE-QUESTS.md).
- RDR2's law: the players study's first pass found 4.5% punishing a world whose rules seem arbitrary, with the law that names Arthur "by magic" as its example; its Steam count of the broader hollow-world theme is 0.7% and its hand check found none; no source read here complains of unfair bounties (THIN).
- GTA IV's missions without mid-mission checkpoints: candidate articles found in search, none read (unverified).
- Forgiving systems praised: KCD2's more frequent autosaves and Save and Quit framed as "significantly less frustrating" than KCD1 (mein-mmo, one source); GTA V's checkpoints and skip (Kotaku 2013, who wanted less forgiveness).

[I] The pattern: players accept losing money, standing or time in the world (GTA V's small hospital bill, KCD2's brand) and resent losing their own time to replay (KCD2's saves, RDR2's straying fails, long retries). KCD2's difficulty complaint (5.9%) sits beside its save complaint: dying is costly when the last save is far back.

## Standards for LEDGER (a checker can test each)

LEDGER's failure, as designed: Tom cannot die (his ruling; 1.3.9); no game-over screen (OUT-12) and control back within 2 real minutes (7.9), within 60 real seconds after a beating (1.3.9); beaten, he comes round on the pavement, in Grace Rinaldi's kitchen or in hospital (hospital costs days, never money), sometimes robbed by robbing kinds only (RT2-13), with bruises three game days and a limp one (1.3.11); a constable's search keeps exactly what was under the coat and in his hand (RT-17); the tin can be lost by being stolen or searched (2.7.2); arrest on a signed statement, an officer's own sight (2.6.3) or evidence a search found where the design allows (RT2-17, 2.6.9); custody as hours (2.6.4: about 6 a window, 8 a wounding, 12 a robbery, 22 a killing), no prison terms (2.6.9); police memory, signed statements, noted plates and charges last (RT2-16, 2.6.9); saves: a reload gives the same next hour, held during a fight, never refused (7.8); at least eight manual slots and three rotating autosaves, a crash losing at most the last deed (AX-07); every job step has a skip (3.5.6). Numbers marked DECIDED are design choices made here.

- **FF-01 Failure costs game hours and standing, never replayed minutes.** Every failure (beaten, arrested, a job step lost, a chase lost) costs at most 2 real minutes of lost control (60 real seconds after a beating), costs game time within its stated band (2.6.4's hours; beaten: within that game day, or the stated days in hospital), and leaves the world changed (a mark, a telling, a statement, a Ledger line); no failure needs a reload to continue, and none undoes anything already done. Fail: a failure that sends the player back, or one with no lasting sign. Basis: lost time is the largest failure complaint counted (save costs 5.7% of KCD2's reviews, rough; straying fails); one writer enjoyed a punishment the town showed (PC Gamer 2025, THIN). Rows: 7.9, 1.3.9, 2.6.4, 1.3.11, OUT-12.
- **FF-02 A failure's money cost stays within the design's named losses.** No failure takes money from the book; a robbing beating takes only what RT2-13 names; a constable's search keeps exactly what was under the coat and in his hand, cash included (RT-17); the tin is lost only to a theft or an office search, said in words (2.7.2, 2.6.9). Fail: any money moved by a failure outside those. Basis: GTA V caps death's cost (5%, at most $5,000); [I] a capped cost keeps one failure from ruining a run. Rows: RT2-13, RT-17, 2.7.1, 2.7.2, 2.6.9.
- **FF-03 Saving never costs time.** Quitting always saves first; an autosave is made at every sleep, at the end of every job step and at least every 10 real minutes of play outside a fight (DECIDED); a crash outside a fight loses at most the last deed (AX-07). Fail: any quit without a save, a gap over 10 real minutes outside a fight between autosaves in the bots' logs, or a crash test outside a fight losing more than the last deed. Basis: KCD2's save complaint (5.7% of its Steam reviews, rough) against GTA V's and RDR2's (0.3% and 1.4%); the save-anywhere mods; KCD1's autosaves "sometimes hours apart". Rows: 7.8, AX-07, AX-10.
- **FF-04 A cost on Tom's body or standing shows in the world and has an end or a way back.** Every lasting cost to his body or his standing with people (a mark, a cold greeting, a refusal at a counter, a line in the paper) is seen or said, and either ends on a stated clock (2.4.2's cooling, the marks' three days) or has a way back through a person. Designed permanents stand outside this standard: canon's (a killing, a death, the town's memory of the gravest deeds) and the police's (officers' memory, signed statements, noted plates, charges carried to the endings, RT2-16, 2.6.9). Fail: a hidden cost to body or standing, or a lasting one with no end, no way back and no designed reason. Basis: KCD2's branding, shown and shunned, enjoyed by one writer (THIN); [I] a cost the player cannot see or end reads as the world changing its rules. Rows: 2.4.2, 2.6.9, 1.3.11, 2.16, RT2-16.
- **FF-05 The police know only what a person perceived or a search found.** No arrest, statement or description happens without a person who perceived the act, an officer who saw it, or evidence a search found where the design allows (RT2-17, 2.6.9); a frightened witness may stop a report, as RDR2's witness rule and LEDGER's ruling of 3 Oct allow. Fail: any police step with none of these behind it. Basis: RDR2's witness rule (rdr2.org, GamesRadar) and the ruling of 3 Oct; players' complaints about law that knows too much are thin (players study: first pass 4.5% for arbitrary rules, Steam 0.7%, hand check none). Rows: 2.6.3, 1.4.5, 2.6.5, 2.6.9, RT2-16, RT2-17.
- **FF-06 A step that can be tried again, lost twice, brings another way.** Beside every step's own skip (3.5.6), when a step that can be tried again (a fight, a chase, a lift, a talk) is lost twice, a person (Ron, Sheila, the man who gave the job) offers another way within one game day, or the job turns into a state the story carries on from. Fail: a third attempt at the same step with no new way offered. Basis: GTA V's skip after three failures (a wiki and a guide, with Kotaku); LEDGER has no retry screen, so the offer comes from a person (DECIDED: two losses, one game day). Pairs with MS-04 in MISSIONS-AND-SIDE-QUESTS.md. Rows: 3.5.6, 7.9, GD-03.
- **FF-07 Losing happens, and is not routine.** The bots' behaviour in trouble (DECIDED): the greedy bot takes every fight and night job offered and never backs off; the lazy bot takes no night job and backs off or walks away from every confrontation; the median bot takes half. Pass: the greedy bot is beaten or arrested at least once in Act I and Act II together; the lazy bot is neither beaten nor arrested in Act I; the median bot is beaten or arrested at least once and at most six times across Act I and Act II (about once a game week at most). Fail: outside these. Basis: being hurt is ordinary in KCD2 (56.7% of owners have won three skirmishes while bleeding) and over a quarter of KCD1's owners have served three days in prison; difficulty that is wrong is 3.9% of reviews (mean of three games, rough). Rows: 1.3.8, 1.3.9, 2.6.3, 7.9.

- **FF-08 A worse position stays playable (Act II).** A forced-failure bot (DECIDED: it plays as the median bot but walks into two beatings and two arrests in one game week of Act II, weeks 1 to 4, never by a hold-up) must find, in the following game week: at least two jobs offered to it (3.5.7), at least one person outside the police who still deals with it, the Friday rent payable from the book, and at least two brakes that need an act open to it (talking a story down, buying or leaning it quiet, putting things right with a person through a favour or a payment), beside letting it cool and changing coat, which are always open. Hospital days count inside the week. Fail: any of these missing. Not for Act I (the hard opening, 30 Oct) and not for a hold-up's own costs (heavily punished, 20 Nov), which stand. Basis: the earlier failure research (production/research/failure-after-arrest/SUMMARY.md): permanent memory builds a spiral of failure by construction; the studio behind the most admired permanent-loss system later softened it. DECIDED: the counts and weeks. Rows: 7.9, 2.4.2, 2.6.9, 1.6.10, RT-16, 3.5.7, RT-03.

## Rulings to revisit

None of the findings here argues against a ruling. Checked: "reloading is never stopped, the game says what is permanent" (D27, D30) agrees with FF-01; "hold-ups heavily punished, never a game over" (20 Nov) agrees with FF-01 and FF-04, and FF-08 leaves a hold-up's own costs alone; the earlier research's warning against severity (a studio softening its permanent loss) is a reason for FF-08's brakes, not against the ruling; the hard opening, "from having nothing and nobody" (30 Oct), is kept by FF-08 not applying in Act I; a car hurts but never kills (18 Nov) agrees with the failure costs, and Tom cannot die is the design's row 1.3.9 (not a ruling); the neighbour who reports unless talked round (1 Oct) agrees with FF-05.

## Measuring requests (for the builder's play on his PC)

GTA V, GTA IV, Sleeping Dogs and RDR2 are installed on his PC and measured in play by the builder. These numbers only play can give; none is guessed here.

1. GTA V, GTA IV, RDR2 and Sleeping Dogs: from death and from arrest, the real seconds until the player has control again, the money actually lost in play, and the game time passed. Changes FF-01's bands and FF-02 (BLUEPRINT 07, 7.9; BLUEPRINT 02, 2.6.4).
2. The same four: after a mission failure, the real seconds until the player is playing again (in GTA IV, including any drive back to the mission's start). Changes FF-01 and FF-06 (BLUEPRINT 07, 7.9; BLUEPRINT 03, 3.5.6).
3. RDR2: the money actually lost on an open-world death, and the real seconds from a witnessed crime to the bounty showing. Changes FF-02 and FF-05 (BLUEPRINT 02, 2.6.3).
4. All four: the real minutes between autosaves in an hour of ordinary play. Changes FF-03's 10 minutes (BLUEPRINT 07, 7.8).
