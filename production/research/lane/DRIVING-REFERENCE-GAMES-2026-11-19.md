## In short

Shortlist, in order (why in one line each, full scores below):

1. GTA V: the genre's yardstick for fun driving and the most footage of any game; the upper bound for grip and forgiveness.
2. GTA IV: the same studio's heavy, swaying cars; the upper bound for body roll and weight, the other side of GTA V.
3. Sleeping Dogs: left-hand traffic in dense, narrow streets with arcade driving players and critics call fun; the closest feel-and-street match.
4. Mafia: Definitive Edition: the only shortlisted game with a speedometer on screen and old, slow cars; good for timings, one data point only.
5. Watch Dogs: Legion: real London streets, left-hand traffic, kerbs and tight corners; used for the camera in tight spaces and street scale, not for handling, which players dislike.
6. The Getaway: London, left-hand traffic, fragile smoking cars, no HUD; used for crashes, damage and what happens after, not for timings (low frame rate).

Contrast games:

- Forza Horizon 4: real 1980s British cars on British roads with a speedometer; shows what a period car does when a studio tunes it for fun, without on-foot play.
- Driver: San Francisco: a driving-only game critics praise for weighty arcade handling; shows how far "fun first" can go.

Biggest gap: no clip below has yet been checked for length, frame rate, mods or minute marks. Search results do not show them. The first part of the measuring sheet is the builder doing that check on his PC (about 25 minutes a game, about 3.5 hours for all eight). Player reception is thin where Reddit could not be searched.

## The scoring

Each game gets 0 to 3 on five tests from the task:

- A. Open world, third person, city streets.
- B. Driving that is fun first, not a simulation.
- C. Its driving well liked by critics and players (quotes and counts below).
- D. Long unedited footage at 60 frames a second with the normal camera, plenty of it; a speedometer on screen counts extra, since it makes timings exact.
- E. Close to LEDGER: left-hand traffic, older or small everyday cars, tight streets.

D is the gate for measuring: a game below 2 on D can still teach behaviours (crashes, damage, the camera) but gives no timing numbers.

## The candidates

### GTA V (2013)
- A: open world, third person, Los Santos city streets [I].
- B and C. Critics: The Drive (Adam Ismail, 21 Aug 2026) reports the critics' view that GTA V "made driving too easy by giving vehicles additional grip", and that its cars "don't buckle like jelly when changing direction" as GTA IV's do [READ] https://www.thedrive.com/news/gta-6-driving-physics-hint-at-return-to-the-realism-of-gta-4 . Traxion (Ross McGregor, undated): "GTA V's cars are certainly much more forgiving" [READ] https://traxion.gg/how-grand-theft-auto-iv-broke-the-open-world-mould-for-vehicle-physics/ . Sportskeeda (undated): "it is the best open-world game to drive around in" [READ] https://sportskeeda.com/esports/5-open-world-games-satisfying-driving-mechanics-and-5-driving-weakest-part . Players: one Steam thread, 11 comments read: 8 negative, 2 positive, 1 mixed ("feels like toy cars with too little G effect") [READ] https://steamcommunity.com/app/271590/discussions/0/613941122582136864 . That thread was started as a complaint, so it leans negative; the wider view is that GTA V is easy and fun, not deep. Scores not fetched.
- D. Footage found (all unchecked for length, frame rate, mods): "GTA V - 1 Hour of Pure Free Roaming Gameplay (4K)", AQUPAS, 7 Mar 2022, about an hour, no mods mentioned https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vAk51fS9z0E ; "Grand Theft Auto 5: Free Roam Gameplay - Next Gen 4K 60FPS - PS5 No Commentary" https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LQpq9kyg-6A ; "GTA 5 FREE ROAM GAMEPLAY (4K 60FPS)" https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BiZtze2u2TA . Earlier lane research found "GTA 5 Relaxing Driving Tour" https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gvusDEF3eh4 (FEEL-TARGETS-DRIVING-2026-11-17.md, section e). Minute marks not found; to be found by the measurer. Many GTA V speed videos use mods: reject those.
- E: right-hand traffic, modern American cars, wide roads. Weak.
