## The common kinds (question 3)
Across the seven games the details players repeat fall into six kinds: the body shows the day; people notice the player (clothes, stare, deeds); the world keeps marks (wear, damage, news); animals live on their own; time of day and weather change people; and small stories placed by hand. Two kinds are LEDGER's own centre and only partly met by the bar: news carried person to person (shown in one KCD2 video among the lists read; Sleeping Dogs and GTA V carry recent deeds into talk and calls, not by a chain of tellers) and absence noticed (none found in the lists read).
