## Q3. Recurs across games

1. Weather alters bodies and crowds: wet clothes to the wading depth, hunched walkers and sheltering NPCs in rain (GTA V, GTA IV via Kotaku 13 and snippet).
2. Dirt and wear on vehicles, comment from passers-by (GTA V, GTA IV car wash [SS]).
3. Bystanders speak about what the player just did (GTA V cashier memory and phone callbacks; Sleeping Dogs overheard talk of hit-and-run; Yakuza Mr. Shakedown who holds your money).
4. Police act on witnessed acts only (GTA IV Kotaku 1, 14; Sleeping Dogs ignores bystander harm; GTA V witnesses phoning [SS]).
5. Period-true street furniture and ambient sound (Sleeping Dogs phone boxes and crossing beep [SS]; Yakuza arcades, static ads, payphones, suits).
6. Time-of-day schedules: GTA IV morning bin lorries [SS]; GTA V sprinklers and weekend traffic; Yakuza day vs night.
7. Shop and contact memory of your last visit (GTA V clerk callbacks, repeated-call reply).
8. Car state is audible and visible: cooling engine tick, interior light, old cars brake longer (GTA V); hazard lights left on (GTA IV).
9. Placed characters on set streets who say one thing (GTA IV preacher, Yakuza named recurring NPCs, Sleeping Dogs ambient talkers).
