# GTA VI as a yardstick: the bar, group by group (20 November 2026)

What GTA VI now sets, phrased as standards a checker could test against LEDGER, each with its source (keys as in MECHANICS.md; the first read is "S18"). Where it raises the bar over GTA V, RDR2 and KCD2, the line says so. What those three games do is from the lane's earlier studies (REFERENCE-MECHANICS-2026-11-20.md, PLAYERS-KCD2-GTAV-RDR2-2026-11-20.md) and widely known play, marked [I] where it rests on the lane's own knowledge rather than a source read today. Systems only; nothing of the story.

A standard here is a bar to measure LEDGER against, never a number copied into a target: his rule of 17 Nov wants the lane's own measurements before any number becomes a target.

## 1 What the player does with his hands

- Y1.1 Damage changes driving. A crash that hits the front must change steering or acceleration in a way the player feels and must correct for, and a broken lamp must darken the road at night. (R, KOT, GT.) Raises the bar: GTA V's damage was mostly cosmetic and did not pull the car [I]; RDR2 and KCD2 have no cars.
- Y1.2 Taking a car is a method, not a button. At least three ways in, chosen by the car's kind (window, lock and wire, a tool), each with its own time and noise. (R, KOT, GT.) Raises the bar over GTA V's single pull-and-go [I].
- Y1.3 What is carried is limited and visible. The body carries a small set; the rest lives in a vehicle; carrying a long weapon openly is seen and reacted to. (R, GT.) Raises the bar over GTA V's bottomless wheel [I]; RDR2 already limited what was on the body and kept the rest on the horse [I], so this is RDR2's idea brought to cars.
- Y1.4 Disguise is per item. Each piece the police know (face or hair, clothes, vehicle, weapon, companion, recordings) has its own way of being shed, and shedding one leaves the rest. (P, GTB-W; R, SHK, KOT on clothes, car and mask.) Raises the bar over GTA V and RDR2, where a wanted level simply ran down or a bounty was paid [I]; KCD2 has clothes and recognition but not item by item [I].
- Y1.5 A fight can end without a death. Aimed shots to limbs, disarming, a subdue and knock-outs end a fight, and the record notices the restraint. (R, GT, TR, GI, S18 for limb shots and disarming; U, SHK for knock-outs.) Raises the bar over GTA V [I]; RDR2 had lasso and fists [I].
- Y1.6 Prep is play. Scouting, mask, a clean car, spare clothes in the boot, a parked getaway, wiping recordings: each a separate verb that changes the outcome. (R, KOT, S18.)
- Y1.7 The body keeps a record. Fitness and weight change running, kicking doors and swimming. (R, GT, TR.) Matches RDR2's cores and KCD2's needs rather than raising them [I]; critics call the chores fussy (S18), so the bar is the effect, not the upkeep.
- Y1.8 Context buttons never misfire. A checker tests that loot, boot, reload and enter-car prompts never fire the wrong action when two are in reach. GTA VI fails this (R, KOT): the bar here is set by its fault.
- Y1.9 Settings an adult expects: lock-on, partial and free aim; aim assist steps; tap-to-hold assists; simplified mini-games; reduced vehicle damage; a combat assist; colour-blind modes; text size; captions; screen narration; tinnitus reduction. (R, PD, RS.) Raises the bar over all three [I].

## 2 The town under the hood

- Y2.1 No crime without a report. A crime nobody sees, hears or records starts nothing; every reaction traces to a witness, an alarm, a camera or a tracked car. (P, GTB-W; R, KOT, TR and S18 on witnesses and cameras.) GTA V started stars on sight with little witness logic [I]; KCD2 already had witnesses [I]. LEDGER's per-person memory already goes further on paper; the bar is that the player can see the rule work.
- Y2.2 The player can read what is known. A display shows which details the law holds, each with its own icon, without a number. (P, GTB-W; R, S18 via TheSixthAxis and Vice on descriptions shed by changes.) Raises the bar over all three [I].
- Y2.3 Searches have a place. Losing sight leaves a marked area round the last known place, searched for a while, and coming back too soon is dangerous. (P, GTB-W; R, S18 on the red search layer.) Raises the bar over GTA V's moving search cones [I].
- Y2.4 The record lasts. A hidden record of how the player works (restrained or violent) changes people's and police's treatment, and its worst level cannot be undone. (R, VGC, KOT, TR.) RDR2's honour was visible and reversible [I]; LEDGER's rule is to derive it from people's beliefs, never a stored meter (S18).
- Y2.5 People are many and varied. A checker counts distinct people in one busy view (Kotaku saw more than 100 at a tram stop) and listens for a repeated line in an hour (one reviewer heard none in about 60 hours). (R, KOT; S18.) Raises the bar over GTA V and RDR2 in density and variety [I].
- Y2.6 People act. Some passers-by are armed and step in; a passenger fights for the car. (R, S18; P.) Raises the bar over GTA V [I].
- Y2.7 A buyer has a taste. Fences differ in what they take and pay by demand; trackers cut the price. (R, S18; KOT, GT; P, GTB-P for damage cutting it.) Raises the bar over GTA V's flat car sales [I].
- Y2.8 The world keeps its timetables (buses, trains) and its weather is local and lasting. (R, S18; P.) KCD2 and RDR2 already kept clocks [I]; GTA VI's own shops all night is a fault by LEDGER's rules, not a bar.

## 3 Story and people (craft only; see STORY-CRAFT.md)

- Y3.1 Jobs start from the world: a phone call or a meeting, no quest list, little signposting. (R, VICE.) Matches KCD2's spirit, raises the bar over GTA V's map letters [I].
- Y3.2 Choices change the ending, and how the player played (not only what he chose in talk) feeds it. (R, VGC, KOT, TR.) GTA V had one choice of ending [I]; RDR2 had honour [I].
- Y3.3 Two people in the car talk usefully: banter that carries hints without feeling like a tutorial. (R, KOT, VGC.)

## 4 The place

- Y4.1 Density over size: GTA VI is about twice GTA V's area (P, TS, GTB-P for size), but critics praise the density first (R, S18 roundup 18 of 18 on the reactive dense world). The bar for a small map is people per street and things to touch per street, not square miles.
- Y4.2 Places stay open: buildings used in jobs remain enterable after (P). A checker lists every interior used once and checks it can be entered again.

## 5 How it looks and sounds

- Y5.1 Lighting, reflections, wet streets and hair at a level Digital Foundry singles out (R, PX). This is the Unreal bar, not the browser's.
- Y5.2 Faces carry feeling without words (R, GI). The bar for close scenes.
- Y5.3 Radio is a world: many stations, talk, adverts that answer what the player did (R, KOT, VGC). Raises the bar over GTA V's mostly fixed adverts [I].

## 6 The interface

- Y6.1 Every HUD piece can be switched off: minimap, compass, GPS, door markers (R, PD). KCD2 already allowed most [I].
- Y6.2 Police are not shown as dots in a pursuit; the player watches and listens (P, GTB-W). Raises the bar over GTA V [I].
- Y6.3 The phone is in the hand, in the world (P; R setting, PD). RDR2 had no phone; GTA V's was an overlay [I].

## 7 The game's shape

- Y7.1 Failure costs and carries on: one fail state found by one reviewer; the rest cut pay (R, S18). Raises the bar over GTA V's restart-heavy missions [I]; LEDGER's canon already says no game over.
- Y7.2 The open world feeds the jobs: money and gear earned outside are needed inside (R, VICE). The bar is that no stretch of play is idle.
- Y7.3 Pace: 8 of 18 critics found a slow opening or a sag (S18). The bar is GTA VI's fault: LEDGER's first 30 minutes must hold a retellable moment (VISION).

## 8 Technical

- Y8.1 GTA VI holds about 30 fps with drops near 20 to 23 at worst on consoles (R, PX; S18). For a PC game in 2026 this is not a bar to copy: LEDGER's own frame target stands; critics and players punish 30 fps (R, IBT early).
- Y8.2 Almost no bugs at release (R, S18; TR). Raises the bar over RDR2's and GTA V's PC launches, which players punished most (players study) [I from that study].

## 9 The machinery

- Y9.1 Handmade content statement: generative AI not in creative content (P, TNW). Not a bar LEDGER can meet by its own method; recorded so the claim check knows the yardstick it will be compared against.
