# GTA VI study: the report for Jafar (20 November 2026)

Spoiler-free.

## Five ideas worth taking

1. Shedding what witnesses know, piece by piece: change the coat, swap the cab, put on a cap; each undoes one remembered detail, and only before the witness has told anyone.
2. The search stays: the street where Tom was seen is watched for days; a constable knocks along it next morning.
3. Crash damage changes the drive: a dented wing pulls the cab left until mended, a dead lamp darkens the coast road, and people remember the dent.
4. A passenger calls the turns in a chase and teaches the streets on fares, in words, never a marker.
5. The boot is seen: opening it on a lit quay is something a window can watch.

All fit his rulings, 1990 and the browser prototype.

## Where it raises the bar most

1. Being seen made readable: no crime without a witness, camera or alarm, and the player can tell what the law knows. LEDGER's memory goes deeper; it must be as readable.
2. Crowds and talk: over a hundred people in one view, and one reviewer heard no repeated line in about 60 hours.
3. Settings and polish: full aim, assist and accessibility options, almost no bugs at release.

Not to copy: 30 frames a second, shops said to open all night, fiddly prompts, a slow start.
