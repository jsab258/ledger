# Sleeping Dogs (2012) gameplay mechanics, outside research

Compiled 2026-11-19 from public wikis, guides, reviews and the Feral manual. Definitive Edition (2014) notes are marked "(DE)". Gambling lines end "(gambling)".

## 1. Verbs

- Light strike: tap the strike button for a quick hit, up to four in a chain. [Feral manual](https://www.feralinteractive.com/en/manual/sleepingdogs/latest/steam)
- Heavy strike: hold the strike button to stun or knock an enemy down. [Feral manual](https://www.feralinteractive.com/en/manual/sleepingdogs/latest/steam)
- Combo ender: a chain of light strikes may end with one heavy strike for a finisher. [Feral manual](https://www.feralinteractive.com/en/manual/sleepingdogs/latest/steam)
- Melee lock-on: hold the lock-on button to focus all attacks on one enemy. [Feral manual](https://www.feralinteractive.com/en/manual/sleepingdogs/latest/steam)
- Counter: a red glow on the attacker warns, one press at the right moment stops the hit and hurts the attacker. [Feral manual](https://www.feralinteractive.com/en/manual/sleepingdogs/latest/steam)
- Counter from any direction: enemies can attack from all sides and each can be countered, grabbing wrist, elbow or shoulder. [PC Gamer review](https://pcgamer.com/sleeping-dogs-review)
- Counter timing: pressing too early fails, so timing is precise. [Prima upgrades](https://primagames.com/eguides/sleeping-dogs-eguide/essentials/upgrades)
- Counter recovery upgrade: leaves the counter stance sooner so early presses cost less. [Prima upgrades](https://primagames.com/eguides/sleeping-dogs-eguide/essentials/upgrades)
- Grapple: grab an enemy to drag, strike, throw, use as a shield or sprint into a wall. [Feral manual](https://www.feralinteractive.com/en/manual/sleepingdogs/latest/steam)
- Human shield: grab an enemy and take him hostage in front of you. [Feral manual](https://www.feralinteractive.com/en/manual/sleepingdogs/latest/steam)
- Enemy dragging: pull a grabbed enemy along while sprinting. [PC Gamer review](https://pcgamer.com/sleeping-dogs-review)
- Grapple throw: toss a grabbed enemy off a railing or upper level, not a context kill. [GameSpot review](https://gamespot.com/sleeping-dogs/reviews/sleeping-dogs-review-6391428/)
- Environmental kill: grapple an enemy to a glowing object such as a fan, furnace, meat hook, dumpster or ice chipper for a one-hit kill. [PC Gamer review](https://pcgamer.com/sleeping-dogs-review)
- Environmental kill highlight: usable objects glow red when a grabbed enemy is near. [Feral manual](https://www.feralinteractive.com/en/manual/sleepingdogs/latest/steam)
- Environmental kill speed-up: these kills fill the Face meter faster. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sleeping_Dogs_(video_game))
- Non-lethal takedown: break bones with holds or knock enemies out with strikes. [PC Gamer review](https://pcgamer.com/sleeping-dogs-review)
- Leg break: grab, light, heavy breaks a leg, grants Face and makes nearby enemies wince. [Prima upgrades](https://primagames.com/eguides/sleeping-dogs-eguide/essentials/upgrades)
- Arm break: stronger than the leg break, grants Face, no wince. [Prima upgrades](https://primagames.com/eguides/sleeping-dogs-eguide/essentials/upgrades)
- Stealth takedown from behind: a leg-broken stunned enemy can be finished instantly. [GameSpot review](https://gamespot.com/sleeping-dogs/reviews/sleeping-dogs-review-6391428/)
- Sweep kick: light then heavy floors a thug so he can be picked up and finished. [Prima upgrades](https://primagames.com/eguides/sleeping-dogs-eguide/essentials/upgrades)
- Charge knee stun: two lights and a heavy stuns anyone who fails to block, breaking through blocks. [Prima upgrades](https://primagames.com/eguides/sleeping-dogs-eguide/essentials/upgrades)
- Stun follow-up: hit or grab a stunned enemy for a heavy-damage finisher. [Prima upgrades](https://primagames.com/eguides/sleeping-dogs-eguide/essentials/upgrades)
- Tackle strike: sprint then grab then light tackles an enemy and lets you pick him up. [Prima upgrades](https://primagames.com/eguides/sleeping-dogs-eguide/essentials/upgrades)
- Spinning heel kick: a fourth strike roundhouse that knocks over several enemies in front. [Prima upgrades](https://primagames.com/eguides/sleeping-dogs-eguide/essentials/upgrades)
- Dim Mak: grab plus counter just before the hit gives a powerful knockback counter. [Prima upgrades](https://primagames.com/eguides/sleeping-dogs-eguide/essentials/upgrades)
- Rising kick: press strike while knocked down to hit nearby enemies. [Prima upgrades](https://primagames.com/eguides/sleeping-dogs-eguide/essentials/upgrades)
- Climbing elbow strike: grab a stunned enemy for a powerful elbow smash. [Prima upgrades](https://primagames.com/eguides/sleeping-dogs-eguide/essentials/upgrades)
- Surprise exchange: roll behind a locked-on enemy to start a quick combo. [Prima upgrades](https://primagames.com/eguides/sleeping-dogs-eguide/essentials/upgrades)
- Melee weapons: knives, tire irons, frying pans and similar items hit harder but break with use. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sleeping_Dogs_(video_game))
- Weapon pickup: walk to a dropped weapon and press the pick-up key. [Feral manual](https://www.feralinteractive.com/en/manual/sleepingdogs/latest/steam)
- Melee weapon sprint attack: a Triad upgrade allows powerful sprinting attacks with weapons. [Prima upgrades](https://primagames.com/eguides/sleeping-dogs-eguide/essentials/upgrades)
- Fast disarm: take a gun from a nearby armed thug quickly (Cop upgrade). [Prima upgrades](https://primagames.com/eguides/sleeping-dogs-eguide/essentials/upgrades)
- Disarm by hand: manually disarming an armed enemy briefly slows time. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sleeping_Dogs_(video_game))
- Cover: a sticky cover system, enter and leave with one key. [PC Gamer review](https://pcgamer.com/sleeping-dogs-review)
- Cover vault slow motion: vaulting a cover object while aiming slows time. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sleeping_Dogs_(video_game))
- Slow motion headshots: chaining headshots in slow motion extends the effect. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sleeping_Dogs_(video_game))
- Slide shooting: sliding under obstacles while aiming triggers slow motion. [PC Gamer review](https://pcgamer.com/sleeping-dogs-review)
- Free aim: hold the aim button to aim a firearm. [Feral manual](https://www.feralinteractive.com/en/manual/sleepingdogs/latest/steam)
- Multi-target shooting: aim at several targets during slowed time. [Feral manual](https://www.feralinteractive.com/en/manual/sleepingdogs/latest/steam)
- Pistol stow: put away or draw a handgun, which is the one gun you can keep. [Feral manual](https://www.feralinteractive.com/en/manual/sleepingdogs/latest/steam)
- Gun scarcity: usually one firearm at a time, discarded when empty, handguns excepted. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sleeping_Dogs_(video_game))
- Firearm melee: a button hits with the gun in hand. [Feral manual](https://www.feralinteractive.com/en/manual/sleepingdogs/latest/steam)
- Grenade launcher: an alternate fire gun used to blow up cover. [GameSpot review](https://gamespot.com/sleeping-dogs/reviews/sleeping-dogs-review-6391428/)
- Alt fire toggle: switch between flashlight and grenade launcher. [Feral manual](https://www.feralinteractive.com/en/manual/sleepingdogs/latest/steam)
- Tire shooting: shooting a pursuer's tires from a moving car flips it in slow motion. [Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/games/sleeping_dogs/b/ps3/archive/2012/06/07/punching-pedestrians-to-death.aspx)
- Free running vault: tap sprint before an obstacle to vault it cleanly. [Feral manual](https://www.feralinteractive.com/en/manual/sleepingdogs/latest/steam)
- Rooftop climbing: climb walls and ledges during foot chases. [Feral manual](https://www.feralinteractive.com/en/manual/sleepingdogs/latest/steam)
- Foot chase: pursue or flee through alleys and across rooftops. [PC Gamer review](https://pcgamer.com/sleeping-dogs-review)
- Swimming: the hero can swim. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sleeping_Dogs_(video_game))
- Driving cars: arcade handling that hugs the road, with drifting. [GameSpot review](https://gamespot.com/sleeping-dogs/reviews/sleeping-dogs-review-6391428/)
- Handbrake turn: handbrake key slides the car through corners. [Feral manual](https://www.feralinteractive.com/en/manual/sleepingdogs/latest/steam)
- Ram attack: a sideways shunt button knocks other vehicles off the road or damages them. [GameSpot review](https://gamespot.com/sleeping-dogs/reviews/sleeping-dogs-review-6391428/)
- Action hijack: leap from a moving vehicle onto another and take the driver's seat. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sleeping_Dogs_(video_game))
- Action hijack indicator: a white arrow toward the target turns green when the jump is ready. [Feral manual](https://www.feralinteractive.com/en/manual/sleepingdogs/latest/steam)
- Action dismount: leave a fast vehicle in slow motion aiming and shoot it to explode. [Prima upgrades](https://primagames.com/eguides/sleeping-dogs-eguide/essentials/upgrades)
- Motorcycles: ride and shoot from them, lean forward and back for jumps and wheelies. [Feral manual](https://www.feralinteractive.com/en/manual/sleepingdogs/latest/steam)
- Boats: sampans, zodiacs, speedboats and yachts can be driven. [Feral manual](https://www.feralinteractive.com/en/manual/sleepingdogs/latest/steam)
- Shooting from a car: fire a weapon from a vehicle while driving. [GameSpot review](https://gamespot.com/sleeping-dogs/reviews/sleeping-dogs-review-6391428/)
- Car as weapon: run down crowds, though missions mostly forbid vehicles. [Game Revolution](https://www.gamerevolution.com/?p=3536)
- Horn: honk the car horn. [Feral manual](https://www.feralinteractive.com/en/manual/sleepingdogs/latest/steam)
- Look back: glance behind while driving. [Feral manual](https://www.feralinteractive.com/en/manual/sleepingdogs/latest/steam)
- Car theft: break a window to enter a parked car, or use Slim Jim to open it quietly. [Prima upgrades](https://primagames.com/eguides/sleeping-dogs-eguide/essentials/upgrades)
- Police trunk key: break into police car trunks to take weapons. [Prima upgrades](https://primagames.com/eguides/sleeping-dogs-eguide/essentials/upgrades)
- Hacking mini-game: crack codes in a Mastermind-style puzzle on safes and cameras. [GameSpot review](https://gamespot.com/sleeping-dogs/reviews/sleeping-dogs-review-6391428/)
- Camera hacking: hack spy cameras from the phone to spot drug deals. [PC Gamer review](https://pcgamer.com/sleeping-dogs-review)
- Safe cracking: open safes with the phone. [Prima upgrades](https://primagames.com/eguides/sleeping-dogs-eguide/essentials/upgrades)
- Bugging: plant a bug at a location such as a payphone. [Supercheats favours](https://www.supercheats.com/guides/sleeping-dogs/favors)
- Photography: take photos with the phone at marked spots. [Prima upgrades](https://primagames.com/eguides/sleeping-dogs-eguide/essentials/upgrades)
- Cell phone tracking: follow a phone signal as a police task. [GameSpot review](https://gamespot.com/sleeping-dogs/reviews/sleeping-dogs-review-6391428/)
- Karaoke: a mini-game moving an arrow up and down to match scrolling green bars. [GameSpot review](https://gamespot.com/sleeping-dogs/reviews/sleeping-dogs-review-6391428/)
- Fast talk: a key prompt during conversations picks a timed response. [Feral manual](https://www.feralinteractive.com/en/manual/sleepingdogs/latest/steam)
- Interact key: the same key counters, talks, enters vehicles and uses objects. [Feral manual](https://www.feralinteractive.com/en/manual/sleepingdogs/latest/steam)
- Prayer at shrines: pray at a shrine to add to max health progress. [Feral manual](https://www.feralinteractive.com/en/manual/sleepingdogs/latest/steam)
- Parking meter smash: ram parking meters with a car for about 500 each. [Game Revolution](https://www.gamerevolution.com/?p=3536)
- Taxi hire: hold the hire key beside a taxi, a tap hijacks it instead. [Game Revolution](https://www.gamerevolution.com/?p=3536)
- Tackle stun by sprint grab: sprint then grab disarms a tackled enemy. [Prima upgrades](https://primagames.com/eguides/sleeping-dogs-eguide/essentials/upgrades)

## 2. The town under the hood

- Heat meter: criminal acts raise a heat level shown on the HUD. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sleeping_Dogs_(video_game))
- Maximum heat: police pursue aggressively at the top level. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sleeping_Dogs_(video_game))
- Heat decay: breaking line of sight with officers lowers heat. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sleeping_Dogs_(video_game))
- Police search: officers keep looking after you leave and resume the chase on sight. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sleeping_Dogs_(video_game))
- Ramming and heat: ramming police cars does not raise the wanted meter. [GameSpot review](https://gamespot.com/sleeping-dogs/reviews/sleeping-dogs-review-6391428/)
- Escaping police: wrecking chasers by forcing them off the road or shooting tires is the fastest way. [Game Revolution](https://www.gamerevolution.com/?p=3536)
- Slim Jim and alarms: quiet entry avoids glass noise that alerts nearby police. [Prima upgrades](https://primagames.com/eguides/sleeping-dogs-eguide/essentials/upgrades)
- Action hijack reduced heat: a successful hijack during a chase lowers heat. [Prima upgrades](https://primagames.com/eguides/sleeping-dogs-eguide/essentials/upgrades)
- Arrest: police can arrest the hero and a mission restarts from the last checkpoint. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sleeping_Dogs_(video_game))
- Civilian injury alerts: punching a pedestrian shows a civilian injured alert, killing shows a pedestrian killed alert. [Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/games/sleeping_dogs/b/ps3/archive/2012/06/07/punching-pedestrians-to-death.aspx)
- Cop XP penalty: property damage and harming innocents lower Police XP. [Softpedia](https://news.softpedia.com/news/Sleeping-Dogs-Uses-XP-to-Give-Players-Moral-Choices-267266.shtml)
- Hostage mode: enemies seize civilians, leaving a short window to kill the taker without hurting the hostage. [Softpedia](https://news.softpedia.com/news/Sleeping-Dogs-Uses-XP-to-Give-Players-Moral-Choices-267266.shtml)
- NPC reaction to dress: outfits, accessories and vehicles change how people treat the hero. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sleeping_Dogs_(video_game))
- Enemy types: thugs, boxers who block often, big men and weapon users, each needing different tactics. [gameplay.tips](https://gameplay.tips/guides/sleeping-dogs-definitive-edition-useful-tips-and-tricks.html)
- Brawler: blocks most strikes, weak to grapples. [Game Revolution](https://www.gamerevolution.com/?p=3536)
- Grappler: strong against grabs, countered by kick finishers. [Prima upgrades](https://primagames.com/eguides/sleeping-dogs-eguide/essentials/upgrades)
- Heavy enemies: fat enemies cannot be thrown unless Face is active. [gameplay.tips](https://gameplay.tips/guides/sleeping-dogs-definitive-edition-useful-tips-and-tricks.html)
- Vendors: food stalls and vending machines sell health items around town. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sleeping_Dogs_(video_game))
- Street shakedowns: extort local vendors for protection money. [PC Gamer review](https://pcgamer.com/sleeping-dogs-review)
- Face currency: Face level gates what shops sell and discounts. [Prima upgrades](https://primagames.com/eguides/sleeping-dogs-eguide/essentials/upgrades)
- Face level 10 discount: Face level 10 gives 40 percent off clothes and cars. [Prima upgrades](https://primagames.com/eguides/sleeping-dogs-eguide/essentials/upgrades)
- Marked car payout: stolen marked cars pay more, and damage cuts the payout. [Game Revolution](https://www.gamerevolution.com/?p=3536)
- Hospital respawn: when health runs out the hero wakes at a hospital or clinic. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sleeping_Dogs_(video_game))
- Dual reputation: Triad standing and police standing are tracked together as an undercover cop. [Softpedia](https://news.softpedia.com/news/Sleeping-Dogs-Uses-XP-to-Give-Players-Moral-Choices-267266.shtml)
- Japan release changes: penalties for attacking civilians and no street race flagger. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sleeping_Dogs_(video_game))

## 3. Story and people

- Undercover premise: a police officer infiltrates Hong Kong Triads. [Softpedia](https://news.softpedia.com/news/Sleeping-Dogs-Uses-XP-to-Give-Players-Moral-Choices-267266.shtml)
- Story missions: sequences mixing fighting, driving, shooting and chases. [GameSpot review](https://gamespot.com/sleeping-dogs/reviews/sleeping-dogs-review-6391428/)
- Cases: police investigations, including serial killers and trafficking, in several parts. [PC Gamer review](https://pcgamer.com/sleeping-dogs-review)
- Undercover infiltration: disguise to enter crime scenes and a hospital. [PC Gamer review](https://pcgamer.com/sleeping-dogs-review)
- Favours: side jobs for strangers and contacts such as escorts, deliveries, revenge. [Supercheats favours](https://www.supercheats.com/guides/sleeping-dogs/favors)
- Escort favour: drive an ally out of enemy territory. [Supercheats favours](https://www.supercheats.com/guides/sleeping-dogs/favors)
- Police lure favour: draw cops to a place, then escape. [Supercheats favours](https://www.supercheats.com/guides/sleeping-dogs/favors)
- Convoy favour: protect or intercept vans and deliver stolen trucks. [Supercheats favours](https://www.supercheats.com/guides/sleeping-dogs/favors)
- Stunt favour: jump ramps, free run, or leap into the sea. [Supercheats favours](https://www.supercheats.com/guides/sleeping-dogs/favors)
- Free run favour: reach markers before time runs out. [Supercheats favours](https://www.supercheats.com/guides/sleeping-dogs/favors)
- Tire iron favour: smash a target car with a tire iron from its trunk. [Supercheats favours](https://www.supercheats.com/guides/sleeping-dogs/favors)
- Favours reveal map data: some unlock shrine, statue, camera and lockbox locations. [Supercheats favours](https://www.supercheats.com/guides/sleeping-dogs/favors)
- Dates: meeting a contact for karaoke, photos, driving or free running. [Supercheats favours](https://www.supercheats.com/guides/sleeping-dogs/favors)
- Date reward: each woman's date reveals one collectible type on the map. [Game Revolution](https://www.gamerevolution.com/?p=3536)

- Tiffany date: a karaoke date reveals jade statues. [Gameranx](https://gameranx.com/features/id/8819/article/sleeping-dogs-secrets-health-shrines-jade-statues-lockboxes-security-cameras/)
- Not-Ping date: a park date with a camera hack reveals security cameras. [Gameranx](https://gameranx.com/features/id/8819/article/sleeping-dogs-secrets-health-shrines-jade-statues-lockboxes-security-cameras/)
- Ilyana date: a parkour race against her reveals lockboxes. [Gameranx](https://gameranx.com/features/id/8819/article/sleeping-dogs-secrets-health-shrines-jade-statues-lockboxes-security-cameras/)
- Karaoke date pass mark: score above 90 percent to finish the date. [Supercheats favours](https://www.supercheats.com/guides/sleeping-dogs/favors)

- Tran jobs: phone assignments to steal and stash marked cars. [Game Revolution](https://www.gamerevolution.com/?p=3536)
- Drug busts: hack cameras to spot deals, then call police to arrest. [PC Gamer review](https://pcgamer.com/sleeping-dogs-review)
- Dojo practice: a North Point dojo lets the hero train against each enemy type. [gameplay.tips](https://gameplay.tips/guides/sleeping-dogs-definitive-edition-useful-tips-and-tricks.html)
- Sifu: a martial arts teacher who trades moves for jade statues. [Feral manual](https://www.feralinteractive.com/en/manual/sleepingdogs/latest/steam)
- Debt collection: a job collecting debts for a contact. [Supercheats favours](https://www.supercheats.com/guides/sleeping-dogs/favors)
- Security van jobs: hijack armoured vans. [Supercheats favours](https://www.supercheats.com/guides/sleeping-dogs/favors)
- Story expansions: Nightmare in North Point and Year of the Snake add their own missions. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sleeping_Dogs_(video_game))
- Year of the Snake: adds six missions and tasing and arresting civilians. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sleeping_Dogs_(video_game))
- Zodiac Tournament: fight arenas on an island, opens early. [gameplay.tips](https://gameplay.tips/guides/sleeping-dogs-definitive-edition-useful-tips-and-tricks.html)

- Garage: owned vehicles are stored and chosen from there. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sleeping_Dogs_(video_game))

- Map legend icons: drug busts, dates, missions, leads, favours, races, stores, safehouses. [Feral manual](https://www.feralinteractive.com/en/manual/sleepingdogs/latest/steam)
