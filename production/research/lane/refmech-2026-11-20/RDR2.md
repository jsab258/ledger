# Red Dead Redemption 2 gameplay mechanics (story mode)

Compiled from public wikis and guides, 2026-11-20. Outside study only.

## 1. Verbs

- Weapon wheel: holding the wheel button opens quick access to weapons and equipment. [source](https://www.powerpyx.com/red-dead-redemption-2-faq-tips-tricks/)
- Dead Eye activation: pressing the Dead Eye button while aiming slows time and reduces damage taken. [source](https://www.powerpyx.com/red-dead-redemption-2-faq-tips-tricks/)
- Dead Eye marking: while in Dead Eye the player marks targets and then fires to hit them all. [source](https://www.powerpyx.com/red-dead-redemption-2-faq-tips-tricks/)
- Dead Eye drain: the Dead Eye core drains while the ability is in use. [source](https://www.gtabase.com/red-dead-redemption-2/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-gameplay-guide-features-missions-health-satchel-and-more)
- Crouch: a button toggles Arthur crouching for stealth. [source](https://www.powerpyx.com/red-dead-redemption-2-faq-tips-tricks/)
- Lasso aim and throw: aiming then throwing a lasso ropes animals or enemies. [source](https://www.powerpyx.com/red-dead-redemption-2-faq-tips-tricks/)
- Hogtie: after roping a target the player approaches and hogties it. [source](https://www.powerpyx.com/red-dead-redemption-2-faq-tips-tricks/)
- Carry hogtied target: a hogtied person can be carried or placed on the horse. [source](https://www.powerpyx.com/red-dead-redemption-2-faq-tips-tricks/)
- Hogtie escape: an unhogtied roped target breaks free after a short time. [source](https://www.powerpyx.com/red-dead-redemption-2-faq-tips-tricks/)
- Focus on NPC: holding the focus button on a person lets Arthur interact with them. [source](https://twinfinite.net/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-talk-to-npcs-start-fight-how/?amp=1)
- Call out: a nearby person out of reach can be called out to stop. [source](https://twinfinite.net/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-talk-to-npcs-start-fight-how/?amp=1)
- Greet: a button press greets or is nice to a focused person. [source](https://twinfinite.net/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-talk-to-npcs-start-fight-how/?amp=1)
- Antagonize: a button press antagonizes a focused person. [source](https://twinfinite.net/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-talk-to-npcs-start-fight-how/?amp=1)
- Repeated antagonizing: pestering can make a person fight, shoot or ask for a duel. [source](https://twinfinite.net/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-talk-to-npcs-start-fight-how/?amp=1)
- Rob a person: a button press robs a focused person at gunpoint. [source](https://twinfinite.net/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-talk-to-npcs-start-fight-how/?amp=1)
- Holster before talking: pointing a gun at people makes them react badly so the weapon is holstered first. [source](https://twinfinite.net/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-talk-to-npcs-start-fight-how/?amp=1)
- Intervene in a fight: a prompt lets Arthur step into an existing fight. [source](https://twinfinite.net/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-talk-to-npcs-start-fight-how/?amp=1)
- Unprovoked attack: hitting a person at any time is a dishonorable crime that can raise wanted. [source](https://twinfinite.net/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-talk-to-npcs-start-fight-how/?amp=1)
- Bullet jump: jumping while diving gets Arthur to safety faster. [source](https://segmentnext.com/red-dead-redemption-2/beginners-guide)
- Warning shot: firing into the air during a robbery scares people off. [source](https://segmentnext.com/red-dead-redemption-2/beginners-guide)
- Tap actions: some actions need repeated taps and are tuned by Tap Assist. [source](https://segmentnext.com/red-dead-redemption-2/beginners-guide)
- Horse bonding compliment: complimenting the horse roughly every 15 seconds raises bonding. [source](https://segmentnext.com/red-dead-redemption-2/beginners-guide)
- Horse stamina tap: tapping the gallop button instead of holding it saves horse stamina. [source](https://segmentnext.com/red-dead-redemption-2/beginners-guide)
- Bows for hunting: bows are quiet and leave clean damage. [source](https://segmentnext.com/red-dead-redemption-2/beginners-guide)
- Animal attract: an attract button raises an animal's head for a headshot. [source](https://segmentnext.com/red-dead-redemption-2/beginners-guide)
- Looting: bodies and containers such as cabinets, drawers and wardrobes can be looted for money and ammo. [source](https://segmentnext.com/red-dead-redemption-2/beginners-guide)
- Call horse: pressing up whistles the horse, and the range grows with bonding. [source](https://www.gtabase.com/red-dead-redemption-2/guides/full-guide-looking-after-your-horse-in-red-dead-redemption-2)
- Horse rear: with bonding level 2 a held-button tap makes the horse rear. [source](https://www.gtabase.com/red-dead-redemption-2/guides/full-guide-looking-after-your-horse-in-red-dead-redemption-2)
- Skid turn and stop: with bonding level 3 the horse skids when turning or stopping. [source](https://www.gtabase.com/red-dead-redemption-2/guides/full-guide-looking-after-your-horse-in-red-dead-redemption-2)
- Piaffe and drift: with bonding level 4 the horse can piaffe and drift. [source](https://www.gtabase.com/red-dead-redemption-2/guides/full-guide-looking-after-your-horse-in-red-dead-redemption-2)
- Saddle and unsaddle: holding up saddles the horse and a held button removes it. [source](https://www.gtabase.com/red-dead-redemption-2/guides/full-guide-looking-after-your-horse-in-red-dead-redemption-2)
- Horse refuses calls: a horse will not come if far away, stabled, dead, or the player is indoors. [source](https://www.gtabase.com/red-dead-redemption-2/guides/full-guide-looking-after-your-horse-in-red-dead-redemption-2)
- Study animals: viewing an animal through a scope, binoculars or up close adds it to the compendium. [source](https://www.gamesradar.com/red-dead-redemption-2-hunting-tips)
- Wind and scent: wind direction and body odor affect whether animals notice the player. [source](https://www.gamesradar.com/red-dead-redemption-2-hunting-tips)
- Clean kill: aiming for the neck or heart gives a one-shot kill that protects pelt quality. [source](https://www.gamesradar.com/red-dead-redemption-2-hunting-tips)
- Knife finish: an animal can be finished with a knife. [source](https://www.gamesradar.com/red-dead-redemption-2-hunting-tips)
- Bait: placing the right bait attracts specific animals to an area. [source](https://www.gamesradar.com/red-dead-redemption-2-hunting-tips)
- Weapon choice by game: varmint rifle for small game, scoped rifle for large game, bow for silence. [source](https://www.gamesradar.com/red-dead-redemption-2-hunting-tips)
- Split-point and poison arrows: special ammunition changes damage and stalking. [source](https://www.gamesradar.com/red-dead-redemption-2-hunting-tips)
- Weapon inspect and clean: inspecting a weapon lets Arthur clean it with gun oil. [source](https://www.powerpyx.com/red-dead-redemption-2-faq-tips-tricks/)
- Dirty weapon penalty: dirty or rusty weapons deal less damage and reload slower. [source](https://www.powerpyx.com/red-dead-redemption-2-faq-tips-tricks/)
- Autopilot riding: holding a button lets the horse follow a road or map target. [source](https://www.powerpyx.com/red-dead-redemption-2-faq-tips-tricks/)
- Autopilot to waypoint: setting a map target lets the horse travel there alone. [source](https://segmentnext.com/red-dead-redemption-2/beginners-guide)
- Fishing rod equip: the rod is equipped and bait or lures are swapped through the item wheel. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-fishing-guide/)
- Bait cast: cast directly onto a fish and aim with the triggers. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-fishing-guide/)
- Bait flick: an occasional rod flick attracts fish but too many scare them off. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-fishing-guide/)
- Hook prompt: a Hook Fish prompt appears when a fish bites. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-fishing-guide/)
- Lure cast and reel: lures are cast past the fish and reeled in slowly. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-fishing-guide/)
- Fight a large fish: moving the rod against the pull stops line being dragged out. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-fishing-guide/)
- Line snap: pulling too hard snaps the line. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-fishing-guide/)
- Reel when calm: reeling only works well while the fish is not struggling. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-fishing-guide/)
- Pet a dog: petting dogs raises honor. [source](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/red-dead-2-guide-to-honor-how-morality-and-the-hon/1100-6462777)
- Throw back small fish: releasing a fish that is too small raises honor. [source](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/red-dead-2-guide-to-honor-how-morality-and-the-hon/1100-6462777)
- Mercy kill an animal: finishing a wounded animal after a missed clean shot avoids an honor loss. [source](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/red-dead-2-guide-to-honor-how-morality-and-the-hon/1100-6462777)
- Intimidate passengers: on a train Arthur threatens passengers to reach the lockbox. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-robbery-guide/)
- Jump aboard a moving train: Arthur rides alongside a train and jumps on. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-robbery-guide/)
- Safe cracking: bank safes are opened by timed analog stick inputs with vibration as the cue. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-robbery-guide/)
- Dynamite safe: a safe can be blown open fast but it alerts the law at once. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-robbery-guide/)
- Shopkeeper hold-up: choosing the rob option makes the clerk open the register. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-robbery-guide/)
- Five Finger Fillet (gambling): repeat button sequences without missing to win laps. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/guides/mini-games-guide/)
- Blackjack (gambling): hit or stay against a dealer aiming for 21. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/guides/mini-games-guide/)
- Poker (gambling): Texas Hold'em style hands with bet, match or fold. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/guides/mini-games-guide/)
- Dominoes (gambling): match tiles to empty the hand and race to 60 points. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/guides/mini-games-guide/)

## 2. The town under the hood

- Witness report starts wanted: wanted status begins only when a witness reports a crime. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/wiki/wanted-system/)
- Wanted stages: a crime moves from witness through reported, investigating, unknown suspect, identified and bounty to law searching. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/wiki/wanted-system/)
- Skipped stages: stages are skipped if the same crime was done in the same area in the same outfit. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/wiki/wanted-system/)
- Lawmen find crime scenes: lawmen can find a crime without a witness if they stumble on it. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/wiki/wanted-system/)
- Dead or alive: hostile towns can escalate to a dead or alive stage. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/wiki/wanted-system/)
- Lockdown: twelve offenses within 60 minutes trigger a town lockdown. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/wiki/wanted-system/)
- White icon witness: a white witness does not know who the player is until the crime is reported and lawmen identify him. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/wiki/wanted-system/)
- Red icon witness: a red witness recognises the player so a bounty is placed right away. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/wiki/wanted-system/)
- Threaten witness: aiming, drawing or firing a warning shot at a witness may keep them quiet. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/wiki/wanted-system/)
- Kill witness: killing a witness is possible but those who see the killing raise the crime's severity. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/wiki/wanted-system/)
- Bandana hides identity: a bandana or large mask stops white witnesses recognising the player. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/wiki/wanted-system/)
- Mask from fence: masks are bought from fences and equipped through the horse menu. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/wiki/wanted-system/)
- Disguise by outfit: changing outfit, hair and facial hair helps avoid recognition. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/wiki/wanted-system/)
- Mask fails after prior crime: a mask does not help in an area where the player already committed a crime. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/wiki/wanted-system/)
- Lawmen recognise on contact: lawmen who find the player identify him regardless of a mask. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/wiki/wanted-system/)
- Prior crime recognition: earlier crimes in a town can trigger instant recognition. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/wiki/wanted-system/)
- Bounty amount: a bounty amount shows under the wanted meter. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/wiki/wanted-system/)
- Red search zone: the player must leave a red mini-map zone to clear wanted. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/wiki/wanted-system/)
- Resisting raises bounty: fighting lawmen increases the bounty. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/wiki/wanted-system/)
- Surrender: giving up avoids being shot on sight but leads to jail. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/wiki/wanted-system/)
- Jail deduction: jail time takes a percentage off the bounty. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/wiki/wanted-system/)
- Gang frees player: gang mates may free the player from jail at no cost. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/wiki/wanted-system/)
- Wanted fades: the wanted meter fades from red to grey after leaving the zone. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/wiki/wanted-system/)
- Bounty hunters spawn: bounty hunters can appear 12 in-game hours after an unpaid crime. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/wiki/wanted-system/)
- Hunters on the mini-map: bounty hunters show as red dots and engage once they recognise the player. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/wiki/wanted-system/)
- Bounty scales hunters: a bigger bounty means more frequent hunters. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/wiki/wanted-system/)
- Road ambush and checkpoints: hunters can ambush in the wild or at road checkpoints. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/wiki/wanted-system/)
- Lockdown hunters: lockdown makes hunters and checkpoints more common. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/wiki/wanted-system/)
- Pay at post office: paying at any post office clears the bounty in that state. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/wiki/wanted-system/)
- State jurisdiction: wanted status in one state does not carry into another. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/wiki/wanted-system/)
- Minor theft no bounty: minor crimes make no bounty if the player was not identified. [source](https://www.shacknews.com/article/108138/how-the-wanted-and-bounty-systems-work-in-red-dead-redemption-2)
- Bounty size range: serious identified crimes carry bounties from about 5 dollars to over 100. [source](https://www.shacknews.com/article/108138/how-the-wanted-and-bounty-systems-work-in-red-dead-redemption-2)
- Bounty area message: entering an active bounty area shows an on-screen message. [source](https://www.shacknews.com/article/108138/how-the-wanted-and-bounty-systems-work-in-red-dead-redemption-2)
- Killing hunters raises bounty: killing bounty hunters raises the bounty. [source](https://www.shacknews.com/article/108138/how-the-wanted-and-bounty-systems-work-in-red-dead-redemption-2)
- Mission bounties avoidable: bounties from story missions can be dodged by avoiding the area. [source](https://www.shacknews.com/article/108138/how-the-wanted-and-bounty-systems-work-in-red-dead-redemption-2)
- Failed intimidation: if intimidating a witness fails the wanted level is assigned anyway. [source](https://www.shacknews.com/article/108138/how-the-wanted-and-bounty-systems-work-in-red-dead-redemption-2)
- Heavy patrols after reports: reported crimes bring well-armed law patrols. [source](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/red-dead-2-guide-to-honor-how-morality-and-the-hon/1100-6462777)
- Witnesses harder to silence at low honor: low honor makes witnesses harder to pay off or threaten. [source](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/red-dead-2-guide-to-honor-how-morality-and-the-hon/1100-6462777)
- Missions blocked by wanted: some missions are unavailable while wanted. [source](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/red-dead-2-guide-to-honor-how-morality-and-the-hon/1100-6462777)
- Bounties regional: bounties are tracked separately by region. [source](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/red-dead-2-guide-to-honor-how-morality-and-the-hon/1100-6462777)
- Paying bounty does not restore honor: clearing a bounty at the post office leaves honor unchanged. [source](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/red-dead-2-guide-to-honor-how-morality-and-the-hon/1100-6462777)
- Honor-linked killcams: kill cams get more gruesome at low honor. [source](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/red-dead-2-guide-to-honor-how-morality-and-the-hon/1100-6462777)
- Honor-linked music: the music changes with the honor state. [source](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/red-dead-2-guide-to-honor-how-morality-and-the-hon/1100-6462777)
- Honor-linked discounts: a good Arthur gets store discounts. [source](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/red-dead-2-guide-to-honor-how-morality-and-the-hon/1100-6462777)
- Honor-linked rewards: helped strangers may reward Arthur later. [source](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/red-dead-2-guide-to-honor-how-morality-and-the-hon/1100-6462777)
- Journal reflects choices: journal entries change with honor. [source](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/red-dead-2-guide-to-honor-how-morality-and-the-hon/1100-6462777)
- Honor tilt near extremes: near the extremes one opposite act shifts honor a lot. [source](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/red-dead-2-guide-to-honor-how-morality-and-the-hon/1100-6462777)
- Honor recovery: honor recovers by good deeds. [source](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/red-dead-2-guide-to-honor-how-morality-and-the-hon/1100-6462777)
- Honor lowered by horse trample: running people down with the horse lowers honor. [source](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/red-dead-2-guide-to-honor-how-morality-and-the-hon/1100-6462777)
- Honor lowered by killing: killing people lowers honor. [source](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/red-dead-2-guide-to-honor-how-morality-and-the-hon/1100-6462777)

- Honor lowered by mugging: mugging people lowers honor. [source](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/red-dead-2-guide-to-honor-how-morality-and-the-hon/1100-6462777)

- Honor raised by greeting: greeting people raises honor. [source](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/red-dead-2-guide-to-honor-how-morality-and-the-hon/1100-6462777)
- Honor raised by helping travellers: helping roadside travellers raises honor. [source](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/red-dead-2-guide-to-honor-how-morality-and-the-hon/1100-6462777)
- Help gives honor: saving a man from an animal attack gives plus 10 honor. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-chance-encounters-guide)
- Standing by costs honor: standing by during an attack gives minus 10. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-chance-encounters-guide)

- Ambush encounters: gang ambushes have no honor effect for fighting or fleeing. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-chance-encounters-guide)
- Alley lure: a woman lures Arthur into a Saint Denis alley and killing her costs honor. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-chance-encounters-guide)
- Coach robbery encounter: saving coach passengers gives plus 10 honor. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-chance-encounters-guide)
- Lawmen avoid wanted fences: fences refuse to deal while the player is wanted. [source](https://www.powerpyx.com/red-dead-redemption-2-faq-tips-tricks/)
- Fence asks no questions: fences buy goods without asking where they came from. [source](https://www.powerpyx.com/red-dead-redemption-2-faq-tips-tricks/)

## 3. Story and people

- Chapters: the story is split into chapters shown in the Pause menu progress panel. [source](https://www.gtabase.com/red-dead-redemption-2/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-gameplay-guide-features-missions-health-satchel-and-more)
- Mission medals: finishing a mission earns a gold, silver or bronze medal. [source](https://www.gtabase.com/red-dead-redemption-2/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-gameplay-guide-features-missions-health-satchel-and-more)
- Mission replay: completed chapters and missions can be replayed from the Pause menu. [source](https://www.gtabase.com/red-dead-redemption-2/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-gameplay-guide-features-missions-health-satchel-and-more)
- Stranger question mark: a question mark blip shows a stranger who wants to talk. [source](https://www.gtabase.com/red-dead-redemption-2/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-gameplay-guide-features-missions-health-satchel-and-more)
- Time-limited missions: missions marked white near camp expire after a while. [source](https://segmentnext.com/red-dead-redemption-2/beginners-guide)
- Failed mission skip: after three failed attempts the game offers a checkpoint skip. [source](https://segmentnext.com/red-dead-redemption-2/beginners-guide)
- Mission marker blips: story missions are marked on the map and radar. [source](https://segmentnext.com/red-dead-redemption-2/beginners-guide)
- Solo missions keep loot: doing missions without allies keeps loot to Arthur. [source](https://segmentnext.com/red-dead-redemption-2/beginners-guide)
- Chance encounter helping: many encounters let Arthur help, ignore or harm a stranger. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-chance-encounters-guide)
- Debt encounter: helping a debtor gives plus 5 honor. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-chance-encounters-guide)
- Wealthy couple encounter: recovering stolen goods for a couple gives plus 10. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-chance-encounters-guide)
- Camping woman encounter: accepting her company or rudeness changes honor. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-chance-encounters-guide)
- Family on the run: terrorising the family costs honor. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-chance-encounters-guide)
- Conman encounter: bringing the conman back costs honor. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-chance-encounters-guide)
- Burial and cholera encounters: harming people there gives large honor penalties. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-chance-encounters-guide)
- Del Lobo gang and the law: not intervening costs honor. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-chance-encounters-guide)
- Bill Williamson encounter: agreeing to help free him gives honor. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-chance-encounters-guide)
- Bounty hunter missions: bounty hunter missions are a tracked activity. [source](https://www.rdr2.org/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-100-completion-guide/)
