## 2. The town under the hood

- wanted level: stars: one to five stars, each more aggressive than the last. [source](https://www.grandtheftwiki.com/Wanted_Level_in_GTA_V)
- wanted level: stars stack: repeated offences in police sight raise the level. [source](https://www.grandtheftwiki.com/Wanted_Level_in_GTA_V)
- wanted level: one star offences: hit-and-runs, vehicle theft, public gunfire and the like. [source](https://www.grandtheftwiki.com/Wanted_Level_in_GTA_V)
- wanted level: two stars: resisting arrest, harming guards or paramedics, robbing stores. [source](https://www.grandtheftwiki.com/Wanted_Level_in_GTA_V)
- wanted level: three stars: attacking police or soldiers, airport tarmac entry. [source](https://www.grandtheftwiki.com/Wanted_Level_in_GTA_V)
- wanted level: four stars: restricted-site entry such as Fort Zancudo or the naval port. [source](https://www.grandtheftwiki.com/Wanted_Level_in_GTA_V)
- wanted level: five stars: relentless retaliation with all units. [source](https://www.grandtheftwiki.com/Wanted_Level_in_GTA_V)
- police need witnesses or sight: a nearby cop starts a chase at once; otherwise a witness must call it in. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/gtav/police-how-to-run-away/z555b9)
- silencing a witness caller: stopping the caller prevents the report. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/gtav/police-how-to-run-away/z555b9)
- wanted level: field of vision indicators: narrow on foot, moderate for cars, wide for helicopters. [source](https://www.grandtheftwiki.com/Wanted_Level_in_GTA_V)
- wanted level: cooldown phase: stars flash once the police lose sight. [source](https://www.grandtheftwiki.com/Wanted_Level_in_GTA_V)
- losing the police by breaking line of sight: hide out of view and the search ends. [source](https://www.grandtheftwiki.com/Wanted_Level_in_GTA_V)
- wanted level: hidden spot greys the arrow: a secluded spot turns the location arrow grey. [source](https://www.grandtheftwiki.com/Wanted_Level_in_GTA_V)
- wanted level: cooldown times: 30 seconds at one star up to 90 seconds at five. [source](https://www.grandtheftwiki.com/Wanted_Level_in_GTA_V)
- wanted level: thorough searches at high levels: officers leave cars to search on foot. [source](https://www.grandtheftwiki.com/Wanted_Level_in_GTA_V)
- wanted level: pursuit resumes on sight: entering a cop's clear view restarts the chase. [source](https://www.grandtheftwiki.com/Wanted_Level_in_GTA_V)
- vehicle swapping to shake police: dispatch tracks colour, make and model, so change out of sight. [source](https://www.grandtheftwiki.com/Wanted_Level_in_GTA_V)
- respray clears wanted level: repair or customisation shops clear it outside a chase. [source](https://www.grandtheftwiki.com/Wanted_Level_in_GTA_V)
- safehouse save clears low wanted level: not at three stars or higher. [source](https://www.grandtheftwiki.com/Wanted_Level_in_GTA_V)
- quick save while wanted: reloading puts you in cooldown. [source](https://www.grandtheftwiki.com/Wanted_Level_in_GTA_V)
- arrest: surrender in vehicle: drivers give up at once when held at gunpoint. [source](https://www.grandtheftwiki.com/Wanted_Level_in_GTA_V)
- arrest: resisting on foot escalates: resisting climbs to three stars. [source](https://www.grandtheftwiki.com/Wanted_Level_in_GTA_V)
- busted: bail and loss of gear: arrest costs money, ammo and armor. [source](https://www.grandtheftwiki.com/Wanted_Level_in_GTA_V)
- death: hospital fee, weapons kept: dying keeps ammo and charges a fee. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/gtav/police-how-to-run-away/z555b9)
- police units: patrols: one star sends cars to arrest. [source](https://www.grandtheftwiki.com/Wanted_Level_in_GTA_V)
- police: backup and shoot-to-kill at two stars: more cars and more shooting. [source](https://www.grandtheftwiki.com/Wanted_Level_in_GTA_V)
- police: vests and roadblocks at three stars: single-car roadblocks, sometimes spike strips. [source](https://www.grandtheftwiki.com/Wanted_Level_in_GTA_V)
- police: helicopter sharpshooters: helicopters carry rifle gunmen from three stars. [source](https://www.grandtheftwiki.com/Wanted_Level_in_GTA_V)
