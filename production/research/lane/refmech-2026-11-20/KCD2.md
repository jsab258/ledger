# Kingdom Come: Deliverance II (2025, Warhorse Studios): gameplay mechanics from public guides

Researched 2026-11-19 from outside sources only (guides, wikis, reviews). Each line is one mechanic with a fetched source.

## 1. Verbs (what the player's hands do: moving, fighting, stealth, using things)

- block: hold the block button to stop attacks with weapon or shield, but it drains stamina and does not stop combos. [Beebom](https://beebom.com/how-to-block-parry-dodge-riposte-in-kingdom-come-deliverance-2)
- perfect block (parry): press block at the last moment inside the shield-icon window, costs no extra stamina and prevents combos. [Beebom](https://beebom.com/how-to-block-parry-dodge-riposte-in-kingdom-come-deliverance-2)
- riposte: after a parry, attack during the crossed-swords window to counter-strike, at a high stamina cost. [Beebom](https://beebom.com/how-to-block-parry-dodge-riposte-in-kingdom-come-deliverance-2)
- dodge: tap the dodge key to duck under an attack or make space, using stamina. [Beebom](https://beebom.com/how-to-block-parry-dodge-riposte-in-kingdom-come-deliverance-2)
- perfect dodge: dodge while the shield icon shows during an enemy swing, best suited to unarmed fighting. [Beebom](https://beebom.com/how-to-block-parry-dodge-riposte-in-kingdom-come-deliverance-2)
- attack direction: the player picks the direction of each strike. [Primagames controls](https://primagames.com/tips/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-complete-controls-pc-controller)
- special attack: a separate button performs a special attack. [Primagames controls](https://primagames.com/tips/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-complete-controls-pc-controller)
- abort attack: an attack in progress can be cancelled. [Primagames controls](https://primagames.com/tips/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-complete-controls-pc-controller)
- lock on: target a single opponent. [Primagames controls](https://primagames.com/tips/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-complete-controls-pc-controller)
- next and previous opponent: switch the target between nearby enemies. [Primagames controls](https://primagames.com/tips/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-complete-controls-pc-controller)
- combat free look: look around freely while fighting. [Primagames controls](https://primagames.com/tips/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-complete-controls-pc-controller)
- combo: chained attacks that cost stamina, with weapon perks that refund or halve that cost. [PCGamesN perks](https://www.pcgamesn.com/kingdom-come-deliverance-2/perks)
- master strike: a sword-only counterattack unlocked by paying the trainer and winning a duel. [Method combat](https://method.gg/kcd2/7-essential-combat-tips-for-kingdom-come-deliverance-2)
- opening strike: hitting an enemy at the moment they expose themselves gives a free hit. [Method combat](https://method.gg/kcd2/7-essential-combat-tips-for-kingdom-come-deliverance-2)
- spacing: keeping distance controls the pace of a duel. [Method combat](https://method.gg/kcd2/7-essential-combat-tips-for-kingdom-come-deliverance-2)
- herding: guiding enemies along a path to isolate one in a multi-enemy fight. [Method combat](https://method.gg/kcd2/7-essential-combat-tips-for-kingdom-come-deliverance-2)
- stamina in combat: every swing, block and dodge drains it and armour fails to protect once it runs out. [Sportskeeda tips](https://www.sportskeeda.com/esports/best-beginner-tips-kingdom-come-deliverance-2)
- stamina regeneration: stamina comes back faster when distance is put between you and enemies. [Beebom](https://beebom.com/how-to-block-parry-dodge-riposte-in-kingdom-come-deliverance-2)
- bow draw and release: draw an arrow, then release it to fire. [Primagames controls](https://primagames.com/tips/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-complete-controls-pc-controller)
- ranged first shot bonus: the Hal Shot First perk makes the first shot deal 15 percent more damage. [PCGamesN perks](https://www.pcgamesn.com/kingdom-come-deliverance-2/perks)
- crippling shot: a marksmanship perk where hits reduce enemy stamina and combat ability. [PCGamesN perks](https://www.pcgamesn.com/kingdom-come-deliverance-2/perks)
- shield use: a shield replaces the weapon when blocking if one is equipped. [Beebom](https://beebom.com/how-to-block-parry-dodge-riposte-in-kingdom-come-deliverance-2)
- battle cry: a Speech perk gives a damage boost after using a battle cry. [PCGamesN perks](https://www.pcgamesn.com/kingdom-come-deliverance-2/perks)
- unarmed combat: fists have their own combos and perks. [PCGamesN perks](https://www.pcgamesn.com/kingdom-come-deliverance-2/perks)
- stealth kill: sneak behind a target with a dagger to kill them. [Method combat](https://method.gg/kcd2/7-essential-combat-tips-for-kingdom-come-deliverance-2)
- knock out: pressing the attack prompt from stealth can subdue a sleeping guard instead of killing. [PC Gamer branded](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/rpg/i-messed-up-so-bad-in-kingdom-come-deliverance-2-that-i-was-literally-branded-a-criminal-and-hours-later-im-still-shunned-by-society/)
- takedown prompt: a blue dagger icon appears and must be pressed quickly to finish the takedown. [Segmentnext tips](https://segmentnext.com/8-beginner-tips-in-kingdom-come-deliverance-2)
- takedown escape block: a green shield icon appears if you are slow, and block stops the target escaping. [Segmentnext tips](https://segmentnext.com/8-beginner-tips-in-kingdom-come-deliverance-2)
- crouch: sneaking makes the player quieter and harder to spot. [Mein-MMO thieves](https://mein-mmo.de/en/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-picking-locks-sneaking-pickpocketing-a-big-guide-for-thieves,1223739)
- toggle crouch: crouch can be held or toggled. [Primagames controls](https://primagames.com/tips/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-complete-controls-pc-controller)
- sprint: runs faster. [Primagames controls](https://primagames.com/tips/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-complete-controls-pc-controller)
- toggle walk: switches between walking and running. [Primagames controls](https://primagames.com/tips/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-complete-controls-pc-controller)
- jump: the character can jump. [Primagames controls](https://primagames.com/tips/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-complete-controls-pc-controller)
- throw stone: throw a stone to distract guards. [Mein-MMO thieves](https://mein-mmo.de/en/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-picking-locks-sneaking-pickpocketing-a-big-guide-for-thieves,1223739)
- visor: lower or raise the helmet visor, which hides the face for disguise. [Primagames controls](https://primagames.com/tips/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-complete-controls-pc-controller)
- torch: equip a torch for light and to avoid night fines. [Primagames controls](https://primagames.com/tips/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-complete-controls-pc-controller)
- lockpicking minigame: keep the marker on the lock's weak spot while turning, or the pick jams and breaks. [Mein-MMO thieves](https://mein-mmo.de/en/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-picking-locks-sneaking-pickpocketing-a-big-guide-for-thieves,1223739)
- lock difficulty tiers: very easy, easy, hard and very hard, with hard needing level 4 and very hard level 9. [Method stealth](https://method.gg/kcd2/lockpicking-and-stealth-101-the-best-build-to-avoid-guards-and-crack-any-chest)
- pick breaking: a broken lockpick is lost and makes noise, so spares are carried. [Method stealth](https://method.gg/kcd2/lockpicking-and-stealth-101-the-best-build-to-avoid-guards-and-crack-any-chest)
- pickpocketing minigame: hold to rummage pockets, and a red indicator warns that the victim noticed you. [Mein-MMO thieves](https://mein-mmo.de/en/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-picking-locks-sneaking-pickpocketing-a-big-guide-for-thieves,1223739)
- pickpocket time window: after preparing, there is a time limit to uncover, mark and return items to the exit. [Mein-MMO thieves](https://mein-mmo.de/en/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-picking-locks-sneaking-pickpocketing-a-big-guide-for-thieves,1223739)
- hidden pickpocket items: some items show as question marks until identified. [Mein-MMO thieves](https://mein-mmo.de/en/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-picking-locks-sneaking-pickpocketing-a-big-guide-for-thieves,1223739)
- search of the unconscious: knocked out or dead targets can be searched without the minigame. [Mein-MMO thieves](https://mein-mmo.de/en/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-picking-locks-sneaking-pickpocketing-a-big-guide-for-thieves,1223739)
- distraction with meat: meat distracts guard dogs. [Mein-MMO thieves](https://mein-mmo.de/en/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-picking-locks-sneaking-pickpocketing-a-big-guide-for-thieves,1223739)
- pat: pet the dog or horse. [Primagames controls](https://primagames.com/tips/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-complete-controls-pc-controller)
- call dog: hold to call the dog to you. [Primagames controls](https://primagames.com/tips/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-complete-controls-pc-controller)
- companion control: give commands to a companion. [Primagames controls](https://primagames.com/tips/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-complete-controls-pc-controller)
- use, talk, pick up: separate interaction verbs for objects, conversation and items. [Primagames controls](https://primagames.com/tips/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-complete-controls-pc-controller)
- drop items: items can be dropped from the inventory. [Primagames controls](https://primagames.com/tips/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-complete-controls-pc-controller)
- unhorse by wound: serious wounds or a halberd hit knock the rider off the horse. [Wiki.gg Horse](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Horse)
- smithing minigame: heat the part in the furnace, hammer it on the anvil, flip, reheat, then quench. [Sportskeeda smithing](https://www.sportskeeda.com/esports/blacksmithing-kingdom-come-deliverance-2-explained)
- furnace bellows: blow air to raise furnace heat while watching the colour and avoiding fire. [Sportskeeda smithing](https://www.sportskeeda.com/esports/blacksmithing-kingdom-come-deliverance-2-explained)
- hammering: strike evenly along the workpiece, and sparks show effective hits, with a heavy strike using stamina. [Sportskeeda smithing](https://www.sportskeeda.com/esports/blacksmithing-kingdom-come-deliverance-2-explained)
- alchemy minigame: boil in a pot using an hourglass, turning it when vapour rises rather than bubbles. [PC Gamer alchemy](https://pcgamer.com/games/rpg/after-75-hours-i-finally-cracked-what-i-was-doing-wrong-in-kingdom-come-deliverance-2s-terribly-explained-alchemy-system)
- vigorous boiling: bellows make a separate boil state used only when a recipe asks for it. [PC Gamer alchemy](https://pcgamer.com/games/rpg/after-75-hours-i-finally-cracked-what-i-was-doing-wrong-in-kingdom-come-deliverance-2s-terribly-explained-alchemy-system)
- grindstone sharpening: restores some weapon quality. [Method combat](https://method.gg/kcd2/7-essential-combat-tips-for-kingdom-come-deliverance-2)
- praying at shrines: grants a buff against mortal injury and restores 25 percent health. [Segmentnext tips](https://segmentnext.com/8-beginner-tips-in-kingdom-come-deliverance-2)
- bandaging: stops bleeding but does not heal the injury. [Segmentnext tips](https://segmentnext.com/8-beginner-tips-in-kingdom-come-deliverance-2)
- skip time: advance the clock by choice. [Primagames controls](https://primagames.com/tips/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-complete-controls-pc-controller)
- change clothing preset: switch between saved outfits. [Primagames controls](https://primagames.com/tips/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-complete-controls-pc-controller)

## 2. The town under the hood (simulation: guards, witnesses, recognition, gossip, NPC schedules, time of day, weather, economy, reputation, crime systems)

- witness: an onlooker who sees a crime may run away or go to a guard. [Fextralife crime](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.fextralife.com/Crime+System)
- crime report: witnesses who see a crime report it to a guard immediately. [Deltias crime](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=134932)
- confrontation: onlookers may confront the player directly, bravely or recklessly. [Deltias crime](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=134932)
- delayed discovery: unseen crimes can still be found and reported later. [Deltias crime](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=134932)
- witness prevention: acting sneakily or removing a witness before they reach the authorities avoids the report. [Fextralife crime](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.fextralife.com/Crime+System)
- pursuit: once a crime is reported the authorities hunt the player. [Fextralife crime](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.fextralife.com/Crime+System)
- crime list: killing innocents, harming people or domestic animals, stealing, breaking in and disturbing the peace. [Deltias crime](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=134932)
- fine: the authorities can fine the player, scaled to the crime's severity. [Fextralife crime](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.fextralife.com/Crime+System)
- jail: serious crimes can lead to imprisonment. [Fextralife crime](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.fextralife.com/Crime+System)
- minor-offence fines: standing outside a tavern after closing or walking at night without a torch can be fined. [PC Gamer branded](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/rpg/i-messed-up-so-bad-in-kingdom-come-deliverance-2-that-i-was-literally-branded-a-criminal-and-hours-later-im-still-shunned-by-society/)
- trespass: entering a war camp counts as its own offence. [PC Gamer branded](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/rpg/i-messed-up-so-bad-in-kingdom-come-deliverance-2-that-i-was-literally-branded-a-criminal-and-hours-later-im-still-shunned-by-society/)
- crime linking: guards and victims connect separate crimes so the player is charged for the whole chain. [PC Gamer branded](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/rpg/i-messed-up-so-bad-in-kingdom-come-deliverance-2-that-i-was-literally-branded-a-criminal-and-hours-later-im-still-shunned-by-society/)
- horse theft: riding off on someone else's horse is a crime, and poached animals yield stolen goods. [PC Gamer branded](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/rpg/i-messed-up-so-bad-in-kingdom-come-deliverance-2-that-i-was-literally-branded-a-criminal-and-hours-later-im-still-shunned-by-society/)
- surrender order: guards may order the player to surrender rather than ask their business. [PC Gamer branded](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/rpg/i-messed-up-so-bad-in-kingdom-come-deliverance-2-that-i-was-literally-branded-a-criminal-and-hours-later-im-still-shunned-by-society/)
- talking down a punishment: a persuasion attempt can lower a punishment but can also fail. [PC Gamer branded](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/rpg/i-messed-up-so-bad-in-kingdom-come-deliverance-2-that-i-was-literally-branded-a-criminal-and-hours-later-im-still-shunned-by-society/)
- bail: some situations need a fixed bail payment, one quest asks 500 groschen. [PC Gamer branded](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/rpg/i-messed-up-so-bad-in-kingdom-come-deliverance-2-that-i-was-literally-branded-a-criminal-and-hours-later-im-still-shunned-by-society/)
- pillory: a punishment that temporarily cuts Charisma by 4 and raises Conspicuousness by 20. [Primagames punishments](https://primagames.com/tips/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-branded-and-other-punishments-effects)
- caning: a punishment that temporarily lowers Strength, Agility and Vitality by 40 percent and slows stamina regeneration. [Primagames punishments](https://primagames.com/tips/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-branded-and-other-punishments-effects)
- branding: a permanent neck mark that halves positive reputation gain and makes merchants and NPCs refuse service. [Primagames punishments](https://primagames.com/tips/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-branded-and-other-punishments-effects)
- brand harassment: a branded player is insulted and avoided in that town. [Primagames punishments](https://primagames.com/tips/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-branded-and-other-punishments-effects)
- brand fading: the brand wears off over time though some effects may remain. [PC Gamer branded](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/rpg/i-messed-up-so-bad-in-kingdom-come-deliverance-2-that-i-was-literally-branded-a-criminal-and-hours-later-im-still-shunned-by-society/)
- execution: repeated serious crime after branding ends in hanging and a reload of the last save. [Primagames punishments](https://primagames.com/tips/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-branded-and-other-punishments-effects)
- unrest: too much crime in a short time makes residents arm themselves and guards call reinforcements. [Deltias crime](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=134932)
- per-community reputation: standing is tracked separately for each settlement. [Deltias crime](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=134932)
- per-class reputation: standing is also tracked by social class. [PC Gamer tips](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/rpg/8-kingdom-come-deliverance-2-tips-that-will-help-you-become-bohemias-next-top-knight)
- reputation tab: the Player screen shows each community's icon and colour for how it regards the player. [Deltias crime](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=134932)
- slow reputation: reputation builds and falls gradually, unlike the instant effect of crime. [Deltias crime](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=134932)
- reputation by quests: helping locals with quests improves standing. [Deltias crime](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=134932)
- night torch: carrying a torch in a settlement at night helps standing. [Deltias crime](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=134932)
- penitent pilgrimage: a priest can set a pilgrimage to repair a bad reputation. [Deltias crime](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=134932)
- indulgence: paying one can repair a bad reputation. [Deltias crime](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=134932)
- trader reputation: successful haggles raise standing with a trader and failed ones lower it. [Sportskeeda haggling](https://www.sportskeeda.com/esports/haggling-kingdom-come-deliverance-2-explained)
- innkeeper disposition: innkeepers refuse to rent beds to players they think poorly of. [Gamerguides gameplay](https://www.gamerguides.com/kingdom-come-deliverance-ii/guide/getting-started/gameplay)
- conspicuousness: how likely people are to look twice at the player, raised by bright or unusual clothing. [Wiki.gg Conspicuousness](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Conspicuousness)
- visibility: how easily others can spot or track the player, lower in darkness and in dark, non-shiny clothes. [Mein-MMO thieves](https://mein-mmo.de/en/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-picking-locks-sneaking-pickpocketing-a-big-guide-for-thieves,1223739)
- noise: how loud the player is, driven by movement, armour, bushes and locks. [Mein-MMO thieves](https://mein-mmo.de/en/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-picking-locks-sneaking-pickpocketing-a-big-guide-for-thieves,1223739)
- heavy armour noise: heavy armour is loud and draws nearby enemies. [Sportskeeda armour](https://sportskeeda.com/esports/armour-mechanics-kingdom-come-deliverance-2-explained)
- quiet armour: cloth and padded armour are quietest and leather slightly louder. [Method stealth](https://method.gg/kcd2/lockpicking-and-stealth-101-the-best-build-to-avoid-guards-and-crack-any-chest)
- day versus night visibility: the player is far more visible by day than by night. [Method stealth](https://method.gg/kcd2/lockpicking-and-stealth-101-the-best-build-to-avoid-guards-and-crack-any-chest)
- recognition by outerwear: noticeability depends on how inconspicuous the outer clothing is. [Mein-MMO thieves](https://mein-mmo.de/en/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-picking-locks-sneaking-pickpocketing-a-big-guide-for-thieves,1223739)
- disguise by visor: visored helmets hide the face. [Mein-MMO thieves](https://mein-mmo.de/en/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-picking-locks-sneaking-pickpocketing-a-big-guide-for-thieves,1223739)
- scent detection: strong body odour lets animals and enemies sniff out the player. [Gamerguides washing](https://gamerguides.com/kingdom-come-deliverance-ii/guide/getting-started/gameplay/washing-clothes-and-armor)
- Reeky debuff: too much dirt and blood on gear gives the Reeky debuff. [Gamerguides washing](https://gamerguides.com/kingdom-come-deliverance-ii/guide/getting-started/gameplay/washing-clothes-and-armor)
- dirt accumulation: gear and the player get dirty from travel and labour. [Gamerguides washing](https://gamerguides.com/kingdom-come-deliverance-ii/guide/getting-started/gameplay/washing-clothes-and-armor)
- blood accumulation: combat leaves blood on the player and gear. [Gamerguides washing](https://gamerguides.com/kingdom-come-deliverance-ii/guide/getting-started/gameplay/washing-clothes-and-armor)
- NPC sleep: most NPCs sleep at night. [Gamerguides gameplay](https://www.gamerguides.com/kingdom-come-deliverance-ii/guide/getting-started/gameplay)
- night patrol: guards question a player found out at night. [Gamerguides gameplay](https://www.gamerguides.com/kingdom-come-deliverance-ii/guide/getting-started/gameplay)
- stolen goods pricing: traders offer lower prices for stolen items. [Sportskeeda haggling](https://www.sportskeeda.com/esports/haggling-kingdom-come-deliverance-2-explained)
- fence perk: the Partner in Crime perk allows selling stolen goods safely. [Sportskeeda stats](https://sportskeeda.com/esports/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-all-stats-explained)
- persuasion depends on reputation: success depends on standing with the person addressed. [Wiki.gg speech](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Speech_and_Persuasion)
- relative comparison: the player's abilities are measured against the other person's. [Wiki.gg speech](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Speech_and_Persuasion)
- target-specific effect: brawlers resist intimidation and nobles see through a peasant's disguise. [Wiki.gg speech](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Speech_and_Persuasion)
- weather and food in travel: hunger and injury make bandit encounters riskier on the road. [Segmentnext tips](https://segmentnext.com/8-beginner-tips-in-kingdom-come-deliverance-2)
- horse whistle noise: calling the horse can attract nearby attention. [Wiki.gg Horse](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Horse)
- bandit camps at night: sleeping bandits can be knocked out or stabbed by sneaking in. [PC Gamer tips](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/rpg/8-kingdom-come-deliverance-2-tips-that-will-help-you-become-bohemias-next-top-knight)

## 3. Story and people (quests, characters, dialogue, choices, endings, relationships)

- main quest path: main-path tasks and side quests run together from the first region. [PC Gamer tips](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/rpg/8-kingdom-come-deliverance-2-tips-that-will-help-you-become-bohemias-next-top-knight)
- dog search: a long search for the player's dog covers the first region. [PC Gamer tips](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/rpg/8-kingdom-come-deliverance-2-tips-that-will-help-you-become-bohemias-next-top-knight)
- faction choices: picking sides between groups can bring bigger rewards. [PC Gamer tips](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/rpg/8-kingdom-come-deliverance-2-tips-that-will-help-you-become-bohemias-next-top-knight)
- intimidation: threatening people when needed is an option in dialogue. [PC Gamer tips](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/rpg/8-kingdom-come-deliverance-2-tips-that-will-help-you-become-bohemias-next-top-knight)
- speech check: dialogue options can be tested against the Speech stat. [Sportskeeda stats](https://sportskeeda.com/esports/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-all-stats-explained)
- persuasion: a dialogue stat that can change outcomes and avoid fights. [Sportskeeda stats](https://sportskeeda.com/esports/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-all-stats-explained)
- impression: a good impression helps in conversation. [Sportskeeda stats](https://sportskeeda.com/esports/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-all-stats-explained)
- domination: acting dominant helps convince others. [Sportskeeda stats](https://sportskeeda.com/esports/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-all-stats-explained)
- presence: built from armour, weapons and fitness. [Sportskeeda stats](https://sportskeeda.com/esports/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-all-stats-explained)
- bribery: offering money the other person finds persuasive. [Wiki.gg speech](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Speech_and_Persuasion)
- non-social skill options: situations such as Horsemanship can be used to influence someone. [Wiki.gg speech](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Speech_and_Persuasion)
- poisoning a rival: drugging a silver-tongued trader reduces his persuasive edge. [Wiki.gg speech](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Speech_and_Persuasion)
- separating a target: luring a guard away from companions makes intimidating him easier. [Wiki.gg speech](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Speech_and_Persuasion)
