# Mafia: Definitive Edition (2020, Hangar 13) gameplay mechanics

Compiled 2026-11-19 from public guides and news pages only (outside study). Alcohol or gambling items are tagged at line end.

## 1. Verbs

- Move, walk, sprint: basic on-foot locomotion with separate walk and sprint inputs. [source](https://gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/keybinds-controls/z5d9a7)
- Sneak: a slow quiet movement input on foot. [source](https://gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/keybinds-controls/z5d9a7)
- Climb: Tommy climbs fences and large crates. [source](https://gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/keybinds-controls/z5d9a7)
- Interact: one input opens doors, picks things up and uses objects, also in vehicles. [source](https://gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/keybinds-controls/z5d9a7)
- Take cover: stand next to a cover object to snap into cover. [source](https://gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/keybinds-controls/z5d9a7)
- Destructible cover: fences and small boxes break under fire, building walls do not, and enemy cover breaks too. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/beginners-guide/z1d9a3)
- Cover stickiness and exposure: the cover system is criticised as sticky, with Tommy standing up when aiming at flankers. [source](https://windowscentral.com/mafia-definitive-edition-review)
- Aim: hold to aim down the weapon. [source](https://gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/keybinds-controls/z5d9a7)
- Shoot and reload: fire and reload inputs. [source](https://gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/keybinds-controls/z5d9a7)
- Hold breath while aiming: steadies the aim for a moment. [source](https://gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/keybinds-controls/z5d9a7)
- Scope aim: sniper rifles have a look-through-scope input. [source](https://gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/keybinds-controls/z5d9a7)
- Aim sway and recoil: aim sways more when moving and shooting, and recoil is strong. [source](https://windowscentral.com/mafia-definitive-edition-review)
- Shotgun knockback: shotgun hits push enemies back. [source](https://windowscentral.com/mafia-definitive-edition-review)
- Headshots: aimed shots to the head are a listed combat option. [source](https://windowscentral.com/mafia-definitive-edition-review)
- Multi-step aim assist: aim help strength changes with difficulty, aimed at controller players. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/remake-how-it-differs-from-original/zbd959)
- Throwables: hold to aim grenades or Molotovs, with a cancel-throw input. [source](https://gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/keybinds-controls/z5d9a7)
- Weapon radial menu: hold the inventory input for a radial weapon picker. [source](https://gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/keybinds-controls/z5d9a7)
- Previous and next weapon: step through weapons directly. [source](https://gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/keybinds-controls/z5d9a7)
- One-handed and two-handed weapon select: quick picks for each weapon class. [source](https://gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/keybinds-controls/z5d9a7)
- Camera shoulder swap: tap the inventory input to switch which shoulder the camera sits on. [source](https://gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/keybinds-controls/z5d9a7)
- Holster firearm: putting the gun away avoids scaring civilians and police attention. [source](https://gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/keybinds-controls/z5d9a7)
- Fist and melee weapon fighting: unarmed brawls and weapons such as baseball bats. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/beginners-guide/z1d9a3)
- Melee dodge and counter: time the dodge against the on-screen circle to counter for extra damage. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/beginners-guide/z1d9a3)
- Optional brawls: unarmed fights can be avoided by backing off and shooting. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/beginners-guide/z1d9a3)
- Grab or drop a body: Tommy can carry and drop bodies. [source](https://gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/keybinds-controls/z5d9a7)
- Stealth mode toggle: switch stealth on, first available in the third story mission. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/beginners-guide/z1d9a3)
- Silent takedown: approach from the side or behind for a quiet kill. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/beginners-guide/z1d9a3)
- Stealth or open combat choice on replay: some missions allow either approach. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/omerta/z5d95c)
- Looting dead enemies: weapons and ammo drop from the dead. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/beginners-guide/z1d9a3)
- Steering, throttle, brake: the core vehicle controls. [source](https://gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/keybinds-controls/z5d9a7)
- Handbrake: slides the car for corners. [source](https://gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/keybinds-controls/z5d9a7)
- Horn: a button on the vehicle. [source](https://gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/keybinds-controls/z5d9a7)
- Look back: view behind while driving. [source](https://gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/keybinds-controls/z5d9a7)
- Enter and exit vehicle: one input, with the driver pulled out if the car is taken by force. [source](https://gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/keybinds-controls/z5d9a7)
- Manual gearbox option: gear up, gear down, neutral and clutch inputs exist for manual transmission. [source](https://gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/keybinds-controls/z5d9a7)
- Speed limiter: a built-in toggle that caps speed so traffic rules are not broken by accident. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/remake-how-it-differs-from-original/zbd959)
- Normal driving model: the default, more forgiving handling. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/beginners-guide/z1d9a3)
- Simulation driving model: cars slide more, turn less easily and brake later, switchable any time in options. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/beginners-guide/z1d9a3)
- Motorcycle riding: bikes are new, with a separate riding model and lean forward and back inputs. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/remake-how-it-differs-from-original/zbd959)
- Falling off bikes: a fall in a chase risks death. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/police-how-to-avoid/zad994)
- Shoot from a vehicle: aim and fire out of the car with a handgun. [source](https://gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/keybinds-controls/z5d9a7)
- Ram vehicles: a manual input, not automatic, useful against roadblocks. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/police-how-to-avoid/zad994)
- Car theft by lockpick: the quietest way into a parked car, taught by Ralphie early on. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/beginners-guide/z1d9a3)
- Car theft by smashing the window: drivers-side window break to enter a parked car. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/beginners-guide/z1d9a3)
- Convertibles and motorcycles need no lockpick: low-risk steals. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/police-how-to-avoid/zad994)
- Carjacking a moving car: throwing out the driver or rider is risky and draws police. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/beginners-guide/z1d9a3)
- Fuel gauge and speedometer: both still shown while driving. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/remake-how-it-differs-from-original/zbd959)
- Camera switch: change vehicle camera view. [source](https://gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/keybinds-controls/z5d9a7)

## 2. The town under the hood

- Police response setting, regular: police react mainly to shooting, theft and hitting pedestrians. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/police-how-to-avoid/zad994)
- Police response setting, simulation: police also react to collisions, speeding and red lights. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/police-how-to-avoid/zad994)
- Police setting independent of difficulty: changeable any time in the Game tab. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/police-how-to-avoid/zad994)
- Adjustable police presence: players can lower police pressure or keep the strict original policing. [source](https://mafia.2k.com/news/mafia-definitive-edition-classic-difficulty/)
- Speeding offence: only under simulation police, avoided with the speed limiter. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/police-how-to-avoid/zad994)
- Red light offence: only under simulation police. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/police-how-to-avoid/zad994)
- Drawn gun offence: openly carrying a gun first brings warnings. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/police-how-to-avoid/zad994)
- Shot-fired offence: shooting or killing, even blind shots, triggers police. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/police-how-to-avoid/zad994)
- Collision offence: hitting pedestrians, cars or objects draws police. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/police-how-to-avoid/zad994)
- Witness calls: witnesses phone police and an eye icon marks the map. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/police-how-to-avoid/zad994)
- Dispatch to witness spot: nearby police cars head to the reported location. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/police-how-to-avoid/zad994)
- One star fine: officers pull Tommy over to issue a ticket, and paying avoids a bigger chase. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/police-how-to-avoid/zad994)
- No money check for fines: Tommy can always pay and no balance is shown. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/police-how-to-avoid/zad994)
- Two to five star pursuit: police try to arrest or shoot Tommy. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/police-how-to-avoid/zad994)
- Wanted stars above the minimap: shows level and a response icon. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/police-how-to-avoid/zad994)
- Escalating response: more stars bring better police cars, more patrols and roadblocks. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/police-how-to-avoid/zad994)
- Losing a pursuit: stay undetected until the stars stop flashing and vanish. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/police-how-to-avoid/zad994)
- Losing sight does not end it: watch police icons on the minimap and turn away. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/police-how-to-avoid/zad994)
- Fresh pursuit on new crime: later crimes start a new chase, not the old level. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/police-how-to-avoid/zad994)
- Stolen police car as escape: a killed officer's car is sturdy and fast. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/police-how-to-avoid/zad994)
- Roadworks block pursuers: at least one chasing car can be stopped by them. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/police-how-to-avoid/zad994)
- Police cars can be shot out: several handgun hits destroy one. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/police-how-to-avoid/zad994)
- Mafia III style slower police in some districts was not carried over. [source](https://www.playstationlifestyle.net/2020/06/24/mafia-definitive-edition-police-settings/)
- Classic police: respond to minor infractions, even slight speeding means a pull-over and fine. [source](https://mafia.2k.com/news/mafia-definitive-edition-classic-difficulty/)
- Classic roadblocks: ignoring police leads to roadblocks, more often than on lower levels. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/difficulty-levels/z4d9a6)
- Easy and medium police: ignore more crimes and violations. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/difficulty-levels/z4d9a6)
- Pedestrians react to armed player: civilians are scared by a drawn gun. [source](https://gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/keybinds-controls/z5d9a7)
- Traffic obeys lights and lanes: ordinary traffic and red lights are part of the city simulation. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/police-how-to-avoid/zad994)
- Time of day and weather: set in Free Ride from the bar, with dynamic weather effects in the engine. [source](https://mafia.2k.com/news/mafia-definitive-edition-free-ride-mode/)
- Enemy flanking AI: on Classic, enemies are more aggressive and dodge attacks better. [source](https://mafia.2k.com/news/mafia-definitive-edition-classic-difficulty/)
- Enemy accuracy by level: easier levels miss more, Classic enemies rarely miss. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/difficulty-levels/z4d9a6)
- Vehicle damage model: cars are destroyed much faster on Classic. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/difficulty-levels/z4d9a6)

## 3. Story and people

- Linear 20-chapter campaign: Tommy Angelo's rise in the Salieri family told in fixed order. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/omerta/z5d95c)
- Chapter 1, An Offer You Can't Refuse: the opening getaway mission. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/omerta/z5d95c)
- Chapter 5, Fairplay: the race mission. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/omerta/z5d95c)
- Chapter 8, The Saint and the Sinner: merges two missions from the original game. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/omerta/z5d95c)
- Chapter 9, A Trip to the Country: ending chase was changed from the original. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/remake-how-it-differs-from-original/zbd959)
- Chapters 10 to 20: Omerta through The Death of Art, listed by name in the guide. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/omerta/z5d95c)
- Tommy's career arc: cab driver, then driver and fixer, then capo. [source](https://windowscentral.com/mafia-definitive-edition-review)
- Story set in Prohibition-era Lost Heaven, with bootlegging as the family trade. (alcohol) [source](https://windowscentral.com/mafia-definitive-edition-review)
- Family and escalation: growing income brings more risk and a threatened family. [source](https://windowscentral.com/mafia-definitive-edition-review)
- Expanded cutscenes and dialogue: new background scenes that leave key scenes unchanged. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/remake-how-it-differs-from-original/zbd959)
- Rival families: the Morello family opposes the Salieri family. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/lost-heaven-map/z6d95d)
- Ralphie's workshop and tutorials: Ralphie teaches lockpicking, and tutorials explain core mechanics. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/remake-how-it-differs-from-original/zbd959)
- No story-mode side missions: optional driving and activities live in Free Ride. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/omerta/z5d95c)
- Character acting and silences: praised for conveying mood. [source](https://windowscentral.com/mafia-definitive-edition-review)
- Optional side missions in remake: new extras offering rewards and new areas. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/remake-how-it-differs-from-original/zbd959)
- Interior set-piece missions: many missions play inside buildings after you drive to a marker. [source](https://windowscentral.com/mafia-definitive-edition-review)
- Assassination sequences: occasional planned hits within missions. [source](https://windowscentral.com/mafia-definitive-edition-review)

## 6. Interface

## 7. The game's shape

- Difficulty levels: easy, medium, hard and Classic, switchable in the pause menu. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/difficulty-levels/z4d9a6)
- Classic difficulty: harder enemies, stricter police, limited UI, and weaker healing. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/difficulty-levels/z4d9a6)
- Classic reload penalty: reloading discards rounds left in the magazine. [source](https://mafia.2k.com/news/mafia-definitive-edition-classic-difficulty/)
- Classic healing: first aid restores less and regeneration stops at 20 percent. [source](https://mafia.2k.com/news/mafia-definitive-edition-classic-difficulty/)
- Classic achievement: needs Classic from the start of the story. [source](https://mafia.2k.com/news/mafia-definitive-edition-classic-difficulty/)
- Switching difficulty can lock trophies. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/beginners-guide/z1d9a3)
- No automatic health regeneration: consumables heal, as in the original. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/remake-how-it-differs-from-original/zbd959)
- First aid points: single-use pickups. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/beginners-guide/z1d9a3)
- Ammo chests: single-use refills. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/beginners-guide/z1d9a3)
- Ammo scarcity: ammo comes from chests and looting. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/beginners-guide/z1d9a3)
- Fairplay race: easier than the original, with assists and a driving model choice. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/remake-how-it-differs-from-original/zbd959)
- Classic Fairplay: set to high difficulty. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/difficulty-levels/z4d9a6)
- Free Ride unlock: opens after the first story mission. [source](https://mafia.2k.com/news/mafia-definitive-edition-free-ride-mode/)
- Free Ride sightseeing, vehicle testing, collectibles and secrets. [source](https://mafia.2k.com/news/mafia-definitive-edition-free-ride-mode/)
- Free Ride side challenges: unlock unique cars and bikes, such as a dam time trial. [source](https://www.gamepressure.com/mafia-definitive-edition/types-of-secrets/z2d9a4)
