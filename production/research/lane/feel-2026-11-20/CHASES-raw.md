## GTA V and GTA IV (on foot from police)
A. Mostly criticism.
- GTA V uses line of sight and a cone of vision, so "hiding in one spot can shake them"; running back to the safehouse no longer ends a chase. GTA IV used a circular radius: "The only way was to run, and run fast." https://gameranx.com/features/id/17567/article/are-the-painfully-tedious-chase-mechanics-of-gta-5-intentional/ (read 20 Nov 2026)
- Criticism: "a lengthy, tedious chase sequence that's as far from enjoyable as I can possibly think of" (same page). One opinion piece.
B. Not found (only a RDR2 search; see below).
C. Escape by breaking sight and waiting is relevant to witnesses; modern cities and wanted stars are not.

## Takeaways for LEDGER (my reading)
- Tackle-to-catch at normal speed (L.A. Noire) beats superhuman bursts; avoid rubber-banding.
- Set-piece chase routes through market and alley, hand-built (Sleeping Dogs).
- Escape by breaking line of sight, not a timer or a safehouse (GTA V).
