## 1. Kingdom Come: Deliverance II (best fit)

A. Feel
- PC Gamer writer calls the crime system "more fleshed out and consequential" than any sandbox RPG they have played, and says it "kinda rules". A guard seeing a horse escape later led to a surrender order; a rescued woman backed away on spotting the criminal brand and called the player a criminal. https://www.pcgamer.com/games/rpg/i-messed-up-so-bad-in-kingdom-come-deliverance-2-that-i-was-literally-branded-a-criminal-and-hours-later-im-still-shunned-by-society/ (read 20 Nov 2026)
- Same piece, criticism: the brand makes buying and selling very hard; innocent acts (standing outside a tavern after closing) can earn fines; writer wonders if it is "too realistic". Same link.
- Gfinity, built on player anecdotes (Reddit): NPCs remember crimes and report them days later. A stable girl saw a player flee after a horse theft and arrest came three days later; guards accused a player of a knockout nobody saw; a vendor who refused a sale remembered the chat and named the thief. Article says exact witness rules are not explained. https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/npcs-in-kingdom-come-deliverance-2-will-snitch-so-dont-leave-witnesses (read 20 Nov 2026)
- Other pieces found, not read (search snippet only): GamesRadar "live as a criminal forever ... a stupid lute" https://gamesradar.com/games/rpg/i-underestimated-kingdom-come-deliverance-2-and-now-ive-got-to-find-2-000-groschen-or-live-as-a-criminal-forever-all-because-of-a-stupid-lute ; OpenCritic news "NPCs Keep Ratting Out Players For Theft" https://opencritic.com/news/11884/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-npcs-keep-ratting-out-players-for-theft ; Fextralife crime page https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.fextralife.com/Crime+System
- Count: 2 praise, 2 criticism (mild), 1 neutral (anecdote report). Small sample.
- Published numbers: none found.

B. Footage: not found. One YouTube-targeted search returned only articles and Steam threads. No video page was reached.

C. Closeness: 3. Everyday town, witnesses, memory, recognition of a face and goods, no supernatural stealth. Closest to LEDGER's town of people who remember.

## 3. Hitman (World of Assassination / 3)

A. Feel
- Gamepressure Hitman 3 guide: a marker fills "to 100%" and then nearby guards or NPCs raise the alarm; "vigilant" NPCs, shown by a white circle, can expose a disguised 47 if he stays near them; crouching, breaking line of sight and activities (bench, railing) help blending. https://www.gamepressure.com/hitman-iii/stealth/zde21e (read 20 Nov 2026)
- PC Gamer on Hitman 2016 NPC AI: author says the AI gives hope for "the best one yet"; guards investigate dropped weapons, civilians fetch a guard instead of fighting. https://pcgamer.com/hitmans-npc-ai-could-mean-a-return-to-form-for-agent-47 (read 20 Nov 2026)
- PCGamesN: Hitman 2 NPCs spot you in mirrors; Friedl: "Now NPCs can actually spot you through a mirror". NPCs read the face, so turning away cuts suspicion. https://www.pcgamesn.com/hitman-2/ai-detect-mirrors (read 20 Nov 2026)
- Title only (search snippet only): PC Gamer "Best Stealth 2021: Hitman 3" https://www.pcgamer.com/uk/best-stealth-game-hitman-3
- Criticism: none found in what was read. Count 3 praise, 0 criticism, so the 3 is partly the reader's bias; check forums for complaints about suspicion being gamey.

B. Footage: not found. The search returned only articles.

C. Closeness: 2. Crowds of civilians who spot you and tell guards is relevant. But the suspicion is a disguise puzzle, and the HUD meter is a game layer.

Over 300 behaviour-tree characters and crowds over 1000 (2016) and over 2000 (Hitman 2).
