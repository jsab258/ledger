## Verdict for LEDGER
Best look match with usable footage: Mafia DE and GTA IV (both 7). Lesson from complaints: RDR2 and Mafia are named sluggish, so avoid long start and stop animation. Best feel reference for animation blending: TLOU2 (talk only, not a town).
