# Footsteps for the player character: recorded, CC0 (research, 30 September 2026)

**Recommendation.** Walking: sturmankin's "paving_11a_sneakers_walk.wav" (Freesound 273077), real trainers on three real paving slabs. Running: BigSoundBank s0514 "Footsteps, Shoe on Concrete" (Joseph Sardin), a man running in trainers on concrete outdoors. Running fallback: sturmankin's "paving_11c_sneakers_run" (273115), same shoe and slabs as the walk. Licences, both checked 30 September 2026 without signing in: the Freesound page reads "Creative Commons 0" (uploaded 1 May 2015); the BigSoundBank page reads "CC0 (public domain): Free and royalty-free", usable "without creating an account" (published 27 May 2012). The Freesound file here is its public HQ preview (188 kbps mono MP3, 48 kHz); the lossless WAV original needs a free Freesound login, optional and his to fetch. BigSoundBank serves lossless FLAC. Processing:

- High-pass the Freesound files at 60 Hz (basement rumble) and s0514 at 100 Hz (wind: 69% of its raw step energy is below 100 Hz).
- Cut each footfall from 5 ms before onset through its decay: at most 250 ms walking, 200 ms running, 15 ms fade-out.
- Keep only steps of 80 to 250 ms with both a heel and a toe transient.
- Pick 8 walking and 8 running; match loudness (running about 3 dB louder); export 48 kHz 16-bit mono WAV.

## Candidates, measured

Measured after the high-pass. "Clean" = 80 to 250 ms with two or more transients. T30 = median time for a step to fall 30 dB, i.e. how much room follows it.

| File | What it is | Steps (clean) | Cadence | Length | <500 Hz / >1 kHz | Centroid | Heel-toe | T30 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| paving_11a_sneakers_walk | trainers, slabs | 98 (51) | 94/min | 148 ms | 41% / 22% | 903 Hz | 80 ms | 80 ms |
| s0514 | trainers running, concrete, outdoors | 28 (23) | 167/min | 130 ms | 37% / 31% | 925 Hz | 30 ms | 250 ms (raw) |
| paving_11c_sneakers_run | trainers running, slabs | 63 (27) | 148/min | 105 ms | 51% / 19% | 803 Hz | 60 ms | 85 ms |
| paving_13c_sportshoes_run | sports shoes running, slabs | 81 (53) | 150/min | 125 ms | 50% / 12% | 686 Hz | 25 ms | 80 ms |
| s1318 | "shoes", quick steps, concrete, outdoors | 147 (107) | 131/min | 150 ms | 18% / 59% | 1539 Hz | 45 ms | 220 ms |

All are continuous sequences to be cut. sturmankin used a ZOOM H2 in a house basement; the paving files are "three larger paving slabs" on earth in a wooden box. s0514 is outdoor, 48 kHz 16-bit mono; s1318 and s1319 outdoor, MixPre-3 and Sennheiser ME66, 24-bit.

Each recommended step has a heel and a toe, body between 250 Hz and 1 kHz, and little above 2 kHz: a hard smooth surface, not grit. The trainer walk and the s0514 run are within 25 Hz of each other in centroid. s1318 is the lossless outdoor alternative for walking, but thin (18% below 500 Hz); it would need about +4 dB below 250 Hz for heel body.

## Rejected

- **sturmankin concrete floor** (272259, 272300, 272250): the basement room follows each step (T30 140 to 350 ms).
- **sturmankin "darkshoes"** (15a, 15c): 89 to 92% below 500 Hz, centroid about 340 Hz; the same dull thud that failed Kenney.
- **BigSoundBank s1319**, running: 58 of 103 steps clean, and thin.
- **OpenGameArt "Footsteps"** (GboxMikeFozzy, CC0, 14 April 2022): six noise-reduced subway steps; 17 to 26 dB signal-to-noise, one transient in five of six, one centred at 5.6 kHz (hiss).
- **OpenGameArt "Footsteps on different surfaces"** (congusbongus): CC-BY 3.0, not 4.0; mixed sources.
- **OpenGameArt "100 CC0 SFX #2"** (rubberduck): no surface or method given; not downloaded.
- **Freesound AlexMurphy53 580433**: CC BY 4.0, lossy m4a only.
- **Paid or non-CC0**: itch.io packs (Sound Armoury, Epic Stock Media, asaksfx, High Impact Media), ZapSplat (account needed), Pixabay (own licence).
