**FOUND:** Sylvia Rennison, in her slippers on the boarding house step by the ferry landing [invented]: "Three weeks behind, he is, and one of Danny Cammack's. Mickey would have had a word."

**OFFERED / WAITS:** Offered once Tom has stopped by the boarding house's step. She waits until Friday.

**STEPS:**
1. Boarding house step. Sylvia gives the sum: three weeks, £54 [invented]. Tom can ask questions, promise nothing, or walk on.
2. Mickey's, on Quay Street. Ron Kirby and Sheila Dunn are there. Ron says Mickey "had a word" and offers to go. Sheila says what the till can spare. Tom can ask either.
3. Quay Street, any time before Friday. Tom may see the lodger leave the boarding house. He can speak to him or let him pass.
4. Boarding house step, Friday. Sylvia asks for his answer. Tom sends Ron, pays her himself, or refuses.

**ENDINGS:**
- Send Ron: the rent is paid. Danny's crew hears that Mickey's is still Mickey's.
- Pay it yourself: Sylvia is grateful and tells nobody, then tells everybody. The street knows Tom settles other people's bills.
- Refuse, "I'm not Mickey": Sylvia repeats it word for word. The town keeps the phrase Tom used.

**IF IT GOES WRONG:** Friday passes with no answer and Sylvia has stopped waiting. She puts the lodger's case on the step, and the street says Mickey's let it lie.

## 29. The rent on Ada's window

**FOUND:** Dawn Pybus, clipboard in hand, on Ada's step across from the cab office [invented]. She says, "It's gone up, love. Ada's not said a word, and I'd sooner you heard it from me." Offered only once Ada's tea is past.

**OFFERED / WAITS:** Offered when Ada's tea is past (the outline's first test). Waits until the next rent day.

**STEPS:**
1. Ada's step, Quay Street. Dawn explains the rise. Tom can press her for the figure or leave it.
2. The cab office. Tom asks Sheila, who admits Mickey paid the difference without Ada knowing.
3. The agent's counter, a firm in the Exchange. Tom can ask what is owed, or pay the difference in cash [invented].
4. Rent day, Ada's step. Dawn comes round again. Tom decides what Ada is told.

**ENDINGS:**
- Pay in secret: Ada never knows. Sheila does, and it counts. The street sees her still at her window.
- Tell Ada: she refuses his money and her tea goes cold, or she takes it and is ashamed. Which she does follows how she rated him after the tea [invented]. Either way she knows, and Dawn and Sheila know she knows.
- Let it stop: Ada moves her chair from the window to the back room. The street notices its watcher gone.

The town keeps who keeps Ada at her window.

**IF IT GOES WRONG:** If rent day passes with nothing done, the story ends as "let it stop". The empty window is the world state.
