# Open-world and third-person action games: player expectations

This is an inventory of expectations, including conditional systems such as driving, crafting and multiplayer. Those apply only when the game includes that activity. Accessibility entries include modern expectations that are still inconsistently delivered.

I checked production disciplines, [engine subsystems](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/gameplay-systems-in-unreal-engine), [input and platform requirements](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/features/steam_controller/getting_started_for_devs), and [accessibility guidelines](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/gaming/accessibility/guidelines), then walked an imagined opening half hour through individual actions and interruptions. No particular project was examined. “Notice” means the earliest likely encounter, not a measured average.
