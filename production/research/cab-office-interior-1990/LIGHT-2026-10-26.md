# Mickey's office: its light and exposure (26 October 2026)

His order of 25 October, item 5, and his answer of 26 October ("then Mickey's office lighting"). The faults to fix, from four fresh reviewers on 25 October (WEAR-2026-10-25.md, the room lit): by day the lit room reads near-black (the door view's mean brightness 12 of 255) and its tubes look off; at night the back wall and the counter blow out (the map 38% and the drivers' board 53% clipped, the counter 39%).

## Why (read in our own code)

- The game holds one exposure for the whole world on an unbound post-process volume (VignetteShot.cpp, "AND THE DAY'S EXPOSURE"): at night it is pinned to the look file's night pin, chosen for the dark street; by day the camera meters itself, and the bright street and window set it. Inside the small room, the day's exposure is far too dark for a room and the night's far too bright.
- The page photographs are taken through an automation camera that writes the same pin onto itself, so they show the street's exposure inside the office too.
- The office's tubes are 180 lumens by day and 300 at night, the desk lamp 150 (shop-interiors.json), set low on 4 October because the street runs on low light values.

## Research (a helper, read 26 October; pages read through a summarising fetch)

- **Epic's own answer** for an interior with a bright window is a post-process volume per room with its own exposure (its tutorial sets Min and Max EV per room volume, a blend radius at the door; Min equal to Max holds it), and local exposure, which its auto-exposure page says should always be set up with Lumen. Engine developers describe the same practice in shipped games (exposure set per region by volumes; God of War's artist-placed exposure and a highlights operator).
- **Physical values:** an office 320 to 500 lux, about EV 7 to 8; an overcast street about EV 12; a lit night street about EV 7 to 8. A white wall at 500 lux reads about 0.65 at EV 7.6, 0.03 at EV 12 (the day fault), about 8 at EV 4 (the night fault): the arithmetic matches both faults.
- **Tubes:** rect lights per fitting with an emissive diffuser panel, 4000 K; a 1990 4 ft tube gives about 2,600 lm.
- **The lamp:** a softer source (a 3 to 5 cm source radius) and a wider cone keep its hotspot from clipping.
- **Ranked:** (1) a bound interior volume with its own held exposure, blended at the door; (2) the lights calibrated at that exposure; (3) local exposure in the volume; (4) the fittings as rect lights with glowing panels; (5) the lamp softened.

## What was made

- CrimeProbe.cpp, BuildMickeysOffice: a post-process volume above the street's (priority 10), weighed in each frame while the player's camera is inside the rooms' box (eased over half a metre at its edges), its exposure held by day and by night from mickeys-office.json's "inside" ("exposure_pin_day", "exposure_pin_night", "exposure_z1"; -OfficePinDay= and -OfficePinNight= to try values), following the street's day or night (LedgerVignetteShot::SunOnNow).
- -OfficeLook: Tom placed inside the lit office, pictures through his own camera (which carries no exposure of its own), by day or at 20:00.

## The ladder and the result

**Try 1: the volume shaped to the rooms** (a box made at run time): no effect at any value, by day or at night (the same pictures at 0.1 to 9): the engine never counted the camera inside a box made that way. Also learnt: the page photographs show neither the room's own exposure nor the player's, since their camera carries its own.

**Through Tom's own camera (-OfficeLook), with no office exposure:** by day the lit room reads well (mean brightness 102 of 255, 0.3% clipped): **the "near-black by day" fault was the page camera's, not the game's.** At night it is real: mean 167, 6.1% clipped.

**Try 2: the volume over everything, weighed in while the player's camera is inside the rooms' box** (eased over half a metre; the yard beyond the back wall keeps the street's): at night held at 2, mean 85 and 0.12% clipped; at 8, mean 35; at 32, mean 10. Set at **1.6** in mickeys-office.json; no day value.

**Fresh reviewers, one per view** (each beside yesterday's failed picture): the darkness by day fixed (mean 12 to 102), the night's blow-out fixed (the map 0% clipped against 77%; the counter top's clipping almost gone); but all four still fail, three on the room's wear and props (placeholder books and binders, hard-edged grime rectangles, rings on hung papers: item 5's wear, not light), and one light fault: **the desk lamp throwing its own, the radio shelf's and the microphone's shadows up the wall across the map**, as if lit from below. The pictures were also half size: the off-screen window needs -ForceRes.

**The lamp, two tries.** Try 1: raised 7 cm into its shade (shop-interiors.json, z 1.05 to 1.12): its own silhouette smaller, but a dark wedge still rose from the filing cabinet to the map (a bare bulb lights upward too). Try 2: the lamp a spot aimed straight down from inside its shade (a new "cone_deg": 50 in the spec; VignetteShot.cpp spawns a spot for a lamp that names a cone, a point light otherwise): the upward shadows gone, the map evenly lit, the pool on the radio desk. Measured at full size through Tom's camera: by day mean 94.7, 0.11% clipped; at night mean 90.2, 0.08% clipped (F:/LedgerTools/gate/new/office/2026-10-26-lamp2; production/previews/office-light-2026-10-26.jpg).

**Reviewed:** at night PASS (the upward shadow gone, the map 0% clipped, max 220 of 255; the radio's hot spot a narrow known point); by day a narrow fail on a new fault, the aimed light burning the radio's pale front and the microphone white (about 3,000 pixels). **That fault, two tries:** 90 lumens and a 40 degree cone (the radio's white patch 3,987 to 2,890 pixels by day, 2,975 to 1,860 at night); then **60 lumens and 30 degrees** (2,009 by day, 1,005 at night; the frame 0.056% and 0.029% clipped; F:/LedgerTools/gate/new/office/2026-10-26-lamp4). A fresh reviewer, by day: **PASS** (the radio's pure white from 1,398 to 197 pixels, one glint behind the microphone; the lamp reads as an anglepoise lighting the desk; nothing brighter anywhere else).

Not done (the research's rest): local exposure in the volume; the tubes as rect lights with glowing panels; the night's black under the bench.
