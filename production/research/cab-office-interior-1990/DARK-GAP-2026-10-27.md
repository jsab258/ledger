# The dark gap under the radio shelf (27 October 2026)

Behind the radio, seen from the counter at night, the shaded surface reads 37 of 255 against 98 beside it; local exposure, finer distance fields and the GI level changed nothing; no pink in coverage (LUMEN-COVERAGE-2026-10-27.md). Web pages read through a summarising fetch, engine source locally.

## Questions

1. How lighting artists lift shadows that bounce would lift; cost, risk.
2. Epic's advice for software Lumen; what scopes to one room.
3. The cheapest sound test and its judging.

## Answers

**1. The options.**
- **Fill light, shadows off.** Placed by hand to fake bounce: "In systems that are not using GI, it's usually faked with spotlights" [G1]. Under Lumen, a practitioner lights deep nested rooms with an unshadowed rect light, a dim point light with Indirect Lighting Intensity 0 and shadows off, and "weak Point Lights" near dark openings [E1]. Cost: low, unshadowed (UNVERIFIED). Risk: light from nowhere; a spot in place of a rect reads "harder, flatter" [E1]. Specular Scale 0 removes its highlights [S1].
- **Per-light Indirect Lighting Intensity.** "Scales the indirect lighting contribution from this light… Default is 1", editor range 0 to 6 [S2]. Lumen multiplies the light's colour by it when lighting the surface cache [S3]. Risks: "a large Indirect Lighting Scale… will cause view-dependent global illumination" [T]; noise, lost contrast, flooded corners [E1]; the whole room lifts.
- **Diffuse Color Boost** (volume): not physical, "keep the value below 2"; brightens reflections too [G].
- **Skylight Leaking** (volume): "an art direction knob (non-physically based) to keep indoor areas from going fully black" [G]; a fraction of the sky light, tintable [S4]. At night: dim, flat, the sky's colour.
- **Emissive card.** "no additional performance cost", but small bright emissives cause noise [G]; the practitioner turns Affect Indirect Lighting off on emissives against "boiling noise" [E1]. Poor for a small dark nook.
- **Quality settings.** Final Gather Quality reduces noise but "greatly increase[s] GPU cost"; Scene Lighting Quality raises the surface cache's fidelity [S4]. Neither is documented to brighten; Max Trace Distance and Scene Detail treat leaks [T].
- **Set dressing.** A paler surface in the cubby reflects proportionally more: honest, but a look choice (own reasoning, unsourced).

**2. Software Lumen and scoping.** Epic's documented answers for "too dark indoors" are only Skylight Leaking and Diffuse Color Boost [G]. Every Lumen setting above is read from the camera's blended post-process settings [S5, V], so the office's volume can carry them, for the whole picture while the camera is inside, the street through the window included. The volume's own "Indirect Lighting Intensity" reaches only baked lighting in 5.8's shaders, not Lumen [S6] (not render-tested). Lumen's short-range AO, which adds contact shadows the coarse probes miss [S7], has its own strength in the volume, 0 to 1 [S4]. Per-light settings are scoped by the light.

## What this means for the office

**Test 1, diagnosis (no game change):** two editor photographs of the shelf view, one with `r.Lumen.ScreenProbeGather.ReferenceMode 1` (1,024 rays per probe, no filtering [S7]), one with Lumen's AO at 0 (`r.Lumen.ScreenProbeGather.ShortRangeAO 0`). If the reference lifts the 37 clearly, Lumen under-gathers: try Final Gather Quality in the office volume, cost measured. If AO off lifts it, lower the volume's Lumen AO strength. If neither: Test 2.

**Test 2, the fix:** one warm-white rect light, colour matched to the tubes, at the cubby's front lip facing in; Cast Shadows off, Indirect Lighting Intensity 0, Specular Scale 0, attenuation just covering the cubby (about 0.6 m); intensity raised in steps. If it passes, bench and walkway alike.

**The measure:** shaded against lit patch in one frame; the radio's shadow edge; clipped pixels; from the counter and a second angle; night and day; packaged frame time. Target, a judgement: 1.5 to 2 stops below its neighbour (about 55 to 65 of 255, display values).

**Fake if:** the radio's shadow vanishes; a sourceless sheen; a pool edge on floor or bench; a colour unlike the room's; Tom's face lit at the counter; light shifting as the camera moves. A fresh reviewer judges beside the last good picture.

**Second choice:** the tube's Indirect Lighting Intensity at 1.5 to 2: one number, but whole-room, view-dependent and noisy [T, E1].
