## The five most likely to change phase 2's plan

Phase 2 is next, once phase 1's exit passes. Every reference number below is a starting point until checked against this game (RULINGS 11 Oct; order rule 6).

1. **s12-03, the street's missing numbers.** People in full detail, a draw distance and shadow lights per frame have no value, yet they cap what phase 2 may multiply. Performance exit: PLAN.md. The lamp pools' shadows are still to be priced against this budget (Study 5, item 3). The Stutter's walks went over 33.3 ms (O02, 6 November), and RULINGS 24 October reopen the 1.7 ms here.
2. **s06-05 with s06-04, the crowd recipe.** A pool of 35 garments (10, 8, 6, 6, 5) at 4 to 8 colourways, against PLAN.md's "one allowed plain outfit" and "Stopped: wardrobe multiplication". At the unit-cost note's 1.6 to 6.8 h a garment, unfinished and low confidence, that is about 56 to 238 h (inferred). PLAN.md needs a smaller pool, or a scope question to him.
3. **s04-05 with s05-01, upper rooms.** PLAN.md calls for "mapped upper rooms" and the asset plan for "mapped rooms for every upper window". Study 4 says sparingly (Mafia 24 in about 8,100 windows), while Study 5 allows them for upper and distant windows. The two studies differ, and the answer could shrink or confirm a whole family.
4. **s08-10 with s08-01 and s08-03, the ambience plan.** PLAN.md says only "P13 ambience". The study's table adds layers unit 10 lacks: spawned voices, at least 30 activity takes in 8 kinds, work through windows. His ear is still owed (`q-sound-listening`), and the rain layers want the saved wetness value PLAN.md places in phase 3, an order to settle.
5. **s04-04, the kit check.** One family of stacked floors with counted reuse (Mafia 18 to 19 facade pieces, kit meshes about 15 times each) turns "the building kit re-seeded" into a failing check. The terrace kit's 39 modules wait on the corner's list at 22 to 28 h (Corner, section 5), and the shops stay one-offs at about 4.25 h each (inferred, unit-cost note).
