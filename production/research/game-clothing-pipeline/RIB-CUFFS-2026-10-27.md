# Rib cuffs that grip the wrist (27 October 2026)

Why the jacket's rib cuffs never close round Tom's wrist in Marvelous Designer (MD) 2026.1, and what to script instead. Web sources were read on 27 October 2026. "Measured" means our own files; UNVERIFIED means no source says it.

## What our own data shows (measured)

- **"Curved" changed nothing.** All 109 of Tom's arrangement points already report type "Curved" (F:/LedgerTools/md-jobs/out/arrangement_points.json, 26 Oct). Drape 8 repeated drape 7.
- **Points are percentages on a bounding volume (BV).** X runs round the limb: Inside 0, Front 25, Outside 50, Back 75. Y runs along it: Arm_1 75, Arm_2 50, Arm_3 25. Z (offset) is 50, and the waist points use 30. The Wrist points sit on their own BV, at its middle (Y 50).
- An MD BV runs between two joints. Its "Hands/Feet" option stretches it to the hands (Add Bounding Volume). If the wrist BV covers the hand, its middle is mid-hand, which would explain both failed drapes (UNVERIFIED).
- A cuff cut at 168 mm is narrower than the 197 mm wrist, and so narrower than the hand. Once it closes below the hand, it cannot climb back.
- The cuffs drape at 20 mm particles: about 8 particles round and 3 rows deep.

## 1. How MD users make rib grip

- **Arrangement.** A pattern's centre goes on the point, curved to the BV. Pieces longer than the BV, "such as cuffs", wrap in a spiral; Flip Wrap Direction fixes that. The Property Editor then offers Position X and Y along the BV, Offset ("offset and curvature" to the BV) and Orientation. The Auto Sewing article says cuffs may use "either arm/wrist Arrangement Points".
- **Cut the band from the sleeve.** Epic's MetaHuman sweater guide offsets a sleeve-end segment as an internal line, uses Cut and Sew, resizes the band and simulates. The band starts life already round the arm (read through a summarising fetch).
- **Shrinkage Weft/Warp** is a pattern property, 100% by default. It shrinks the 3D piece without touching the 2D pattern, and the manual suggests it "to draw the inner layer against the avatar". A forum expert: weft is pattern width (x), warp is length (y), and it is not a fabric setting. The same expert fits a waistband, freezes it, and drapes a band over it at 5 mm particles with weft shrinkage changed.
- **Elastic** is a property of one line: on/off, Strength, Ratio and Length. CLO: strength defaults to 10; ratio runs 0 to 200, where 100 is unchanged.
- **Fabric.** Weft, warp and shear stretch are set in the fabric's properties. Strengthen hardens cloth and stops it stretching: not for rib.
- **Seams.** Tension is "Ease" or "Stretch" (pulls harder when lengths differ), with Strength and Ratio; fold angle 180 is flat.
- **Freeze** holds a pattern still while it keeps colliding: "draping and freezing inner Garments first".
- **Pins and tacks.** Pins can be attached to the avatar; Tack on Avatar and Line Tack on Avatar hold cloth to the body; Attach to Measure ties a line to an avatar tape.
- **Particle distance.** Coarse particles cannot fold round the body and poke through. Go a little below the avatar's quad size there (about 5 mm for gloves), only where needed (forum expert, seconded by MD staff).

## 2. What the 2026.1 API reaches

Signatures are from developer.marvelousdesigner.com/list.html. The changelog, read in full, ends at 2025.1.201 and names none of these.

- Arrangement: `pattern_api.SetArrangement(_patternIndex:int, _arrangementIndex:int)`, `SetArrangementPosition(_patternIndex:int, _positionX:int, _positionY:int, _offset:int)`, `SetArrangementOrientation(_patternIndex:int, _orientation:int)` and `GetArrangementOfPattern(_patternIndex:int)`.
- Shrinkage: `SetWidthShrinkagePercentage(_patternIndex:int, _width:float)`, `SetHeightShrinkagePercentage(...)` and `GetShrinkagePercentage(...)`.
- Elastic, per line (-1 = all lines): `SetPatternPieceElastic(_patternIndex:int, _lineIndex:int, _bElastic:bool)`, `SetPatternPieceElasticStrength(..., _strength:float)`, `SetPatternPieceElasticStrengthRatio(..., _ratio:int)` and `SetPatternPieceElasticSegmentLength` or `...TotalLength(..., float)`.
- Other piece settings: `SetPatternFreeze(_patternIndex:int, _bFreeze:bool)`, `SetPatternStrengthen`, `SetPatternHide3D`, `SetPatternLayer`, `SetAddlThicknessCollision`, `SetPatternPieceGrainDirection(_patternPieceIndex:int, _degree:float)`, `SetParticleDistanceOfPattern(_patternIndex:int, _length:float)` and `OffsetAsInternalLine(...)`.
- **Out of reach:**
  - Pins can only be counted or removed (`GetPinListSize`, `RemovePin`, `RemoveAllPins`).
  - There is no tack call.
  - There is no fabric-physics setter. `SetFabricInformation` writes labels; `GetFabricInfo(fabricIndex)` returns JSON.
  - Nothing sets a seam's tension or fold.
  - Nothing simulates only selected pieces: `Simulate(_steps)` runs everything, so freeze the rest.
- **In the live 2026.1.237 program but not in the docs** (signatures unknown): `CutPatternAlongInternalShapes`, `AddSeamlinePairGroupPartial`, `AttachPatternToAvatarMeasure`, `SetPatternEdgeToAvatarMeasure`, `ReDrape3DArrangement`, `ActivateAllPatterns` and `GetAvatarMeasurements`.

## 3. Recipe

0. **Probe (no drape):**
   - print `__doc__` for the live-only calls;
   - read `GetFabricInfo` for the rib's weft stretch;
   - read `GetArrangementOfPattern` on a placed cuff.
1. **Cheapest test: arrange only.** Cut both cuffs at 1.0 × the wrist (197 mm).
   - Right cuff (control): stays on Wrist_Outside_R.
   - Left cuff: on the arm's own BV, with `SetArrangement(cuffL, Arm_Outside_3_L)` then `SetArrangementPosition(cuffL, 50, 5, 30)`; if it sits high, try Y = 0.

   Sew, run no Simulate, export separate objects, and measure:
   - the ring's centre against the wrist joint along the forearm (target within 30 mm, above the hand);
   - whether the ring goes round the forearm's axis, not beside it.
2. **Settle loose.** Set the cuffs to 6 mm particles and drape as now (400 steps), with cuffs at 100%.
3. **Grip.** Freeze the fronts, back, collar, bands and tapes, leaving the sleeves free to gather. Run `SetWidthShrinkagePercentage(cuff, 85.0)` and `Simulate(200)`, then unfreeze and `Simulate(100)`.
   - If shrinkage does not grip: elastic on the cuff's top and bottom lines, strength 10, ratio 85.
   - If step 1 never wraps: Epic's method. Lengthen the sleeve by the cuff, drape it, cut the cuff off along an offset internal line (undocumented call), then assign rib, sew and shrink.
4. **Pass marks:**
   - lower-edge loop 175 to 195 mm round;
   - cuff centre within 30 mm of the wrist joint;
   - largest gap from cuff to skin under 5 mm;
   - the sleeve's end gathered into the cuff, with no lobe.

### Fresh review (27 October, night)

**Verdict: SOUND WITH FIXES.** Every cited page, the dump, the jobs and the live list read 27 Oct. Every call named is in the live list and matches the docs' signatures; the seven live-only ones are absent from the docs; all 109 points are Curved; the quotes stand.

- **Must fix, pass marks:** 175 to 195 mm cannot pass. His wrist's skin girth is 197.1 mm at its narrowest (body_measurements.py); a loop round it adds 2π × skin offset (3 mm in the docs' SetAvatarProperties example: about 216). The 5 mm gap is near the offset-plus-thickness floor. Set both from GetAvatarProperties and GetAddlThicknessCollisionValue, read in step 0.
- **Wrong:** drapes 4 to 8 ran at 10 mm and 1000 steps (job_v4 to v8.py), not 20 mm and 400.
- **Overstated:** drapes 7 and 8's cuffs differ by 16 and 35 mm per vertex (median): "did not fix it", not "changed nothing".
- **Guessed:** SetArrangementPosition's docstring gives no unit, range or direction; no manual page says "percentage"; offset 30 has no basis (arm points report 50).
- **Unweighed:** a BV has one Length X/Y, so the arm BV at Y 5 is likely upper-arm wide; the cuff may close beside the arm again, or spiral (no API call flips wrap). Give step 1's axis check a number.
- **Thin:** the waistband thread sets weft shrinkage to 115%, not below 100.
- **Minor:** AddFabric takes .jfab JSON (a rib-physics route); AddPinsForFabricValidation adds pins.
- **Support, measured:** an OBJ girth profile puts the left cuff's centre 45 to 80 mm past the wrist's narrowest girth, on the hand, in drapes 4, 6, 7 and 8. Centre to joint needs the FBX skeleton; "no lobe" has no number.
- **Not checked:** whether an unsimulated export shows the arrangement (every job so far simulated); the videos; his avatar's BV settings.

**Fixes taken (27 October, night):** the pass marks are set in step 0 from the avatar's own skin offset and collision thickness (GetAvatarProperties, GetAddlThicknessCollisionValue), not fixed beforehand: a loop round the wrist cannot be under its 197 mm girth plus 2 pi times that offset (about 216 mm at 3 mm); "the second try changed nothing" is corrected to "did not fix it" (drapes 7 and 8 differ); SetArrangementPosition's units and the offset value are tested in step 0 (GetArrangementOfPattern read back), not assumed; the step-1 check of the ring round the forearm gets a number (its centre within 30 mm of the forearm's axis line, beside the narrowest girth). The try itself waits on his word (the two-tries rule).
