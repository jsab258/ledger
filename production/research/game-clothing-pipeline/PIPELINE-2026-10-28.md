## 2. The pipeline as studios publish it

- **Pose.**
  - Supermassive drapes in A-pose [H]. Marvelous's manual drapes on "a T-pose avatar which can be easily draped", then poses.
  - DigixArt (Tides of Tomorrow, 26 Aug 2026) advises **zero gravity**, so the game does not carry the T- or A-pose's creases and sag.
  - Pose comes later, by skinning. CLO's rigging guide rigs in the bind pose.
  - No source mentions straight elbows; the project found that need by measuring.
- **Patterns:** real sewing patterns, from tailors, pattern sites or books (DigixArt; [H] EA, Ahmad).
- **Particle distance.**
  - Manual: 20 mm while building, "under 5mm" once done; range 0.8 to 700.
  - A CLO staff reply: 10 mm quad or 5 mm triangle is enough; subdivide elsewhere [H].
  - One artist uses 3 mm, with 2 or 1 at the cuffs [H].
- **Export:** thin, unwelded, single, UVs as the pattern (DigixArt; Supermassive [H]). Boisvert also exports a flat copy for retopology [H].
- **Detail in ZBrush** (DigixArt): polygroups per part, weld overlaps, subdivide, simplify some folds, sculpt seams, wear and dirt, then add thickness last.
- **Retopology.**
  - Maya Quad Draw is the most common [H].
  - Some retopologise the flat pattern copy and wrap it onto the drape (Supermassive, Alan Wake 2's artist [H]).
  - Boisvert's rules: all quads, edges along borders, equal vertex counts across sewn seams [H]. The project's retopo_pieces.py already meets them.
- **UVs:** from the pattern.
- **Bake:** Substance Painter or Marmoset (normal, occlusion, curvature) [H].
- **Texture:** DigixArt paints black-and-white masks only and colours them in an Unreal shader, as Mafia's single master material does.
- **Skinning:** weights transferred from the body, hand-fixed, tested in range-of-motion animations [H].
- **Levels of detail:** Gears Tactics level 1 at 75% [H]. Epic's MetaHuman outfits remesh to 50, 30 and 10%.
- **Rib and bands.**
  - Epic's MetaHuman sweater: bands are offset as internal lines, then cut and sewn off the draped garment [H].
  - CLO forum: ribbing is cut "slightly smaller" than the edge, with the fabric's weft stretch lowered (support.clo3d.com post 360000697127). Another reply: weft-stretchy, warp-stiff fabric, or weft shrinkage (360020807153).
  - Folded cuffs: a fold line, a Turned seam with Sublayer "Back", and Strengthen while folding, then off (900003087483).
  - Puffy cuffs, Marvelous's community manager: press tool, Turned seam, or the Binding tool (48272311635097).
  - No artist gives a shrinkage %, an elastic ratio or a double-layer recipe [H].

**What makes a cuff grip.** The sources and the project's numbers agree on five things:

1. **It starts round the wrist.** It is cut from a sleeve already round the arm (Epic) or placed on a T-pose, never wrapped beside a bent forearm.
2. **The bigger edge is gathered:**
   - the band is cut a little smaller than the edge;
   - the sleeve's extra length is eased in by elastic on its edge or an Ease seam;
   - it is not forced through a seam joining pieces of very different length and mesh size.
3. **The rib stretches across:** weft-stretchy and warp-firm, never Strengthened.
4. **The pull stops at the skin.**
   - A loop cannot be shorter than the wrist girth plus 2 pi times (skin offset + collision thickness). For Tom: 188 + 2 pi × 5.5, about 223 mm (measured, round 3).
   - Tighter is "solved" by pushing cloth into the body.
5. **The mesh is fine where it bends:** 5 to 6 mm on the cuff and on the sleeve's end.

**What try 3 and drape 19 lacked, against that:**
- Point 1 was wrong until round 4's straight elbows.
- Point 2: a 231 mm sleeve end was sewn to a 168 mm cuff (ratio 0.73) at 10 against 6 mm particles. That fits the lump and slits.
- Point 3: the stock rib's weft stretch was never read.
- Point 4: the grip asked for about 168 × 0.85 = 143 mm, about 0.64 of the 223 mm floor. That fits the points inside the wrist and the edges stretched to 57.5 mm.
- No script can set Turned or Sublayer, and Marvelous turned each cuff inside out at its seam.

**The cuff recipe** (derived, not tested):

- **Drape:**
  - on the straight-elbow body;
  - body pieces at 10 mm, 400 steps;
  - low gravity in the final settle (to test).
- **Cuff length:** its rest length is **0.90 to 0.95 of the floor**, 200 to 212 mm for Tom.
- **Rib fabric:** weft stretch lowered, through an exported and edited fabric file (route UNVERIFIED).
- **Gather by elastic on the sleeve's bottom line:**
  - Elastic on, **Strength 10**;
  - **Ratio = 100 × cuff ÷ sleeve end**: for Tom about 205 / 231 = **89**, leaving the seam near 1:1;
  - **Shrinkage 100** (not used to grip).
- **Particles:** **6 mm on the cuff and the sleeve**, 5 for the final.
- **Placement:** at the wrist on the forearm's volume:
  - position Y 25 (measured at the wrist);
  - offset 40;
  - shape style set after the position;
  - FlipPatternNormal if it wraps inside out.
- **Order:**
  1. drape the body;
  2. freeze the body pieces;
  3. sew the cuffs, side seams late;
  4. 200 steps at Normal;
  5. unfreeze;
  6. 100 steps at Fitting.
- **Optional:** pin the cuff's lower edge to a wrist girth measure (SetPatternEdgeToAvatarMeasure), if the avatar has one (UNVERIFIED).
- **Marks:**
  - centre within 30 mm along and off the axis;
  - gap 10.5 mm or less;
  - cover 90% or more;
  - no arm centre-line crossing;
  - nothing over 1 mm inside the body;
  - no edge over 1.2 times its pattern length (the strain map's red is 120%).

## 4. The Python API the skill needs

Sources:
- developer.marvelousdesigner.com: list.html (all 298,706 characters, read in three parts), scenario.html and changelog.html.
- The changelog now says the docs were **synced with 2026.1**: 139 functions added, JSON pattern files deprecated (always False; use ExportPatternCPX/ImportPatternCPX), and functions "declared but not implemented" removed.

"Docs" = in the synced docs.

**Calls the skill will use** (all docs):

- **Avatar:**
  - `SetAvatarProperties(i, {"SkinOffsetMM": ...})` and `GetAvatarProperties` (the keys given are examples);
  - `GetAvatarMeasurements(i) -> list`;
  - `ImportPose(path[, translation, toGround]) -> bool`.
- **Pattern and sewing:**
  - `CreatePatternWithPoints`, `OffsetAsInternalLine`, `GetPatternPieceArea -> float` (unit not stated);
  - `AddSeamlinePairGroup -> bool` (its docstring says it returns a name);
  - `AddSeamlinePairGroupPartial(... ratios 0 to 1 ...) -> int`, where 0 is success and negative is an error. New against the 27 October note, which called it live-only;
  - `FlipPatternNormal(list) -> bool` (all-or-nothing; simulate after).
- **Physics:**
  - `SetParticleDistanceOfPattern(p, mm)`, `SetPatternLayer`, `SetAddlThicknessCollision` and `GetAddlThicknessCollisionValue` (no unit given);
  - `SetWidthShrinkagePercentage` and `SetHeightShrinkagePercentage`;
  - `SetPatternPieceElastic(p, line, bool)`, `...ElasticStrength(float)`, `...ElasticStrengthRatio(int)`, `...ElasticTotalLength`;
  - `SetPatternFreeze` and `SetPatternStrengthen`;
  - `SetMeshType(p, "Triangle"|"Quad")`. It sets the type "used when getting mesh count information"; whether it changes the simulated mesh is UNVERIFIED.
- **Fabric:**
  - `AddFabric(.zfab|.jfab) -> int`, `AssignFabricToPattern(f, p, 1)`, `ReplaceFabric`;
  - `GetFabricInfo -> JSON`;
  - `CombineZfab(out, physicsFrom, imageFrom)`.
  - No setter exists for stretch, bending or density.
- **Pins:**
  - `SetPatternEdgeToAvatarMeasure(p, line, measure, attach) -> bool`: a line pin, after which the solver reruns;
  - `AttachPatternToAvatarMeasure -> JSON` (lengths in mm; height and linear measures refused).
  - No tack call exists; other pins can only be counted or removed.
- **Simulation:**
  - `SetSimulationQuality(q, mode)`: q is 0 Normal, 1 Animation, 2 Fitting ("accurate fabric") or 3 GPU;
  - `SetSimulationGravity` (default -9800; CLO's "low" -1600) and `SetSimulationAirDamping`;
  - `SetSimulationLayerBasedCollisionDetection`;
  - `Simulate(steps)`, `ResetClothArrangement` and `GetClothPositions`.
- **Zipper:**
  - `GenerateZippersFromObj(out, options, zipperIndex) -> bool` turns the project's own OBJ into teeth, slider, puller or stopper;
  - the style setters (`SetZipperStyleTeethWidth` in mm, and others).
  - **No call places a zipper on a garment.** The Zipper tool is clicks in the 3D window (47358168494745). Since 2026.0, custom OBJ teeth can be registered (47358323508377).
- **Topstitch:**
  - `AddSeamlineTopstitch(group, start, end, style) -> bool`;
  - `AddSegmentTopstitch(p, line, style) -> bool`;
  - `PlaceTopstitchOnInternalLine(0, style, p, line, anchor) -> bool`;
  - `SetTopStitchWidthValue(style, n, w)`.
- **UV and export:**
  - `UVPacking(all, fixScale, advancedSearch, padding, fidelity 1 to 5, rotations) -> bool`: padding 0.001 is one unit; advanced search is a genetic algorithm;
  - `ResetUVTo2DArrangement`, `FitAllUV`;
  - `BakeUVTexture(path, options, all) -> list[str]`;
  - `ExportOBJ` and `ExportFBX(path, options) -> list[str]`, `ExportUSD`;
  - the live options carry **weldType** (default FULLY_UNWELDED). That corrects the 26 Oct "no weld option"; its other values are UNVERIFIED.

**CLO's "Game-Ready Export" scenario:**
1. particle distance 15 mm on every piece;
2. `Simulate(60)`;
3. `UVPacking(True, False, True, 0.005, 2, [0, 90])`;
4. export options: thick, single object, unified UVs, 2048 unified diffuse and normal, scale 0.1;
5. `BakeUVTexture`, then `ExportOBJ` and `ExportFBX`.

It does **no retopology, levels of detail or skinning**.

**CLO's "Garment Inspection Report" scenario:**
- Per piece: name, area (mm²), bounding width and height, fabric, particle distance and layer.
- It writes `pattern_report.csv` and `.json`, then tints the largest piece.
- The skill should add `GetMeshCountByType` (faces and vertices), `GetShrinkagePercentage`, `GetAddlThicknessCollisionValue` and `GetSeamlinePairGroupCount`.

**Out of reach by script:**
- fabric physics;
- seam Turned, fold, tension and sublayer;
- internal-line fold;
- tacks;
- placing a zipper;
- remesh, retopology and the Quad (Grid) style;
- EveryWear;
- strain maps.

`CutPatternAlongInternalShapes` is in the live list but missing from the synced docs, which dropped unimplemented functions. That may explain its false returns in try 3 (inference, UNVERIFIED).

## 5. Marvelous's own game route against the bake chain

**What Marvelous offers**, all of it **window-only, none in the API**:
- The Quad (Grid) and Quad (Optimized) mesh styles: aligned quads per piece, density from particle distance (47358347315481). CLO advises triangles except where a detail must round, such as collars and piping (47358367597081).
- Remeshing to Retopology: a typed polygon count (47358258770073).
- Topology and Patch Topology: quads drawn by hand, with "ghost" points snapping across sewn edges (47358214227353). 2026.0 improved their performance and quality.
- EveryWear (CONNECT), per support-connect.clo-set.com articles 45304301912857 and 45304285349273:
  - polygon optimisation to a typed count;
  - Auto Seal;
  - rigging by weight transfer, with a maximum of joints per vertex (8 in CLO's Unreal guide, 47358145573401);
  - weight brushes;
  - it needs Auto Fitting on a fitting suit and an avatar with joints.

**Triangles (measured on drape 19).**
- Marvelous's mesher gives about 3.5 triangles per (particle distance)² of cloth: 0.0353 per mm² at 10 mm, median edge 8.1 mm.
- The jacket's 1.217 m² therefore comes to about 4.3 million ÷ d² triangles:
  - about 19,100 at CLO's 15 mm, inside Study 6's 18,000 to 22,000;
  - 10,700 at 20 mm;
  - 43,000 at 10 mm.
- So a 15 mm export meets the budget, but:
  - its triangles are uneven, with no edge loops at elbows and shoulders;
  - it has no folds finer than 15 mm;
  - nothing is baked from a finer drape (its bake carries only the fabric's own maps);
  - the 12 mm zip tapes need 4 mm cloth.
- The project's chain makes 18,976 to 21,448 triangles of seam-matched quads carrying a 10 mm drape's folds.

**UVs.**
- Both lay out the pattern.
- Marvelous's raw OBJ UVs overlap, in fabric-tile units (measured).
- Its genetic `UVPacking` may beat uv_pack_low.py's 77% fill (UNVERIFIED).

**Skinning.** EveryWear and bind_garment.py both copy the body's weights. Only the project's is scripted and posed in five poses.

**Published comparisons** [H]:
- CG Channel (2020): no edge-flow controls, "a lot of clean-up".
- A CLO community manager: the retopologised mesh is not for simulation; use it at the end.
- Supermassive: the newer tools "could now be used", but its artist prefers flat-pattern retopology.
- No measured comparison was found.

**Verdict.**
- The project's chain wins for hero garments.
- Marvelous's export may win only for background crowd garments (a 15 to 20 mm triangle export with packed UVs and no bake), or on UV packing.
- Both are cheap tests.

## 6. Acceptance checks per step

**Mafia's numbers (Study 6):**
- jacket 18,000 to 22,000 triangles (vest-jacket 19,131);
- levels of detail about 50, 25 and 10%;
- 2048 textures;
- no cloth on crowd garments.

**Other studios [H]:**
- Gears Tactics heroes have 100,000 to 120,000 vertices;
- the MetaHuman body has 30,500 vertices.
- **No studio publishes** a per-garment budget, a character texel density, a stretch limit or a clipping test. The marks without a source are the project's own.

**Strain.**
- Marvelous's strain map runs blue at 100% to red at 120% (Global; 47358286228505).
- Its Fabric mode reads "Force", where 100% is the fabric's tensile limit, "too uncomfortable to wear" (56518392287513).
- Both maps are window-only. The project computes the same ratio itself: draped edge ÷ pattern edge, from the OBJ's pattern coordinates.

**Penetration.**
- The rulings (2 October) say garment reviews count body points showing through first.
- Drape 19: 2 of about 1,175 under the cuffs.
- Skinned: points more than 3 mm inside the body, counted per pose.

**Texel density** (project arithmetic from RUBRIC.md):
- one metre fills 1280 ÷ d pixels;
- so at talk distance (4.4 m) cloth is about 0.29 px/mm on screen, and at street-near (3 m) 0.43;
- 0.9 px/mm in the texture gives twice the nearest view;
- the baked jacket has 1.55 to 1.79 px/mm at 2048.

**The Hook sheet.** Clothes are judged on the clothing floor and RUBRIC.md, not the Hook sheet (RULINGS 3 Oct):
- plain, period, nothing modern;
- no clipping or holes;
- nobody stiff;
- passers-by at 8 m, talkers close.

The Hook sheet then governs only the cloth's colour and sheen under the street's light.

## Steps for the skill

1. **Reference and pattern.**
   - Tool: dated 1990 photographs (production/research/plain-1990-clothes); the FreeSewing drafter (tools/meshgen/freesewing_draft.mjs); templates looked at only.
   - Check:
     - pattern girths against body plus ease;
     - the extended inspection report (areas, particles, layers, mesh and seam counts) against the plan;
     - a fresh reviewer against the references.
2. **Avatar and pose.**
   - Tool: the straight-elbow drape body (tools/md/pose_body.py), skin offset 3 mm; bent back by tools/md/unpose_garment.py.
   - Check (sleeve_wrap.py): every sleeve covers 90% or more round to its end, and the arm's centre line crosses no cloth.
3. **Drape.**
   - Tool: the bridge job. 20 mm while building; 10 mm body and 5 to 6 mm at bands, cuffs, collar and sleeve ends to finish; Fitting quality last; low gravity in the final settle (to test).
   - Check:
     - no edge over 1.2 times its pattern length outside seams;
     - body through cloth: 0 beyond 1 mm;
     - cloth inside the body: 0 beyond the skin offset;
     - silhouette against the reference.
4. **Rib cuffs, bands and hems.**
   - Tool: elastic on the gathered edge (Strength 10, Ratio 100 × band ÷ edge); weft-stretchy rib; rest length 0.90 to 0.95 of the collision floor; body frozen; bands sewn late. Tom's jacket: cuffs built in the bake (his page, 28 Oct).
   - Check:
     - centre within 30 mm;
     - gap 10.5 mm or less;
     - cover 90% or more;
     - no crossing;
     - no point inside;
     - no lump or slit at talk distance.
5. **Zipper and topstitch.**
   - Tool: the floater teeth (zip_floaters.py), or Marvelous's Zipper tool with the project's own OBJ teeth (`GenerateZippersFromObj`; placing needs the window). Topstitch by `AddSegmentTopstitch`/`AddSeamlineTopstitch` in the project's own style. Both only in the high model, baked to maps.
   - Check:
     - teeth = length ÷ pitch;
     - 8 px or more per tooth, or a tiling teeth normal;
     - no CLO trim in any shipped file.
6. **Detail in Blender (for ZBrush).**
   - Tool: import_md_drape.py (weld, pieces); a sculpt pass (multires) for seam puckers and wear; thickness last (Solidify).
   - Check: no non-manifold edge; the expected openings; boundaries within 0.05 mm of the plan.
7. **Game mesh and UVs.**
   - Tool: retopo_pieces.py and uv_pack_low.py. Marvelous's 15 mm export and `UVPacking` serve as one-off comparisons.
   - Check:
     - triangles in Study 6's row (jacket 18,000 to 22,000; Mafia 19,131);
     - seams shared 29 of 29;
     - cloth at 1.5 px/mm or more at 2048 (floor 0.9);
     - no overlap; padding 8 px.
8. **Bake, texture and wear.**
   - Tool: zip_bake.py (normal, occlusion, ID); the ID-tinted Unreal material with tint, grime and wet.
   - Check:
     - hits 97% or more;
     - ID areas within 1%;
     - normals over 60 degrees off under 1% per piece;
     - colour under street light against the Hook sheet's palette;
     - the clothing floor.
9. **Skinning, levels of detail, game.**
   - Tool: bind_garment.py and skin_garment.py (body weights, 8 influences at most, bind pose); levels of detail about 50, 25 and 10% (Mafia's screen sizes 1.0, 0.4, 0.15, 0.08).
   - Check:
     - rest, arms down, arms up, walking and sitting: points over 3 mm inside counted per pose, worst edge stretch;
     - RUBRIC.md lines 9 to 11;
     - a fresh reviewer per view at talk 4.4 m and street 3 to 28 m;
     - the packaged walk.
