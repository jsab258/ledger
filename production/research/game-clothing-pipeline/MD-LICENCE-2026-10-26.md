# Marvelous Designer's licence, read for game use (26 October 2026)

His ruling of 25 October: "its licence for game use is read before anything made in it goes in." He signed up on 26 October for one month of the Personal plan (renamed Individual on 13 October 2026, the same plan), billed monthly, signed in on this PC. Read by a research helper on 26 October 2026 from CLO's own legal pages (saved copies in the session's scratch folder). Not legal advice.

**Can his garments go in a sold game? Yes, on the written terms,** under these conditions:
- **One person.** The terms cover a natural person for personal use, or a self-employed one-person maker "for the internal requirements of one's business" (MD Additional Terms). Selling his own game is the second. CLO may ask for proof of self-employment and may move a user to Enterprise at its discretion (a company card or email, or a second person using the licence). No revenue limit found on this plan.
- **He owns his work.** CLO claims no rights in his work product (General Terms 7(h)); it keeps a non-exclusive licence over it to run and improve its services.
- **After the month.** Ownership has no end date and nothing read takes it away on cancelling, but no page says in words that garments may stay in a shipped game after he stops paying (unverified). Export every garment to our own files before the month ends, since account content may not be reachable afterwards. A written yes from CLO's support would settle it; writing to them is his.
- **Nothing of CLO's ships:** its avatars (attribution required on any store page or advert, a penalty of up to 25,000 USD: Additional Terms 4.1), its "restricted" samples (dummies, ready-made garments, trims, buttons, shoes: 4.3), and patterns built on its samples (their licence lasts only the licence term: 3.1b). CONNECT library items only under their Free or Extended licences, never the Basic one.
- **AI.** No ban found on building a game with AI help or on scripts through the program's own Python editor; no scraping or data mining of the program. MD's own AI features are under separate terms (their output may not train other AI): not to be used.
- **No free-trial work** goes in (the trial excludes commercial use; his ruling of 24 October).
- No attribution or watermark duty on his own output; notices embedded in exports must not be removed. New York law, London arbitration; he may opt out of arbitration within 30 days of agreeing (about 25 November 2026).
