# Waiting against the week's appointments (research note, 29 September 2026)

For town list 6ci. A separate helper was given the problem, not a theory, with about thirty minutes and web sources only; its note is kept here as it gave it, with the links and dates it cited. "Inference" marks the helper's own reasoning. Some findings (the Kingdom Come Steam threads, the Majora's Mask 3D interview) were seen only in search summaries, as it says.

LEDGER's street runs on a clock. The week holds appointments that come to the player (a night errand, a constable, a detective) and ones he has to go to (tea at nine, a Sunday question answered by six). A wait or a sleep that jumps past one could cost him something the game never warned him of. A wait that refuses too often would not get used.

## Findings

1. **Refusing to start.** Skyrim will not let the player sleep or wait with enemies nearby or while trespassing (UESP, current: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:Sleeping). Kingdom Come: Deliverance II refuses sleep, wait and fast travel during some timed quests, with a "no time to lose" message (GamerBlurb, 24 February 2025: https://gamerblurb.com/articles/quests-with-time-limits-explained-kcd2-kingdom-come-deliverance-2-guide).
2. **The complaint is expiry nobody warned of.** In the first Kingdom Come (2018), timed quests could fail while the player slept. Guides told players to read each quest's text at once and to save often (Gamepressure, 19 February 2018: https://www.gamepressure.com/kingdom-come-deliverance/why-did-i-fail-a-quest/z3aa01). The players' complaints were about not being told, not about the limits themselves (seen only in search summaries of Steam threads). The sequel was reported as much clearer: most of its quests no longer expire before they are started (GamerBlurb, 2025).
3. **"Wait until" the appointment.** Shenmue had no time skip, so players idled until events at set hours. Shenmue II added a fast-forward to a scheduled event, and reviewers named it as the fix (Wikipedia citing GameSpot: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shenmue_II; HonestGamers, 2 June 2022: https://www.honestgamers.com/15419/dreamcast/shenmue-ii/review.html).
4. **Choosing the hour, and reminders.** Majora's Mask (2000) skipped only to the next dawn or dusk. The 2015 remake lets the player choose any hour, and its notebook has alarms, so his companion reminds him of an event (Zelda Dungeon Wiki, current: https://www.zeldadungeon.net/wiki/Song_of_Double_Time). The developers presented the change as making sure players do not miss things (seen only in a search summary).
5. **A warning before spending time.** Pentiment warns that a search or conversation will take a while and asks whether you are sure; meals and sleep always pass time (Game Rant, 14 December 2022: https://gamerant.com/pentiment-manage-time-effectively-guide/). Its director on the scarcity: "you're operating on someone else's timetable" (Josh Sawyer, MMORPG.com, 8 December 2022: https://www.mmorpg.com/interviews/the-rpg-files-building-obsidians-pentiment-interview-with-game-director-josh-sawyer-2000126821).
6. **Deadlines on screen, and missing them made explicit.** Persona 5 marks each deadline on its calendar and the party reminds the player daily. Missing one means a bad end and a rewound week (Game Rant, 29 June 2025: https://gamerant.com/persona-5-phantom-x-no-palace-deadline-calendar-time-limit/). Invitations arrive as texts he answers (https://megamitensei.fandom.com/wiki/Confidant_Invitations, current).
7. **An arrival interrupts the wait.** In The Sims, the doorbell wakes sleeping Sims, and enough players dislike it that a mod softens it (CurseForge, updated 8 September 2026: https://www.curseforge.com/sims4/mods/ding-ditch-smart-doorbell). Dead Rising Deluxe Remaster's time skip has a normal and a fast speed, and the fast one can miss the radio calls that announce events (Push Square, 19 September 2024: https://www.pushsquare.com/guides/dead-rising-1-how-to-skip-time).
8. **Appointments nobody announced forgive.** In Oblivion, Glarthir comes back every midnight until the player turns up, with no penalty (UESP, current: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Oblivion:Paranoia). Skyrim's courier finds the player in any settlement (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:Courier). Stardew Valley sends a letter before each festival and a notice when it starts; going is optional (https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Festivals, current).
9. **Missing things as the design.** In Disco Elysium, time passes only in talk or reading, and timed events expire for good (https://discoelysium.wiki.gg/wiki/Time, current). The Last Express runs in real time, expects players to miss events, and offers a rewind clock (Game Developer, 28 November 2008: https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-last-express-revisiting-an-unsung-classic).
10. **Design writing.** Within the cap, the helper found no GDC talk or postmortem on time skips past scheduled events; the nearest are the Sawyer interview and the Last Express article. No source gives a standard lead time before an appointment.

## What it suggests for LEDGER (inference)

- **Sort appointments by what missing them costs.** Arrivals that cost little (the constable, the detective) forgive, like Glarthir. The errand man knocks, waking him from a wait or a sleep as the Sims doorbell does, rather than the ask passing silently.
- **An accepted appointment that costs him** (tea at nine, Sunday by six) stops any wait or sleep shortly before it, 30 to 60 game minutes (the helper's number), with one line naming it. He may then wait on past it knowingly: Pentiment's warning, not Kingdom Come's silent failure.
- **Refuse a wait outright** only when something is happening in front of him, as Skyrim does; otherwise stop it.
- **Offer "wait until" each known appointment**, so that waiting is how he reaches appointments, not how he misses them.
- **Only appointments he was told of may cost him.** Whether the errand may still pass silently is for Jafar.
