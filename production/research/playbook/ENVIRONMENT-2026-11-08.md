## 1. The method that worked

**Start from the reference games' numbers, then a written target.** The day-one values in "Universal AAA starting values" (sections 2 to 5: kits placed about 15 times a street, variety from material values on few meshes, few shadow-casting lights, costly effects only on heroes) came from sixteen studies; evidence production/research/aaa-reference/studies/. Every piece after that starts from an exact written target: parts in millimetres, materials with colour and roughness, every number tagged photo, read or judgement with its error, and a check with a wrong copy for every row ("13 Oct 2026, unit 13: exact targets for nine kinds of small street furniture"; "unit 9: the exact target for the doors"; "unit 8: the exact target for a family of repeated pieces"; evidence production/cloud-week/targets/).

- Measure photographs the way the check will measure the render: the same tight box on the object's own face, the same statistic ("unit 8", TARGET-REVIEW.md).
- Name the object's states (bare, net, draped; open, shut) before writing bands ("unit 8"; "unit 9").
- Photographs win over books, element by element ("What came before 13 October", PROVEN, The street's art; production/cloud-week/targets/pillar-box/PHOTO-CHECK.md).
- Test every placed piece against the place's own pieces, its doors and the walker's strip at body and head height, not against the target's own numbers ("unit 13", second list item 1; sd_place.py).

**Build by one command from the target, and prove it.** One script per family reads every number from the target; generalising a worked example must keep its output identical (same file, placement, pixel, check) ("13 Oct 2026, unit 7 stage 2: five more shopfronts"; "unit 9 build: ten door kinds and 29 fittings"; evidence production/cloud-week/kit/). Run every frame-free check (intersection, floating, real size, coplanar faces, printed words) before any long render: a two-minute layout test replaces an hour-long render ("14 Oct 2026, unit 7 stage 2, batch B"; "unit 7 stage 2", Try 2 method point 2).

**Bring delivered pieces in by one fixed route.** Split the glass from the rest so the street's own glass, reflections and breakage still work; join many-part models whole before import; dress material slots by name from one data file; fix faults at the source script and rebuild, never patch the export ("21 Oct 2026, item 5, the shop family's sample"; "item 5, first piece: a kit model brought into the game street"; evidence production/audits/gate-ritas-front/GATE.md, production/specs/kit-materials.json). The next piece of a family reuses the first one's route and settings unchanged, reading only its own places ("22 Oct 2026, item 5, the next shop family"; "a second delivered front worn by the first one's bake").

**Calibrate before tuning surfaces.** Confirm the exposure in force from the run's own log line, read a known chart through the player's camera, validate the base-colour view with the chart, then move base colours by measured factors toward researched real bands ("29 Oct 2026, a game street's surfaces calibrated to real reflectances"; production/research/street-wear/WET-GROUND-METHOD-2026-10-29.md). Read the engine's installed source before tuning a setting: the street was lit at 41% of its sky by a project setting ("What came before 13 October", PROVEN; production/lab/LAB-REPORT.md section 1).

**Wet ground is two layers.** Saturated rough stone at its real darkened colour, with water as a clear coat over part of it, built as a switch on the same material graph, measured against the reference's road regions; passed at its second try ("29 Oct 2026, wet ground drawn as two layers"; F:/LedgerTools/gate/new/look/2026-10-29-wet-t6-coat0.15).

**Wear: bake for unique pieces, decals for shared ones, by studied counts.**
- Unique delivered pieces: masks from the model itself (height, occlusion near and far, up-facing, convex edges, distance from a hand's place) baked into its own textures, dirt toward a warm hue at a fraction of the paint's luminance, grime only slightly duller than the paint ("22 Oct 2026, item 5, Rita's sample: a delivered model worn at the foot by baking"; production/research/street-wear/FRONT-WEAR-BAKE-2026-10-22.md).
- Kit pieces with named stains: bake each texel's position and facing in the piece's frame and draw seeded runs from their sources, laid over the shared material by an alpha that defaults to off ("22 Oct 2026, item 5, the lamp"; production/research/street-wear/LAMP-STAINING-BAKE-2026-10-22.md).
- Shared fronts: decals by the studied counts (a median of one a wall, at most six where the player looks, leaks about two in five), each mark measured in the base-colour and roughness buffers on against off, with anti-aliasing and streaming off ("30 Oct 2026, one shop front's weathering laid by a studied method"; F:/LedgerTools/gate/new/chandler-wear).
- Rooms: soft-edged decal pictures that fade to nothing at every edge; staining by height as one thin projection per wall, never one room-sized projection ("26 Oct 2026, a room's second wear pass"; "29 Oct 2026, a lived-in room's smoke staining by height"; production/research/cab-office-interior-1990/WEAR-2026-10-25.md).

**Light by measurement.** Take the designer's own light plan first; sweep each light's strength in the game's camera and set it by a written bar (no channel clipped over 1% of the window) ("22 Oct 2026, item 5, Rita's sample: a shop window's night light"). A lit room inside a street with one pinned exposure gets its own unbound post-process volume weighted by the camera's place in the room's box ("26 Oct 2026, a lit room's own exposure"; production/research/cab-office-interior-1990/LIGHT-2026-10-26.md). Find a shadow's light by switching light kinds off one at a time ("27 Oct 2026, a room's highlight burn answered by local exposure"). Readable night: one soft desaturated directional fill and a tinted haze, measuring near-black and clipped shares together ("A night that stays readable (4 November 2026)").

**Tiling scans: look, grade on the right part, break the repeat per element.** Per-unit re-tone of a masonry scan, simulated in numpy before any engine run ("A photographed tiling texture that repeats on a big wall (3 November 2026)"; F:/LedgerTools/gate/new/brick-tones). Give a part family its own material row before changing it; grade a two-colour scan on its paint alone ("Photographed trims on small parts (5 November 2026)"). Compress patches that are both pale and glossy ("Pale, glossy patches in a roof scan").

## 3. Traps: what failed, why, and how it was caught

1. **Long renders before frame-free checks.** The newsagent's first full gate (66 minutes) found 13 intersecting pairs a two-minute test would have found, then a second room render and gate (2 hours) followed ("13 Oct 2026, unit 7 stage 2", Try 2 point 2). Coplanar sill faces were found in a close frame after 3 hours of frames ("14 Oct 2026, unit 8", failed item 2). About 0.9 h (try 2) and 1.6 h (try 3) of renders were thrown away (same entry). Caught by reviewers and close frames; the rule now is to run every frame-free row first and the check on the first dozen frames.
2. **Reviewing only the views that changed.** The launderette's right view was first reviewed in round 3 and found a flat return behind side glass ("24 Oct 2026, the next shop family: a seventh delivered shop"; production/audits/gate-launderette/GATE.md); the chandler's door faults were in frames from round one, unseen for four rounds ("23 Oct 2026, item 5: a sixth delivered shop"). Fix: review every view, at night and from both slants, before calling a piece done.
3. **A world-aligned pattern for wear that has a source.** Rain runs in the shared master read as even grain after two tries ("21 Oct 2026, item 5: weathering a kit piece in the shared master material"); noise-drawn chips thin out with distance as mipmaps lose extremes ("30 Oct 2026, painted joinery chipped"). Fix: place stains at their sources and bake chip coverage into the paint's UV.
4. **One uniform water film over real-albedo ground** read as a milky sheet or polished tile; a brighter pavement then burned a night pool and stood a stain decal out ("29 Oct 2026, a game street's surfaces calibrated"). Caught by per-view reviewers; fixed by the two-layer method after research.
5. **Decals cannot reach everything.** A projected decal prints onto loose objects lying on the surface ("29 Oct 2026, wear decals printing onto loose objects"); a clear-coat water layer is beyond every decal ("30 Oct 2026, a contact shadow under footwear"); a decal's tint multiplies the picture's own grey, and a flat mark climbs anything upright in its box ("Ground wear at street level (5 November 2026)"). Read the floor's shading model before choosing a decal.
6. **Coplanar faces.** Two faces on one plane render black in a path tracer or fight in the engine: a filler block on the frontage plane, a kit head lining on the side linings' plane, scraper blades and beads ("22 Oct 2026, item 5, Rita's sample ... baking", failed item 4; "unit 8" try 2 lesson 4; "unit 9 build", failed item 3). Found by ray-casting through the black pixels; now a shared-plane row with a wrong copy.
7. **Light that leaks or burns.** A window lamp's end burned a side lining; a lamp with shadows off lit the next shop through a party wall ("23 Oct 2026, item 5: a fourth delivered shop"); a neighbour's window "lost its goods" because the old lamp had been lighting them through the wall ("21 Oct 2026, item 5, the shop family's sample"). Test by filming with the lamp off.
8. **Delivered materials that do not survive the trip.** A glTF specular factor arrived as twice the engine default and darkened paint 45% ("30 Oct 2026, painted joinery"); a near-mirror export read as sky from below ("23 Oct 2026, item 5: a fifth delivered shop"); see-through cloth and lettering misdrew ("fourth delivered shop"); flat-shaded goods striped under hard night light ("fifth delivered shop"). Check each exported material against the real one, and the night views first.
9. **Picture level and camera.** A room passed at the editor's top picture level and failed at the shipped level ("26 Oct 2026, a room passed at the editor's picture level"); the first diagnosis there was wrong (classic shadow maps were in use), corrected by halving the level groups ("26 Oct 2026, a quality setting raised only while the player is inside one room"). The page camera carried its own exposure ("26 Oct 2026, a lit room's own exposure"). Photograph at the shipped level through the player's camera.
10. **Night shots before the light settles.** Pictures 6 s after the clock jump differed everywhere; the wait is now 30 s ("22 Oct 2026, ... shop window's night light"). A wall-clock settle wait can be used up by a loading stall with no frames drawn; wait for frames as well ("A baked reflection caught before the scene was lit (4 November 2026)").
11. **Content that never shipped.** The masonry scans were absent from the shipped build for a month while the build machine stayed green ("A photographed tiling texture ... (3 November 2026)", the "And" paragraph). Compare one shipped picture with the editor's whenever a look depends on files read at run time; see TESTING.
12. **Far vegetation as opaque triangles** trades floating specks for clean lumps; three tries, set aside, a different method (cards or impostors) probably needed, unverified ("Distant trees and houses as plain geometry"; "Far trees: third try").
13. **Geometry for tiny wear** (paint flakes half a millimetre proud) read as confetti close and vanished far ("Worn paint as geometry over edges, set aside (7 Nov)").
14. **Placeholder states in fetched models.** A fetched clock fixed at 10:10 is wrong at most hours; anything printed with a time must follow the game clock or be time-free ("A room's small faults from one review ... (3 November 2026)").
