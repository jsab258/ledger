## 1. The method that worked

**Reference first: neither shipped game uses a searched locomotion method.** Walk on a state machine of chosen clips (starts, stops and turns for each foot), stops picked by remaining distance and stride quarter, turns in 45 degree steps, play rate within about 10% faster and 20% slower than authored, and the full foot rig only at close range ("Universal AAA starting values", section 7; production/research/aaa-reference/studies/07-animation.md). The project tried the searched route first and set it aside (see traps 1 and 2).

**Measure the source before fixing the runtime.**
- A planted foot in a clip's own frame must not travel; measure every clip's feet in its own data before adopting a clip set ("25 Oct 2026, the walk's slide"; "26 Oct 2026, stops chosen by stance and distance from a vendor's clips"; production/research/sit-and-turn/WALK-2026-10-25.md).
- Measure the driving body and the driven body at once, so a fault is put to the clip or to the copy ("22 Oct 2026, item 7, the walk"; "24 Oct 2026, the walk's second go"; "26 Oct 2026, the sit's third try").
- Find the planted foot by its height, never by a contact curve, and test that a measure moves as it should before believing it ("28 Oct 2026, a character sitting on a seat treated as an interaction spot").
- Before designing a fix, name the visible fault in a measure that moves with it: a frame where the eye objects must read worst ("30 Oct 2026, a visible fault every measure passed"; "30 Oct 2026, a replacement clip measured against the faulty one"; production/research/sit-and-turn/SIT-BOW-2026-10-30.md).

**Judge motion by eye on a fixed time step.** Pictures on a running clock stall frames and change the walk; picture every step around each contact with a camera held still, and have a fresh reviewer measure in pixels against the foot's own length ("24 Oct 2026, the walk's second go"; "A side-on film of a walk at full rate (8 November 2026)", F:/LedgerTools/gate/new/side-walk-2026-11-08). Ten pictures a second hid a slide lasting a tenth of a second ("25 Oct 2026, the walk's slide").

**Interactions as data derived from the clips.** Seats as spots in one spec (slot, cap, users, clips, exit); measure each entry clip once (root travel, warp bone end, seated pelvis), derive where the body must start, play every entry inside the room from that start and the envelope's corners, keep only entries with no limb contact, and write the kept list into the spec by a tool from the test's log, never by hand ("28 Oct 2026, a character sitting on a seat"; production/specs/seats.json, tools/seats_kept.py). Offer a seat by plain rules (in reach, in the room, free), not a traced line ("25 Oct 2026, the player sits"). Judge where the body sits, not the capsule (same entry).

**Living crowds without new animation.**
- Idle breaks: keep each character's own standing loop and add short breaks every 6 to 14 s as additive clips against their own first frame, layered only on limbs nothing else animates; test every clip on the narrowest body ("Standing characters that never freeze: idle breaks added to a loop (2 November 2026)"; F:/LedgerTools/tmp/idle-film-6).
- Walk-offs: clothing follows the body's pose; an in-place walk clip blended over the idle and carried at the clip's own speed measured over the whole clip; turn clips warped to the angle when over 50 degrees; the simulation's body moves at once while the figure walks; removal only out of view ("People walk off instead of vanishing ... (2 November 2026)"; F:/LedgerTools/tmp/walkoff/proof13).
- Freeze check: sample within the window and take the furthest reach; end-to-end comparison reads a loop as stillness ("A check that a figure is not frozen", 5 Nov; F:/LedgerTools/gate/new/corner-people-2026-11-05).

**Faces** were built by script from the character system's presets, finished by landmark moves and measured colours, judged blind and then by the owner's eye ("What came before 13 October", PROVEN, People; DECISIONS.md 28 and 30 Sep). Cost not recorded.

**Behaviour.** Witnesses: read the raw per-witness records behind a summary; when every sample gives the same empty answer at any distance, suspect the measure; an opening's own frame must not block sight through it; run old against new behind a switch ("A town deaf to its own crime (5 November 2026)"; F:/LedgerTools/tmp/witness-lines). Conversation line-of-sight must pass see-through surfaces ("30 Oct 2026, a visible fault every measure passed"). Before porting a behaviour, work out what it would do at the moments the game actually has; wire it only when every reachable rung has something to show ("27 Oct 2026, a behaviour ladder ported"; production/audits/sweep-2026-10-05/U3-DESIGN-2026-10-27.md).

**Review pictures.** Tag a body as undressed when its garments do not cover top and lower body; the picture mode hides tagged people, and every later path that shows a person checks the tag ("Keeping unfinished people out of review pictures (4 November 2026)"). Frame a whole person away from flat untextured ground props ("A garment cleared of the head mesh from behind", the judged paragraph, 8 Nov).

## 3. Traps: what failed, why, and how it was caught

1. **Adopting a vendor's searched locomotion before measuring its clips.** Motion matching brought onto another skeleton slid 20 to 100 cm on first steps; graph nodes of that kind cannot run from code in a shipped build; all clip sets searched at once let one set's bias win ("22 Oct 2026, item 7, the walk"; production/research/sit-and-turn/TOM-MOTION-MATCHING-2026-10-22.md). The puppet route kept 11 to 35 cm at worst ("24 Oct 2026, the walk's second go"). Contact marks rewritten left two moments sliding, because the clips mark those feet as moving ("25 Oct 2026, the walk's slide"). Every forward stop clip dragged its trailing foot 11 to 18 cm inside every allowed start ("26 Oct 2026, stops chosen by stance"). Caught by per-step pictures and fresh reviewers. The lesson the entries draw: the reference games' chosen-clip method, and measuring a clip set before adopting it. Still open in the walk-off entry (7 to 9 cm a step, 27 cm on the first step, 38 cm at the stop).
2. **Harness faults read as results.** Before any number counted, four harness faults: the puppet made before the game placed the player, strafing, a worn body that froze under a test camera, heights from the capsule ("24 Oct 2026, the walk's second go"). A foot "sliding" four metres means the measure is wrong (same entry). Extracting root motion pins the root and makes every planted foot travel a stride ("25 Oct 2026, the walk's slide").
3. **A measure that does not see the fault.** Holds on neck and back corrected what the measures saw and the eye did not object to; the head's pitch was unmeasured ("30 Oct 2026, a visible fault every measure passed"). A candidate clip was set aside on the neck joint's line when the face's direction showed it was the better clip ("30 Oct 2026, a replacement clip measured"). Caught the same morning by re-measuring by the face's direction.
4. **Speed over a frame window that ran past the clip's end** gave 140 cm/s for 180, so every planted foot slid 25 cm back ("People walk off instead of vanishing"). Turning the whole body in place slid a planted foot 41 cm (same entry).
5. **Numbers passing a motion the eye fails.** The sit landed within its numbers while a reviewer failed a held foot, a tiptoe slide and a folding head ("25 Oct 2026, the player sits"). The contact-mark reading passed a walk that visibly slides 10 to 15 cm ("24 Oct 2026, the walk's second go").
6. **A small room, not the chooser, limits interactions.** Only 1 or 2 of 10 seat entries fit a bench beside a counter, from 100 to 150 cm of travel; the stride was out of step on every walk-in ("28 Oct 2026, a character sitting on a seat"). Switching a spawned mesh's collision off straight after spawning did not stay off; name the no-collision profile (same entry; FINDINGS.md collision).
7. **Whole-clip breaks carry another skeleton's stance** (shop-dummy arms); breaks planned from the spine parted the head from the neck ("Standing characters that never freeze").
8. **Clothes cannot follow poses outside their skinning range**: a leg-crossing turn stretched shorts to the shin; the clothes return to their own idle during such clips ("People walk off instead of vanishing").
9. **Engine animation classes must list every node they run**, or the source pose is never read and the body stands in its reference pose ("30 Oct 2026, a visible fault every measure passed").
10. **Hidden people coming back.** Undressed figures hidden once at the start were shown again by a schedule's own show call ("Keeping unfinished people out of review pictures").
11. **Judging a rendering setting on one near frame.** Hair environment light lifted near hair and read blond and speckled at middle distance ("Hair's sky light, set aside").
12. **A summary line hiding a dead system.** Witnesses missed a deed for nights while a check passed nightly by counting onlookers measured, not informed ("A town deaf to its own crime").
13. **Face colour by script.** One face route failed because a baked brow colour cannot be set by script ("What came before 13 October", FAILED, People).
