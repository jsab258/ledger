All numbers below are **proposed ceilings/estimates**, not measured productivity. W = one ordinary weekly Claude allowance. Spend at most **0.70W production + 0.10W independent review** per week; reserve 0.20W. Record the account meter before/after tasks; never substitute API dollars for subscription usage. Adopt this plan once, replacing conflicting lists.

Today, with approximately 10% remaining, cap stabilisation at **0.05W**; pause new production until reset. Measure accepted-family cost and reuse time in phase 1 before extrapolating town production.
