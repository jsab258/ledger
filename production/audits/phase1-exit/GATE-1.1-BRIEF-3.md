- Not the maker's verdict, and not any other view.

## What the reviewer returns, in this order

1. **What changed** between the baseline and the new picture, each change marked fault or not.
2. **New faults**: anything wrong in the new picture that the known list does not name. These block.
3. **Known shortfalls** still seen, each marked narrow (a later step deals with it) or not.
No overall pass or fail: the maker reads the three lists against the ruling.
