Repository snapshot: main at [f3ef107](https://github.com/jsab258/ledger/commit/f3ef1070dff0dc745d3cfd20796f7f170451ac94).

**Recommendation: acquire finished garment shapes, commission tightly bounded corrections, and reuse an approved wardrobe.** Keep MetaHumans. Buy no new software first. Test this route on one suit before expanding. I cannot substantiate a near-free, fully automated route to forty distinct AAA garments; the documented small-team methods still require skilled art work.

Your failures do not establish that tailoring is impossible in these tools:

- **Cut and deformation remain separate failures.** Skinning governs deformation; it does not supply a convincing cut. The [MakeHuman review](https://github.com/jsab258/ledger/blob/f3ef1070dff0dc745d3cfd20796f7f170451ac94/production/art/clothing/suit-jacket/review-3.md) still describes an unsuitable silhouette on Darren. The [MD review](https://github.com/jsab258/ledger/blob/f3ef1070dff0dc745d3cfd20796f7f170451ac94/production/art/clothing/md-suit-jacket/review-1-ingame-ron.md) records collapsed lapels, ballooning sleeves, trousers penetrating the jacket and seated distortion. Fitting is not solved either.
- **The agents’ approach is the demonstrated limitation.** The [MD review](https://github.com/jsab258/ledger/blob/f3ef1070dff0dc745d3cfd20796f7f170451ac94/production/art/clothing/md-suit-jacket/review-1-ingame-ron.md) records nine unsuccessful drapes; that does not prove MD cannot make suits. Your [API investigation](https://github.com/jsab258/ledger/blob/f3ef1070dff0dc745d3cfd20796f7f170451ac94/production/research/clothing-pipeline/MD-LAPEL-FOLD-TURNED-2026-10-02.md) even corrects an earlier claim that a strengthening function was unavailable. Your [“ready” basics were subsequently rejected or qualified](https://github.com/jsab258/ledger/blob/f3ef1070dff0dc745d3cfd20796f7f170451ac94/production/art/clothing/sheila/ready-pieces-against-the-bar-2026-10-01.md). - **Forty garments × six bodies is an estimate, not a requirement.** The [wardrobe summary](https://github.com/jsab258/ledger/blob/f3ef1070dff0dc745d3cfd20796f7f170451ac94/production/research/wardrobe-at-scale/SUMMARY.md) labels its counts as inference. Every principal does not need every outfit immediately.

Actual production examples contradict the idea of a missing automatic solution. **Manor Lords** is a historical, solo-led game whose [developer describes outsourcing after funding](https://www.unrealengine.com/developer-interviews/solo-dev-makes-sophisticated-sim-manor-lords-using-unreal-engine?lang=en-ca). Its [character artist](https://mggeymonat.artstation.com/projects/14vGJK) used MD/ZBrush, Maya retopology and skinning, Substance and Unreal. **DigixArt’s Tides of Tomorrow** had one character artist for two years: [four body types, Houdini fitting, manual vertex corrections, shared garments and material-mask variations](https://www.marvelousdesigner.com/en/learn/userspotlight/post/9aba618a803e4e5eb7adb3f7ac375ee9). **Innoloop’s Rhythm Towers** combines [Character Creator, MD, sculpting and Unreal cloth setup](https://www.marvelousdesigner.com/learn/userspotlight/post/ce320914f6c243e1b38299ebd6b26ddf). The latter two are stylized; they demonstrate workflows, not proof of your budget or realism target.

These examples still use simulation during garment creation. Changing the runtime method to skinning never eliminated the need to author and finish a convincing garment. Even KCD2’s [Warhorse presentation, slide 24](https://media.gdcvault.com/gdc2025/Slides/Molnar_Gabor_Dressed_to_Quest.pdf), describes abandoning runtime clothing adaptation in favour of body-part segmentation and standardized seams. Controlled compatibility is a legitimate production strategy.

The cheapest meaningful proof of this route is **step 1**, not buying another tool. Keep cloth simulation off initially. Film both bodies walking, sitting, turning and raising their arms, in street lighting and at dialogue distance. Pass only if cut, collar, sleeve volume, layering and deformation hold. Test reduced LODs and record GPU time/VRAM on the RX 6700. Permit one bounded correction; do not accumulate failed purchases.

Do not confuse an adjustable suit with an appropriate suit. If shoulder structure, button stance and length all need reconstruction, reject that source before paying for adaptation. A successful male jacket also does not establish a skirt-suit or mac pipeline: require one female tailored look next. Keep agents on repeatable imports, material variants, body masks and test captures; reserve disputed silhouette judgements for you or the commissioned artist.

The first-street envelope is approximately **$225–690 including the proof**, conditional on reuse. It does not fund ten bespoke outerwear commissions. If that exceeds your available money, the honest next milestone is two to four finished outfits for the playable slice. Keeping the entire bespoke scope, close-up AAA bar and negligible art budget simultaneously is not a plan I can verify.

**Stop** procedural tailoring from scratch, blanket six-body fitting, new garment families before this proof, repeated blind reviews without a fixed rubric, and treating inaccessible websites as negative evidence.
