# LEDGER — draft production charter

**Goal.** Deliver thirty minutes of Quay Street that the owner wants to play and friends can launch on his PC. They accept the street’s appearance, observe the town remembering their actions, and experience responsive conversation. This charter proposes a successor to the current list; it changes no repository instructions.

**Scope.** One continuous playable street, Mickey’s usable front office, three existing conversational principals, and only the additional people/places needed to make the approved first-week beats visible. A deed must produce an intelligible later consequence, surviving reload. Every visible person participates in perception/memory, unless explicitly ruled scenery. No new acts, combat programme, district production or crowd of hundreds before this works. ([First-hour design](https://github.com/jsab258/ledger/blob/0a6ca2af8c0c6e49df106e8539d771e56f4403dc/game-design/first-hour-2026-09-29.md); [ROADMAP](https://github.com/jsab258/ledger/blob/0a6ca2af8c0c6e49df106e8539d771e56f4403dc/ROADMAP.md); [RULINGS](https://github.com/jsab258/ledger/blob/0a6ca2af8c0c6e49df106e8539d771e56f4403dc/RULINGS.md).)

**Bar.** Hook-quality street composition, materials, depth, wear and atmosphere in the actual moving game, including reverse views. Preserve approved identities and voices. Clothes retain the explicit exception: plain, period-plausible, no holes, clipping, modern giveaways or frozen bodies; close inspection for speakers, eight metres for extras. A static approved picture cannot approve a broken walk. ([References](https://github.com/jsab258/ledger/blob/0a6ca2af8c0c6e49df106e8539d771e56f4403dc/production/reference/README.md); [RULINGS](https://github.com/jsab258/ledger/blob/0a6ca2af8c0c6e49df106e8539d771e56f4403dc/RULINGS.md).)

**Weather.** Your latest ruling supersedes constant wetness: grey dry overcast, drizzle and rain; surfaces gradually wet and dry; rain changes attendance, hearing and visibility. Sunshine disabled for the friends’ build.

**Constraints.** AI does implementation; no hiring, new subscriptions or unapproved purchases. Use verified AI-permitted sources; exclude NoAI assets from game, project and references. One RX 6700, 10 GB, shared with voice. Respect the normal weekly allowance and existing $1/day measurement and $5/friends-evening caps. No reset or paid overage assumed. ([RULINGS](https://github.com/jsab258/ledger/blob/0a6ca2af8c0c6e49df106e8539d771e56f4403dc/RULINGS.md); [budgets](https://github.com/jsab258/ledger/blob/0a6ca2af8c0c6e49df106e8539d771e56f4403dc/production/research/pre-production/4-BUDGETS.md).)

**Authority.** The owner decides taste, identity and money. Agents own implementation, sequencing within the agreed phase, evidence and corrective work. P5’s already-requested one-time account setup is a declared technical exception, not a recurring management duty. ([P5](https://github.com/jsab258/ledger/blob/0a6ca2af8c0c6e49df106e8539d771e56f4403dc/production/playtest/friends-account-2026-10-03.md).)

**Stop condition.** If a representative street-and-person sample cannot pass the stated bar within its capped proof budget, report the failed capability. Do not disguise failure as another plan or promise a town.
