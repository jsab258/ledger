**E** = evidenced for at least one representative asset; **P** = partial, proposed or legacy-only; **—** = no evidence found. **Risk order; minimum corrections for one owner and AI:**

1. **Validate the actual assembled game.** This affects every department. [Automation retains stand-ins](https://github.com/jsab258/ledger/blob/6de07a0/production/specs/street-people.json), while [packaging uses Development](https://github.com/jsab258/ledger/blob/6de07a0/.github/workflows/ledger-probe-unreal.yml) and the [launcher still requires checkout files](https://github.com/jsab258/ledger/commit/b91d60a1). Minimum: one repeatable packaged walk using the actual cast, dialogue, lighting and audio; periodically test Shipping without the source checkout. Retain deterministic simulation tests separately.
2. **Approve complete characters before multiplying them.** [Sam’s age mismatch](https://github.com/jsab258/ledger/blob/6de07a0/FINDINGS.md) is already recorded. Minimum: one sheet per person—age, occupation, front/profile/full body, clothes, hair, three voiced lines—approved together. Then watch that person talk and move in street lighting.
3. **Prove manufacture before committing to the asset family.** The [jacket trial](https://github.com/jsab258/ledger/blob/6de07a0/production/research/clothing-pipeline/TRIED-2026-09-24.md) exposes export, body and hardware incompatibilities after character assembly. Minimum: one garment fitted, deformed, imported and packaged on two different bodies before further wardrobe work.
4. **Reapprove content when canon or consumers change.** The [alcohol line](https://github.com/jsab258/ledger/blob/6de07a0/FINDINGS.md) and [pending interior reviews](https://github.com/jsab258/ledger/blob/6de07a0/production/art/interiors-2026-09-23/manifest.json) demonstrate failed checkpoints. Minimum: inspect changed outputs, record one acceptance against their commit, and invalidate affected approvals when their source changes.
5. **Require a representative quality sample before batches.** [Prop checks](https://github.com/jsab258/ledger/blob/6de07a0/production/specs/asset-interface.md) establish importability; [music code](https://github.com/jsab258/ledger/blob/6de07a0/ledger/Assets/Scripts/Core/MusicModel.cs) establishes behavior. Neither establishes artistic fitness. Minimum: one approved prop/material, sound palette and minute of music in context before expanding each family.

Unbuilt interiors, vegetation and release collateral carry less immediate rework risk; apply the same sample approval when their production starts. These priorities are **medium-confidence judgments**; hours cannot be quantified from this evidence. Keep approvals beside existing specs/assets and a short [FOR-JAFAR](https://github.com/jsab258/ledger/blob/6de07a0/FOR-JAFAR.md) sitting summary; additional studio roles and reporting documents would not close these checkpoints.
