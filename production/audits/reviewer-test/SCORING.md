# How the reviews are scored (written 7 October 2026, before any review was run)

1. **Caught**: the reviewer names a fault at the planted fault's place (the object or area it is on) and says something is wrong there that fits the planted defect, in any words. A general complaint that happens to cover the area ("the brick looks flat") does not count unless it singles the planted place out.
2. **Caught high**: caught among the first three faults listed (the gate lists worst first; his page shows the worst).
3. **Missed**: anything else, including a verdict of FAIL given only for other faults.
4. **Every other fault named** is checked against the picture: **real** when it is there to be seen at the place named (a fault the base picture already had, or a fair judgment against the bar, such as flat light beside the Hook sheet); **false** when the thing described is not in the picture at the place named, or the picture plainly contradicts it. False ones are the false alarms, counted on clean and faulty pictures alike.
5. **Clean pictures**: the verdict is recorded too; a FAIL on a clean copy is not by itself a false alarm, since every base is unfinished work that already falls short of the bar.
6. **The independent check**: a fresh helper, given only the key and the reviews, scores caught or missed again; every disagreement is listed with the reason it was settled.
