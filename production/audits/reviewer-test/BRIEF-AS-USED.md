# The brief every reviewer in this test was given

The gate's brief is `production/audits/phase1-exit/GATE-1.1-BRIEF-2.md`, the latest one a fresh reviewer was given (7 October, the second review of item 1.1). This test kept its standing parts word for word and changed only what belongs to one round of item 1.1. Each reviewer got its own copy with its own picture's path.

## What each reviewer was told

> You are a fresh reviewer for LEDGER's quality gate, given its brief; you have not seen this work being made. You are read-only: create, change or delete nothing, run nothing, and read only the files the brief names. First read the brief: `<path>`. Then read the pictures it names with your Read tool and return exactly what the brief asks the reviewer to return, in plain words, no preamble.

Each reviewer was a fresh general-purpose helper on Opus (PLAN.md: "Opus for ... each gate's fresh review"), started in this repository, so CLAUDE.md was in its context as it is for the gate's reviewers on his PC.

## What was changed from the gate's brief, and why

1. **Kept word for word:** the title, the opening paragraph, the requirement, the bar and what the reviewer returns.
2. **The outputs:** one picture each, named by its view and said to be a reduced copy, cut where it is cut.
3. **Left out:** "What the first review failed", "What was done", "This round's outputs" and the maker's known faults. They belong to item 1.1's second round, name other pictures, and list several of the very fault kinds planted here (a black block, a ragged white strip in the top pane, a sawtooth black slab), so they would have told the reviewer where to look. The return line's last clause ("which of the first review's seven points are answered") went with them.
4. **Added one line:** the Hook sheet comes from a copy of its upper part, because its lower part has passers-by and people stay out of this test; the KCD2 frames live only on his PC and cannot be reached from the cloud.
