## False alarms

Both runs taken together named 87 faults in the ten reviews of clean pictures. A fresh helper checked each one against the picture: 66 are there to be seen, 20 are fair judgments against the bar ("no wear", "not 2026 grade"), and it flagged 1 as false: a pale patch on the road, which it took for the kiosk's reflection. A hard-edged patch is there where the reviewer said, under the brick block, so that claim stands too (CHECK-FALSE-ALARMS.md): 67 there, 20 judgments, **no confirmed false alarm**. Nine real faults carry a wrong detail: "no downpipes" where one shows faintly in the dark, "no papers" with papers on the bench, "same gold lettering" where one sign is cream.

In the faulty pictures I checked by eye every claim of a particular mark or object I was not sure of, about a dozen of them. All were there. One had a detail wrong (a kettle "seen through" the blind when it stands below it). Nobody blamed a clean picture for a change that was not made. In the realism check, with two days' real work between the pictures, one reviewer called a real change worse (the office blind gone darker), which is a fair call, and another named real changes as improvements.
