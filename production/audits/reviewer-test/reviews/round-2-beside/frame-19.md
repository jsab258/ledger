# Round two, beside the clean original: frame-19

Planted: texture tiling: the corner wall's lower part is one small stained tile repeated in a grid, seams showing. The reviewer was not told. Its reply, word for word:

---

FAIL

1. The side wall right of the corner has a broken patch, all in frame-19.jpg. It is a large rectangle running from fascia height down to the pavement and filling most of that wall. Inside it the brick shows heavy vertical dark-and-light stripes that repeat across its width, and its brick courses don't line up with the wall above. It has a hard straight top edge at fascia level and a seam at the corner. It reads as a texture or decal glitch, not as weathering. frame-19-before.jpg shows the same wall as continuous brick with soft grime streaks, so this change brought the fault in. On its own it is a visible fault that fails the step.

2. The first-floor window is wrong for the Hook sheet (frame-19.jpg, present in the before picture too). It has a modern-looking plain white frame with a venetian blind, in a flat-topped opening with no brick arch over it. The Hook sheet's frontage has tall sash windows under arched brick heads.

3. The shop window glass shows almost no reflection of the street (frame-19.jpg). The interior shows through as if the glass were not there, but the requirement asks for real glass reflecting the street.

4. There are no rainwater pipes (frame-19.jpg). Neither the corner nor the side wall has the black downpipes the Hook sheet shows on every frontage, so the side wall reads as a bare block.

5. The shopfront is too clean for a 1990 cab office (frame-19.jpg). The navy pilaster, fascia, door panel and white door surround show almost no chips, dirt or wear, though the brick above them is grimed.

6. The brick courses may not turn the corner properly (frame-19.jpg, uncertain at this size). The brick on the two faces seems to meet in a straight vertical seam instead of bonding round the corner.

Points 3 to 6 are narrow and could be left to later steps. Point 1 can't be, and point 2 is a clear mismatch with the Hook sheet. I judged against the Hook sheet's upper part only, because the two KCD2 frames could not be reached.
