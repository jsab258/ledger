# Round two, beside the clean original: frame-23

Planted: nothing (a clean copy). The reviewer was not told. Its reply, word for word:

---

FAIL

All faults are in frame-23 unless marked otherwise. Worst first:

1. The glass does not reflect the street. This is meant to be a daytime view, but the window reads like a lit shop window at night: the room shows through clean and bright, with only faint ghost shapes on the glass. The requirement asks for real glass reflecting the street, and this is not it.
2. Across the top row of small panes there is a flat grey band with a ragged, torn top edge. It looks like a broken reflection, not a reflection of real rooftops. It is a visible glitch.
3. The picture is not 2026 quality next to the Hook sheet. The Hook sheet shows weathered brick, paint and timber. Here the materials are clean and flat. The office walls have a blotchy mustard texture, the window bars are uniform flat paint with no wear, grime or chipping, and the carpet is plain grey with no wear.
4. The pillar between the neighbour and Mickey's window is black with a bubbly, speckled texture, like foam or pumice. It does not read as painted wood or stone. In the Hook sheet, Mickey's shopfront is painted blue-grey woodwork.
5. The required 1990 cab-office clutter and wear are missing or too thin. No ashtray can be seen anywhere. The counter is clean and unscuffed, and the room is tidy and sparse. The kettle, telephone, filing cabinet and a box that may be the radio set are there. The notes on the corkboard and the papers are blank coloured rectangles.
6. Objects in the window look like placeholders. The two pale yellow slabs at bottom right are untextured and it is unclear what they are. The black cylinder beside them is plain, with no surface detail.
7. The neighbour's doorway at the far left is solid black with no detail. It reads as an unlit hole.
8. The blind on the left half of the window looks like it floats in front of the room. There is a small bright white sliver near its lower left corner, roughly a third of the way in from the left and just over halfway down.
9. Frame-23 and frame-23-before look identical by eye; I compared them only by looking, not with a tool. Nothing changed visibly since the last picture of this view, so there is no visible change for him to judge.
