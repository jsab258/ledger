# Round two, beside the clean original: frame-21

Planted: test-colour block: the notice board on the right wall is flat magenta (a missing material). The reviewer was not told. Its reply, word for word:

---

FAIL

Faults, worst first:

1. The notice board on the right wall, beside the back door, is now solid bright magenta behind its yellow notes. That is a missing-material or placeholder colour. In the before picture it was a cork board, so this change caused it and made the picture worse. (frame-21.jpg; it is not in frame-21-before.jpg)

2. The glass does not reflect the street. The shop window is almost completely clear and shows only a faint ghost near the top. The requirement asks for real glass reflecting the street, and in the Hook sheet's flat grey daylight a window like this would show a strong reflection. (frame-21.jpg, and the same in frame-21-before.jpg)

3. Across both upper panes of the window there is a flat cream shape with a torn, ragged lower edge. It does not read as ceiling, blind or reflection; it looks like a geometry or reflection glitch. (frame-21.jpg, and the same in frame-21-before.jpg)

4. The dark pier to the left of the window has a coarse, sponge-like holed texture. The holes are far too large for the surface, and it reads as noise rather than stone, render or painted wood. (frame-21.jpg, and the same in frame-21-before.jpg)

5. The light does not read as day. The street side is dim and the inside glows warm like evening, which does not match the Hook sheet's even, overcast daylight or a view described as "by day". (frame-21.jpg, and the same in frame-21-before.jpg)

6. There is too little wear and clutter for a 1990 cab office:
   - The counter top is clean and nearly bare.
   - The walls and floor are even, and no ashtray can be seen.
   - The red pilaster and blue-grey window frames are flat and unworn, next to the weathered shopfronts in the Hook sheet.

   (frame-21.jpg, and the same in frame-21-before.jpg)

I could not compare against the two KCD2 frames because they could not be reached. The judgement above is against the upper part of the Hook sheet and the brief's requirement only.
