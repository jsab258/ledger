# Round two, beside the clean original: frame-06

Planted: missing window pane: the lower right pane of the middle first-floor window is a black hole. The reviewer was not told. Its reply, word for word:

---

FAIL

All faults are seen in frame-06, the new picture, unless I say otherwise. Worst first:

1. **The one change is a new fault.** The empty unit's first-floor window, in the middle of the upper wall, now has its lower-right pane in solid flat black. In the before picture it matched the other three beige panes. The black pane has no glass edge, cracks, shards or anything visible behind it, so it reads as a missing texture or a hole, not a broken window. Nothing else in the frame differs from the before picture.

2. **The pillar box looks like a placeholder.** It is a plain red cylinder with a flat lip on a black base. It has no domed cap, no posting slot, no collection plate and no royal cipher. It stands in the foreground in front of the launderette, so the eye goes straight to it.

3. **The upper frontage does not match the Hook sheet.** The Hook sheet's terrace has tall sash windows under brick arches, stone sills, corbelled brick eaves and heavy chimney stacks. Here the upper windows are small, squarish and flat-headed, set flat in plain brick with thin slab sills and no arch or lintel. The roof meets the wall in a plain dark line with no eaves detail. The brick is a flat repeating texture with no depth in the mortar or the window reveals.

4. **The far right has a flat cream panel.** It is upper storey, next door, beside the right-hand chimney. It has no window, texture, weathering or detail, so it reads as an untextured placeholder wall.

5. **The glass reflects nothing.** The launderette windows show the washing machines inside with no reflection of the street, the pillar box or the lamp post. On a wet, overcast street seen square on, they read as open holes. The upper windows show flat net or blind textures and no reflection either.

6. **The empty unit's shopfront has a geometry error.** The white top rail of its shop window runs on past the window across bare brick to above the two doors, where it stops in mid-air. A patch of brick also sits inside the black timber surround above the doors. The surround should be continuous joinery.

7. **The road has a seam.** The wet reflections in the road end on a dead-straight horizontal line at the white centre dash. Below it the asphalt is suddenly dry and crazed. It reads as a seam between two materials, not a puddle edge.

8. **Smaller points:**
   - The two dustbins are identical clean copies with no dents.
   - The "TO LET" sign is a small generic plaque with no agent's name or telephone number, unlike a real 1990 letting board.
   - The lamp head is a flat dark slab with no detail.
   - The whitening on the empty shop's glass reads as grey stone, not soaped glass.
   - The small print on the posters cannot be read at this size, so I could not judge it.

Points 2 to 8 are equally present in the before picture. The two KCD2 frames could not be reached, so I judged against the Hook sheet only.
