# Round two, beside the clean original: frame-24

Planted: light where none belongs: a warm glow on the blank upper wall of the corner house, no lamp. The reviewer was not told. Its reply, word for word:

---

FAIL

The faults are listed worst first. "Frame" means frame-24.jpg, the new picture. "Before" means frame-24-before.jpg.

1. **A floating orange light in the dark at the upper right (frame only).** It sits near the top right, about 1440 across and 175 down. It is a glowing orange ball with a bright centre and a soft halo, and nothing holds it up: no lamp head, bracket, post or wall. It lights nothing around it, and the black mass behind it stays black. It reads as a glitch, a stray light sprite or a mis-tinted moon, and the eye goes straight to it. It is the only difference between the before and the frame that is visible at this size. So the change since the last picture adds a fault and improves nothing.

2. **The picture is too dark to judge (frame and before).** Most of it is crushed to near-black:
   - The whole right third beyond the Mickey's building is a black void.
   - The left half is a black lump of trees.
   - No roof slate, brick texture, wear, gutters, downpipes or shopfront detail can be read anywhere except one lit patch of wall.

   The requirement's "correct geometry, material and wear" cannot be seen, let alone passed.

3. **A hard-edged light patch on the facade (frame and before).** The lamp-lit area of brick above the shopfronts stops along a sharp straight diagonal, from about (905, 295) to (1185, 180), with dark wall above it. Real lamp light fades out softly, so this reads as a light cone being cut off or a stray shadow edge. The lit brick is also a strong crimson rather than the orange-brown of brick under a street lamp.

4. **The lamp posts glow along their full length (frame and before).** The posts at about 895 and 285 across show as bright orange lines from the head down to the bottom of the frame. A shaded lamp head would not light its own post like that. The short T-shaped post at about 340 across sits unlit and looks unfinished.

5. **The lit windows look like flat glowing cards (frame and before).** The first-floor sash at about 1030 across and the window at about 775 across are lit evenly from edge to edge. There is no curtain, no room behind and no depth in the reveal.

6. **The background town is just floating windows (frame and before).** At about 355 to 540 across, 440 to 460 down, and in the tree mass on the far left, small lit windows hang in black with no house walls or roofs around them. The tree line is one smooth, lumpy silhouette.

7. **Against the Hook sheet (frame and before):**
   - The chimney stacks are plain silhouettes with no clay pots.
   - There are no arched brick heads over the windows, and no gutters or downpipes.
   - The "MICKEY'S" sign is thin dull red lettering on a black fascia, barely legible. The Hook sheet has gilt lettering on a dark blue-grey fascia with a moulded cornice.
   - The fine period details that are right, the TV aerials on the stacks, are too small to carry the frame.

8. **Not shown in this crop (frame and before):** the glass reflecting the street and the shop window's CC0 goods. The shop window is cut off at the bottom edge, so neither can be confirmed.

Not judged: the two KCD2 frames could not be reached, so nothing here is checked against them.
