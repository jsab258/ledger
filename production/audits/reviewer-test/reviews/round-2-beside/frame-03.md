FAIL

Faults, worst first:

1. frame-03: there is a broken texture on the near building's side wall, top right (about 1330 to 1530 across, 120 to 290 down). It is a hard-edged rectangle of smeared horizontal orange-brown streaks with no brick courses, so it reads as a stretched or misapplied texture. It is not in frame-03-before, so this change introduced it.

2. frame-03 against frame-03-before: apart from fault 1 the two pictures look identical. The change made nothing visibly better.

3. frame-03 and the before picture: the far hillside (left half, about 0 to 680 across, 280 to 410 down) looks like a placeholder backdrop. The trees are lumpy grey blobs and the houses are plain boxes with flat painted windows. The Hook sheet has a misty, layered hillside of real-looking houses and terraces.

4. frame-03 and the before picture: the near block's brickwork (right half) is clean and evenly repeated, with a repeat you can see on the side wall. There is no staining, patching, soot or streaking under the sills, gutter or downpipe. The Hook sheet's wall is patched, stained and uneven. The frontage does not yet show 1990 wear.

5. frame-03 and the before picture: there is a flat, featureless black shape left of centre (about 230 to 320 across, 420 to 450 down), under the roofs beside the lamp post. At this size it looks untextured. Someone should check it in the full-size frame.

6. frame-03 and the before picture: the near block's upper windows sit almost flush, with shallow reveals and plain flat heads. The Hook sheet has deep reveals and arched brick heads, which give the wall its shadow and depth.

7. frame-03 and the before picture: the chimney stacks seem to carry one small cap each. The Hook sheet's stacks carry clusters of terracotta pots. This is a narrow point.

8. frame-03 and the before picture: the smaller shop signs down the parade (about 590 to 790 across, 455 to 485 down) are unreadable smudges. "FRESH FISH" is the only one you can read. This is a narrow point.

Fault 1 alone fails this step: it is a glitch, and it is the only visible change since the before picture. The two KCD2 frames could not be reached, so I did not check the picture against them.
