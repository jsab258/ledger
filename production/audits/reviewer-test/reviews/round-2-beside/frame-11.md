# Round two, beside the clean original: frame-11

Planted: nothing (a clean copy). The reviewer was not told. Its reply, word for word:

---

FAIL

The faults, worst first:

1. **Nothing has changed.** frame-11 and frame-11-before look the same everywhere: the same camera, sky, hillside, terrace, signs, satellite dish, lamps and Mickey's fascia. I could not compare them pixel by pixel because I was told to run nothing, but I found no difference by eye. The gate needs a visible change since he last looked, so this fails on its own.

2. **The hillside looks like a placeholder** (frame-11, left half of the background). The trees are rounded, low-detail lumps that look cut out and repeated, and some are lollipop shapes. The houses on the hill are plain white and cream boxes with no depth. The Hook sheet's hillside is real-looking terraces, walls and roofs fading into mist. This one would not pass in a 2026 game.

3. **There is a black block between the left-hand buildings** (frame-11, around 230 to 320 across and 420 to 450 down). It is a flat black rectangle with no surface detail and reads as an untextured or broken piece of geometry.

4. **The brick is too clean and too even** (frame-11: the corner building's end wall and front, and the whole terrace). It is one even orange-red, with no soot, staining, patching, rubbed or mismatched bricks, and it looks like a repeated tile. The Hook sheet's brick varies in colour, has darker bricks mixed in, a patched render scar and weathering under the eaves.

5. **The terrace looks machine-repeated** (frame-11, the right-hand row going into the distance). The roofline is one perfectly straight line. The chimneys are identical small stacks at even spacing, and the first-floor windows are copies of each other. The Hook sheet's row has big chimney stacks of different heights, each with several pots, a roof that steps and sags, and a stone-faced house breaking the run.

6. **The windows are flat and plain** (frame-11, upper floors, both buildings). They are plain rectangles with no brick arch over the head and no visible depth into the wall. Several are modern-looking frames with blinds. The Hook sheet has tall sash windows set back into the wall under brick arches, with stone sills. The side window of the corner building shows a flat yellow fill behind the glass.

7. **The white upper front looks blank** (frame-11, the house above the "FRESH FISH" sign's left neighbour, about 710 to 800 across). It is a bare white flat surface with no texture, dirt or edge detail.

8. **The roofs and satellite dish lack detail** (frame-11). The slate barely shows and the roofs read as dark bands. The dish on the corner wall is a large flat white disc with no detail.

9. **Distant shop signs are smeared** (frame-11, the row of fascias left of "FRESH FISH"). Apart from "FRESH FISH" and "MICKEY'S" they are blurred and unreadable, and they look soft rather than far away.

10. **The view is a mirror image of the Hook sheet** (frame-11 compared with the Hook sheet). The terrace with Mickey's is on the right and the hill on the left; in the Hook sheet the terrace is on the left and the hill on the right. This may be intended, but it should be confirmed.

11. **The mood is drier and flatter than the Hook sheet's** (frame-11). The light is an even grey with no damp sheen on walls or roofs, and the haze gives little depth between the street and the hill. This is a narrow point that later lighting work could fix.

What I could not judge: the two KCD2 frames could not be reached, so I judged against the top part of the Hook sheet only. Only the upper part of the frame was given, so the street level, the shop window and the glass reflections could not be judged beyond the top edge of Mickey's window, which shows ceiling strip lights and little reflection.
