# Round two, beside the clean original: frame-07

Planted: debug text: the engine's red LIGHTING NEEDS TO BE REBUILT message top left. The reviewer was not told. Its reply, word for word:

---

FAIL

Faults, worst first:

1. frame-07 only: there is red engine warning text in the top-left corner. It reads "LIGHTING NEEDS TO BE REBUILT (37 unbuilt object(s))" and "'DisableAllScreenMessages' to suppress". It is not in the before picture. It is a visible glitch on its own, and it also says the lighting in this frame is unbuilt for 37 objects, so the light shown is not finished light. As far as I know this message does not appear in a shipping build, so the frame may not come from the packaged release build. That is worth checking.

2. frame-07 against frame-07-before: apart from that warning, I can see no difference between the two pictures. The camera, lamps, lit windows, colours and frontage all look the same. In this view the change has added a fault and shown nothing gained.

3. Both pictures: about half the frame is crushed to black with nothing readable in it. On the left, the hill town and trees are one black mass with lit squares floating in it. You cannot see any house shapes or roofs. On the right, the corner building (roughly the last fifth of the frame) is pure black. In the Hook sheet the layered hillside town is a main part of this view, and here it is lost.

4. Both pictures: the frontage does not read against the Hook sheet. The Hook's arched brick window heads, downpipes, brick courses and slate roof slopes cannot be seen. The roofs are black against a black sky. The Mickey's fascia shows dim reddish letters on what looks like the same dark wall as above it. It does not read as the Hook's painted blue-grey board with gold letters.

5. Both pictures: every lamp post glows orange from top to bottom, as if it were lit from inside. This shows on the three on the left and on the one in front of Mickey's. A post standing under its own lantern should be mostly dark.

6. Both pictures: the upper floor around Mickey's is washed a saturated crimson. The lit area has a hard, straight diagonal edge rising from near the lamp towards the upper right. It looks like a light's cut-off, not a shadow from anything in view. The colour also does not match 1990 sodium street light, which would turn the brick dull orange-brown rather than deep red.

7. Both pictures: the sky is a flat brown-black with no cloud and no orange glow from the town's lamps on low cloud. This is a narrow point.

8. Both pictures: the shop window shows only the lit interior, with no reflection of the street visible in the glass. The window is cut off at the bottom edge of the supplied part, so the glass, goods and street level could not be judged. This is a narrow point.

What I judged against: only the upper part of the Hook sheet. The two KCD2 frames could not be reached, so nothing was judged against them.
