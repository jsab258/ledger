# Round two, beside the clean original: frame-09

Planted: hole in a wall: a ragged hole through the corner wall with sky behind it. The reviewer was not told. Its reply, word for word:

---

FAIL

The faults, worst first:

1. **A placeholder shape on the side wall (seen in frame-09).** There is a flat, pale grey eight-sided shape on the side wall, right of the corner and about two-thirds of the way down. In the 508 x 900 frame it sits at roughly 390 to 455 across and 560 to 622 down. It has no texture or shading and isn't lit like the brick around it. It looks like a placeholder or an object with a missing material. It isn't in frame-09-before, so this change put it there. On its own it fails "no visible fault or placeholder".

2. **Nothing has improved in this view (frame-09 against frame-09-before).** Apart from that shape, the two pictures are the same. The only visible change since the last good picture is a new fault.

3. **The side wall's wear looks like a repeated texture (seen in frame-09, already in frame-09-before).** The side wall is one big blank brick surface. Its only wear is dark vertical streaks that repeat at even spacing across both faces of the corner, like a tiled dirt texture rather than weathering. There are no downpipes, patched or repointed bricks, old fixings or render patches. The brick colour is even. The Hook sheet's end wall has a downpipe, a plaster patch and mixed burnt and pale bricks.

4. **The upper window doesn't match the Hook sheet (seen in frame-09, already in frame-09-before).** It is small and set high under the eaves, with a thin flat white frame, venetian blinds and only a sill. There is no brick arch or lintel above it. The Hook sheet's upper floor has tall sash windows under curved brick arches.

5. **The street doesn't show in the shop window glass (seen in frame-09, already in frame-09-before).** The interior shows through clearly, but at this size no reflection of the street can be seen. The requirement asks for real glass reflecting the street. This needs checking in the full-size frame.

6. **The door surround is flat (seen in frame-09, already in frame-09-before).** The white surround is a flat strip with no depth, moulding or lintel, set straight into the brick.

Points 3 to 6 alone would be narrow points for later steps, but point 1 is not. I couldn't reach the two KCD2 frames, so I judged against the upper Hook sheet only.
