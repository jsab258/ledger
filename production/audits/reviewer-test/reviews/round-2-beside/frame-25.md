# Round two, beside the clean original: frame-25

Planted: floating object: a copy of the yellow box files hangs in mid-air in front of the blind, nothing under it. The reviewer was not told. Its reply, word for word:

---

FAIL

Faults, worst first:

1. New frame (frame 25): a cream paper-ream box floats in mid-air in front of the blind, upper left of the main window, about a third of the way down. It looks like a copy of the boxes on the sill at the bottom right. The before picture doesn't have it, and it is the only difference between the two pictures. So this change adds a plain glitch and makes the view worse.

2. Both pictures: a ragged, torn-looking pale band runs across the two upper glass panes. It reads as a rendering fault, not as a reflection of anything in the street.

3. Both pictures: the glass shows almost none of the street. In the Hook sheet the shop glass under grey overcast daylight is pale and full of sky and street, with the room dimmer behind it. Here the window reads like open air onto a warm, lamp-lit room. The "by day" view doesn't look like day, and "real glass reflecting the street" isn't met.

4. Both pictures: the dark pilaster just left of the window has a coarse black speckle like foam or tarmac, not painted timber, render or stone. It looks like a wrong or placeholder material.

5. Both pictures: the office is too tidy and bare for a 1990 cab office. The counter top is clean and has no scuffs, and the floor and walls show little wear. There is little clutter. At full size the radio set and telephone are tiny and hard to make out. An ashtray is barely visible at all.

6. Both pictures: the door at the far left edge is a flat black slab with almost no detail.

I checked against the Hook sheet only. The two KCD2 frames couldn't be reached, so nothing here was checked against them.
