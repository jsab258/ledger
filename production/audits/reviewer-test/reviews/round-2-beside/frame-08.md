# Round two, beside the clean original: frame-08

Planted: nothing (a clean copy). The reviewer was not told. Its reply, word for word:

---

FAIL

Faults, worst first:

1. Nothing has changed since the last good picture (new frame against the before picture). I compared every part by eye and found no difference: sky, trees, lamp positions, the red light on the wall, the lit windows, the MICKEY'S sign and the shop window all match. The gate needs a change since he last looked, and this frame shows none. I was told to run nothing, so I could not do a pixel diff. Any change is too small to see at full size.

2. The frame is far too dark to judge or to look good (new frame). About the left half is near-black: the houses and street on the left can't be read, and the right edge past the corner building (from about x 1280 onwards) fades into a black void. On the main terrace the roofs, slates, gutters and eaves are lost, so it does not show the readable brick-and-slate street on the Hook sheet. The sky is flat dark brown, with no gradient, no cloud and no orange glow from the town's lights.

3. There is a lighting fault on the frontage above Mickey's (new frame). A strong, flat red light covers the brick from about x 920 to 1180, reaching as high as the first-floor windows. It does not match the orange of the nearby streetlamp. Its upper edge is a hard diagonal running from the lamp head up to the right, and no object explains that edge. It looks like the cut-off of a light cone, not a real shadow.

4. The frontage geometry and detail don't match the Hook sheet (new frame against the Hook sheet):
   - The upper windows are flat-topped rectangles, not sashes under arched brick heads.
   - The chimney stacks are plain blocks with no pots.
   - The eaves have no brick detail, and there are no gutters or downpipes.
   - The shopfront has no cornice, pilasters or brackets.
   - The MICKEY'S lettering is dull red and hard to read, not gilt letters on a dark blue-grey fascia.

5. The glass doesn't work (new frame). Mickey's window shows no reflection of the street, the lamp or the terrace opposite; it reads as an open, lit hole with a ceiling tube and blinds behind. The lit upper windows (about x 1030, 260 and x 775, 360) are flat, even panes with no room, curtain or reflection, like glowing cards.

6. There are smaller problems on the left of the new frame:
   - Small lit window squares float inside the tree mass with no building visible around them (about x 155, 325; 230, 360; 495, 350; 600, 385).
   - The left streetlamps (about x 300, 368 and x 590, 425) light nothing around them, so they cast no pool of light on walls or the street.
   - The distant shopfronts at the bottom centre are too dark to read.

Limits of this check: the two KCD2 frames could not be reached, so I judged against the Hook sheet's upper part only. The office interior, the goods in the window and the street level are outside this crop, so I have not judged them.
