# Round two, beside the clean original: frame-14

Planted: a ragged white strip across the top pane over the blind (the night picture had none). The reviewer was not told. Its reply, word for word:

---

FAIL.

Faults, worst first:

1. **A new glitch, and it is the only change.** In frame-14 only, a bright white patch sits in the top-left upper pane. It has a hard straight top edge partway down the pane, a jagged skyline-like lower edge, and it is cut off at both sides of the pane. It is daylight-white in a night scene, and no other pane shows anything like it. It reads as a broken reflection or sky capture. The before picture has plain beige there and is otherwise identical, so the change made the picture worse.
2. **The glass does not read as glass.** Apart from that patch, nothing reflects from the window: no street lamps, no opposite frontage, no sheen, smears or dirt. The window looks like an open hole, which misses "real glass reflecting the street". Seen in frame-14 and the before picture.
3. **The frontage to the left of the window looks unfinished.** The surrounds are flat, untextured orange and red planes with no shading or wear. The dark upright strip has a coarse bark-like or rock-like texture rather than painted timber or brick, and the brick above is almost black. This is far from the Hook sheet's painted shopfront and real brick. Seen in frame-14 and the before picture.
4. **The window joinery is plain and computer-made.** The mullions and transoms are bare dark boxes with no mouldings, putty, chipped paint or contact shadow. Seen in frame-14 and the before picture.
5. **There is a featureless void through the back door.** The open door shows a flat grey-white nothing beyond it. Seen in frame-14 and the before picture.
6. **The office lacks a 1990 cab office's wear and clutter.** The walls are only lightly mottled, and the floor and counter are clean. No ashtray can be seen, and no object reads clearly as a cab radio set. The light is even, warm and flat, so the room looks like a showroom rather than the photographic look of the Hook sheet. Seen in frame-14 and the before picture.
7. **The boxes in the window look like stand-ins.** The stacked yellow boxes at bottom right are flat-lit with only faint labels, and the grey cylinder beside them is a bare shape. Seen in frame-14 and the before picture.
8. **A minor point on the blind:** the slats look like a flat texture with a visible seam where two sections meet. Seen in frame-14 and the before picture.

The two KCD2 frames could not be reached, so this view was not checked against them.
