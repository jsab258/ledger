# Round two, beside the clean original: frame-17

Planted: low-resolution texture: the fish shop's upper brick is blocky and blurred either side of its window. The reviewer was not told. Its reply, word for word:

---

FAIL.

1. In frame-17, the brick on the fishmonger's upper floor has broken up into coarse, blurry squares. From the eaves down to the fascia there are no brick courses or mortar lines, just a red patchwork, and the dentil band under the gutter is affected too. Only a rectangle around the window (the head above it and the panel under the sill) still shows real bricks, so the edge of that patch shows as a visible seam. In the before picture the same wall is crisp, weathered brick. This is the only difference between the two pictures, so the visible change is a regression. It looks like a texture that failed to load or a wrong low-resolution map.

2. In frame-17, and the same in the before picture, the telephone kiosk is a plain light-grey box. The side facing the road is one blank panel with no glazing, frame divisions, door or street grime. Only the small "Telephone" header and the dark open side say what it is. It looks like a placeholder, not a 1990 kiosk.

3. In frame-17, and the same in the before picture, the low brick block between the kiosk and the fishmonger's stall riser has no clear purpose. It looks like a leftover blocking shape rather than a bin, planter or pier. This is a narrow point.

4. In frame-17, and the same in the before picture, compared with the Hook sheet: the chimney stacks have no pots, but every stack on the Hook sheet has them. The roof is a smooth grey band with no visible slate courses or weathering, while the Hook sheet's slates are clearly coursed and worn. These are narrow points.

How this was judged: the KCD2 frames could not be reached, so the frame was checked against the upper part of the Hook sheet only. Only the reduced 625 x 900 copy was available, not the full-size frame. Fault 1 is plain even at this size.
