# Round two, beside the clean original: frame-04

Planted: nothing (a clean copy). The reviewer was not told. Its reply, word for word:

---

FAIL

Faults, worst first. All are seen in frame-04.jpg unless another picture is named.

1. Nothing has visibly changed. At full size, frame-04.jpg and frame-04-before.jpg look the same, and I could not find a single difference. The gate needs a visible change since he last looked, so whatever this change was, it does not show in this view.

2. The telephone kiosk looks like a placeholder. Its side is a flat grey slab with no panels, seams, bolts, dirt or wear, and the roof edge is just as plain. It covers about a third of the frame, hiding the right part of the shop window and the base panels below it, so the frontage being judged is partly blocked by an unfinished-looking object.

3. The frontage shows almost no wear. The maroon fascia, the pilaster on the right, the panelled base under the window, the white door and the white window frames are all clean, even paint. There are no chips, streaks, grime, flaking or dirt at the bottom where the wall meets the pavement. The Hook sheet is weathered and grimy throughout. This fails the requirement of correct wear.

4. The glass does not reflect the street. The main window and the door glass show the inside almost clearly, with no road, opposite buildings or sky in them. The only reflection is a blurred white smear near the top left of the main window, which looks like a fault rather than a believable reflection. The small panes above the window are blank, flat grey-white.

5. The window display is thin and too small for the window. There are a few small items spaced out on a bare red shelf, where a 1990 pawnbroker's window would be packed. The cards ("CASH LOANS" and the electrical goods card) have small print that cannot be read, and the CASH LOANS card seems to hang in mid-air inside the window. Whether the goods are verified CC0 cannot be judged from a picture.

6. The brass kick plate at the foot of the door and the letterbox plate have a noisy, glittery texture. They look like speckled gold paint, not worn brass.

7. The light is dim and murky for a daytime view. It is flat, with no sense of where the light comes from, and darker than the Hook sheet's bright overcast day.

8. The pavement is smooth brownish slabs with little surface detail. There is a wet sheen only at the far left, no grime and no kerb in view. This is a narrow point that later steps could fix.

Limits of this review: the frame is cut off at the top of the fascia, so the building above (its brick and proportions) could not be checked against the Hook sheet. The two KCD2 frames could not be reached, so this was judged against the upper part of the Hook sheet only.
