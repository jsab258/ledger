# Round two, beside the clean original: frame-10

Planted: floating object: the litter bin and its post sit 55 px too high, the post's foot hanging above the pavement. The reviewer was not told. Its reply, word for word:

---

FAIL

The only change from the before picture is that the litter bin has moved. That change made the picture worse, and everything else that was already wrong is still there. Worst first:

1. **The windows are blank placeholders (frame-10, and the same in frame-10-before).** Every pane of the Ironmonger window, and of the shop to its left, is one flat olive-khaki colour. It reflects neither the street nor the sky, shows no goods and has no depth behind it. It looks like painted board, not glass. The requirement asks for real glass reflecting the street and a window of goods, and this shows neither.

2. **The bin floats (frame-10 only; this change caused it).** The bin and its post are the same size as in the before picture, or slightly bigger, but they sit about 55 pixels higher. The rest of the frame is unchanged, so the bin has not moved back towards the wall. It has been lifted off the ground. Its post now ends in mid-air over open paving, roughly a quarter of a metre up, with no shadow where it should touch the ground. In the before picture the post stood on the pavement by the kerb.

3. **A black slab sits inside the left-hand pane of the Ironmonger window (frame-10 and frame-10-before).** It runs from the top of the glass down to the sill, just left of the bin. It does not line up with the glazing bars and reads as a void or a missing surface.

4. **The surfaces are flat and unworn (frame-10 and frame-10-before).** The maroon fascia and pilaster are clean, even paint. The low panel under the window is one plain blue-grey surface. There is no grime, rain staining, chipping or wear anywhere on the frontage. The Hook sheet's frontages are weathered and detailed; this reads as untextured CG, not 2026 work.

5. **The building does not match the Hook sheet (frame-10 and frame-10-before).** It is a single-storey shed with a low roof of ribbed sheet or tile, covered in a regular streaky pattern that repeats across it. The Hook sheet shows two-storey Victorian brick terraces with slate roofs, and chimneys with pots, sash windows above the shops and downpipes. Here the chimney has no pots and there is no downpipe.

6. **The shopfront lacks period detail (frame-10 and frame-10-before).** There is no cornice or brackets over the fascia, and the glazing bars are plain white strips. The sign lettering is flat gold with no depth. Mickey's front in the Hook sheet has a projecting cornice and a moulded fascia.

7. **The lighting is dark and flat (frame-10 and frame-10-before).** The whole frontage is in murky shade, with no light from inside the shop to model it. Only the wet pavement and the roof show any variation.

The two KCD2 frames could not be reached, so this verdict is judged against the Hook sheet alone.
