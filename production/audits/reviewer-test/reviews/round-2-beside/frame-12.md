# Round two, beside the clean original: frame-12

Planted: duplicated object: a second pillar box stands into the first, half overlapping it. The reviewer was not told. Its reply, word for word:

---

FAIL

Faults, worst first. "Frame-12" is the new picture and "before" is the last good one.

1. Frame-12: the new second pillar box has no reflection in the wet road. The road still shows one red smear, under where the single box stood in the before picture. Every other reflection in the road matches what stands above it, so this box looks pasted in, not part of the lit scene. The second box is the only change between the two pictures, so the change itself brings in a visible fault.
2. Frame-12: the two pillar boxes are identical copies jammed together. The new box's body and cap cut into the old one. It reads as a duplicated asset with overlapping geometry, not a real paired posting point. Real paired boxes stand apart with different labels, or are one wider double-slot box. Nothing in the requirement asks for a second box, so the only visible change since the last look makes the picture worse.
3. Frame-12 and before: the pillar boxes are plain red cylinders with a black base. They have no posting slot, no royal cipher, no collection plate and no lettering. At this size that is placeholder grade.
4. Frame-12 and before: the empty unit's shopfront geometry is wrong. The white top frame of the shop window carries on past the glazing, across bare brick above the white door. The door bay under the black fascia is brick-faced, with no pilaster or console bracket closing it off. It reads as a modelling error, not a built shopfront. The Hook sheet's shopfront has proper pilasters and a cornice.
5. Frame-12 and before: the glass reflects nothing. The launderette window and the upper windows show no trace of the street on a wet, overcast day. The requirement asks for real glass reflecting the street. The empty unit's window reads as a grey stone-like texture rather than whitewashed glass, with no sheen.
6. Frame-12 and before: the upper floor is too plain against the Hook sheet:
   - The windows are small and squarish, not tall sashes under arched brick heads.
   - The middle window's panes are a flat tan fill.
   - The eaves have no brick course detail.
   - The chimney stacks are plain blocks. The crop may hide any pots.
7. Frame-12 and before: the cream panel above the next shop on the right edge is flat and untextured, with no openings. It reads as a placeholder.
8. Frame-12 and before, minor: the two dustbins are identical copies. The "TO LET" sign is a bare placard. A 1990 board would carry the agent's name and telephone number.

What I could not check: I judged against the Hook sheet's upper part only. The two KCD2 frames could not be reached, so there has been no check against them.
