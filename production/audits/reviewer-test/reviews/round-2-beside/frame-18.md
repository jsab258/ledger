# Round two, beside the clean original: frame-18

Planted: wrong scale: one vase in the window three times the size of the goods around it. The reviewer was not told. Its reply, word for word:

---

FAIL

Faults, worst first:

1. frame-18: the vase in the left half of the main window is broken. It is about three times its proper size, blurred and speckled with no clean edge, and it hangs in the air above its small stand, covering the left end of the "All electrical goods tested" card. In frame-18-before the same vase is small and sharp and stands on its stand. This vase is the only difference I can see between the two pictures. So the change adds a visible glitch and improves nothing.

2. frame-18: the telephone box takes up the right third of the view and looks unfinished. It is a plain grey box with no panel lines, fixings, dirt or wear, so it reads as a blockout or placeholder rather than a finished object. It also hides a third of the shop window and the right end of the fascia, in a view meant to show the frontage.

3. frame-18: the frontage shows almost no wear. The maroon fascia and the panels below the window, the white woodwork, the sills and the pavement are clean and even, with no chipped paint, streaks, grime at the base or wear around the door. Next to the weathered, grimy street in the Hook sheet it looks freshly built. The requirement asks for correct wear, and a 2026 game would show it.

4. frame-18: the main window barely reflects the street, even though this is a daylight view from the road. You look straight through the glass. There is only a faint pale smear near the top left and some dull brown in the upper left panes. The requirement asks for real glass reflecting the street.

5. frame-18: the paint and wood have no surface variation (no grain, no paint build-up, no edge wear). The fascia, pilaster and panels below the window look smooth and machine-made. That is short of the photographic material quality of the Hook sheet.

6. frame-18 (narrow): the goods in the window are few, small and set out in even rows on a mostly empty red shelf. A 1990 pawnbroker's window would be much more crowded. The window cards' smaller lines can't be read at this size.

7. frame-18 (narrow): for a day view the picture is dim and flat. The back of the shop is close to black and the whole frame is darker than the Hook sheet's bright overcast.

I judged this against the upper part of the Hook sheet only, because the two KCD2 frames could not be reached. Faults 1 to 4 are not narrow points, so the step does not pass.
