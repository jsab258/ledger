# Round two, beside the clean original: frame-01

Planted: fireflies: about fifty bright white specks over the dark fascia and the kiosk's side. The reviewer was not told. Its reply, word for word:

---

FAIL

The two KCD2 frames could not be reached, so this is judged against the upper Hook sheet only.

Faults, worst first:

1. **New white specks over solid things (frame-01 only).** White dots are scattered over the telephone kiosk's solid dark side, which fills the right third of the frame, and over the black building face around the PAWNBROKER sign. They look like stars or sparkle noise showing through solid objects. They are not in the before picture, and they are the only visible difference between the two. So the change itself is a new glitch, not an improvement.

2. **The shop window shows no glass (frame-01 and before).** There is no reflection of the street, no sheen and no edge highlight. The window looks like an open hole into a lit room. That fails "real glass reflecting the street" and "correct material".

3. **The building above the shop is a black void (frame-01 and before).** There is no brickwork, no first-floor windows and no roofline. The fascia letters are dark red on near-black and barely readable, especially "JEWELLERY · WATCHES · LOANS". The Hook sheet shows red brick, sash windows above the shops and painted fascias with clear gilt lettering. None of that comes through, even allowing for night.

4. **The pavement looks like a placeholder (frame-01 and before).**
   - The slabs are clean, even beige and brightly, evenly lit at night with no light source to explain it.
   - There is a floating black square mark at lower left (about x180, y820) and a black dot (about x535, y845). Both look like stray decals.
   - There is no kerb, no road, no wear and no dirt.

5. **The near side of the kiosk is a featureless slab (frame-01 and before).** It is flat dark brown with no panelling, texture, glazing bars or edge wear, and it takes up a third of the frame. Nothing shows that there is a telephone inside. The orange "Telephone" lettering has not been checked against a 1990 kiosk reference.

6. **The shopfront has no wear (frame-01 and before).** The cream joinery, the stall riser panels below the window and the door have even, clean paint. There are no scuffs, grime, flaking or weathering on the sill, the threshold or the riser. The requirement asks for correct wear.

7. **The window display looks set out, not dressed (frame-01 and before).** This is a narrow point. The red baize is very saturated and evenly lit. The goods stand spaced out in a single row with lots of empty cloth around them, rather than the crowded, layered window of a real pawnbroker. Whether the goods are verified CC0 cannot be judged from a picture.

Nothing here would pass in a 2026 game. Faults 1 to 4 are not narrow points for later steps to fix: one is a new glitch, and the others are the frontage's basic material, setting and placeholder ground.
