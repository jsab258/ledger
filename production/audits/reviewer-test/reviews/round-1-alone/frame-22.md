# Round one, alone: frame-22

Planted: z-fighting: jagged pale stripes flicker through one panel of the counter's front. The reviewer was not told. Its reply, word for word:

---

FAIL. Every fault below is in frame-22 (Mickey's office through its window, at night). Worst first:

1. No glass can be seen. The window looks like an empty opening. It shows no reflection of the street, no sheen, no tint and no dirt on any pane. The "0632 960418" and "MINICABS · 24 HOURS" lettering floats in the air with no surface behind it. The requirement asks for real glass reflecting the street, and this frame has none.

2. The frontage on the left looks like a placeholder. The pilaster and fascia are flat, saturated orange and red slabs with no grain, paint edges or wear. Next to them is a dark post with a coarse, noisy texture that looks like cork or rough stone, and it reads as neither painted timber nor brick. None of it matches the Hook sheet, where Mickey's is a dark slate-blue painted shopfront with gold lettering, set in red brick.

3. There is a glitch on the counter front, at the centre of the room. One panel, just right of the FARES card, has a wavy cream zig-zag or zebra pattern. The other panels are plain brown, so it reads as a broken texture or a reflection artefact, not a design.

4. The office has none of the wear and clutter a 1990 cab office needs. The counter is clean, even and unscuffed. The walls and floor are clean apart from light mottling on the right wall. No ashtray can be seen anywhere. The kettle, telephone and what may be the radio set are small, tidy and few, so the room looks sparse and new.

5. The blind is wrong. Its lower slats turn see-through, so the kettle, the lamp and the lamp's shadow show through them. Its right edge also runs past the centre window bar and stops mid-pane instead of at a frame member, so it looks as if it clips through the glazing line.

6. The stacked boxes on the sill at bottom right are plain yellow blocks that glow. They have no print, labels or worn edges, so they read as placeholders.

7. Overall the frame does not reach the 2026 bar. The lighting is even and flat, so the office looks like a lit set rather than a room at night under one strip light. The materials throughout are simple and clean.

The two KCD2 frames could not be reached, so the frame was judged against the upper part of the Hook sheet only.
