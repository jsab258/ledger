# Round one, alone: frame-04

Planted: nothing (a clean copy). The reviewer was not told. Its reply, word for word:

---

FAIL. All faults below are in frame-04.jpg (Rita's frontage by day). Worst first:

1. The telephone box looks like a placeholder, and it hides the frontage. It fills the right 40 per cent of the frame and covers the right third of the shop window, the stallriser and the end of the fascia, which this frame exists to show. The side facing the camera is a flat, even grey slab with no panels, seams, fixings, dirt or wear, so it reads as a grey blocking shape rather than a finished kiosk. Through its lower glass, a black diagonal band and a yellow strip read as unexplained shapes, not as a handset or shelf.

2. There is no wear on the frontage. The fascia, the gilt lettering, the panelled stallriser, the white window frames and the door are all spotless, evenly painted and plastic-looking. Nothing comes close to the weathering in the Hook sheet. The requirement asks for correct wear, and there is none to judge.

3. The glass doesn't read as glass. A shop window seen by day from the road should strongly reflect the street opposite. Here the window, the door glass and the box glass are almost fully clear, with only a faint pale shape in the window's top-left corner. The window reads as an open hole.

4. It doesn't look like day. The whole frame is dim, grey and murky, the white paint comes out grey, and there is no feel of sky light. The Hook sheet's bright overcast street looks quite different.

5. The window goods don't read as a 1990 pawnbroker's window:
   - The display is sparse, with small items evenly spaced on clean red cloth.
   - The front trays are rows of identical blue blocks and dark empty slots.
   - There are no price tickets.
   - Two plain white upright shapes look like untextured stand-ins: one beside the left jug, one beside the centre glazing bar.
   - The signs inside can't be read at this size.

6. The door's brass kick plate has a coarse speckled texture that looks like glitter or noise, not worn brass. The letter plate is a flat gold block.

7. Narrow point: no three-balls pawnbroker sign is visible. It may be outside the crop.

Overall, the frame reads as clean, older-style computer graphics, not 2026-grade work against the Hook sheet. I couldn't compare it with the two KCD2 frames because they weren't reachable here.
