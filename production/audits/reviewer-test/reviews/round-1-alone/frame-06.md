# Round one, alone: frame-06

Planted: missing window pane: the lower right pane of the middle first-floor window is a black hole. The reviewer was not told. Its reply, word for word:

---

FAIL. All faults below are in frame-06, worst first:

1. Broken shopfront geometry on the empty unit. Above the white door and the black door, to the right of the shop window, bare wall brick shows inside the black shopfront surround where the shopfront should be. The white transom frame over the window runs on past the window and across that brick, with no glass in it. Either the shopfront model doesn't fill its opening or the frame sticks out into the wall. It is plainly visible.

2. There is no believable glass on the row.
   - The empty unit's whitewashed window looks like a grey speckled stone or concrete wall behind the frame, not whiting smeared on glass. The same texture repeats in all three panes, with no glass sheen and no reflection.
   - The three transom panes above it are flat, pure black, and read as holes.
   - The launderette window shows its interior with almost no reflection of the street, while the wet road mirrors everything. The lighting on the glass and on the road doesn't agree.

3. The pillar box looks like a placeholder. It is a plain, bright red cylinder with a cap and black base: no posting slot, no collection plate, no royal cipher, no chipped paint or grime.

4. The empty unit lacks the wear a vacant 1990 shop would have.
   - Fascia, pilasters and stall riser are flat, untextured matte black, with no flaking paint and no ghost of the old shop name.
   - The white frames are spotless.
   - The posters are crisp flat decals with no paper texture, creases, curling or fading.
   - The two dustbins are identical, spotless copies, each standing right in front of a door and blocking it.

5. The street lamp is generic: a thin black pole with a plain slab head, not a recognisable British lantern of 1990. Its arm points sideways along the pavement over the launderette instead of out over the road.

6. Below the Hook sheet's level, so not 2026-grade.
   - The brick has no arched or gauged window heads and no decorative brick course under the eaves.
   - The chimney stacks are plain boxes.
   - The roof is a flat grey strip with no slate detail.
   - The upper windows are small and squat beside the Hook sheet's tall sashes.
   - Overall it reads as simplified computer graphics rather than the photographic street the sheet shows.

7. Both downpipes (left of the empty unit, and at its right edge) stop dead at the top of the shop fascias, with no shoe, hopper or run to the ground.

8. Narrow points:
   - The next building's upper floor at the right edge is a blank, flat cream panel with no window or texture. Only a strip is in the crop, so this one is tentative.
   - The red poster's top line reads "DON" and looks clipped at the poster's own edge.
   - The "TO LET" board is bare and pristine, with no estate agent's name or phone number as a 1990 board would have.

Judged against the Hook sheet only, since the two KCD2 frames could not be reached. The cut-off launderette sign at the left edge comes from the crop and is not counted.
