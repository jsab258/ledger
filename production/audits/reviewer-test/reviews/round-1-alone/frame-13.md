# Round one, alone: frame-13

Planted: untextured placeholder: the fish shop's upper window is a flat grey panel. The reviewer was not told. Its reply, word for word:

---

FAIL. Every fault below is seen in frame-13.jpg. Each is compared with the upper part of the Hook sheet; the two KCD2 frames could not be reached.

1. The telephone kiosk is a placeholder. It is a flat, untextured grey slab with no glazing, door, handle, framing or weathering. The small black "Telephone" strip is the only detail. It looks unlit and pasted in, and it is too wide and boxy for a 1990 kiosk, at about twice the width of the brick block beside it. It also stands in front of much of the pawnbroker's window.
2. The fishmonger's upper-floor window is a blank grey rectangle. It has no frame, glass, reveal or reflection, only a thin sill. It looks like a missing material.
3. Neither shop window has visible glass. The pawnbroker's and the fishmonger's windows show their interiors with no reflection of the sky or the street opposite and no dirt on the glass. On this overcast, wet day they look like open holes. That breaks the "real glass reflecting the street" bar.
4. A brick block stands on the pavement between the kiosk and the fishmonger, about knee to waist high, with a cap. It serves no clear purpose and looks like a stray or misplaced asset.
5. The frontage has almost no wear. The cream render on the pawnbroker's upper floor is spotless, and both fascias and stallrisers look freshly painted. There is no staining under the sills, no grime at pavement level, and no notices, stickers or hours in the windows. The Hook sheet shows weathered brick, patched render and streaks, so the frontage misses the "material and wear" requirement.
6. The upper floors are thin next to the Hook sheet. Each unit has one small window, with no sash glazing bars and no arched brick heads. There is only one downpipe, at the join. Both façades look like flat textured boxes, not the deep, detailed terrace in the Hook sheet.
7. Narrow points:
   - The chimney stacks sit over the middle of each unit. In the Hook sheet they sit on the party walls.
   - The pawnbroker has no three-ball sign.
   - Neither shop shows a door in this part of the frame; the pawnbroker's may be hidden behind the kiosk.
   - The road is a nearly uniform black gloss with a repeating crack pattern and little variation in its puddles.
