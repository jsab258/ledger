# Round one, alone: frame-19

Planted: texture tiling: the corner wall's lower part is one small stained tile repeated in a grid, seams showing. The reviewer was not told. Its reply, word for word:

---

FAIL. Every fault below is seen in frame-19, the right-hand part of Mickey's frontage. It was judged against the upper part of the Hook sheet. The two KCD2 frames could not be reached, so nothing was judged against them.

Worst first:

1. **Side-wall brick panel.** The lower half of the side wall, from fascia height down to the pavement, is a separate rectangle of darker, differently coloured brick. Its texture repeats so plainly that it shows as vertical stripes. A hard seam runs across the wall at fascia height, where it meets the normal brick above. It reads as a wrong or placeholder material and would not pass in a 2026 game. This alone fails the frame.

2. **Bare brick over the house door.** The maroon door sits inside the shopfront bay, between the shop window and the right pilaster. The gap between the fascia and the door head is filled with bare, stained brick. A real shopfront would have joinery or a fanlight there, so it reads as a hole in the shopfront. The door's white surround is also a flat strip with no depth, reveal or step, and looks pasted on.

3. **No reflection in the shop glass.** The window and the glazed shop door show the lit office inside, with no sign of the overcast sky or the street reflected. It reads as an open hole rather than glass. The brief asks for real glass reflecting the street.

4. **Upper window doesn't match the Hook sheet.** It has a plain square head with no brick arch or lintel, where every upper window on the Hook sheet is a tall sash under a red-brick arch. The fully drawn white blind also makes it read flat.

5. **No drainpipes or gutter.** Neither the front nor the corner has a downpipe, and the gutter at the eaves barely shows. The Hook sheet's black cast-iron downpipes are a strong feature of that street. This is a narrow point.

6. **Shopfront colour is off.** It is a deep navy, against the Hook sheet's muted slate blue-grey. This is a narrow point.

7. **Small finish points.** The right pilaster stops at the pavement with no base block. The door's kick plate reads as a bright gold slab. This is a narrow point.

8. **Window contents unverifiable.** The window holds office furniture (a desk, coloured binders, a beige computer) rather than displayed goods. Whether its contents are CC0 cannot be told from a picture, so that part of the requirement is unverified, not passed.

**What holds up against the Hook sheet:**
- the gold serif fascia lettering
- the stepped brick course under the eaves
- the chimney
- the wet flagstone pavement and double yellow lines
- the flat, overcast light
- the fluorescent tube in the shop window
- the brick on the front wall itself

The frame is a 508 by 900 reduced copy, so fine detail such as mortar, door hardware and the tiles under the window could not be judged.
