# Round one, alone: frame-17

Planted: low-resolution texture: the fish shop's upper brick is blocky and blurred either side of its window. The reviewer was not told. Its reply, word for word:

---

FAIL

Judged against the upper part of the Hook sheet only. The two KCD2 frames could not be reached. The text cut off at the frame's edges ("FRESH F", the "P" of Pawnbroker) comes from the crop and is not counted. All faults below are in frame-17 (the shop row from the road, right part), worst first.

1. The telephone kiosk looks like a placeholder. It is a flat, untextured grey box with a small "Telephone" strip on top: no glass panels, no door, no handset, no wear. It looks nothing like a 1990 British kiosk, and it blocks most of the pawnbroker's window.
2. The fishmonger's upper brick wall is a blurred, blocky texture. At this size you see large squares, not bricks. Below the upper window there is a patch of brick in a different scale and colour, which shows as an obvious seam.
3. The shop windows do not look like glass. Neither the pawnbroker's nor the fishmonger's shows any reflection of the street or sky. You see straight into evenly lit interiors as if there were no glass at all.
4. There is no wear anywhere. The cream render, fascias, stallrisers (the panels below the windows) and window frames are all spotless. There is no staining under the sills, no grime at the pavement line, and no posters, stickers or clutter. Next to the weathered, patched brick of the Hook sheet it looks clean and computer-made, not like a 1990 street.
5. The roofline is too simple. The chimney stacks are bare brick boxes with no chimney pots, while every stack on the Hook sheet has pots. The roof is a flat grey strip with no visible slates, ridge or gutter detail.
6. A free-standing brick pillar stands on the pavement between the kiosk and the fishmonger. It has no clear purpose and looks like a stray block left in the scene. Its brick is a third, different texture.
7. The road has problems. Its crack pattern is one texture visibly repeated, and the surface is close to black. Two flat pale rectangles lie on the carriageway, one at the left near the yellow lines and one in the middle. They look like floating decals or reflection artefacts.
8. The window displays are thin. The pawnbroker's goods are few, small and low in detail. The fishmonger's fish look like one model repeated in a row. The fishmonger's stallriser is a plain flat panel.
9. Minor: the left upper window is filled with a flat yellow surface with no depth. Neither upper window has the deep-set reveal and shadow of the Hook sheet's sashes.
10. Minor: the pawnbroker has no hanging three-ball sign, which a 1990 pawnbroker would be expected to have.

The overcast light matches the Hook sheet, and the fascia lettering is clean. Even so, this would not pass in a 2026 game. Faults 1 to 6 are not narrow points for a later step to deal with.
