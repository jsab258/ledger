# Round one, alone: frame-05

Planted: black square: a flat black block over the houses at the street's far end. The reviewer was not told. Its reply, word for word:

---

FAIL. I compared the frame with the upper part of the Hook sheet only, because the two KCD2 frames could not be reached. Every fault below is in frame-05.jpg (the hook camera by day, upper part), worst first.

1. **Black square, a plain glitch or placeholder.** A solid black square hangs in mid-air at the far end of the street, left of centre, in front of the distant houses (about 450–505 across, 390–445 down). It is the first thing the eye finds and is not a narrow point.

2. **Black buildings with no surface on the left.** On the left-hand terrace, behind the left lamp post, a gable and a flat slab beside it show as pure black (about 170–320 across, 360–450 down). They read as a hole in the street or a missing texture.

3. **Hillside backdrop below 2026 grade.** The hill behind the street looks like a flat painted board: round trees, toy-like houses and a hard cut-out edge, with no depth and no fog. The Hook sheet shows real terraces stepping up the hill, softened by mist. The frame's version reads as fake straight away.

4. **No wear on the frontage or the terrace.** Mickey's corner building and the terrace beyond have clean, even, single-colour brick and clean roofs. There is no soot, no staining under the sills or downpipes, no patched or replaced brick and no weathered slate. The Hook sheet has all of these, and the brief asks for a frontage with correct wear.

5. **Architecture weaker than the Hook sheet.**
   - The chimney stacks are thin, with one or two pots each; the Hook sheet's stacks are large with several pots.
   - The upper windows have no arched brick heads.
   - Mickey's shopfront is one plain blue box with little moulding.
   - The white-painted house in the middle of the terrace (about 712–800 across) is a flat, featureless panel.

6. **Window glass reflecting the street not shown.** In the strip of Mickey's window that is visible, the shop's interior lights show clearly but there is little or no reflection of the street. Most of the window is cut off in this crop, so the requirement can't be judged from this picture.

7. **Framing is mirrored compared with the Hook sheet.** Mickey's is on the right and the street runs off to the left, and the hill sits smaller and lower. If the hook camera is meant to match the Hook sheet's framing, this is a fault; if the mirroring was chosen on purpose, it is not.
