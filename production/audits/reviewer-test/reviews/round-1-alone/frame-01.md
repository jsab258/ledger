# Round one, alone: frame-01

Planted: fireflies: about fifty bright white specks over the dark fascia and the kiosk's side. The reviewer was not told. Its reply, word for word:

---

FAIL. Every fault below is in frame-01 (Rita's frontage at night, from the road), worst first. The two KCD2 frames could not be reached, so the frame was judged only against the upper part of the Hook sheet.

1. White specks are scattered over the whole frame. They sit on the solid face of the phone box, over the dark band above the shop and down the right edge. They are a visible rendering glitch, the first thing the eye catches, and that alone fails the frame.

2. The building above the shopfront can't be seen. Above the window line the frame is pure black: no brick, no upper windows, no fascia board edges, no gutter or roof. The "PAWNBROKER / JEWELLERY · WATCHES · LOANS" lettering is dark red on black, hard to read, and seems to float in empty space. The Hook sheet shows weathered brick, sash windows, chimneys and slate. Here the frontage's geometry, material and wear can't be judged above shop height, and what does show doesn't come close.

3. The telephone box covers about a third of the frame and hides the right side of the frontage. Its front is a flat, plain, unlit dark slab with no frame, panels or glazing. It is empty: no phone, no handset, no interior light. The orange mixed-case "Telephone" header looks like a placeholder. It doesn't read as a 1990 British kiosk, which had glazed panels in a frame and a lit header.

4. The glass shows no reflection. The shop window and the glazed door show no street, no highlight, no smear or dirt. The window reads as an open hole, so "real glass reflecting the street" isn't shown.

5. Pavement:
   - There is a hard black square at lower left (about a sixth of the way in, near the bottom) and a black dot further right. Both look like a stray decal or a missing texture.
   - The flags are clean, uniform beige. There is no kerb, staining, gum, cracking or wet sheen.
   - The pavement is evenly bright with no visible light source, which doesn't match the black night around it.

6. Shopfront joinery is blockout grade:
   - The cream paint is flat and spotless, and the stall riser is plain brown with no chips, grime, sun-fade or wear.
   - There are no consoles, cornice or mouldings that a period shopfront would have.
   - The transom over the main window is opaque cream panels, while the left transom shows dark glass, which is inconsistent.
   - It wouldn't pass in a 2026 game.

7. The window display doesn't read as a pawnbroker's window:
   - The goods are sparse and evenly spaced on a red step, like a catalogue layout, not the crammed window of 1990.
   - The jewellery trays read as empty blue ribbed strips, though the sign promises jewellery and watches.
   - The "CASH LOANS" card seems to hang in mid-air in front of a cabinet.
   - Whether the goods are verified CC0 can't be seen in a picture and wasn't judged.

8. The lighting overall:
   - The blacks are crushed to no detail, and there is no street lamp or other outside light.
   - The scene reads as a lit box in a void, with none of the Hook sheet's street depth, density or weathering.
