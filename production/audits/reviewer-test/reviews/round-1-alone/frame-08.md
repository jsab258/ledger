# Round one, alone: frame-08

Planted: nothing (a clean copy). The reviewer was not told. Its reply, word for word:

---

FAIL. Every fault below is seen in frame-08 (the street at night, upper part), worst first:

1. Most of the frame can't be read. About two-thirds of it is near-black: the sky, every roof, the background hill and the whole right side. None of what defines the Hook sheet comes through: the brick, the slate roofs, the chimney pots and the town on the hillside.
2. A crimson light with no visible source washes the upper wall from just left of the MICKEY'S sign to the right edge of the lit wall (about x 960–1300, y 150–400). It ends in a hard diagonal edge below the eaves. It is brighter far from the street lamp than right beside it, so the lamp can't be its source. The brick reads as saturated crimson, not the Hook's orange-red brick with visible mortar.
3. The street lamp poles glow orange along their whole height while everything around them stays dark. This shows on the centre pole (about x 895) and the three on the left (about x 283, 345 and 597). They look self-lit. The lamp heads throw almost no light onto the walls beside them.
4. The right fifth of the frame (about x 1250–1600) is a black void. A building stands there, but none of its shape or surface can be made out.
5. The sky fills nearly half the frame and is one flat brown-black. It has no clouds, no gradient and none of the orange glow a 1990 British town gives off at night.
6. The background on the left is a black, lumpy mass of trees. Small lit windows float inside it with no houses around them. The Hook's hillside town is missing.
7. The upper-floor windows are flat glowing panels with no net curtains, no depth in the frames and no room behind them. This shows in the tall lit window left of the sign (about x 1015–1048), the small lit one in the centre (about x 775) and the blank grey pane by the lamp (about x 905–925). They read as placeholders.
8. The MICKEY'S sign is dull red letters on a near-black band, unlit and hard to read. Unlike the Hook's shopfront (gold letters on a blue-grey painted board), it has no painted fascia board, no cornice and no pilasters.
9. The visible upper part of the shop glass shows the lit room only. It reflects neither the nearby street lamp nor the street, even though the requirement asks for real glass reflecting the street.
10. The neighbouring shop signs along the bottom centre (about x 615–890) are smeared and can't be read. The frame was scaled down, so this needs checking at full size.
11. The roofs are solid black with no slate or ridge showing. The chimney stacks are bare silhouettes with no pots, where the Hook sheet shows clear terracotta pots. The TV aerials are right for the period and are not a fault.

Not checked: the two KCD2 frames couldn't be reached, and the shop window goods, the counter and the street level are below the part of the frame supplied.
