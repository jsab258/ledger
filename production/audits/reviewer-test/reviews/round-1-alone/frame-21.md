# Round one, alone: frame-21

Planted: test-colour block: the notice board on the right wall is flat magenta (a missing material). The reviewer was not told. Its reply, word for word:

---

FAIL. Every fault below is seen in frame-21 (Mickey's office through its window, by day). Worst first:

1. **Magenta placeholder.** The noticeboard on the right-hand back wall, beside the door, is bright fluorescent magenta. It looks like a missing material and is the most obvious thing in the picture. This alone fails the frame.
2. **Glitch at the top of the window.** A grey band with a torn, ragged pale lower edge runs across the top panes, above the transom bar. It is not a recognisable reflection of the street or the sky, and it reads as broken geometry or a bad reflection.
3. **The glass does not reflect the street, and the light is not daytime.** Apart from that band, the glass is almost completely clear. There is no visible reflection of the opposite terrace, the sky or the pavement. The outside surfaces (pilasters, door, frame) are dark and unlit while the inside glows warm, so the frame reads as dusk or night, not the overcast day of the Hook sheet. The requirement's "real glass reflecting the street" is not met in this view.
4. **The black pilasters have the wrong surface.** The two dark pilasters left of the window have a coarse, pitted texture like volcanic rock or foam, and its scale is wrong. A painted timber or rendered shopfront, as on the Hook sheet's Mickey's, would not look like this. It is the second most eye-catching surface after the magenta board.
5. **Too little wear and clutter for a 1990 cab office.** The room is tidy and sparse:
   - The counter top and panelled front are clean and even, with no scuffs.
   - The walls, floor, window sill and glass are clean, with no grime, smears or marks.
   - The required props are hard to read at this distance. The kettle and telephone can be made out. The radio set is a small object on the counter. No ashtray can be clearly identified.
6. **The blind looks unfinished.** The venetian blind and its bright bottom rail stop at a hard vertical edge a little right of the middle mullion, lined up with neither the window nor the room. An unexplained dark vertical streak and a dark shape sit inside its right end.
7. **Flat, low-detail objects at the edges.**
   - The two pale yellow boxes or books on the sill, bottom right, look flat-shaded and untextured.
   - The black door at bottom left has no detail at all.
   - The window sill is a flat, plain strip of light wood with no wear.

Note: I judged against the upper Hook sheet only. The two KCD2 frames could not be reached, so they are not evidence either way.
