# Round one, alone: frame-03

Planted: stretched texture: the brick on the corner house's side smeared into horizontal streaks. The reviewer was not told. Its reply, word for word:

---

FAIL

All faults are in frame-03, the hook camera by day (upper part). They are listed worst first.

1. **Broken texture on Mickey's upper wall.** On the upper brick wall to the right of the window over the shop, a large patch about the width of a window is covered in smeared horizontal stripes instead of brick. It looks like a stretched or broken texture, or a bad decal. It is plainly visible and would stop the frame on its own.

2. **The hillside town behind looks like a toy.** The houses on the hill are simple flat-fronted boxes, and the trees are soft blob clusters drawn in a cartoon style. They don't match the realistic street in front of them. The Hook sheet's hillside looks like a real misted town. This one would not pass in a 2026 game.

3. **Frontages are too clean for the wear asked for.** The brick on Mickey's terrace and its neighbours is even and fresh. There is no soot, no streaking under the sills or gutters, no patched render, no spalled or replaced bricks and no grime at the joints. The Hook sheet shows all of these: the render patch, darkened brick and uneven pointing. The requirement asks for a frontage with "correct wear", and this one has almost none.

4. **Upper windows show flat colour behind the glass.** Several upper windows along the terrace show a plain tan or beige fill. The clearest are the sash above the lamp post and the window in the white-rendered house. There are no curtains, no depth and no sky reflection. Only the venetian-blind window over Mickey's reads as a real window.

5. **Shop window shows no street reflection.** In the strip of Mickey's shop window that is in frame, the lit interior with its strip lights shows straight through, with no visible reflection of the street. The requirement asks for real glass reflecting the street. Only the top of the window is in this crop, so a lower frame should settle it.

6. **A black block on the left of the street.** Below the gable of the low building, near the left street lamp, is a featureless black block. At this size it reads as untextured or unlit, a placeholder look.

7. **Weather and mood are off.** The street is dry and crisp, while the Hook sheet is damp and misty, with wet slate and haze that softens the far buildings. The frame has haze only on the hillside, and the street itself has none of the damp. This is a lesser point that later lighting steps could deal with.

The two KCD2 frames could not be reached, so this was judged against the Hook sheet's upper part alone.
