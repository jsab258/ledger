# Round one, alone: frame-14

Planted: a ragged white strip across the top pane over the blind (the night picture had none). The reviewer was not told. Its reply, word for word:

---

FAIL. All faults below are in frame-14 (Mickey's office through its window, at night). I compared it with the upper part of the Hook sheet only. The two KCD2 frames could not be reached, so it was not judged against them.

1. There is a glitch in the top-left pane above the window. A hard-edged white rectangle with a jagged brown silhouette sits in that one pane. The other top panes show only the beige interior. It looks like a stray texture or a broken reflection of a daylight sky, which is wrong for a night scene. The requirement says "no glitches".

2. The glass does not reflect the street. No street, lamps, kerb or buildings show in any of the main panes. The window looks like an empty opening, not glass, so the "real glass reflecting the street" requirement is not visible.

3. The frontage beside the window looks unfinished. On the left are flat red and orange strips with no material, edges, mouldings or wear. Next to them is a coarse pier with a noisy texture that looks like bark. None of it matches the Hook sheet's shopfront for Mickey's: painted dark blue-grey timber with gilt lettering, set in weathered brick. It does not show "correct geometry, material and wear".

4. The objects at bottom right look like placeholders. Two pale yellow boxes on the sill have no texture or label, and they glow overexposed. A plain grey cylinder sits next to them.

5. The room is too clean and sparse for a 1990 cab office. The walls are uniform and the counter front has no scuffs. The carpet is plain and there is very little clutter. No ashtray can be made out, and the small grey item on the counter cannot be identified as a radio set. The telephone is a tiny shape on the filing cabinet. Only the kettle is clearly there.

6. Through the open back door there is only a flat, pale, featureless area, like an empty space beyond.

7. The venetian blind looks see-through, with ghostly bright shapes among the slats around the height of the clock. The faint shape near the right of the blind looks like a lamp.

8. The overall look is flat, evenly warm computer lighting on plastic-looking surfaces, and the scene does not read as night. It is far from the photographic standard of the Hook sheet and would not pass in a 2026 game.
