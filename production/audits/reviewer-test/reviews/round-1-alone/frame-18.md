# Round one, alone: frame-18

Planted: wrong scale: one vase in the window three times the size of the goods around it. The reviewer was not told. Its reply, word for word:

---

FAIL. Every fault below is seen in frame-18, Rita's pawnbroker by day.

1. The telephone kiosk on the right looks like a placeholder. Its side is a plain flat grey box with no panels, seams, texture or wear. Its glass shows almost black with a flat yellow band, and the "Telephone" sign is the only detail. It does not read as a real 1990 kiosk, and it hides about a third of the shop window.

2. The large blurred egg or vase shape at left of centre in the window, on a thin stand, is a visible glitch. It is soft and smeary while everything around it is sharp, like a low-resolution texture, a flat cutout or a focus error.

3. There is no wear anywhere. The maroon fascia, the gold lettering, the white door and frames, the stallriser panels and the brass kick plate are all clean, flat, even paint. There is no grime at the base, no chipped paint, no rain streaks and no tired timber. Next to the Hook sheet's heavily weathered brick and dirty shopfronts, this reads as untouched computer graphics. It fails the "material and wear" part of the brief.

4. The glass does not reflect the street. The main window shows the inside with a few white streaky smears at its top left, which look like artefacts rather than reflections. The left pane has only a patch of red brick. No road, opposite buildings or sky can be seen in it.

5. The light does not read as day. The whole frame is dim, grey and flat, the interior and the kiosk glass go to black, and there is no daylight on the fascia or pavement. The Hook sheet's overcast light is soft but bright, with readable detail everywhere.

6. The window goods do not convince at this distance:
   - The pieces are small, sparse and low in detail.
   - The blue ring trays are copies of each other.
   - The signs ("CASH LOANS", "ALL ELECTRICAL GOODS TESTED") are too small to read and look stuck on.
   - There are odd white upright sticks at left and centre.
   - The back shelves read as grey blocks.
   Whether the goods are verified CC0 cannot be judged from the picture.

7. The pavement is clean, smooth, even slabs with a single dark smudge near the door. It has no kerb, dirt, gum, cracks or wet grime of a 1990 high street.

8. These are narrow points:
   - The fascia's top edge is cut off by the framing.
   - Its left end and console cannot be seen.
   - There are no hanging three balls, which a period pawnbroker would usually show.

The KCD2 frames could not be reached, so this judgement is against the upper Hook sheet only. On that alone, this frame is well short of 2026 grade.
