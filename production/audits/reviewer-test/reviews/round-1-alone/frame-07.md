# Round one, alone: frame-07

Planted: debug text: the engine's red LIGHTING NEEDS TO BE REBUILT message top left. The reviewer was not told. Its reply, word for word:

---

FAIL. All faults below are in frame-07 (the night street, upper part), listed worst first.

1. Engine warning text is burned into the picture. Two lines of red text sit in the top-left corner: "LIGHTING NEEDS TO BE REBUILT (37 unbuilt object(s))" and "'DisableAllScreenMessages' to suppress". This is a plain visible fault. It also suggests the frame was not taken from a finished release build.
2. The lighting is unbuilt, so all the light in the frame is a preview. The engine says 37 objects are unlit or wrongly lit. None of the light and shadow can be judged as final.
3. Most of the frame is crushed to black.
   - The left half is a black mass. The trees are a flat cut-out silhouette, and the background houses have disappeared, leaving small lit windows floating in the dark.
   - The right fifth of the frame is a black void. The end of the terrace stops at a hard vertical edge, about four-fifths of the way across.
   - The sky is a flat, even dark brown. It has no depth and none of the orange glow a lit British town gives off at night.
4. There is a wrong-coloured light on the right-hand building. The upper brick face is washed in a saturated red-magenta glow that ends abruptly. It does not match the orange of the street lamp in front, so it reads as a lighting error rather than lamplight. It also looks nothing like the Hook sheet's brick.
5. The building detail the Hook sheet shows is missing.
   - No brick courses, arched window heads or sills can be read.
   - There are no gutters or downpipes, and no slate on the roofs.
   - The chimneys are flat dark shapes without readable pots.
   - The worn patches and repairs on the Hook sheet's walls have no equivalent here.
6. The lit windows look like flat glowing panels, with no curtains, frames, depth or room behind them. This shows in the first-floor window above Mickey's, the small windows down the terrace and the lit windows in the background.
7. The shop glass shows no reflection of the street lamp standing just in front of it. The requirement's "real glass reflecting the street" cannot be seen in this frame.
8. The "MICKEY'S" fascia sign is dark and only just legible. Nothing lights it.

Note: the brief gave only the upper part of the frame, so the frontage's lower half, the goods in the window and the street surface could not be judged. The two KCD2 frames could not be reached, so the judgement is against the upper Hook sheet only.
