# Realism check, beside the same view two days earlier: frame-19

Planted: texture tiling: the corner wall's lower part is one small stained tile repeated in a grid, seams showing. The reviewer was not told. Its reply, word for word:

---

FAIL

Every fault is in frame-19.jpg (the new frame), worst first:

1. **Broken patch on the side wall.** A large rectangle of brick covers almost the whole side wall, from the height of the fascia top down to the pavement. Its bricks are about twice the size of the ones around it, look stretched, and carry a repeating pattern of dark vertical stripes. Its top edge is a hard straight line across the wall. It looks like a weathering layer or a second wall surface at the wrong scale, not like brick. It is not in frame-19-before.jpg, so this change caused it. It is the first thing the eye lands on, and on its own it fails "no visible fault".

2. **The shop window glass does not reflect the street.** The window to the left of the door reads as an open hole onto the interior. There is no reflection of the street, the sky or the opposite side. The requirement asks for real glass reflecting the street. frame-19-before.jpg looks the same, so this has not been fixed.

3. **The upper-floor window is wrong for the period and the Hook sheet.** It is a flat white modern casement with a venetian blind, cut straight into the brick with no arch or lintel above it. The Hook sheet (upper part) has timber sash windows under arched brick heads. frame-19-before.jpg has the same window. This is a narrower point that a later step could deal with.

4. **The side wall is bare.** Apart from the fault in point 1, it is one large flat area of brick with no downpipe, sign or other detail. The Hook sheet's frontages carry black downpipes and similar things. This is a minor, narrow point.

The changes that do work: the scrolled bracket at the top of the pilaster, the brass furniture and kick plate on the red door, and the glazed panel above the shop window with the ceiling light behind it. These read well and are a visible improvement on frame-19-before.jpg. They do not outweigh point 1.
