# Realism check, beside the same view two days earlier: frame-22

Planted: z-fighting: jagged pale stripes flicker through one panel of the counter's front. The reviewer was not told. Its reply, word for word:

---

FAIL

Faults, worst first. All are seen in frame-22.jpg unless noted.

1. There is a texture glitch on the counter front. One panel, left of centre and just left of the middle window bar, is covered in wavy zigzag stripes that no other panel has. It reads as a corrupted or wrongly scaled texture, or a moire pattern. It is not in the before picture, so this change introduced it.

2. The glass does not read as glass. Nothing from the street shows in it: no street lamps, no opposite frontages, no sheen, smears or dirt. The window looks like an empty opening. The requirement asks for real glass reflecting the street. The before picture has the same fault.

3. The room lacks the wear and clutter of a 1990 cab office. The walls are clean, with only a faint even mottling. The counter top and front are unscuffed, the carpet is spotless, and the dressing is sparse and tidy. I could not pick out any ashtray. The radio is a small grey box that only just reads as one. The room looks like a clean CG set, well short of the weathered, photographic quality of the Hook sheet. That is not 2026-grade.

4. The frontage on the left does not match the Hook sheet and looks unfinished. The large orange and red surfaces are flat colour with no texture or wear. The dark pilaster beside the glass has a coarse, exaggerated noise texture that looks like bark or rusted iron rather than painted timber or stone. The window frame reads dark brown and black rather than the Hook sheet's blue-grey painted shopfront. Night lighting may account for some of the colour. The before picture shows the same.

5. The venetian blind now looks like a see-through striped sheet rather than solid slats. Light fittings behind it show through as faint ghost shapes. Its shape is also confusing: the slats on the left slant as if on a side wall, and the blind stops just past the middle window bar. It looks worse than in the before picture.

6. The right-hand upright of the window leans, and is much wider than the straight left upright where it meets the top bar. It reads as skewed geometry. The before picture is the same.

7. The half-open back door shows a flat, featureless pale void instead of a room.

8. The two boxes on the sill at bottom right look like plain, over-bright blocks, almost as if they glow. The grey cylinder beside them is equally plain.

The change is visible against the before picture: there is a new kettle and filing cabinet, a white desk lamp, a radio, a red bench and redesigned counter panels. Fault 1 is a regression, and faults 2 to 4 are not narrow points left for later steps. I judged against the upper part of the Hook sheet only, because the two KCD2 frames could not be reached.
