# Realism check, beside the same view two days earlier: frame-25

Planted: floating object: a copy of the yellow box files hangs in mid-air in front of the blind, nothing under it. The reviewer was not told. Its reply, word for word:

---

FAIL. Faults, worst first. "New" means frame-25.jpg (after the change). "Before" means frame-25-before.jpg.

1. A floating object, seen only in New. A copy of the two stacked yellow boxes from the window sill (bottom right) hangs in mid-air above the left end of the counter, in front of the blind. It may be a reflection gone wrong or a stray copy of the object. Either way it is a plain glitch, and Before does not have it, so this change broke something.

2. It does not read as day, and the glass does not reflect the street. Seen in both. The inside of the room is the brightest thing in the picture. The shopfront, its pilaster (the upright beside the window) and the left-hand door are all in deep shade, so the shot looks like dusk or night, not the overcast daylight of the Hook sheet. The glass is almost invisible. All it shows is a faint, unreadable dark smudge just right of the centre glazing bar, near the top. No sky, no buildings opposite and no street show in it, so "real glass reflecting the street" is not met.

3. The strip of glass above the window looks broken. Seen in both. It shows a flat beige-grey band with a wavy dark edge along its top, like a smeared low-resolution reflection or a painted backdrop, not a reflection of anything real.

4. The room is too sparse and clean for a 1990 cab office. Seen in New. The counter is nearly bare, with only a phone, the radio set, a bookings card, the lamp, a small white dish that might be the ashtray, and one parcel. The counter front and top are not scuffed. The walls have an even, repeating mottled stain, not real wear. There are no mugs, no logbooks, no papers, no cigarette packets and no overflowing ashtrays. It does not reach the brief's "wear and clutter".

5. The back doorway opens onto nothing. Seen in both. Through the half-open door at the back is a flat, featureless grey space, which reads as a placeholder.

6. The yellow boxes on the sill look like placeholders. Seen in both. They are plain untextured slabs with a tiny label, not convincing goods.

7. The shopfront surfaces are flat. Seen in both.
   - The door at far left is pure black, with no detail or material.
   - The dark pilaster is covered in a coarse, even speckle. It does not look like the painted timber shopfront in the Hook sheet.
   - The window frame and sill are clean new paint, with no grime, chips or weathering.

8. Narrow point, seen in New: the counter top under the lamp is overexposed to a patch of pure white.

9. Narrow point, seen in New: the right end of the red bench seems to run into the left end of the counter, where they meet below the counter top. It is hard to be sure at this size.

Since Before, the change did improve some things: a panelled counter front, a kettle, a radio set and a working desk lamp. Fault 1 is new, though, and faults 2 to 7 are still there. The KCD2 frames could not be reached, so the picture was judged against the upper part of the Hook sheet only, at full size.
