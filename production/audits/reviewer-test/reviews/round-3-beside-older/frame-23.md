# Realism check, beside the same view two days earlier: frame-23

Planted: nothing (a clean copy). The reviewer was not told. Its reply, word for word:

---

FAIL

1. It does not look like daytime (frame-23; also in the before picture). The office is lit warm like an evening lamp scene. The frontage outside is in deep shade, and the brick at top left has a warm spotlight fade. The Hook sheet has flat, grey, overcast daylight.

2. The glass barely reflects the street (frame-23; unchanged from the before picture). The two big panes look like empty openings. Only a faint diagonal streak near the middle glazing bar and the gold lettering show there is glass at all. The upper panes show a flat beige band with a ragged dark top edge, which looks like a smear rather than the far side of the street.

3. The blind looks worse than before (frame-23 against frame-23-before). It is now an almost black block with a heavier dark patch on its right half. By day, seen from the street, its slats should catch the light, as they did in the before picture. Its bright bottom rail floats across the middle of the window. The blind also runs past the middle glazing bar and stops partway into the next pane, where no frame member meets it.

4. There is too little wear and clutter for a 1990 cab office (frame-23):
   - The counter front and top are clean, with no scuffs, kick marks or worn edges.
   - The counter top is nearly bare.
   - The carpet is flat and spotless.
   - No ashtray can be clearly made out.
   - The blue-grey shop frame and pilasters outside have no chips, dirt or weathering, unlike the worn shopfront in the Hook sheet.

5. The desk lamp burns the BOOKINGS card and the counter around it out to white (frame-23).

6. The half-open back door shows a flat grey blank instead of a room (frame-23; also in the before picture).

7. The two yellow boxes on the inside window sill look like plain blocks with labels that can't be read. They seem out of place in a cab office window (frame-23; also in the before picture).

8. Outside, the black stone pier has an even, speckled, sponge-like texture. Beside it is a flat, untextured dark strip. Both look computer-made (frame-23; also in the before picture).

9. The door at far left is a flat black slab with no detail (frame-23; also in the before picture).
