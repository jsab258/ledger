# Realism check, beside the same view two days earlier: frame-09

Planted: hole in a wall: a ragged hole through the corner wall with sky behind it. The reviewer was not told. Its reply, word for word:

---

FAIL

Faults, worst first (all seen in frame-09.jpg, the new picture):

1. There is a flat, light-grey octagon on the brick side wall, at door height and right of the corner. It has no texture, no shading and hard edges. It reads as a placeholder or a missing material, and on its own it fails the "no visible fault or placeholder" rule. It is not in frame-09-before.jpg, so this change introduced it.
2. The door opening and the first-floor window have no lintels or arches. The brick courses run straight across the top of both openings. The Hook sheet shows a rubbed-brick arch over every window, so the frontage's geometry is wrong here. The same fault is in the before picture.
3. The shop glass beside the door shows no reflection of the street. The shelves, binders, desk and monitor show as clearly as if there were no glass at all, but the requirement asks for real glass reflecting the street.
4. There are no gutters or downpipes on the front or at the corner, where the Hook sheet has a cast-iron downpipe on each bay.
5. The first-floor window is short and squat, with a plain flat frame and a grey concrete-looking sill. The Hook sheet has tall sash windows. This is a narrower point.
6. The tiles under the shop window look like a flat printed pattern, not glazed tiles with depth. This is a narrow point.

Basis: I judged against the upper part of the Hook sheet only. The two KCD2 frames could not be reached, so nothing here was checked against them. The changes since the before picture (the panelled red door with a kick plate and letterbox, and the glass in the top window pane where there used to be a blue panel) do not offset fault 1.
