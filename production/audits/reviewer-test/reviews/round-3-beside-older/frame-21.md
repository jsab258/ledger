# Realism check, beside the same view two days earlier: frame-21

Planted: test-colour block: the notice board on the right wall is flat magenta (a missing material). The reviewer was not told. Its reply, word for word:

---

FAIL

Faults, worst first. All are seen in frame-21. I checked each one against frame-21-before.

1. There's a bright magenta rectangle on the back wall, right of the door. That's a missing-texture placeholder. In the before picture the same spot held a cork noticeboard with notes pinned to it, so this change made it worse.

2. The glass doesn't reflect the street, and the view doesn't look like daytime. The room shows through as if the pane were open air. The only trace of glass is a faint dark smudge behind the "0632" and below it, which looks like a stain more than a reflection. The street side (brick, pilaster, door) is dim like dusk, while the warm interior is the brightest thing in the frame. On an overcast day like the Hook sheet's, the pane would mirror the bright street and partly hide the room. The before picture has the same fault.

3. The upper panes show a ragged, wavy grey band across the ceiling, with torn-looking edges. It reads as a rendering glitch. The before picture has it too.

4. The window display has a stack of plain yellow blocks at bottom right with tiny unreadable labels, plus a bare grey bin. They read as stand-in boxes, not real goods. The before picture has them too.

5. There's too little wear and clutter for a 1990 cab office. The walls, carpet and counter front are clean, and the counter is nearly bare. The requirement asks for ashtrays, and none can be clearly made out. The only candidate is a small white speck on the counter. The scuffed counter in the requirement isn't scuffed. Outside, the red and navy paintwork is spotless, unlike the weathered brick and fascia on the Hook sheet. The black pilaster's speckled texture looks like coarse noise, not stone or paint. This change added a kettle, a lit desk lamp and a radio-like set, but the room still looks new and tidy.

6. Smaller points:
   - The desk lamp burns as a pure white blob and blows out the counter top in front of it.
   - The ceiling strip light runs straight through the gold phone number, so the lettering doesn't read as being on the glass.

The KCD2 frames couldn't be reached, so the visuals were judged against the upper Hook sheet only.
