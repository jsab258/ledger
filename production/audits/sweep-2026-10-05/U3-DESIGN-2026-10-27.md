# U3, the reaction ladder: design note before code (27 October 2026)

The sweep's last medium row (port-vs-core.md, U3): the Core decides per person what a witness does about what they got (ignore, notice, investigate, alarm, flee, deliver, fetch the law, intervene); the game's port has only the arrest part (Reaction.h), and at the window the one reaction is a shout from anybody who saw it (Sighting::WitnessShouts, rung 1 or more).

## What the Core says happens at a broken window

- **Severity** (Reaction.Severity) comes from what the witness actually got, not the event: 0.10, plus 0.30 for the act, 0.25 a victim, 0.35 an aftermath, 0.20 a weapon drawn, 0.05 the flight.
- At the window, a full sighting is the act and perhaps the flight: **0.40 to 0.45**. A noise alone is 0.10.
- **Decide** (Reaction.Decide), at those severities:
  - under 0.2: a noise alone, **Notice** (a head turns);
  - 0.40: **Notice**;
  - 0.45 with nerve over 0.4: **Investigate** (walks toward it);
  - 0.45 with nerve 0.4 or less: **Alarm** (a shout, itself a loud sound others hear: LoudnessOf).
- Flee needs severity over 0.5 with nerve under 0.25. Deliver, FetchTheLaw and Intervene need a body or severity 0.75 and over. So **at a window nobody flees, fetches the law or steps in, by the Core's own rules.** The sweep's "nobody flees or fetches the law" is not a gap for this deed.

## What that would change in the game

- **Today:** everyone who saw it shouts (rung 1 or more).
- **Under the ladder:** the one who saw the act and the flight shouts only if their nerve is 0.4 or less. A bolder witness walks toward the window instead; one who saw only the act turns their head.
- **The game cannot show Investigate.** No townsperson walks anywhere in the game (CrimeProbe.cpp has no movement for them; their bodies stand at their spots, Sighting::OnlookersAt).
  - Wired in now, a bold witness would do nothing visible where today they shout: a loss at the game's central moment.
- **Temperament is missing.** Decide needs nerve, dutiful and willingness per person. The Core's Gossiper has Nerve but no Dutiful (Reaction.cs's comment that both come from Gossiper is stale). The game's cast files (quay-cast.json, hook-cast.json) carry neither.
  - A value per named person is a reading of character. It belongs with the cast, from canon: Sheila "guarded but fair", Darren "trades in being useful", Ron.

## His rulings that bear on it (RULINGS.md)

- 1 Oct: Mickey's people (Ron, Sheila) never go to the police about one of their own. FetchTheLaw is unreachable at a window anyway; at a body, the cast's dutiful values must keep it so for them.
- 3 Oct: a threat can stop a report to the police, never the street's talk.
- 12 Oct, combat: deeds are perceived at their own weight; police arrive only for what warrants them. That is consistent with severity driving the ladder.

## Decision (within canon and his rulings; recorded in DECISIONS.md)

1. **Port the rules now, as the sweep's other rows were:**
   - Reaction::Decide, Reaction::Severity and Reaction::LoudnessOf into Reaction.h;
   - golden rows over a grid of severity, nerve, dutiful and willingness with both flags, and over every combination of the six observation slots;
   - CoreTests on the window's own cases (a noise, the act, the act and flight; a timid and a bold witness).
2. **Do not wire it into the window yet.**
   - The shout stays as the visible stand-in for Alarm and Investigate until townspeople can walk, which the street's life will need anyway (phase 2).
   - Then Investigate is the first rung to show: the Core's own note calls it the rung of highest value.
3. **Temperament with the cast, when wired:** nerve and dutiful added to the cast files from canon and the casting sheets, Ron's and Sheila's dutiful kept below the law's threshold by his 1 October ruling. That reaches his page only if a value changes who a character is.

The route's weight stays Medium until wired; with the rules ported and golden, the gap is the game's missing movement, which the phase list already owns.
