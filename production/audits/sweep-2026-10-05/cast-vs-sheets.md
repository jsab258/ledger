# The game's cast against the casting sheets and canon

Date: 5 October 2026 (phase 0, item 0.4's cast check; named in CATALOGUE.md, "Where two versions disagree today", item 3).

**What was compared.** The casting side: canon.md, RULINGS.md ("Faces, hair and voices", "Clothes"), production/casting/CASTING.md, the fourteen principals' sheets, the regulars' sheet (REGULARS.md), the voice keys and the approval files. The game's side: the three talk cards (production/cast/cards, the only cards the talk program reads), the Core's street facts, own lines, walk-round and cast-day code, production/specs (hook-cast, quay-cast, street-people, street-sounds, garments, in-game, voice-engines), the Unreal source (ue-probe/Source/LedgerProbe), content/dialogue and content/brands, the old tier-two cards, the voice clips in game-design/picked-clips, the playtest reports of 3 and 4 October, FINDINGS.md and CLOTHES.md.

**How.** Read-only. Every sheet and card was read in full; the game's files were searched by name and by fact (Mickey's past, years, ages, trades, clothes, voices, retired names); the face and concept pictures were looked at; tools/names-gate.py and tools/approvals.py were run read-only. Unreal and Blender were not run, nothing was built, and the MetaHuman files and the packaged build (outside git) were not opened. Where a claim could not be checked it is written as not checked.

Ratings are for a thirty-minute play of Quay Street, which meets three talkers (Sheila, Ron, Darren), Ada's invitation, DS Ellis's name, a few passers-by and the street's placeholder figures (production/playtest/thirty-minutes-2026-10-03.md:55). High means a friend would see or hear it in the first thirty minutes and it breaks the cast as the sheets draw it. Medium means it shows only in some talk or some places. Low means it sits in records or in parts of the game that a thirty-minute play never reaches.

**Count: 32 disagreements. High 3, medium 8, low 21.**

## What agrees

- **Names.** Sheila Dunn, Ron Kirby, Darren Milner, Geoffrey Agar, Maureen Jensen, Danny Cammack, Alison Sedman, Father Walsh, DS Ellis, June, Ada and Tom Nowak read the same in canon, the sheets, the cards, hook-cast.json (production/specs/hook-cast.json, "name" and "called") and the Core (Empire.cs:136-138). tools/names-gate.py finds no retired name in the played files. The ladder of names for Tom (the new owner, Nowak, Tom, Tommy) and Sheila's exception match canon.md:88-91 (PlayerIdentity.cs:37-42; hook-cast.json:362).
- **Ages and years.** Every age in the sheets works out against its birth year and canon.md:77-87. Sheila's thirty-one years is in her card, in StreetFacts.cs:83-86, and in play ("Thirty-one years", thirty-minutes-2026-10-03.md, part b). Ron kept on in 1989 matches canon.md:77 and StreetFacts.cs:78-80.
- **Roles and Mickey's facts.** Bookkeeper, the door and the rank, the street hustler, the widow with a window, the daughter back for the funeral, the Irish-born priest, the reporter: the game's role words agree (hook-cast.json "called"; CastDay.cs:554-563). Mickey died three weeks before Tom came, left him the office, and Tom missed the funeral (StreetFacts.cs:33-52), as canon and the sheets have it. Mickey's is a minicab office on Quay Street in canon, the specs and the brand bible's kind field.
- **Frozen faces.** The live encounter asks first for S4, P2 and S6 (CrimeProbe.cpp:932), and in-game.json lists MH_LenaS4, MH_RoccoP2 and MH_SamS6, as RULINGS.md:53 freezes them. The built assets themselves: not checked.
- **Voices in the game.** Sheila p267, Ron p227 and Darren p241 are the clips the voice server reads (game-design/picked-clips; clip_for takes the .wav first, so p267 beats the old lena.parler-d.mp3), as RULINGS.md:56 says. Agar p226, Ada p276, Ellis p231, Father Walsh p245 and Maureen p244 match their sheets. Alison, Philip and Keith Garbutt have no cast voice anywhere, as their sheets say; Tom needs none.
- **The people who are silent in the game agree by absence.** Philip Danby and Keith Garbutt are not in the game at all; Alison and June exist only as ids, roles and own street lines. Nothing in the game's biography of Tom (suggested-lines.json) says where he grew up, as canon.md:75-76 requires.
- **Mickey's own people.** Ron and Sheila never go to the police (hook-cast.json "keepsQuiet": "owner"; RULINGS.md:105). Only regulars who knew Mickey describe him, in two approved lines (RULINGS.md:95; StreetFacts.cs:76-80).
- **The regulars' ids.** All 30 sheet ids are in hook-cast.json except the café and the takeaway pair, which REGULARS.md:24-27 hands over; the six folded into the crowd are marked as such.
