## What it shows

- **Seen by Sheila, never trusted.** When she sees the slice's window herself,
  she never comes to trust him and day 7 is over the day-book, whatever else
  he does. Noted on the trust question on his page.
- **Taken in only when Ada is stood up.** Ada, seeing it, goes to the police
  only once he has stood her up for her tea; he is then taken in on day 5 and
  charged. Sheila never goes; nor does anybody for a window nobody saw.
- **Trust earned before an arrest holds.** In those runs Sheila came to trust
  him on day 4, a day before he was taken in, and he still gets the real book
  on day 7. Noted on the trust question too.
- **DS Ellis** comes on day 4 or 5 on the street's talk whenever there is a
  story of his nights about; never when he told Ron no and did nothing seen.
- **His answer** reaches 9 of the 41 by Monday noon in every run.
- **Nothing collided**: every piece ran together, and the arrangement, the
  tea, the police, custody, the damage and the week's end each did what its
  own measurement said.
