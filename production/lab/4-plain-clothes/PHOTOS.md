# Reference photographs: plain knitted jumper and plain trousers, adult man, about 1985 to 1995

Searched 8 October 2026, twice: a first pass (1987 to 1993, Britain, Ireland, Western Europe) and a widened second pass (1985 to 1995, Finland and North America allowed, V-neck allowed). Brief: a man in a plain knitted jumper (crew or round neck best), plain trousers, seen at least from shoulders to thighs, full length best; any build, heavier a bonus; free licences only.

## Result, plainly

Still fewer than two good matches. One period photograph is a real match on cut (photo 01, a heavier man in a knitted crew-neck jumper and grey trousers, full length, 1988), but its jumper is patterned, not plain. One is a weak period alternative (photo 02, V-neck, hips up). One is modern and marked as such (photo 03, plain crew-neck, to mid-thigh, not a heavy man). No period photograph of a man in a plain crew-neck jumper with plain trousers was found.

## What was searched

First pass: Commons keyword searches (English), Commons categories "Men wearing sweaters" (all 235 files), 88 "Men wearing ..." clothing categories, "Sweaters in the United Kingdom", "1980s/1990s fashion", "Men of the United Kingdom / England / Wales / Scotland / Ireland / Netherlands in 1986 to 1992", "People of ... in 1988 to 1992", "1988 to 1992 photographs of England", all 1,557 files of "Photographs by Tyne & Wear Archives & Museums", Anefo and Bundesarchiv searches, the Wellcome catalogue (nothing). Europeana's search service refused the request (HTTP 400), so it was not searched.

Second pass (widened): keyword searches in Finnish, Swedish, Norwegian, German and Dutch (villapaita, tröja, genser, Pullover, trui; nothing useful); the whole "JOKA Press Photo Archive" category (19,767 files, 551 dated 1985 to 1995, 252 events; every non-fashion, non-politics event was reviewed by title and about 128 images by contact sheet); Commons "Men of [country] in 1985 to 1995" categories for France, Germany, Austria, Spain, Belgium, Ireland, Sweden, Norway, Denmark, the Netherlands and Italy (991 files; keyword filter for knitwear found none; the 617 dated ones are mostly politicians and sportsmen and were not reviewed one by one); a short look at modern "Men wearing sweaters" portraits for photo 03.

Not done in the time: Geograph (CC BY-SA) people-in-scene search (no way to filter by clothing, and the earlier dated categories showed almost no people), National Library of Ireland and Swedish museum collections directly (Commons search could not isolate them), Nationaal Archief beyond keyword searches (its 1980s Anefo material is mostly black and white public figures).

## What could change the result

1. Accept photo 01 for cut, build, trousers and collar-under-crew-neck, and take "plain" from photo 03 (the jumper surface), and say so to the reviewer.
2. Widen to 1993 to 1997 or accept a modern CC0 or CC BY photograph of a heavier man in a plain crew-neck jumper and straight trousers, full length (Commons has many; none was found with all of: heavier, plain crew neck, plain trousers, full length, in the time).
3. Jafar or a reviewer supplies two or three photographs of his own.
