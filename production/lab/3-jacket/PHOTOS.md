## Notes

1. IWM photographs (side-01, side-02). The Commons file pages tag them public domain (PD-UKGov, Crown copyright). The IWM's own website offers the same pictures under its non-commercial licence. If the project wants zero doubt, it can rely on the date and photographer's death (1941) and on the Commons tags, or drop these two.
2. side-04 shows two men held in Mountjoy Prison days before they were executed (14 March 1921). It is the clearest adult near-profile pair of lounge jackets I found, but the context is grim. Use only the clothing; it is not among the best three. The NLI record marks the identification as uncertain.
3. side-01 and side-02: the people are recruits of military age; a few look very young. Faces are mostly hidden in side-01. Check ages and the game's no-children rule before using either one, or use side-03 and side-04 instead.
4. True full-length profile photographs of men in lounge jackets are rare in open archives. Only side-02 is close to a true profile. Clean profile pictures I found but rejected: Horace Nicholls' Downing Street series (IWM Q 30736, a man walking in profile, is a morning coat, not a lounge jacket).
5. front-05 needs attribution (CC BY 2.0). The others need no attribution, but I have kept the creator column so the credit is on file.
6. Small originals (front-07, front-08, side-02) are the best open copies I found. They give a usable silhouette, not fabric detail.

Best three for outline comparison: front-04 (Foley, 1905), front-02 (Boundary Commission, 1924) and side-01 (recruits, 1917). side-02 is the true-profile alternative if resolution matters less.

- NLI "Prisoner" (1922): tweed single-breasted jacket worn open, but armed guards in frame and the subject's situation is sensitive.
- NLI Poole Studio portraits (most are seated or half-length), Tyne & Wear Archives mug shots (head and shoulders only, and 1873 or 1914-1916), Wellcome hospital staff photographs (white coats), and Hook family album pictures other than front-05 (seated, half-length or children).

## 1988-1992 (game's own period)

None included. I searched Commons categories and text for British men in suits dated 1988-1992 and found only Dutch Nationaal Archief photographs (CC0, Anefo, 1988-1989) of Neil Kinnock in Amsterdam, which are half-length, and unrelated pictures. No usable full-length photograph with an open licence turned up in the time spent.
