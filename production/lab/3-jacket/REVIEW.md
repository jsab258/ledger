# Fresh review: lounge jacket v9

## 1. A lounge jacket of 1905-1925?

No. It reads as a loose wrap blouse or smock. Biggest differences, worst first:

1. **Sleeves and shoulders.** The sleeves balloon to twice the arm's width, bunch into crumpled folds and gather at the wrist like a peasant sleeve. The sleeve heads drop off a soft shoulder with no shoulder line. The photographs show a firm shoulder and a narrow, straight sleeve to the wrist.
2. **Front and chest.** The fronts fall open in a deep V to mid-chest and cross like a wrap at one crumpled point. Below, they curve away into round lobes open over the crotch. The photographs show a buttoned front (high in 1905-11, at the waist by 1921-24), straight front edges, and lapels covering the upper chest.
3. **Length.** The back stops at the top of the seat, the front above the crotch, and a bunched band across the small of the back gives a blouson look. In the photographs the jacket covers the seat and hangs straight.

## 2. Lapels

Neither lies turned back; neither has a lapel face, roll line or notch.

- Wearer's right (viewer's left): collapsed into a thin rolled ridge with a ragged edge, shoulder to mid-chest.
- Wearer's left: the same ridge, standing up a little in the three-quarter view, twisted into a knot where the fronts cross.

## 3. Collar

No. From the back it is a thick, puffy ring lying on the shoulders, clear of the neck, with no stand and no turn-down. Its front ends crumple into knots on the shoulder tops. In the photographs the collar hugs the neck.

## 4. Simulation faults, apart from the cut

- Open seams: both back armholes gape, showing the body; a gap at the wearer's right armpit; the centre-back seam shows a pale line its full length, apparently open, as do the side seams near the hem.
- Ragged, saw-toothed edges on lapels, collar, cuffs and sleeve seams.
- Crushed, faceted crumpling in the sleeves, worst at the cuffs.
- Stepped, jagged patches of darker shade on chest and sleeves, as if layers pass through each other.
- No long spikes seen.

## 5. Verdict

**Fail.** Not narrow points: shoulders, sleeves, closure, lapels, collar and length are all wrong, and seams are open. A tailor would not recognise this shape as a lounge jacket.
