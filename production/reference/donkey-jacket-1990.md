## What a 1990 donkey jacket is

- D: thick unlined melton wool, black or navy ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donkey_jacket), read 28 Sep 2026; museum records).
- D: back length 81 to 90 cm; hem from crotch to upper thigh; Wikipedia: 3 to 4 inches below the crotch. I: 85 cm for a 40 to 42 inch chest.
- D: straight from the shoulders, no waist shaping or back vent, square front corners.
- D: one yoke across both shoulders, front and back. Ordinary: black leather, PVC or vinyl. Issued: orange (NCB) or yellow (British Coal, water board), employer's name on the back, reflective tape.
- I: yoke depth: front 8 to 10 cm (V&A), 12 to 15 cm (NCB); back to about armpit level, 28 to 32 cm from the collar seam; straight lower edges.
- D: four to five buttons to the neck (Wikipedia); three show below the collar; dark. I: 15 to 17 cm apart, about 2 cm across.
- D: two large flapless patch pockets low on the front. I: 18 to 20 cm tall, bottoms about 8 cm above the hem.
- D: broad stiff pointed collar, turned up against weather; no lapels.
- D: stiff and boxy, creasing, not draping; about 1 kg.
- Varied: navy or black; yoke material and colour; names and tape (issued jackets only); strike slogans and badges; open or buttoned.
- I: by 1989 dockers here wear nylon jackets; the donkey jacket is one garment among many.
