# Work trousers and the flat cap, 1990

Looked at 29 September 2026 by the clothing session, in the browser only; no image saved. **D** plainly visible in the photograph; **I** my inference. The research behind the choice of sources: production/research/clothing-pipeline/TROUSERS-AND-CAP-2026-09-29.md.

## Photographs, as worn

All from Peter Fryer, [Smith's Dock, North Shields](https://sidegallery.co.uk/collection/smiths-dock-north-shields), 1990 to 1991, black and white, © Peter Fryer (Side Gallery/Amber), for looking only. The numbers are the gallery's image files.

1. **149-009** (shipyard workshop, two men). D, the man on the left, from behind, standing at a bench: dark work trousers, wide and straight in the leg, not tapered; heavy cloth; soft horizontal creases behind the knees; the hems bunched in a break on black work boots; a jacket over them to the hip. D, the man on the right: a boiler suit and a flat cap, sitting low on the brow, peak short.
2. **149-006** (a man seated on a bench in a locker room). D: dark trousers, sitting: the cloth pulled across the thighs, folds at the knee and the lap, the legs loose over the shins; black boots.
3. **149-012** (a man seated, arms folded, head and shoulders). D: a checked tweed flat cap: the crown flat and pulled forward over the peak, the peak short, the cap low and close on the head, the side band shallow; no button, no panels.
4. **149-005** (two men in boiler suits by a crane hook). D, the man on the right: a dark flat cap, flat on top, peak level with the crown's front edge.

## What they are, for us

- Trousers, I: dark grey twill (the casting sheet's colour), belt loops and a belt, a flat front, a straight leg a little wide at the hem (54 cm round for Ron), no turn-ups; the waistband under a heavy man's belly; the hem resting on the boot in a half break, creasing above it.
- Cap, D from 149-012 and 149-005: a one-piece flat top pulled forward and fixed to a short stiff peak, so the front of the cap is nearly level; tweed, often checked or flecked; worn low. Not an eight-panel cap with a domed crown (what the 25 September cap looked like).
