# The photographs: the second reference, and why none of them is a file here

THE RULE THEY SERVE, Jafar 2026-09-22: **the Hook sheet governs mood, palette
and composition; the photographs govern what things actually looked like;
where they disagree, the photographs win.**

WHY THERE IS NO IMAGE IN THIS DIRECTORY. The instruction was conditional —
add them "where their licences allow keeping them in `production/reference/`"
— and, checked one by one against the research's own rights records, **not one
of them allows it.** `production/research/atlas-01/RIGHTS.md` says it in its
own words: "Newly inspected period photographs and archive PDFs are LINKED
RATHER THAN REDISTRIBUTED", "Reference access never grants permission to ship
a photo as a texture", and "These files are reference evidence, not a cleared
game-asset library." Every row in `CONTINUATION.md` that cites a photograph
ends "Photographer copyright" or "All rights reserved". The licence allowlist
is law and its process clause is "every asset and generated output carries a
license tag; untagged fails the license gate" — a copied photograph with no
licence would be exactly that, sitting in a production directory.

SO THE SECOND REFERENCE IS THIS PAGE. It is a reference you OPEN rather than
one you `Image.open`. Each row says what that photograph actually establishes,
so an argument about what a thing looked like can be settled by going to the
source instead of by remembering it. Nothing here may become a texture, a
tracing, a crop in a tool, or an input to image generation.

## The non-photographic sources, same rule

`kasbah-character-09.png` and the NELC statement's facade pages (varied roof
heights, infilled openings, sheet-clad repairs) are a MODERN condition record
and the research says plainly that "the 2017 empty/ruinous condition must not
be backdated wholesale". The Hull 1842 map and the Kasbah 2017 map carry Crown
and Ordnance Survey copyright and the research traced no coordinates from
either; neither is a 1990 land-use record. Full locators are in
`production/research/atlas-01/CONTINUATION.md` and `RIGHTS.md`.

## What to do when the sheet and a photograph disagree

Follow the photograph, and write the disagreement down in
`hook-sheet-audit.md` so the next person does not rediscover it. The sheet is
not wrong to look at — it is the mood, the palette and the composition, and
it was approved for those — but it was made by an image model from a prompt
and it invents. The audit lists what it invented.
