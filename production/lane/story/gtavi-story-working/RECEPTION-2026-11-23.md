# GTA VI: reception of the story, update at four days

Gathered 23 November 2026 (release 19 November; reviews out 18 November). Extends RECEPTION.md (21 November); where the two differ, this file wins. Console only, PS5 and Xbox Series. Contains story spoilers (section 3). British spelling.

Method: web search and page fetches only. Fetched pages were summarised by a small model, so wording is paraphrase unless in quotation marks; check before quoting officially. Failed to load: Primetimer, The Scotsman, Dot Esports. Not reached at all: Reddit (no thread read), ResetEra, Metacritic and OpenCritic user pages themselves, IGN, GameSpot, Eurogamer, Guardian, NYT and Polygon full texts. Everything about players is second-hand, via news sites.

Read this carefully: the critic gap to RDR2 is two points (99 against 97). The user gap is 3.4 points the other way (5.6 against 9.0).

Why the user score is low, so it is not read as a verdict on the story:
- Review bombing is widely reported: many zeros, some from accounts that say they have not played, newly made accounts, AI-generated comments, off-topic political or abusive remarks (JournalArta 22 Nov, PlayFront). GTABase says some zeros are a fan war over Ocarina of Time's record.
- Genuine complaints listed: the 30 fps cap with no 60 fps option, bugs, no disc, no car theft in the starting area, "too realistic" (Sims-like eating and sleeping), and story points below.
- PlayFront's own estimate of a filtered score is 6.5 to 7, which it says is its editorial guess, not data.
- Metacritic user scores are open to anyone, so they are not proof the reviewer finished the game. The PlayStation Store rating needs ownership and is 4.84 of 5. The two are far apart.

Praise: the Jason and Lucia relationship (almost universal); Lucia as the stronger lead (Kotaku, Push Square, ScreenHub, Creative Bloq, TheGamer, VGC); grounded, mature tone; satire; the finale.

## 3. Player reception of the story (EARLY)

How much exists: more than on 21 November, but mostly second-hand and mostly about the game in general. No Reddit or ResetEra thread was read. No poll specifically on the story was found.

How many have finished: a few named people have. The main story is 60 to 80 hours (reviewers; Kai Cenat took about 67). Four days is enough only for heavy players and streamers. No source gives a count. Treat every story or ending verdict below as EARLY and from a very small, loud sample.

### 3.1 Metacritic user reviews (see section 1 for the numbers)
- Story complaints named in articles about those reviews: "woke" storylines "with none of the biting, satirical, politically incorrect humor found in the series' older games" (CBR quoting users, 21 Nov); Jason called a "spineless, weak-willed simp and a doormat" (CBR); too much weight on the love story against crime (CBR, JournalArta); slow pace, including fixing up Jason's house in Chapter 1 (GTABase); "very slow narrative pace" (PlayFront). GTABase calls the "woke" complaint the most common. Treat that label with care: much of it sits in the bombed zeros.
- Story praise from users: PlayFront says some praise "relatable characters" and the calm presentation; one commenter says the story "picks up speed" in chapter 2; another would give 7/10 "primarily because of the flat character designs" (comment on PlayFront, not a Metacritic review).
- Nobody read quotes a user on the ending from Metacritic.

### 3.2 Players and streamers who finished
- Sultan Emir (X, about a million views reported): finished in 60 hours, "final review" 3/10; "Rockstar calls it a love story. I call it a babysitting job with a wanted level." Jason is "the least gangster GTA ever made". Said on 22 Nov the post-game "didn't" save it. Sources: https://cosmicbook.news/gta-6-ending and https://tribune.com.pk/story/2641880/gta-6-user-score-falls-to-56-on-metacritic-amid-player-backlash
- Anonymous X posts quoted by Cosmic Book News: "Easily the worst ending in GTA history"; "Rockstar really turned a GTA protagonist into the ultimate simp"; the Criminal Profile "punishes you for playing it like GTA". That article is built on one viral review and a few unnamed posts; it cites no Reddit; no count. Its headline overstates what it shows.
- Streamers (Wisevoter, 21 Nov, https://wisevoter.com/world/2026/11/21/streamers-divided-gta-6-jason-duval): Jynxzi calls Jason "one of the worst video game characters ever created", compares him unfavourably with Arthur Morgan, says GTA V is funnier (BELOW RDR2 on character, from a player not a critic); Asmongold 9/10, calls Jason an overly accommodating boyfriend type; Kai Cenat 8.8/10 after about 67 hours; StableRonaldo criticises Jason's voice acting. IShowSpeed finished it on stream (Cosmic Book News; no verdict reported).
- Gamefile first-impressions (from RECEPTION.md): leads "win him over quickly".
- Early X poster @ItsRealAlec 2/10 after four hours (IBTimes, earlier file; repeated by Geeks and Gamers): only the opening, not a story verdict.

### 3.3 Wider numbers
- PeakMetrics, launch-day social posts with an opinion: 54.5% favourable, 45.5% unfavourable, across X, Reddit, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and Bluesky; not a sample of buyers, not about story (Geeks and Gamers, https://geeksandgamers.com/gta-vi-players-roast-rockstar-as-glowing-critic-reviews-clash-with-fan-backlash/).
- Most player talk is about the 30 fps cap, bugs, crashes and indestructible NPCs, not the story.
- Reddit: one mention only, a user on low-20s frame rates (same article). No story thread read.

### 3.4 What players are saying about the ending (SPOILERS, EARLY, partly unverified)
The endgame turns on a standoff on a bridge, then a rooftop, with a choice between surrender and fighting. Reported outcomes, with the sites disagreeing on the details:
- Power Up Gaming (https://powerupgaming.co.uk/2026/11/21/gta-6-all-endings/): five described endings: No Surrender (both die at a roadblock); Swim for It with both dying; Swim for It with Jason arrested and Lucia free and later visiting him in prison with their son; a bail-out ending (Lucia pays $2.5 million; reported needs $2 million in family savings, a maxed relationship and clean Criminal Profiles), called the best reported outcome; and a "Swamp" ending where Captain Mendez has them killed after a "Take Deal" choice. Three more are unverified. It says fans reacted critically to No Surrender.
- Beebom (https://beebom.com/gta-6-endings/, 20 to 21 Nov): six endings with a different breakdown and internal contradictions (says four, then six; $3M needed against $2.5M bail). Cites no community reaction. Treat as unreliable on details.
- Rockstar is reported (second-hand) to have said there are "a lot more than two" conclusions.
- Net: no study, poll or large thread on whether players like the ending. The negative quotes in 3.2 are about the both-die and chore-based design, from a handful of X posts. A "best ending" gated behind money, relationship and a clean profile may split players who died or were jailed.

Reading for the story team: early players who finished are split by what they wanted. Those who wanted a harder-edged, funnier GTA V-style story call Jason weak and the ending flat; those who wanted a character-led story rate the pair highly. The weight of critic praise and the PlayStation Store rating (4.84 of 5) point the other way from Metacritic users. Nothing yet separates genuine story dislike from review bombing.

## 5. Caveats
- The Metacritic user score is contaminated by review bombing; the PlayStation Store rating is far higher; neither measures the story.
- Numbers disagree between sites on dates, review counts and RDR2's critic count; ranges are given.
- Story and ending talk by players rests on a few X posts and a handful of streamers.
- The Primetimer "Lucia and Jason die" viral post was not reached.
- Nothing here has reached Reddit or ResetEra at source.
