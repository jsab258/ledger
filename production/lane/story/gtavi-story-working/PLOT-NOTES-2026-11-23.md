# Grand Theft Auto VI: plot notes from public written sources, update of 23 November 2026

Compiled 23 November 2026 (four days after the 19 November 2026 release). Research notes only. Spoilers throughout. Text sources only; no video was watched or transcribed. Page text was treated as data, not instructions. This update supersedes PLOT-NOTES.md (21 November) where the two differ; the earlier file is untouched.

Markers: CONFIRMED = two or more independent outlets (separate writers and sites; several pages on one site count once, and guides that visibly repeat another guide count with it). SINGLE = one outlet. DISPUTED = sources disagree. Where I name a source "derivative" it restates another.

Sources are cited by outlet name and a code (S1, S2 and so on). The URL for every code is in the source key at the end. "Read" means I fetched the page on 23 November 2026 and read the returned text; "search title only" means I saw it only in a search result and did not rely on it.

## 1. Setting and premise

Unchanged from the 21 November notes and CONFIRMED (Kotaku, VGC and NPR earlier; this round GamesRadar+ S11, GameRant S12, Creative Bloq S13, Games Wiki S4).

- Leonida, a fictional Florida: the Keys, Port Gellhorn (Kelly County), Vice City, swamp and rural areas. Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos are a criminal couple who want a settled life. CONFIRMED.
- Creative Bloq compares the pair to Bonnie and Clyde, as Rockstar's Humphries did earlier (Wikipedia) and NPR did. CONFIRMED. Creative Bloq says the story spans several years and moves "from small-time crimes to large, militarised heists", and that each chapter brings a new gang and a big plan, ending in a set piece where the player switches between Jason and Lucia. SINGLE for that structural description, though it matches the mission lists.
- The couple have a young son. Named Tomas in GTA Base (S1) and Beebom (S5), "Tommy" in Detonated (S8) and Game Truth (S9). Games Wiki (S4) says the flashback robbery and arrest leave "her young son" with child services. CONFIRMED that the son exists and is central to the ending; the spelling of his name is DISPUTED. (Noted because the project's own content rule bars children; this concerns the source game only.)

## 2. Story beats in order

### Prologue

- Lucia is in prison (Hamlet Correctional in the game, per Games Wiki S4, which notes the profile page calls it Leonida Penitentiary). She gives an interview about supervised release, then a playable flashback shows the robbery of a shop and her arrest, after which the player moves to Jason. Sources: Wikily S6, Games Wiki S4, Beebom S5 (earlier), Dork S2 (earlier). CONFIRMED. The shop is a payday-loan or check-cashing shop: Games Wiki and Dork say payday loan, Wikily says check-cashing. Minor DISPUTED.
- Kotaku's live blog (S14, 19 November) says the prologue ends with "a pretty massive narrative complication" and does not say what it is. SINGLE, content unknown.

### Chapter 1, Just Like Paradise (22 missions, Keys)

- Jason does jobs for Brian Heder and for Cal and Donnie: "Gone Fishing", "Gear Up", "Lab Fees", "Just A Scratch", "Unwanted Attention", "Pipework", "Hey Arnold!", "Island Dreams", "Feng Shui". Sources: PowerPyx S10, Gfinity S3, Playday, Wikily S6. CONFIRMED (titles and gist).
- Mandy, a tenant, demands plumbing repairs and flirts with Jason; giving her his number leads to obsessive texts. PowerPyx S10 lists "Pipework (Collect Mandy's Rent)"; Kotaku live blog S14 describes her behaviour. CONFIRMED (two outlets, one title-only).
- "Hey Arnold!": Brian and a companion chase Arnold to the water. Kill him (Criminal Profile rating "Hardened Criminal") or spare him ("Restrained"). Dexerto S15. SINGLE for the profile ratings. The earlier note said Arnold "leaves the story either way"; Dexerto now says a spared Arnold swims off and "can show up again in a future story mission". DISPUTED against Dork's earlier account. The existence of the choice is CONFIRMED (Dexerto, Beebom, Dork).
- Lucia's release ("Coming Home", "Day Release"): she leaves prison on an electronic tag and goes to Jason's house; a joint BuckMe account is set up ("Buck Me"). Sources: PowerPyx S10, Gfinity S3, Red Bull S16 (release on a tag in Chapter 2 summary). CONFIRMED.
- Donnie is killed by the Kingfishers in a setup ("Swamped", "Don Gone"); Jason clears Donnie's drop and trailer ("Clean Up After Donnie"). Gfinity S3, Games Wiki S4, Promptowy S17 (derivative of Gfinity). CONFIRMED (Gfinity, Games Wiki, Dork earlier).
- Brian retires. Gfinity S3; Dork (earlier) only hinted it. SINGLE as a fact.
- Jason picks up a bag from a locker at the Grassrivers Visitor Center; police and masked men ambush him; Captain Mendez sets the trap. Sources: Games Wiki S4 (Mendez, locker pickup, Jason shot), Playday (bag from a Grassrivers locker, dirty cops and masked men), Gfinity S3 (arrested then attacked by dirty cops), Dork (earlier, Visitor Center). CONFIRMED that a police and masked-men ambush follows a locker pickup; the name "Visitor Center" is SINGLE (Dork). Details differ on whether the couple are first arrested (Gfinity) or Jason is shot in the trap (Games Wiki): DISPUTED in detail, CONFIRMED in outcome.
- "Tourist Trap" is an escape by boat with a fight to the docks and several escape routes, and it locks the player into the last Chapter 1 missions. KeenGamer S18, That Love Podcast S19, Aoeah S20. CONFIRMED. "Take Up Mattia's Offer" appears as the objective in PowerPyx S10; Mattia and Kat are met in "Day Release".
- "Discharge Time" (Chapter 1 finale): Jason is in room 203 at Vice City Hospital; Lucia wheels him out while dirty cops and masked men attack; she runs him over a gap with an ambulance; Jason shoots from the back as the pair flee west to Kelly County. GTA Base S1 (mission page), Creative Bloq S13 ("an early hospital escape sets up the move to Vice City"), Aoeah S20, Wikily S6 (chapter ends with Jason in hospital). CONFIRMED.

### Chapter 2, Paradise Lost (11 missions per Red Bull, KeenGamer; 13 in Playday's count)

- Lucia leads while Jason recovers at the Starlet Motel, Port Gellhorn. Motel manager Linda wants rent and holds Lucia's necklace as collateral; Lucia pays $900 to recover it. Gaming ProMax S21, Wikily S6. CONFIRMED.
- Lucia seeks painkillers ("Girls' Trip"): Vicki sells pills, Dunk arranges stronger ones; "Slam Dunk" is an airstrip fight; Dr Bill, the doctor, is shot by the Kolts gang on arrival and cannot be saved (Promptowy S17 only, SINGLE). Gaming ProMax, GTA Base S1, That Love Podcast S19 for "Girls' Trip". CONFIRMED (Girls' Trip, Dunk, Vicki).
- Moses, a mechanic, buys stolen cars ("Moses", "Daily Driver"). Gaming ProMax, GTA Base strangers list. CONFIRMED.
- Lucia robs Delights, Vicki's boss, for $10,000 ("Afternoon Delight"); "Breakfast Run" is a montage robbery spree with the police chasing; "Coffee Break" unlocks free switching. Gaming ProMax, Aoeah (Breakfast Run praised). CONFIRMED.
- "Checking Out" is a point of no return and needs at least $15,000 saved for Vice City; masked or armed men attack the motel; Lucia takes a biker's motorcycle and jumps onto Jason's car; the two escape the police. Gaming ProMax S21 (full beats), Gfinity S3, KeenGamer S18, Dork (earlier, "$15,000"). CONFIRMED.

### Chapter 3, Tits Ass Drug Money (26 missions; Playday counts 28)

- "Welcome to Vice City": arrival and a borrowed apartment. Dre'Quan Priest introduces them to Boobie Ike, who houses them for work; they also work for Tyrone and Raul. Games Wiki S4, Gfinity S3, KeenGamer S18, Playday. CONFIRMED.
- The "Kitty Litter" thread: labs run by a drug network; Dook sends an address in "Here, Kitty Kitty"; later the player shuts the labs and the "main synthetics operation". Wikily S6, Gfinity titles, GTA Base strangers list. CONFIRMED (titles and gist).
- Raul Bautista is introduced in "First Impressions" with a police chase; his crew is met in "Credit Check" where Valentina, "the banker", collects marked bills. That Love Podcast S19, PowerPyx Valentina page S22. CONFIRMED.
- Other jobs: the Zorrusso car for Randy, the Real Dimez live show ("Top Billing") turning chaotic, Jason alone in "Ride or Die" posing as a fan. That Love Podcast S19, Gfinity S3 (titles). CONFIRMED.
- Chapter climax: "The Cartel Heist", the robbery of Sinfrontera National Bank. Raul briefs the crew at the Stockyard, cell towers are blown, Lucia works the vault floor and a guard named Lex, Nicole Park drills the vault, Jason clears the building and roof and gets Nicole out through the basement, then a bus chase where Lucia defends the bus from vehicles and a helicopter and Jason in an armoured truck pulls the bus onto the freeway. Reward $120,000. GTA Base S1 (mission page), That Love Podcast S19, Aoeah S20, Creative Bloq S13 (praises a vault-hacking bank job), Gfinity S3. CONFIRMED. The earlier note treated the bank job and the Cartel Heist as separate; Gfinity and Playday list "Sinfrontera National Bank" as a prep mission and "The Cartel Heist" as the heist itself.
- Gfinity and Playday also list "Meet the Heders" (Brian's family reappear in Vice City) and "South Leonida Real Estate" with Mia (Playday: "real estate with Mia"; "Mami Knows Best"). SINGLE or titles only.

The earlier note that the couple must not be seen together from "Making Gainz" until the airport job rests on Dork alone: SINGLE.

## 3. The endings

### 3.1 Common ground (CONFIRMED)

- There are several endings, decided by earlier saved state plus at most two or three live choices. Sources: GTA Base S1, ComicBook S7, Beebom S5, Detonated S8, Digital Nexus S28, Outputlag S27, Game Truth S9, Vice City Playbook S29, Aoeah S20, Cosmic Book News S30, Creative Bloq S13 (several endings seen), GameRant S12 (choices shape the bond).
- Three standing measures: Jason and Lucia's relationship (four ranks, from "Running on Empty" to "Hot Together", the top rank needed for both good endings: GTA Base, ComicBook, Beebom); the BuckMe "Family Savings" account (deposits cannot be withdrawn: GTA Base); and the Criminal Profile (Psycho or Irredeemable pushes towards a bad outcome: GTA Base, ComicBook, Beebom). Rank names seen across sources: Restrained, Professional, Hardened, Psycho, Irredeemable (Dexerto, GTA Base, Beebom); the exact order was not given in the pages I read.
- The final live choice is at a police roadblock on a bridge: "Swim for It" or "No Surrender". "No Surrender": the couple are killed (shot in their seats holding hands, per GTA Base and ComicBook), whatever the save. "Swim for It" has bad and good versions fixed by the save; Jason's first line tells which: "Don't draw your weapon" signals a good version (GTA Base, Outputlag, Game Truth).
- A second, earlier choice: spare Mendez with "Shoot" or accept "Take Deal". Take Deal ends the game early in a swamp ending (the couple retrieve the drive for Mendez, are cuffed "for the optics" and driven away, and the credits end on an alligator beside two skulls): GTA Base S1, ComicBook S7, Outputlag S27, Beebom S5 ("Alligator Lunch"), Digital Nexus S28. CONFIRMED. The Cal post-credits scene does not play in it (GTA Base).
- Afterwards the game returns to a point just before the final job; the finale stays on the map for replays; there is no new game plus; the 100 per cent checklist unlocks. GTA Base S1, FinalBoss S23, Game Truth S9, Cosmic Book News S30, Vice City Playbook S29, Detonated S8, KeenGamer S18. CONFIRMED. This contradicts Europeans24's version (return before Chapter 5), which is now contradicted by many sources.
- Post-credits: Cal waits at his dock or laptop for the hard drive to decrypt; the contents are never shown. GTA Base S1, Game Truth S9, Vice City Playbook S29, Detonated S8 (calls it an email). CONFIRMED that the scene exists; what it means is not shown.

### 3.2 The endings as the best-supported guide lists them (GTA Base S1, 21 November)

GTA Base lists five. Each is CONFIRMED in outline by at least one other outlet, as marked.

1. Mendez's Deal (bad): Take Deal at the end of Protective Custody; the swamp ending; no bridge. Also ComicBook ("The Swamp"), Outputlag, Beebom. CONFIRMED.
2. Failed Escape (bad): "Swim for It" on a save that fails the good conditions (a Psycho profile for instance); Jason exits with his hands up but still holding his pistol; deputies shoot both. Also ComicBook ("Botched Surrender"), Beebom, Outputlag. CONFIRMED. Epilogue: the son is raised by Lucia's family (GTA Base, Detonated); ComicBook says only that the son is orphaned and money decides his fate. CONFIRMED in kind.
3. No Surrender (bad): Jason drives through the roadblock; both die; the son knows his parents only by a framed photo (GTA Base, ComicBook). CONFIRMED. GTA Base adds that Lucia had been offered a deal to leave alone and refused. SINGLE.
4. Jason Takes the Fall (good): "Swim for It" with Hot Together, a clean profile and under the money mark; both leave unarmed; Jason gives Lucia a phone and on a countdown lets go of her hand; Lucia escapes into the water and takes the government deal; Jason is pinned and goes to prison; the last scene is Lucia visiting with their son, who presses his hand to the glass. GTA Base, ComicBook, Beebom, Game Truth, Outputlag. CONFIRMED.
5. Two Years Later (best): the same bridge, then a jump of two years; Lucia contacts Abe; a $2,500,000 transfer appears in her banking app; Jason is released and the family drives away. GTA Base, ComicBook (calls it "Happy Together"), Game Truth, Beebom (Lucia pays his bail). CONFIRMED.

### 3.3 Disputed points

- Number of endings. GTA Base 5; ComicBook 6 headings and a seventh variant, so its own count is inconsistent; Beebom's updated page mentions four, six and "three good and three bad" in different places; Detonated 5 (different list); Digital Nexus 7 variants (footage-based); Outputlag 4 (one unconfirmed); Aoeah ranks 8 including "rumoured" and "pre-bridge" ones; Vice City Playbook "at least four". DISPUTED. Nobody has an official list.
- Money threshold for the best ending: $2,000,000 in GTA Base, Game Truth, Cosmic Book News, Detonated, Vice City Playbook (citing GameSpot); $2,500,000 in ComicBook (this is the transfer shown in the epilogue, not the saved amount, in GTA Base and Game Truth); at least $3,000,000 in Beebom. The $2m figure is CONFIRMED by 4 or more; the others are not. Outputlag says the in-game ending shows no savings check, so the trigger is not verified. Treat $2m as the best-supported but unproven.
- A rooftop or tower branch. ComicBook S7, Beebom S5 and Aoeah S20 describe "Stay Down" and "Defend Jason" (Jason dies or both die). ComicBook says Lucia then robs a shop holding a gun, mirroring the opening; Beebom says she is arrested, makes a deal and is released. Digital Nexus S28 says footage showed "Stay Down" but not "Defend Jason". GTA Base, Outputlag, Game Truth and Vice City Playbook do not list it. DISPUTED. Three outlets say it exists; they disagree on the aftermath.
- Which profile helps. Detonated S8 says the good endings need a low profile for one character and reverses the rest; its labels (Dead Fish, Bonnie and Clyde, Lucia Escapes, Jason Escapes, Happily Ever After) and its claim that the good ending takes them to Liberty City appear nowhere else. SINGLE and contradicted. Treat as unreliable. Detonated also adds "saving the cop" as a condition; Outputlag S27 calls it a guess and says no such prompt exists. SINGLE.
- A "Lucia takes the fall" ending (Jason escapes): Detonated and Aoeah (marked rumoured). SINGLE, unconfirmed.
- Mattia and Kat have a son (Detonated, post-credits). SINGLE.
- Cosmic Book News S30 (21 November) reports that players call the ending the worst in the series, citing one reviewer's X post and unnamed fan posts. SINGLE, and about reception, not content.
- Earlier single-source claim (Beebom only): the bridge and its two choices. Now CONFIRMED by 8 outlets.
- Earlier disputed claim (Beebom against Europeans24): who survives. Now RESOLVED against Europeans24 for the "Swim for It" path: the good version has Lucia escaping and Jason arrested (and, with enough savings, released two years later); the bad version has both shot. Neither source said "both live together and move to Liberty City" except Detonated, which is unsupported.
