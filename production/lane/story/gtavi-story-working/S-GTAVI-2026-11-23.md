## What it is about

In the present day, in an invented coastal province on the warm north-east shore of a southern federation, with a chain of reef islands at its northern tip, a run-down port town, a large coastal city of towers, beaches and nightclubs, and mangrove swamp and cane country inland, a criminal couple try to get out of crime. They want what ordinary people have: a bank account, a home, a family. He is an army veteran and she is a robber just out of prison on an ankle tag, neither can get legitimate work, and each job meant to set them free pulls them further in. The player moves between the two. The question is whether two people with little but each other can buy their way to an ordinary life, and what they will do to protect each other on the way.

## The lead

There are two leads, and the player can switch between them, play them together, or phone one to drive the getaway for the other. The veteran is an army veteran, dishonourably discharged, who does odd jobs and drug runs for his landlord in the islands and goes fishing with his best friend. He is reserved and loyal, the voice of reason between the two, and unsure whether he is a good man. The thief is a petty thief and car thief who has lived from one pay day to the next. She is less troubled by her flaws than he is by his, and pushes for bigger risks. She opens the story in prison.

Three measures run under both leads for the whole game. One is how close the couple are, in four levels, raised by time spent together, small kindnesses and what each says to the other. One is each lead's criminal record, which runs from restrained and professional through hardened to violent and irredeemable, and the worst levels cannot be undone. The third is a joint savings account at a bank, from which money paid in cannot be taken out. The player's hands drive, fly, fish, shoot, steal cars, rob shops and banks, and talk, and in many jobs choose between stealth and open gunfire, and between killing and wounding.

## The people who matter

The veteran's best friend is his fishing partner, a nervous man who wants a quiet life and fences stolen goods. His landlord is a long-time drug runner in the islands who employs him through the first stretch and then retires. The landlord's associate is killed early on. The thief's mother spends all her money on a rundown house she means to do up and sell. A corrupt police captain sets the traps that drive the couple from place to place and faces them again at the end.

In the big city a record label boss and a businessman house the couple and give them work. A charming veteran bank robber recruits them; his banker finds them a hotel room and later betrays him. A lawyer arranges new identities for a fee they must work off. Late in the story a shipping heir, whose ships carry cocaine for a foreign crime family, takes them in, and a pair of his associates stand beside him.

## The opening and the scene players retell

The story opens with the thief in prison, giving an interview about release. A playable flashback shows how she came to be there: she robbed a moneylender's shop and was arrested. Control then passes to the veteran in the islands, doing jobs for the landlord and the best friend. He fishes, buys gear, makes a pickup from a drug lab and fixes pipework for tenants. One tenant flirts with him and, if he gives her his number, sends him a stream of obsessive messages. On one dive a shark attacks a man, and the veteran can save him with two hits of a speargun. In a chase the veteran and the landlord run a fleeing man down to the water, and the player may kill him or spare him. The choice feeds the criminal record, and a spared man swims away.

The thief leaves prison on her tag, moves into the veteran's house, and the couple open the joint account. The landlord's associate is killed by a swamp gang in a setup, and the veteran clears out the dead man's stash and caravan.

Then the veteran collects a bag from a locker at a visitor centre in a nature reserve, and police and masked men ambush him. The captain has set the trap. The couple escape by boat, fighting their way to the docks. The scene picked out most often comes next. The veteran lies shot in a hospital in the big city. The thief wheels him out while corrupt police and masked men attack, takes an ambulance and jumps it across a gap, and the veteran shoots from the back doors as they flee west to the port town.

Early in the story there is also a scene at the thief's mother's house: an old sofa lies at the bottom of the empty swimming pool and a large python drops onto the mother and coils round her.

## The middle

The short second stretch belongs to the thief. She hides the wounded veteran in a roadside lodge in the port town. The manager holds her necklace against the rent until she pays nine hundred. She buys painkillers from one dealer and stronger ones through another, which leads to a fight at an airstrip. She sells stolen cars to a mechanic, robs a dealer's boss of ten thousand, and runs a spree of robberies with the police behind her. Its last mission is a point of no return and needs fifteen thousand saved for the move to the big city. Armed men attack the lodge, and the thief takes a biker's motorcycle and jumps from it onto the veteran's car as they escape.

The third and fourth stretches fill most of the game, more than half of about ninety story missions, in sixty to eighty hours. The robberies climb from corner shops to banks: a job, an escape, a celebration. In the third stretch the couple work from a borrowed flat for the record label boss, the businessman and a drug supplier: drug deals, shutting down rival drug labs, car thefts, and a live show by the label's act that turns chaotic. The bank robber arrives through a police chase, and his banker collects marked notes. The stretch ends with a bank robbery for a cartel's money. Phone masts are blown, a safecracker drills the vault while the thief works the floor, and the veteran brings the safecracker out through the basement. Then the thief defends a getaway bus from cars and a helicopter while the veteran, in an armoured van, drags it onto the motorway. Their share is a hundred and twenty thousand.

In the fourth stretch they live in the banker's hotel room, take on the lawyer and do favours. They guard a rich businessman during a hostage crisis at a television station. They rescue a man from kidnappers, a job in which the businessman's head of security dies and is shown to have been working with an armed group. They get the label's act on stage at a launch. And they learn that the bag from the visitor centre holds a locked hard drive, which the bank robber, his banker and the lawyer plan to sell.

Two side stories stand out. The thief is drawn into a wellness company selling vapes that runs like a cult: she climbs its chain of sellers, pays its joining fee and takes part in its rituals. A hunter recruits the veteran to search the swamp for a legendary forest beast and the carved totems said to mark its ground. Beside them run about fifteen named strands: the veteran's old army friend's hunting business, a crop-spraying pilot, street racers, delivery flights, robbery tips, dates and errands.

Two scenes are meant to be funny. In one the veteran and his best friend clean a sitting room and carry a heavy sofa upstairs. In the other an elephant breaks loose from a circus lorry and tramples people.

## The turn

The turn is the airport robbery late in the fourth stretch. The bank robber plans to rob another crew of a vault at the airport and turns on them. The thief and his crew empty the vault, police swarm the runway, and the thief fights her way onto a cargo plane and throws the rival crew's boss towards its open door, while the veteran holds off federal agents. The thief and the bank robber fly the plane until it comes down in the harbour. Two of the crew and several hired men die. Their share is two hundred and fifty thousand.

The banker has already sold the bank robber out, and she rings the thief while she bargains with buyers for the drive. The veteran finds the bank robber on his moored boat, beaten and barely alive. He says he was given up and begs the veteran to end it, and the couple end his suffering. This and the ambush at the visitor centre are the gravest scenes. They fight past a police task force and escape, sending an empty boat off as a decoy.

In the last mission of the stretch, with the city searching for them, they recover the drive and a statue, and these lead them to the shipping heir, whose bag it was. He takes them into his penthouse. All of this is fixed: the player cannot change the betrayal, the bank robber's fate or what follows.

## The last act

The last stretch has seven missions. The heir's cocaine has been seized by the coastguard, and he is in debt to the foreign crime family. He needs the cargo back and a rival's shipment destroyed, and he offers the couple pardons in return. An older enforcer from the crime family comes to threaten him. The couple scout the coastguard compound by helicopter with a hired crew and carry a man from a cruise ship to a helicopter. In the raid the thief rigs a bridge with charges, the rival's cargo ship is cleared and blown up, and the thief dives into the coastguard offices and holds the cargo while the crew winch it out. Two of the crew are killed. The enforcer collects the drugs, and the couple pay the lawyer's fee.

Then the heir betrays them after a meeting with the lawyer. The police take them separately. The thief's prison van goes into the water and she escapes; the veteran breaks out of his cell with another prisoner and fights through the station. They fight through an armoury and a warehouse against an armed police assault, reach each other, and beat the captain, who has fired on them from a mounted gun. He lies against a tyre with both their guns on him. The player can shoot him, or wait, and after about a minute a choice to take his deal appears.

If he is shot, the final mission follows. The couple arm at the landlord's marina and storm the heir's waterfront tower. The thief covers from a helicopter's gun, the veteran fights to the heir's office and copies the drive with the best friend's equipment, and the thief is rescued from a collapsing crane. They flee by car, shoot down a helicopter, and make for a plane at an airfield. The road ends at a police roadblock on a bridge.

## The endings

There are five endings. What decides them is the choice over the captain, the choice at the bridge, and the three measures: the couple at the highest level of closeness, a criminal record short of the worst levels, and at least two million in the joint account.

If the player takes the captain's deal, the couple fetch the drive for him, are handcuffed for the sake of appearances and driven away. The game ends early, and the last image is a python coiled beside two skulls in the swamp.

At the bridge the player chooses to leap into the river or to stand and fight. If they stand and fight, the veteran drives at the roadblock and both are shot dead in their seats, holding hands. Their son grows up knowing them only from a framed photograph, which is the last image.

If they leap without the highest closeness or with a record at the worst levels, the veteran steps out with his hands up but still holding his pistol, and the deputies shoot both. Their son is left to the thief's family.

If they leap with the highest closeness and a record short of the worst levels, the veteran first tells her not to draw her gun. Both step out unarmed, he gives her a phone, and on a count he lets go of her hand. The thief escapes into the water and takes a deal from the government; the veteran is pinned down and goes to prison. The last scene is a prison visit, the family on one side of the glass and the veteran on the other, a hand pressed against it.

If the savings are met as well, the same scene is followed two years later by the thief calling the lawyer, a transfer of two and a half million reaching her account, and the veteran's release. The last image is the family driving away together.

In every ending but the deal, a closing scene shows the best friend waiting for the drive to unlock; what it holds is never shown. Afterwards the game returns to just before the final mission, and play continues.
