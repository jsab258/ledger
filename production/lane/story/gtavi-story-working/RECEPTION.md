# GTA VI: reception of the story

Gathered 21 November 2026 (two days after release on 19 November; reviews out 18 November). Console only at launch, PS5 and Xbox Series; no Steam page, PC port later. British spelling. Plot is kept to what reviewers say; most reviewers avoided spoilers, so almost nothing on the ending itself exists.

Method note: web search and page fetches only. Several pages were fetched through a summarising tool, so quotes below are as the tool returned them; check a quote against the source before it goes anywhere official. Two pages (ResetEra thread, Yahoo roundup) failed to load. IGN, GameSpot, Eurogamer and Guardian full reviews were not reached directly; their entries rest on roundups and are marked THIN.

## 1. Aggregates (as at 18 to 21 November; these move)

- Metacritic (PS5): 99 from 147 critic reviews, all positive, 118 perfect scores. Ties Ocarina of Time for highest ever; beats GTA IV (98). Xbox Series X: 99 from only 14 reviews. (TechPowerUp, GamerFuzion, The National.)
- OpenCritic: sources disagree. GamerFuzion (18 Nov): Top Critic Average 99, 99% recommend, 81 reviews, "Mighty". OpenCritic's own page (fetched later): 99% "Mighty" from 90 critics. Use "about 99, 80 to 90 reviews, still rising". Beebom quotes 98 (unreliable aggregator).
- Lowest scores found: 9/10 on OpenCritic; Metacritic lows listed by TechPowerUp: New York Times and IGN Japan 90, ScreenHub 80 (it is a 4/5), GameStar 97, Wccftech 96. These lows are the ones that talk about pacing.
- Metacritic user score: none yet. Metacritic removed fake pre-launch user reviews (VGC, 17 Nov) and blocks user reviews for at least 36 hours after release. A Polish site reported the fakes had dragged it to 6.5 before removal (not checked at source).

That is 14 outlets with real story content and about 10 more with a line or two.

### What critics praise in the story (recurring)
1. The Jason and Lucia relationship: named in almost every review, many calling it the best romance or relationship in a game; the relationship tracker that shapes the ending.
2. Lucia as the stronger lead (Kotaku, Push Square, ScreenHub, Creative Bloq, VGC), Cal as the funniest supporting character.
3. A grounded, personal, more mature tone than GTA V; satire of influencers, gym and wellness culture, pyramid schemes, music scene.
4. Multiple endings shaped by the relationship and the Criminal Profile. No reviewer found describes the ending in detail.
5. Missions that mix quiet life and set pieces.

### What critics complain about in the story (recurring)
1. Pacing: slow opening (TechRadar, TheSixthAxis, ScreenHub, Gfinity, Worthplaying) and a dragging back half or third act (Kotaku, Push Square, TechRadar, Siliconera, NPR, ScreenHub's Chapter Four).
2. Padding: Push Square "Story feels padded at points"; ScreenHub filler. Length is 60 to 80 hours main story.
3. Jason thinner than Lucia (Push Square, ScreenHub, one at The National).
4. Scripted, "on rails" or prescriptive missions (Game Informer, Siliconera, Creative Bloq).
5. Uneven or stale satire (ScreenHub, Ars Technica); predictable and few shocks (Gfinity); convoluted or farcical at times (Pure Xbox).
6. Choices that feel like an illusion (Push Square).

Balance: overwhelmingly praise. Every review found rates the story well; complaints are almost all about pacing and padding in the back half, with a few on Jason and on predictability. No reviewer called the story weak.

### Comparison to RDR2 and GTA V
- RDR2 is the main benchmark. Most say VI inherits RDR2's tone, chapter structure, camps and emotional register (Kotaku, Vice, VGC, TechRadar, Creative Bloq, Push Square). VGC: "more a sequel to Red Dead Redemption 2 than to GTA 5". Pure Xbox: "basically GTA 5 mixed with Red Dead Redemption 2". Vice calls it a mix of RDR2's writing and character work in GTA's world.
- Which is rated higher: no reviewer found says VI's story beats Arthur Morgan's outright. Siliconera is the closest: VI handles open world and missions better than RDR2, which has a "very stark divide" between the two (a systems point, not about the writing). Push Square: "the deepest, densest, most accomplished open world since Red Dead Redemption 2" (so RDR2 is placed just behind or level). Vice: money matters in VI, unlike the criticism RDR2 got.
- GTA V: VI is repeatedly called more mature and grounded; Gaming Trend says V's three leads were "three stories that occasionally intersected"; Siliconera calls V's cast "too cynical"; Push Square says VI sheds V's "cartoonish cultural caricatures". No one rates V's story above VI's.
- GTA IV: little. Siliconera says the writing recalls IV; GTA IV is otherwise not compared. (Also its Metascore of 98 is beaten.)

## 3. Early player reception (EARLY: two days after release)

How much exists: very little, and it is thin and anecdotal. No Metacritic user score (blocked at least 36 hours; fake pre-launch reviews were purged). No Steam page. I found no Reddit thread or forum summary I could actually read about the story (ResetEra failed to load; Reddit not reached). Only outlet round-ups.

- Gulf News round-up (updated 19 Nov): players talk about Mandy (a character not in the marketing), viral jet-ski dog and cat clips, heavier driving, a base-PS5 frame-rate post with over 2 million views. It cites an Output Lag count that 16 of 18 reviews praised the two leads. Character switching unlocks at about 35% story completion. It does not report anyone finishing the story.
- IBTimes (18 Nov): one X user (@ItsRealAlec) gave it 2/10 after about four hours, calling Jason and Lucia without GTA V's personality and the missions tedious ("$70 screensaver"). Fans replied that four hours is too little to judge an 80-hour story. One voice only; mostly about performance (30fps).
- Gamefile (an eight-hour first-impressions piece): Jason and Lucia "win him over quickly"; flawed, bad choices make them interesting; contrasts with GTA V's "boors and louts".
- Had players reached the ending? Not as far as anything found shows. The game is 60 to 80+ hours of story, release was 19 November, and the only people reported to have finished are reviewers (a few reported 100 to 150 hours). No spoiler or ending discussion by players was found. Treat any claim about player verdicts on the ending as unsupported.

Pre-release context (not reception): Warhorse's Daniel Vavra (KCD2) on the trailer, 28 Aug 2026: "two uninteresting sociopaths chaotically committing mass murders of innocent people for other sociopaths and money" and "zero story hook for me". It was about a trailer, not the game, and criticised as premature by GamesRadar. Useful only as a marker of what a KCD2 audience weighs: a theme, internal and external conflict, complex motives.

## 4. What bears on how players will rate the story against RDR2, GTA V, GTA IV and KCD2

Things players most weigh, and what the evidence says so far:
- Characters worth caring about: the strongest point. Reviewers repeatedly say the leads made them emotional. The risk is Jason (blank slate, thin) and the 2/10 voice finding the pair lacking GTA V's personality. Note GTA V's cast were more individually loud; VI's are a pair who work as one.
- Writing and humour: praised, with caveats. Some reviewers find the satire sharp, others uneven or stale. Hard-edged edgelord humour misses for some. Players who loved V's cartoon satire may find VI quieter.
- Moments that hit: reviewers cite quiet scenes (living room, drywall, gym, coffee), the opening, and the finale ("final operatic moments"). Emotional payoff is the headline, closest to RDR2's appeal.
- Ending: unknown. Multiple endings, driven by the relationship and Criminal Profile. One reviewer's ending "didn't fully land"; others say the final stretch or mission is gripping. Players will judge this over the coming weeks; RDR2's ending is the bar. Expect spoiler discourse after the first week.
- Surprise: mixed. One says predictable with few shocks; another says shocking twists and a late reveal about Lucia's motive (one complaint it uses a familiar device).
- Padding and forced missions: the main risk. Back-half drag, a bloated Chapter Four, 25 slow hours, "on rails" scripted missions. RDR2 was criticised on similar grounds (slow, restricted, scripted missions), so expect the same split between "slow burn" and "padded".
- KCD2: the comparison is about story hook, theme and consequence-driven choice. VI's multiple endings and relationship system are its answer; no critic compares them. KCD2's audience, per Vavra's own criteria, will want stakes, a point and complex motives, which VI's reviews give to the romance but partly deny to the back half.

## 5. Reliability notes

- Reliable: reviews at kotaku, vice, gameinformer, shacknews, gamingtrend, siliconera, pushsquare, VGC, TechRadar, ScreenHub, NPR (via member stations), OpenCritic's own page.
- Roundups (TechPowerUp, GamerFuzion, The National, Beebom, Esquire India, Dot Esports, ITechPost): useful for counts but they disagree on details (OpenCritic 98 vs 99, 81 vs 90 reviews; WaPo 4/5 vs unscored). Beebom and ITechPost gave thin, partly wrong summaries; treat as unreliable. Several scraped-looking domains (rosenberryrooms, nerdsnack, xiaomitoday, jfeed, fandomwire) appeared in search and were not used.
- Fetched pages were summarised by a small model, so wording is paraphrase unless in quotation marks; verify before quoting publicly.
- Not reached: IGN, GameSpot, Eurogamer, Guardian, NYT, Polygon, Forbes full texts; Reddit; ResetEra; Metacritic and OpenCritic user pages.
