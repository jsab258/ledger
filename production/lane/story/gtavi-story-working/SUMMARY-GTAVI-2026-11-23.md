## What it is about

In the present day, in Leonida, a fictional Florida with the Keys at its southern tip, a run-down port town in Kelly County, Vice City with its towers, beaches and nightclubs, and swamp and farm country inland, a criminal couple try to get out of crime. Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos do not want to rule a city or take revenge. They want what ordinary people have: a bank account, a home, a family. He is an army veteran and she is a robber just out of prison on an electronic tag, neither can get legitimate work, and each job meant to set them free pulls them further in, from small crimes to large, armed heists. The player moves between the two. The question the story puts is whether two people with little but each other can buy their way to an ordinary life, and what they will do to protect each other on the way.

## The lead

There are two leads, and the player can switch between them, play them together, or phone one to drive the getaway for the other. Jason is an army veteran, dishonourably discharged, who does odd jobs and drug runs for his landlord in the Keys and goes fishing with his best friend. He is reserved and loyal, the voice of reason between the two, and unsure whether he is a good man. Lucia is a petty thief and car thief who has lived from one pay day to the next. She is less troubled by her flaws than he is by his and pushes for bigger risks. She opens the story in prison.

Three measures run under both leads all game. One is how close the couple are, in four ranks, raised by time together, small kindnesses and conversation choices. One is a criminal profile, with ranks from restrained and professional to hardened, psycho and irredeemable; the top ranks cannot be undone. The third is a joint savings account, into which money paid cannot be withdrawn. The player's hands drive, fly, fish, shoot, steal cars, rob shops and banks, and talk, and in many jobs choose between stealth and open fire, and between killing and injuring.

## The people who matter

Jason's best friend, Cal, is a nervous fishing partner who wants a quiet life and fences stolen goods. His landlord, Brian, a long-time drug runner, employs him in the Keys and retires after the first stretch. Brian's associate Donnie is killed early. Lucia's mother spends all her money on a rundown house she means to sell. A corrupt police captain, Mendez, sets the traps that drive the couple from place to place and faces them at the end.

In Vice City a record label man, Dre'Quan, and a businessman, Boobie Ike, house them and give them work. Raul, a charming veteran bank robber, recruits them; his banker, Valentina, finds them a hotel room and later betrays him. A lawyer, Abe, arranges new identities for a fee they must work off. Late in the story Giorgio, a shipping heir whose ships carry cocaine for a foreign crime family, takes them in, and his associates Mattia and Kat stand beside him.

## The opening and the scene players retell

Lucia, in prison, gives an interview about supervised release. A playable flashback shows how she came to be there: she robbed a payday-loan shop and was arrested. Control passes to Jason in the Keys, doing jobs for Brian and for Cal: fishing, buying gear, a lab pickup, plumbing for tenants. One tenant flirts with him and, if he gives her his number, sends him a stream of obsessive messages. On one dive a shark attacks a man, and two hits of a harpoon save him. In one chase Jason and Brian run a man named Arnold down to the water, and the player kills or spares him; the choice feeds the criminal profile, and a spared Arnold swims off. Lucia leaves prison on her tag, moves into Jason's house, and the couple open the joint account. Donnie is killed by a swamp gang in a setup, and Jason clears out his drop and trailer.

Then Jason collects a bag from a locker at a wetlands visitor centre, and police and masked men ambush him; Mendez has set the trap. The couple escape by boat in a fight to the docks. The scene writers single out most comes next. Jason lies shot in a Vice City hospital. Lucia wheels him out while corrupt police and masked men attack, takes an ambulance, jumps it over a gap, and Jason shoots from the back doors as they flee west to the port town. Early in the story, too, is a scene at Lucia's mother's house, with a mattress in the swimming pool and an alligator that attacks the mother.

## The middle

The second stretch is Lucia's. She hides Jason at a motel while he heals. The manager holds her necklace against the rent until she pays nine hundred dollars. She buys painkillers from one dealer and stronger ones through another, which leads to a fight at an airstrip. She sells stolen cars to a mechanic, robs a dealer's boss of ten thousand dollars, and runs a spree of robberies with the police behind her. Its last mission is a point of no return that needs fifteen thousand dollars saved: armed men attack the motel, Lucia takes a biker's motorcycle and jumps onto Jason's car, and they escape to Vice City.

The third and fourth stretches fill most of the game, 51 of 91 story missions, in a story of 60 to 80 hours. In the third the couple work from a borrowed flat for Dre'Quan, Boobie Ike and a drug supplier: drug deals, shutting rival drug labs, car thefts, a music act's live show that turns chaotic. Raul arrives through a police chase, and Valentina collects marked bills. The stretch ends in a bank robbery for a cartel's money: cell towers are blown, Lucia works the vault floor, a safecracker drills the vault, Jason clears the building and gets her out through the basement, and Lucia defends a getaway bus from cars and a helicopter while Jason, in an armoured truck, drags it onto the freeway. Their share is 120,000 dollars.

In the fourth they live at a hotel, take on Abe, and do favours: guarding a businessman during a hostage crisis at a television station, rescuing a man named Rafa from kidnappers, a job in which the businessman's security chief dies and is revealed as working with an armed group, and getting the music act on stage. They learn the bag from the visitor centre holds an encrypted hard drive, which Raul, Valentina and Abe plan to sell.

Two side stories stand out. Lucia is drawn into a wellness company selling vapes that works like a cult, climbing its chain of sellers, paying its fee and taking part in its rituals. A hunter named Kirk recruits Jason to search the swamp for a legendary ape-like creature and its tree effigies. Besides these there are a hunting business run by Jason's old army friend, a crop-spraying pilot, street racers, delivery flights, robbery tips, dates and many errands, about fifteen named strands in all.

Two scenes are meant to be funny: Jason and Cal cleaning a living room and moving a television, and a polar bear escaping a zoo transport and killing people.

## The turn

The turn is the airport heist. Raul plans to rob another crew of a vault at the airport and turns on them. Lucia and his crew empty the vault, police swarm the tarmac, Lucia fights her way onto a cargo plane and throws the rival crew's boss towards its open door, while Jason holds off federal agents. Lucia and Raul fly the plane until it comes down in the bay. Two of the crew and several hired men die. Their share is 250,000 dollars.

Valentina has already sold Raul out, and she calls Lucia while she negotiates with buyers for the drive. Jason finds Raul on his houseboat, beaten and barely alive. Raul says he was given up and begs Jason to end it, and the couple end his suffering. It is the gravest scene in the middle of the story. They fight past a police task force and escape by sending an empty boat off as a decoy. In the last mission of the stretch, with the city searching for them, they recover the drive and a statue, and these lead them to Giorgio, whose bag it was. He takes them into his penthouse, and they sleep there.

## The last act

The last stretch has seven missions. Giorgio's cocaine was seized by the coastguard, and he is in debt to a foreign crime family. He needs the cargo back and a rival's shipment destroyed, and is said to promise the couple pardons. An older enforcer of the crime family threatens him. The couple scout the coastguard compound by helicopter with a hired crew, and carry a man from a cruise ship to a helicopter. In the heist Lucia rigs a bridge with charges, the rival's cargo ship is cleared and blown up, Lucia dives into the coastguard offices and holds the cargo while the crew winch it out. Two of the crew are killed. The enforcer collects the drugs. The couple pay Abe's fee.

Then Giorgio betrays them after meeting Abe. Police take them separately. Lucia's transport goes into the water and she escapes; Jason breaks out of his cell with another prisoner and fights through the station. They fight through an armoury and a warehouse against a tactical assault, reach each other, and beat Mendez, who has fired on them from a mounted gun. He lies against a tyre with both their guns on him. The player can shoot him; if the player waits about a minute, an option to take his deal appears.

If he is shot, the final mission follows. The couple gear up at Brian's marina and assault Giorgio's waterfront tower. Lucia covers from a helicopter's gun, Jason fights to Giorgio's office and copies the drive's files with Cal's equipment, Lucia is rescued from a collapsing crane, and they flee by car, shoot down a helicopter, and head for a plane at an airfield. The road ends at a police roadblock on a bridge.

## The endings

Five endings are best supported. What decides them is the Mendez choice, the bridge choice, and three measures: closeness at the top rank, a clean criminal profile, and at least two million dollars in the joint account.

If the player takes Mendez's deal, the couple fetch the drive for him, are handcuffed for appearances and driven away. The game ends early, and the credits close on an alligator beside two skulls in the swamp.

At the bridge the player chooses to swim for it or to refuse to surrender. If they refuse, Jason drives at the roadblock and both are shot in their seats, holding hands. Their son knows them only from a framed photograph.

If they swim for it without the top closeness rank or with a psycho or irredeemable profile, Jason steps out with his hands up but still holding his pistol, and deputies shoot both. Their son is raised by Lucia's family.

If they swim for it with the top rank and a clean profile, Jason's first line is to tell her not to draw her weapon. Both step out unarmed, he gives her a phone, and on a count lets go of her hand. Lucia escapes into the water and takes a government deal; Jason is pinned down and goes to prison. The last scene is Lucia visiting him with their son, who presses his hand to the glass.

If the savings are also met, the same scene is followed two years later by Lucia contacting Abe, a transfer of two and a half million dollars, and Jason's release; the family drives away.

In all but the Mendez ending a closing scene shows Cal waiting for the drive to decrypt; its contents are never shown. Afterwards the game returns to just before the final mission, and play continues.
