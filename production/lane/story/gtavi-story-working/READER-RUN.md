## Limits (read before using the mark)

1. The page is thinner than the other eight. Two days after release the public plot sources are thin (PLOT-NOTES.md section 0): the third act rests on one walkthrough, the endings on secondhand guides that disagree, no side story is described, and the disguised page is 1,802 words against the format's 1,900 to 2,300 floor (nothing was invented to pad it). A page that cannot show side stories, quoted moments or named supporting arcs will score lower than the game may deserve.
2. Reputation runs one way. All three readers recognised all eight shipped games (95 to 99%), whose players' standing they know; they knew GTA VI only from before release (70 to 85% guess) and had no player reception to lean on. Round 1 showed recognition lifts famous stories; GTA VI got no such lift.
3. So this mark is a floor on thin sources, not a settled verdict.

## Reception (numbers from RECEPTION.md)

- Critics overall: Metacritic 99 from 147 reviews (PS5), all positive; OpenCritic about 99 from 80 to 90. RDR2's Metacritic is 97 (known figure, not re-fetched today). These are whole-game scores, not story scores.
- Critics on the story: overwhelmingly praise, led by the couple; recurring complaints a slow opening, a back half that drags or is padded, and the male lead thinner than the female. Of about 16 named reviews read in full and a dozen more seen only in snippets, none says its story stands above RDR2's; most call it RDR2-like in tone and structure, one (Pure Xbox) puts it "probably still in the top three" GTA stories.
- Players: EARLY and almost none. No user score yet (Metacritic held them back and purged fake ones), no Steam page, no readable Reddit or forum story thread, no sign players have reached the ending.

## Where GTA VI sits among players' ranking of the eight

Cannot place yet. The players' ranking comes from hand-coded story reviews (praise minus complaint per review); GTA VI has no body of player reviews two days after release, and almost no one has finished it. What would place it: 30 or more player reviews that speak about the story, coded the same way (production/research/lane/STORY-PATTERNS-STEAM-2026-11-20.md), taken once players have reached the ending (a few weeks; a Steam page if a PC release comes, else Metacritic user reviews or a Reddit sample), and a rerun of this reader on a fuller summary once full plot write-ups exist.

## Does the bar move?

No. GTA VI's story stands below RDR2 by the checked reader (7.1 against 9.5, all three NO) and no critic puts its story above RDR2's. His ruling (the higher bar wins) therefore leaves RDR2 as the story bar. GTA VI joins as a ninth reference; recheck when player reviews and full plot sources exist.
