## What it is about

In the present day, in an invented American state modelled on Florida, with a chain of islands at its southern tip, a run-down port town, a large coastal city of towers, beaches and nightclubs, and swamp and farm country to the north, a criminal couple try to get out of crime. They do not want to rule a city or take revenge on anyone. They want what ordinary people have: a bank account, a home, a family. The wish keeps failing. He is an army veteran and she is a robber on an ankle tag, neither can get legitimate work, and each job meant to set them free pulls them further in. The story is told through both of them, and the player moves between the two. The question it puts is whether two people with little but each other can buy their way to an ordinary life, and what they will do to protect each other on the way.

## The lead

There are two leads, and the player can switch between them, play them together, or phone one to drive the getaway for the other. The veteran is an army veteran, dishonourably discharged by one account, who flies drug runs for his landlord in the islands and goes fishing with his best friend. He is reserved and loyal, the voice of reason between the two, and unsure whether he is a good man. The thief is a petty thief and car thief who has lived from one pay day to the next. She is less troubled by her own flaws than he is by his, and she pushes for bigger risks and bigger goals. She opens the story in prison.

Two measures run under both leads for the whole game. One is how close the couple are, in four levels. The other is each lead's criminal record, which runs from professional to hardened to irredeemable, and irredeemable cannot be undone. The player's hands drive, fly, fish, shoot, steal cars, rob shops and banks, and talk, and in many jobs the player chooses between stealth and open gunfire, and between lethal and non-lethal force.

## The people who matter

The veteran's best friend is his fishing partner, a nervous man who wants a quiet life and who gives him side work and fences stolen goods. His landlord is a long-time drug runner in the islands who employs him through the first stretch and hints that he will retire. The landlord's associate is killed by a rival gang early on. The thief's mother spends all her money on a rundown house she means to do up and sell. A police captain pins a set of killings on the couple and comes back for them at the end.

A charming veteran bank robber from the port town recruits talented people who take risks. He is reckless and tells his crews to commit or walk. His associate gives the couple a hotel room and later betrays him. A lawyer takes the couple's case for a fee they must work off. In the big city they work for a record label boss, a local businessman and a pair of women, and later need favours from them. A rival crew boss plans a robbery of a vault at the airport while posing as an authenticator. Late in the story a cocaine smuggler steps in, and behind him stands a state senator.

The sources give a fate for the bank robber, who dies, and for a few minor figures. The fates of the best friend, the landlord and the mother are not stated.

## The opening and the scene players retell

The story opens with the thief in a women's prison. A meeting with a counsellor and an attempt to escape teach the controls. A flashback shows how she came to be there: she robbed a payday-loan office and was arrested. Control then passes to the veteran in the islands, doing odd jobs and smuggling for his landlord. He goes out on the boat, buys gear, makes a pickup from a drug lab and fixes pipework. On one dive a shark attacks a man, and the veteran can save him with two hits of a harpoon; saving him changes only the talk at the end of the mission. In a job with no crime in it at all, the veteran and his best friend clean a living room and move a television, with video games along the way.

The thief is let out on day release and moves into the veteran's house. The player decides whether she goes with him on that first day out, and her errands in this stretch set how close the couple start. They open a joint account in a banking app, and what they keep or put into it counts later. The landlord's associate is killed by a rival gang, and the veteran clears out the dead man's trailer and car. In a swamp job the couple rescue a woman held captive, and she is then taken onto another boat whatever the player does. In a chase the player catches a fleeing man and may kill him or spare him. He leaves the story either way, and the choice feeds the criminal record.

The stretch ends in a police ambush at a visitor centre in the wetlands. People are killed, the police captain pins the killings on the couple, the veteran is wounded and taken to hospital in the big city, and the thief gets him out.

No source names a single scene that players retell. The scene picked out in print as the emblem of the story is one at the thief's mother's house: a mattress floats in the swimming pool and an alligator attacks the mother.

## The middle

The second stretch is short. The thief hides the wounded veteran in a motel in the port town while she wears the ankle tag, and the player, as her, finds the rent, painkillers and a car. Its last mission is a point of no return and needs 15,000 dollars for the move to the big city.

The third and fourth stretches fill most of the game, about 51 of 91 story missions. In the third the couple work from a borrowed apartment in the big city for the record label boss, the businessman and the bank robber. The jobs are drug deals, car thefts and a bank robbery. The robberies climb from corner shops to banks, and the shape repeats: a job, an escape, a celebration. The stretch ends with a heist against a cartel.

In the fourth the couple live in a hotel room the robber's associate provides, work off the lawyer's fee and call in favours from their employers. For a long run of missions they are forbidden to be seen together, and they work apart. The bank robber and the rival crew boss plan the airport vault robbery. Along the way the inside man on one heist, head of security to a rich man, is killed, and in a surveillance job footage of the senator surfaces. Other jobs include a shootout that runs from the street onto a highway, hanging in a net under a cargo helicopter carrying cocaine, a bank robbery in the rain, and a chase at the highest police alert that ends in a railway yard. One more choice lies somewhere in the story: at a car wash the player can defuse a bomb. The main story runs about 60 hours by one count and 50 to 80 by another.

The sources describe no named side stories, so none can be told here. Talking to people in the street leads to side missions. The couple can go on dates, to clubs and on robberies together, or spend time with friends instead. After the story the list of remaining tasks includes scanning cars, watching animals, clearing out rival gang operations and fencing contraband.

Two scenes are meant to be funny. One is the job of cleaning the living room and moving the television. In the other, a polar bear escapes from a zoo transport and kills people. Around them, the thief imitates the veteran's deep voice, a stuntman does a backflip and runs a crypto scam, drag queens flirt with the police, people filming themselves for the internet jump off a bridge, and in one mission the player sprays manure over the house of a farmer's boss.

## The turn

The turn comes with the airport job near the end of the fourth stretch. The bank robber decides to take the vault score for himself. His crew turns on the rival crew boss's crew, and people die in the airport, one of the crew among them. His associate has already sold him out. Afterwards the couple find the bank robber tortured on his houseboat, and they end it, killing him. This and the ambush at the visitor centre are the gravest scenes the sources give.

In the last mission of the stretch the police are hunting the couple and their accounts are frozen, so they start again from nothing. They go back for a hard drive and a statue, and these lead them to the cocaine smuggler. All of this is fixed: the player cannot change the arrests, the bank robber's fate or what follows.

## The last act

The smuggler appears at the end of the fourth stretch. He tells the couple the hard drive holds blackmail material on the senator, which protects his cocaine smuggling. He offers them pardons from the President for one last job: to destroy a rival's shipment. They do it, and two other people are killed in the operation. Then he betrays them. The couple are arrested separately, fight their way out through corrupt police and face the police captain again. In the final mission they go to the smuggler's penthouse for the drive. One source places the last choice at a police roadblock on a bridge in this same mission. No source joins the penthouse and the bridge, so the order between them is not known.

The whole of this act rests on one detailed source. It matches the outline the makers give, in which the pair uncover a conspiracy across the state after a failed bank job and must protect each other.

## The endings

There is more than one ending; that much several sources agree on. The fullest account gives two main branches. At the roadblock on the bridge the player chooses between two actions: to swim for it, or to refuse to surrender. In one branch the thief escapes to raise their son and the veteran goes to prison. In the other both die together in a shootout at the roadblock. The same account says at least four variants exist and that the makers have said there are many more than two. It does not tie each action to a branch.

What decides the ending is a set of factors whose exact effect is not known: how close the couple are, across the four levels; each lead's criminal record, with irredeemable fixed once reached; story choices such as killing or sparing the fleeing man; and how much money the couple keep or put into their joint account.

The last image of the first branch is the thief free and the veteran behind bars. The last image of the second is the two of them dead at the bridge. After either ending the game goes back to a point before the final mission, with the couple outside a law office, and play continues in the open world. One source puts that return earlier, before the first mission of the last stretch.
