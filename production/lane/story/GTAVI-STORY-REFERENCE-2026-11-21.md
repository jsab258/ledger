# GTA VI as a story reference, 21 November 2026

Spoiler-free: what was measured, never what happens.

Sources. Two days after release the public plot write-ups are thin: reviews avoid late-story detail, one walkthrough covers most of the game, and secondhand plot guides disagree with each other. Story reception: about 16 named reviews read in full, a dozen more in snippets. Player reception is early and almost absent: no user score, no Steam page, no sign players have finished it.

The checked reader. The mended reader from the reader check, three fresh readers, nine disguised pages. GTA VI: 6th, 7th and 7th of nine; mean 7.1 against RDR2's 9.5; all three say it does not stand at or above the best. The reader stayed fit on the eight (correlation 0.91, 22 of 23 pairs). This is a floor: GTA VI's page was thinner, and readers knew how players received the other eight but not GTA VI.

Reception. Metacritic 99 from 147 reviews, OpenCritic about 99; RDR2's Metacritic is 97 (whole-game scores). On story, no critic read puts GTA VI above RDR2. Among players' ranking of the eight: cannot place yet; that needs 30 or more coded player reviews after players finish it.

The bar does not move. RDR2 stays the story bar; GTA VI is the ninth reference, to be rechecked when player reviews and full plot sources exist.
