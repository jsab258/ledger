# Summariser's notes on bible M3 (fresh Opus, format version 2), 20 November 2026

The bible was mended on the five points below after this summary was made, and two sentences of the summary were changed by the session to match (the never-turned branch no longer leads to the third ending).

Word count: 2,278 (headings included, counted with wc).
Unclear or contradictory in the bible:
1. It says one thing alone decides the ending (whether he signed for his own night), but also sends him to the third ending if he "did not turn round" on the 24th, whatever he signs.
2. If he never turns round, the doorman is arrested at dawn on the 25th, yet the last week's scenes (ride-along, funeral, quay wall, Friday drive) have him free in a sling, with no version given for that branch.
3. The bookkeeper is said to have walked home for thirty-one years sooner than be driven, but section 10 says she stopped driving and being driven only after the night of the fire, a year ago.
4. Section 16 says "nobody is killed by the story", while the man in the chair dies of the first night as a fixed event.
5. The fifth, finished story about the old gang is promised but never placed in any scene. Process note: I briefly wrote a working draft to a scratchpad file to count words and then deleted it.
