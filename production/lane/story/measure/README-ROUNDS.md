# How a measured round is run

For a later session. Everything a round needs is in this folder.

1. The bible. Copy the new bible without its head note and section 0 (they give away the turns and say what was changed) under a neutral heading.
2. The summariser. A fresh Opus helper, given only FORMAT.md and that copy: "You are a neutral summariser. Write a faithful summary in the format; do not improve or weaken; give instances and leave the bible's claims about itself out; never write 'the bible says'; 'What it is about', the lead and the people must not give away the turn, who dies or the endings; eight headings, 1,900 to 2,300 words, names replaced by roles; reply with the text only."
3. The packs. MEASURE.md, then the four summaries under the fixed labels in LABELS.md (Q is GTA V, V is The Passenger, H is KCD2, D is RDR2), in a new order for each reader. Put them outside the repository so a reader cannot wander.
4. Three fresh Opus readers, one pack each, with these lenses and nothing else: a games critic whose favourite stories are slow, grounded, character-led and set in a real period, a hard marker who distrusts reputation; a player who finishes big open-world games for their stories and quits a boring middle; a script editor for crime drama and story games who reads for cause, set-up and pay-off. Each is told: judge only from the page, name no game, open nothing else, return the full scoring as text with the MARKS lines.
5. The fourth reader, only if the three put ours at or above the best reference on the checklist and overall with no fault item failed: a seasoned games writer who has shipped story-driven open-world games, given the pack and the bible copy, told plainly that three pages are shipped games and one is a plan, and given the frame that cannot change. Asked to score the pages, then to say for each core item whether the bible writes it, asserts it or lacks it, then YES or NO to "built competently inside its frame, would it stand at or above the best of the three", with the faults that can be mended on paper kept apart from what only a build can show, and up to six changes of shape.
6. Save every reply whole as REVIEW-M<N>-<lens>.md (tools/extract_reply.py does this inside the session that ran the helpers), add the marks up with tools/score_marks.py, write the round into STORY-LOG.md, commit, push, and write the row.
