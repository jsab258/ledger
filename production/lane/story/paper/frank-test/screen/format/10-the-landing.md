## Setting
A boat landing on a cold autumn night, with a firework display fired from a barge. The whole town stands along the quay wall. The tide is low, so the ramp up from the stage is steep and wet.

## People
Ada keeps the books. Abel is the doorman, who has had four driving lessons. Bess works the radio. Bram and Cass are the rival boss's hired men; the lead once spared Bram. Dorran is an old man who has stood by the ticket window every boat night for thirty years. Ezra is the deck hand. The lead wants the rival's load off the landing and one of his own men standing there by morning.

## What happens
That afternoon the lead laid out the plan on the office counter in tea things, with Ada, Abel and Bess watching. At night the last firework broke, the boat came in, and Bram and Cass started a loaded cage up the ramp. The lead met them halfway. Bram let go and walked off. Cass fought and went down the slope. The lead held the cage alone. Abel brought a learner's car along the quay through the crowd, calling out landmarks, and they loaded four cases of tobacco. Cass came up the ramp with a gun and demanded the boot. Dorran took hold of his sleeve, and the gun went off. Dorran sat down against the shutter with his arm bleeding. Cass drove off. The lead laid Dorran on the back seat, took the wheel and drove the edge of the quay to the hospital while Abel pressed his cap to the arm.

## The lines that matter
Ada says the stapler is hers, so the milk jug will be the van. Abel calls out each landmark as the car passes it. Dorran says not here, not once in thirty years. Bram says that makes them even.

## What the player does
The lead holds the cage until Abel comes. He may say stop and go back for Dorran, or let Abel drive on. He takes the wheel for the narrow run. At the hospital he pays Abel a night's cut or leaves his wage as it stands.
