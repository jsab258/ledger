## Setting
The back room of a small café in the city, three in the afternoon, rain on the window and a gas fire lit. The day before an inspector starts work, the mood is quiet and guarded.

## People
Abel is a mourner in black who leaves early. Ada keeps the café and speaks through a hatch. Bram brokers between three bosses and wants everyone to stay for tea. Bess and Cass run gangs; Dorran owns land and a licence. Ezra washes pans. The lead holds a rank, and has one arm in a sling.

## What happens
Abel gave his verdict on the turnout and left. Bram told the lead to stay and take his coat off, since someone had to sit as family. The lead could not manage it alone. Cass offered, and Bess said to let him ask. The lead asked Bess, and she hung the coat up. He took the family chair. Ada put a few coins by his saucer. The lead laid down a signed paper giving Dorran an option on a road and slid it to Bess, who put it in her bag. Ezra was seen at the sink, and the lead told Cass to take him home. Bess said her cases had come back unopened and she did not like to owe at a funeral. The lead asked for her name behind his gate entries. Bram ended the tea and sent them out in turn.

## The lines that matter
Bess says to leave him, he can ask for himself. Dorran says he will take it that the paper may one day be read. Cass says he will leave the lead's board alone if the young man comes home. Bess says she owes the lead and will pay in her own way, on her own day. Bram says everybody has had a cup.

## What the player does
The lead chooses who takes his coat, then which boss receives each thing he has brought. Handing the paper to Bess holds her and makes Dorran an enemy. A boss he brought nothing for is free to claim terms.
