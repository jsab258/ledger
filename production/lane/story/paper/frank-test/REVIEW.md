## Must-fix

1. RESULTS.md, "Is Frank still the quietest?": "On principle 2 (the lead loudest), four readers give 2 and two give 1; in phase 2, four of six gave 1." Wrong against the sources: in phase 2, A, B, C, D and F gave 1 and only E gave 2 ("2 (narrowly)"), so five of six gave 1. The "before" panel used everywhere else (D, E, F) gave 1 from two of three. (judging/RESULTS.md has the same miscount; it is not in this review's scope to edit.) Fix: "in phase 2, five of six gave 1 (D, E, F: two of three)".

2. RESULTS.md: "New faults raised: the old man's death is thinly prepared (four readers)" and, under Limits, "Two readers marked down the old man's death as thinly prepared". The two counts disagree, and neither fits the notes. All six mention it under P13 (G "underprepared", H "thinner", I "cheaply prepared", J "little shared play behind him", K "less prepared", L "lighter, a figure more than a companion"), yet all six still give P13 a 2, so nobody marked it down there. Three (G, I, K) name it as scene 10's weakest point. Fix both lines to: "all six note it under principle 13 (none lowers that mark); three name it as the Landing's weakest point."

3. RESULTS.md, the screen table ("| 01 | 0-0-4 | 1-0-3 |" and the rest) and "Wins against admired scenes: 9 of 20 before, 13 of 20 after"; SUMMARY.md: "The scene judge agrees in direction: the five scenes beat 13 of 20 admired shelf scenes, against 9 before." For 01, 10, 14 and 15 the "before" screen is of the first drafts, not the revised scenes these replaced: judging/RESULTS.md section 1 says "The screen read the drafts before the revision round; it was not rerun after it." Only 17's "before" is the version replaced. Fix: label the column "Before (01, 10, 14, 15: first drafts; 17: the revised scene)" and add one line saying so. In SUMMARY, say the judge's earlier marks were for an older draft of four of the five, or drop the line.

4. RESULTS.md, Verdict: "the quietness note has not gone: it has narrowed from ...". The test's second condition, as RESULTS itself reports it, is met: "No reader calls him the quietest." Saying the note "has not gone" makes the verdict sound as if both conditions failed. Fix: "Condition two is met as worded (no reader calls him the quietest; the complaint has narrowed to ...). Condition one fails (0.43 against 0.7). By the test's rule that means the fully written lead is recommended." The outcome stays the same; only the reason is stated honestly.

5. RESULTS.md, Verdict: "The readers' remaining complaint is the one a fully written lead answers: Frank's lines at decisions are picked from a list or spoken live, so no version of a peak carries one authored act and one authored feeling." This goes beyond what the evidence shows, in two ways. Only L ties the menus to Frank's own lines. Fix: say the fully written lead is the rule's fallback, not a proven cure. Name the phase 2 result. Add to the recommendation (item 1) that each peak is cut to one route or one thing that decides it (for example the torch alone in 17), since that is what most readers asked for. SUMMARY's choice A should carry the same caveat in one plain clause.

9. Scenes 10, 14 and 17, and DEEDS, disagree about who stands under the overhang after the Landing.
   - Scene 10, What the town remembers: "Danny's two lads were under the empty overhang by midnight, minding it. On Monday the caff says Cammack took the landing, and Danny lets it." This is stated for every path. The original scene made it Danny's telling ("Danny tells it by morning with himself in it"); the new text makes it fact.
   - The same scene, What the player can do: "Whoever is stood there on Sunday morning has it."
   - Scene 14, for every path: "the man Frank put on the landing after the display". DEEDS F5's conditions also need "the landing his".
   - Scene 17: "a young man in a new cap, who has stood there for Danny since the old man took to his bed", and Danny's "My lad's been stood there a fortnight". The old man was back under the overhang on the Wednesday (scene 10) and stood "three more nights" (scene 17). A fortnight before Monday 26 November is the 12th, before he went to bed.
   Fix: make 10's bullet depend on the player ("If nobody of Frank's is stood there at first light, Danny's two lads are, by midnight ..."). Make 17's young man depend on Danny holding the landing, with a one-line variant for Frank's man. Change Danny's "a fortnight" to "since Saturday week" or "nine days".

10. Scene 10, Variants: "With the Widow allied, the load is his, and the gun is his lad's." On that path Danny's lad fires the gun. Yet these are written for every path:
   - scene 10, What the town remembers: "The van driver is on the morning boat with a bag. The Widow has sent him across the water"
   - scene 17, the Widow: "That was never ordered. ... The man who did it is across the water and he'll not be back."
   - scene 17, Danny: "She's sent him to the seaside."
   - DEEDS week 4: "Maureen Jensen sends the van driver across the water"
   On the Widow-allied path all four are wrong. Fix: add a Widow-allied variant to 17 (Danny's lad fired it; what Danny does with him, and the Widow's line turned on Danny), make the 10 bullet and the DEEDS line depend on the path, and write "Danny's" for "his" so the variant cannot be misread.
