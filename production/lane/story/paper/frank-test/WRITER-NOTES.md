# Writer's notes: Frank's deeds on five scenes

24 November 2026. All five run about 2,290 words.

## Changed and kept

- 01. New: the 9.20 boat, Julie Kettlewell's call, Pauline's count on the air, the tenfoot backwards, the hatchback taking the boat's fares. Kept: the cash book and "what a man started with", the bare hook, "He can spell", the week's aim. Cut: most of the walk-round.
- 10. New: the gun is fired once on every path and hits the old man; "Stop", the wheel taken off Ron, the top road; "Next of kin? Mickey's." Kept: the tea things, the arm out of the window, the ticket window, "On mine. Hup", "Faster, Ron", the cut in the car. Cut: the gun shown.
- 14. New: the load, the dock wall backwards on the handbrake, "I was. Till ten o'clock." Kept: Ron's whole act, the spellings, the bench answers, the cap. Shortened: the four windows.
- 15. New: Kevin's own grievance, Fewson's roll, the corked gateway, one cause for the arm. Kept: the bulb, Julie's call, Darren's pound, "Mine's Fridays". Cut: Screeton, the tin thief, the Yale route.
- 17. New: the cortege, the infirmary's parcel, the torch as the claim to the landing, the heads arguing. Kept: the coat, the four pounds, the three fetched things.

## Facts the cards and cast would need changed

- S24, S32, scene 19: a gun is now fired in every game, so scene 19's "guns have been fired" variant and S32's wake must key on a gun Frank or his men fired, or the quay is never fists.
- The old man is shot in week four and dies of his chest; the Widow's van driver leaves the Hook.
- S38, S39: Fewson is on Mickey's rank that same night; Wardell holds a second fact for Danny; the load assumes Frank holds the landing.
- S44, CAST: Fewson is always outside; Screeton is gone from the yard; Kevin can be kept by an answer about the miles (D01).
- S01, S05, D09: Julie is met on the first morning and has a trial across the water.
- S46: the Man in Black marks seven; the torch is a fourth thing on the cloth.
