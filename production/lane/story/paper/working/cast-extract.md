# LEDGER story lane: cast extract

Made from the revised frame (STORY-FRAME-2026-11-26.md), SPINE-CARDS.md (S01 to S61), SIDE-CARDS.md (D01 to D25), the 23 scenes, the earlier frame (for ties and ages), REGULARS.md (for ages and spare voice lines) and the people data (ids). Nothing here is new design: it is a reading of what the sources say. Where the sources disagree, the row says so and cast-diff.md has the quotes.

## Counts

- Core: 6 (Frank Nowak, Ron Kirby, Sheila Dunn, Danny Cammack, Maureen Jensen the Widow, DS Carol Ellis).
- Supporting: 46 (45 living people, plus Mickey Suddaby, who is dead and offstage but drives the plot).
- Resident: 3 (Hal Metcalfe, Karen Grummitt, Doreen Harland). Only these three are named in the revised set and only in passing. Edith Stockill and Susan Coverdale, two of the thirty regulars, are not named in the revised frame, cards or scenes at all (see cast-diff.md (b)).
- Named people in all: 55. Unnamed recurring roles: 23 (second table).

Layer rule used: core is the six named by the lead. Supporting is anyone with a thread of their own (a spine card, a side story, a frame section of their own) or a named, speaking part in a key scene. Resident is named but only in passing (a fallback carrier, a step, a line of gossip). Ages: "frame", "scene" or "card" mean the revised set states it; "data" means only the people file does; "regulars" means REGULARS.md.

Where-column key: frame §N is the section of the revised frame; spine S.. and side D.. are card ids (ranges joined with a hyphen); scenes are the numbers 1 to 23. Found by name (full name, surname or distinctive first name, including the capital-letter speaker tags), so a bare mention counts.

## Notes the table could not hold

- Frank Nowak is "Frank" to the player and to the town's ladder; Frank Stathers is "Frank" on the gate (scene 8: "There's one Frank on this gate, and he was here first."). The frame (§9, answer 3) keeps both.
