Verdict: pass after must-fixes.

Reviewed 24 November 2026 by a fresh reviewer: SPINE-CARDS.md checked against canon, PAPER-BRIEF.md (sections 1 to 5 and 7, and the lead's two later decisions), the revised frame, the 23 scenes, the economy summary, the casting files and SIDE-CARDS.md. The content rule is clean: no em-dashes, no italics, no Tom or Tommy, no drink, gambling, children, drugs, sexual content, slurs, torture, Mafia or GTA VI, and no quotation of the owner. All 61 cards have every field (act and week, conditions, priority, weight, cooldown, a shape card with all seven fields, carriers and fallback, sign, aim, what the town remembers, fails into). No condition uses real time. There is one heavy card in each of weeks 4 to 7, and each is more than 60 game hours from the last. The 1990 calendar is right: Monday 15 October, Remembrance Sunday 11 November, Friday 16 November, Monday 26 November, Saturday 1 December. The faults are all in how the conditions chain together, plus a few facts that differ from the scenes.

## Must fix

1. S33 Lesson five: the condition "Ron has had four lessons" has no way to come true if the player skips lessons. Lessons two to four have no cards, and the card leaves out scene 12's rule that Kevin gives any skipped lesson. S35 needs S33, and everything from S36 to S61 hangs off S35, so a player who skips lessons never reaches the turn or an ending. Fix: write Kevin's rule into S33's condition and say by which day of week 5 lesson five wakes anyway, or card lessons two to four.
2. S48 The first morning: the condition "a man paid off the book in the record" stops the card waking for an honest firm, or for any firm with nobody paid off the book. S51, S52, S54 and S55 all need S48, and S56 to S61 need S55, so Act III and the endings never play. Fix: make the first morning need only S46 and the Tuesday, and let the paid-off man (or no such man, which grades the item) decide what it contains. Make S51 to S55 need S46 and their day, not S48.
3. S59 to S61, the closes: their conditions do not cover every outcome, so some runs reach no ending. Three outcomes fall through: putting Danny's name to it with the books held (the frame lets this reach the Kingdom's plainer turn); putting Danny's name to it with a friend still counting him; and leaving the line as it stands, then losing a morning, with a friend still counting him. Fix: rewrite the three conditions so that together they cover every outcome of S58, for example S59 "the books held, and the line left or put to Danny's name"; S60 "written true, and a friend counts him"; S61 "everything else".
4. Several cards require the carrier to be alive in their conditions, but their fallbacks cover that carrier being gone. The card then never wakes, so the fallback can never be used. These are S08 (Alison alive, fallback Danby), S22 (Garbutt alive, fallback Hal Metcalfe), S45 (the Widow alive, fallback Stathers's plate), S51 (Kevin gone with the log, fallback the log at Terry's sister-in-law's), S53 (Ellis alive, fallback Wardell) and S54 (the Widow alive, fallback Stathers signs). Fix: move the person out of the condition and into the carriers line. S31 has the same fault: it says "guaranteed; it comes to the office if no evening was spent", but its condition needs an evening spent, so make that condition "an evening spent, or the end of game week 5 near".
5. S24 The Landing: the night sets a cooldown of 12 game hours. That stops S25 The cut in the car from playing the same night in the yard, as the card and scene 10 both say it does. Fix: "none before S25, which is the same night; 60 game hours before any other heavy card", worded as S44 has it.
6. S23 and S24, and S35 and S36: the calendar fixes these beats to set days, but their conditions do not. The Landing is fixed to the Saturday of the display and the search to Friday 16 November. As written, the director could wake the day of looking on the Monday of week 4 (after Garbutt's aim or rung 3) and the night with no display. The turn could also come before the Friday. Fix: add "the Saturday of the town's display" to S23 and S24. For S35 and S36, either add "by the Friday of game week 5" or drop the fixed date from the S36 heading and the calendar line.
7. S46 The room at Grace's says "six in the evening". Scene 17 has it in the afternoon, at three o'clock, at the funeral tea. Fix: change the card to three in the afternoon (the cooldown to the first morning still holds).
8. S44 The yard leaves out the man who calls the settle. In scene 15 Kevin Needham calls it and has the gate key; if Kevin was sacked in week one, Fewson calls it with Danny's money. The card's fallback says instead "fewer than three grudges, Fewson brings two from away". Fix: name Kevin as the caller with the key, and take the scene's fallback.
9. S60 and S61: neither card says who counts as a friend. The owner's decision of 24 November (frame section 8, scene 22) counts Ron and Sheila beside Alison and Danby, but the only friends the cards name are Alison and Danby (S08). Fix: S60 "a friend still counts him (Ron, Sheila, Alison or Philip Danby)", and the same four in S61's "neither".
10. S22 Garbutt's card puts the week-4 aim at Garbutt's table in the caff, with a card on the counter as the sign. Scene 10 has Garbutt himself at Mickey's counter on the Monday, drinking Sheila's tea, and saying the aim there. Fix: make the place the office counter and the sign Garbutt in the doorway, or bring scene 10 into line, whichever the lead prefers.
11. S46 calls Agar "seen for the first time". In scene 14 Frank sees Agar at the head of his stairs on the night between, and scene 17 says "seen before, if at all, only at the head of his own stairs". Fix: use scene 17's words.
12. S05 Five debts says "These are men the docks laid off". The casting says otherwise. Julie Kettlewell is a woman laid off from the laundry. Lee Drewery never had steady work. Wayne Harrison works at the pawn. Dennis Iveson is a scrap dealer who has the money. Darren has been out of work since a training scheme. Fix: change it to something like "people the docks' closing has left short" (the frame's week-1 line needs the same change).

## Should fix

1. S39 The empty door: Lily's "One" is written as if it happens on every path. The brief lists it as a loss only when Ron goes in, and S60 has "Lily counted two". Fix: "If Ron is inside, Lily counts out one...". If Frank let Fridays lapse in D04, there is nothing for her to count.
2. S30 The gate: Ron cornered says "Danny's go at Ron is then another card" when the Widow is allied, and scene 11 puts that go at the ferry rank. No such card exists. Fix: add a short card, or point to whichever card stands in for it.
3. S56 Car two not answering only covers Danny turned, or Danny on the top line who stayed. The opening rules also give the key to Fewson when Danny is dead or gone. Fix: add "if Danny is dead or gone, Fewson".
4. S46: "The coat (one-armed, he must be helped or take a minute)" reads as if Frank always comes in one-armed. Fix: "if he lost the arm in the yard".
5. S58 The counter at five: "one charge goes, his or Ron's" reads as Danny's charge. Fix: "Frank's, or, if he asks it, Ron's", as the frame has it.
6. S47 The Customs letter's sign is "Hebblewhite met in week 4 as a fare who asked for a receipt", but no spine card or side story carries that fare. Fix: add it to a week-4 card (S23 or S26) as a passing fare, or drop the sign.
7. S38 The night between: the "sign first" branch leaves out two of the brief's costs: the drivers go to Danny unless paid in full from the tin, and Ellis's case on Frank closes the Kingdom's better turn. The S59 conditions depend on that second cost. Fix: add both, in a few words.
8. The opening pacing rules say a light or funny card follows each heavy one, but S57 is followed by S58, which is middle weight. The checks section explains this, but the rule the director reads does not. Fix: add "except the quay, whose rest is the counter at five" to the rule. The checks section also says S18 is at the office counter, when it is in Mickey's locked office; correct that.
9. S53 Ellis's trade: "never the Widow, whom his line covers" will not make sense to a reader who is not a programmer. Fix: say plainly why the Widow cannot be given, for example that the line in the book shields her load.
10. S02 The ask and the want: "tell Ron no (a plain yes to his question)" is a puzzle as written. Fix: "tell Ron no, so the arrangement ends", or quote the question the yes answers.
11. S50 Ron in the last week takes "Father Walsh brings a folded paper" as a condition for the inside half. D17 says that without the rides the message comes by post, two days late. Fix: make the condition "visiting time, and Ron's message has reached Frank (by Father Walsh, or by post)".
12. SIDE-CARDS.md calls S21 "The gate at dawn" (D08 and the table), but its spine title is "The gate at dawn: the fifth morning". Every other id and title used in SIDE-CARDS.md matches. Fix: use the full title in SIDE-CARDS.md.
13. Period detail (fairly sure, about three in four): in 1990 a Customs and Excise VAT officer would ask for takings, invoices and VAT records, not wages. Wages and PAYE belonged to the Inland Revenue. S48's "Wages. Everybody you paid" sits oddly with Hebblewhite as a VAT officer. Fix: make the first morning about a driver's takings kept off the book, or let Hebblewhite bring a note from the Revenue.
14. S12 Lesson one says "Kevin gives any skipped lesson". S23 and S24 still plan for Ron having fewer than four lessons. Fix: once must-fix 1 is settled, say in S12 when a skipped lesson is given, so it is clear whether "fewer than four lessons" can still happen at the Landing.
15. S46 and S40: the frame and scene 17 make the room the old landing man's funeral tea, but S46 never names whose funeral it is. S40's sign (Grace clearing up after a funeral tea, a week earlier) reads like a second funeral. Fix: name the funeral in S46, and give S40 a different sign.
