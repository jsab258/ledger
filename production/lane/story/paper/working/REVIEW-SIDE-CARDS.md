## Must fix

1. D17 Father Walsh's rounds, Returns: "Without the rides, the message comes by post, two days late" contradicts scene 18 and S50. In both, Father Walsh brings the folded paper on every inside path, and the late fallback is Father Walsh bringing the three things on Friday, not the post. Fix: the rides change only how he hands it over (for example, he guesses who arranged the car and says one sentence more), never whether he carries it.
2. D15 The ferry parcel, Waits: "until June leaves (about two game weeks)" cannot hold, because D05's return needs June at her door at S48 in week 7, and D15's own return puts her in the close. Fix: say June stays in town to the close, and that the parcel waits until a set point (for example the night between), after which Gordon sends it back across the water.
3. D15 Returns: "June is at the chained gate" in the closes is in none of scenes 21 to 23 and no carrier list of S59 to S61. The scenes are the reference. Fix: either add June's lines to the close scenes and S59 to S61 (a job for the scene writer), or move the return to a beat that already has her, such as June's door at S48.
4. D03 The night lodger, Returns: "If thrown out, he has the gate key the pool's men use" contradicts scene 15. There Kevin Needham holds the gate key on car one's fob and calls the settle, and Craig Fewson does it if Kevin was sacked. Fix: thrown out, Darren is among the men at the gate, or he tells Kevin where the spare key hangs, but the key stays Kevin's.
5. D02 Two real minutes, Returns: Pauline saying "Mr Nowak" from the next day if the booking went right contradicts the name ladder. Frame section 3 (the rung table) and S18 tie "Mr Nowak" on the air to rung 2. D02 can play on day one at rung 1. Fix: a right booking makes her say "Mr Nowak" the day rung 2 comes and not a day later. A wrong one keeps her saying "the new owner" for a week past rung 2.
6. D20 Friday means Friday, Returns: Sylvia saying it back "at the chained gate" in the closes (S59 to S61) does not match scene 23. She says it only in Burn Both, from her boarding-house step, and scene 21 has no Sylvia. Fix: the return is S61 Burn Both, on her step; drop S59 and S60 from the card and the table, or add her to them.
7. D13 The name board, Returns: "Taken, Dennis is the leak the player watches" contradicts the frame's worked example 2. There Iveson leans in at Ellis's car window because Frank leaned on what he bought out of the burned warehouse, and taking the £80 is what puts Neil Screeton in the yard. Fix: use the frame's cause for the leak. Taking the board can feed it (Dennis bears a grudge) but must not be the only cause.
8. D10 The window at the caff, Endings: "hurt him (Grace bars Frank)" collides with beats he cannot miss at the caff: Garbutt's table (S22, S27, S40), the room at Grace's (S46) and Grace's kitchen after a lost yard (S44, D10's own return). Fix: say what the bar covers. For example, he is served nothing and gets no seat at the counter, but Grace lets him into the back room when Garbutt sends for him, and still has him carried in after the yard, saying so.

## Should fix

1. D01 The long way round, Returns: S51 needs Kevin gone with car one's log in week 7, but the sack ending sends him to the Widow in week 1 or 2. Fix: give the sacked branch its own line. Either Kevin kept the log when he went, or S51 uses its fallback (Terry's sister-in-law's), as scene 15 already does for the yard.
2. D02 against D09: if the booking went wrong, "Pauline will not share the desk with anybody he picks". D09 can still give Julie the night desk "beside Pauline", and S44 and S49 lean on Julie being there. Fix: say which wins. For example, Julie still gets the desk but Pauline works days only, and the yard's call then comes from Julie alone.
3. D04 Mickey's Friday, Returns: "Lily counts out one of number nine" at S39 assumes Fridays were kept. If they lapsed or Lily was asked to stop, there is nothing to count. Fix: give the other two endings a return, or say Lily counts anyway because she remembers Mickey's order, not Frank's.
4. D13, Steps and Returns: "the £80 he owes Mickey's" may already be paid, since S05 collects it ("has it") before D13 can start. "Only if Frank fronted him for the eighty pounds" is unclear ("fronted" can mean lent money). Fix: "only if Frank took the eighty pounds off Dennis at the debts (S05)", and say that the price talk changes if the debt is already settled.
5. D24 The back room, Endings: "refuse (no front yet; the first wash waits for the contract's money)". The contract pays fares, and S19 and scene 09 wake only on money "no fare explains". Fix: "the first wash waits for a round's first collection or a run's cut".
6. D06 The name on the board, Returns: "nobody answers" in Burn Both does not match scene 23, where Shaun is answered if Frank tells him ("Nobody tells him, unless Frank does"). Fix: "and nobody answers unless Frank does".
7. D11 Closing time at the Tangs', Returns: "Peter Tang's door is open to Frank in the last week (S48)", but the frame has it "on the last day". The humiliated leader's line in the yard is also not in scene 15, and neither is D03's Darren line ("I live here, me", the starting handle). Fix: line up the day with the frame, and send both yard lines to the scene writer to add to scene 15, or soften them to "if the yard scene carries him".
8. D19 Looking costs nothing, Returns: Rita's line differs from scene 14 ("Fourteen pound and the fortnight. I'll look at your money. Looking costs you nothing."). Fix: quote the scene word for word.
9. D19, Waits: "until the ticket runs out (about four game weeks)", after which the watch is for sale in the window. Under the Consumer Credit Act 1974, a pawn in 1990 could not be redeemed for less than six months and the pawnbroker could not sell before then (fairly sure, about 80 per cent). Fix: either keep the four weeks as game time and stop calling it the ticket running out (Rita simply puts it in the window, which she should not), or say Rita shows it in the window "to be looked at" before it can be sold.
10. D10, Tie: "Danny Cammack (the newest of his crew, a man of about twenty)" reads as Danny being about twenty, but he is 27 in CASTING.md. Fix: "Danny Cammack, through the newest man of his crew, about twenty".
11. D09 Twelve weeks' notice: the card never says what the laundry did wrong. The draft has it holding back her last weeks' money, so "lean on the owner (he pays)" has nothing to pay. Fix: add the grievance to Giver or Steps ("the laundry held back her last weeks' money").
12. D17, Waits and Tie: "until the cold weather ends (to game week 6)" reads oddly, since the weather only gets colder from mid-October to December. "Ellis's cells (Father Walsh visits the remanded)" is also wrong, because remand prisoners are on the prison's remand wing (scene 18), not in police cells. Fix: wait "until Father Walsh's winter sick calls end with Advent" or a set game week, and tie it to "the remand wing Ellis's case sends Ron to".
13. D16 All on the forms, Waits: "the one telling" is unclear for a reader who is not a programmer. Fix: "Christine tells it once; if Frank does nothing that day, it is gone".
14. The check at the foot: "the empire's own parts ... carry the other eight" should read six. Nineteen cards have a rival tie, and D02, D03, D04, D05, D15 and D19 are the rest.
15. D19, Returns: "Frank can knock Rita up that night" is period-correct British for waking her by knocking, and scene 14 uses it too. A modern player will hear the other meaning, though, beside a woman in her nightdress. It is within canon's innuendo, but probably not meant here. Fix: "knock at Rita's door that night".
