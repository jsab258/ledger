## Must fix

1. Scene 06, the chalk, "The top line": Sheila's "Then one is two and two is three. I'll alter the sheets" renumbers the cars, so Mickey's own car becomes car three on that path, which breaks the frame's fixed "car two" (car two not answering on the last day, car two on the quay, scene 05's last memory). Keep the numbers: Sheila says something like "Cars keep their numbers, love. He can have the top of the board."
2. Scene 12, "What wakes it" and "Fallback: none": the lesson needs a leak watched this week and the player taking Ron's invitation, yet the turn cannot wake until lesson five has played and must come by the end of week five, so a player who sees no leak or never gets in the car stalls the spine. Add the frame's rule (if no leak has been watched, one is played in front of him first) and say that Ron asks again each day and the lesson plays the next time Frank gets into car two, before the week ends.
3. Scenes 02 and 06, the 48-minute day: scene 02's "about ten real minutes into play" is wrong, because 9.10 in the morning to 9.40 at night is twelve and a half game hours, about 25 real minutes; scene 06's ride of "about three real minutes of play, and six by Danny's count" would be 90 game minutes, so neither Danny's six nor Pauline's eleven and a half can be true on the clock. Take real-minute placements out of the headers, and state once (in the frame or the scene) what the game clock does during an authored ride, for example that it slows to the pace of the talk.
4. Scenes 02, 03 and 04, the half line: the frame says Pauline wrote the 10.00 line "at ten", but in scene 02 the carbon is already in Ron's envelope at twenty to ten and "Look inside" shows the 10.00 line on it. In scene 02 show Pauline writing "10.00, Office, R.K." on Ron's word ("He said he was off the rank at ten", scene 04) before she tears the pink sheet, change the frame's "at ten" to "for ten", and say Terry's later fares that night go on a fresh sheet (scene 04 has the sheet end at the 10.00 line with only ten fares).
5. Scene 11, "What the town remembers", last item: "Ron will let a cheaper way be done in his name only if Frank came for him here" drops the second condition the brief and frame both give ("or redeemed Mickey's watch"). Add "or if Mickey's watch is back on his wrist".
6. Scene 01, "Who must be present" (and the frame's section 4 and CAST.md): "Ada Vane" gives Ada a surname that CASTING.md does not have, and Vane is the retired name of the old-money head (Aldous Vane, now Geoffrey Agar), which canon says survives only as an internal id. Call her Ada, as approved casting does, or mint a new surname and record it.
7. Scene 01, Ron on the key ("Stops in her. That's how he had it. Car two's lives on the board") contradicts itself, and scene 06 needs car two's key "on its hook on the board, where it has been all day". Make it one fact, for example "Lives on the board. I fetch it every morning and hang it back."
8. Scene 12, "It is the first question he has asked since the morning Frank got off the boat" (and scene 04's "It is the only time"): Ron asks Frank "You want me to tell them no to the envelope, boss?" in scene 02 and "Is that me?" in scene 10. Change it to "the first thing he has asked for himself", or cut those two questions.
9. Scene 12, the truth with a fare, Ron's "I did say you were off the rank" contradicts scene 04, where Pauline says Ron told her he himself was off the rank at ten and Ron answers "I weren't off it. I were at the wall." Change Ron's line to "I did tell Pauline I were off the rank."
10. Scene 03, "What the town remembers", the Karen item: "which is half of what opens her own story later" points at a story that is not among the 25 (her "one way" is kept on file, not used). Cut the clause, or tie it to a story that is on the list.

## Should fix

1. Scene 11, staging and the call: nine men in the pen with Ron the ninth, six called, leaves three including Ron, yet the scene has "three men... a yard clear of the fourth" and "three men who were not called" beside Ron. Make it ten men this morning, or five called.
2. Scene 08, the packers: "he asked all six packers", three ride in the first car and two in the fourth, yet the fight is "in front of the pen and three cars of women" and going round squeezes them "six to a back seat". Pick a total (twelve packers, three to a car, fits every line) and use it throughout.
3. Scene 06, staging: "lighting-up time, about ten to five" in week two (22 to 28 October 1990) is about an hour early, because British Summer Time ran until Sunday 28 October and sunset on the Humber was near ten to six (fairly sure). Make it "about ten to six", or set the scene on the Sunday after the clocks change.
4. Scenes 01, 04, 05 and 09, the book and the tin: the economy puts fares in the book and keeps "the tin" for night cash in Mickey's inner office, but the scenes put the drivers' halves "to the tin" on the counter in week one (while the inner office is locked) and keep night cash in a cocoa tin. Use the economy's words: fares to the book, night money to the tin, and leave the cocoa tin as Sheila's name for cash kept off both.
5. Scene 04, the cover fare: the economy adds a quarter from 22:00, and this fare is timed 10.00 at night, but Sheila prices it at the day rate. Keep £2.80, which the frame fixes, and give Sheila half a line on it ("On the stroke. I'll call it day rate.").
6. Scene 05, "Seventeen pound sixty. Count it": if Frank gives Danny his change, the twenty stays with the firm, so "leaves him nearly nothing for tomorrow" does not follow unless the scene says the twenty goes into the book and the rent must still come from his own pocket. Say where the note goes.
7. Scenes 05 and 06, the board: CAR 3 is chalked only on the quay-edge path, but scene 06's third-line choice ("after CAR 3") and Ron's aim in scene 07 assume it is there. Say what the board shows after the corner path and after the dock path.
8. Scenes 03 to 05, the path where the ask first reaches Frank on Wednesday: no carbon went down on Monday, so the lock-up cannot be found "by Tuesday night", and Danny's opening line quotes Monday's boarding-house fare. Give that path its own cause (Wednesday's sheet has the fare that sends the van) and its own opening line for Danny.
9. Scene 07, Ron's aim, "Get a third car on that board": the board may already carry CAR 3 (Danny's hatchback) or Danny's name on the third line. Make it "a third car with a driver in it" here and in the frame's table, so the aim reads plainly to the player.
10. Scene 07, the route: scenes 01 to 03 put the ferry landing and its ticket window at the foot of Quay Street, but lesson one reaches the ferry rank "by the ticket window" via the fish dock corner, the dock road, the pen and the gate (and scene 12 adds a "ferry road"). Either put the ferry rank somewhere else on the map and say so, or give a reason for the long way round.
11. Scene 09, "Pauline has gone home" a little after six, against scene 02, where she is on the radio at twenty to ten at night "closing the evening", and scene 04, where she is in at a quarter past eight. Set her hours in one line (for example, she stays late on ask nights) or give the evening radio to somebody named.
12. Scene 12, "What the town remembers": "In the station, two days on" fixes a gap the turn's state rules do not promise (the notice, the clearing and the search wake on state). Say "on the morning of the search" instead.
13. Scene 10, staging, "drinking Sheila's tea", and scene 04, "taught young to guard her sums": both are harmless, but the first is a word the content gate's list may stop and the second points at a childhood. Write "has a cup of Sheila's tea standing up" and "as a person does who has guarded her sums all her life".
14. Scene 08, "What the player can do", Pay: the receipt to Mr Nowack and the second in the Widow's own hand is the frame's first-look beat (Kevin at the dock gate, Act I to IIa). Say which beat gives the receipt when both play, so it is not given twice.
15. Scene 10, staging: "as it does every year on the Saturday" does not say which Saturday; in 1990 the Fifth was a Monday, so make it "the Saturday after the Fifth" (a week late for some towns, but plausible; moderately sure). Also say Saturdays have no late boat, since scene 03's last boat sails at twenty to eleven and this one ties up at twenty past seven.
