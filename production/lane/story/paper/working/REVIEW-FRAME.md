## Must fix

1. Section 1, "How the rise is felt", first sentence: "the blueprint's four rungs: the nephew, Nowak, Frank, the port's man" contradicts the owner's ladder (the new owner, Nowak, Frank, Frankie). Replace with the owner's four rungs; "nephew" is Danny's needle, outside the ladder, as section 3 already says.
2. Section 1, "Three people say Frankie. The Widow, to own him. Danny, to needle him. Ron, once, if ever." Frankie is the town's top rung (section 7: "the name ladder is the town's verdict, spoken"; the fourth rung turns allies), so it cannot be limited to three speakers; and Danny's needle in section 3 and every scene is "nephew", while scene 21 has him say "Evening, Frankie" only once put under Frank. Rewrite: the fourth rung is the town saying Frankie; the Widow's and Ron's are the weighty ones; Danny says it only when he has come under Frank.
3. Section 10, item 1: it writes "Tom" and "the rung Tommy", which the owner's decision bars from anything written, and "the echo of an older crime game that two readers named" points at Mafia's lead by another road. Cut the item to "The lead is Frank Nowak and the last rung is Frankie", with no earlier name and no game.
4. Section 1 ("An honest firm stops at three cars and a name") against section 5, week three (Tharratt's aim: four cars) and Act IIa ("The honest firm: the same rise by lawful work"): the honest firm can never meet the week's aim, so a condition is never true on that path, and Sign first's "Tharratt drops the contract" has nothing to drop. Either let the honest firm take a fourth car for lawful contract work while still capped at the second rung (the economy caps the rung, not the cars), or give the honest firm its own week-three aim, and say which.
5. Sections 2, 3 and "The night between": Ellis "is never bought twice", but the night between's Ellis way asks for £800 cash without saying what happens if she was bought earlier, and the frame never says why a bought Ellis still searches. Scene 13 has the answer (a superintendent reads the tip, so she must come; the bought line is "Friday's business"). Write it into the frame: already bought, she still searches; at the night between her one lost line is this statement, with no new £800; not yet bought, £800 in hand.
6. Section 3, Danny, the grievance: "On the third line, or nowhere, he chalks a small arrow pointing up beside his name" cannot work for "nowhere" (there is no name on the board), and scene 06 has him chalk CAMMACK a foot high on the empty unit's shutter instead. Make the frame follow the scene: third line, the arrow; nowhere, the shutter three doors up (which is also his week-three gain).
