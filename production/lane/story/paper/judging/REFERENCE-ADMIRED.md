# Reference shelf: scenes players and critics single out (disguised, flat retellings)

Each is a retelling of an admired scene from a shipped story game, in a fixed flat format with names and places replaced. They show the bar in content, not prose. Do not try to identify them.

## Reference 1

## Setting
A harbour-side district in warm weather and the countryside behind it. The scene covers a remedies shop, a boatbuilder's yard, a farm with a grave under trees, a cleared patch of woodland and a cliff path above a river mouth.

## People
Ada, who keeps the remedies shop, asks the lead to find her adult daughter, Bess. Abel is a dock worker who shouts about a man called Bram. Cass is a farmer with an old quarrel against Ada. Dorran is a man who has taken Bess. The player already knows Ada helped the lead earlier and owes a landowner a debt.

## What happens
Ada says Bess did not come back from her errands. The lead finds only a knitted hat in her room. Abel will say why Bess and Bram might leave town only under pressure. The lead questions a boatbuilder, and a cafe owner confirms a fight. Cass, at a grave near his farm, speaks of his son and of what passed between him and Ada. In a clearing south of town the lead finds blood and a torn piece of dress. His dog follows the scent along the river and up the cliff path. A dog attacks and the lead climbs a ladder to a loft where Dorran holds Bess. The lead talks Dorran down. Bess kills him herself. Back at the shop Ada asks the lead to kill Cass, because she believes his hands did the harm. The lead can refuse, kill him, or try to reconcile them.

## The lines that matter
- Ada: "She went out for the day and never came home."
- Cass: "I have buried enough. I do not want more."
- The lead, to Ada: "I came here to kill him. I will not."
- Ada: "If you will not do it, who will?"

## What the player does
The player questions each person, follows the trail, and negotiates or fights at the loft. The final choice is whether to kill Cass, refuse, or make peace. A reconciliation offer includes money, and the player decides who keeps it.

## Reference 2

## Setting
A large seaside hotel, shut for the winter, at night, with long corridors, a lift, a caretaker's office and guest rooms. The long empty corridors are lit in pale green.

## People
The lead is an undercover police officer working inside a gang. Abel is a young member of the gang who has been hurt and is in a bed upstairs. Bram is an older gangster who helps the lead. Rival gang members have come to finish Abel. The player already knows the lead is guarding Abel and that Abel does not know the lead's secret.

## What happens
Abel phones the lead and asks him to come at once. The lead drives to the hotel. In the entrance a scene shows armed rivals inside. The lead takes a pistol from the first man he fights and takes cover. He clears the ground floor and goes up in the lift.

On the upper floor he forces a blocked door and fights a group. A timer starts: the lead has to clear the remaining men, reach the caretaker's office and key in the release code at the door panel before time runs out. Bram joins him and they hold the office together while men come from both ends of the corridor. When the last man falls, police sirens start. The lead drives Abel away and takes him home. The job ends when the lead goes to sleep at home, which opens the next job. The player sees no more of the hotel.

## The lines that matter
Abel tells the lead on the phone that men are after him and he cannot move. The lead tells him to stay where he is. Bram says they will hold the room as long as needed. Abel thanks the lead for coming.

## What the player does
The player drives to the hotel, fights through two floors, keys in the code before the timer ends, covers the corridor with Bram, and drives Abel clear of the police.

## Reference 3

## Setting
A fish-processing quarter with a tall cold store, then a hide-out in the hills, near the end of the lead's life. Hired men hold the roads. The crew has broken apart, and the weather is bitter.

## People
Ada is the lead's closest friend and wants Bess freed. Bess is held in a back room by the hired men. Abel is a younger man the lead has long looked after, and he is hunted. Bram is the crew's founder, who has abandoned his own people. Cass is a violent member who stays at Bram's side. The player knows the lead is dying of a failing heart.

## What happens
The lead and Ada drive to the fish quarter. The lead climbs the cold store roof and fires a rifle at hired men closing on Ada until she is taken. The lead goes into the quarter alone and fights through the lanes to the back room, where two guards are shot in quick succession. Inside, an attacker nearly overpowers the lead until Bess strikes him down. The lead drives out with both women, chased, then drives alone along a coast road at dawn. At the founder's hide-out, the hired men are attacking. The lead finds Abel and they run through a disused tram depot toward the vehicles. The lead's old car is shot up and the lead says goodbye to it. Cass appears, and the lead must choose between going back for the money and helping Abel escape.

## The lines that matter
The lead tells Abel to go home to his family and make something of what is left. Bram, asked for help, says he has nothing more to give and walks away. Cass tells the lead he is finished, weak and slow, and seems pleased. The lead says Abel was always worth the trouble.

## What the player does
The player covers Ada from the roof, fights alone through the quarter, escapes by car, and runs through the depot with Abel. The final choice is the money or Abel. Taking the money ends in a knife fight the lead loses. Helping Abel ends in a fist fight the lead also loses. The lead dies either way.

## Reference 4

## Setting
A kitchen-fitting firm's showroom and warehouse, and a large house with a long lawn in a wealthy suburb, on a bright morning. A household is going about its day.

## People
The lead is a young man who runs errands for a boss he does not respect, to work off an old favour. Abel owns the firm and bullies his staff and customers. Bram is a customer unsure about an instalment contract. Cass is a middle-aged robber hiding in a lorry. Ada is the lady of the house. Dorran is her piano tutor, and Ezra is her cook. The player already knows the lead wants a better life than this.

## What happens
In the showroom Abel presses Bram to sign the contract, then sends the lead to bring back the firm's delivery lorry from a customer's house. The lead goes over the side wall, knocks out the cook and crosses the grounds. Inside he passes the household, who are arguing, chatting and doing crosswords, and a lesson that is more flirtation than music. Anyone who sees him raises the alarm.

He finds the lorry in the garage and starts back. Cass, who was lying among the crates in the back, puts a gun to him through the hatch and tells him to keep driving. Cass believes the firm cheats customers on its contracts, and the lead agrees Abel is responsible. At the warehouse Cass tells the lead to drive through the loading doors. Abel is stunned. Cass pays the lead and sends him off, then pins Abel against the lorry with its door and tells him never to cheat him again.

## The lines that matter
Abel calls the lead his apprentice and tells him to get on with the house. Cass tells the lead to keep both hands on the wheel. Cass says he will deal with Abel himself. He tells Abel that if he has to come back, it will be worse.

## What the player does
The player moves the lead unseen through the house and garage, then drives to the warehouse as ordered. A detour fails the mission. The player drives through the doors on Cass's order, and the lead and Cass part ways.

## Reference 5

## Setting
A rain-dark coastal city, then a flooding dry dock beneath the shipyard and a chapel. The lead's career and marriage are gone.

## People
Ada is a newspaper columnist the lead has wronged and who now wants the saboteurs stopped. Abel is the lead's partner on the building-fraud desk. Bram is the lead's old friend from a war abroad, now working against the corrupt. Cass is a former engineer from the lead's old unit, who brings the buildings down. Dorran is the head of the planning board, who is part of the plot. The player knows the lead once gave an order in the war that he has never forgiven himself for.

## What happens
Cass grabs Ada after a surveyor is attacked in his office. The lead and Abel work through a list of contractors, then search the office and Cass's room, finding a cutting rig, knotted wire charms and photographs from the war. Bram calls for a meeting, but the board head has ordered him killed. The lead helps Bram reach the shipyard in a car chase under fire. They split up in the dock. The lead fights corrupt guards, crosses a flooding floor along a gantry, and uses the rig at close range. In a pump house he finds Ada and Cass. A flashback shows the lead ordering a convoy of refugees' trucks blown at a bridge in the war. Bram deals with Cass, and the lead saves Ada, then dies when the sluice gives way. At the funeral, the men who ruined him praise him. Ada speaks and walks out.

## The lines that matter
Ada tells the lead she knows what he did. Cass says the lead made him what he is. Bram tells the lead to get Ada out and leave the rest to him. Dorran, at the chapel, calls the lead a good man and a credit to the service.

## What the player does
The player follows leads, searches rooms, drives and shoots through the chase, fights in the dock, and tries to persuade Cass to put down his weapon. The player then sees the funeral.

## Reference 6

## Setting
A district of low terraces just after a family retirement party, then a goods yard on the edge of the city, a railway line through the hills and a quarry with a tall water tower.

## People
Abel is the lead's friend, who phones with news. Bram is the lead's cousin, who rides along. Cass is the target, an older criminal boss with armed men. Ada was the lead's girlfriend, killed at the party, and is spoken of only by others.

## What happens
Right after the party Abel phones the lead. He and Bram have found some of Cass's men in a garage. The lead joins them in a car and they follow the men. The long chase ends at the goods yard. The lead fights inside and reaches Cass, who blames him for Ada's death and says things would have gone better if the lead had worked with him. Cass runs to the tracks, leaves guards behind to be killed, and climbs aboard a freight train. The lead chases along the line in a stolen car. Abel and Bram arrive in a heavy truck and the lead leaps aboard from a bridge. They drive alongside the train and Abel fires into the engine. It catches fire, but a loose girder thrown from a wagon also hits the truck, and both end up in the quarry. Cass runs beneath the water tower. The lead finds him and kills him at close range.

## The lines that matter
Cass says the lead caused Ada's death and that working together would have gone better. As the credits start, the lead says the thing he wanted has come. In a call afterwards, a former ally says Ada should not have died, and Bram says he is moving away from the city.

## What the player does
The lead drives in the chase, fights through the yard, drives the stolen car, times the jump onto the truck and shoots Cass. Missing the jump, losing Cass's men or wrecking the car ends the mission early. Abel and Bram handle the truck and its gun.

## Reference 7

## Setting
A boat ramp on the coast, the open water off a marshy estuary, and the deck of a large cargo barge under way. It is the middle of the day.

## People
The lead is an unstable criminal in hiding with his old partner, Abel. Bram is the lead's nervous associate. Cora is a rich man's wife whom the lead has kidnapped and who is held in a rented house. Cass is the captain of a private haulage company's barge. The player already knows the lead and Abel are lying low and that the company is dangerous.

## What happens
Bram arrives at the rented house and tells the lead that the haulage company is moving a load of guns by barge. Abel stays to watch Cora. The lead and Bram go to the ramp, where the lead takes a small fast boat and runs low along the shore to stay out of sight.

Cass tells him by radio to turn away. The lead ignores him. The crew fire flares and rifles from the rail. The lead ties off beside the barge and climbs a hoist cable to the cargo deck. Inside he fights the guards using trucks and sliding crates as cover and kills Cass in the wheelhouse. He takes the wheel. Two patrol launches order him to steer into a restricted naval base. The lead refuses and they shoot out an engine. He leaves the barge by jumping clear, and it runs aground on a sandbank.

## The lines that matter
Abel tells a friend that the lead has taken a hostage. The lead tells Cora he may have to kill her, and she says she values his honesty. Bram tells the lead where the guns are. The captain says the lead is in a restricted zone and must turn back, and the lead does not answer.

## What the player does
The player drives the small boat low, climbs aboard by the hoist cable, shoots the guards, steers the large barge and bails out by jumping before it goes down.

## Reference 8

## Setting
A tall private surgery on the edge of the city, with a generator room in the basement, a service lift in the next building, an operating suite and a last chamber at the top.

## People
Ada is the surgery's owner, who is finishing a procedure that will erase her face, her records and her trail for good, so that she can never be brought to account. The player knows she did terrible things to get here. Bess is an ally who watches by video call and wants Ada stopped. Guards defend the building.

## What happens
A message from Ada's former driver tells the lead where Ada is. The lead enters with a stolen pass and takes control of the generator room. A lift in the neighbouring building leads to the operating suite, where Ada is beginning the final stage. Bess says that once it finishes Ada will be beyond reach, so it must be stopped. The lead cuts the left and right power feeds in turn, then fights off an ambush by guards. Ada appears on wall screens and tries to talk the lead out of it. In the last chamber Bess on a screen and Ada in person argue with each other, and the lead must decide Ada's fate. Ada is wired to the equipment, so if the lead cuts it she dies and Bess is glad. If the lead lets it run, Bess is angry and hangs up. Afterwards she apologises and still stays on the lead's side. The news report that follows differs with the choice.

## The lines that matter
Bess tells the lead to stop the procedure before it finishes. Ada pleads with the lead to leave the equipment alone. Ada says that whatever the lead decides, neither she nor Bess can change it, and the choice is the lead's. Bess, if the procedure is allowed to finish, swears at the lead and ends the call.

## What the player does
The lead takes the generator room, cuts the two feeds and fights through guards. The player then makes a single choice: end Ada or spare her.

## Reference 9

## Setting
A farming district beyond the city, hit by a bad harvest. The scene moves between a roadside cafe, a neighbouring hamlet, a gang's squat in a derelict factory at night, and a cottage in a third village.

## People
Ada is a woman who wants a lucky pendant to ease a hard run of years. Abel is a cafe owner who sends the lead to her. Bram holds the pendant and will only part with it in a fight. Cass and Dorran are farmhands who took it. Ezra is a gang leader. Bess owns the pendant now, and Fenn is her sick husband. The player already knows the lead takes jobs for strangers.

## What happens
Abel sends the lead to Ada, who needs the pendant. Bram will not sell it, and after a fist fight he admits it was stolen and names two farmhands. Cass says he and Dorran took it to give themselves courage against a gang led by Ezra. Ezra captured Dorran. The lead sneaks into the squat at night, frees Dorran after dealing with a guard, and kills Ezra in his sleep. Ezra had already sold the pendant. Dorran says it was traced to Bess in another village. Bess spent all her family's savings on it and believes it cured her husband Fenn. The lead finds that Fenn is not ill in body but afraid. A hard persuasion attempt lets the lead talk him back to his feet. Bess gives up the pendant and the lead brings it to Ada. It is not magical.

## The lines that matter
- Cass: "I am not worried for myself. I am worried for Bess."
- Bess: "I gave every penny we had for it."
- The lead, to Fenn: "Do not run from the fight. Least of all the one in your head."
- Ada: "It is only a trinket, but I feel better."

## What the player does
The player chooses to talk, fight, steal or sneak at each lead, and decides how to treat the farmhands. The persuasion attempt with Fenn changes whether he recovers.

## Reference 10

## Setting
A rented flat above a shop, then the takings office of a ferry company on a busy quay in daylight, then a rail goods yard behind the harbour.

## People
Abel leads the crew and wants the money taken without losing any of his own; the lead knows him as a friend who gives him work. Bram is his brother and argues easily. Cass is a young crew member. Dorran is a clerk. The player knows the crew trusts the lead to drive.

## What happens
At the flat, Abel tells the lead to put on tradesman's overalls and find a car with room for four. At the ticket office the crew orders the staff to the floor. Dorran, who looked like a customer, turns out to be armed. Bram and Abel argue, Cass is shot dead, and Abel and Bram kill Dorran in return. The crew cuts open the strongroom and the lead fills the bags. Police cars arrive in numbers, and Abel orders everyone out. After a gunfight at the doors and a run across three streets, Abel leads the crew over a fence into the goods yard, where an engine shunts on one line. They pick a path between standing wagons and come out at a depot gate. The lead steals a car, loses the police, and drives Abel and Bram to their father's workshop. The money is split four ways, the last share going to Cass's sister.

## The lines that matter
- Abel, at the flat: "Wear the overalls and get a car that takes the four of us."
- Abel, in the office: "Leave now, with whatever is in the bags."
- Abel, once clear, with some pride: "We stayed calm."
- Abel: "Take me and Bram to the workshop, to Dad."

## What the player does
The lead drives, helps rob the office, shoots a way out, picks a path through the yard clear of the engine, then steals a car and loses the police. The job fails if Abel, Bram or the lead dies, too many staff are shot, or the money is destroyed.

## Reference 11

## Setting
A lane beside a ferry terminal at night, outside a small cafe, then a morgue, a house and a bus station.

## People
Abel is the lead's partner on the road-collision unit. Bram is a dead man lying in the lane. Ada is a witness who was outside the cafe. Cass is the cafe's owner. Bess is the dead man's wife. Dorran is the dead man's business partner. Ezra is the driver of the van that struck him. The player knows the lead is new to the unit and wants to be thorough.

## What happens
The lead and Abel are called to what looks like a hit and run. They gather a life-cover letter, a wallet, tyre marks, drag marks in the blood and drops of blood. A filleting knife may be found in a skip behind the cafe. Ada describes a green van and an argument, and she points to the wife. Cass admits he knew the dead man, mentions an argument, points to Dorran and hints at a business deal. The plate leads to Ezra. Bess denies everything and lies about the partnership, and the life-cover letter contradicts her. Ezra runs and the lead chases him to a bus station. At the morgue the coroner says the dead man was already dead when the van hit him, and had been stabbed. The case then turns on the knife. Dorran shoots Bess and runs, or is found at the cafe, and the lead chases him.

## The lines that matter
Ada says the dead man shouted at his wife that night. Cass says the dead man and Dorran were planning something together. Bess says she and Dorran have no deal. The lead tells her the letter says otherwise, and she goes quiet. The coroner says the van did not kill him.

## What the player does
The player collects the clues, chooses the order of visits, puts truth, doubt or accusation to each person, chases Ezra, and decides whether a warning shot or a kill ends the final chase.

## Reference 12

## Setting
A wooded lorry park, then a burning cold store held by private security men, then a farmyard where a family firm loads vans to move. The lead carries an old wound that never healed.

## People
Abel is the lead's gang boss and wants the deed box in the store office. Bram is a gang member who is eager for a fight. Cass heads a family firm of hauliers whose people the security men have killed. Dorran is his father and begs him to stay out of the feud. Ada and Ezra are the lead's allies in the fight, and Fenn is an elder of the firm. The player knows the lead has begun to doubt Abel.

## What happens
Cass asks the gang to drive with him to the store. Dorran begs his son to stay home, since the feud killed his wife and his elder son, and asks the lead to help. The lead agrees. At the store the security men have set fires. The lead clears them from a car, then on foot, against a man on a roof walkway and a rifle mounted on a pickup. The lead saves Cass on a stair and keeps Fenn alive. The lead and Abel take the deed box from the office. On the way out a falling beam hits the lead and the security men close in. The lead calls to Abel, who looks and walks off. Cass saves the lead and is shot himself. The lead shoots the man and takes Cass to the farmyard, where he dies. Ezra tells the lead to leave. The lead collapses on the road and strangers in a passing van help him.

## The lines that matter
Dorran says his son will end like his wife and elder son. The lead tells Abel that he left him behind. Abel says he did no such thing. Ezra tells the lead to go and help those who still need him.

## What the player does
The player fights through the store, protects Fenn, and takes the deed box.

## Reference 13

## Setting
A large hall above a restaurant near the harbour, on the evening of an old crew man's retirement dinner. White cloths, bright lamps and a seated crowd. Guests are chatting quietly until a noise comes from the stairs.

## People
The lead is a reporter secretly passing a crew's business to a newspaper while working as its driver. Abel is the host, the lead's closest friend in the crew. Ada is his wife, who has welcomed the lead warmly. Bram is a senior crew elder. Rival crew members attack the room. The player already knows the lead likes this couple and is lying to them.

## What happens
The lead buys a tie at a shop and arrives at the hall. He goes out to the car to fetch a box of presents, and screams come from inside. He finds the stairs guarded, climbs in at the fire escape and fights the attackers in the restaurant below.

He takes a gun from one of them and uses it on the rest. Further in, he finds Abel and Ada on the floor, bleeding on the white cloths and her green dress. Abel is dying. He tells the lead to get Ada out because she does not deserve this. The lead then clears the balcony and the main hall of attackers, collecting their weapons as he goes. Afterwards he finds Bram waiting behind a door, unhurt, and the mission continues with Bram's escape.

## The lines that matter
Abel, dying, asks the lead to take Ada away. Abel says she did nothing to deserve this. Ada, earlier, tells the lead she is glad he is there. Bram says nothing of his own plans.

## What the player does
The player buys the tie, fights through the restaurant, takes a gun by disarming a man, clears the balcony and hall, and speaks to the dying host.

## Reference 14

## Setting
A smallholding yard and a quiet lane in the farmland beyond the city, in the middle of a hot afternoon.

## People
Abel and Bram, two partners in a robbery, appear in a flat far away. Cass, a detective from a national crime unit, visits them. The lead is a volatile local criminal who runs a dealing business. Ada is the lead's girlfriend. Dorran is a scrap merchant and the lead's rival. Ezra and Fenn are the lead's nervous hangers-on. The player already knows the lead believes his old partner is dead.

## What happens
Abel and Bram talk about the money from a ferry terminal robbery. Cass arrives, says he knows Abel is alive, and warns that the lead is dangerous. A news report mentions the robbery. Abel denies all.

Far away, the lead leaves Ada's house. Dorran arrives angry and is held back by Ezra and Fenn. The lead mocks him and kills him in a fight with a car door. He then says he has seen a ghost in a newspaper photograph and names his old partner.

The lead sends Fenn to the city to look for him. At the smallholding two of Dorran's drivers confront the lead and flee when he taunts them. He chases them, shoots them down and attacks their yard. He sends Fenn for tools and drives with Ezra to a rival's mobile home, which he drags into the quarry pool. The rival begs for mercy and the player decides his fate. Ezra is nervous either way. The lead says he will take over the county, and Ezra doubts it.

## The lines that matter
Cass tells Abel the lead is not a man who stays patient. The lead tells Dorran he has no claim on Ada, and laughs when Dorran is pulled away. After seeing the photograph, the lead tells Ezra that his old partner is alive and owes him answers. Ezra says the lead is going to get them all killed.

## What the player does
The player drives the lead through the chase, shoots the drivers, drags the mobile home and chooses whether to spare the rival. The lead's search for his old partner is now under way.

## Reference 15

## Setting
A cold stretch of coast in midwinter. The scene begins in a rented workshop above a quay, moves out across a salt marsh at night, and ends in a derelict ice factory standing on a frozen quay.

## People
Ada is a clever outsider who wants to undo an old tie that joins her to the lead, and she is slow to say so. Abel, a hypnotherapist who ran sessions for them both, is already dead. Bram, his old partner, is a hard man guarding what Abel left. The player already knows the lead and Ada were close once and have been apart a long time.

## What happens
Ada asks the lead to meet her at the workshop. She says Abel has vanished and that he kept a recording she wants. They cross the marsh on foot, and the lead wades through channels with mud and rusted wire working against him. At a pump station he finds half of a torn ticket stub. They move to the ice factory. Before they go in, the lead asks why she wants the recording. She says the sessions long ago tied them together and she needs to know whether what they feel is real or planted. Inside the cold rooms the lead finds Abel crushed under fallen racking and joins the two halves of the stub. On the loading floor Ada confronts Bram. He is too strong for her, and the lead fights him. Ada pins him and offers him his freedom in exchange for undoing the tie. He agrees and speaks the words. She waits to feel a change and feels none.

## The lines that matter
- Ada: "I have to know whether any of it was ever ours, or only the sessions."
- Ada, afterwards: "I expected something to shift. Nothing has. It is all as it was."
- The lead, depending on the player: "I still want you," or "I think we should stop."

## What the player does
The player wades, searches and fights Bram, then picks the lead's answer: he still loves her, he wants to part, or he stays distant. That decides whether they reunite or settle for friendship.

## Reference 16

## Setting
A large house in a wealthy district, empty for years, then a sealed basement flat at dusk, fitted like an old family sitting room.

## People
Ada is the absent owner, who runs a private clinic keeping dying patients on machines against their wishes. Abel is a voice on the lead's phone. Bess is Ada's mother, who is old and weak. Cora is an ally who wants Ada stopped. The player knows Ada has hurt many people.

## What happens
The lead breaks in, puts down one guard, and forces the door. A friendly, crackling voice on an intercom mistakes the lead for Ada's staff and sends the lead below. A shed key starts the lift. The voice sends the lead to watch tapes in the sitting room, and a watch in a drawer has the door code engraved on its back. The tapes show Bess refusing Ada's treatment, having seen what it did to the family dog. In the garage the lead finds the dog caged and wired to a drip, whining, and switches off the drip. Another tape shows Ada injecting Bess against her will. In the cellar the lead finds kidnapped people asleep in rows of beds. The voice plays Ada's log: after twenty-nine days Ada kept her mother alive in the sealed room, speaking only through the intercom. The voice offers Ada's location if the lead switches off the machines. The lead does, and Abel receives the location and a thank-you. Upstairs, Cora admits she went down earlier and could not do it.

## The lines that matter
- Bess, on a tape: "This is not my home, and I do not agree to it."
- Ada, in her log: "My mother stays with me now."
- The voice: "I will tell you where she is, if you let me go."
- Cora: "Stop the clinic before thousands more suffer."

## What the player does
The lead picks locks, finds door codes, watches tapes and switches off the dog's drip. The only choice is the voice's bargain, which ends her.

## Reference 17

## Setting
A busy coastal city in late autumn. The scene moves through a hospital ward, a records office, the old quarter, a bus depot and a derelict ferry terminal.

## People
Abel is a bookkeeper and the lead's friend. He wants his business partner Ada, who lies injured, to live. Bram is a nurse who notices odd wounds. Cass is the council's housing officer, who hands out files and favours freely. Dorran is a stern charity director. Ezra cleans buses. The player knows Abel trusts too easily.

## What happens
Abel hears that Ada was attacked in a lane and taken to the hospital. Bram says her wounds match those of a woman he nursed earlier, who died. The lead reads that woman's file in the records office. Cass mentions a cleaner who sees everything, and Dorran tells the lead to stay out. The lead searches the lane, then a flat where a tailor was killed. Ezra talks if flattered or paid, and says the killer chalks a mark on each victim's door the day before. A new mark appears on a businesswoman's door. Bess is dead when the lead reaches her, and the police blame him. A second mark appears on another woman's door. At a house across the city the lead finds Dorran hurting Cora. Dorran says only Cass knew of his dealings and names the terminal. There Cass admits he has killed for years, gives his reasons and fights with a spanner. Afterwards Abel talks of working again with Ada.

## The lines that matter
- Bram: "I have seen these marks before, on someone who did not survive."
- Dorran: "Ask the housing officer. Whatever happens in this city, he knows of it."
- Abel, at the end: "She will be back at her desk, and I will be at the next one."

## What the player does
The player follows clues and chooses whether to talk to Dorran, fight him, or spare him. The player can kill Dorran and treat the case as closed, missing the truth about Cass, or keep going to the terminal.

## Reference 18

## Setting
A haulage yard at the edge of the docks, the same yard where the lead found his first work when he came to the city years before.

## People
Abel is the target, a man the lead once trusted who betrayed him; he is sending a load of lorries out of the city tonight. Armed guards crowd the yard. Bram is the lead's cousin, who hears of it afterwards. The lead wants Abel dead.

## What happens
Abel tells his men to load the lorries because they leave tonight. The guards tell the lead to turn round and say Abel does not want to see him. The lead keeps walking, and they say they have to kill him. He climbs the side steps, takes cover on a loading deck and shoots a guard on a high stack of containers.
