## Setting
The yard behind a cab office after eleven on a cold, dry night. Brick walls on three sides, a double gate to the street, and a plank door to a back lane where a van idles. The lamp has been unscrewed and set down. The mood is closed.

## People
Abel is a hired man the lead once let down over money, and he holds a wheel brace. Bram is a man the lead struck when he was down. Cass is a rival's man who has come for the office board. Dorran is the doorman, loyal to the lead. Ezra is a young driver who sleeps in the spare car. Ada watches from her window. Bess works the night desk. Fenn is a driver.

## What happens
The lead pushed the gate and found four men waiting, each naming a wrong he had done them. A fifth carried the board out of the office; another had the cash tin. The lead fought them with fists. Men he put down stayed down, and he could hit them again. Dorran came through the office, took the board back and stood it against the wall, then fought at the lead's back. Ezra climbed out of the car in his socks with a starting handle. Ada's light came on, and Bess answered the phone and called every car to the yard. Fenn arrived inside two minutes with a wrench. When Abel lifted the brace, Dorran took the blow on his forearm. The lead stayed on his feet, and Dorran rehung the board.

## The lines that matter
Abel says he has the lead's change, for the money owed. Bram says the lead should have given him his cap back. Ezra says he lives here. Bess calls all cars to the yard and tells Fenn it is not a fare.

## What the player does
The lead fights, and may hit a man who is down. He may take the iron himself and lose the use of an arm, or let another man take it. He may chase the tin alone, leave it, or run. He cannot die, but he can lose.
