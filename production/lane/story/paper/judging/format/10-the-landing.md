## Setting
A boat landing on a cold autumn night. The whole town stands along the quay wall for a firework display from a barge. The tide is low, and the ramp is steep and wet. The mood is crowded and loud.

## People
Ada keeps the books. Abel is the doorman, who has had four driving lessons and wants to prove himself. Bess works the radio. Bram is a hired man of a rival boss; the lead once spared him. Cora sells food on the quay. The lead wants the rival's load off the landing.

## What happens
That afternoon the lead laid out the plan on the office counter in tea things, with Ada, Abel and Bess watching. At night the last firework broke, the boat came in, and two of the rival's men started a loaded cage up the ramp. The lead met them halfway. Bram, remembering the earlier favour, let go and walked off. The other man fought and slid to the foot of the ramp. The lead held the cage alone while it crept back. A learner's car came slowly along the quay through the crowd, with Abel calling out landmarks. He tied down the boot over four cases, and the lead got in. Their pursuer walked beside the car with a hand on the roof and showed a gun in his belt as a warning. Cora stepped into the road. At the yard Abel stopped in three goes and said they had left the landing.

## The lines that matter
Ada says the stapler is hers, so the milk jug will be the van. Abel calls out each landmark as the car passes it. The rival's man says he shows the gun so the lead cannot say he was not shown. Abel says "on mine" and the two of them lift.

## What the player does
The lead holds the cage, and may go back for the cartons. He chooses whether to stop for Cora. Afterwards he pays Abel a night's cut or leaves his wage as it stands, and names who will stand on the landing at first light.
