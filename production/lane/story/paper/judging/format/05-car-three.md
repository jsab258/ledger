## Setting
A street beside the harbour at about eleven on a dry grey morning, with a wind off the water. A white hatchback is parked across the cab rank outside the office. The mood is public and watchful.

## People
Abel is a quick young rival who wants the lead's rank; the lead has heard he has read the office's book. Bram is his heavy, who waits on a wall. Cass is the office doorman, loyal to the lead. Ada keeps the books, and the rent is due tomorrow.

## What happens
Abel's car blocked the rank with the keys in it. He sat on the office counter eating breakfast and reading the day book, and told the lead he had come to be run. Asked to move it, he said he was having his breakfast. The lead got in. Bram started across the road; Abel told him to sit and watch. Cass walked beside the car with a hand on its roof, then ahead of it, and at the quay edge turned and beckoned the lead on with both palms. The front wheels went over the edge stones. Cass closed his fist, the lead braked, and the car hung over the water. The lead took the keys, hung them on the spare hook, and chalked a third car on the board. Ada charged a fare for the time stood on the rank. Abel laughed, left a note, and sent Bram for the car. Bram sat in it and spun the wheels in the air.

## The lines that matter
Abel says the last owner would have asked nicely for a fortnight. The lead says it was on his rank, so it goes on his board. Ada asks whether Abel wants a receipt. Abel says to call it an account, because he will be back on that rank.

## What the player does
The lead may talk and leave the car, drive it round the corner, or drive it to the edge on Cass's signal. If he drives through the fist the car goes in the water and Abel moves against him early. He may also take, change or refuse the note.
