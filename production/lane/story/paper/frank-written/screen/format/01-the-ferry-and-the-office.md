## Setting
A harbour city on a grey, damp morning a little after nine. A ferry has just docked. The street up from the water is quiet and the curtains stay shut. A taxi office stands near the quay.

## People
The lead is a young man in a new black suit who has come to take over a taxi firm left by his uncle, Abel, now buried. Nobody met him off the boat. Bram takes tickets at the landing. Cass paints a window above the street. Dorran is a big driver in the second car. Ada keeps the books. Bess runs the radio. Cora needs a ride to the boat or loses a job.

## What happens
Bram told the lead the office door had the dead man's name on it. Cass called him the nephew before he gave his name. At the office Ada finished her line before she looked up. The lead asked if the photograph was his uncle. She said it was, that the funeral was three weeks gone, and that Dorran would walk him to the graveyard. Bess was on the telephone with Cora, who needed a car for the boat. The lead took the second car and put Dorran beside him. Dorran pointed the way with a flat hand while Bess counted minutes aloud. They found Cora at the end of a narrow lane of back yards. The lead reversed the whole lane, stopped short of a lorry and reached the boat with two minutes left. Back at the office he laid out the money he had arrived with.

## The lines that matter
Ada: "You are three weeks late for him, love. He is not going anywhere."
The lead: "He is not coming for the car. I am."
The lead, later: "Nobody came down for me."
Dorran: "Nobody said which boat. I would have walked down."
Ada, over the cash: "I am not keeping it. I am writing down that it was there."

## What the player does
The player picks the car over the graveyard, drives where Dorran points, then reverses out alone against the clock. The graveyard waits, and rent is due Friday.
