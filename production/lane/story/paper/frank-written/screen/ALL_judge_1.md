At the quay Ezra asked the hearse whose man it was, and the lead answered for the dead man. In the cafe Dorran scored the funeral. Bess gave the lead the dead man's things, including a pencil torch. The lead could not remove his coat with one arm. Ada undid it after he asked. He stood the torch on the cloth, and each boss bid for the landing it stood for. Bram offered a renewed licence, Ada no tax and cover at the gate, Cass a place in the queue. Abel left a coin and mentioned the inspector.

Ezra: "Whose man?"
The lead: "The old firm's."
Ada: "Leave him. He can ask."
Bess: "He had my window table thirty years and I never had a name to put on the slip."

--- SCENE 2
## Setting
A flat above a shop in the old quarter, where an older woman gives work to people she trusts, then a lorry park in daylight where a deal is about to be made. The roof of a nearby building overlooks the spot.

## People
Ada runs the flat and wants a stranger to watch a deal she does not trust. Abel is a crew member buying something from people he does not know; the lead knows him from earlier work. Bess is Ada's friend, who brings the lead and vouches for him. Bram is Ada's doorman.

## What happens
Ada tells a story of how she once put a man in his place, and Abel makes a crude remark. Bram greets the group. Bess introduces the lead as her brother's friend and vouches for him. Ada, an old friend of Bess, offers the lead work and asks whether he knows protection work. He says he does. She needs a face nobody knows to oversee a purchase she distrusts, and offers the lead as extra cover. Abel accepts. Outside, Abel sends the lead to a rifle position on the roof beside the meeting spot, to watch for trouble while Abel and his crew arrive. The lead asks why Abel trusts him, and Abel says he has a good feeling. - Ada: "Do you know protection work?"

- Ada: "I need someone nobody knows."
- Abel: "I have a good feeling about you."
- If an attacker escapes, Abel blames the lead, who says he was busy keeping Abel alive.

The lead goes to the rooftop and holds fire until the exchange begins. He then shoots attackers who go for Abel and stops anyone escaping. The job fails if he fires early, if Abel dies, or if an attacker gets away.

--- SCENE 2
## Setting
The coast and farmland around the city. The job starts at a notice board outside a harbour-side shop, beside a large estate, and takes the lead to a quarry in the hills, then far south to a farm, and then to a stretch of shore lined with wrecked boat hulls. It is windy.
