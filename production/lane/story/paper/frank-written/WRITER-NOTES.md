# Writer's notes: a written Frank on five scenes

24 November 2026. The five run 2,269 to 2,288 words.

## Frank's want

To be somebody's own on the Hook: the man who turns up when he is sent for, with one person who would come down for him. Nobody met his boat, and he missed the one funeral that was his.
