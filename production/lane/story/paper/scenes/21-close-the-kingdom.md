# 21. Close: the Kingdom

- Where it sits: the close, Saturday 1 December, from a quarter past five until he sleeps. The Harbour Board chains the old dock gate tonight in every ending.
- What wakes it: at five the line was left as it stood, and the books held. The firm survives.
- Who must be present: Pauline; Sheila, unless she has left; the man at Frank's shoulder (the first man, where Ron used to stand); Frank Stathers at the gate; Maureen Jensen. Ron, by path. Up to five more, each holding a deed of his at first hand. Fallback: if the Widow is gone, Stathers says the last name.

Frank is trying to walk home through a town that is his. He has already given what it cost; the close shows him the receipt.

## Staging

Quay Street under its lamps. The rank is full and the board is full. Over the door the paint is new: MICKEY'S, as it always said, and under it, if he told Shaun Hewson so, a small line with his own name, spelt right.

Three doors up, a shutter that once said CAMMACK in chalk a foot high says MICKEY'S. The only key it ever had is the Yale on the third hook.

On the counter is a spike, and on it a thick pad of pink carbon sheets. Nobody has carried one to the water in weeks. In the drawer beneath is a brown window envelope, soft as cloth, empty.

Down the dock road a Harbour Board lorry stands at the gate with its lamps on.

The camera walks with him, a little behind, as it did off the ferry.

## The scene

The office. Pauline is giving out fares faster than she can write them.

PAULINE: Car four, the Tivoli. Car six, boarding house. Car one, Fairview, and he says take the top road.

She does not say who "he" is. She has not needed to for a fortnight.

She tears the first pink sheet of the night along its perforation. She does not fold it. She lays it on the counter in front of Frank.

He reads it with one finger, from the top, lips moving. The finger stops two lines from the bottom.

FRANK: Chandler's. Landing. Ten past six.

The man at his shoulder nods and goes out. A car leaves the rank with its sign unlit. Frank puts the sheet on the spike.

Sheila writes the night's line in the red book. The camera can see the third column. It does not say R. Kirby tonight. It has a newer name, three times down the page, in the same small hand. She shuts the book and puts it in her bag.

SHEILA: Goodnight. (If she has ever said his name: Goodnight, Frank.)

The street. People on the parade step off the kerb for him and do not look up. Brenda puts a wrapped parcel into his hand, says, "You're never him that put Cammack's motor on the edge," as if it were long ago, and takes no money. At the takeaway Lily Tang has the order bagged before he reaches the hatch.

LILY: Number nine. (She counts.) One. (Or, if Ron is on the door: Two.) No charge, Mr Nowak.

She used to say that she remembered. She does not say it.

Then, as he passes, by people who were there:

- Darren, outside the phone box, hands in his pockets: "By Christmas, you said. It's the first of December. You're early."
- Sylvia Rennison, on her step, if he ever said it to her: "I'm not Mickey. That's what you told me."

The gate. Stathers passes the chain through both leaves himself and closes the lock. A man from the Harbour Board wires a notice to the ironwork. The pen stands under its tin roof with nobody in it.

At the post, in front of a gate that will not open again, Frank's man is standing.

THE MAN ON THE GATE: I'm stood here while you say different, Mr Nowak.

A man in a black coat and hat has walked down behind the lorry at a funeral's pace. He looks the gate over.

THE MAN IN BLACK: Five. No flowers, and done in the dark. (He turns and looks up the road at the lit sign over the rank.)

GRACE (from her step): And that one?

THE MAN IN BLACK (putting his hat on): I don't mark those.

Maureen Jensen has had a kitchen chair carried out to the cobbles to watch her gate chained. She is sitting on it in the fur collar with her bag on her knee. As Frank passes she does not turn her head.

THE WIDOW: Frankie.

It is the top of the ladder. She is the one who has said it.

Ron. What is left is one of three.

- He went in. The Kingdom opens a door for Frank that it opens for nobody else: an officer on the remand wing, at lock-up, walks him down the landing and drops a hatch in a steel door. A man is sitting on a bed in a black overcoat, a second blanket folded beside him. He looks at the hatch.

RON: Boss.

Nothing comes after it. The hatch is shut from the inside.

Frank drives home down the dock road. The ice house goes by on the left. The car is quiet.

- Frank signed. Ron is on the door at Mickey's, as every night. Sheila has left a paper on the counter: the magistrates, the tenth. He has the Hook, and a date. Ron holds the door.

RON: Boss.

The name is his to say now. He does not say it.

- The better turn. Ron was kept out a cheaper way; there is a friend who still calls (Ron himself, Sheila, Alison or Philip Danby); the street holds no racket of his as a fact; nobody was given to Ellis. Whoever sold the cheaper way is in the close, holding it: Agar's notice wired to the yard gate, for Lady Day; or Wardell on the customers' bench, waiting for a cab he will not be charged for; or a plain car at the kerb, engine running, a woman writing, because it is nearly Friday.

Ron drives him back from the gate, on the L-plates, naming nothing. Then, if Frank came for him at the pen and Mickey's watch is on his wrist:

RON: That's the ice house.

One building. He does not say it was coming, or that it has gone.

The last thing, in every turn, is the telephone in the office, and Pauline answering it.

PAULINE: Mickey's. (She listens.) He's in.

She does not ask who is wanted.

## What the player can do

- Walk it, in any order, or go to bed from the counter. Then the gate is chained without him, and the Widow says the name on Monday.
- Read the carbon and say the line. Sign: Pauline lays it in front of him and waits. If he will not, she spikes it herself, and the car stays on the rank. It is the last choice the game gives him.
- Pay Lily, or Brenda. They give it back as change.
- Go to the remand wing, or not. Not going, the last word between them is whatever was said at the visit.

## What the town remembers

- Everything; and from tonight nobody on the Hook tells him any of it to his face.
- The third column of the red book has a new name in it, and Sheila goes on writing it.

## Variants by path

- Danny was put back on the board under him. He is on the rank, third line, no arrow, eating. "Evening, Frankie." He is the second to say it.
- He is hunted for a killing. None of it is said to him. It is the same street with him not on it: Pauline to an empty counter, "He's not been in"; Lily with an order bagged and nobody to count it to; the Widow on her chair, to Stathers: "Frankie's away."
