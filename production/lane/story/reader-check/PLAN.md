With 18 to 43 reviews a game, a gap under about 0.6 cannot be told from chance. RDR2 is put alone at the top because the large study separates it plainly from GTA V and KCD2 (47% against 28% and 32%) and GTA V's hand sample is the smallest. Tiers (ties within a tier):

1. RDR2
2. GTA V, GTA IV, KCD2
3. Sleeping Dogs, Hitman WoA
4. Watch Dogs: Legion, L.A. Noire

That leaves 23 of the 28 pairs ordered; the 5 pairs inside a tier are not scored.

## The test, fixed now

Each reader ranks the eight disguised summaries 1 to 8. Per reader and pooled (the mean of the three ranks): Spearman's rank correlation against the tier ranks (ties as average ranks), and the count of the 23 ordered pairs the reader puts the same way round. A reader ranking at random scores about 0 and about 11 or 12 of 23.

The reader is fit to decide only if all three hold:
1. pooled Spearman at least 0.60;
2. pooled order agrees on at least 18 of the 23 pairs;
3. at least two of the three readers put the RDR2 summary first or second (the decision is about the top).

Reputation leak is measured separately: each reader says afterwards which games, if any, they believe they recognised.
