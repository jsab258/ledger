# Disguise rules (on top of FORMAT.md)

The summary will be read by judges who must not be able to tell which shipped game it is from reputation. So:

1. No real or in-game name of any person, place, organisation, gang, company, brand, ship, vehicle, game, studio or series. Use roles ("the lead", "his cousin", "the handler"). Where a place is needed, use an invented name or a plain description.
2. Move the setting to an invented equivalent of the same kind: a real or fictional city or country becomes an invented one of similar size, climate and character in a different part of the world or under a different flag; keep the rough era (the decade or century) only if it does not on its own point at the game; otherwise shift it a little. The social world (police, gangs, frontier, feudal war, intelligence agencies) stays the same kind.
3. Replace any well-known line, catchphrase, signature image, signature weapon, signature illness, tattoo, mark or set piece that a player of games would recognise with an equivalent that does the same job in the story (same cost, same meaning, same moment).
4. Keep the story itself whole: every beat, its order, who wants what, what it costs, who dies, the turn, every ending and what decides it, what the player's hands do, where the player chooses and where not. Change the surface, never the shape. Do not improve or weaken.
5. Follow FORMAT.md exactly: eight level-two headings in order, 1,900 to 2,300 words, present tense, British spelling, plain prose, no lists, no bold, no italics, no em-dashes, no judging words, no mention of performances, music, graphics, sales, awards or reviews.
