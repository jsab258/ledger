## Each system, and what the blueprint needs

### 1. The empire's stages and the name ladder: IN
Four rungs on exactly their holdings (the nephew, Nowak, Tom, the port's man); the town's names (the new owner, Nowak, Tom, Tommy), each heard within a game day, never going back; Sheila's exception. Needs: nothing for the story; the blueprint's own gaps stand (what earns "Nowak" before rung 2, rung 4's fleet size, NUMBERS-RUN's finding that rung 3 is out of reach in a five-week Act II). The frame PROPOSES who says "Tommy" (the Widow, Danny, Ron once): fills gap "who says Tommy undecided".

### 2. Felt progress: PARTLY
Has: rungs open doors (2.12). Needs: a rule that each rung change is said to Tom's face by a named person within a game day, Pauline's on-air name follows the ladder, and each rung adds one new kind of night job (the frame's week table). Small; mostly line banks and a trigger.

### 3. Rivals' ladders and the fail-safe: IN
Noticed, a visit, pressure, a threat, a sit-down; lifts as written; the highest rival climbs a rung a day from four game days before Act II ends. Needs: the rivals' lieutenants and men named (gap); the frame PROPOSES Frank Stathers (the Widow's), Craig Fewson (Danny's), Simon Ombler (Agar's solicitor), Mark Billany (a taxman).

### 4. Escalation dial: IN

### 5. Factions beyond the rivals: PARTLY
