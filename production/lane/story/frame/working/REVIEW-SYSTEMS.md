Line: "| 24 | The returning-people pool | MISSING | none (fates are told, 2.11.3; nothing casts them back) |"

Fault: the blueprint does bring people back. STORY-05 (decided, attached to 1.3.11, 2.10.1 and 2.10.2): "a rival's man meets Tom twice; every man he beat meets him within three game days". STORY-03 (decided, on 2.4.4, 2.5.1, 2.18, 7.6): "half the moments come from witnesses, recognition or a returning person". What is missing is the pool as save data and casting a beat's carrier from it, not the idea.

Fix: status PARTLY; home "STORY-05, STORY-03, MEM-07, MEM-04 (on 1.3.11, 2.10.1, 2.10.2); 3.7 (the helped stranger); gap 1.3.11 (a mark on the beaten man)". Body: "Has: every man he beat meets him within three game days; a rival's man meets him twice; witnesses can be met again; a helped stranger returns. ### M2. System 26, the yard: the home gets combat wrong, and the conflict with Tom's three states is not named

It is a change to a ruled entry (1.3.7 is ruled), not just an addition.

Fix: status PARTLY, with a CHANGE note. Home "1.3.9, 1.3.11, RT-11, RT2-11, 2.18". Needs: "a lasting arm injury as a new state beside 1.3.7's three (ruled: needs his yes or the keeper's, as the status says), with its days and its effect on fighting and driving; FF-04 (a cost shows and has an end) holds."
