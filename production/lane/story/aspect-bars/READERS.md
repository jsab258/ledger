## Their standards, in short (the best game's)

- A1: the lead's want is tested until it breaks; a fixed cost turns want into deed; midway he changes aim while doing the same work.
- A2: each companion wants his own thing, shows it by action, and moves where the lead stands.
- A3: one relationship at the centre from the first scene, through doubt, breach and a last settled state that is the ending's verdict.
- A4: the opponent comes from inside the bond, beside a visible usurper; both faced in person, neither removed off stage.
- A5: subtext and dry reversals in short lines; small strands planted and paid off.
- A6: losses prepared over hours; each changes how the group behaves afterwards.
- A7: satire and character comedy through main and side missions, tone steady beside violence.
- A8: the turn makes the player reread scenes; signs placed in plain sight; points at the wrong person until it lands.
- A9: follows from who the lead has become; bond settled in the last scene; a quiet earned last image; epilogue carries the cost.
- A10: each stretch changes the situation; no stretch cuttable; every named person settled.
- A11: story decisions are actions under the player's hands; key scenes played, not watched.
- A12: small early choices recorded and returned as named consequences and a different ending.
- A13: optional strands with their own arcs, turns and endings that echo the theme.
- A14: the period's change drives the plot; daily life played, not described.
- A15: the player searches, reads faces, backs an accusation with evidence; wrong calls come back.
