**Overall mark: 8.** Every part of this story is tied to the same idea: finding your way in a place that no longer matches its map, and being carried versus carrying. The central bond with the doorman moves through about six distinct phases. The lessons, the receipts and the badge number are planted early and paid late. The reversal at the gate boss's hut recasts the whole investigation. Choices feed a visible count that shapes three different endings. Its weakness is a quiet one. Much of the drama is small-scale, and a player wanting set-piece action gets lessons and dawn runs. The finale also takes the controls away at the moment of highest tension.

**What holds it back**
1. The climax gives "no new choice, only lines the record has earned." After a heavily player-driven game, the final drive is mostly watched.
2. In a taxi story, the opening weeks are "a fifth of it driving." The routine round risks feeling like chores before week three's mystery starts.
3. The hustler stays thin. He is a running gag until his invented jobs pay off late, with little want shown beyond "a seat in the car."

**Overall mark: 6.** This is a solid, weighty story. It has a clear want, a father-son bond that visibly sours, and a strong ending image. Its side strands are memorable: the clockwork bird and the veteran's horse. But it moves along the path its premise announces. The loud newcomer betrays the gang, the leader's last scores fail, and the loyal man dies. Around the leader, the cast is described more than shown. For a player who quits slack middles, the middle "fills most of the story." It detours through a feud and an island whose fighting goes nowhere, and after the lead's death a long epilogue of fencing and chopping follows.

**What holds it back**
1. It is predictable. The recruit's betrayal and the gang's collapse can be read from the setup, so the reveal recasts little.
2. The supporting cast is mostly labels ("loud and violent", "quiet, principled", "capable"), with few deeds only that person would do.
3. The middle is long and episodic, with the island stretch's rebel war having no bearing afterward.

H's supporting people each act in ways only they would, against K's labelled companions. H's choices also visibly return to shape endings, while K's middle carries a long detour that leads nowhere.
