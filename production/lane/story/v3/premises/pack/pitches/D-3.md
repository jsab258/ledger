In a cold lakeside city of an invented federation around 2013, one private system runs the lights, cameras, bridges and phones, and those who sold it and the gangs who use it trade in everyone's secrets. The lead, a hacker and thief, was traced after a hotel job, and a hitman's attack on his car killed his young niece; he wants whoever ordered it and means to keep his sister and her son safe. His former partner kidnaps the sister to force his help; a young woman hacker joins him; a fixer hires them; the engineer who built the system hides. The player's hands profile strangers by phone, hack lights, barriers and cameras to win chases and slip past guards, drive, sneak and shoot, a reputation rising or falling with how bystanders are treated. At the turn the lead learns that an old crime boss who blackmails the mayor ordered the hit, and that the woman hacker helped find him for it. He kills the gang leader whose servers hold the evidence, frees his sister and sends her and her son away, then kills the old boss. His former partner turns the system against the city, and the woman hacker is shot dead at the niece's grave. The lead kills his former partner. At the end the player decides whether to kill or spare the hitman who fired on the car, and the lead stays in the city, watching.
