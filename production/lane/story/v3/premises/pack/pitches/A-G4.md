In a vast, cold harbour city in a rich federation, a little after the turn of the century, each borough is held by its own criminal community. The lead, a former soldier from a small country wrecked by civil war, steps off a freighter wanting a clean start and, secretly, the man who betrayed his unit. His cousin, who wrote of mansions, runs a failing taxi office deep in debt; a mob boss's calm lieutenant speaks of friendship; a reserved woman from a family of brothers becomes his love; a gun-selling islander becomes his steadiest friend. The player's hands drive, take cover and shoot through a chain of employers, each passing him on or dying, between calls, dates and outings, sparing or killing several men by choice. Killing a loan shark for his cousin traps him in paid killing. The turn comes when the traitor is found: a ragged addict who sold the unit for money, whom the lead kills or frees, without relief. Then a provincial don wants a drug deal made with the lieutenant who burned his cousin's home. Deal or revenge decides the two endings and what is lost for good: take the deal, and the cousin dies at his own wedding; take revenge, and the woman is killed there instead. Either way the lead hunts the man responsible to a lighthouse island and kills him, left with the money or with his living cousin.
