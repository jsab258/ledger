You are judging scenes from story-driven games. Each scene below is a retelling of one scene, in a fixed plain format of the same length, with names and places replaced. Every scene has been rewritten in the same flat voice, so do not judge the prose. Judge the scene underneath: the people, what happens, what is said, and what the player does.

For each pair, decide which scene players and critics would remember and praise more, if both were played in full in a finished game of the same quality. One must be chosen.

What tends to make a scene remembered (from studies of player reviews and of the best game stories):
1. People the player cares about, far more than plot or spectacle. A person with a want of their own, and one deed or line only they would do or say.
2. The lead wants something, acts, and pays for it. The heaviest moments happen to the lead or to someone close, and the lead had a hand in them.
3. A bond under strain: trust, a test, a break, a last word.
4. Stakes that are personal (a person, a body, freedom), not only money or a target.
5. A turn that is earned by what came before, not a surprise for its own sake. Pay-offs of things planted earlier.
6. The player's action matters to the meaning of the scene, not only to its mechanics. A choice with a cost.
7. Specific, lived detail, and comedy that comes from who people are.

What tends to make a scene forgettable: errands and set-ups that exist to move the player to the next job; people who only give orders or explain; danger without anyone the player cares about at risk; a plot turn that changes nothing for the people; action with no feeling attached; a scene any game could have.

A disused textile mill by the river, reached by a goods hoist. It is day, shortly after a meeting of the lead's team is broken up. Every floor is trapped, and the search for answers is long.

## People
Abel is the lead's contact on the phone, who reads papers sent to him as photographs. Ada is the secret leader of a group the player knows has set warehouse fires; a film speaks for it. Bram is a security chief who wants power. Hired gunmen fill the final section.

## What happens
A call from the arson group interrupts the team's meeting. The lead follows a courier's motorbike to the mill. The hoist needs a code, and a wrong entry sends armed guards. Above, tripwired steam valves and locked shutters protect an office with a wall screen showing the group's leader. Abel cannot make sense of the papers, so the lead searches them. They show the group was behind the phones the team uses, a hidden tap on the team's bank account and Abel's own personnel file. A folder shows a former inspector framed for the fires. A screen shows the fires, and Bram claims he now holds power, then shoots the inspector. A second film shows Ada meeting Bram: she wanted the bank, the deal ended when a fire was put out early, Bram tried to kill her, and she escaped. Abel finds a plan built to seize the team's funds and safehouses. Ada appears behind the lead and says the plan will tear down the city's order so it can be built again. She says she is the group's only member. Gunmen flood in, and the lead fights while Abel opens the shutters. Outside, gunmen attack the team's other members.

## The lines that matter
Abel says he cannot read the papers. Bram says he holds power in the city. Ada says the plan will tear down the order so it can be built again. Ada says she is the only member.

## What the player does
The lead follows the courier, enters the code, fights the guards, searches the office and survives the gunmen until the shutters open. Abel then arranges a safe meeting place.

## Setting
A large seaside hotel, shut for the winter, at night, with long corridors, a lift, a caretaker's office and guest rooms. The mood is quiet and still, with a danger that does not show itself at first and long empty corridors lit in pale green.

## People
The lead is an undercover police officer working inside a gang. Abel is a young member of the gang who has been hurt and is in a bed upstairs. Bram is an older gangster who helps the lead. Rival gang members have come to finish Abel. The player already knows the lead is guarding Abel and that Abel does not know the lead's secret.

## What happens
Abel phones the lead and asks him to come at once. The lead drives to the hotel. In the entrance a short scene shows armed rivals inside. The lead takes a pistol from the first man he fights and takes cover. He clears the ground floor and goes up in the lift.

On the upper floor he forces a blocked door and fights a group. A timer starts: the lead has to clear the remaining men, reach the caretaker's office and key in the release code at the door panel before time runs out. Bram joins him and they hold the office together while men come from both ends of the corridor. When the last man falls, police sirens start. The lead drives Abel away and takes him home. The job ends when the lead goes to sleep at home, which opens the next job. The player sees no more of the hotel.

## The lines that matter
Abel tells the lead on the phone that men are after him and he cannot move. The lead tells him to stay where he is. Bram says they will hold the room as long as needed. Abel thanks the lead for coming.

## What the player does
The player drives to the hotel, fights through two floors, keys in the code before the timer ends, covers the corridor with Bram, and drives Abel clear of the police.

--- SCENE 2
## Setting
City streets, a ferry quay on the harbour, a walled hospital garden with an orangery, and the roads around it, in the run-up to a house-warming party. The mood is light and errand-like, with small tasks piled on top of one another.

## People
The lead is a man secretly paid by a rival firm to report on a gang. Abel is a member of the gang who is about to move in with his partner. Ada is his partner, who is exacting about small things. Gardeners tend the orangery. Wardens chase the lead briefly at the end. The player already knows the lead is close to Abel and Ada.

## What happens
The lead picks up Ada and drives her to a shop, taking care not to anger her, since she notices every bump in the road. At the shop Abel phones the lead and asks him to find the launch carrying the party food and bring it to him. The lead runs the length of the quay, jumps onto the launch's stern, moves along its rail to the wheelhouse and keeps his balance.

He brings the food to a function room, then takes Ada on. She asks for a cutting of a pale rose from the hospital garden. The lead climbs the wall, talks his way past the gardeners, and gets close to the rose. The gardeners see through his story. The lead can fight or run to the car. A wardens' van chases him and he loses it, then drives Ada to her destination, which ends the job.

## The lines that matter
Abel asks the lead to find the launch and says the food must reach him safely. Ada tells the lead she wants only a particular rose. The lead tells a gardener he is there to check the drains. Ada tells the lead to hurry. Abel tells the lead she is nervous.

## What the player does
The player drives Ada carefully through the traffic, chases the launch, jumps onto it, brings the food to the function room, climbs the wall, talks to the gardeners, takes the cutting and loses the wardens.

## People
Abel is the target, a man the lead once trusted who betrayed him; he is sending a load of lorries out of the city tonight. Armed guards crowd the yard. Bram is the lead's cousin, who hears of it afterwards. The lead wants Abel dead.

## What happens
Abel tells his men to load the lorries because they leave tonight. The guards tell the lead to turn round and say Abel does not want to see him. The lead keeps walking, and they say they have to kill him. He climbs the side steps, takes cover on a loading deck and shoots a guard on a high stack of containers. Inside, he clears offices, then three heavily armed guards upstairs, then a larger group in the yard. At the crane cabin he opens a repair shed, drops gas canisters in, waits, and jumps down. He faces Abel, who backs into a corner and orders his guards to kill the lead. The lead stays in cover, uses explosives on the guards, and reaches Abel, badly hurt. The lead can kill him or spare him. He leaves by the back gate, jumps off the loading ramp and phones Bram to say Abel is dead and that this was where he started.

## The lines that matter
The lead tells Abel he will not take the offer because he has unfinished business with him. Abel asks whether the quiet years have made him soft. The lead calls Abel a coward who sells out friends and says that is how he will die. Abel warns that many will mourn him, and the lead says he was never liked. If spared, Abel begs.

## What the player does
The lead fights through three areas using cover, a rifle and explosives, then finishes Abel by killing or sparing him. Killing him brings a call from Bram, who accepts it and reminds the lead to collect their aunt from the station on Sunday.

--- SCENE 2
## Setting
A walled former tram works in the middle of the city, ringed with fences and scaffolding, with a vehicle pit below. It is night. The mood is heavy with guards.

## People
Abel heads the private debt-collection firm that dominates the city and wants to keep power; the player knows he was linked to a string of arsons. The lead is an infiltrator working for a small resistance team, picked before the mission and unable to hand over to anyone once inside. Guards and dogs patrol. The mission is the last of a long run of jobs against his firm.

## What happens
The lead's team exposed Abel's part in the arsons, and the public heard of it. He refused to step down and shut himself inside his headquarters, threatening the streets. The lead climbs the outer fence on a hoist, crosses scaffolding to the inner wall and goes along a roof, taking a key card from a desk in a site cabin. Guards and dogs can be knocked out or avoided, and some steps skipped by sending a small remote-control car through a gap. The lead opens the gate and shuts off a power box. A notice at the pit lift warns that the lead cannot leave or hand over until Abel is beaten. Abel comes out in a large armoured digger that gunfire cannot hurt. The lead closes coolant valves to expose its weak plates and shoots them in short windows, in three rounds, while reinforcements keep arriving and the digger fires at marked spots. When it is stopped, Abel climbs out and the lead shoots him in a few shots. He says a few words, dies, and the lead is carried outside.

## The lines that matter
Abel, once exposed, refuses to step down. A notice asks the lead to choose: return later, or be ready. As he dies, Abel says he used to believe in people. He says nothing more about what changed in him.

## What the player does
The lead sneaks in, takes the key card, and uses guns or tricks against the digger. The final fight against Abel is short and takes only a few shots. The mission closes the run.

--- SCENE 2
## Setting
A disused salt works on a tidal flat, reached by a causeway and cut off at high water. A watchman's cottage stands on a rise, with a pump house on the far bank and a stony shore beyond. Rain falls without stopping. The mood is damp, cold and still.

## People
Abel is a caretaker who guards the cottage and will not open the door. Bram, Cass and Dorran are his three workmates, who are missing. The lead wants to get inside, because someone he has searched for a long time is behind the door. The player already knows the lead crossed the causeway to get here.

## What happens
The lead crosses the causeway and walks up to the cottage. Abel says he cannot let strangers in until his three workmates are found. The lead goes to look for the first, Bram, behind the rise. A gang of thieves stripping copper is in the way. He beats them, and finds Bram trying to walk down a track and failing. The lead then goes to the pump house for the second, Cass. Cass blacks out without warning, in the open, on the track and at the water's edge. The lead must escort him back to the cottage, driving off a pack of loose dogs on the way and waking him each time he collapses. The third search is along the shore. The lead finds only a hostile drifter there, beats him, and searches the body. He reports to Abel. Abel unlocks the door and the lead goes in to meet the woman he came for.

## The lines that matter
- Abel: "Bring back my three friends first. Then we can talk."
- The lead, to Cass: "On your feet. We are not finished walking."
- Cass, waking: "Did something happen? Where am I?"
- Abel, at the end: "That is all we wanted. Come inside."

## What the player does
The player fights the thieves, the dogs and the drifter, and keeps waking Cass while walking him back. The player can search the shore in any order. The quest ends when the player reports to Abel, and nothing else is asked of him.
