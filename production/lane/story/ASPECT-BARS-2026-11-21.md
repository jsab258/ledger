## The details

### How it was done

The aspects (aspect-bars/ASPECTS.md, fixed before any reader ran) merge the ruler's fourteen tests (measure/MEASURE.md) with the sub-themes players name. Two kinds of evidence per aspect:

1. Players. The Steam story study (STORY-PATTERNS-STEAM-2026-11-20.md): 18 to 43 hand-read story reviews per game, so a share rests on a handful of reviews. Where an aspect has a row in the larger players study of KCD2, GTA V and RDR2 (about 5,700 to 7,800 kept Steam reviews each, coded by script), that is given too.
2. The checked reader. The reader brief that passed the reader check this morning (players' rank correlation 0.91; READER-CHECK-2026-11-21.md), turned to one aspect at a time (aspect-bars/BRIEF-ASPECTS.md). Three fresh Opus readers, each given the nine disguised summaries of the reader check (eight shipped games and The Passenger M3) under fresh letters and a fresh order (aspect-bars/LABELS.txt), marked every story on every aspect from 1 to 10 and named the best. Raw marks: aspect-bars/scores.txt; means: aspect-bars/means.txt.

Best game: players first where their count separates the games; the readers where players are silent or too thin; both named where they disagree or tie. Reader means within a third of a point count as a tie.

### How far to trust it

1. Every reader recognised every shipped game (85 to 99% sure); none took P for a shipped game (reader 1 called it original, readers 2 and 3 tied it to this project). Marks may carry what they remember of the games. Two readers read the KCD2 page partly as the first Kingdom Come, so its marks may carry that game's story.
2. P is a plan; the others are shipped games summarised. A plan on paper tends to hold together and keep its choices, so P's leads on pacing, choices, the turn and play are the least to trust. Its gaps on the lead, opponent, moments and humour are the more telling.
3. P's page quotes lines; the shipped pages mostly do not (noted by reader 2). Its writing mark may be high for that.
4. Hand counts rest on 1 to 19 reviews per cell; the large study's on hundreds. Where a row says "thin", read it as a direction.
5. The readers saw summaries, which strip performance and play alike.
