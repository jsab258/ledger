# The story measure: report

For Jafar. 20 November 2026, 23:20.

What the ruler is. Fourteen pass-or-fail tests worth 80 points, each a thing players themselves name when they write about story in Steam reviews, and each named in at least five of eight games (KCD2, GTA V, RDR2, Sleeping Dogs, GTA IV, L.A. Noire, Hitman, Watch Dogs: Legion). The largest: characters worth caring about. A reader must quote the line that shows each pass.

Like for like. KCD2, RDR2, GTA V and The Passenger were each written down in the same format at the same length, names removed, and scored side by side by three fresh readers.

Where they land (of 80; overall of 10): RDR2 66 (7 to 8); KCD2 60 (6 to 7); GTA V 47 (4 to 6); The Passenger 80 (8 to 9). The order of the three references matches what players say, after one pilot and one fix.

Do not take our 80 at face value. Our page is a summary of a plan; the others are summaries of shipped games. A plan states its intentions; a plot account cannot. The ruler also cannot see missions that force the way, and it has a ceiling: it shows a story has none of the faults players name, not how good it is beyond that.

So the real test was a fourth reader: a games writer shown the whole plan and told the truth about what it was. Asked whether it would stand at or above RDR2's story if built well, he said no. Four different such readers said no across four versions, each about 65% sure.

The gap, and what closes it. They agree the friendship with Ron and the planting are better made than the shipped stories' pages. What keeps it below the best: the heaviest moments happen to Ron and Sheila while Tom watches; the stakes are small; who drove can be guessed. Three rewrites answered these in turn (the last gives Tom a bad night of his own); none moved the verdict.

Rounds run: one pilot, two calibrations, three rewrites; 22 fresh readers.

Is it exceptional by the measured bar? No. It is a very good small story on paper. The next proof is not on paper: build the lesson, the count and Tom's night, and judge those.
