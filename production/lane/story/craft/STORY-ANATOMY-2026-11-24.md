# The story craft anatomy, 24 November 2026

For Jafar, before Tuesday's pick. We studied RDR2, KCD2, GTA V, GTA IV and Sleeping Dogs, their writers, craft books and players' reviews, and drew fifteen principles. Three AI readers scored the three new stories against them.

## What the best game stories do

1. The lead wants something loud and a little wrong from the first hour. Arthur wants the gang to hold.
2. The heaviest scenes happen to the lead, and he causes them.
3. One bond, built on jobs and rides, runs through almost every chapter: trust, strain, a break, a last word.
4. A few people, each with his own want and one deed only he would do.
5. The opponent is close, wants the same thing, gains ground you see, and is faced in person.
6. Stakes climb from money to a person to the lead's own body and freedom.
7. A routine early job comes back as the turn. Arthur's debt collection gives him his illness.
8. The middle climbs. No two chapters alike; the midpoint changes how you play.
9. You always know what you want next.
10. The last act matches the middle and pays off what was planted.
11. One question, scored by the game, answered by the ending, never said aloud.
12. A world ending, shown in play.
13. Losses prepared for hours, touched by your choices.
14. Comedy from who people are, every week.
15. Choices and side stories that come back and shape the ending. Nothing forced.
