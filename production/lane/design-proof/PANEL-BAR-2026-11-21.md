# The panel bar for an evening and a week (rows 7.2 and 7.3), 21 November 2026

Design proof run 3, step (a). Source: the players study, production/research/lane/PLAYERS-KCD2-GTAV-RDR2-2026-11-20.md, line "Steam's totals (English, read 20 Nov)".

## The reference figures

| Game | Steam reviews positive | Read |
|---|---|---|
| KCD2 | 92.9% | 12 Oct 2026 |
| RDR2 | 92.1% (313,312 of 340,278) | 20 Nov 2026 |
| GTA V | 83.8% (544,042 of 649,445) | 20 Nov 2026 |

A Steam review is a yes or no to "do you recommend this game?". The best reference (KCD2) is recommended by about 93 players in 100.

## The bar

The panel is eight kinds of player. Each answers the same yes or no after the run: would you recommend it, and would you keep playing.

With eight voices the bar cannot be 93% exactly. What a game of each reference's quality would score on eight voices (binomial, each voice a yes with the game's Steam rate):

| Game quality | 6 or more of 8 | 7 or more of 8 | 8 of 8 |
|---|---|---|---|
| KCD2 (92.9%) | 98.5% | 89.4% | 55.5% |
| RDR2 (92.1%) | 98.0% | 87.3% | 51.8% |
| GTA V (83.8%) | 87.4% | 61.9% | 24.3% |

So:
- 8 of 8 would fail a KCD2-grade game nearly half the time: too strict to be fair.
- 6 of 8 passes a GTA V-grade game 87% of the time: it does not ask for the best.
- 7 of 8 passes a KCD2-grade game about 9 times in 10 and a GTA V-grade game about 6 in 10. It is the bar nearest "at the best reference" that a game at that level reliably clears.

Bar for 7.2 (an evening) and 7.3 (a week): at least 7 of 8 panel players recommend, and the one who does not names no reason from the punished list the players study ranks highest (bugs or a bad PC version, boring or the same loop, slow controls, a thin story). The earlier figures, 4 of 8 for the evening and 5 of 8 for the week, stay as the first milestone (M1) after the one-shot; 7 of 8 is the gate's bar for the release-grade prototype.

## Honest limits

- Steam reviewers wrote after a median of 77 to 127 hours; an evening and a week are the first 3 to 20 hours. The panel judges a shorter stretch than the players did.
- The panel is AI players. Its bar now rests on real players' figures, but whether the panel's yes means what a player's yes means is checked separately (the panel's calibration against his own words, piece 8 of the panel research). Until that check, a pass is a pass on the panel, not proof players would agree.
