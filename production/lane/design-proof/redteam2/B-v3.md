Section 2 calls gossip "the engine under the hood", but the document never says who tells whom, how often, how far, how fast, how a told version loses certainty from teller to teller, or what "the talk cools" or "fades" means in game time. Witnessing, the walk, the street on edge, the name ladder, the rivals' eyes, Ellis's "three people of his day world" and the endings all depend on it, so the core of the game would be guessed.

With no numbers on screen, the hidden numbers still have to exist and be tuned.
