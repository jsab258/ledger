# Red team 2, helper A

Gaps in GAME-DESCRIPTION-COPY-2.md against a AAA open-world crime game at the KCD2 / GTA V / RDR2 bar. Ordered by importance. Deliberate exclusions (no meters, minimap, stats, game over, jump, swimming and so on) are not listed.

1. UNDEFINED: Act III morning grading. The section under "Further rules of play" is empty: only a "To pass" line. The game's final act and its ending both depend on how each inspection morning is graded from the books, and nothing says how many off weeks, or how far off, makes a morning go warm or cold.
2. UNDEFINED: how the ending is worked out. Three endings are shown and "five built states" sit underneath, but only two of the five are named ("Both", "Quiet"). No rule turns the inputs (books held or not, friendship held, Ellis's charges, the hunted state, rival rungs, the successor's offer, the business alive) into a state. The team cannot build the payoff of a 15 to 20 hour game.
3. MISSING: the tuning table. Every number is said to "live in the one tuning table", but the table is not in the document. Money amounts, rates, decay times and limits that are not written inline do not exist for a team that has only this page.
4. MISSING: the economy's numbers beyond the opening. There are no figures for: the distance rate on fares, drivers' weekly wages, the envelope cut, what break-ins and lifts pay, the honest prices goods are fenced against, car prices, coat, jacket and overcoat prices, the doctor's fee, bribe prices, the Fixer's prices, what a front's share costs and earns, what rounds and accounts bring in, the front's extra wash per week, or the hot-goods decay rates. A "week of fares pays the rent" and "a sink at every act's end" cannot be balanced or tested without them.
5. UNDEFINED: what happens when the rent is missed. "Doing nothing misses Friday's rent" is the only line. Nothing says who the landlord is, whether there is a warning, eviction, debt or loss of the office, or what the town says. It is the first deadline in the game.
6. UNDEFINED: the firm failing for good. Nothing covers the book going below zero, wages never paid, every driver leaving, every car wrecked or sold, Sheila or Pauline leaving or dying, or the office lost. Without a defined floor, a player can wreck the core loop with no defined way back and no ending.

8. UNDEFINED: attacking the police. Striking, disarming, knocking out or killing a constable, Ellis or the patrol crew has no rule beyond the general deed rules: no charge weight, no effect on Ellis's stage, no manhunt trigger. It is the first thing GTA players test.
