28 November 2026. Paper chaos test, job 4. Each scheme is played by a player who wants to see what breaks: soft locks, things that cannot be undone, the edges of the map and the save.

Marks and questions as in Part 1. Fix numbers continue the register.

## R01 The cab off the quay
Frank drives his own cab at the quay edge, then tries to push it in with a body in the boot.
1. Drives at the edge. H: 4.2.4 (cars cannot leave the quay; bollards and the edge stop them and a car backs out).
2. Puts a body in the boot. H: CS-16 (4 s), GV-01.
3. Pushes the car off the quay. S: CS-16 says "a car pushed off the quay with a body is a big loud deed: divers come, the registration is traced"; 4.2.4 says no car can leave the quay. One of the two is wrong.
4. Divers and the trace. H: CS-16, G13.
- Q1 yes: the player is shown a deed the map forbids, or a rule describes a deed that cannot happen.
- Q2 no. Q3 no until settled.
- Verdict: fail Q1. Fixes: F30.

## R02 Burn the fleet

- Verdict: fail Q2. Fixes: F31.

## R03 Dark street

1. Breaks a lamp. H: 1.3.5 (light can be changed, a bulb broken), G19 (damage changes what an object does until mended).
2. Sight falls in the dark. H: 2.2.3 (a face at 8 m and light 0.7 or more).
3. Somebody mends it. M: no part says who mends a public lamp, a phone box or a railing, or how soon.
4. The town notices. H rule only: G27 (three small unseen deeds become a town question; owners add locks), but a lamp has no owner in any part.
5. Breaking it is a deed when seen. H: 2.2.1.
- Q1 no. Q2 no (step 3). Q3 no: without mending, a dark street is a lasting easy win against every face witness.
- Verdict: fail Q2 and Q3. Fixes: F31.

## Fix register (this part)
- F29 (law 3, people; 8.10): anyone whose way is blocked by Frank answers by temper: asks him to move, then pushes past (a shove, an act with slots), waits a set time, or goes round, and remembers it. Mends R10.
- F30 (law 2, things; 4.2.4, CS-16): one water-edge rule. Either a car can go in at a named unbollarded point (a slipway or the fish dock's open stretch) with CS-16's consequences, or CS-16's line is struck. Mends R01.
- F31 (law 2; G19, G13): every object's damage ladder has a top step, destroyed, with who replaces it, at what cost and how long; public things (lamps, kiosks, railings) have an owner who mends them on a set delay (the council's men, the Post Office), sooner after repeated damage; containers such as the tin are objects like any other and can be thrown away, burnt or given. Mends R02, R03, R04, R11.
- F32 (law 2; law 7, story reads the world; RT-01): the books are objects and records at once. The desk book can be destroyed; what survives (Sheila's memory, the bank's paying-in slips, the drivers' sheets) is what Act III grades, and a destroyed book is itself an item that falls. Mends R05.
- F33 (law 7; G22): every role in the cast list, Act III's inspector included, has its fallback written two deep, and the story bot's kill test runs over the whole cast, not only the shielded few. Mends R06.
