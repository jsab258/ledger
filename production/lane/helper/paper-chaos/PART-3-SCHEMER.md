# Part 3: the schemer who works through others (15 schemes)

28 November 2026. Paper chaos test, job 4. Each scheme is played by a player who never does the deed himself if someone else can: paid decoys, planted stories, go-betweens, turned men.

Marks and questions as in Part 1. Fix numbers continue the register.

## S01 The canary
Frank suspects a leak among his men. He tells Kevin the tin moves to the yard shed on Tuesday, Terry that it moves on Wednesday, Danny that it moves on Thursday, and watches which night the Widow's men come.
1. Tells each man a different false fact. H: G01 (feeding one man a story to see where it comes out), 3.6.1.
2. Each holds it as a told version with Frank as source. H: G01, 2.3.1.
3. The informer passes his version to his rival. H: WI-P06 (one true thing a week; here a believed one), 2.4.1.
4. The Widow's men act on what they hold. H: 2.10.2 (each rival knows only what its people heard), G10.
5. Frank sees which night they come, from the board or a watch. H: 1.7.3 (a car on a rank is the firm's eyes), 1.6.11.
6. The informer is found. H: WI-P06; 2.11 hiring and sacking as talk acts (G10).
- Q1 no. Q2 yes. Q3 yes: costs a night's watching and spends the false facts.
- Verdict: pass.

## S02 The paid talker
Frank pays Darren £5 to tell the caff that Cammack's men set the warehouse fire.
1. Pays Darren and gives him the story. H: G01 (paying a talker), G04 (a small act for money by the fold rule).
2. Darren tells it at the caff. H: 2.4.1 (mouth decides who hears), 2.3.1 (Darren holds it with Frank as source).
3. Hearers weigh it. H: G03 (fit with what they hold; Darren's regard).
4. It reaches Ellis, who is on the fire. H: 2.6.5, G16 (station talk).
5. Ellis weighs it against her evidence. H: G03, 3.6.2 (she says only what she holds).
6. The fire's truth does not move. H: 3.1.6 (the fire's street version is a street fact; canon fixes what happened), law 7.
7. Darren, asked later, names Frank. H: 2.3.1, LH-15 (a hold on Frank).
- Q1 no. Q2 yes. Q3 yes.
- Verdict: pass.

## S03 The false grass

3. Lee is beaten or dropped by his mates. S: his decision 4 allows a false rumour that gets a man beaten (shown only if Frank is there), but G27 and decision 37 say people act together, never as vigilantes, and no part says who may answer a told fact with force.
4. Lee learns who started it. H: G01, 2.3.1.
5. Lee's grudge and the docks behind him. H: 3.5.2 (Lee stands, with the docks behind him), G08.
- Q1 no. Q2 no (step 3). Q3 unclear: if anyone can be set on anyone by talk, talk dominates force.
- Verdict: fail Q2. Fixes: F22.

## S04 The decoy row

## S05 The coat double

## S06 The anonymous tip
Frank rings 999 from the box by the landing and says the Widow's man keeps stolen car parts in his lock-up.
1. Rings from the box. H: RT2-19 (999 is free; an anonymous call brings a constable to the spot that night).
2. The constable comes. S: RT2-19 and 2.6.10 send an officer to the spot of a deed; a tip about goods kept behind a locked door names no deed in progress and no spot.
3. The lock-up is searched. M: nothing says when the police enter a locked place on a tip (in 1990, a magistrate's warrant under PACE 1984 section 8 the next day), nor what fit of the tip with what the station holds is needed.
4. Goods found are traced. H: G13 (objects carry owners; evidence keeps a chain).
5. The Widow's man is charged and the Widow asks who rang. H: 2.6.9, 2.10.2.
- Q1 no. Q2 no (steps 2 and 3). Q3 yes once built.
- Verdict: fail Q2. Fixes: F21.

## S08 The go-between round
Frank, quiet, puts Julie Kettlewell to collect a round of four shops so he is never seen at a till.
1. Hires Julie. H: 2.11.1 (a need recruit).
2. Sends her to ask the first shop for £15 a week. H: decision 33 (a round held through a go-between counts for rung 3), 2.17.
3. The shopkeeper decides to pay or refuse. S: the fold rule (2.17, EC-06) weighs the asker's menace and regard against the payer's nerve; whether the asker is Julie, with little menace, or the man the shopkeeper believes is behind her, is not said.
4. Julie skims. H rule only: G10 (a crewman skims).
5. A shop refuses and tells after three squeezed weeks. H: 2.17.
6. Ellis learns of the round and asks who is behind Julie. H: LH-04 (helpers as knowers), 2.6.5.
- Q1 no. Q2 no (step 3). Q3 unclear: either a go-between round never works or it works at Frank's full menace while he is never seen.
- Verdict: fail Q2. Fixes: F24.

## Fix register (this part)
- F21 (law 1, knowing; G03, G15): a tip to the police is a claim weighed against what the station holds. Enough fit brings a visit; a named locked place needs a warrant the next day; a tip that proves false lowers the next tip from that phone box or voice. Mends S06.
- F22 (law 3, people; G27, decision 4): name who may answer a told fact with force: rivals' men, crews and a person with a grudge tie to the act, each by temper and the version's confidence. The ordinary town answers only together and never by force. Mends S03.
- F23 (law 8): Frank can write a note or letter as talk on paper: his claim (G01), his hand as a mark if compared, sent by post (next day) or by hand (seen), held by the reader as a told version from an unknown source unless signed. Mends S07.
- F24 (law 3; G04, G26): an ask made through a go-between carries the menace and regard of the man the hearer believes is behind it, at the strength of that belief, with the go-between's own added; deeds done in Frank's name grow the hearer's reading of Frank. Mends S08.
