## P05 The hollow friendship

## Fix register (this part)
- F38 (law 3, people; G10): anything a rival may do to Frank, Frank may do to a rival. A plant is a hire the rival makes by its own rule (need, regard, a word from someone it trusts), and a plant weighs two loyalties each week, pulled by the regard each side earns. Mends P03.
- F39 (law 7, story reads the world; RT2-04, 3.2.5): the friendship state reads what the friend holds of Frank (truths he told, lies she holds as lies, deeds she knows), with three calls as a floor, not the test. Mends P05.
- F40 (law 1, knowing; G03, RT-07): a claim is also weighed by how the speaker could know it. A true secret with no source the hearer can see is met with "who told you?", raising alarm and grudge rather than yielding, and is told on as a strange thing Frank knew. Mends P13.
- F01 is widened: the post-ending town keeps adapting for as long as play goes on, and RT2-21's test runs beyond 14 game days. Mends P14.
