Every playthrough runs through the revised engine of STORY-ENGINE-PASS-2026-11-28.md (SE1 to SE9, A1 to A15, the chairs, PF1 to PF5, the conflicts as settled there) and the scenes of KEY-SCENES-2026-11-28.md. Rule references are to the blueprint zip of 28 Nov evening.

## How each is judged

Three questions, the same for all twenty:
- Q1 Does anything break (a stuck state, a beat that cannot fire, a contradiction)?
- Q2 Does the story answer by rules, with no special case for this player?
- Q3 Is it fair and enjoyable, with stakes the player feels?

Every fail gets a fix at rule level, named under its law, in the register (section 3). Laws: 1 knowing; 2 things; 3 people; 4 exchange; 5 time; 6 recovery; 7 story reads the world; 8 the player acts only through what he can do and say.

## 3. Fix register (rule level)

FX1 (law 7; G26; 2.4.4). A deed of weight is any deed the town retells at fact strength within a game day: a fight refused in front of watchers, an old man driven to the infirmary, a stand against a rival's man at the gate, an amend paid in public, as well as a crime. SE8's weekly slot reads it. Mends PT1.

FX2 (law 5; WQ-02, part 1 section 6). Act II's stages end on any change on the board a returning player can point to (a rival rung, a car, a driver, a post, a front, an arrangement), at least one every 8 game days, measured in game days, not real hours. Mends PT1, PT7.

FX3 (law 3; law 7; crew draft R2.2; G21). A chair's generic holder is a person like any other. After two deaths in one chair inside two game weeks, the next holder is sent from outside the map (a letter: "my brother's lad is coming over from the coast"), arriving after two game weeks; the firm stays at "noticed" and climbs from there. Canon's three rivals stay; the respawn ends. Mends PT2.

FX4 (law 1; law 8; G01, G13, G15). No new rule: rumours, planting and tips are build-class and need working values before stage 1, or every schemer route in the scenes is empty. Mends PT3 when built.

FX5 (law 7; part 1 A8). After a second officer of one power is harmed, that power stops visiting: the remaining items are asked by letter and graded on what Frank posts by the last day; the police take the visit's place in the street. Mends PT5.

FX6 (law 7; law 6; RT-04, 2.9.2, 3.5.5). When Frank will not answer, the street answers for him.
