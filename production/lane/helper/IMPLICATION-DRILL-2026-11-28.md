## How to read the tables

Levels: 1 what must exist; 2 who notices, and how; 3 what it costs or changes; 4 how the player sees and manages it; 5 what happens a week later. Verdicts: D designed (part or rule cited), P partly, N nothing designed. Gap B is build-shaping (changes how the game is built); gap C is content-only (can wait for the playtesting loops). A G rule counts as designed only where a part carries it with values; a rule that is only proposed (BLUEPRINT-12 header: "working values from Monday's pass") counts as partly.
