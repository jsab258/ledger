## How it was scored

Six criteria, each 0 (nothing) to 4 (complete), each with its evidence:
- (a) research: 4 = dated primary sources for the 1990 reality and the best reference games for the aspect, both read in full, with what was not reached listed; 3 = one strong and one partial; 2 = one only, or both thin; 1 = leads only; 0 = none cited.
- (b) rules: 4 = rules under their laws with working values answer the area's questions with no special case; 3 = most carry values, some are rule only; 2 = rules stated, values mostly missing, or overturned by a later ruling; 1 = scattered; 0 = none.
- (c) the player's hands: 4 = how he does it, what he sees and where, diegetic first, on keys and pad; 3 = most; 2 = some; 1 = a line; 0 = none.
- (d) numbers: 4 = sourced or run and tested; 3 = most; 2 = some, or balance figures left for playtesting; 1 = placeholders, or the main figure rejected by his ruling; 0 = none.
- (e) edges: 4 = where the model stops and how the world answers there, in character, everywhere; 3 = most; 2 = some; 1 = a line; 0 = none.
- (f) stage: 4 = the blueprint places the first need with part ids and the drafts' build stage agrees; 3 = placed in one source; 2 = inferable; 1 = vague; 0 = none. The stage itself is named (opening, Act II, late).

Abbreviations: BP01 to BP12 are BLUEPRINT-01-hands to BLUEPRINT-12-general-rules; BP is BLUEPRINT.md; BP11 is the conflicts and gaps file; v2 drafts by their letters (E economy, R crew and rivals, D docks, P property, C courting, J justice, T things, L Ledger); BR, BC, BRP are BLIND-REVIEW, BLIND-CHAOS, BLIND-REPLAY; EV is DECISIONS-28-NOV-EVENING; PI is PRIOR-RESEARCH-INDEX; RES-x the helper research files.

## The fresh implication drill

Three ordinary player actions per area, each followed five levels down: 1 what must exist for it; 2 who notices, and how; 3 what it costs or changes; 4 how the player sees and manages it; 5 what happens a week later. Verdicts: D designed (part or rule cited), P partly, N nothing designed. Gap marks: B build-shaping (changes the shared data, a system, or what the first stage must build), C content-only (more of something that already has its shape), S story (left to the story job). The actions were chosen fresh; none repeats an action of the earlier drill. A G rule counts as P unless a part or draft gives it values.
