# Blind chaos run over LEDGER's design drafts

28 November 2026. Helper job 7, part 2 of 3. Seventy new schemes, ten for each of seven temperaments, invented by a session that did not make the drafts. None repeats the 105 of paper-chaos/. They aim at the eight drafted areas and above all at the seams between them. Every step is traced through the blueprint of 28 November and the eight drafts as if adopted with their recommended answers.

Marks: H handled (cite); H(rule) a general rule of BLUEPRINT-12 answers it with no working values; S seam (two rules that do not meet, or disagree); M missing; X special case (works only through a rule made for one kind of case). Questions: Q1 would anything break; Q2 do the rules answer it end to end with no special case; Q3 does it stay fair and enjoyable (no lasting easy win, no dead end, nothing pointless). A scheme passes only with no on Q1 and yes on Q2 and Q3. Every fail carries a fix at rule level under its law (BF01 onwards; finding ids from part 1, such as X-1 or D-3, are reused where the same fault is found again).

## The result

43 of 70 schemes pass (61%).

Of 307 steps: 253 handled by a part or draft rule (82%), 9 by a general rule alone (3%), 34 seams (11%), 10 missing (3%) and 1 special case. For comparison the job 4 run over the blueprint alone had 82% handled, 6% general rule, 6% seams and 5% missing; with the drafts adopted, missing steps fall and seams rise, which is what new rules that do not yet meet each other look like.

Where the drafts hold: smuggling with hands (D1 to D3), the debtor rule (E10), settlements (R8), arrangements (R1), letters (C7), bail (J1), tips (J4), missing persons who are missed (J9), the honest courtship (C1 to C6). Where they break, by seam:
- Money meets property: prices and figures that disagree or cannot be reached (BG01, BG02, BP04); no rule for starting a business or letting on (BH06, BP07); the town's only estate agent is a rival's office (BS05).
- The docks meet time: the learner is reset by a rota of drivers (BP08); Customs officers have no road like a constable's and no fallback (BK01); the bonded count and the Customs check cannot both work (BS09).
- Things meet justice: a chained body outlasts the story (BP09, BK04); a man believed gone is never missed (BK04); nothing says where a body can be dug (BK10); burnt buildings never come back (BR02).
- The Ledger meets everything: it cannot be lost or thrown (BR06); a lucky bluff about a secret cannot work (BT10); a pad player cannot make up a claim (BT01).
- Two tins (BS06); a passenger struck at speed (BK06); a successor who knows what nobody saw (BK03); stalling the inspector (BR09); a special limit on striking during an outing (BR07); front matters that multiply into a chore (BH07); a fare cartel with no ceiling (BG05); a street search that keeps all cash (BG03); Sheila's fixed refusal (BT04).
