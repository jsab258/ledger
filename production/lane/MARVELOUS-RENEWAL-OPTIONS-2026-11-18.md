### Renewal, cancelling, pausing

- Monthly licences "shall renew automatically on the next day of the date when the previous" term ends, and he must pay for every month that renews. Yearly prepaid licences are never renewed automatically. After a cancellation no fee is charged for the next term. Cancellations are generally not refunded. Source: Marvelous Designer Additional Terms, clauses 5.1.1(b), 5.1.1(c), 5.2, last updated 2 April 2025, read 18 Nov 2026: https://legal.clo-set.com/additional-md
- How to stop it: My account, License page, "Stop Subscription". The plan then shows "cancel scheduled" and "you can continue using it for the remaining period until the license expires"; then it is "completely canceled, and no additional charges will be incurred." Source: support, "Can I cancel my subscription?", undated, read 18 Nov 2026: https://support.marvelousdesigner.com/hc/en-us/articles/47358190456089-Can-I-cancel-my-subscription
- Refunds: a monthly plan bought directly, with no trial, gets no refund; stopping only ends the renewals and access runs to the end of the month. A trial that turned into a monthly plan can be refunded within 24 hours of that change. Source: support, "Refund Policy for Cancellations", undated, read 18 Nov 2026: https://support.marvelousdesigner.com/hc/en-us/articles/50937459914777-Refund-Policy-for-Cancellations
- Pausing: allowed, "Pause Subscription", for up to 6 months; he can resume any time before the pause ends, and "A new billing cycle will begin when you resume". Whether the program still works during a pause, and whether the pause starts at once or at the month's end, the page does not say (unverified). Source: support, "Can I temporarily pause my subscription?", undated, read 18 Nov 2026: https://support.marvelousdesigner.com/hc/en-us/articles/47358159780505-Can-I-Pause-my-Subscription
- Coming back later: the cancel page says Enterprise users "can re-subscribe at any time"; for Personal it says nothing either way. The General Terms let CLO "change Fee prices" at any time. So he can most likely buy a new month later, but nothing promises today's $39 (unverified). Sources: the cancel page above; Marvelous Designer General Terms of Use, clause 9.2, page dated 28 Feb 2024, read 18 Nov 2026: https://marvelousdesigner.com/terms

### What happens to the licence, the program and the files on lapse

- The licence to run the program lasts "for the limited duration of the applicable License Term". Source: Additional Terms 3.1, link above.
- When it ends: "the licenses granted to you by CLO shall cease," "you may not be able to access or retrieve any content associated with your Account," and "you will promptly remove the Software from your device." Source: CLO General Terms of Use 15.2, undated page (copyright 2009 to 2026), read 18 Nov 2026: https://legal.clo-set.com/general/general-terms-of-use
- Opening files without a subscription: the program needs a signed-in licence (Personal needs internet and sign-in, production/research/wardrobe-at-scale/4-MARVELOUS-MONTH.md, from search summaries). CLO-SET, CLO's web service, lists .zprj (project) and .zpac (garment) among its supported files, but the page does not say whether a free account can open them or edit them. Source: CLO-SET support, "Supported File Formats in 3D Viewer", undated, read 18 Nov 2026: https://support.clo-set.com/hc/en-us/articles/45303203188121-Supported-File-Formats-in-3D-Viewer. So: plan on Marvelous files being unopenable and uneditable after the lapse, and on exported meshes, pattern files and fabric files (opened by Blender, Kiln and our own scripts) being the only lasting copies (inferred).

### Using exported garments in a sold game after cancelling

- Ownership: "retain all rights and ownership of your Content." and "We do not claim any ownership rights to your Content." Content includes "work product and other data created using our Services or Software". CLO takes only a licence to run the service and to analyse use. Source: CLO General Terms 7.5.2, 7.5.3, 7.5.4, link above. The Additional Terms 1.6 tie the user's "work products" to that same Section 7.5.
- That part outlives the subscription: 15.3 says obligations that by their nature continue survive expiry, naming "Sections 7 and 27 through 30". Source: CLO General Terms 15.3, link above. Together with 7.5.2 this supports garments made under a paid month staying his to ship after it lapses (inferred from the terms; no sentence says so in those words).
- Two limits found:
  - Marvelous's own sample patterns (the library modules): the licence for a sample inside his modified work is granted under 3.1(b), which runs "for the limited duration of the applicable License Term". A garment built on a library module may lose its licence when the month ends (inferred from the clause; not tested by a lawyer). Restricted samples (CLO avatars, pre-designed clothing) may never be changed or distributed (Additional Terms 1.6, 4.3(c)). The project's own reading of the installed terms on 2 Oct reached the same clause (production/research/free-garments/pc/MARVELOUS-LIBRARY.md). Our garments are drafted with FreeSewing and GarmentCode, not library modules, so this should not touch them; any garment that started from a library module needs checking before it ships.
  - Trial work is barred from commercial use (Additional Terms 2.2.1(b)); his work was made under the paid month from 26 October.
- Who the Personal plan is for: the Additional Terms 2.1.1(a) describe a personal user as "an individual, natural person" with the materials "exclusively licensed for personal use", and 2.1.1(b) covers a one-person company using it for "internal requirements" of its business. The support page says "Freelancers are allowed to use personal licenses" while studios and game companies "must purchase enterprise licenses", and a purchase on a company card or company e-mail may count as a company purchase. Sources: Additional Terms (link above); support, "What is the difference between Personal and Enterprise License?", undated, read 18 Nov 2026: https://support.marvelousdesigner.com/hc/en-us/articles/47358297616153-What-is-the-difference-between-Personal-and-Enterprise-License. Whether an individual making a game to sell is "personal use" is not settled by the wording. His ruling already holds the licence judgement as his and says what is made stays in the game (RULINGS, Clothes, 6 to 8 Nov); this note flags the wording, it does not reopen the ruling.
