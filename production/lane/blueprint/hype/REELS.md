# Twenty 15-second reels (25 Nov 2026)

The best 15-second moments for Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Shorts. A reel is watched with the sound off more often than on, by someone who has never heard of LEDGER. For each: what is shown in 15 s, whether a stranger understands it without context, and what has to be on screen for that.

Two things make most of these work and both are designed: subtitles shown with speech (6.1.2) and the belief line low on screen (6.1.3, "Ada saw you at the yard gate, he thinks"). A reel cut with both off loses about half its meaning. Captions a creator adds are not counted.

Counts: 20 reels. A stranger understands 11 with nothing added beyond the game's subtitles (1, 4, 6, 7, 8, 10, 11, 12, 14, 17, 20; 6, 7 and 8 also need their build gaps closed), 8 only with the game's other on-screen text (the belief line, the Ledger page, a readable clock or map: 2, 3, 5, 9, 13, 15, 16, 19), 1 only with a creator's caption (18). Backed: 16 designed, 2 partly (7, 8), 1 BUILD-owed (6), 1 missing (20).

What this asks of the build (all small, listed so the one-shot prompt carries them):
- Subtitles readable on a phone at 1080p (the 24-unit floor in 6.1.2 is for a monitor; a phone-crop test at 9:16 should be added to the subtitle test). CONTENT (a test case).
- The belief line and the Ledger page legible when the frame is cropped to 9:16 (the line sits low and centred, so it survives the crop; check). CONTENT (a test case).
- Overheard talk subtitled at distance (subtitles show only with their audio; at 4 m the bench must be audible). Part of SM-05's build gap.
- No HUD to hide: LEDGER's clean screen is a gift for reels; nothing to add.
