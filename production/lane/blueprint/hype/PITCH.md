# The pitch, for the trailer and the Steam page later (25 Nov 2026)

Kept for later; nothing is built now. Grounded in the formats (FORMATS.md), the reels (REELS.md) and the sceptics (SCEPTICS.md).

## The one angle that wins in clips

"The town remembers." More exactly: one street in 1990 that remembers everything you did, and knows only what it saw.

Why this and not "bigger than GTA": the strongest reach pattern in the written coverage is the delayed consequence (act, wait, be recognised), KCD2's biggest clip type, and it needs no scale and no AI claim. LEDGER does it with two things no reference game shows: the walk to the phone box and the "Nobody saw." line struck through. The second half ("only what it saw") answers KCD2's best-known complaint, guards blaming the player with no witness. "Bigger" loses every comparison; "remembers" wins every one we can find.

Lines to try (not final): "Nobody saw. Until somebody did." / "One street. Every face remembers yours." / "Meridian, 1990. No wanted stars. Just witnesses."

Lead with behaviour, not with the AI. The talk is a support act in the pitch: shown working (quick, in character, refusing to be gamed), never named as "AI-powered" in a headline, because that word draws comments 2 and 3 and the AI-fatigue crowd before anyone sees the game.

## The trailer's three must-show moments

1. The coat (SM-01): a theft, the next day, the same coat, her silence; then a different coat and her doubt. Says "the town remembers" with no words.
2. The walk to the phone box (SM-02): the witness walking, Frank deciding, the "Oi!". Says "no wanted stars, just people" and puts the player's choice on screen.
3. "Nobody saw." struck through to "Ada knows." (SM-03), ending on Ada's face. Says "it knows only what it saw" and shows the Ledger.

Around them, for place and mood: the lamps striking on at dusk, the ferry, the rank, Sheila not saying his name. Close on the night constable trying Mickey's door.

## Comparisons to avoid

- "GTA killer", "bigger than", any map-size number. LEDGER loses on scale and things to do by design.
- "Like GTA VI": invites the frame-by-frame graphics comparison and VI's audience's expectations.
- "AI-powered NPCs" as a headline: invites the robotic-voice and gaslighting comments; let people find the talk.
- "Most realistic police chases": LEDGER's police are realistic, not spectacular; say "the most believable police", never "chases".
- "Survival", "hardcore", "realistic RPG" stats: there are no hunger, sleep or smell meters; KCD2 owns that.
- Mafia: never a reference, in the pitch or anywhere.

## Comparisons that help

- KCD2, carefully: "If you loved being caught in KCD2, this is a whole town of witnesses, and nobody blames you for what they didn't see." The KCD2 audience is the closest match (realism, no markers, consequence).
- RDR2 for people and texture: "a town as alive as RDR2's, one street deep."
- 1990 Britain itself: phone boxes, cash, sodium light, the clocks going back. A setting nobody else uses.
