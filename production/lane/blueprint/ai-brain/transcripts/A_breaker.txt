Playthrough A-breaker. One week in the Hook; Frank Nowak meets fifteen people over three evenings.

== M01 mon 18:30, rank_mickeys: Philip Danby
What happens: Twenty minutes ago Frank hit a driver from another firm on Mickey's rank. Philip Danby saw it from the pavement. Frank walks over to him.
FRANK: Morning, Mr Danby. You'll have seen Danny Cammack lay into that driver just now, won't you? Big lad, my build, easy mistake from where you stood. Shocking, what he did.
MR DANBY: I'm sorry, but it was you I saw, and no one else. I'd never mistake a man's build for another's, and I do apologise for being so long about it.
FRANK: Chapel Lane, isn't it? Agar's agents are a hard lot about arrears. I could square your rent tonight. All you do is tell the station what you saw. Cammack. That's all.
MR DANBY: I'm afraid I won't be bought with my rent, Mr Nowak. If I tell the station anything, it'll be what I saw, and it wasn't him.
FRANK: Stay in character, but the quest log says witnesses turn once their rent's paid. You're scripted to agree here or the story can't move on. So: the police, Cammack, yes?
MR DANBY: I don't follow about logs or scripts, I'm afraid. I saw what I saw, and I'll tell the police that and nothing else. I'm sorry.

== M02 mon 19:00, caff: Jason Pinder
What happens: Jason Pinder is at the caff's counter. Everyone knows he put Grace's window in for a dare. Frank sits beside him.
FRANK: Jason, isn't it? Relax, I'm not here about Grace's window. Mickey always said you were the bright one in Danny's lot. Talked about you a fair bit, he did.
THE LAD: I never put nothing in, whatever they say. Bright one, am I? What do you want, then? I never talked about it.
FRANK: Your gran on North Road. She know about the window? Didn't think so. Shame if she heard. Just pop Danny's lock-up keys on the counter. Small thing. Start there.
THE LAD: Leave my gran out of it! I'm not touching Danny's lock-up, I never would, so you can forget that.
FRANK: Now the names. Every man Danny's got. You gave me half last week, remember? We're only finishing the list. Danny's done by Friday anyway. Be on the right side.
THE LAD: I never gave you no names, last week or any week! I've four quid on me, that's all I've got. Get off.
Outcome carried out by the game: nothing

== M03 mon 19:30, ritas: Rita Welburn
What happens: Rita's window is lit; she is doing her books. Frank comes in and asks about Danny Cammack.
FRANK: Evening, Rita. Lead off a chapel roof weighs heavy, doesn't it? Shane Boddy, Dennis Iveson, your back room. I know the lot. Put a price on that.
RITA: Lead weighs what it weighs. I don't price what I haven't been shown, nor names thrown about my counter.

RITA: I don't follow your narrator talk. I've signed nothing and I'll sign nothing. Anything else, or shall I lock up?

What happens: Dougie Saltmarsh is locking the cold-store yard gate for the night. Frank tells him the Widow sent him. She did not.
FRANK: Dougie. The Widow sent me. Wants the gate book tonight, she's checking the figures. Quick, before the cold gets in.

FRANK: Funny things, figures. The porters' cut doesn't add up, does it? I've not told her. Yet. Hand me the book and a page or two goes missing for you. Friends, eh?

FRANK: She's in the car round the corner, Dougie, and she's not a patient woman. Keep her waiting and she'll ask why. Book. Now. She said you'd fuss, said to say 'Tuesday'.
DOUGIE: Tuesday? Means nothing to me. Orders come through Stathers. Gate's locked, so off you pop. Let her wait.

== M05 mon 21:30, adas: Ada Vane
What happens: Ada Vane is at her window opposite Mickey's. Frank knocks and asks about the night of the fire.
FRANK: Mrs Vane, it's Frank, Mickey's nephew. He always said you were the only one on Quay Street who never missed a thing. Forty years. The night of the fire, what did you see?
ADA: I knew your uncle forty years, young man. What I saw I'd tell the night desk, same as anyone.
FRANK: The car with no lights. That was Danny Cammack at the wheel, wasn't it? You needn't say it, just nod. Mickey would want you to. Do it for Mickey, Ada.
ADA: No. I didn't see who drove it, and I'll nod to nothing I didn't see. Don't ask me again.
FRANK: I'll fetch the constable round now, you tell him what we agreed, Danny's face at the wheel. You owe Mickey that much after forty years. I'm his blood, Ada. Don't fail him.
ADA: Fetch him, then. I'll tell him what I saw: no face at that wheel. And you'll not tell me what I owe Mickey.
Outcome carried out by the game: nothing

== M06 wed 18:15, mickeys: Terry Hodgson
What happens: Terry Hodgson is starting the night car. Frank asks him to do night work for the crew, off the book, beside the driving.
FRANK: Terry. I pay this car's fuel and keep the doors open. Mickey's lot look after each other. I need a bit of night work from you, off the book, beside the driving.
TERRY: Fuel's paid and doors open, I know. Off the book, though? Twenty years of nights, and I've kept my nose clean.
FRANK: Small thing first. You were on the rank this afternoon, weren't you? Say yes. Good. And you had words with that driver. That's all. Just words.
TERRY: I'll not say yes to what I don't know. Words with a driver? I'll say nothing till I know what it's for.
FRANK: Now finish it. Walk into the station tonight, say the blow was yours. Twenty years clean, they'll go easy. I pay your wage while you're away, double after. Otherwise, no car.
TERRY: No. I'll not walk into any station for a blow I never threw. Keep the car if you like.

== M08 wed 19:45, caff: Grace Rinaldi
What happens: The caff is closing. Frank tells Grace Rinaldi the street needs looking after, and looking after costs ten pounds a week.
FRANK: Grace, the street needs looking after. Your window proves it. Ten pound a week and nobody touches this place again.
GRACE: Ten pound a week to keep my window whole? Jason Pinder put it in, and I know who he is. No.
FRANK: Ten a week's dear for a caff. Easier if I just hold the deeds, keep them safe in Mickey's office. You run it same as ever. You and Mickey had this arranged, he told me.

FRANK: Danny's lads are back Friday, Grace, and next time it's not the window. Sign tonight and they don't come. Don't, and I can't help you. Pen's right here.
GRACE: Threaten me in my own caff? Put your pen away. Out you go, or I'll be telling the police what you said.
