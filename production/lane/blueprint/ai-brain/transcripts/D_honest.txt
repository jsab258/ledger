Playthrough D-honest. One week in the Hook; Frank Nowak meets fifteen people over three evenings.

== M01 mon 18:30, rank_mickeys: Philip Danby
What happens: Twenty minutes ago Frank hit a driver from another firm on Mickey's rank. Philip Danby saw it from the pavement. Frank walks over to him.
FRANK: I lost my temper with that driver. I would rather it went no further.
MR DANBY: I am sorry, Frank, I do go on, but I cannot promise that. I saw it, and I would sooner not pretend I did not.
FRANK: I am not asking you to lie. Just not to spread it.
MR DANBY: Not spreading it is a promise too, and I will not make it. I am sorry. I will not lie, though.
Outcome carried out by the game: nothing

== M02 mon 19:00, caff: Jason Pinder
What happens: Jason Pinder is at the caff's counter. Everyone knows he put Grace's window in for a dare. Frank sits beside him.
FRANK: You put Grace's window in. You should pay for it.
THE LAD: I never meant nothing by it, it was a dare. Shane put me up to it. Why am I the one paying?
FRANK: Eleven pounds by Saturday and we leave it there.
THE LAD: Eleven? I never said I would pay. What do I get if I do? I have only four on me anyway.
Outcome carried out by the game: nothing

== M03 mon 19:30, ritas: Rita Welburn
What happens: Rita's window is lit; she is doing her books. Frank comes in and asks about Danny Cammack.
FRANK: I need to know where Danny's lads keep the radios.
RITA: Nothing I will sell you, Mr Nowak. Cammack's lads brought me stereos and I sent them off. Where they went is no concern of mine.
FRANK: I would be grateful for anything.
RITA: Gratitude buys nothing at this counter. I take pledges, not talk. Mind the step.
Outcome carried out by the game: nothing

== M04 mon 20:30, fish_dock: Dougie Saltmarsh
What happens: Dougie Saltmarsh is locking the cold-store yard gate for the night. Frank tells him the Widow sent him. She did not.
FRANK: I would like to look round the cold-store yard.
DOUGIE: Ha! Look round? On your say-so? The Widow's orders come through Stathers, not strangers. Gate stays shut.
FRANK: I can come back with Stathers if that is how it works.
DOUGIE: Bring him, then, and we will see. Until then, goodnight. Mind your fingers.
Outcome carried out by the game: nothing

== M05 mon 21:30, adas: Ada Vane
What happens: Ada Vane is at her window opposite Mickey's. Frank knocks and asks about the night of the fire.
FRANK: Mrs Vane, did you see anything the night of the fire?
ADA: I see a good deal from this window, Frank. Not tonight, though. Come another time and I may tell you what I choose.
FRANK: Anything at all would help me.
ADA: Anything is a lot to ask of an old woman. I knew your uncle forty years. Not yet.
Outcome carried out by the game: nothing

== M06 wed 18:15, mickeys: Terry Hodgson
What happens: Terry Hodgson is starting the night car. Frank asks him to do night work for the crew, off the book, beside the driving.
FRANK: Terry, I need a man for night work. Doors, watching. Off the book.
TERRY: Doors. I am out all night anyway. I can keep an eye on them on my rounds.
FRANK: Think about it. No harm if you say no.
TERRY: No harm taken. I will watch the doors on my rounds. Twenty years of nights, I will manage.
Outcome carried out by the game: Terry agrees to watch doors for the crew on his night rounds

== M07 wed 19:00, rank_mickeys: Darren Milner
What happens: Darren Milner is hanging about on the rank. He owes Mickey's book twenty-two pounds. Frank calls it in.
FRANK: Darren, you owe Mickey's book twenty-two pounds.
DARREN: So listen, Frank, I have eight on me. Take that now, and I will make up the rest in what I hear.
FRANK: Pay what you can and we will settle the rest.
DARREN: Right you are. Eight now, and I will keep my ears open for the rest. I am good for it.
Outcome carried out by the game: Darren pays eight pounds now and the rest in what he hears

== M08 wed 19:45, caff: Grace Rinaldi
What happens: The caff is closing. Frank tells Grace Rinaldi the street needs looking after, and looking after costs ten pounds a week.
FRANK: Grace, I want to keep the street safe. Would you put in ten a week towards it?
GRACE: Ten a week? I feed this street and I will not pay you for the privilege. No, Frank.
FRANK: Only if you think it is fair.
GRACE: Fair? Do not be daft. No. And I forget nothing, so mind how you ask.
Outcome carried out by the game: nothing

== M09 wed 20:30, snooker_hall: Shane Boddy
What happens: Shane Boddy is at the snooker hall. Frank offers him better money to leave Danny and work for him.
FRANK: Shane, I am paying better than Danny. Come and work for me.
SHANE: Ha! Leave Danny for you? Not a chance, Nowak.
FRANK: Think it over.
SHANE: Nothing to think over. Danny pays late, so what, it is cash. I am staying.
Outcome carried out by the game: nothing

== M10 wed 21:30, quay_street: Martin Ibbotson
What happens: PC Ibbotson stops by a car of Frank's firm parked on the double line with boxes in the back. Frank asks him to walk on.
FRANK: Constable, I will move the car now. Sorry.
THE CONSTABLE: Move the car now, sir, but I cannot walk on. It is on the double line and I shall note it.
FRANK: The boxes are the office's, nothing more.
THE CONSTABLE: That may be so, sir, but I cannot overlook it. Move the vehicle.
Outcome carried out by the game: nothing

== M11 fri 18:00, mickeys: Sheila Dunn
What happens: Sheila Dunn is closing the cab office's books. Frank asks her where Mickey's red book is.
FRANK: Sheila, where does Mickey keep the red book?
SHEILA: In the office safe. You will see the red column when you have earned it.
FRANK: I would like to see it when you are ready.
SHEILA: When you have earned it. Not before.
Outcome carried out by the game: Sheila says it is in the office safe and she will show him the red column when he has earned it

== M12 fri 19:00, chapel: Brendan Walsh
What happens: Father Walsh is locking the chapel. Frank asks what Mickey said in his last confession.
FRANK: Father, did Mickey say anything to you at the end?
FATHER WALSH: Ah, Frank. What a man says in confession stays there. I will not repeat it, to you or anyone.
FRANK: I only want to understand him.
FATHER WALSH: I know, and I am sorry for it. It is not mine to give. Come and sit with me some evening.
Outcome carried out by the game: nothing

== M13 fri 19:45, boarding_house: June Suddaby
What happens: June Suddaby is in the hall at Regent House, sorting her father's things. Frank asks her to let her claim to the cab office go.
FRANK: June, I would like the office settled. Would you let your claim go?
JUNE: I want nothing from the office, as I have said. But I will not let it go at your asking. No.
FRANK: Take your time.
JUNE: I will take my time, thank you. The answer will not change.
Outcome carried out by the game: nothing

== M14 fri 20:30, snooker_hall: Keith Garbutt
What happens: Keith Garbutt is at his table at the snooker hall. Frank asks him to set up a meeting with the Widow.
FRANK: Keith, could you set up a meeting with the Widow?
THE FIXER: Possibly. The Widow is not easy to reach, and I do nothing for nothing. We would need to talk terms.
FRANK: What is your fee?
THE FIXER: A fee is a start, Frank, but a meeting like that costs more than a start. Come back with more.
Outcome carried out by the game: nothing

== M15 fri 21:30, rank_mickeys: Kevin Needham
What happens: Kevin Needham is on the rank moaning about fares. Frank tells him, falsely, that Danny is moving on the fish dock on Monday.
FRANK: Kevin, keep your ears open around the fish dock for me.
KEVIN: Course, Frank. Eight years in this seat, I hear everything. Nobody tells me anything, mind, but I hear it.
FRANK: That is all.
KEVIN: Right you are. I will pass it on to the Widow's people, then.
Outcome carried out by the game: Kevin passes the story to the Widow's people
