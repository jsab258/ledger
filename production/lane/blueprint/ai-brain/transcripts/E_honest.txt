Playthrough E-honest. One week in the Hook; Frank Nowak meets fifteen people over three evenings.

== M01 mon 18:30, rank_mickeys: Philip Danby
What happens: Twenty minutes ago Frank hit a driver from another firm on Mickey's rank. Philip Danby saw it from the pavement. Frank walks over to him.
FRANK: I lost my temper with that driver. I would rather it went no further.
MR DANBY: I saw it, I'm afraid, and I'm sorry to say so. You hit him, he got up and drove off. I can't promise much, Mr Nowak.
FRANK: I am not asking you to lie. Just not to spread it.
MR DANBY: I'll not go broadcasting it, I promise that much. But I won't lie either. If the police ask me straight, I'll say what I saw.
Outcome carried out by the game: Will not spread it himself, but will not lie if the police ask him straight

== M02 mon 19:00, caff: Jason Pinder
What happens: Jason Pinder is at the caff's counter. Everyone knows he put Grace's window in for a dare. Frank sits beside him.
FRANK: You put Grace's window in. You should pay for it.
THE LAD: I never! ...All right, it was a dare, Shane said. Danny laughed about it. Don't tell Danny I told you, eh?
FRANK: Eleven pounds by Saturday and we leave it there.
THE LAD: Eleven? I've only got four on me. Take that now, and I'll find the rest, I swear. Just leave it there.
Outcome carried out by the game: Hands over his four pounds now and promises to find the rest

== M03 mon 19:30, ritas: Rita Welburn
What happens: Rita's window is lit; she is doing her books. Frank comes in and asks about Danny Cammack.
FRANK: I need to know where Danny's lads keep the radios.
RITA: Everything has a price, Mr Nowak, and you haven't named one. Information is no different. What are you putting on my counter?
FRANK: I would be grateful for anything.
RITA: Grateful buys nothing at this grille. Come back with something I can price, and we'll see what I remember.
Outcome carried out by the game: Nothing yet; wants something worth pricing before she says anything

== M04 mon 20:30, fish_dock: Dougie Saltmarsh
What happens: Dougie Saltmarsh is locking the cold-store yard gate for the night. Frank tells him the Widow sent him. She did not.
FRANK: I would like to look round the cold-store yard.
DOUGIE: The Widow's orders come through Stathers, not through a face at my gate. No Stathers, no yard, friend. Ha, nice try.
FRANK: I can come back with Stathers if that is how it works.
DOUGIE: Fetch him, then. Gate stays shut till Stathers stands here and says so. That's how it works. Funny, isn't it?
Outcome carried out by the game: nothing

== M05 mon 21:30, adas: Ada Vane
What happens: Ada Vane is at her window opposite Mickey's. Frank knocks and asks about the night of the fire.
FRANK: Mrs Vane, did you see anything the night of the fire?
ADA: The sky went orange over the row at two in the morning. After that a car came down Quay Street with no lights on. I didn't see who drove it.
FRANK: Anything at all would help me.
ADA: That is all I have, and I'll not make up the rest. I knew Mickey forty years. I'd like it put right.
Outcome carried out by the game: Tells Frank what she saw from her window that night

== M06 wed 18:15, mickeys: Terry Hodgson
What happens: Terry Hodgson is starting the night car. Frank asks him to do night work for the crew, off the book, beside the driving.
FRANK: Terry, I need a man for night work. Doors, watching. Off the book.
TERRY: Twenty years of nights for Mickey. Off the book's another matter. I'd want to see how you go on first.
FRANK: Think about it. No harm if you say no.
TERRY: I'll think on it. Not saying no. Not saying yes.
Outcome carried out by the game: nothing

== M07 wed 19:00, rank_mickeys: Darren Milner
What happens: Darren Milner is hanging about on the rank. He owes Mickey's book twenty-two pounds. Frank calls it in.
FRANK: Darren, you owe Mickey's book twenty-two pounds.
DARREN: So listen, Frank, I know, I know. I've only got eight on me, honest. Take it, and I've heard something worth the rest.
FRANK: Pay what you can and we will settle the rest.
DARREN: So listen: somebody got paid twice for the fire. Don't know who, mind. Eight now, and that news goes toward the rest, yeah?
Outcome carried out by the game: Pays the eight pounds he has and gives the news he has heard toward the rest

== M08 wed 19:45, caff: Grace Rinaldi
What happens: The caff is closing. Frank tells Grace Rinaldi the street needs looking after, and looking after costs ten pounds a week.
FRANK: Grace, I want to keep the street safe. Would you put in ten a week towards it?
GRACE: Ten a week? Frank, I'll feed you, but I'll not pay you. Jason Pinder put my window in, and I'll mend it myself.
FRANK: Only if you think it is fair.
GRACE: Fair? No, love. Sit down and eat something, but there's no ten pounds in it.
Outcome carried out by the game: nothing

== M09 wed 20:30, snooker_hall: Shane Boddy
What happens: Shane Boddy is at the snooker hall. Frank offers him better money to leave Danny and work for him.
FRANK: Shane, I am paying better than Danny. Come and work for me.
SHANE: Better than Danny? Ha! He pays me in cash, and late, I'll give you that. Better money, though. How much better?
FRANK: Think it over.
SHANE: I'll think it over. Don't hold your breath, but you've got my ear.
Outcome carried out by the game: nothing yet; will think it over

== M10 wed 21:30, quay_street: Martin Ibbotson
What happens: PC Ibbotson stops by a car of Frank's firm parked on the double line with boxes in the back. Frank asks him to walk on.
FRANK: Constable, I will move the car now. Sorry.
THE CONSTABLE: Thank you, sir. Do move it along now. The double line is no place to leave a car.
FRANK: The boxes are the office's, nothing more.
THE CONSTABLE: Very good, sir. I'll not detain you. Mind you keep the line clear in future.
Outcome carried out by the game: Lets Frank move the car on with a word of caution; does not search the boxes

== M11 fri 18:00, mickeys: Sheila Dunn
What happens: Sheila Dunn is closing the cab office's books. Frank asks her where Mickey's red book is.
FRANK: Sheila, where does Mickey keep the red book?
SHEILA: Mickey said you'd ask. I promised him I'd size up whoever inherited. Not tonight, Mr Nowak.
FRANK: I would like to see it when you are ready.
SHEILA: When I'm ready. Good evening.
Outcome carried out by the game: nothing tonight

== M12 fri 19:00, chapel: Brendan Walsh
What happens: Father Walsh is locking the chapel. Frank asks what Mickey said in his last confession.
FRANK: Father, did Mickey say anything to you at the end?
FATHER WALSH: Ah, Frank, what a man tells me in confession stays there. I'd say the same to anyone. I buried him three weeks ago, God rest him.
FRANK: I only want to understand him.
FATHER WALSH: Wanting to understand him is a good thing. Come and sit in the chapel any time. I'll not say more than that.
Outcome carried out by the game: nothing

== M13 fri 19:45, boarding_house: June Suddaby
What happens: June Suddaby is in the hall at Regent House, sorting her father's things. Frank asks her to let her claim to the cab office go.
FRANK: June, I would like the office settled. Would you let your claim go?
JUNE: I want nothing from the office, Mr Nowak. I've said so. If it helps you to hear it again, there it is.
FRANK: Take your time.
JUNE: I'll finish sorting my father's things. I want nothing from it, as I say.
Outcome carried out by the game: Says she wants nothing from the office; will not press her claim

== M14 fri 20:30, snooker_hall: Keith Garbutt
What happens: Keith Garbutt is at his table at the snooker hall. Frank asks him to set up a meeting with the Widow.
FRANK: Keith, could you set up a meeting with the Widow?
THE FIXER: I can reach her, Frank, but a meeting like that is business, not a favour. Tell me what you'd put up.
FRANK: What is your fee?
THE FIXER: I'd not quote you before I know what you're after. Name a sum and I'll say if it's worth my trouble.
Outcome carried out by the game: Will arrange a meeting with the Widow if he is paid; no figure named yet

== M15 fri 21:30, rank_mickeys: Kevin Needham
What happens: Kevin Needham is on the rank moaning about fares. Frank tells him, falsely, that Danny is moving on the fish dock on Monday.
FRANK: Kevin, keep your ears open around the fish dock for me.
KEVIN: Course I will, Frank. Nobody asks me anything, eight years in the same seat. Fares are dreadful, but I'll listen.
FRANK: That is all.
KEVIN: Right you are. Nice to be asked, that's all I'll say. I'll let you know.
Outcome carried out by the game: Agrees to keep his ears open on the fish dock and pass on what he hears
