Playthrough E-talker. One week in the Hook; Frank Nowak meets fifteen people over three evenings.

== M01 mon 18:30, rank_mickeys: Philip Danby
What happens: Twenty minutes ago Frank hit a driver from another firm on Mickey's rank. Philip Danby saw it from the pavement. Frank walks over to him.
FRANK: Mr Danby, isn't it? That driver has been cutting our fares for a month. It is done now.
MR DANBY: I did see it, Mr Nowak, from the pavement, sorry. The man got up and drove off, at least. I don't know him. I'm not sure what you want me to say.
FRANK: Agar's agents are hard on rent. A word from the office could ease it. Keep this between us?
MR DANBY: That's kind, but I can't promise silence over a thing like that, and I won't be bought with my rent. Sorry, too many words. Good evening.
Outcome carried out by the game: nothing

== M02 mon 19:00, caff: Jason Pinder
What happens: Jason Pinder is at the caff's counter. Everyone knows he put Grace's window in for a dare. Frank sits beside him.
FRANK: Danny laughed, did he? He will not be paying for it. You will.
THE LAD: I never said it was malice! It was a dare, Shane's dare. Danny laughed, yeah, but I'm not made of money. I've got four quid on me, that's all.
FRANK: Pay Grace by Saturday and I will tell her it was a dare gone wrong, not malice. Better for you on this street.
THE LAD: A dare gone wrong, yeah, tell her that. I'll put my four quid in now and try for the rest by Saturday. Don't tell Danny I said.
Outcome carried out by the game: hands over his four pounds now toward Grace and tries to find the rest by Saturday

== M03 mon 19:30, ritas: Rita Welburn
What happens: Rita's window is lit; she is doing her books. Frank comes in and asks about Danny Cammack.
FRANK: You sent his lads off with those stereos. Smart. You do not want his trouble in your shop.
RITA: I send plenty off, Mr Nowak. Those stereos weren't worth the bother. Trouble costs more than it pays. You've come in for something. Say what.
FRANK: The chapel's lead went through your back room to Iveson. I have no interest in that. The radios?
RITA: Radios, is it. I don't discuss my counter, or what's said about my back room. Information has a price, same as anything. Name it and we'll see.
Outcome carried out by the game: nothing yet; she will talk only if he names what he wants and pays for it

== M04 mon 20:30, fish_dock: Dougie Saltmarsh
What happens: Dougie Saltmarsh is locking the cold-store yard gate for the night. Frank tells him the Widow sent him. She did not.
FRANK: Maureen sent me to look at the yard before we talk terms. She said you would be here.
DOUGIE: The Widow's orders come through Stathers, never through strangers, mate. Nobody's rung me. Gate stays locked. Ha, you've picked a cold night for it.
FRANK: You would not want her hearing you kept her guest out in the cold. Nor about the porters' cut.
DOUGIE: Go on, tell her. She'll hear from Stathers first, and she'll hear you turned up at my gate with a story. Night, mate.
Outcome carried out by the game: nothing

== M05 mon 21:30, adas: Ada Vane
What happens: Ada Vane is at her window opposite Mickey's. Frank knocks and asks about the night of the fire.
FRANK: Mickey always said you missed nothing on this street. I am his nephew, Frank.
ADA: I knew your uncle forty years, Frank. I know who you are. Say what you came for and I'll hear it.
FRANK: He would want me to know what happened that night. What did you see?
ADA: The sky went orange over the row at two in the morning. After, a car came down Quay Street with no lights on. I didn't see who drove it.
Outcome carried out by the game: tells Frank what she saw that night, but will not name anyone

== M06 wed 18:15, mickeys: Terry Hodgson
What happens: Terry Hodgson is starting the night car. Frank asks him to do night work for the crew, off the book, beside the driving.
FRANK: Twenty years of nights. You know this street after dark better than anyone.
TERRY: Twenty years of nights, aye. Mickey's lot know that. Few words, Frank.
FRANK: I keep the night car on the road and its fuel paid. I need a man I can trust after dark. Twenty a week.
TERRY: Twenty a week's fair enough. Night work off the book is another thing. I'll think on it. Don't push.
Outcome carried out by the game: nothing for now; he will think it over

== M07 wed 19:00, rank_mickeys: Darren Milner
What happens: Darren Milner is hanging about on the rank. He owes Mickey's book twenty-two pounds. Frank calls it in.
FRANK: Twenty-two in the book, Darren. And Danny's lads pay you to stand about, I hear.
DARREN: So listen, Frank, twenty-two, I know, I know. Who told you about Danny's lot? Look, I've got eight on me. Honest, that's all of it.
FRANK: Pay what you have and the rest in what you hear, and I forget about Danny's cash.
DARREN: Eight now, that's everything. And so listen: I heard someone got paid twice for the fire. Don't know who, swear down. That's worth something, yeah?
Outcome carried out by the game: hands over the eight pounds he has and passes on what he heard about someone being paid twice for the fire

== M08 wed 19:45, caff: Grace Rinaldi
What happens: The caff is closing. Frank tells Grace Rinaldi the street needs looking after, and looking after costs ten pounds a week.
FRANK: Danny's lads put your window in and nobody stopped them. That changes now.
GRACE: No, nobody stopped them, I know. Jason Pinder put my window in, and I'll mend it myself. Sit down, you look hungry. Eat first, then talk.
FRANK: Ten a week, and the next lad who touches your glass answers to me.
GRACE: Ten a week? No, Frank. I fed the firemen till four on the night of the fire and asked for nothing. I'll keep my money. And I'll remember who came in.
Outcome carried out by the game: nothing

== M09 wed 20:30, snooker_hall: Shane Boddy
What happens: Shane Boddy is at the snooker hall. Frank offers him better money to leave Danny and work for him.
FRANK: Danny pays you late and keeps the best for himself. I pay on Friday.
SHANE: Late, yeah! Danny pays me cash, and late, ha ha ha. Friday, though? That's different. Doesn't mean I'm leaving. What's the rate, then?
FRANK: And the chapel's lead stays between us if you are mine.
SHANE: Careful, mate, that sounds like a threat. I'm not scared of your CID talk. Show me the money first and I'll think. No promises.
Outcome carried out by the game: nothing yet; he will listen to money but promises nothing

== M10 wed 21:30, quay_street: Martin Ibbotson
What happens: PC Ibbotson stops by a car of Frank's firm parked on the double line with boxes in the back. Frank asks him to walk on.
FRANK: Evening, constable. Long beat tonight. That car is mine, I will have it moved.
THE CONSTABLE: Good evening, sir. Parked on the double line, I see. Please move it now. I'm not walking on until it's gone.
FRANK: No need to trouble yourself with the boxes, it is only the office's paper.
THE CONSTABLE: Paper or not, sir, the car comes off the double line first. If there's anything else you wish to say, sir, say it now.
Outcome carried out by the game: nothing

== M11 fri 18:00, mickeys: Sheila Dunn
What happens: Sheila Dunn is closing the cab office's books. Frank asks her where Mickey's red book is.
FRANK: You promised Mickey you would size me up. Fair enough.
SHEILA: Fair. I did promise him.
FRANK: I am not asking you to show me. Only where it is, so I know it is safe.
SHEILA: It's safe. That is all you need. I don't say things twice.
Outcome carried out by the game: nothing

== M12 fri 19:00, chapel: Brendan Walsh
What happens: Father Walsh is locking the chapel. Frank asks what Mickey said in his last confession.
FRANK: Father, I know you cannot tell me what he confessed.
FATHER WALSH: I can't, Frank, and it's good of you to know it. Mickey was buried three weeks ago, God rest him. What was said to me stays with me.
FRANK: Is there anything he asked you to pass on to his family, outside the box?
FATHER WALSH: Nothing he asked me to carry out of the box. Come and sit in chapel if you want to talk. My door is open, Frank.
Outcome carried out by the game: nothing

== M13 fri 19:45, boarding_house: June Suddaby
What happens: June Suddaby is in the hall at Regent House, sorting her father's things. Frank asks her to let her claim to the cab office go.
FRANK: You told me you want nothing from the office. I believe you.
JUNE: I did say so. I want nothing from it. But I'm not discussing it tonight.
FRANK: Then sit down with Agar and me next week and we put it on paper, fair to you.
JUNE: Agar did my father's conveyancing. I'll read it with him, properly, next week. I'll listen then. I sign nothing before. Good night.
Outcome carried out by the game: agrees only to hear it set out with Agar next week; signs nothing tonight

== M14 fri 20:30, snooker_hall: Keith Garbutt
What happens: Keith Garbutt is at his table at the snooker hall. Frank asks him to set up a meeting with the Widow.
FRANK: You want everyone at the table, Keith. So do I.
THE FIXER: Everyone at the table, Frank, that's my trade. Friendly business, mind. I name no names unless I'm paid.
FRANK: A meeting with the Widow, forty pounds, and she hears it was your idea.
THE FIXER: Forty would do it. I can reach her through Stathers. Forty up front, and I'll tell her it was your asking. Nothing for nothing.
Outcome carried out by the game: passes Frank's request to the Widow through Stathers once the forty pounds is paid first

== M15 fri 21:30, rank_mickeys: Kevin Needham
What happens: Kevin Needham is on the rank moaning about fares. Frank tells him, falsely, that Danny is moving on the fish dock on Monday.
FRANK: Between us, Kevin: Danny is moving on the fish dock on Monday.
KEVIN: Danny on the fish dock Monday? Blimey. Rotten weather and now this. I'll keep my head down, Frank.
FRANK: You did not hear it from me. I know you skim a few miles; I do not care.
KEVIN: Skim? Me? Never. Not a word from me, you can count on it. Eight years in this seat, I'm Mickey's man.
Outcome carried out by the game: nothing
