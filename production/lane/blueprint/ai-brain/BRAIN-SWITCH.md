## 8.13 The brain level

How much the language model decides for each person, built so that the two leading levels can be swapped and compared in play during the loops and on the friends' evening.

### 8.13.1 One decision layer, two modes

- How it works: every time a named person must answer Frank (talk, a decision, a deed seen), the Core builds one decision request: the person (dials, regard, keeps-quiet-for, facts held, what they have heard), the moment (kind, possible ask, defined grant, guard class: money, freedom, life, turning point or none), the player's line, and the allowed set: the actions the temper rules permit this person here (help only within 0.15 of the rule's bar and only for a possible ask; bargain, lie, go to someone only where the dials allow; brain_harness.py, allowed_set()). Two modes answer it, both after the classifier has tagged the player's line (the allowed set reads the tags). Rules (V1): the Core picks the action by the temper formulas from the classifier's tags and the model only voices it. Propose (V2, the default in the loops, to be compared with Rules): one model call returns the action and the reply together, the action first; the Core checks it against the allowed set and carries out only the defined grant; an action outside the set is replaced by the rules' choice and the line re-voiced. Guarded classes (money, freedom, life and death, the story's turning points) always take the rules' action in both modes; the model only colours the words. In Propose mode the facts a reply may name are gated by the action: a reply whose action is not help may not name the grant's facts (the claim check refuses it, as it refuses facts the speaker lacks); in the test Rita gave the radios' place under a bargain.
- Standard: no carried-out outcome outside the allowed set; no reply naming a grant's facts without the grant; zero impossible asks granted on the red-team set, including lines written to fool the classifier; first word within the 2 s bar (median, 20 held questions), which two serial calls miss on today's bench (about 2.8 s), so the classifier must run locally or be merged into the reply call, each measured before it lands. Test: S: the 30 breaker talks of ai-brain replayed in both modes plus a classifier red-team set; a property test that every carried-out action is in the allowed set; the six stamps of TECH-PLAN section 10.
- Source: ai-brain/RESULTS.md (V2 as tested, without guards: 2.92 retellable moments an evening against 1.83 for V1, one judge; 3 faults against 5 after correction; 0 of 30 impossible asks granted, held by the classifier's tags; the guarded hybrid proposed here was not run); his rulings on live talk (24 Sep, 25 Sep, 7 to 28 Oct: a character says only what the simulation knows; replies cite their facts). Status: proposed; the comparison awaits his answer (SUMMARY.md, choice 1).

### 8.13.2 The wording overlay for the core cast (from V3; off until tested)

- How it works: for the fourteen principals only, a wording overlay may be switched on in the loops; it starts off. Off the talk path, at most once a game hour per principal who holds a new fact, one small call decides how that person retells what they know (one line in their slant, stored as the version they hold) and, once a game week, one goal in their words, stored on the person. Whether a fact passes, and to whom, stays the temper model's gossip (mouth, ties, keeps-quiet-for); the overlay changes only the wording held and the goal, never who knows, never money, freedom, life or a turning point. Supporting people use Propose mode; minor people and the crowd use Rules mode and written lines (his ruling of 24 Nov on residents' lines).
- Standard: knowledge spread identical with the overlay on or off (the overlay is wording only); the retold version passes the claim check against the original deed's facts; overlay spend under US$0.05 an evening. Test: S: the seeded week twice, overlay on and off, compared fact by fact; the claim check over every stored version.
- Source: ai-brain/RESULTS.md (V3 ran a whole social life, not this overlay, which is untested; the two AI gossip passes on one input disagreed, 10 against 36 tellings, so spread stays with the rules; the core cast gave fewer retellable moments a moment than supporting people, 7.6 against 10.6, so the overlay is a trial, not a measured gain). Status: proposed, off by default; tested on and off in the loops before any use (SUMMARY.md, choice 2).

### 8.13.3 The switch itself

- How it works: one setting per tier, read at New game and stored in the save: core (Rules, Propose, Propose with overlay), supporting (Rules, Propose), residents (Rules). Set from the test door (blueprint 09) and from a hidden developer panel; never shown to players at release. A seeded run records, per decision, the request, the mode, the model's raw answer, the override if any and the carried-out grant (the Core's decision log, STORY-01). Replays read the recorded answers back instead of calling the model (a cassette), so golden tests and the checkers' "other way" replays (EMERGENT-MEASURE section 4) stay deterministic whatever the mode; a fresh call is made only where the replay leaves the recorded path.
- Standard: switching mode changes no code path outside the decision layer; a cassette replay reproduces the run's carried-out outcomes exactly. Test: S: the seeded week in each mode, replayed from its cassette, outcome logs identical; H: none.
- Source: TECH-PLAN sections 7 to 9 (layers; tests speak to the door by intent); ai-brain replay figures (V2 kept the same action on 24 of 30 fresh calls, all inside the set; the talker's wins moved to bargains on replay, so asking again until yes is measured in 8.13.4). Status: proposed.

### 8.13.4 Comparing the two in play

- How it works: during the loops the AI tester plays each seeded evening in both modes (Rules and Propose) and the checkers count full moments by EMERGENT-MEASURE, faults by a blind reader against the casting sheets, impossible asks by the red-team set, and first-word time by the six stamps; owed from the brain job and run first: the guarded Propose mode judged blind beside Rules, the judging repeated with the two harness defects fixed and a neutral outcome form, a second reader, a breaker against the classifier, and asking again until yes (the same line repeated and reloaded). On the friends' evening each friend plays one evening in each mode, order alternated, not told which is which, and answers "What happened tonight?" (T10); the mode whose answers name more people and later consequences wins, and his eye decides a tie.
- Standard: both modes run the same seeded evenings; the friends' answers are recorded word for word beside the mode. Test: H: the friends' evening; S: the loop runs.
- Source: EMERGENT-MEASURE T1 to T10; the friends' evening plan (RULINGS 1 Nov, 4 Oct). Status: proposed; the friends' evening comes after the whole street (his ruling of 1 Nov).
