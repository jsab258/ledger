# Combat and stealth: summary

25 Nov 2026. Ready for review.

## For him, short

Shortfalls first: the feature list is not yet complete enough (a fresh designer's check still scores 19% missing against a 5% target); answers often leave a detail open (12 of 20 on a clean test); nothing is built or measured yet, so all comparisons with other games are on paper.

What a fight should feel like: fast and ugly, over in seconds; most trouble ends with a look, a word or a blade shown; anything a man could try, the game answers; sneaking runs on dark, noise and turned backs; the street watches and retells how it was done; every blow has a price later and always a way back; Frank's strength is his people.

What changed: one rule for injuries (what struck, how hard, where), one for each person's nerve, one for the senses, in place of special cases. Your fists rule stays. Crouch helps only in the dark.

Test fights (44,000 simulated): a careful player wins about 4 in 5 one-on-one, a button-masher 1 in 5; two against one about 3 in 10; with Ron about 4 in 5. One death in 2,000 fist fights; a knife used kills in about 9 fights in 100. Sneaking in a dark doorway works about 6 times in 10, by day 1 in 7.

Your decisions:
- D1. A choke held on after he passes out can kill: yes recommended, only if the player keeps holding.
- D2. Kicking a man on the ground can kill, rarely: yes recommended (fists alone still cannot).
- D3. A gunshot can kill at any time, not only once the town is at war: yes recommended; guns stay rare and late.

## What else

- On paper, against what the census found of GTA, RDR2 and KCD2: LEDGER's rules cover more on memory, retelling, menace as a verb, 1990 police and lasting consequence; the stealth senses are of KCD2's kind; it leaves out GTA's physical comedy, RDR2's and GTA's gunplay and KCD2's sword skill on purpose.
- The case rules replaced: "a knife kills only when finishing" and the like; your fists rule stays as a cap; whether a gunshot can kill before War is D3.
- Rulings to revisit for the keeper (RULINGS-TO-REVISIT.md): a shove into a wall kept at serious; the quay edge counts through drowning; your 12 Oct design's hospital bill stands against the blueprint's "never money"; a knife beats one unarmed man nearly always, as in life; burns and scalds out as cruelty; hostages as shields out; every animal safe.
- Shortfalls in detail: the census target (under 5%) is missed (miss rate 19%: 6% missed outright, 26% named only in general, counted at half); the clean set's 0 of 20 unanswered is too few to prove the 5% target; 81 census answers were mapped by the designer and not re-checked; the swap test (Frank's settings on a townsperson) is owed; weak answers are the larger share (the clean set: 12 of 20); the first outside judging found 20% unanswered and the design was revised against those same questions, so only the clean set is an honest measure; the simulator has no geometry and rests rare-event rates on few cases; the research found no published game detection or hits-to-down numbers and no stab lethality by body region (blocked medical sites, to read from the PC); every melee timing stays a lead until the footage drills.
- Left for a later run (in order): the clean set's thirteen additions (TESTS.md); a third blind check, run per heading; the come-round, sends, growth and police procedure in the simulator; the fire spread model for the place parts; how the story re-routes when a principal dies (the story pass); guns' feel once measured.
- One subagent published its question list as a private artifact page while editing it (nobody else can open it); it is not part of the deliverable and can be deleted.
