## 2. The hype segments

Two kinds of video sell systemic games: "I tried to break the NPCs" and "sneak up and...". Each line: the trick, what the reference games do, LEDGER's answer by rule, and a verdict on paper. A cell marked (not in the census) has no census source behind it and is the designer's general knowledge, to check from the PC; nothing here is measured, and LEDGER is a paper design set against shipped games.

Where LEDGER trails (on paper): GTA's and RDR2's sheer breadth of physical comedy (Euphoria ragdolls, ragdoll chains, vehicles in fights), gunplay depth (cover systems, Dead Eye), and KCD2's depth of melee skill (directional attacks, master strikes) which LEDGER leaves out on purpose (no combos, no skill trees). On paper LEDGER's rules cover more on memory, retelling, menace as a verb, period police and lasting consequence; whether that shows in play is for the build.
