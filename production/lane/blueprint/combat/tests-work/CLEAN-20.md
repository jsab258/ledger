C01. Can I pay a witness to say they saw nothing, then hit them myself an hour later, and do they remember the bribe or the beating when the police come round?
C02. What happens if I start a fight inside my own minicab office; do my own drivers take my side, stand back, or ring the police on me?
C03. Can I pick a fight with a man while his mate is in the toilet, and does the mate come back already knowing, or only once someone tells him?
C04. What happens if I knock a man down and then walk off, and he gets up and follows me home; does he know where I live from that alone?
C05. Can I start a fight with a stranger, lose on purpose, and does the town now think I am weak and try their luck with my drivers?
C06. What happens if I grab a man's coat collar in the middle of a calm talk; does the conversation carry on, or does the game jump straight to a fight?
C07. Can I use a cab as a weapon by opening the door into someone on the pavement, and is that treated as an assault, an accident or a crash?
C08. What happens if two people I have both wronged meet in the street; do they team up against me, or fight each other?
C09. Can I throw a bag of shopping, a dustbin lid or a chair, and does a thrown object hurt the same whether it hits a face or a back?
C10. What happens if I fight a man on a staircase or a sea wall; can I push him off, and does the game let him die from a fall when it never lets me?
C11. Can I keep punching a man who is already on the ground, and is there a point where witnesses turn on me or the police charge me with worse?
C12. What happens if I threaten someone with a gun I have not got, by keeping my hand in my coat pocket; does he believe me?
C13. Can I give a rival's informer a lift in my cab and quietly drive him somewhere remote, and does anyone notice he got in my car and never came back?
C14. Can I walk into a shop's back room wearing one of my drivers' firm jackets, and do staff treat me as a driver collecting a fare rather than a stranger? (stealth)
C15. What happens if a watcher sees me from a high window across the road; does perception reach that far through glass at night, and through net curtains? (stealth)
C16. Can I hide in plain sight by standing at a bus stop or in a queue, and does a searching man check faces in a crowd or only empty spaces? (stealth)
C17. What happens if I break a street lamp first and then sneak past; does the darkness help, and does anyone notice the lamp went out and come to look? (stealth)
C18. Can I follow someone on foot for ten minutes, and does he slowly grow suspicious if I take every same turning, or only if I get too close? (stealth)
C19. What happens if I am spotted, walk round the corner, change my coat and come back; does the witness remember my face or only my clothes? (stealth)
C20. Can I make noise on purpose, like slamming a car door or setting off a burglar alarm down the street, to pull a guard away, and does he grow wise if I do it twice? (stealth)
