# FRESH-30: player probe questions (combat and stealth)

N01. Can I crouch-walk right up behind a dock watchman in a fog-lit yard and choke him out, and does he remember my face when he wakes? (stealth)
N02. What happens if I knock out a man in an alley and drag his body behind the bins, then a passer-by finds him ten minutes later? (stealth)
N03. Can I smash a street lamp with a thrown half-brick to make a dark patch, and does anyone notice the lamp going out? (stealth)
N04. What happens if I hide in the boot of one of my own minicabs while the police search the car park? (stealth)
N05. Can I sneak through a terraced house at night while the owners sleep upstairs, and does a creaking stair or a dropped ashtray wake them? (stealth)
N06. What happens if a crew member I bring along on a quiet break-in starts chatting loudly or lights a cigarette in the dark? (stealth)
N07. Can I wear a borrowed donkey jacket and flat cap so a shopkeeper who saw me yesterday does not recognise me today? (stealth)
N08. What happens if I take down someone from behind on an empty-looking street with no one about, but an old woman across the road is watching from her net curtains? (stealth)
N09. Can I slip into the harbour and swim under a jetty to lose two coppers chasing me on foot, and do they wait on the quay or call for a boat? (stealth)
N10. What happens if I am caught halfway through a sneak, then just stand still and talk my way out by claiming I am the minicab driver come to collect a fare? (stealth)
N11. Can I pick up a broom handle, a wrench from a garage, or a fire extinguisher and use each as a weapon, and do they hit differently?
N12. What happens if I fight five lads at once in a narrow chip-shop doorway so only one can reach me at a time?
N13. Can I shove a man off the harbour wall into the water mid-fight, and does he drown, climb out, or come back for me later?
N14. What happens if I hit a woman or a pensioner who swings at me first, and how do the town and my crew react?
N15. Can I run someone down with a minicab, then claim to the police it was an accident with no witnesses about?
N16. What happens if I start a fight and a uniformed constable arrives on foot; can I fight him too, and what does that bring down on me?
N17. Can I surrender halfway through a beating by putting my hands up, and does the other side respect it, keep going, or rob me?
N18. What happens if the man I am fighting surrenders and I keep hitting him; does word get round and change how people treat me?
N19. Can I talk mid-fight, threaten him or offer him money between punches, and will the live conversation actually respond while we are swinging?
N20. What happens if I set fire to a rival's lock-up while someone is still inside, and does the fire spread to the houses next door?
N21. Can I order two of my crew to hold a man down while I question him, and what happens if one of them refuses or turns on me?
N22. What happens if I get beaten unconscious in the back room of a rival's scrapyard office, since I cannot die: where do I wake, and what have I lost?
N23. Can I leave a body in a car I then push into the docks, and will the police ever find it or link it to me?
N24. What happens if I keep kicking a downed man with no health bars to show me, and how do I know when I have gone too far and killed him?
N25. Can I throw a pan of hot chip fat or a kettle of boiling water in someone's face in a kitchen fight?
N26. What happens if the lad I humiliated in a fight last week turns up with his brothers and a cricket bat a fortnight later?
N27. Can I get a shotgun early by robbing a farmhouse, even though guns are meant to be rare and late, and what happens if I fire it in the town centre?
N28. What happens if I fight on a moving bus, a ferry deck or the roof of a car, and does anyone get thrown off?
N29. Can I grab a hostage, any adult bystander, and walk backwards to my car to stop the police or a rival crew closing in?
N30. What happens if I beat a man, then come back an hour later all friendly as if nothing happened; does he remember, does he run, or does he call the police?
