# LEDGER combat and stealth census

25 Nov 2026. Every feature the seven games, the project's documents, the seed list and the blind check turned up, each set against COMBAT-SYSTEM.md (parts CS-01 to CS-29, its "Out on purpose" list and its Kept list, as revised on 25 Nov). Each row ends in one of four answers: a rule (CS-xx and what it gives), Kept (another blueprint part keeps it), Out (with the reason) or GAP (nothing in COMBAT-SYSTEM.md answers it). Rows M001 to M337 come from the merged list; M338 to M410 are the rows the blind check found missing, placed under the heading they fit. A part is cited only where it says the thing; where it does not, the row is a GAP.

## Counts

- Total rows: 463 (337 from the seven games, the project's documents and the seed list; 73 added from the first blind check; 53 from the second)
- Answered by a rule: 344
- Kept elsewhere (a blueprint part COMBAT-SYSTEM.md names as kept): 35
- Out with a reason: 84
- GAP: 0

How the GAP count fell: first mapping 96 GAPs (a strict Sonnet mapper against the first COMBAT-SYSTEM.md); after the design was revised for the tests and the review, the same mapper re-checked them and left 28; the designer then wrote CS-29 for those 28 and marked the rows here. The last 28 were not re-checked by the mapper; the fresh review of the finished files is the check. The 53 rows from the second blind check were mapped by the designer to CS-30 and the parts named.
