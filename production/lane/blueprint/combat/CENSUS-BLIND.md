# CENSUS-BLIND: how complete the census is

25 Nov 2026. Two fresh Opus designers, each without sight of the census, wrote every combat, confrontation and stealth feature they would expect LEDGER to need or rule on (200 items each). A third and a fourth fresh Opus compared each list strictly against the census. Miss rate = (missed + half of partly) / 200. Target: under 5%.

## First blind check (against the census of 337 rows)

- Covered 116, partly 60, missed 24. Miss rate 27.0% (hard misses alone 12%).
- What was missed was mostly LEDGER-specific or practical: the camera in groups and tight rooms, rebinding and hold-or-toggle, sparring, pulling punches, hand injuries, ammunition and stashes, doors and light in a fight, wet cobbles and fog, bribes and extortion in a confrontation, hostages, crew nerve and injuries, outfit styles, raids, spreading brawls, friendly blows, the 1990 police force and the justice path, informants, washing clothes, telling out from dead, the paper and radio, settling a grudge, the town's mood over weeks, losing crew and ground, saves, subtitles and colour-blind cues, a gore setting, simplified controls, faces and clothes in a fight, attacks on story people and during scenes.
- All 73 suggested rows were added (census rows M338 to M410) before design began.
- Files: census-work/BLIND.md, census-work/COMPARE.md.

## Second blind check (against the census of 410 rows, after design)

- Covered 135, partly 53, missed 12. Miss rate 19.25% (hard misses alone 6%).
- What was missed: input buffering and footwork, the opening's weapon ladder, a single clean knock-out blow, dirty fighting, bystanders taking a dropped weapon, aiming a throw, property damage, dock hazards, tobacco in a confrontation, the first gun, possession, flagged armed men, misfires, getting rid of a gun, backing down and the town's memory of it, lies held, threats to relatives, lying under pressure, who struck first, barred player lines, talk timing in a fight, crouch looking wrong, long memory of watchers, alarms, a choke warning, takedown resistance, giving orders, crew deaths, credit for crew violence, rival outfits fighting each other, ambushes, protection jobs, hired muscle, hospital questions, blood in the street, grief, vehicle evidence, prison, honest police and bribes, raids on the firm, alibis, place weighing a deed, recovery, Frank's body, climbing and falls, a car into the water, stalking and repeat beating, shutting people in, impersonating police, asking others to fight.
- All 53 suggested rows were added (M411 to M463) and answered (45 by a rule, most in CS-30; 6 kept in named parts; 2 out).
- Files: census-work/BLIND-2.md, census-work/COMPARE-2.md.

## Verdict

- The target (under 5%) was not met: 27% at the first check, 19% at the second. Hard misses halved (12% to 6%). Most "partly" verdicts are a census row that names the feature without the specific question (for example "standoffs" against "a standoff with three men while a partner works").
- Each check found fewer new things than the last, and every row found is now in the census with an answer. A third blind check on the 463-row census is the next measure; at the rate of fall it would likely land near 10 to 12%, still over the target.
- What would close it: a blind check run per heading (one fresh designer for stealth alone, one for police and law, one for feel), since the misses cluster where a broad row stands in for many specific questions.
