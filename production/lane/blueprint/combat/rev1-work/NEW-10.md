# Ten new player questions: hit locations, weapon sources, searching the downed

N01 If I cosh a man across the face, does he walk round town next week with a broken nose and missing teeth, so everyone who saw it knows it was me?
N02 Can I break a man's knife arm with a bat so he drops the blade and cannot use that hand again, and does it stay broken for days?
N03 What happens if I stab someone in the leg only to stop him running, can he still bleed out from it, and does the town then call me a murderer?
N04 If I fire the sawn-off at a man's legs point blank versus across a room, is the result different, and does the spread hit the bloke stood behind him?
N05 Can I buy a kitchen knife or a hammer from a hardware shop in the Hook, and will the shopkeeper remember me and tell the police when a body turns up?
N06 If I carry a cosh or a blade in my coat and get stopped and searched, does the copper find it, and can I stash it under a seat in one of my minicabs instead?
N07 Can I take the revolver off a man who pulls it on me early in the game, before guns are meant to arrive, or does it vanish from his hand?
N08 Can I go through an unconscious copper's pockets for his radio, notebook and warrant card, and what happens when he wakes up and remembers my face?
N09 If someone only sees me kneeling over a dead man in an alley going through his coat, do they gossip that I robbed him or that I killed him?
N10 If I take a dead man's keys and wallet, can I then open his flat or his car, and do his mates notice his things turning up on me?
