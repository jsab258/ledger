- C09: give the throw's action force (for example the jab's 1.0 times the thing's row) and force rows for common light throwables (a dustbin lid, a bag of shopping), or say which are not flagged graspable.
- C10: a fall-from-height rule (stairs, a sea wall onto stone, a loading bay) by drop and the man's state, inside the body rule and the chance rule.
- C15: what glass, net curtains and a lit or dark room do to sight from a window, and whether height changes anything.
- C16: how an alert searcher treats a crowd or a queue: whether he scans faces, at what rate, and at what distance a face he knows is picked out.
- C18: whether repeated sightings of the same shape or coat on a tail add up to doubt (and how many or how long), alongside the look-back rhythm.

- N04: the sawn-off's pattern when aimed at the legs at each range band, and whether the point-blank mass can carry into a man directly behind.
