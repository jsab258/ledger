## Q1 Gunshot wounds by region

| Figure | Source (read 2026-11-25) | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Stockholm, 235 firearm patients 2005-2016: 30-day mortality 12.8% (30); 24 died within 24 h, 23 of them with head/neck or chest injuries; single-site early deaths head/neck 6, chest 4. Not found: percentage mortality for neck, abdomen, lower limb; prehospital split. UNVERIFIED. Direction only: head and chest dominate deaths; limbs rarely fatal.
