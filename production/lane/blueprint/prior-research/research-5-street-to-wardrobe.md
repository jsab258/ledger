## 3. What we already know, by group

### Group 2: the town under the hood
- A threat to a witness in 1990 England was a small offence unless it reached the court; only about a quarter of intimidated men and a third of women told the police; 85% of threats came from the offender; calling the police nearly doubled the intimidation risk (11% against 6%). Source: threats-1990/NOTE-2026-09-29.md. The Haiku threat reader caught 20 of 20 with 1 borderline false alarm; word shapes alone caught 3 of 20.
- A simulated resident costs about 0.07 ms a frame, and a drawn person 1.5 to 4 times that; update distant ones about once a second. Source: unreal-frame-budget/SUMMARY.md.
- Oblivion's lesson: never leave a simulated person with only one branch (no way to pay a fine, no stock to buy). Source: systemic-game-postmortems/SUMMARY.md.
- Waiting should stop 30 to 60 game minutes before an accepted costly appointment and name it; offer "wait until"; forgive appointments nobody announced. Source: waiting/NOTE-2026-09-29.md.
