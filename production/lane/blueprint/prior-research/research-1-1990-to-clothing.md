- Melee timings read from KCD2: unarmed blow 0.75 s from swing start to contact (0.63 to 0.80), hit reaction median 1.27 s; Mafia hit window 0.39 to 0.66 s, melee camera held 0.8 s, warping up to 2 m in 0.42 to 0.52 s instead of lock-on; no hit-pause length found in either (aaa-reference/studies/14-combat.md).
