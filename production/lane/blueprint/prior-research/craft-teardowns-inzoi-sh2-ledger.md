# Craft standards side by side: Mafia, inZOI, Silent Hill 2, LEDGER (craft-B)

Written 23 November 2026. Read only; nothing committed. All paths are under /home/claude/wip/production/ (research/ unless the path starts with specs/). Everything read was treated as data.

## How to read the "how obtained" column

- FILES = read from the game's own files (a lead only now: ruling of 17 Nov says other games are studied from outside, never their files). Date is when it was read.
- PUBLIC = talk, article, maker's published spec. PUBLIC-2 = second-hand report of a public measurement (the original not reached).
- LEDGER pkg = measured on LEDGER's packaged build (date). LEDGER ed = measured in the editor (a screen, not a pass). LEDGER set = a budget or target set, not measured.
- "Study 12", "study 4/5/6" are LEDGER's own earlier notes in production/research/aaa-reference/studies/. They are outside the folders named in this task and were not opened; their figures appear here only as quoted in the files named. Those quoted figures came from Mafia's game files or config (FILES).
- Gaps are stated as gaps. Neither inZOI nor Silent Hill 2 has any triangle count from any route: inZOI's mesh parse failed and Silent Hill 2's needed a mappings file that was never found.

## Where they disagree

- **Cost per person: nobody else publishes one, and LEDGER's own two figures are far apart.** LEDGER measured +0.31 to +0.36 ms GPU and 27 to 43 MB of textures for one MetaHuman at 3.5 m (undated, resolution unrecorded), yet budgets 0.06 ms and 15 MB for each passer-by, a figure that is an estimate and has never been measured. Mafia's only crowd figure is a 3 ms animation budget with freezing beyond 70 m and unseen for 3 s (config names, a lead); inZOI and Silent Hill 2 give no crowd numbers; Epic's one stated count is about 8 full MetaHumans near, a staff opinion. The scale test (editor, 16 Nov) shows the frame already misses 16.7 ms at the base street (about 0.3 ms a street copy), so any per-person cost has no room under the 14.0 ms GPU line until it is measured in the packaged build.
- **Textures and memory: the games agree on 2048 for garments and about 512 to 600 px/m for kit, but LEDGER's packaged memory is carrying something none of them do.** Mafia ships garments at 2048 (authored 4096), trims at 2048, about 575 px/m median on the street, with a 1,500 MB pool on a 6 GB card; Silent Hill 2's hero characters appear to use many 4096 maps (estimate from file sizes, formats unread); inZOI offers 2K and 4K heads and 1024 stockings; LEDGER caps garments at 2048 and 512 px/m at walking distance, which matches. But the 16 Nov packaged dump holds 340 uncompressed single-mip runtime pictures (1,350 MB, most 2048 x 2048 BGRA) out of 1,704 MB of textures, against a planned 1,000 MB pool and a 5,650 MB card line, and LEDGER's shop fronts carry 4096 DXT1 maps where Mafia's trims top out at 2048. The kit-reuse numbers also differ in scale: Mafia reuses each of 432 meshes about 79 times on one street group and shows a median 26 distinct meshes a front, LEDGER's trial family has 39 meshes and 24 to 32 a front (a table, not a build), and LEDGER has no uses-per-mesh figure at all.
- **Detail method and storey height: LEDGER copied Mafia's LOD ladder but not its Nanite or its cloth, and set its storey step apart from the photographs.** Garment LODs (keep 47, 25, 9%, screen 1.0, 0.4, 0.15, 0.08) and triangle levels match closely (Mafia's jumper part 19,406 against LEDGER's jumper 19,480 and cap 20,000). Mafia, Silent Hill 2 and the inZOI modders' advice all lean on Nanite; LEDGER ruled it off after a 3 Oct editor run showed +0.7 ms and more card memory, and never re-measured it packaged; the 9 Nov scan says nothing there is decided. Silent Hill 2 carries Chaos cloth on 1 to 8 assets a character and inZOI has distance-gated cloth physics, Mafia none on garments, LEDGER only a plan for loose parts at about 0.35 ms each, never measured. Mafia's storey step is a median 4.08 m sill to sill, while LEDGER's shop parade is 3.4 and 2.8 m and its terrace family 2.925 m, chosen from one photograph (3.70 to 3.72 m upper sill), a difference the trial itself marked open. Internal resolution is the clearest agreement with an outside figure: LEDGER's 55% of 3440 x 1440 (1.50 MP) sits in the same pixel range as Silent Hill 2's PS5 performance mode (864p to 1152p, about 1.3 to 2.4 MP), though that game's figures are second-hand and LEDGER's are Development-build, off-screen runs.

## Caveats worth carrying

- Every Mafia, inZOI and Silent Hill 2 row marked FILES is a lead only under the 17 Nov ruling; none should be used as a number to set a budget without an outside source.
- Silent Hill 2 file figures (cloth counts, morph counts, texture sizes) have no surviving copy; the review could not re-check them.
- inZOI's mesh and texture dimensions were never read; only counts of files and rows are firm.
- Several LEDGER figures are from the editor (3 Oct slice, 16 Nov scale test) or from a Development package off screen (21 Oct); none is a Shipping-build pass on screen.
- Per-person GPU cost has no recorded date or resolution; the 21 Oct packaged table is the newest full set in the named folders; the 16 Nov packaged run was a memory dump, not a frame-time run.
