# Question writer brief

The game: LEDGER, a third-person crime RPG for PC, set in Britain in 1990 (October to December), in a northern-ish English port town called Meridian; the playable map is one district, the Hook, plus its docks. The lead, Frank Nowak, inherits his late uncle's minicab office (Mickey's) and can build a crime empire: crew, bribes, protection, rival gangs fought or played off, a bent detective. The town sees, remembers and gossips: every townsperson is an AI person with memory, nerve, loyalty and a temper, who talks live (free speech with Frank), tells others what they saw, and can go to the police, who act only on what witnesses saw and said. Guns are rare and late; most violence is fists, coshes, knives, bottles are NOT used (no alcohol anywhere). PEGI 18. No alcohol, pubs or gambling shown or spoken of; tobacco allowed; no children anywhere in the game. Cars: arcade-ish 1990 cars, minicabs, a few chases. The promise: "can you" should reach far wider than Kingdom Come: Deliverance II, Red Dead Redemption 2 and GTA V: if a player thinks of something sensible to try, the world should have an answer.

His ruling (25 Nov): every job and story beat has at least three routes (talk or menace, stealth or cunning, force), each leaving different traces; Frank's reputation is the build (the town's reading of him: feared, trusted, quiet, cruel, generous changes what each route allows); there is no skill tree. The story runs about seven weeks of game time, October to December 1990.

Your job: write about 27 questions about the GAME AS A WHOLE from each lens you are given, NOT about single situations. They ask how a whole playthrough goes for a style of play, how the hours feel, what changes over the weeks, whether systems hold up over a full run. Each concrete and specific, e.g.:
- Can a player who never hits anyone reach every ending, and how does week four look for him?
- If Frank wins every fight, does fear stop mattering by week five because nobody is left to frighten?
- On a second playthrough, which story beats happen on the same day whatever Frank does?
Rules: no alcohol, gambling or children; no real brands; plain British English; no em-dashes. Do not research the game (unreleased). Vary widely; avoid near-copies of each other and of the examples.

Output: write to the file path you are given, one question per line, each line "- [lens name] question". Nothing else. Reply with just the counts per lens.
