# The giant question pass: summary (25 Nov 2026)

## For Jafar, short

996 player questions were put to the blueprint: 675 new ones from 14 kinds of player, 315 asked in earlier passes and 6 from the final check. Shortfalls first: 217 missing, 464 partly answered and 10 where the blueprint contradicts itself. 270 are answered well, and 35 are ruled out by your rulings or canon.

Almost every gap falls under 24 general rules, so the work is 24 rules rather than 691 separate fixes. The biggest six:

- things carry their owner and the facts of how they moved, and can be found (70 questions)
- Frank as a source of talk (48)
- favours, debts and bribes as one ledger (48)
- every claim checked against what the listener already knows, police interviews included (45)
- regard moved by deeds and kindness (43)
- the firm's name moving its trade (39)

545 of the 691 change how the game is built, so they must be settled before the first full build.

The miss rate is 10%, against a target under 5%. A fresh check wrote 40 new questions, and 4 of them were gaps this pass had not listed (6 by the strict count): losing a fight costs Frank nothing, there are no plain-clothes police, nobody says whether a nurse reports a stab wound, and rain does not wash away traces. They have now been added, but nobody has checked them independently.

32 decisions are yours, in DECISIONS-FOR-HIM.md. The top three:

- a man held hostage and walked past watchers (recommend yes)
- whether a drowned man can die (recommend yes, since the combat pass already allows it)
