# How LEDGER's people differ: summary

24 Nov 2026. Every named person now has five dials, each from 0 to 1:

- Nerve: how hard they are to frighten. Who folds to a word, runs from a knife, pays a debt or stands. Fear is a trait of its own.
- Greed: how readily money moves them. Who takes a bribe, sells what they know or leaves for a rival's pay. Honesty predicts wrongdoing best.
- Mouth: how much they pass on. Who tells everyone and who tells only a sister.
- Law: how readily they go to the police. Who walks to the phone box and who never would. Most people keep quiet because it seems not serious enough or the police could do nothing.
- Grudge: how long they hold a wrong. How far they cool after a slight, and whether they come round.

From the old four: nerve stays; dutiful becomes law; willingness was never a trait and goes; keepsQuiet becomes the setting the game already expects. Three unused fields go too.

In a test week over 118 townspeople the old values gave 3 kinds of behaviour, the new 56. One word frightened 86% of the town before, 42% now. 15 of 63 witnesses reported (24%), near the British figure of 29%.

What the player will notice: the same deed seen by Ada, Darren and Grace goes three ways, and an honest witness can rarely be bought.
