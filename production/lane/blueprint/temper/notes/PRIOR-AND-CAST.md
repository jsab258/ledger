# Prior knowledge bearing on each person's temper (read 24 Nov 2026)

## A. What the AAA studies found (production/research/lane/aaa-studies/)
All player counts are thin (studies say so); critics and makers better reached.
- Interchangeable people are the most punished fault: Watch Dogs: Legion gave citizens routines and memories but traits were "drearily one-note", swapping agents cost nothing (PC Gamer, Brown, 5 Nov 2020). Of 6 voices, 5 punish interchangeable people, 3 repeated or ill-fitting voices; Steam (7 reviews) praised "recruit anyone" 3, flat people 2. Some citizens hate the player too much to recruit; harm spreads to friends and family; someone hurt by one operative may not know a different one (Game Developer, 9 Dec 2020).
- Nemesis: a few named orcs with a name, face, scar, voice. 19 of 22 Shadow of War Steam reviews value it (only 2 read in full). Taunt with the history in the words is the most praised memory. Stories: 21 Mordor stories, 10 came back, 9 named the orc; a cowardly captain who kept escaping rose to warchief. Comedy from honest rules: "a coward orc running on sight". A faction-wide stat was built and abandoned by the makers.
- Rules must be readable. Hitman: 5 of 9 players coded punish noticing that is unfair or unreadable (seen through walls, random trespass flags); 2 of 13 Steam reviews praise readable, consistent NPC reactions; routines are set and predictable on purpose; a private memory per person plus shared knowledge; disguises seen through only by the same kind of person (clear, welcome). Players punish rule-breaking more than harshness.
- KCD2 (K9 to K14): opinion kept per person, rolled to group and village; witnesses report to a guard or confront; unreported crime can be found later; lines react to the exact act (about half the recorded lines are not story). Recognition and witnesses: 21 stories, 7 came back; the teller blames or admires the person, not the system. Widow who recognised stolen gear; stable hand saw a horse theft, arrest three days later. Clothes and cleanliness change talk odds, overdone intimidation costs standing, past help can skip a test.
- RDR2: most crimes need a witness; threatening a witness usually stops the report (rdr2.org, no date). Players: 4.5% punish arbitrary-seeming law.
- Talk (TALK-AS-PLAY): a choice that does not do what it says is much punished (L.A. Noire "Doubt" made hero shout threats; 2 of 12 long reviews). Personality without consequence is an AI-NPC failure (ai-npc-demos-hollow, Sep 2026; 77 to 83% negative sentiment on generative AI).
- Standards already written that touch temper: MEM-03 (every person whose temper reaches the refuse rung refuses Tom something within two game days), MEM-05 (no reaction without a printable chain), MEM-07 (witness meetable again, own face/place/line, two witnesses never same line in a day), MEM-09 (nothing buys a memory away; money, threats and friendship change what he does with it), MEM-11 (a refusal always leaves another road), TALK-05 (unsettled deal or threat cited in next talk), TALK-09 (a person answers what he knows, or refuses in character for a reason of his temper or loyalty, never an empty dodge), FF-04, FF-05 (a frightened witness may stop a report; police know only what was perceived).
- Failure study: nothing on personality beyond the RDR2 witness rule.

## C. Earlier research (PRIOR-RESEARCH-INDEX.md), with verdicts
- police-response-1990 (29 Sep 2026, holds): reporting rates (1991 survey) vandalism 27%, burglary with loss 92%, common assault 25.5%, wounding 47.7%, robbery 47.2%, overall 43%; witnesses on high-crime estates report 29%, fear of reprisal behind 22% of silence.
- threats-1990 / game-design/threats (29 to 30 Sep, holds; fed a ruling of 3 Oct): no witness-intimidation offence until 1994 (CJPOA s.51); vague threat at most POA 1986 s.5 (fine to 400 pounds); perverting the course of justice rare (816 convictions 1992); Home Office work found threats silenced about a quarter. Index note: 2.5.2 should use these rates to set how often a threat works (not yet done).
- eyewitness-testimony (14 Sep, partly holds): ID accuracy 96% at 5 m, 42% at 20 m, about 30% at 40 m; weapon focus; talking witnesses corrupt each other; confidence rises with retelling. Ruling 30 Sep: a witness's story is only as sure as the witness was.
- rumour-propagation (14 Sep, partly): levelling/sharpening not reliable in natural rumours; max-confidence merge ignores corroboration.
- coverage-audit-rdr2 (14 Sep): coat not face recognised; the minute between being seen and reported (witness must walk to tell).
- crime-and-combat-coverage (21 Sep): Core had crew loyalty, feuds, four-stage police inquiry; missing order verbs.
- ending-reading (28 to 30 Sep): hidden loyalty thresholds not readable by signs; friend says "Tom" from 0.45 but counts only from 0.55.
- ai-npc-demos-hollow, research-openworld-audit (July; witnesses report to authority adopted), cast-noor-draft (superseded; cards had traits and romance as loyalty thresholds), dialogue-crime-witness-v1 (24 witness lines; some lines void by the content rule).
- aaa-reference 01-npc-life (25 Oct, reviewed, pass with fixes): KCD2 witness chooses among about 43 interruptions (look, flee, attack, arrest, warn, report), violent crime raises priority; reports go to a guard or friend, carried 10 m, report within 15 m, up to 150 m (500 m important); fleeing keep 30 m away 7 min; reputation weighted median person:faction:wider 5:3:1, reaches faction after about 3 h; murder -0.6 person/-1.5 reported, theft -0.25/-0.15, assault -0.3/-0.3; idle gossip carries no crime information. Mafia: no heat, no gossip; civilians three states (patrol, patrol without panic, panic). LEDGER already has: gossip by routine meetings within 6 m, confidence falling per hop, who-told-whom, 96 h rumour half-life (body never), witnesses who walk, can be stopped, report permanent on arrival. Take: crime expiry by kind (1 to 7 days).
