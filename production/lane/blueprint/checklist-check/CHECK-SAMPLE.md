## Systematic errors across the whole of CHECKLIST-VS-BLUEPRINT.csv (then merged.csv)

Each rule says which rows it touches and what the verdict should become. Rules 1 to 5 change verdicts on clear grounds. Rules 6 and 7 are about keeping the same kind of item consistent. Rule 8 is housekeeping, and rule 9 is about the basis rather than a helper's error.

### 1. AX-05 not read in full: two rows marked missing that it covers

The helpers who wrote "add to AX-05" did not check AX-05's whole text. Its test includes "a rebind of every key with no conflict left unflagged", and its body gives aim assist three steps once guns exist. H4 itself marked A42.18 (aim-assist settings) covered by AX-05.

- A19.18: missing to **covered**, parts AX-05 7.10, note "partly: aim assist off, light, strong once guns exist; no pad-specific tuning".
- A42.09: missing to **covered**, parts AX-05, note "partly: the rebind test flags every conflict; no wording for the warning".
- Checked and left as missing: A03.19, A03.20, A42.12, A42.14, A42.15, A42.17, A45.04, A45.08 (AX-05 does not cover these).

### 2. Wardrobe items marked out under OUT-01 or OUT-06, despite 1.6.10

OUT-01 bars other playable leads and OUT-06 bars stats. Neither bars Tom's three outfits, dark coat, barber, or scarf and hat (1.6.10, 5.1.4), and those choices change play.

- A38.08: out to **missing**, note "Decide in or out: no preview when buying a coat or at the barber".
- A38.10: out to **covered**, parts 1.6.10 5.1.4, note "outfits, barber, scarf and hat show on Tom, and witnesses recognise them (mark rung)".
- A38.13: out to **missing**, note "Needs a way for the player to learn what a coat or the barber changes; every outfit is mechanical".
- A38.09 stays out (no character creation: canon).

### 3. Out on items about reading Tom's own state or a blocked action, which the same kind of item elsewhere got covered

H4 cited OUT-06 (no meters or skills) on two items whose need the design meets by other means. H2 and H1 marked the matching items covered: A17.11, A20.06, A20.07 and A11.09.

- A34.09: out to **covered**, parts 1.3.7 RT-11 1.3.11, note "partly: states shown in the body with set durations and cures; no words explain them".
- A34.04: out to **covered**, parts 1.3.2 1.1.3 FIT-P34, note "partly: spent wind shows in breath and recovers in 4 to 8 s; a locked door says so; no general unavailable cue".
- Rule: an OUT-06 row stays out only when the feature needs a number, stat, perk or gear statistic (A36.09, A38.05 and O6 stay out).

- Mechanical test: an out row whose parts hold no OUT-nn and whose note cites no RULINGS or canon line. That test finds only these two.

### 6. Animation and face items split three ways for the same kind of feature

H1 marked body animation unreal in section 9, but marked items of the same kind covered or missing in sections 6, 8, 10, 13 and 19 (H2). Every animation part in chapter 5 (5.2.1 to 5.2.9) has status "outside", meaning Unreal-only.

Rule: if an item is about how bodies, faces, eyes or held things move or look, and the only parts supporting it are chapter-5 parts with status "outside" (or none), its verdict is **unreal**. Keep the same parts, gap and "partly:" note, and use "none" plus what is needed where nothing covers it. Where a non-chapter-5 part supplies the rule, keep the verdict (for example A09.17, A09.19 to A09.21, A10.02, A10.12, A32.05).
