# Review: blueprint data file 10, lines/ (24 Nov 2026)

Fresh reviewer, about 30 minutes. Read: lines/README.md and every file in lines/, tools/wave2/build_lines.py, the source bank (production/cloud-week/lines/lines.json), suggested-lines.json, the story frame, TECH-PLAN section 3, schemas.json (files_to_come lines/<file>.json), the blueprint parts named in the brief, map.json landmarks, places.json, content-words.json, people/. Sampled about 90 shared lines across all 14 situations and 9 kinds, all 58 Frank lines, all 31 story lines, all asked_way lines for 13 places, Ron's, Darren's and June's own lines. Content gate: no alcohol, gambling, child, slur, brand or post-1992 word found in any written text (the boarded pub and the old school are allowed places, canon D17/D18). `validate_data.py`: 324 passed, 0 failed, but it checks only the format of lines/ (see point 15).

## Must fix

1. **Shared lines say Frankie.** shared-street.json office_worker.greet-frankie.01 to .09 and pensioner.greet-frankie.01 to .09, and own-brendan_walsh.json father_walsh.greet-frankie.01 to .09. These are the old greeting.tommy lines with the name swapped. That means Sheila and Alison (office_worker) and Ada (pensioner) would greet him "Frankie" ("Frankie, I owe you a word of thanks"; "Frankie, dear. I knitted this"), and so would Father Walsh. The fourth rung's pet name belongs to two or three people only: the Widow, Danny, and Ron once. Should be: take these 27 lines out of the shared and Walsh banks, and give Frankie only to those three, marked TO WRITE in their own files. Source: decision of 24 Nov (the ladder); BLUEPRINT-07 7.7 ("Tommy to two or three"); STORY-FRAME section 2 (three people say it); story.json story.name_said.

2. **The fourth rung has no greetings.** In shared-street.json every greet-frank line has `standing: ["frank"]`. The only lines for `frankie` standing are the 18 in point 1, so at the top rung seven of nine kinds, plus Ron, Darren and June, have nothing to greet him with. The street goes quiet just as he reaches the top. Should be: the fourth rung (the port's man) gets greetings from every kind using "Mr Nowak" or "Frank" (either widen the greet-frank lines to `["frank","frankie"]` or write new ones, TO WRITE), and the situation is renamed for the rung (for example greeting.portsman), not for the pet name. Source: 7.7 (each rung heard within a game day, a step never goes back); 2.12.4.

3. **No greetings by regard.** The bank sorts greetings by rung only, and nearly all of them are warm. At the Frank rung, with five cars and men at the gate, he gets "Hello, Frank. Cadge a fag?", "Frank, love. Let me look at that coat." and "Frank. Heard of any work yet?". Nobody is afraid, angry or cold. Should be: add regard (afraid, angry, knows and likes him, his appearance, neutral) as a condition, at least 6 lines per regard per kind. Tag the existing warm lines "knows and likes him" and list the rest as TO WRITE. Source: BLUEPRINT-02 2.12.4; BLUEPRINT-03 3.6.4 ("greetings by regard are how the rise is heard").

4. **Banks the blueprint requires are missing, but the README says "all, none to write".** Missing: neighbours greeting each other, talking to oneself at work, a body or a man down (3.6.4); the comment, refuse and confront steps of the knowing ladder ("you want to watch yourself, son", 2.4.3); bystanders talking about his recent deeds second-hand (3.6.4, catalogue 23 Nov). Should be: a TO WRITE record for each, with the count needed (6 per kind), in shared-street.json or story.json. The README table should list them too. Source: 2.4.3, 3.6.4.

5. **Too few situations are live in an ordinary hour, and there is no line table.** The bank's 14 situations are nearly all event-gated: one greeting rung, bumped, farewell, plus rain, crime, after a deed, or being asked the way. In a dry, quiet hour about three are live, against the 14 the blueprint needs, so shared lines will repeat within the hour. Schemas.json says the fixed situation table lives in lines/situations.json, but that file was not made. Should be: make lines/situations.json (each situation, when it is live, and its kinds), and add the roughly 120 everyday lines the inventory asks for as TO WRITE. Source: FIT-F19; 3.6.3 (no shared line twice in a real hour); NUMBERS-RUN P3; INVENTORY section 2; schemas.json x-note.

6. **`who` cannot tell a kind from a person, and most speakers have no kind.** Shared lines use bare bank kinds (`office_worker`, `pensioner`, `anyone`). directions.json uses `passenger`. story.json uses `any`, `rivals`, `fare_people` and `givers`. All of them pass the schema only because its pattern also accepts any person id, and none uses the schema's `kind:` form or its kinds (man, woman, older_man, older_woman, docker, shopkeeper, constable, driver). On top of that, 78 of 118 people (all the minors, officers and rival men, Ron and Danny among them) and every crowd extra in crowd.json have no speaker kind, so the one-shot has to guess who may speak a shared line. Should be: write `who` as `kind:<k>`, set the schema's kind list to the bank's kinds (or map one onto the other in writing), define `passenger` as a list of people ids, and give every people file and the crowd look table a speaker kind. Source: TECH-PLAN section 3 (lines); schemas.json lines `who` pattern; shared-street.json open point 1.

7. **The conversion dropped `calls_police` and `dialect`.** build_lines.py copies bank, context, rung, conditions, weight, group and delivery, but not `calls_police` (18 lines) or `dialect: local` (19 lines). So "Somebody ring the station." (shopkeeper) can reach Rita, Danny's fence, and Lee Drewery, the Widow's eyes, can be given a police line. Local dialect lines can reach voices that are not local, such as Father Walsh or the Tangs. Should be: keep both fields on every record, and give `never_police` a home in the data, since none exists today. Source: the bank's `fields` (calls_police, dialect); rules speaker_overrides.never_police.

8. **People files point to own-line files that do not exist.** 64 people files set `lines.own` to lines/own-<id>.json, and no such file exists. That includes sheila_dunn, alison_sedman and ada_vane, who by ruling keep shared lines only, the officers (carol_ellis, pc_haigh, sgt_kitson and the rest) and all 54 minors. Should be: for Sheila, Alison and Ada, `lines.own` names their shared kind. Everyone else either gets a 30-line TO WRITE file ("everyone else keeps 30") or is plainly marked shared-only. Source: 3.6.3; FIT-L01; people/<id>.json schema (`own`: line ids or a TO WRITE marker).

9. **Chase lines cannot turn right, and no line says which way it turns.** Only way.*.06 to .08 are tagged direction.chase: "sharp left", "He'll lose you there" and "Turn here". In a chase no line ever says right. "Left after X" and "Right at X" are tagged direction.way. No record has a turn field (left, right, straight) or a side field ("X on your left"), so the picker cannot match a line to the road ahead. Should be: add `turn` and `side` to every direction record, give chases both left and right forms, and let the way lines serve in chases. Source: GV-03 (one landmark line per 20 s of chase, each on the way; test logs lines against the map).

10. **The foot-only and three-beat direction lines do not work in a car chase.** way.footonly.01 to .03 send a driver down ways no car fits ("nothing on four wheels gets through there"), have empty tokens and name places that are not landmarks. "He cannot follow you" is stiff for northern speech. way.*.01 repeats Ron's running joke ("Coming up on X. That's X. Leaving X.") 16 times: it gives no direction yet counts as one. Should be: tag foot-only lines `mode: foot` with map.json way ids and use them only in foot chases ("he can't"). Keep Ron's three-beat as a few voice lines that do not count toward the GV-03 rate. Source: GV-03; STORY-FRAME section 2 (Ron's voice, the running joke).

11. **Ron's own lines say he does not know the way.** own-ron_kirby.json ron.way.* (asked_way): "Never been. I stay on the rank.", "Haven't left the quay in years, friend.", "It's that way, up or down. I forget." Ron is the blueprint's named passenger who knows the way, and the frame has him as a docker of thirty years who drives the dock road. Should be: replace these with TO WRITE lines in which Ron knows every road and gives it by landmark. Source: GV-03; STORY-FRAME section 2 (Ron Kirby).

12. **The asked_way bank describes the old prototype street.** These are shared-street.json anyone.way-* and the 34 old places, marked as written. Examples: "Grace's? Across from Rita's, a bit up." (map.json: the caff is on the quay by the ginnel, about 58 m west of the phone box by Rita's); "The chapel's on the hill. You'll hear the bell." (map: Chapel Lane, the green door); "The open market? ... Tuesday, Friday, Saturday. Up the top." (places.json: the Thursday market pitches on the Quay); "Pensions are paid at the newsagent's" (the old pension counter). boatyard, customs_shed, warehouse_row, flats and boarding_house have no places.json id. Should be: check every asked_way line against map.json and places.json, set `destination` to a places.json id, and mark the wrong ones TO WRITE. Source: map.json landmarks `said`; places.json; shared-street.json open point 2.

13. **Frank's decision lines carry their stance only in the id, and the personal lines are not gated by rung.** frank.deal.*.yes/no/keep/refuse: the plain yes the game takes is coded only in the id string. Should be: a `stance` field (yes, no, keep, refuse). Also: frank.suggest.personal.05 "I'm still finding my feet here", .06 "It's all new to me, this" and .09 "Do you get many strangers down here?" need a rung condition (first two rungs only). And the source's rule "never the same line twice in one conversation" was lost: `once: never` drops it. Source: 3.6.5, 3.6.6; TALK-01 (stance-tagged); suggested-lines.json `about`.

14. **The own-line TO WRITE briefs leave the story lane guessing.** For example own-kevin_needham.json kevin_needham.own.to_write says "spread over greet, comment, weather, work, about_frank, about_the_street, close". These situations match neither the shared table nor the people schema (own, greet, close, situations). The brief carries no rung or regard condition and does not say what the person calls him at each rung: `name_ladder` is null on 117 of 118 people. Should be: each brief names its situations from the fixed table, says that greetings vary by rung and regard, and gives the name the person uses at each rung (nobody but the three says Frankie; nobody says Tom). Source: 3.6.3; 7.7; people/<id>.json lines schema; decision of 24 Nov.

15. **The validator passes a broken file 10.** validate_data.py checks lines/ for format only. It does not run the content gate on lines/ (the cross check covers `files`, not `files_to_come`), does not check that `who` is a real id or kind, and does not check that people `lines.own` targets exist. Points 6 and 8 pass as a result. Should be: add those three checks, plus "no Tom or Tommy in any text or situation". Source: schemas.json x-rules (every text passes content-words.json); cross_checks.

## Lesser points

1. Ron (94), Darren (93) and Father Walsh (112) are over the 60 quota, and June (102) is over her 30. That is harmless, but every one of these lines is tied to a situation (rain, crime, rung greeting, asked way). None is an everyday remark of their own, which is what FIT-L01's own street lines mean. The README should say the quota is a floor and that everyday remarks are still owed.
2. Ron's greet-frank lines call him "Frank" ("Frank. Sit down a minute."), but his voice is "boss" in his own greet-nowak lines and throughout the frame. Use "boss".
3. story.ron.icehouse names "the ice house", which is not a map.json landmark (the nearest is the ice hut at the fish dock). The story lane or the map should settle it. The "voice" samples carry `once: game` but are voice examples, not one-off lines.
4. Danny, the Widow, Agar and Garbutt each get 30 own street lines TO WRITE, though the source bank's `no_kind` says rival heads speak only in authored scenes and live talk, never a passer-by's greeting. Decide which rule holds and record it.
5. The register leans soft and general ("Whoops", "Heavens, that's cold", "Lovely to see a friendly face"). Only 19 of 1,626 bank lines are marked local. The 1990 idiom list (research/street-lines-1990) has not been applied as an acceptance check, as 3.6.4 asks.
6. The old hero's background leaks into the rung greetings: "Frank. How are the locks holding up?", "That lock of yours is on its last legs" (tradesman), "How's the paperwork treating you?" (office_worker). Read them against Frank's card.
7. The `stranger` standing sits outside the four-rung ladder. The README should define it as someone who knows nothing of him; 2.4.3 says such a person shows nothing, so check that stranger greetings stay neutral.
8. The people files carry only `lines.own`; the schema's `greet`, `close` and `situations` are absent.
9. The frame's own rung list still reads "the nephew, Nowak, Tom, the port's man" (section 2), while 7.7 and story.name_said say "the new owner". Flag it to the story lane so its scenes use the 24 Nov ladder. The frank.fixed.* lines are marked TO CHECK, which is right.
10. The README counts 131 direction lines, but the 171 asked_way lines the inventory counts as directions live in shared-street.json. Say so in the README so the one-shot finds them.
