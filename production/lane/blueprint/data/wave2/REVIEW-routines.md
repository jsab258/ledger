# Review: routines.json (blueprint data file 4)

Fresh, independent review, 24 Nov 2026. Read: routines.json, build_people.py (the check), cast_src.py and routines_fix.py through the build, places.json, map.json (doors, graph), timetables.json, police.json, fares.json, people/, BLUEPRINT 2.1.1, 2.5.5, 2.6.1, 3.2.2, 3.4.1, CLK-03, and the story frame of 26 Nov.

Re-runs: `build_people.py --check-only` gives 118 people, 0 fails, 18 warnings (all the provisional Tangs' takeaway). `validate_data.py` gives 324 passed, 0 failed, but it does not check routines.json at all yet (its header lists routines as still to come). So the only check on this file is build_people.py's, and that check is too weak to catch what follows. All walking figures below come from build_people.py's own graph and speeds (1.4 m/s, 1.1 m/s at 70 and over, 2 real seconds a game minute).

## Must fix

1. **The check does not apply CLK-03 or 2.1.1.** Both say a person's places lie within about 200 m of walking, with longer moves only between blocks of several game hours. The check has no distance limit at all: it only asks that a walk use no more than three quarters of the stay before it. 174 moves are over 200 m; the longest is 736 m (Lee Drewery, home to the dock gate, 263 game minutes on foot), and many are near 700 m (Maggie Firth, Mark Billany, Bill Huxtable, Jimmy Raw, Ken Dobbin, Paddy Rourke). It should: enforce a distance cap per CLK-03, restated for the 120 s hour (200 m is about 71 game minutes at 1.4 m/s; CLK-03 and 2.1.1 still give "2 game hours", the 60 s figure, which the keeper should correct), and fix the long commuters by rehoming them near their work, or sending them in and out by the bus stop or a soft edge. Source: CLK-03 (BLUEPRINT-07), 2.1.1.

2. **The rule "the walk comes out of the end of the stay before it" lets people leave their posts hours early, and the check never sees it.** A row says where someone is, but with long walks they are gone for most of it. 80 moves spend more than half the stay walking. Examples: Maggie Firth leaves the tea stall at about 01:55 though it runs to 06:00; Norman Dent leaves the warehouse row at about 03:30; Joe Cairns (70) leaves the second-hand shop at about 14:25 though it is open to 17:30; Susan Coverdale leaves the customs house at 14:25 (open to 16:30); Bill Huxtable leaves the pen at 09:45; Mark Billany is at the dock gate's tax hut for under two of his six hours; Terence Bould has 1.5 hours at the shipping office; Danny leaves the lock-ups at 17:40. It should: work out each departure (next row's time minus the walk) and fail when a keeper or worker leaves before his place's closing, or when time actually present is under half the row. Source: 2.1.1 (where they are, hour by hour); places.json hours; timetables.json shift_shopkeepers (keepers close 15 minutes after the hours).

3. **26 day people set off from home between 01:24 and 04:39.** Because of point 1 and 2: Dougie Saltmarsh 01:24, Lee Drewery 01:32, Jimmy Raw 01:32, Ken Dobbin 01:35, Bill Huxtable 01:37, Mark Billany 01:41, Eddie Birtwhistle 01:59, Brenda Laverack 02:16, Albert Suggitt (78) 02:53, Colin Mastin 02:57, Stathers 03:01, Edna Coulson 03:36, Paddy Rourke 03:45, Doreen Harland and Gordon Leckenby 03:50, Harry Holt 04:24, Ron Kirby 04:29 (for a 06:30 breakfast; his day then runs to 23:00), the Widow 04:35, and others. That is not a 1990 working day, and it puts a crowd of named people on the night streets inside the night beat's hours, breaking "people sleep at night". It should: no day worker leaves home before about 05:00 (dock and fish-dock men about 04:30 at the earliest, Hull bobbers excepted per 2.1.1's prior research); the check should flag it. Source: 2.1.1; 2.1.2 (01:00 to 05:30, 0 to 2 people besides the constable).

4. **The "(police.json)" exemption is too wide.** Any row whose act, or whose previous row's act, contains "(police.json)" is not reach-checked. So PC Ibbotson goes from the caff at 12:30 to the cold stores at 13:00: 584 m in 30 game minutes, which needs 209 game minutes at 1.4 m/s and 266 at the constable's own 1.1 m/s. It should: exempt only the beat's own route rows, check every other move of a constable, and use police.json's 1.1 m/s for constables. Source: police.json beat_constable walk_speed_mps; 2.6.1.

5. **The opening-hours check tests only the first minute of a row and only acts matching a word list.** Missed as a result: Danny (to 23:30), Craig Fewson and Shane Boddy (to 23:00) at the snooker hall, which shuts at 22:30 (Vince goes home at 22:45); Bill Huxtable at the caff 14:00 to 15:30 Monday to Thursday, when it shuts at 15:00; Pat Brannigan at the boxing club 16:00 to 18:00 Monday to Saturday, when it opens only Monday, Wednesday and Friday 18:00 to 21:30 and Saturday 10:00 to 13:00; Margaret Lofthouse leafleting at the market pitches on Monday, Wednesday and Friday, when the market is Thursday only. It should: test the whole stay, for every row at a place with hours whose person is not that place's keeper or staff (list staff by place, not by verb). Source: places.json hours; timetables.json market.

6. **The 2.5.5 finders list is hand-written and not checked against the routines, and Edna Coulson's entry is wrong.** FINDERS says the cleaner at six does the Harbour Board, the Copper Kettle and the agents' office (her card says the same); her routine cleans Mickey's at 06:00, the tea room at 07:00 and the snooker hall at 08:15, and never goes near the Harbour Board or Agar's. Nobody reaches the Harbour Board before Christine Pickering at 08:45. It should: Edna's rows follow her card (Harbour Board at 06:00, then the Copper Kettle, then Agar & Co), or the card and finder change; and build_people.py should fail when a finder's routine does not reach each "passes" place at the stated time. Source: 2.5.5 (the cleaner at six).

7. **Two more finders do not do what the list claims.** Bill Huxtable, "the first dock worker along the quay each morning (05:15, past the landing)": his routine walks from North Road straight to the dock gate (694 m; by the landing it is 960 m), and by the walk rule leaves home at 01:37; his own fares.json ride instead has him at the bus stop 05:00 to 05:45. Norman Dent "the warehouse row, all night, including the net loft and the burned warehouse": his only row is Dent's warehouses, 300 to 350 m from the other two, and he leaves it at about 03:30. Eddie Birtwhistle (04:45) leaves home at 01:59. It should: Bill's home or route put him on the quay road past the landing at 05:15 and at the pen by 05:45; Norman's round name the three places in turn until 07:00; the finder times match the rows. Source: 2.5.5 (the first dock worker); story frame worked example 2 (Norman Dent at Iveson's hut).

8. **The firm's shifts contradict timetables.json.** Kevin: timetables 06:00 to 18:00 every day; routine 07:00 to 19:00 Monday to Saturday and 12:00 to 18:00 Sunday. Terry: timetables 18:00 to 06:00, and "the man on the radio set in the car between fares" from 19:00 to 07:00; routine sends him home to bed at 03:00. So from 03:00 until Pauline arrives (06:45, Sunday 09:00) no one has the radio, though the 24 Nov decision says it is manned 24 hours. Pauline: timetables 07:00 to 19:00 every day; routine Sunday 09:00 to 16:00. It should: one set of hours, in one home, with the routines matching it (drivers' takings settle at 06:00 and 18:00 per timetables weekly). Source: timetables.json shifts (shift_driver_day, shift_driver_night, shift_radio_day); 3.2.2.

9. **Sheila is never at Mickey's on Sunday, and her hours contradict the counter shift.** The story frame: "every Sunday, Sheila reading back the week's nights", and Act I "closes on Sheila's Sunday question". Her Sunday row is home all day. Timetables shift_counter says Sheila holds the counter and the book 07:00 to 23:00 every day; her routine is 08:30 to 17:30 (Saturday to 13:00). Friday's reckoning (17:00 to 17:30) is also cut short by her 17:15 walk to the bus. It should: a Sunday row at Mickey's for the read-back (for example 10:00 to 13:00, chosen); the counter shift say who holds the counter when Sheila is gone (Ron on the door from 18:00 is the obvious one); her Friday row run to 17:30 at the counter. Source: STORY-FRAME (the question; Act I); timetables.json shift_counter and weekly reckoning.

10. **The constables do not cover the police hours.** police.json and timetables give a day beat 06:00 to 22:00 every day and a night beat 22:00 to 06:00 every night. PC Ibbotson works 08:30 to 16:00 Monday to Friday only, so 06:00 to 08:30, 16:00 to 22:00 and all of every weekend have no day constable, though the knock along the street "from 09:00 the next morning" falls on any day. PC Haigh walks the night beat seven nights a week for all seven weeks; Sgt Kitson is at the custody desk 24 hours a day, every day. It should: a named relief (police.json already has beat_constable_second) or a stated rota for both beats, with rest days, and the custody desk in turns. Source: police.json beat_constable day and night, after_a_deed knock_street_rules; timetables.json shift_beat_day, shift_beat_night; 2.6.1.

11. **3.4.1: fares do not go where the routines go.** 35 of the 80 rides in fares.json do not match a move in the rider's routine. Brenda's dawn fare is the shop to the fish dock, but her routine goes home to the fish dock. Alison's ride is the bus stop to the burned warehouse, and her routine never goes there, though "Alison's fare to the warehouse row" is a week-one beat of the frame. Malcolm Ruddock rides from the cold stores to the boarding house; he lives at d_cs_5. Terence Bould rides to the ferry landing, which his routine never visits. No named person leaves the night packing shift at 05:30 (Tharratt and Ruddock arrive then), and no lodger takes the last ferry. It should: each fare's leg be a row in the routine, marked "by cab" or "waiting for a car", or the fare change to fit the routine. Source: 3.4.1 (each a named person going where their routine takes them).

12. **Arrivals from off the Hook are left for the one-shot to guess.** The rule says such a person is "placed out of sight at the nearest soft edge, bus stop or door". Nearest to what, and when? The walk from that point is never timed, so Sheila appears at Mickey's at 08:30 from edge_north, 420 m away, and leaves at 17:30 by the bus stop but arrives by no stated route. Her row says "home across the river", which would be the ferry, not the bus. It should: every off-Hook row name its entry point (bus_stop_qs, edge_north, edge_west, the landing, a door), with the person appearing there at the row's time minus the walk, and leaving the same way. Source: 2.1.3 (no cheat where Tom can see it); routines.json rules.from_off_the_hook.

13. **The Tangs' takeaway does not exist on the map or in places.json.** 18 rows (Peter and Lily Tang every evening, Jason Pinder at 21:00) use the provisional id tangs_takeaway, and the check only warns about it. The build cannot place them. It should: the keeper add the place (the open point suggests Back Parade) with hours and a door before this file is final. Source: routines.json open_points; places.json; story frame (Mickey's Friday with Lily and Peter Tang).

## Lesser points

1. build_people.py's comment on REACH_SHARE says "half the stay"; the value is 0.75 and the rules text says three quarters. Make them agree.
2. build_people.py counts its warnings but never prints them; print them, and add routines.json to validate_data.py.
3. Mass times are scattered: Father Walsh says "Masses" 08:30 to 13:00 and evening Mass at 18:00; people arrive at 09:00, 09:30 and 10:00. Weekday Mass is at 07:30, before the chapel's public hours (08:00). Put one Mass timetable in timetables.json and match the arrivals to it.
4. Special days have no routine overrides: Remembrance Sunday at the chapel (the frame has the men of the pen there, keeping their backs to Ron), Bonfire Night, and the Landing night.
5. Ada's Sunday row says Father Walsh brings her communion; his Sunday never goes to Ada. His weekday "call on Ada" runs four hours, six days a week.
6. Dobson goes up to bed at 23:45 on Friday and Saturday, while the chip shop is open to 00:30. Julie's 18:00 row is "the rent man at six", but no rent man comes (Dawn calls at 12:15; Mr Askew is home at 17:00).
7. Carol Ellis's people record gives her home as police_station; her routine lives at edge_north. Sgt Kitson's record home (edge_north) never appears in his routine.
8. Pat Brannigan's "van to the last boat" stays at widow_base from 22:00 to 04:00. The last boat arrives at 22:55 and leaves at 23:00; for the Landing's road way in the van should be at the ferry landing about 22:45 to 23:15.
9. fares.json uses home_cl_9, which is not a door id (d_cl_9), and sends Winifred Duggleby to the Copper Row market on a Saturday, when the market is Thursday.
10. The distance method runs a straight line from each door to its nearest graph node, which can cross water or a building; snapping each door to its own node (the beat already uses n_door_ nodes) would be sounder.

The check is sound on these points: the speeds (1.4 and 1.1 m/s), the age split at 70, the midnight wrap (last row of each day to the next day's 00:00 row, counted from the last row's time to 24:00), and the exemption of "by cab" rides and truly off-map places.
