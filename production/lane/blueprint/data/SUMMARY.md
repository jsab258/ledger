# Data files, wave 1: summary (24 Nov 2026)

Thirteen of the nineteen data files the one-shot will build from are made, checked and reviewed. The map of the Hook and its docks is now exact: every road, building, door and house number, lamp, ladder, phone box and back way, in metres, matching the road plan (the chase loop 1,532 m, 552 m across by road, 477 m on foot). Its drawing, map-topdown.svg, is made from the file, so the advisor can show it as it stands; paper-map.svg is the lead's own folded map, places hidden until known.

On top of the map: the rooms (63), the places and their hours, the calendar, the police beat and patrol, the crowd by hour, the cars, one tuning table on the 48-minute day, the Ledger's words, letters and the paper, every on-screen word, light and weather, sound, and the record formats with a checker for all nineteen files.

Each file was checked by a fresh reviewer and fixed once. What is left is listed in each file and in DATA-BUILD-STATUS.md: the crowd numbers are still provisional, some gun and crowd numbers are owed from footage, and the content gate catches two words the design needs.
