## 1. The budget

About 45 Fable-builder hours in the one-shot's week (premortem way 7; way 11 adds that another project shares the account, so it may be less). The first one-shot built its systems first, then passed 6 of 41 stories in about 7.5 hours: 25 to 57 minutes of story work each (about 44 on average), about 75 minutes each counting its checks and the cab work. At 44 minutes the 41 stories take about 30 hours; at the measured 75 minutes about 51, which alone is more than the week. With TECH-PLAN's plumbing and the gate (about 4 hours), only the faster pace leaves room for content: about 11 hours. Content that would cost more than that has to arrive ready as data (TECH-PLAN section 3) before 7 Dec.

How hours were estimated: loading and wiring ready data is about 0.5 hours per kind of record plus a check run (one-shot ONE-SHOT-LOG shows a story's check-and-fix loop at about an hour); writing content inside the one-shot is taken at about 400 checked lines an hour (writing, tagging each line's situation and cool-down, the content-rule and period check, and fixing what the check finds). Both are estimates; the first is from the log's pace, the second is a judgement and should be measured on the first make-ahead batch.

## 3. What does not fit

1. Writing the lines inside the one-shot. About 5,300 new lines at about 400 an hour is about 13 hours alone, more than the hours left at the best pace. They must be written ahead.
2. The full map at G1's lengths and the 24 rooms (about 13 hours if built inside). The chase loop (1,528 m) and the docks depend on it.
3. Everything together: about 62 hours inside against at most about 11 free. Even with the stories done faster than last time, content cannot be made inside the week.
4. Voiced lines are not in this count: the browser game speaks in subtitles with a tone per speaker (audio.js `voice`). Voices belong to the builder's pipeline for the Unreal game and are not one-shot work.
