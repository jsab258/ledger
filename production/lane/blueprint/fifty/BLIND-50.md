# BLIND-50: the outside list, put to the blueprint (24 Nov 2026)

Written first by a fresh Opus 5.5 helper that had not read the blueprint, from one paragraph: a 1990 British port-town crime RPG whose AI townspeople see, remember and gossip, where police act on what witnesses saw. It wrote the 50 things it would most want a "50 incredible details" video to show. The lead then put each to the blueprint on lane-2026-11. Columns as in FIFTY.md: status (designed, partly, missing); catalogue game or LEDGER's own; for anything not designed, BUILD (changes how the game is built: must close before the one-shot) or CONTENT (left to the loops). "By design, no" marks a wish the blueprint rules against on purpose; it counts as missing but is not a gap unless he wants it.
