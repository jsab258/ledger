# Player questions about open-world crime and immersive-sim games

Date: 24 Nov 2026

Method: web search only (standard mode), about 45 searches and page reads. Search result titles pointed to forum threads, wikis and guides. Where a snippet was thin, the page behind it was read. The questions are what players keep asking in those threads and guides ("can you...", "what happens if..."), rewritten to apply to any crime game. The "what that game does" clause comes from the page named in the last column, not from play. Forum threads themselves (Steam, Reddit) could not be read in full, so no thread is cited; guides and wikis that answer the thread questions are cited instead. KCD1 pages are used where a KCD2 page was not reachable, and are marked KCD1. Mafia games and GTA VI story were left out. Anything touching alcohol, gambling or children was left out.

Games covered: GTA V, GTA IV, Red Dead Redemption 2, Kingdom Come: Deliverance II (and KCD1 where marked), Sleeping Dogs, Hitman (2016 to World of Assassination), Watch Dogs: Legion, L.A. Noire.

Line form: question | game(s) | what that game does | source
