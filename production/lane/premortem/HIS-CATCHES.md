## Would three blind passes have found them? (the method tested, 23 Nov)

Last week's passes read files and judged the plan. They missed nearly every catch of types T9, T12, T13 and T14, because no lens asked about things outside the plan's own files: what already exists, what he sent, what he sees, what he does by hand. Lenses added for every future pass:

1. What do we already have that new work should build on (research, installed games, credits, tools, his links and videos, earlier teardowns), and is any of it unused?
2. Every ruling and every request of his in the last week: is it carried into every prompt, page, brief and file it touches, and acted on?
3. What does he see (each page, update, alert), is it current, in the unit he asked for, with a verdict and a recommendation, not numbers alone?
4. What will he have to do by hand (taps, confirmations, installs, deleting, playing), and could anything do it instead?
5. What resource is idle or used twice right now (allowance under the line, credit, disk, his PC, a session waiting)?
6. What has the world changed since the plan was written (new games, models, prices, tools), and has anyone looked?
7. Was the question he asked the one answered, at the level he asked it?
8. Do the numbers of parts that depend on each other fit (scale, time, money), checked with numbers?
