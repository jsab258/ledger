## Gaps

1. P7 and X2: price, length and value. Propose "Price, length and value statement", parent 10.2 (store, rating and disclosure). Players punish a short game at a high price (13.9% mean) and praise it at a fair one.
2. S4 (with S6): the two clocks. Propose "Gossip timeliness gate, first 30 real minutes", parent 2.4 (gossip). It should state the clock conversion and measure a rumour reaching Tom within 40 game hours.
3. A9: "Photo mode", parent 5.8 (cameras and cut scenes). Allowed by RULINGS 23 Sep, in no part.
4. A10: "Mod support position", parent 10.6 (after release). Needs one ruling line, not a build.
