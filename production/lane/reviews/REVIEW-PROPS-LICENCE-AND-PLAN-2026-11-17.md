Fix: mark the PC-only items "held on the PC". modular_chainlink_fence is rightly marked not fetched.

The forum answer of 13 Oct 2026 (Fab_Josh) says the EULA prevails, bars use "as training inputs" and leaves out tools that "solely operate on the original content". It does not label the poster as Epic staff. Fix: write "Fab forum account" in place of "Epic staff".
