Option A is within his rulings: rare guns (28 Oct), built after melee (12 Oct), not a shooter (D24 as changed 12 Oct, fighting only), Tom cannot die, fists never kill (30 Oct), no cover shooting (COMBAT.md line 359). Nothing breaks the content rule: no alcohol, gambling, children, torture or cruelty as spectacle; killing stays rare and remembered. Costs are marked as judgement. No em-dashes, no italics. The fixes below are a few wrong or overstated numbers and law points, one missed approved design (the July weapons spec), one invented mechanic, and two decisions that his rulings say the session should take itself.

## Fixes

1. Summary line 2: "about 58 people a year were shot dead" is wrong; 58 is 1990 alone. Replace with "38 to 58 people a year were shot dead in England and Wales from 1988 to 1991 (58 in 1990)".
2. Line 36 and line 75: "nearly a third" is wrong. Supposed plus imitation is 920 of 3,477, 26 per cent. Replace both with "about a quarter". In line 36 add "other 30" after "rifles 53" so the parts sum to the total.
3. Line 37: S10 does not say air guns are included. Change "(air guns included)" to "(air guns apparently included: the 1986 total with air guns was 9,363 [S9]; S10 does not say)".
4. Line 39: S12 says the Act restricted "certain smoothbore guns", not "most such shotguns". Replace "and most such shotguns" with "and restricted certain smooth-bore guns". Also, S12 says only fourteen sections started on 1 Feb 1989 and does not say which; add after "certificate holders": "(which parts were in force in 1990 is not confirmed; the whole Act was in force from 2 April 1991)". Make the same hedge in line 72, "shells scarce because only certificate holders can buy them": "shells scarce (by 1991 only certificate holders could buy them)".
5. Line 77: "anything automatic (illegal from 1989 ...)" is wrong by the file's own line 39 (the 1988 Act extended an existing ban to burst fire; full-auto was banned earlier, not confirmed here) and clashes with line 74's "small automatic pistol". Replace with "Not in the game: rifles, pump-actions, machine guns or burst-fire guns (prohibited well before 1990), all wrong for the tone. The small pistol above is self-loading, not automatic." Rename line 74's gun "Small self-loading pistol".
6. Line 40 and decision 2: the conclusion "a provincial port town most likely has no armed car" leaves out what S15 itself says: West Yorkshire ran "instant response cars" from 1976 and the Met had an unpublicised armed car in Brixton in the 1980s. Add to line 40: "Earlier exceptions existed: West Yorkshire's instant response cars from 1976 and an unpublicised Brixton car in the 1980s [S15]." Soften to "a provincial town could go either way; most forces had no armed car until 1991 to 1993". Also "British police did not carry guns on patrol" has no source; mark it "(general knowledge, not from a cited source)" or cite one.
7. Missed design: game-design/weapons-spec.md (v5, approved in full 29 Jul 2026) already has a firearms family (a .22 target pistol, a .38 snub revolver, a .45 automatic, a suppressed .22, a sawn-off shotgun; phase 5) and section 7.3 on where weapons come from (bought from a person, stolen, taken, found once at Mickey's, no random loot), with his own words asking for a silenced pistol. Line 23 "Not settled anywhere: which guns exactly, how Tom gets one" is therefore wrong. Add a bullet to "What canon and your rulings already settle" naming the July spec and saying the 12 Oct design (sawn-off and old revolver) is the later word on the roster; in "which guns exist" say which July guns are dropped and why (the suppressed .22: a silencer in 1990 British criminal hands is very rare, judgement; keep it as a one-off story item or drop it, said plainly), and add the "found once, at Mickey's" route to "how Tom gets one".
8. Decision 3 asks what section 7.3 of the approved July spec already answers (bought from a person is one of its four approved routes). Remove decision 3, or turn it into a line in the file saying the session follows 7.3. Decision 2 (how armed police come) is a period-detail question the research answers; by the 30 Oct "decide it yourself" ruling the session decides it, records it in DECISIONS.md and carries on. Remove it from the decisions and state the choice as the session's, overturnable. Leave decision 1 (scope) as his only decision, with option A's estimate beside it: "about 6 to 10 agent weeks after melee (estimate)".
9. Line 91: "DS Carol Ellis takes a shot personally: it lifts her interest a full step" is not in canon, combat-design, the July spec or Core. Mark it "(proposal, not in any design)" or drop it.
10. Line 90: "A hit goes to hospital, which reports a gunshot wound" has no source for 1990 practice. Add "(judgement; no 1990 source found)" and list it under "Not reached or not found".
11. Line 93: "the bill" is wrong for 1990 Britain: NHS hospital treatment was free. Replace "the wound, the bill and a doctor" with "the wound, the days lost and a doctor". Note for the town session that combat-design.md line 97 has the same word.
12. Line 32: the KCD2 gun lesson ("slow, fragile early gun ... Mafia's gun-led game is not") is in COMBAT.md row R04, not Study 14; only the witness-flees line is Study 14 (line 63). Change to "From LEDGER's own studies: KCD2's slow, fragile early gun is the model for a rare gun, Mafia's gun-led game is not (COMBAT.md, R04). A KCD2 witness flees once any weapon is out (Study 14)."
13. Line 31 (GTA): S8 says putting on or taking off a mask or sunglasses in the search-cone phase removes one star, not all. Replace "or by changing a mask" with "and putting on or taking off a mask drops one star".
14. Line 27 (Sleeping Dogs): S1 says "several dozen" environmental finishers and treats guns "as power ups"; it implies, but does not state, that ammunition runs out. Change "dozens of finishers" to "several dozen finishers" and "ammunition runs out and the player is pushed back to hands" to "the hero keeps using up his guns and is pushed back to hands and disarms".
15. Line 29 (Mafia): "deliberately no easy firepower" comes from the Popverse writer's note on scarce ammunition, not the studio. Change to "set in 1905 Sicily; ammunition is scarce, so the knife carries the fighting (the article's words); the studio frames combat as surviving and scavenging, the knife as tradition [S4]".
16. Line 18: "A shot is reported by someone walking to a phone" is an inference, not canon. Move it out of the "Canon (era)" bullet or add "(so)" before it.
17. Plain words for him: line 22 names a code path ("Core/Homicide.cs"); replace with "the killing code already built". The "Automatic checks" section uses test names (Soak, SaveChaos, PerceptionGolden, CoreTests); either add one plain word each ("the long run", "save and load", "who sees and hears", "same result twice") or move the section below the decisions as an appendix for the builder.
