- Canon: the banned list covers alcohol, gambling and children; nothing proposed breaks it.

## Findings and fixes

1. The carried ear list is dropped. REVIEW-v1's ear-only points (does the radio read as a radio, the day bed, stray tones such as the 1281 Hz rain whine) are sent to "a fresh reviewer and the checker", who cannot hear. Fix: put unit 10's amb_radio_day and the rain bed in clips 1 and 3, and say the clips close q-sound-listening.
2. Question 2 hides the licence change inside a taste choice. Fix: one line, "Widen D26 to CC BY 4.0 for sound, each recorder credited, never NoAI, as for clothes on 30 Oct? (Recommended: yes)", and name the allowlist line and credits.json that follow.
3. Not one-line decisions: options run to two lines and Q3 B depends on Q2. Fix: three short questions, Q3 conditional stated in a few words, or fold the UK ring into Q2.
4. Sonniss, Pixabay and Fab are "needs a pick" but not asked. Fix: say plainly they are not proposed now, so no open licence question is left implied.
