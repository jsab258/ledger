## Licence findings

1. Free again. Quixel's notice of 5 Nov 2026: the full library is free on Fab, and Quixel's terms now let AI models train on Megascans and Megaplants; no end date (quixel.com/news/update-megascans-are-going-free-again, read 17 Nov). Digital Production, 13 Nov 2026 (digitalproduction.com/2026/11/13/quixel-megascans-free-again, read 17 Nov): Fab acquisitions fall under the Fab Standard License, which "allows commercial projects" and bars standalone redistribution; files come as FBX and glTF, not Unreal-only; the 2024 free period ended in January 2025, so this may not last.
2. After Bridge: Epic's content licence change log records "Deleted content restrictions specific to Megascans in the Megascans Content Addendum" (unrealengine.com/eula-change-log/content, undated entries, read 17 Nov). Items now sit under the Fab Standard License.
3. Personal or Professional: Personal is for buyers under US$100,000 gross revenue in 12 months, Professional above (dev.epicgames.com Fab "licenses and pricing", read 17 Nov). The page does not say whether Personal covers a sold game. At price 0 either tier costs nothing; past US$100,000, Professional is the safe pick.
4. NoAI. Fab's documentation: NoAI means "must not be used for generative AI data collection" (same page). A Fab forum account (the page does not mark the poster as Epic staff), 13 Oct 2026: the Fab EULA "prevails and should be treated as binding"; NoAI content may not be used "as training inputs to Generative AI Programs", and tools that "solely operate on the original content" fall outside (forums.unrealengine.com/t/noai-content-inputs-or-training-inputs/2838630, read 17 Nov). The thread notes Fab's Terms of Service (3.3(i)) say "inputs", unresolved; Epic's content licence section 17 says "as inputs" too.
5. For this project: placing and showing Megascans in the game, and sending game pictures to the AI tester and reviewers, is what his 13 Oct ruling allows. Image-to-3D (TRELLIS) or an image model fed a NoAI item makes new content from it: barred by the "inputs" wording and outside the ruling's "places and sets it up" basis. Rule: no Megascans file, render or crop goes into a generating tool unless its listing read AI Yes when acquired.
6. Flags are mixed today: Paper Trash, Junkyard Crate, Dirty Paint Can and Old Metal Oil Can read Yes; Cardboard Box, Wooden Storage Box, Wooden Pallet and Rope Coil read No. The target said all Yes; press said listings update through 12 Nov. Either not all updated or the tool misread. In-game use is unaffected.
7. fab.com/eula itself was refused to the fetch tool today: unreached.

## Period claims in the target

- "Never a wheeled bin" is a choice, not a period fact: wheeled bins were trialled in Britain from 1980 (Broxtowe) and spreading in Bury by 1983 (wordhistories.net, "wheelie bin", 30 Jun 2023, citing dated local papers; read 17 Nov). Keep dustbins, recorded as a choice.
- Pallets: UK standard 1200 x 1000 mm, Euro 1200 x 800 (gwp.co.uk UK pallet guide, read 17 Nov); the held target agrees.
- "Reflective sleeves" as period-wrong: a British cone standard, BS 873-8:1985, was published 31 Jan 1986 (knowledge.bsigroup.com, read 17 Nov); whether it required sleeves was not reached. Do not strip the held cones on the list's word.
- Content: canon allows pubs as places; what must not show is drink, drink brands or signs for alcohol. No prop here touches it; no toy, pram, school item, betting slip or bottle crate is planned.
