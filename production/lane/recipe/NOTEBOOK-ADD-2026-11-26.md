## 24 to 26 Nov 2026: the design method, written up from the advisor's chat and the cloud jobs (entries the builder's own units did not cover)

Tags (his pick of 26 Nov): [ANY] holds for any game, the method; [GENRE] holds for open-world social simulation games; [LEDGER] this game only. Evidence on lane-2026-11 unless named otherwise; the 25 Nov evening files (new kinds, the hunt, the GTA VI check) go to production/lane/blueprint/questions/angles/ with Monday's cloud job.

- 2026-11-24, physical facts come from reality, games only set the rules on top [ANY]: lamp spacing had been set from game sight distances; real things (spacing, sizes, distances, prices, timetables) come from dated sources for the setting, reference games only tune feel and rules, and every number carries its source kind (real, game, ruling, judgement). A reality check of 1,164 numbers found 441 unsourced, 75 from the wrong kind of source and 93 judging themselves (reality-check/).
- 2026-11-24, describe the game only from the design document [ANY]: the advisor stated how the game behaves from what it should do ("tells her sister") and was wrong; any claim about how the game works is read from the design first, and said as unknown when it is not there.
- 2026-11-24, gaps were found by the owner's chance questions, never by our checks [ANY]: every check graded the design against itself (coverage, validation, conflicts); nothing tested it from outside (a player, the period). Fix: questions from outside, asked on purpose, with a blind miss rate as the measure, and the owner's chance catches counted (HIS-CATCHES.md); the method fails while his catches do not fall to zero.
- 2026-11-24, the what-if sweep [GENRE]: every verb times every kind of person times every place and moment, asked as "what happens if", answered against the design, then scored worse, same or better than the best reference game; the bar is consequences better than the best reference, talk far beyond it, physical verbs at least equal (what-if/).
- 2026-11-24, the town's fabric must be data the rules read [GENRE]: 119 named people had 147 untyped ties and family only in prose; ties need kinds (family, friend, grudge, debt) in data, and ties that form, strengthen and break in play, or the talk and gossip rules have nothing to travel on.
- 2026-11-24, outside tests from hype formats [ANY]: "50 things you didn't know you could do", "I tried to break the NPCs", 15-second reels, the likely top comments and the Steam reviews written in advance show the game as a stranger meets it; the best moments become acceptance tests the build must pass (hype/SIGNATURE-MOMENTS.md, fifty/).
- 2026-11-24, compare against every reference game, not the famous one [ANY]: the strongest reference for each aspect leads (for a town that notices and judges, KCD2 over GTA); every outside comparison names all of them.
- 2026-11-25, never propose a scope from memory [ANY]: a combat pass was proposed without stealth until the owner asked; every pass starts with a census of its subject from the design and the references, checked blind, before any plan.
- 2026-11-25, readable rules over hidden dice [GENRE]: outcomes follow readable rules (stealth works when done right); chance only at the violent end, drawn from one seed held in the save so a reload never rerolls; effects by body zone (arm drops the weapon, leg stops running, face marks, neck most deadly) (combat/).
- 2026-11-25, the relevance test [ANY]: before proposing any work, say whether the next build needs it; if not, it waits for the stage that does.
- 2026-11-25, the question pass [ANY]: many player lenses (14 here) each ask about 50 concrete questions, every earlier question added; each is answered against the design (well, partly, missing, contradictory, out) with a proposal; proposals written as general rules, never case fixes; a fresh blind set measures the miss rate. 996 questions gave 691 shortfalls under 24 rules; miss rate 10% against a 5% target (questions/).
- 2026-11-25, situation questions miss whole-game questions [ANY]: all 14 lenses asked about moments; play styles, a second run, pacing over weeks and long-term reactivity were never asked. A whole-game pass (13 lenses, 350 questions) found 8 new rules from far fewer questions: new rules come from new angles, not from more questions (questions/SUMMARY-WHOLEGAME.md).
- 2026-11-25, three routes and reputation as the build [GENRE]: every job and story beat has at least three routes (force, talk, stealth or scheme); the town's reading of the player per person is the character build; style bots walk each route.
- 2026-11-25, the cut-off rule [ANY]: before the build only gaps that change how the game is built must close; content gaps go to the loops.
- 2026-11-25, one run cannot build a big game [ANY]: one overnight run built 21 systems in 3.5 h but a fresh check found 7 of 8 parts thin, and story by story it passed 6 of 41 stories in 7.5 h; the public long-run builds of the strongest model run days but read shallow; the vendor's own write-ups agree (one feature at a time, a pass list, a separate checker). Method: build in big stages in playing order, each playable from the start, finish lines fixed in advance, an automatic go or no-go, fixes carried as a short list, no re-planning between stages, the design complete before the first stage (ONE-SHOT-LOG.md; advisor 395, 396).
- 2026-11-25, slice, not layers [ANY]: build in playing order with every system at depth (each stage is judged by real play and the joins between systems meet early) rather than all systems first; the foundation is designed for the whole game in stage 1; the opening is polished again at the end.
- 2026-11-25, a final fold needs the rule, not a note [ANY]: a scheduled session refused to run past its standing budget line when only a note on the start asked it to; the exception had to be written into its own instructions.
- 2026-11-25, unasked kinds of question exist after any pass [ANY]: six kinds barely asked (players breaking the AI people, institutions acting on their own, two things at once, losing, the builder's questions, feel and learning) plus a well-asked control: the control found no new rule, every new kind did (27 of 48 shortfalls not covered).
- 2026-11-25, three levels: laws, rules, data [ANY]: a flat list of rules grew without end and mixed levels; all rules fitted under eight laws (for this genre: knowing, things, people, exchange, time, recovery, story, the player); a new question is answered by finding its law; the hunt stops when fresh angles need no new law; rule-level growth is the design itself.
- 2026-11-25, the angle hunt [ANY]: map every angle a game can be questioned from, from professional sources (Schell's lenses, QA test design, MDA, motivation models, systemic design, narrative review, red-teaming of language-model characters, ratings review, accessibility guidelines, Steam reviews and refunds, period authenticity, askers beyond players); mark what earlier passes covered; test the uncovered in batches of six plus a covered control; a blind writer, fresh judges, a fresh reviewer of every new-rule claim. 100 angles, 30 tested, 350 questions: no new law, 23 candidate rules, controls needed new rules on only 4 of 50 questions.
- 2026-11-25, law-like proposals often belong elsewhere [ANY]: the record (replay and explain any outcome), chance (one seed), versions (saves across updates) and content limits by meaning were proposed as laws; the review placed them in the technical chapter, under the time law and in canon.
- 2026-11-25, a specific case never goes to the owner when a general rule covers it [ANY]: a decision about facial hair was too specific; looks are content under the appearance rule.
- 2026-11-25, rules carry costs for later production [ANY]: many design rules commit the engine phase to faces, hair, clothing layers, two-body animation, props and crowds; every rule gets a line on what it asks of the engine with a cost band, and the ranking counts it.
- 2026-11-25, pad players get the same powers as typists [GENRE]: every talk act (an offer with its sum, a threat, a lie, a rumour, a bluff) is reachable from suggested lines; free wording adds colour, never power; a whole run per style by pad alone is a stage test.
- 2026-11-25, check claims of uniqueness against the real competition [ANY]: "no other game does this" was a judgement; a check against the newest market leader's coverage (gameplay only) found it does 16 of 122 such claims and a weaker version of 17; the leader's things nobody asked about (49, nine fitting) become candidates.
- 2026-11-25, capability is not experience [ANY]: question passes test whether the world can respond, not whether it is good to play; ten experience risks (frustration, confusion, invisible depth, aimlessness, tedium, flat pacing, feeling cheated, save anxiety, one best way, disappointing talk) come from playability heuristics (Desurvire's, PLAY, games user research) and become measures in every stage check, taken by a clumsy-player bot and a newcomer bot; fail forward: every failure opens something as well as costing something.
- 2026-11-25, judges from more than one model family [ANY]: every story judge so far was one model family; an outside model (the owner can paste a pack into another vendor's model) checks the readers' ranking before more writing rests on it.
- 2026-11-25, scale per person, not per map [GENRE]: depth per person is affordable in one district of about a hundred people; a bigger world needs full memory and routines for the people who matter and a lighter level for the crowd, with costs per person and per deed stated (witness budget).
- 2026-11-25, the advisor misses results while it talks [ANY]: results landed and went unreported for up to an hour four times; while any job runs, the results list is read before every message, however short.
- 2026-11-25, timers in an idle cloud container do not fire [ANY]: background waits died when the container slept; wake-ups are scheduled messages bound to the session.
- 2026-11-25, allowance at a weekly reset [ANY]: what is left before a reset is lost; a job on the remainder saves after every step, keeps a state file and is restarted after the reset if cut off, since no reading of the remainder may be available.
- 2026-11-26, lessons are tagged and checked far away [ANY]: every lesson is tagged ANY, GENRE or LEDGER; at each distillation every ANY lesson is checked on paper against two far games (an RTS like Age of Empires in ancient Rome for systems, a Spider-Man-like action game in Mesopotamia for production); a small roguelike is the one real end-to-end test of the recipe, after stage 1 and only on the owner's yes.
- 2026-11-26, lessons from the advisor's chat and cloud jobs were not written down [ANY]: two days of method lessons existed only in the chat and job summaries; whoever learns a lesson writes it to the notebook the same day (the advisor hands its lines over daily; every cloud job brief ends with its notebook lines).
