## 5. The far check

Method (the owner's pick of 26 Nov): every ANY line of the draft recipe was checked on paper against two far games: an RTS like Age of Empires in ancient Rome (no hero, many units, economy and balance, short competitive matches) and a Spider-Man-like action game in Mesopotamia (one hero, movement feel, animation, combat, authored set pieces). The question for each: would it hold there as written? The check was made on the recipe's merged lines, so each raw lesson inherits the verdict of the line it sits in. A lesson that simply does not arise in a far game (garments seen from an RTS camera) counts as holding once the line states when it applies; the 1.8 opening note does that for the whole of production.

Moved to the genre pack (11 ANY lessons, wholly or their genre-specific half):
1. Opening pacing numbers (control within 1.5 min, something new every 3 min, no 3.5 min without control, shot lengths, activity and prompt counts): an RTS match gives control at once and its pacing comes from the opponent. Kept in 1.4 step 13: hints on hesitation; few, short, skippable scenes.
2. Budget numbers (3 ms animation, 3.5 ms people, 70 people within 150 m, freeze rules): wrong for hundreds of units. Kept in 1.8.7 step 1: budgets first, tested fuller than play.
3. Light numbers (8 shadow casters, the lamp's spot and bulb): genre measures. Kept in 1.8.1 step 6: small lights cast no shadows.
4. Material numbers (4 to 8 colourways a garment mesh, master counts): genre measures. Kept in 1.8.1 step 9: variety from material values, colourways and decals on few meshes.
5. Animation starting values (chosen-clip state machine, 12 starts and 6 stops, no motion matching in either reference): a finding from two open-world games that would mislead an action game built round traversal. Kept in 1.8.4 step 1: start from the method the references use, read from their files.
6. Costly effects on heroes, its examples (cloth on named characters, interiors where the story goes, the camera moving for conversations): genre measures. Kept in the 1.8 opening note in neutral words.
7. Fail forward, every failure opening something (NB 25 Nov, tagged ANY): a short competitive match ends in a loss. Moved to the Recovery law.
8. to 11.
