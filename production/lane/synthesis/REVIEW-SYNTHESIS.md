# REVIEW: the synthesis (VISION, DESIGN, TRACE, BUILD-ORDER, QUESTIONS)

19 November 2026. A fresh reviewer, reading only canon.md, RULINGS.md and the five synthesis files on branch lane-2026-11. Line numbers are those files as they stand today.

## Verdict

The synthesis is strong and close to ready. The vision sells a tense crime game about a minicab firm, not a gossip simulator. The design gives every major system a place in the loop and a set of experience rules, and the build order builds one whole evening first, round by round, each round naming its section, study lines, rules and measure. No em-dashes or italics were found, and there is no first person. Five points must be fixed before it goes to him, and each one is a narrow wording fix. The escalation dial's top state makes guns "common", but his ruling says guns are rare. A car knock-down followed by a fall could kill under the fight rule, but canon says a car never kills. One core rule puts knowledge on the screen that Tom cannot have, which breaks "never other minds". June Suddaby, Philip Danby and Father Walsh as a person are missing, while TRACE says June is in the first hour. And Iveson's "lad" brushes the no-children rule. The should-fix list is mostly about TRACE: rows that point at sections which do not carry the line, a question his ruling already settles, and two open calls of his that the design quietly settles.

Counts: 5 must fix, 16 should fix, 15 minor.

## Must fix

### M1. The War state makes guns common and killing routine

- RULINGS.md 12: "Combat (18 Nov): end state A, guns rare, after melee, AAA when used; an escalation dial once guns exist".
- canon.md 115: "Killing is possible, rare, permanent, and the town remembers it forever."
- DESIGN.md 229: "3, War. Guns are common among the crews. ... men on both sides can be shot dead for good."
- Fix: rewrite DESIGN 229 as: "3, War. A few named men on each side now carry, and everyone knows who. The police bring in armed cars (his words), stop and search on the street, and everyone is jumpier: watchers run sooner and report more, night fares fall. A shot can now kill, and each death is a named, permanent event; guns stay rare." Then add a rule under section 7: "ER-FIGHT-09 Guns stay rare and killing stays rare at every dial state: at War, at most a few named carriers per side and no more than [first value] deaths an act. Check S: three seeded game weeks at War, carriers and deaths counted."

### M2. A car knock-down plus a fall can kill under the fight rule

- canon.md 116-118: "a car can knock a person down and hurt them badly, and never kills".
- DESIGN.md 217: "A fall against a kerb, a wall or a step after a blow can kill, rarely". DESIGN.md 238, ER-FIGHT-05: "only a fall against a kerb, wall or step within 3 s of a blow can." DESIGN.md 175: "a car aimed at a man is an attack with a weapon (rung 7, section 7)".
- Nothing says that a car strike is not a "blow". A person knocked onto a kerb by the car meets the fall rule, and a car aimed at a man sits one rung below "kill".
- Fix: add to DESIGN section 5, after line 175: "A car strike is never a blow under the fall rule: whatever the person lands on, a car never kills, and rung 8 is never reached with a car." Add to ER-FIGHT-05: "A car strike never counts as a blow." Extend the ER-DRIVE-03 check: "player hits at 5, 15, 30 and 50 mph, including hits that throw the person against a kerb, wall or step, aimed and unaimed; the hurt scales and nobody dies."

### M3. ER-CORE-02 shows Tom what he cannot know

- RULINGS.md 116: "he sees his own position, never other minds".
- DESIGN.md 61, ER-CORE-02: "Every change that matters (a job failed, a door shut for good, money lost, a witness, an arrest coming) is said by the world or the screen within 3 s and written in the Ledger within a game minute."
- This goes against DESIGN.md 282 ("It holds only what Tom saw, heard or was told"), DESIGN.md 486 (ER-SCREEN-01, "Nothing is on screen that Tom could not know") and the vision's tension at VISION.md 16 ("who can see him ... who is walking to the phone box"). An unseen witness put on screen within 3 s takes away the game's central question.
- Fix: rewrite ER-CORE-02 as: "No silent failure. Every change Tom could perceive (a job failed in front of him, a door shut in his face, money lost from his pocket or tin, a witness he could see or hear, an officer coming into view) is shown by the world within 3 s and written in the Ledger within a game minute. A change he could not perceive surfaces later only through the world (it comes back, ER-EYES-08). Check S: trigger each; assert the line and entry for the perceivable cases, and assert no screen text or Ledger line for the unperceived ones."
