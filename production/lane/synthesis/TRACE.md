# LEDGER: the trace

18 November 2026. Where every study line, every ruling that bears on play and every canon pillar sits in DESIGN.md, and how the older documents' contradictions are settled. "§" is a DESIGN section; "ER-" is an experience rule.

Three verdicts are used:
- a section or rule: the design carries it;
- "left out": the design does not carry it, with the reason;
- "conflict": it pulls against canon, a ruling or a process rule, and is his or the Monday order's to settle.

Most "left out" lines are craft (look, clothes, animation, performance, pipeline). They are not dropped from the project: his tap of 8 Nov keeps them for their Unreal phase. They are only not design.
