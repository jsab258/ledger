# One-shot builder: law (witnesses who walk to tell, and the police)

Branch os/law (pushed as oneshot-law). The system is src/game/law.js with law_beat.js; police.js is reworked. Numbers in src/game/tune/law.js, kinds in src/game/kinds/law.js, the two bought officers in src/game/cast/law.js. Rule pieces for Unreal to port: law_custody, law_statement, law_stages, law_town, police_beat (src/game/pieces/), 106 hand-written cases in test/cases/, run by `node test/pieces.mjs`: all pass. In-game test: test/game/law.mjs, 60 checks through the test hook LEDGER.game.law, all passing at 10:20. It plays the game's own rules in Node through test/game/law_headless.mjs (no browser, no drawing; about 10 seconds), because nine builders' browsers could not share the machine's six gigabytes: the kernel was killing tabs in every browser test, old ones too. Any builder's test can use law_headless.mjs the same way.

One road: everything that reaches the police goes through police.js record(E, by, how), whoever decided it: a witness's walk ending, the night's run at four for those who slept on it, or the real town's morning ('town:hour'). Once G.x.town.live is true, the nine people the town's cast holds are the town's to decide; everyone else and all violence stay on the prototype's rule (the town has no call that reports violence).

## Parts

- 2.5.1 The walk to tell: built. A witness who saw him plainly and will not keep quiet sets off after about 4 s, to a friend near by, the phone box, or the station for the worst; she is seen going (a line when it starts in his sight); nothing is on record until she arrives; in his sight the walk is given at least 30 real seconds (45 with the longer-walks assist, read as G.settings.longWalks). Two witnesses walk for one deed; the rest sleep on it and go up at four. Test: game/law "walk" checks; pieces law_statement walkTo, walkLasts (ER-EYES-04).
- 1.4.5 Reaching the witness: built. The walk is the timer; a talk with her while she walks opens the decision. Thin: no "Oi!" to stop her at a distance (the manners key is the foot builder's).
- 2.5.2 Stopping a witness: built. In talk she raises it and four answers are offered: talk her round, pay (asks twice), threaten (asks twice), help; leaving lets her go. Each answer's effect is the piece law_statement stopWitness (10 cases): a threat stops the police and never the street. Test: game/law, pieces.
- 2.5.3 Crowd reaction to a deed: built shallow. After a seen deed people within 14 m look, one comes nearer and drifts back after 9 s, witnesses are marked as talking quieter for a game hour. Thin: "quieter" is a flag others can read (G.hooks.lawTalksQuieter); nothing lowers their voices yet.
- 2.5.4 A street on edge (A37): built. Three seen deeds on one street inside two game days: witnesses set off in half the time, the constable walks from six in the evening and once at dinner time; it eases two days after the last. One line and a Ledger note when it starts. Thin: doors locked earlier and shops shut early are not built. Test: pieces law_town onEdge (5 cases), game/law.
- 2.5.5 Finding an unseen deed (A38): built. A deed with a trace (kind flag trace, E.trace, or a body) and no witness is found by the first named person whose routine brings them within 7 m in sight; the finder holds only an aftermath memory that names nobody; a hidden or sunk body is never found. Test: pieces law_town finder (5 cases), game/law.
- 2.6.1 The beat constable: built. PC Ibbotson and PC Pashby on alternate nights, 21:30 to 04:30, down Quay Street and along the quay; his boots are said when he is within 30 m. The carry's own constable in jobs.js keeps the nights of a carry; this one stands back then. Test: game/law.
- 2.6.2 Challenge and search: built. Cause: carrying at night, running, bloodied, a weapon in the hand, trespass, or back at a watched spot. He is heard at least 3 s before he may call out, and calls out before he walks up (ER-POL-02). Then: stand and be searched (he finds exactly G.x.crime.carried, the weapon under the coat and what is in the hand, and keeps it), have a word (talked round only when he has no cause), offer a note (PC Pashby looks away once a night; offered to an honest man it is a crime he saw), or run. The chase is lost after 7 s unseen or 70 m and ends inside 90 s; then he walks to where he last saw him and looks round (A3). Test: pieces police_beat (18 cases, 50 seeded chases three ways), game/law.
- 2.6.3 Statements and the arrest rule: built. ER-POL-01 in record(): no arrest without a named statement or an officer who saw it. Mickey's six never go; people marked nostmt (their trade) never go. Test: pieces law_statement willState, law_custody mayArrest; game/law.
- 2.6.4 Custody as hours: built. Six hours for the least deed, two and a caution if he owns up (a third answer at the door), eight for a wounding, twelve for a robbery, twenty-two for a killing and bail; two more if he ran; the duty solicitor is free; the coat is kept; never twice for one deed. Test: pieces law_custody (16 cases), game/law.
- 2.6.5 DS Ellis and the four stages: built. G.x.law.stage 0 to 3, one stage at a time; a stage's sign must have been shown (he must be in play to see it) and 30 game minutes passed before the next; a manhunt's arrest cannot land until its sign has been up a game hour. Ellis comes at nine for a body, for a reported wounding, robbery or killing, and from day 4 when three people of his day world hold told talk of his crimes, besides the prototype's loudness rule. Thin: the signs are lines of text with a Ledger note (and a paper headline for the manhunt); no police car is drawn. Test: pieces law_stages (21 cases), game/law.
- 2.6.10 Police after a deed (A36): built shallow. A statement or a find puts an officer at the spot from 20 game minutes on (the beat constable walks there), the ground is watched until six in the morning (being there is cause for a challenge), a body brings tape (in words) and Ellis next morning. Test: game/law.
- 2.6.8 Bought men (A13): built. PC Pashby (twenty pounds, looks away once a night) and Sergeant Thwaite (fifty pounds, loses one statement, ever; he smokes outside the station at dinner time). A colleague in sight of the payment books it. Anybody else offered a note books it. Whether DS Ellis can be bought is left open. Test: pieces law_town boughtDoes (7 cases), game/law.
- 2.6.9 Charges, bail and the office searched: built. A charge joins G.x.law.case = { charges, statements, officeFinds, strength }. At investigating the office is searched once: notice at six the evening before (said to him), the search at nine, taking what G.x.crime.office holds unless marked hot: false. Test: game/law (ER-SEARCHED-01).
- 2.6.6 Armed response: built shallow. Dial 2: an act flagged gun brings armed officers to its spot after 25 game minutes. Dial 3: stop and search on the street every three game hours by day, through the beat constable. Thin: no armed officers are drawn; "three seeded weeks at War" is the dial builder's count; mine is the piece law_town response (5 cases) and one in-game check.
- 2.16 Death, bodies and the missing: built shallow. A killing leaves a body; found, it is news that never cools (its heat is kept up); hidden or sunk (G.hooks.lawBodyMoved), it is never found and after a game day the man's close people start asking, again every two days. Test: pieces law_town missingAsked, game/law.
- 7.9 Failure without reload (my routes): built. Searched, chased, caught, arrested: each ends in play, with a Ledger line; custody is one fade. Timed in game/law (under two real minutes).
