street research's thirteen steps (production/research/aaa-street/5-PROOF-FRAME.md), each judged side
by side against the Hook sheet and the KCD2 frames by the builder's own check and a fresh reviewer,

its frame lives on the drive (F:/LedgerTools/renders/proof-<step>/; pictures never go into git).

only, linked from the overview's first lines.
