      THE MOMENT NO OTHER GAME PRODUCES: a stranger referring to something you
      did THAT THEY DID NOT WITNESS, whose route you could trace.

  Every clause is load-bearing and none is decoration:
  - A STRANGER. Not a quest-giver, not a named character you have met. Someone
    with no relationship to you, which is why `Acquaintance.HeardOfYou` exists
    at 0.20 and sits deliberately BELOW recognition: gossip travels further
    than faces do.
  - REFERRING TO SOMETHING YOU DID. Not a reaction to your reputation as a
    number, but to a specific act.
  - THAT THEY DID NOT WITNESS. This is the whole moat in four words. A witness
    reacting is perception; a non-witness reacting is TRANSMISSION, and
    transmission is what no other game has.
  - WHOSE ROUTE YOU COULD TRACE. It is not enough that it happened. The player
    has to be able to find out HOW it reached them, or it is indistinguishable
    from a scripted line.
acceptance: one such moment, produced by the real simulation and not a canned

  recoverable

  IT IS THE MERIDIAN TEST'S SECOND CONDITION MADE CONCRETE, which is why it
  belongs in the record rather than in a plan: "Within those 30 minutes the
  world visibly knows them at least once: recognized, gossiped about, or
  confronted with something they did earlier." This item says WHICH of those
  three is the one worth aiming at, and why: recognition is perception and any
  game can do it; being confronted by a witness is memory and several games do
  it; being told about yourself BY SOMEONE WHO WAS NOT THERE is the one that
  is ours.

  WHAT IT MEANS FOR QUEUE 138, whose smallest version is already agreed with
  Jafar (one crime, one sighting, one overheard line, one sitting): the
  OVERHEARD LINE must come from a non-witness, and the route must be
  recoverable. A loop that delivers the line from the witness is a smaller and
  much easier thing that proves nothing this project needs proved.

  AND IT IS WHY QUEUE 365 IS NOT A SUSPICION BAR. The route being traceable is
  what turns a system into something a player can tell is there. A number tells
  them a system exists; a stranger saying the wrong thing about them, and a
  path back to why, is the thing itself.
