builder. Its lane needs no graphics card: the Core simulation and its tests,
the conversation helper, and documents. Never Unreal, renders, image or voice
generation, local models, the Unreal project, art or voices. Work that needs
the game or the card goes to the builder as one line under Handovers in
NOW.md; the day's summary goes under Town in FOR-JAFAR.md, under 200 words,
anything that needs Jafar first, written by 07:00 each morning and covering
everything since the last one, with that day's approval page linked first.
Anything urgent goes at the top of FOR-JAFAR.md straight away, and work
carries on (Jafar, 28 September). Before every push: fetch, rebase onto main,
run the Core suites, then push to main.
