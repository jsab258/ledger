## 5. The wet ground

- Read its shading first (wet-ground-coat-beyond-decals): since 29 Oct road, flags and kerb use M_LedgerGround (the look file's ground_coat), made by tools/ue/make_ground_material.py through make_base_material.py. A decal writes colour, normal, roughness, metal and specular, never the coat; software Lumen traces static, instanced and landscape meshes only, never skinned ones, and culls traced parts under 30 cm radius.
- The method (WET-GROUND-METHOD, revised; from Lagarde's Water drop series): wet is two states. Saturated stone: real dry base colour times a porosity darkening per surface, less water's 0.02, still rough (0.54 to 0.68), its relief kept. Water as a coverage c in the clear coat: c from the stone's own grain against a water level over a margin of 0.4, no metre-scale noise; the coat's F0 is the engine's fixed 0.04; the top normal eases from the stone's to flat by c, since Lumen's diffuse reads it. One normal serves both layers: a separate bottom normal needs r.ClearCoatNormal for the whole project (make_base_material.py). Its coat roughness, GROUND_COAT_ROUGHNESS there: 0.03 mirrored, 0.3 darkened every region, the broader 0.15 passed (WET-GROUND-METHOD, first trials; DECISIONS 29 Oct 23:26).
- Mafia: one global wetness; puddles as colourless decals carrying roughness, height and occlusion (Study 5; MAFIA-WEAR). Standing water as such decals waits on the corner's list (CORNER-PLAN-2026-11-02.md §5).
- Where a sole meets the coat, its reflection shows as a pale line: nothing occludes a coat's reflection, and the ray under a sole finds the sky (PALE-LINE-RESEARCH §1, §2). The cut lives in the ground's material: it reads the contact decal's roughness through the DBuffer Texture node (its R) and takes the coat away there (CONTACT_COAT_ROUGHNESS 0.6, CONTACT_CUT_EXPONENT 1.5 in make_base_material.py; puddles write 0.02, so they keep their coat). Passed seen whole in the editor on 2 Nov after three rounds, close-up traces listed as narrow; its packaged check and a pale patch on the far pavement wait for the morning pictures (DECISIONS 2 Nov 09:31).
