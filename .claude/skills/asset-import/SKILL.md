# Asset import and checks

Why: CLAUDE.md ("One home per fact; reuse first"; "Builds, git and disk") and RULINGS.md ("Sources, licences and money"; 1 Nov, the week's rules 5 and 6). The licence allowlist (ledger-v2/research/license-allowlist.md) is law. A packaged game places only assets that exist as cooked packages, so every asset is a build product made by a script, never by a person in an editor (tools/ue/import_prop_meshes.py, import_figure.py, their docstrings). Garments are made by the garment skill (.claude/skills/game-garment/SKILL.md, its step 9 imports them here); every piece ends in the gate (.claude/skills/gate/SKILL.md) and the packaged walk (.claude/skills/packaged-walk/SKILL.md). Memory notes are named by file (C:\Users\Jafar\.claude\projects\C--Users-Jafar-ledger-local\memory\). "Check first" marks what the sources do not settle. Reviewed once: production/research/skills/ASSET-IMPORT-SKILL-REVIEW-2026-11-02.md.

**Always** (none of these bends):
- Nothing ships off the allowlist. Nothing marked NoAI is used, even as a reference, except content Epic itself publishes on Fab (Megascans, the MetaHuman garments, the Game Animation Sample), used inside the game only (RULINGS 3 Oct, narrowed 13 Oct). CC BY 4.0 only for clothes and their accessories (shoes, belts, hats, bags), never one marked NoAI, each maker credited, any brand mark painted out (RULINGS 30 Oct; allowlist SHIP-SAFE 2). Sound "is a lane, CC0 only" (RULINGS, D26); voices and fonts have their own entries (SHIP-SAFE 1, 7). Unreal-only content (MetaHuman, Epic's animation) never leaves an Unreal build (SHIP-SAFE 3, 8). Never ship: real brands, logos, car models or likenesses; Marvelous's own avatars, samples, patterns and trims, or anything from its trial (NEVER SHIP 5). Nothing from Mafia or Kingdom Come II, study extracts included, enters LEDGER or git: only our own words and numbers (RULINGS 11 Oct).
- Money, purchases, his sign-in and licences outside the list are his alone (CLAUDE.md; RULINGS 16 Sep). Claude never signs in; free allowlisted, non-NoAI content is downloaded without asking and named in the summary (RULINGS 24 Sep; DECISIONS 24 Sep).
- Reuse first starts at home; anything fetched is looked at before it is used, or DECISIONS.md says why it is replaced (CLAUDE.md).
- Git holds text, small files and previews (tools/push_guard.py; tools/git-size-guard.py); MetaHuman files never (RULINGS 3 Oct, Disk). Write only where production/retention.json names; lasting inputs on F:\LedgerTools, nothing large on C: (CLAUDE.md).
- One Unreal job at a time, room checked first (`python tools/retention.py space --job "what" --drives CF`), committed before every editor session, any result short of an explicit success a stop (CLAUDE.md; the packaged-walk skill, step 2). Routine imports of designed pieces run on Sonnet; Opus for diagnosis and every final review (RULINGS 25 Oct).
- Helpers and reviewers never click and never use computer use or UI Automation (RULINGS 1 Nov, rule 7).
- Two tries per fault, then research; a third needs Jafar's yes (CLAUDE.md). An asset passes only in the packaged walk.

**Requests this covers**, for example: "a pillar box for the corner"; "Poly Haven's crate, but it has a firm's name on it"; "bring Epic's boots in from Fab"; "import the new sit clip"; "re-import the street after the export"; "the prop is ten times too big"; "it draws grey in the game"; "Tom walks through the bench"; "attribution says no row"; "the push was refused for a picture".

## 1. Already in the project?

- Search in this order before making or fetching anything: production/specs/garments.json, CATALOGUE.md, the game, canon, production/research (`ls tools | grep -i <subject>` too), F:/LedgerTools (garments, game-inputs, polyhaven, fab: what is already downloaded) and every branch; then Epic's MetaHuman clothing; then outside (CLAUDE.md; RULINGS 1 Nov, rule 5). One character's shoes were researched outward, a scan downloaded, painted and fitted, while another character's passed boots stood in the game; MetaHuman bodies share one layout, so a finished piece usually carries to another body (reuse-first-own-pieces; DECISIONS 30 Oct 12:00). A bridge was rebuilt while tools/md held one (search-tools-before-building).
- A family is made by the asset plan (production/research/asset-plan), its kits and sources, never improvised (RULINGS 3 Oct). Reference first: how Mafia or Kingdom Come II does it; the order's item carries a "Reference:" line (a number or method, or "Reference: none found") or its study's path, or tools/order_ledger.py fails the push (CLAUDE.md; its usage text).
- Before any kit import, a design check: each prop reads at the game's distance, its textures are sharp enough, it carries no real name (RULINGS 25 Oct).
- Epic's MetaHuman presets can be looked at without a build (a JPEG inside each preset .uasset, red and blue swapped), but the preview is not what the build gives (metahuman-preset-thumbnails).
- **Check:** the item's plan opens with "already in the project:", naming what was searched and found (reuse-first-own-pieces).

## 2. Source and licence

- Sources in order: free assets that fit (Poly Haven, ambientCG, CC0 Sketchfab scans with his token, unmarked CC BY with credit for clothes and their accessories); else our own, made in Blender from real photographs and generated textures; buying, commissioning or hiring only by his ruling (RULINGS 3 Oct, 30 Oct). Not allowed: CGTrader's licence (RULINGS 2 Oct). His Sketchfab token is kept beside the LEDGER key, outside the project (DECISIONS 3 Oct); Mixamo needs his account and a token he supplies (SHIP-SAFE 3). A new tool enters only by a DECISIONS record naming its weights licence (PROCESS 2); every asset and generated output carries a licence tag (PROCESS 1).
- Read the licence and the NoAI mark from the source's own page or data, dated (DECISIONS 3 Oct: each Fab listing's isAiForbidden). When Jafar says he owns or is licensed for something, record his word and go; a licence that excludes the use, or an install on his account, still goes to him as a question (his-word-on-ownership-settles-it).
- Fab: downloads need his Epic sign-in (fab-needs-jafar-signed-in), so he downloads, sent the exact link and steps (RULINGS 31 Oct): an item on his one page (RULINGS 2 Nov) and a NEEDS YOU message before any report (RULINGS 1 Nov; the his-page skill). The project's Fab plugin is off only because an Epic update broke every build, to be undone when Epic ships matching rules (DECISIONS 28 Oct 21:30). Once a .mhpkg is on disk, an import task brings it in (step 5).
- Look at the model itself, not its title, and at every map, the normal map included: Poly Haven's crate was rebuilt to its measured box because its colour map carried a real firm's name (DECISIONS 13 Oct 07:00); a scan's brand marks, one moulded only into its normal map, were painted out of all five maps by tools/meshgen/paint_out_marks.py, a second pass placed from the normal map after the first missed what the colour map hid (DECISIONS 30 Oct 09:59). What it shows passes canon.md's content rule.
- **Check:** licence, link and date in the item's DECISIONS line; a THIRD-PARTY.md row (and production/specs/credits.json for anything CC BY: SHIP-SAFE 2); nothing from the never-ship list.

## 3. Where the files live

- Sources on F: in protected folders (F:/LedgerTools/fab, garments, game-inputs, bodies, polyhaven), never only in F:/LedgerTools/tmp (deleted after 7 days) or scratch (3 days), though some usage examples read from tmp (production/retention.json).
- Git: the commit guard refuses pictures, sounds, films and models outside the folders the build reads from git (production/assets and ue-probe/Content up to 25 MB a file, fonts, reference, decals, base-mesh, acks) (tools/git-size-guard.py); the push guard then refuses any file over 1 MB and any picture outside production/previews, a preview at 500 KB or more (tools/push_guard.py). So a large mesh or new picture goes to F:\LedgerTools\game-inputs under its repository path, its folder in .gitignore (push-guard-1mb-blocks-program-cs; new-pictures-live-on-game-inputs); ci-checks does not run the push guard. Each importer has its own game-inputs rule (its GAME_INPUTS lines): import_street and import_shop_rooms take the newer of checkout and F:; import_shop_displays, import_front_joinery and retarget_sitting read F:; an importer without one (import_vehicles, import_people, import_sounds, import_figure) needs its source in git, under 1 MB.
- In Unreal: our own and free content under /Game/Ledger, which DefaultGame.ini cooks whole; anything that must never ship goes in its DirectoriesToNeverCook (Marvelous's trial jacket, DECISIONS 5 Oct). MetaHuman-derived files under /Game/Ledger/MetaHumans, outside the public repository (import_garments.py, import_jacket.py docstrings; step 5 for which project ships).
- **Check:** `python tools/push_guard.py --staged` passes before every commit that adds a file.

## 4. Size and scale, measured

- Real size comes in at scale 1: the spec's box is the GLB's own measured size and nothing is ever scaled (import_prop_meshes.py, the contract with vignette-scene.json's held_props.pivot_note). The old fetched kits' FBX units are not metres; read their proportions (`python tools/prop-dimensions.py [NAME] [--parts]`, FBX under ledger/Assets/Props). Bodies by heads tall and neck fraction, a series with no threshold (`python tools/body-proportions.py`, FBX under ledger/Assets/Characters).
- Read it back after the import: the street's sign within 5 cm of where the export put it (streetImportSign=AGREES, import_street.py); each prop's bounds against its spec box (import_prop_meshes.py, no gate yet: read the series); a garment's size in cm (import-garments.txt); a clip's root at scale 1 and its pelvis about a metre up, since a sit once came in a hundred times too big (import-anim.txt; import_anim.py). import_figure.py measures its one body's height for the no-children rule (CLAUDE.md, project facts); any other imported body is unmeasured: check first.
- **Check:** each read-back line recorded beside the item; a difference explained before the gate.

## 5. The import, by its script

- The build's import step: tools/ue/make_base_material.py, run by the build machine on every package, calls import_figure, import_street, make_root_motion_clips, retarget_sitting, import_people, make_tom_retarget, import_vehicles, import_shop_displays, import_front_joinery (materials only, import_mesh=False), import_glass_bake, import_shop_rooms, nanite_audit and import_sounds; each import script's `--selftest` runs without Unreal. No live workflow or tool calls import_prop_meshes.py. Locally, one script as a commandlet: `UnrealEditor-Cmd ue-probe/LedgerProbe.uproject -run=pythonscript -script="C:/Users/Jafar/ledger-local/tools/ue/<script>.py" -EnablePlugins=PythonScriptPlugin -unattended -nopause -nosplash -stdout` (the workflow's arguments); a relative -script, as some usage texts show, fails quietly (unreal-commandlet-script-full-path). From Git Bash, `export MSYS_NO_PATHCONV=1` before any /Game argument (git-bash-rewrites-asset-paths). After any street export, re-import it locally before any picture (reimport-street-after-export).
- The MetaHuman projects: import_garments, import_anim, import_cap, import_jacket, import_fab_clothes and fab_closure run as `set LEDGER_MH_SCRIPT=<name>` (plus the script's own LEDGER_ variables) and `UnrealEditor.exe <project> -unattended`. Opened without LEDGER_MH_SCRIPT, a project runs the cast maker (its Content/Python/init_unreal.py). Only C:\LedgerTools\mh-assemble\Content\Ledger\MetaHumans reaches the game, mirrored by the build machine; others reach it through junctions there, and nothing under /Game/Fab is copied (fabClothesCopied=NONE-NoAI) (ledger-probe-unreal.yml). So what lands in the dressing project (F:/LedgerTools/mh-dress), under /Game/Fab or in import_anim's default /Game/Ledger/Cloth/anims is not in the game: check first how it gets there.
- Epic's packages: import_fab_clothes.py, one package a run named by LEDGER_FAB_ONLY (seven at once ran the PC out of memory; without it each run replaces fab-clothes.json), asked under /Game/Fab/<name>, though a package may keep its own layout: fab-clothes.json says where its assets landed. Epic's boots, worn by a cast member, drew in the engine's grey default material in the editor's pictures, their materials left in the dressing project; Epic's garment meshes cannot be exported (two engine asserts); a game MetaHuman is rebuilt over only after a hashed backup (DECISIONS 2 Nov 13:16).
- Kinds: a garment is a skeletal mesh on its wearer's body skeleton keeping the weights it came with; only hems, skirts and coat tails simulate (import_garments.py; game-clothes-are-skinned-meshes). It needs its row in production/specs/garments.json (LEDGER_GARMENTS=<Name>,... limits a run). A clip from Blender goes onto the MetaHuman body skeleton (import_anim.py: LEDGER_ANIM_FBX, LEDGER_ANIM_DEST, LEDGER_ANIM_NAME). retarget_sitting.py carries only its named Mixamo clips (CLIPS) and Epic's sample idles, so a new clip is an edit to its lists: each character's T-pose imported as a skeletal mesh and its clips onto that skeleton; importing each clip with its own skeleton took its first frame as the rest pose and the seated clips came out standing and twisted.
- **Check:** in ue-material.txt, the run's own new line, counted before the run, never the file's last line or the exit code; the MetaHuman scripts rewrite their whole report, so its time is after the run's start and it holds no RAISED line; no "LogPython: Error" in the log; the status words read (streetImportStatus=OK, its NaniteOff count equal to its meshes).

## 6. Materials and textures

- Materials are made by scripts (tools/ue/make_*_material.py; import_garments.py rebuilds its graph each run, since a broken graph once stayed). A garment baked to maps wears one material on every slot, and the FBX's slot materials are not imported (DECISIONS 28 Oct 22:31).
- Each texture compressed for its role: normal maps TC_NORMALMAP with sRGB off, packed maps TC_MASKS with sRGB off, colour maps sRGB on; the role is read from the name's ending (asset-budgets.json texture_roles), so a weave named T_<Name>_Detail was read as colour until renamed _Detail_Normal (DECISIONS 30 Oct 13:24). Never a normal map from a JPEG; sides a power of two, within the kind's texture_max and 4096 overall (asset_checks.py rules 5 and 6; asset-budgets.json).
- No slot empty or on the engine's default material, no material sampling no texture, no texture of one flat colour (rule 7, placeholders); no see-through slot that is not glass (rule 9): the jacket's first build drew Unreal's grey grid because its material had not compiled (asset_checks.py docstring).
- **Check:** the asset checks' rules 5, 6, 7 and 9 pass (step 8); the piece seen in a game picture, not only the editor's asset view.

## 7. Collision, levels of detail, Nanite

- By the budget kind (asset-budgets.json): full-detail triangles under the kind's cap, a warning above 90% of it (warn_fraction); collision for a small prop none or one box, a medium prop simple with at most 10 hulls, a building module simple boxes, accessories none, garments and people the body's capsule (rules 2 and 11). Today the spec's folders map only the garment folders to kinds (the rest exempt), so props, kit pieces and the street are checked by none of this until their folders get a kind. The street collides complex-as-simple on purpose: it is exported one mesh per material, and a box round each would be one shape as big as the whole street (import_street.py); rule 11 would fail it, so its folder needs an exempt kind or a waiver.
- A collision count of -1 is the editor refusing in a commandlet, not zero (import_prop_meshes.py). Levels of detail: the kind's count, each at most lod_step_max (60%) of the one before (rule 3); garments regenerate theirs with Unreal's reduction from the project's groups (import_garments.py; DECISIONS 29 Oct 00:12, Mafia's shares).
- Nanite off on every static mesh of ours, read back after the import (rule 4; import_street.py, import_vehicles.py): a mesh left on draws its coarse stand-in. tools/ue/nanite_audit.py lists any left on (LEDGER_NANITE_FIX=1 fixes them; the build's import step runs it).
- **Check:** the asset checks' rules 2, 3, 4 and 11; a walk-through test in the packaged walk, since only a sweep in the built level proves collision (import_prop_meshes.py).

## 8. The asset checks, before any reviewer

- The thresholds' one home is production/specs/asset-budgets.json: a new piece gets an entry (its folders and parts; a garment's parts name SKM_<Name>, which `--check-spec` enforces), and each new folder a kind, or its meshes fail with "no budget kind". A changed number is a spec edit with a DECISIONS line, never a code edit; a set-aside fault is a waiver there, naming its DECISIONS and FINDINGS lines (its folders_rule, waiver_rule; DECISIONS 28 Oct 16:57).
- Without Unreal: `python tools/ue/asset_checks.py --check-spec` and `--selftest` (both in ci-checks). Inside the editor, with LEDGER_CHECK_PIECE=<piece> (LEDGER_CHECK_FOLDERS overrides its folders, asset_checks.py main()): `UnrealEditor-Cmd.exe <full path>/LedgerProbe.uproject -run=pythonscript -script=<full path>/tools/ue/asset_checks.py -EnablePlugins=PythonScriptPlugin,GeometryScripting -unattended -nopause -nosplash -stdout` (no -nullrhi), or `asset_checks.run("<piece>")` from an import script's own session (its usage text).
- Names: Epic's prefixes (SM_, SKM_, T_, M_, MI_, AS_ and the rest in the spec's names) for our own new assets in the checked piece's folders; imports keep their names (asset-budgets.json, its study13 source). Rule 8 refuses a stale import (its source's MD5 changed since) and a missing part: reviewers had been catching stale imports by eye (asset_checks.py docstring).
- **Check:** the run's own line, `assetChecks=PASS piece=<piece> ...`, appended to ue-material.txt beside whichever project ran it, and its report production/asset-checks/<piece>.json read, warnings too; the exit code is no verdict.

## 9. Catalogue, attribution, canon

- `python tools/catalogue.py`: a folder or file directly under production/art or production/research with no line in CATALOGUE.md fails the build, and so does a line naming one that no longer exists (CLAUDE.md; catalogue.py).
- `python tools/attribution-check.py`: every directory of third-party content names its THIRD-PARTY.md rows (the WATCHED table) or is recorded as ours (OURS); adding one is an edit to that table plus a row holding its token. An asset file outside both, or a file kind in neither suffix set, fails. `--package` traces every shipped file to a row and refuses its NEVER_SHIP paths, /Content/Fab/, Megascans and Quixel among them, until Epic's folders are named there as exceptions with their rows: check first before any of Epic's Fab content is cooked.
- Words in production/specs and content pass the canon gate (`python tools/canon-gate.py <file>`, or `--corpus`): "shell" reads as a brand there, so write hull, outer or body pieces (canon-gate-flags-shell). The content and names gates run in ci-checks too.
- **Check:** `bash tools/ci-checks.sh` ends in failed=none (after `unset MSYS_NO_PATHCONV`; the packaged-walk skill, step 3).

## 10. Staging what the game reads at run time

- A packaged copy reads its run-time files only from Content/LedgerData, which tools/ue/stage_game_data.py fills from its FIXED list, its folder walks (the checkout only) and the lists it computes from the specs (the street's sidecar, the pieces, sounds, wear and shop pictures). A new spec, picture or sound the game reads goes into that list in the same commit, or it works in the editor and fails only packaged (packaged-copy-needs-ledgerrepo); a picture kept only on F: is staged only when one of those specs names it.
- **Check:** the new path is in FIXED or named by a computed list, and `python tools/ue/stage_game_data.py --list` shows the count risen and missing=0 (a file never listed cannot show as missing).

## 11. Then the gate and the packaged walk

- Editor pictures and the checks above are screens. The gate skill (pictures at the played level, one fresh reviewer per view) and the packaged-walk skill (into that night's build, walked by the tester) decide. Anything touching taste or identity (a face, a voice, the look) reaches his page before merging (CLAUDE.md).
- **Check:** the walk names no new fault in the asset; "done" only then.

## Records

- DECISIONS.md one line per material choice: source, licence and its date, what was searched first, why anything found was replaced. THIRD-PARTY.md and credits.json for each third-party source; CATALOGUE.md for any new art or research; FINDINGS.md for each waiver or set-aside fault.
- Deleting (RULINGS 29 Oct): Claude's own unused leftovers (an import's stray copies, test files) without asking, one line each; his yes for anything of his, page-approved, bought, downloaded at his request or not makeable again; never what the game or a build uses or what the backup holds. When in doubt, it is his.
- NOTEBOOK.md a few setting-free lines when the unit finishes; the time log against the item, helpers' time too.
