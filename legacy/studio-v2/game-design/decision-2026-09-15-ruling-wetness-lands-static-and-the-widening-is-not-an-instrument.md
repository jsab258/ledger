## 2. What I verified, claim by claim, and where the brief to me was wrong

THE ALGEBRA, done from the source and not from the comment. Unity,
`LightModel.cs` 594 to 598: `Smoothness(dry, rain) = Clamp01(dry + (0.92 -
dry) rain)`. Put `dry = 1 - R` where R is the roughness texel and take one
minus the result: `1 - (1 - R) - (0.92 - 1 + R) rain = R - (R - 0.08) rain =
R (1 - rain) + 0.08 rain`, which is `Lerp(R, 0.08, rain)` and 0.08 is `1 -
0.92`. The clamp never bites: for dry and rain both in 0..1 the expression
lies between dry and 0.92, inside 0..1, so the identity is exact and not an
approximation, which is what the 441-point sweep at 1e-12 asserts at the
test's 2920 to 2942. The conversion sits at `RoughnessFromSmoothness`
(SurfaceBind.h 751 to 754) and the floor is derived from the ceiling at 765
to 768, not typed; the generator types 0.08 at line 194 and its `--selftest`
reads the ceiling out of the header and refuses a disagreement (1452 to
1463). The resident's finding 2 stands.

THE CONVENTION IS RIGHT TOO, which the brief did not ask and I checked
because it is the second place this port could be plausible and backwards:
Unity Standard's smoothness is one minus PERCEPTUAL roughness and Unreal's
Roughness pin IS perceptual roughness (both square it inside the BRDF), so
`1 - s` is the map and not a guess about which of two roughness definitions
the other engine meant.

ONE NAMED DIFFERENCE, not a fault, for whoever reads the wet ladder rows when
309 lands. Unity's `SetSmoothness` (AssetLibrary 756 to 810) scales a gloss
MAP by target over its own mean, preserving the map's variation, and drops
the map for the uniform scalar once that scale passes 4. Unreal's lerp
compresses each texel's distance from 0.08 by `1 - rain`. At 1.0 both go
uniform. At 0.6 the two agree in mean only if the pack roughness file's mean
smoothness is near the scalar datum, which nobody has measured; the verdict
says so on every wet line in its own words (`NOT-the-pack-roughness-texel`,
SurfaceBind.h 1778 to 1779).

THE ORDER OF THE TERMS. Unity: `mat.color = BaseColour x albedo` in gamma,
converted on upload (AssetLibrary 841 to 843). This side: `AlbedoGradeFor`
(the legacy pair, walked back) times `WetAlbedoScale` in gamma, converted
once (SurfaceBind.h 925 to 941). The walk-back is affine and not a multiply,
so the order is a different number and the test plants the other order and
asserts it differs by more than 1e-4 (3113 to 3117: 0.4025 against 0.3620).
