# D24: what LEDGER is not

STATUS: DECIDED 2026-09-14 by Jafar, dictated and recorded by the resident.
Recorded **in canon beside the pillars**, because it is a premise-level fact
about the work and belongs where the pillars are read.

## The rule, as given

LEDGER is **NOT an open-world sandbox, NOT a shooter, NOT a driving game, NOT
an economy simulation, and NOT a story-first narrative game.**

**It is a small dense town where what people know about you is the mechanic.**

## The budget consequence, which is the operative half

**Anything that does not feed perception, memory, gossip or consequence gets
the smallest budget that keeps it from looking wrong.**

That is a spend rule, not a ban. Driving, fighting and trade may exist and must
not look broken; they may not take budget from the four things that are the
game.
