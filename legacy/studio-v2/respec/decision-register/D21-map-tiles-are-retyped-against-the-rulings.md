STATUS: DECIDED 2026-09-14 by Jafar, dictated and recorded by the resident.

## The rule, as given

**The map's tiles are re-typed against the rulings.**

- Under **D16** anything that exists **only in the Unity build reads ABSENT**,
  because Unreal is the game now.
- **D17, D18 and D19 retire what they forbid.**

## The standing rule it creates

**A decision record names the tiles it changes and they are re-typed in the
same batch.** Not a follow-up item, not a sweep later: the same batch. A ruling
that changes the map and leaves the map saying otherwise has produced two
sources of truth, which is the fault this rule exists to stop.
