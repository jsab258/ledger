# D23: the bar for Phase A is the in-house Hook sheet

STATUS: DECIDED 2026-09-14 by Jafar, dictated and recorded by the resident.

## The rule, as given

**The bar for Phase A is the in-house Hook sheet, AS A FLOOR AND NOT A
CEILING.**

## The test, and it is a test a person performs

**When a frame of the built street stands beside it and I cannot say which way
the gap runs, the budget moves to the next stage.** The reason for moving is
not that the work is finished: it is that **the same bar then has to be met
across a whole town rather than one street**, which is the harder problem and
the one the budget is better spent on.

## What the studio may not do with this

**Raising the bar is a decision I make when the town exists, not a thing the
studio stops short of.** A floor is not permission to stop at it, and it is
also not licence to polish one street past it while the town does not exist.
