## The rule, as given

**Body language is part of the MOAT, not polish.** In a game about people
watching people, **how somebody holds themselves is information.**

- **A person who half-recognises me should read differently from one who does
  not.**
- **Somebody lying should hold themselves differently.**
- **A person who has heard about me should show it before they speak.**

**The five-rung identification ladder is currently INVISIBLE except in
dialogue, and body language is how a player reads it without being told.**
