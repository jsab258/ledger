# D25: residents are tiered by authoring, never by memory

STATUS: DECIDED 2026-09-14 by Jafar, dictated and recorded by the resident.

## The rule, as given

**Residents are tiered by AUTHORING, never by MEMORY.**

**Everyone perceives, remembers and gossips; that is pillar 1 and NO PLAN MAY
GIVE ANY RESIDENT NO MEMORY.**

## What the tiers actually are

The tiers are **how much of a person someone is**:

1. **Fully authored.**
2. **Assembled from parts.**
3. **A varied body with a routine.**

A tier says how much authoring a resident has had. It never says how much the
resident remembers.

## What tier counts are, and what they are not

**Tier counts are a production schedule, not a scope limit.** The target
remains **a town of three to five hundred residents who all remember**, and
**the near-term counts exist to measure what a resident COSTS so the schedule
can be planned rather than guessed.**

**NO PHASE TARGET IS EVER RECORDED AS THE FINAL SCOPE.** See D27, which is the
general form of this.
