# D28: the presentation layer is built early, not last

STATUS: DECIDED 2026-09-14 by Jafar, dictated and recorded by the resident.

## The rule, as given

**The presentation layer is built EARLY, not last, because it is what makes a
frame read as a real game per hour spent.**

## Its steps are not a separate plan

**Its steps are the numbered list of the visual slice and are NOT a separate
plan.** They sit inside D22's visual path order. One list, not two.

The list, as given on 2026-09-14:

1. Fix the exposure fault so the same camera and conditions give the same
   picture.
2. Light and weather: bring the sky down so the sun lands and the street has
   dark in it, measured against the in-house Hook sheet, with sodium lamps at
   dusk.
3. Post-processing: film grain, a vignette, bloom on the lamps, slight
   chromatic aberration, lens dirt, and a colour grade toward the period's film
   stock.
4. Camera: field of view, slight head bob, motion blur, depth of field for
   close conversation.
5. Wire wetness. It is parsed and read by nothing, and the reference street is
   wet.
6. The surfaces: a real material library with roughness, normal and wear.
7. Wear and dirt through the decal system that already exists.
8. The twelve-package batch through all five stations, onto the street as real
   meshes, with cost per verified piece on the throughput ledger.
9. Typography and the title card.
10. The cab office from its Blender blockout, through GLB and the mesh route,
    standing on the street and photographed.

## What is deliberately excluded until that list is done

**More geometry, more unique buildings, animation polish.** They are expensive
and **read as improvements only after presentation is right.**

## The target that ties them

**One screenshot of the street at dusk, wet, lamps lit, a figure in
silhouette.** That single frame is how Jafar judges whether this looks like a
real game.

## Amendment A1, 2026-09-17: the deferral has run its course, and item 1 was never closed

Ruled by Jafar on 2026-09-17, after he looked at our own Hook sheet put beside
the built street at the same viewpoint. His words:

"I have looked at the pair. They are far apart, and the gap has changed
category. What landed is real: wet road, grade, overcast light, materials at
close range. What the comparison shows is that lighting is no longer the
difference. The reference is a street of buildings with faces, shopfronts,
windows, doors, signage, roofs, chimneys, and two hundred metres of depth to
hills. Ours is a corridor of blank walls with fog at thirty metres doing double
duty as sky and as a cover for there being nothing beyond. So D28's deferral
has run its course: presentation is now good enough that geometry and facades
are the blocker."

WHAT THIS CHANGES. The exclusion above, "more geometry, more unique buildings",
is LIFTED by the person who set it. It is not lifted by the studio noticing the
street is bare, and it was not lifted early: the comparison that lifted it is
the one the ladder's rung 1 exists to force.

WHAT IT DOES NOT CHANGE, and he said so in the same message: "Nothing starts
before the reset." And: "The night exposure fault comes before the figure,
since a figure cannot be judged in a frame whose exposure is random."

ITEM 1 OF THE LIST ABOVE WAS NEVER CLOSED, which nobody had noticed until his
ordering made it matter. "Fix the exposure fault so the same camera and
conditions give the same picture" is the first step of this decision's own
list, and on 2026-09-17 it is still open and now measured: ten of twelve
controls in run 54 and eight of twelve in run 53 disagree with their own second
take by up to 0.46 of whole-frame mean luma. Queue 384 carries the readings.
The target this decision names, one screenshot of the street at dusk, wet,
lamps lit, a figure in silhouette, cannot be judged until item 1 closes, which
is exactly his ordering restated.

THE COST QUESTION HE ASKED WITH IT is answered separately and is not a
forecast: "tell me what a single authored building facade costs, end to end,
because that is the unit Phase A is actually made of and nobody has measured
it. If the answer is that it cannot be measured yet, say what would produce
it."
