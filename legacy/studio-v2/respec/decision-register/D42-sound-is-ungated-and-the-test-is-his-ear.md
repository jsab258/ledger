# D42. Sound is ungated, and the test is his ear at the end

Ruled by Jafar, 2026-09-15, alongside D41, on the same terms and with one
difference that changes the method.

## The rule, as given

**"Sound is ungated on the same terms. Foley, ambient beds, the mix. There is
no reference sheet for it, so the test is my ear at the end rather than my
approval at each step: build it, put a clip in the brief so I can hear the
direction, and come to me when you believe it is right or when you are
stuck."**

## The difference from D41, and it is the whole of this record

D41 has a reference: the Hook sheet is approved and its numbers are measured,
so the studio can compare against something and know where it stands. SOUND
HAS NO SUCH SHEET. There is nothing to measure against and no number that
means right.

So the substitute he names is CONTINUOUS EXPOSURE rather than a gate: a clip
rides the brief so he hears the direction as it develops, and the judgement
happens at the end. That is not the same as approval at each step, and the
record says so because the temptation will be to treat each clip as a request
for a verdict. It is not. It is him being kept in earshot.

## What this means for the brief

The daily brief carries a clip when sound work has moved. No question attaches
to it, by the same rule D41 sets for the frame, unless the studio believes it
is right or is stuck.

## The two cases, unchanged from D41

When the studio believes it is right. Or when it is stuck and cannot say why,
in which case it says what it tried and what that did.

## The licence rule is untouched and outranks this

Voice and audio sourcing stay bound by the allowlist and by the consent rule:
only corpora whose contributors donated their voices to build speech
technology, and no identifiable public figures, ever. Ungating the JUDGEMENT
of sound does not ungate where sound comes from.
