# D20: no minimap for Phase A

STATUS: DECIDED 2026-09-14 by Jafar, dictated and recorded by the resident.

## The rule, as given

**No minimap for Phase A.** The navigation surface is **the what-they-know
HUD**, with **a paper map in the Ledger** and **street signage for
orientation**.

## The reason, which is the moat and not the interface

**A minimap showing people would tell me what the town knows before the town
does.** The game's subject is what is known and by whom; a surface that leaks
positions for free is not a convenience, it is a hole in pillar 1.

## The scope, stated so this is not read as a veto for ever

Scoped to the CURRENT TOWN. **If Phase B's region lands, or the town grows past
what a person can hold in their head, a minimap returns as an open question**,
and that reason is what it has to answer. A later proposal is judged on whether
the town has outgrown a person's head, never on whether this ruling can be
overturned.
