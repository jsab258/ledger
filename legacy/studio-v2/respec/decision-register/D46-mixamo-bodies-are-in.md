## What changed in the same batch, because a ruling that changes the law edits
## the law

`ledger-v2/research/license-allowlist.md`, SHIP-SAFE item 3, previously ended
"Mixamo animations." It now reads "Mixamo characters AND animations, with
Jafar's account and a token he supplies", plus "Nothing is purchased; a Mixamo
character is a download." Those are the words already used by CLAUDE.md
section 0, `ledger-v2/studio-v2/casebook-claims.md:73`,
`game-design/shopping-list.md:19` and `game-design/the-gap.md:203`, which is
what the option asked for.

## Why it was asked rather than assumed, which is the part worth keeping

Four documents including CLAUDE.md, the file the studio reads every session,
already said bodies were in. The allowlist said "animations" and the allowlist
OUTRANKS CLAUDE.md. A resident reading the narrower line generously is exactly
the move the licence law exists to prevent, so the studio refused to settle it
by doing. The cost of asking was one line from him; the cost of guessing wrong
is a body in a shipped frame under a licence nobody checked.

## One claim corrected on the way, recorded so it is not re-derived

The queue item behind this card said "the Unity path renders one" of the
eighteen bodies. IT DOES NOT AND NEVER HAS: `ledger/Assets/Resources` holds no
`Characters/Body`, no body prefab or fbx exists anywhere in the tree, and
`RealBody.cs:1287` carries the fallback
`Why = "Resources/Characters/Body not in the build"` under a comment saying the
likeliest cause is that the Editor step did not run. The path is WIRED and no
body asset is committed. The correction strengthened the question rather than
weakening it: no Mixamo body has ever been rendered here, so the first one to
reach a frame was still ahead of us, which is when a licence line needs to be
right rather than after.
