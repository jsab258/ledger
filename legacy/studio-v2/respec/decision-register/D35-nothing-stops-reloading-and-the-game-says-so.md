# D35: nothing stops reloading, and the game says so

STATUS: DECIDED 2026-09-14 by Jafar, dictated and recorded by the resident.

## The rule, as given

**Nothing mechanically stops reloading to un-see a witness, and the game
says so plainly.**

**Scarce saves punish the ordinary player and stop nobody determined.**

**Instead the game states which things are permanent, so a player who
reloads knows they are opting out of the only thing the game is about.**

## What this answers

The Kingdom Come 2 research audit asked what stops a player reloading to
un-see a witness, and offered three options: accept it, make saving cost
something, or autosave at the moment of being seen. The Disco Elysium audit
asked the adjacent question, whether the game marks a moment as permanent up
front, and asked for it to be answered together with this one. This ruling
answers both at once: no save cost, no forced autosave, a stated warning
instead.

## What this does not decide

The warning's wording or where it appears. UI work against the standard,
waiting for a frame.
