# Studio v2 Constitution (carried and extended from the Measured Studio)

1. A claim without an instrument decays. Every milestone, phase and content piece carries a check that can fail.
2. Classification, not adjudication. LLMs choose from closed sets assembled from live state; deterministic Core computes every outcome the player feels.
3. Canon supremacy. canon.md outranks every other document and every agent. Violations are gate failures, not style notes.
4. The two-horses rule (Warhorse). No feature without full support. If it ships, everything that touches it works.
5. Authored breadth. Every shipped piece has a spec, an author and a verifier. Procedural output is raw material, never shipped content, until it passes an authoring and verification pass.
6. The licence allowlist is law (`ledger-v2/research/license-allowlist.md`): nothing ships that is not on it, and `tools/attribution-check.py` walks the tree for that half. The other half, that a new tool enters only through a decision record naming its weights licence, is a standing order that NO TOOL CHECKS: the director reads for it at every landing that adds an external tool. Voice corpora: only those whose contributors donated their voices to build speech technology, and no identifiable public figures, ever.
7. Precompute by default. Anything generable at build time ships as static data. Runtime generation is reserved for the moat (live conversation) and must live inside measured latency and VRAM budgets.
8. Documents obey the same law as code. Row caps, verified dates, decay gates. A stale document is a failing test.
9. Taste is calibrated, not assumed (D7). No agent self-certifies quality on a taste dimension.
10. Time budgets accompany taste gates. Any milestone whose exit is judgment gets an attempt or time budget at kickoff.
11. Formatting law: no em-dashes and no italic text in anything written from 31 August 2026 on; older text is corrected opportunistically, never rewritten wholesale. The only tool that applies it is `tools/slopcheck.py`, over game text under `ledger/Assets/Scripts` and as a ratchet at its printed SLOP_CEILING, not at zero. In documents it is a reading rule that no tool checks: whoever reads a document applies it, and a violation found is fixed where it is found.
12. Evidence layers by audience (2026-09-03). Every claim still carries its instrument; what changes is where the instrument sits. On AGENT-FACING surfaces evidence sits BESIDE the sentence, inline, unchanged. On JAFAR-FACING surfaces (Producer messages, the brief, the console glance) evidence sits BEHIND the sentence, one tap away, never inline, and the link is REQUIRED rather than optional: a claim with no artifact behind it may not be sent. A word cap without a required link teaches vagueness instead of layering.
