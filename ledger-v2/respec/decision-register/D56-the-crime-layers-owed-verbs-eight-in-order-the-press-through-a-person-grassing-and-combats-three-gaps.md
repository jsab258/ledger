## His framing, verbatim, because it corrects the premise the audit was sent with

**"The premise was wrong in our favour. The boss half is not thin: operation
planning, the weapons model, lingering injuries that identify a man, blood
that washes off with water and privacy, weapons with a provenance and a
disposal risk, feuds, the four-stage inquiry, rivals who summon, crew loyalty
with a ceiling set by nerve, and the rule that a man past it talks to the
police are all built. What is missing is one act, and it is the one I asked
about. There is no verb between deciding a man should be hurt and the book
recording that he was, and a job's target is a building, never a person."**

## The eight verbs, IN, in his order, verbatim

"The eight verbs, in, in this order, because the first two are the missing
act and the rest build on them:"

1. **"Order a named man hurt, through a named crew member. The man you send
   becomes a second person who knows, with his nerve, his loyalty ceiling and
   the rule that he talks when it breaks, all of which exist. The accomplice is
   the risk, not the alibi."**
2. **"Point a plan at a person, not a building: a target field for a man, and
   a resolver. Everything downstream already runs."**
3. **"Frame someone, by steering who the police point at, entirely through
   what the town believes."**
4. **"Lean on a witness to change what they say. The payment machinery exists.
   A bought retraction that the street then learns about is the gossip mill
   doing what it is for."**
5. **"Move or hide a body. The record already stores where a killing happened,
   and the gap between the act and the finding is where the tension lives."**
6. **"Sanction your own crew: dock, expel or beat. A man punished is a man who
   knows everything and now hates you, carried by the feud system and the
   talks-to-police rule."**
7. **"Send men at a rival's property rather than his person, so the player can
   escalate without spending a life."**
8. **"Have someone vouch for you. Contradictions are already detected, so an
   alibi another person must sustain is a lie with a dependency rather than a
   dice roll."**

The ORDER is part of the ruling. Verbs 1 and 2 are the missing act; 3 to 8
build on them.

## The press, IN, only through a person

**"The press, in, but only through a person. Feeding or killing a story
happens by dealing with a reporter or an editor who is a resident, who then
remembers you, and who can talk. There is no control that sets how loud the
paper is. The research was right that a slider would turn a living town into a
meter the player manages."**

D34 applies (the town has no opinion, only people): the paper is a person who
remembers and gossips, never a meter.

## Grassing, IN; turning yourself in WAITS

**"Grassing on someone, in. Informing already exists as a thing done to the
player and the machinery is symmetrical. The grass becomes someone the police
know and, in time, someone the street knows. Turning yourself in waits until
the endings are traced, since it is an ending path rather than a verb."**

## Combat, three gaps, all IN, in his order

**"Combat, three gaps, all in, in this order. Being outnumbered first, because
a fight currently resolves one against one and being outnumbered is what makes
a crew worth having. Then running away as a way out of a fight rather than a
move inside one. Then the police arriving while it is still happening rather
than only afterwards through the body."**

D24's budget line applies: LEDGER is not a shooter; fighting gets the smallest
budget that keeps it from looking wrong, and it may not take budget from
perception, memory, gossip or consequence.
