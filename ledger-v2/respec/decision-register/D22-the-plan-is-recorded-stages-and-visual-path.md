# D22: record the plan, because it currently lives only in conversation

STATUS: DECIDED 2026-09-14 by Jafar, dictated and recorded by the resident.

## The rule, as given

**Record the plan. Two documents.**

## Document one: the stages, above the ladder, as the arc the ladder serves

1. **One street that looks right.**
2. **One street that lives**, with residents on schedules, varied bodies, a
   face that moves and a voice, foley and an ambient bed.
3. **One street that knows me**, the crime loop visible in a place that looks
   right. **THIS IS THE MILESTONE TO PROTECT IF ANYTHING SLIPS.**
4. **The player's shell**: menus, save, settings, controls, the Ledger and the
   first hour.
5. **The block becomes the Hook**, with Mickey's interior, more buildings, more
   residents and a thirty-minute session worth repeating.
6. **Then the town.**

**Every stage is a step toward the pillars, never a replacement for them.** The
ladder's rungs hang under whichever stage they serve.

## Document two: the visual path, as a decision record naming the order and the reason

1. **Light and shadow first**, because it is most of the gap and shadows are
   what make a frame read as a photograph.
2. **Then surfaces**, meaning a real material library with roughness, normal
   and wear rather than colour maps.
3. **Then wetness**, because the reference street is wet and wet is mostly
   reflection.
4. **Then density of clutter** through the batch pipeline.
5. **Geometry detail LAST**, because it matters least and is the thing everyone
   reaches for first.

**D28's presentation steps sit INSIDE this order, not beside it.**

## The estimate, and what kind of number it is

**Estimates are mine and untested: six to ten weeks to a frame I would call
convincing at a glance, against one measured piece of production data, so the
record says PLAN-TO-TEST rather than SCHEDULE.** No gate reads this number and
no plan may quote it as a date.

## The obligation to roadmap-v2

**Fold roadmap-v2's phases into this or retire them, and say which.** Two plans
in one repository is how the ladder came to disagree with the roadmap before.
