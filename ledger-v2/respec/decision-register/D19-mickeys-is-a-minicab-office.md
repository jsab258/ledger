# D19: Mickey's is a minicab office

STATUS: DECIDED 2026-09-14 by Jafar, dictated and recorded by the resident.
Premise level: it settles what the anchor interior IS, and it voids decisions
already taken. THIS IS THE RULING D17 CALLED "ruling 6" AND HELD WORK AGAINST.
It SUPERSEDES D15.

## The rule, as given

**Mickey's is a minicab office.**

## Why the cab office wins, in his words

It wins **on its information room**: a screen that stops your hands and never
your ears, a book of every fare, a radio nobody can help overhearing, a yard
with two escapes, a rank outside.

## Why the pub case loses, on its own hinge

The pub's room **needs a reason invented for every quiet night**, while the cab
office **generates knowledge as ordinary business**. That is the hinge, and it
is a design argument rather than a preference: an information room that has to
be justified nightly is a room that will be quietly dropped.

## What it applies to, and the pub decisions are void

Apply it to **canon, the atlas, the plans and the Hook sheet**. The pub
decisions are VOID. D17 already voided the drink-dependent ones (the beer
store, the pump clips, the cask line, the free-house question) and held
everything pub-specific pending this ruling; that hold is now resolved by
replacement rather than by release.

## What pubs remain

**Pubs remain as buildings on the street, boarded or serving food, never
entered for drink.** They are facade and siting, not interiors to author.
