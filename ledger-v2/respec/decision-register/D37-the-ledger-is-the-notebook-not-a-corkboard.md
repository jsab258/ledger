# D37: the Ledger is the notebook, not a corkboard

STATUS: DECIDED 2026-09-14 by Jafar, dictated and recorded by the resident.

## The rule, as given

**The Ledger is the notebook, not a corkboard.**

**D12 stands.**

## What this answers

The Shadows of Doubt research audit asked whether the player's memory
should be a notebook, a list that holds any amount and never gets confusing,
or a corkboard with string, a space that shows the connections the player
drew because the player drew them. D12 already committed to per-person and
per-event entries, tagged witnessed, heard or deduced. This ruling closes
the open question in the notebook's favour rather than reopening D12 to ask
it again.

## What this does not decide

The Ledger's visual design and information density, which D12 already names
as UI work against the standard, waiting for a frame.
