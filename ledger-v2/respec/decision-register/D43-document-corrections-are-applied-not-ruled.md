# D43. Research findings that only correct a document are applied, not ruled

Ruled by Jafar, 2026-09-15, alongside D41, D42, D44 and D45.

## The rule, as given

**"Research findings that only correct a document or a number are applied by
you and reported, not ruled by me. The stale speech figure, the clock rate,
the frame instrument: those are corrections, and routing them through my phone
buys nothing. Only findings that change scope, canon or a pillar come to me as
cards."**

## The test

Ask what the finding CHANGES. If the answer is that a document was wrong and
is now right, it is a correction: apply it, report it, do not card it. If the
answer is that the game is a different game, or that canon now says something
else, or that a pillar moves, it is a card.

## What this retires in practice

The habit of writing a decision card for every measured discrepancy. A stale
figure in a document is not a decision he has to make; it is a fact the studio
got wrong and has now measured. Carding it spends his attention on agreeing
with arithmetic.

## What it does not retire, and the distinction is narrow

The CORRECTION still gets recorded. D43 moves it out of the card channel, not
out of the record: the standing requirement that a false claim is corrected
wherever its sentence appears, not merely where it was found, is untouched,
and so is the requirement to say what was wrong when reporting what is now
right. The brief still carries what the studio got wrong and corrected, in one
sentence, which he ruled on this same day.
