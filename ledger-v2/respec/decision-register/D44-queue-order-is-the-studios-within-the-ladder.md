# D44. Queue order is the studio's, within the ladder

Ruled by Jafar, 2026-09-15, alongside D41, D42, D43 and D45.

## The rule, as given

**"Queue order is yours within the ladder. Anything already on the ladder or
in the stages runs when you reach it, in whatever order makes sense, without
asking. Bring me only what changes what the game is rather than when it gets
made."**

## The distinction, in one line

WHAT the game is, is his. WHEN a thing on the ladder gets made, is not.

## What this ends

Asking him to sequence items he has already approved onto the ladder. An item
that is on the ladder has had its "should we" answered; asking again in the
form of "which first" is the same question wearing a scheduling costume.

## What still comes to him

Anything that changes what the game IS: a new system that was not on the
ladder, a scope change, a pillar, canon. And, under his standing rule,
anything that spends a meaningful share of the week, because that is a
resource question rather than an ordering one.

## The one thing this record will not let go unsaid

His order for the visual slice (the bins, then wetness, then the dusk frame)
was given as an ORDER and not as a suggestion, and D44 does not dissolve it.
An explicit instruction from him outranks the studio's discretion over
sequence; D44 grants discretion where he has NOT sequenced, which is the
common case, not where he has.
