# D27: the ambition is the end state and the phases are the route

STATUS: DECIDED 2026-09-14 by Jafar, dictated and recorded by the resident.

## The rule, as given

**The ambition is the END STATE and the phases are the ROUTE.**

**The pillars stand unchanged**: as dense and alive as GTA 6 and KCD2 within a
small footprint, with people who genuinely perceive, remember, gossip and
speak.

## What every phase plan must do

**Any phase plan that reads as a reduction of that is a SCHEDULING DOCUMENT and
says so IN ITS OWN FIRST LINE.** Not in a footnote and not by implication: the
first line.

## The thing that is never inferred

**If a phase target is ever proposed as the final scope, that is a decision for
me and a ruling, never an inference from a plan.**

This is the general form of D25's last clause, and it exists because a phase
number sitting in a plan long enough starts to read as the answer.
