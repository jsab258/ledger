# D41. Visual work is ungated, and the sheet is the judge

Ruled by Jafar, 2026-09-15, in the same message as D42 to D45. It removes the
studio's own process from everything whose only consequence is what a frame
looks like, and it names the boundary so nobody has to ask where the line is.

## The rule, as given

**"Visual work is ungated and I do not judge it frame by frame. The Hook sheet
is the reference, it is approved, and its numbers are measured, so compare
against it yourself and iterate. No director review, no written predictions,
no ruling records for anything whose only consequence is what a frame looks
like: the grade, the sky, lighting, post, materials, decals, props, camera.
Verify runs and the tests gate as now."**

## The boundary, which is his and not an interpretation

**"If a wrong answer is undone by another render, it is visual. If undoing it
means a migration, a golden file, a canon edit or a schema change, it is
structural and keeps its full review. Wetness is structural by that rule."**

So the test is not what the change LOOKS like, it is WHAT UNDOING IT COSTS. A
grade constant is visual because the next render replaces it. A fourth status
in a schema is structural because undoing it is a migration. Wetness is named
by him as structural even though its whole point is how a road looks, because
it added a material parameter, a generator node and a readback key.

## When he is come to, and it is only these two

**"When you believe the street matches the reference, send the pair and I
confirm or send it back. Or when you are stuck and cannot say why, in which
case say what you tried and what it did."**

## The instruction that stops this becoming number-chasing

**"Judge it the way I would, not only by number. The street hit both band
statistics within 0.007 last week and still read flat where the reference
reads alive, so numbers matching is necessary and not sufficient. If you
cannot tell whether it looks right, that is the second case above."**

THIS IS THE HARD HALF AND IT IS WORTH SAYING PLAINLY. The studio's whole
apparatus is built to stop claims that outrun measurement, and he has just
ruled that measurement agreeing is not the standard. The two are not in
conflict: the rules against unmeasured claims stay exactly as they are, and
what changes is that a matched number no longer ENDS the question. "Within
0.007 and still flat" is the case both halves have to survive.

AND THE HONEST ADMISSION THIS RECORD OWES: the standing rule of 2026-09-15
that the eye reads contrast rather than value, and invents differences across
time, remains true. It is why a frame is compared against the sheet in one
picture at one scale rather than from memory. It is not a licence to go back
to reading two crops minutes apart.

## What did not change

Verify runs. The tests gate. Canon, the pillars, scope and the moat stay his.
Anything that spends a meaningful share of the week stays his.
