# D28: the presentation layer is built early, not last

STATUS: DECIDED 2026-09-14 by Jafar, dictated and recorded by the resident.

## The rule, as given

**The presentation layer is built EARLY, not last, because it is what makes a
frame read as a real game per hour spent.**

## Its steps are not a separate plan

**Its steps are the numbered list of the visual slice and are NOT a separate
plan.** They sit inside D22's visual path order. One list, not two.

The list, as given on 2026-09-14:

1. Fix the exposure fault so the same camera and conditions give the same
   picture.
2. Light and weather: bring the sky down so the sun lands and the street has
   dark in it, measured against the in-house Hook sheet, with sodium lamps at
   dusk.
3. Post-processing: film grain, a vignette, bloom on the lamps, slight
   chromatic aberration, lens dirt, and a colour grade toward the period's film
   stock.
4. Camera: field of view, slight head bob, motion blur, depth of field for
   close conversation.
5. Wire wetness. It is parsed and read by nothing, and the reference street is
   wet.
6. The surfaces: a real material library with roughness, normal and wear.
7. Wear and dirt through the decal system that already exists.
8. The twelve-package batch through all five stations, onto the street as real
   meshes, with cost per verified piece on the throughput ledger.
9. Typography and the title card.
10. The cab office from its Blender blockout, through GLB and the mesh route,
    standing on the street and photographed.

## What is deliberately excluded until that list is done

**More geometry, more unique buildings, animation polish.** They are expensive
and **read as improvements only after presentation is right.**

## The target that ties them

**One screenshot of the street at dusk, wet, lamps lit, a figure in
silhouette.** That single frame is how Jafar judges whether this looks like a
real game.
