## How the work runs

- 5 and 7 Nov (his tap 8 Nov): Efficiency is a philosophy, not a wish: continuous and meticulous; every piece of work is also a chance to make the next cheaper. Look at everything: tokens, idle, handovers, rework and thrown tries, reviews, pictures, startup tools, build waits, and his time. Measured, not felt: the daily measures and the Monday audit; one change at a time, kept or dropped on its numbers; one waste noted at each item's end. The goal is falling cost per piece, each repeat of a kind clearly cheaper than the first. Never at quality's expense: waste goes, no bar is lowered. Its other half is full use of the week's allowance on useful work, no idle and no busywork, a tenth kept for Monday morning. Records and process stay under about 15% of spend, no bureaucracy; no extra money.
