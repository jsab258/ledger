# Mickey's office: the brief (item 6, 1 October 2026)

What the room is for, before any wall is placed (production/research/interior-blockout, stage 0). Drawn from canon.md: "Mickey's is a minicab office ... its information room is the business: a book of every fare, a radio nobody can help overhearing, a yard with two escapes, a rank outside." Sheila Dunn keeps the books (here since she was 22); Ron Kirby minds the door and the rank. Tom arrives a stranger, having inherited it. Content rules hold: no alcohol, no gambling, no children; tobacco allowed (ashtrays, smoke).

**The radio desk's chair.** Canon names nobody who works the radio. Until Jafar rules (his page), the blockout follows the research's recommendation: Mickey worked it himself, and the chair stands empty. A mark, and no new character.

**What the blockout must prove** (the research's checklist, in short): Tom walks in from the street, reaches every room and the yard and leaves by both exits without snagging; the camera never shows a wall through the picture or jumps, in any corner or doorway; talking to Sheila at her desk and to Ron at the door frames both faces; the book, the radio, the phone and the doors are easy to reach and to spot; the radio is heard from the bench and the counter; the rank is visible from the window; the room reads under plain light through the game's own camera.

**Where it stands.** Mickey's bay on Quay Street, 6 m along the street (X 3 to 9) and 8 m deep (Z 5.1 to 13.1), its front door at X 4.6 (0.9 m), the side door to the stair at X 3.8, the window from X 5.1 to 8.7. In the street today it is a solid block behind the shopfront with a painted card for an interior. The numbers are in production/specs/mickeys-office.json; the game builds the room from them.
