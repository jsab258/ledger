# The man at the landing

Town list 6cj, decided by the town on 29 September under Jafar's ruling that
detail is ours; he may overturn it (the twelfth sweep,
production/research/checklist-sweep-2026-09-28/NOTE-12.md). Most friends will
carry the first envelope on night one, about ten minutes in. The man they hand
it to said nothing: nothing told them the job was done, or when the next one
comes. He was just as silent if they came empty-handed, on a night with no
ask, or after telling Ron no.

He now has a few fixed lines, chosen by where the arrangement stands. He is
there from ten till one, as his routine has it. None of his lines says what is
in the envelope. They show as plain text: no voice is cast for him without
your yes, and he has no talk card.

| When | He says |
|---|---|
| Tom comes up, on an ask night | "Mickey's?" |
| Tom hands it over | "Right. Wednesday, same again." (the night of the next ask) |
| Tom hands it over, having stayed away from the last one | "You kept us waiting last time. Don't make a habit of it. Sunday, same again." |
| Tom has nothing for him | "Nothing for me? Then you've no business down here." |
| A night with no ask | "Nothing tonight. Tomorrow, after ten." |
| After Tom told Ron no | "Ron's been down. We're done, you and us." |
| After three nights away | "You had your chances. We're done, you and us." |
| After Tom told Sheila he is winding it down (once Ron has been, at eleven) | "Winding it all up, Ron says. We're done, you and us." |
| Tom tries to talk, the job done | "You've done your bit. Go home." |
| Tom tries to talk, otherwise | "I've nothing to say to you." / "Not here. Go on." / "I don't do talking." |
| Tom tries to talk, once they are finished | "We're done. Go on." |

Each line passes the content rules. The game shows each once a night; the
brush-offs may come round again.
