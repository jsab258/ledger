# When Sheila trusts him

For Jafar's page, 30 September (town list 6bz; the first hour's day 7, the
outline's Act I). Her card has her size up whoever inherited Mickey's office,
as she promised him: she calls Tom "new management" until he earns a name,
and keeps Mickey's real book back "until I fully trust the new owner". You
ruled on 29 September that she names him only on trust. Nothing yet decides
when that is, so today she never names him and never shows the book.

## One question for you

- **(A) Time and a clean week** (recommended): once he has talked with her
  on three different days (so day 3 at the soonest), as long as she has
  never seen him, or heard of him, about the place when something was done,
  never caught him lying, and is not wary of him. Whatever he says about it
  afterwards does not win her back this week. Then she calls him by name and the game may show him the
  book. A player who keeps coming by and keeps his nights out of her sight
  earns it in the first hour; one she sees or hears of at a deed does not.
  Note: in the slice's crime Sheila herself sees the window go in, from
  Mickey's rank, so a friend who plays that crime never earns her trust in
  week one and gets the day-book on day 7. And trust once earned holds: in
  the whole week on paper, a friend Sheila trusted on day 4 who was then
  taken in for the window on day 5 still got the real book on day 7
  (production/playtest/week-on-paper-2026-09-29.md).
  Built this way meanwhile. (Weighing his answers instead failed four fresh

- **(B) Only the book's scene**: she trusts him on day 7 whatever he has
  done, the same for every player. Simplest, but nothing he does matters.
- **(C) As A, and DS Ellis's pressure too** (the outline's other road): once

## What it does now (A)

- Counted from his own lines to her: only days he said something to her
  count, with live talk on or off, not the game's other records of her.
- Once earned it holds, even if she later catches him out: the book, once
  shown, cannot be unshown. It is kept with the saved game.
- The talk tells the game the turn it is earned, a turn she answered him in
  her own words, so the game can raise the book then.
