# What the town calls him

For Jafar's page, 30 September (town list 6ch; the twelfth sweep,
production/research/checklist-sweep-2026-09-28/NOTE-12.md). Canon gives one
readout of his standing: the new owner, then Nowak, then Tom, then Tommy, and
"the gate is knowing, not liking". You ruled that everyone but Sheila follows
it. Today nobody does: the game sends no name, so in talk Darren calls him
"the new owner" all week, even after "I'm Tom"; and the rule written for the
game runs on liking, backwards, so after one evening of tea Ada would call
him Tommy, which canon keeps for two or three people, ever.

## One question for you

- **(A) By knowing him** (recommended; canon's rule): Nowak once they know
  his name (he told them, somebody who knows it passed it on, or they are
  Mickey's own people, as your answer on Mickey's people knowing his name
  settles); Tom once they have talked with him on two different days, or
  sooner if he asks them to; Tommy for nobody in the first week. A step up
  never goes back down. Sheila stays the exception, and Ron's "boss" stays
  his. Built this way meanwhile.
- **(B) By liking, as the code has it**: Tom at once, Nowak for those who
  cool on him, Tommy after Ada's tea.
- **(C) Only Mickey's own people use his name** in week one.


## What it does now (A)

- The talk works it out whenever the game sends no name, and says it in each
  reply: the new owner; Nowak once they know his name; Tom from the second
  day he talks with them, or at once if he says "Call me Tom"; never back.
- It reads him giving his name only from a plain sentence of his own ("I'm
  Tom Nowak, Mickey's nephew.", "The name's Nowak."), never a question
  ("Is Tom here?"), a quotation, or somebody else's words.
- Told his name, a person holds it as the street's plain fact and the town
  passes it on; nobody makes anything of it.
- Mickey's own people, Sheila, Ron and Darren, know it from the start, as
  your answer on Mickey's people knowing his name recommends; Sheila still
  calls him new management until she trusts him.

## Decided, and what is left (29 September)

Decided by the town as (A), under Jafar's ruling that detail is ours; he may
overturn it. Four fresh reviews: the first three failed it, and each fault
was fixed:

- days before they knew his name counted;
- Mickey's own never reached Tom;
- a name the game sent was forgotten on the next line;
- common introductions were missed.

The fourth passed it. Its narrow points, left as they are:

- Some introductions are still missed: "I'm Tom - and you are?", "I'm Tom
  Nowak, Mickey told me about you.", "Everyone calls me Tom.".
- A few lines count that should not: "Tom! Good to see you.", "Nowak, is
  it?".
- On the turn Sheila's trust is earned, she still says "the new owner"; she
  names him from the next line.
- On a first meeting, the reply can say "Nowak" while the model is told he is
  "the new owner" until he says who he is.
- His name told first-hand is remembered as seen ("I saw it myself").
- Midnight counts as a new day for "two days of talk".
