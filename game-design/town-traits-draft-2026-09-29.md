# The town's nerve, loyalty and greed: a draft

A DRAFT, NOT IN THE GAME, 29 September (town list 6bj). Taken back out of the cast
file when the independent check found how much else these values move
(bribes, debts, who walks with Tom, intimidation, a street-voice rule the
wrong way round); kept here for Jafar's answer on its scope. DS Ellis's rules (the page
beside this) decide who goes to the police from each person's nerve and their
loyalty to Tom; until today the cast file gave nobody either, so everybody was
ordinary and no witness ever went. This gives each of the forty-one their own,
from their cards and canon for the named, from their trades for the rest.

- **Nerve:** how little they frighten, 0 to 1.
- **Loyalty:** to Tom at the start, 0 to 1: a stranger starts at 0.3, Mickey's
  own people higher. It moves in play (Ada's tea, a killing seen).
- **Greed:** how far money moves them (bribes).
- **Police never:** their trade keeps them from it, whatever they see.

Measured: 14 of the 41 would report a wounding they saw (34%; the research
found 29% of witnesses did), none of Mickey's own people; 32 would report a
killing.
