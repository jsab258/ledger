# Word that the police are asking

For Jafar's page, 29 September (town list 6bq; the checklist's A15.06, a
warning before any arrest, and the audible half of A15.09). The first hour
you approved has DS Ellis on Quay Street on days 4 and 5 if the street has got
loud about Tom. Until now the people she asked forgot her at once: nobody said
so, and a character who did was refused as inventing it.

## What happens

- **Whom she asks:** on a visit about him (the street's talk, or a crime of
  his that a detective takes), everybody of his day world who holds a story of
  his nights, seen or heard. A visit for a body asks nobody about him.
- **What they keep:** each remembers being asked, so in talk they can say so;
  and the visit becomes the street's news, passed on like any story. It is no
  secret of his, so it never counts towards bringing her back.
- **At his face:** whoever she asked, or whoever heard it, may say so as he
  passes, in their own words, once each, while it is news; it changes nothing
  in how they stand to him (being asked is no proof of anything he did), and
  once it fades it is not remarked on.

## The lines

Whoever she asked:

- "That detective stopped me about you."
- "Had a detective on at me about you. Ellis, she said."
- "Ellis was asking me about you. Thought you'd want to know."
- "There's a woman detective asking about you."
- "The police asked me about you."
- "I've had the police at me over you."

Whoever heard it:

- "That detective was asking after you, I hear."
- "Police were round asking about you, they say."
- "You've got the police asking questions, you know."
- "Word is Ellis was down the street after you."
- "Heard a detective's been asking about you."
- "They say the police have been asking round about you."

## Measured

On the first hour on paper (TownReach --first-hour), when he takes the
envelope every night she comes on day 5 at minute 48, asks six people, and by
minute 60 nine hold it; Sheila says it to his face at minute 49 ("That
detective stopped me about you."). On the other paths she does not come.
