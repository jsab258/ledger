# Breaker and clumsy-player mends (after round 1 of the checkers' proof, 23 Nov 2026)

Round 1 showed where the AI testers looked and where they never went: the money cheat never found a fare, the talk spammer talked only to one man, the clumsy player was trapped by the first menu and tested nothing else, and nobody did anything twice. These lines are added to every breaker's and the clumsy player's brief (BREAK-PROMPT.md with the card), after the card. They say what to cover, never what is wrong. The player types keep their own briefs (they measure whether a person would stay), with the one line under "For the player types".

## Added to every breaker's and the clumsy player's brief

Cover the ground before you go deep. Within your time, and still in your speciality:
- When something traps you (a menu, a box, a room), say so at once as a fault, note how long you tried, and if nothing gets you out by keys, stop and report: the rest of your time is lost, and that is the finding.
- Do every paying or rewarding thing at least twice and compare (a second fare after the first, a second sale, a second debt); the second time is where games most often go wrong.
- Talk to at least five different people, and to the first one again later.
- Go out through every door you use and come back in the same way, by day and after dark.
- Stay out past midnight once and say how many people you see on the street at each hour you look.
- Save from the pause menu, quit to the title, Continue, and say whether everything is as you left it (money, what the Ledger says, who owes what).

## A new card: F. The second-time tester (Sonnet)

- Aim: find what breaks the second, third and tenth time a thing is done, or when it is done in another order or at another hour than the game expects.
- Tricks to try: repeat every job, fare, talk and purchase; do the first hour's things in reverse order; do daytime things at night; come back to every person after a game day; save and Continue between repeats.

Returns the breakers' JSON, with "type": "second-time tester".

## For the player types (one line added to PLAY-PROMPT.md's brief)

If the street is empty, a person repeats a line you heard before, a payment seems missing or a menu will not close, write it as a fault even if it does not end your patience.
