## 6. The British crime drama fan
- Came for: the feel of a 1990 British port town and its crime stories (Get Carter, The Long Good Friday, Prime Suspect, Cracker, The Bill); menace in ordinary places; sharp, regional dialogue.
- Punishes: dialogue that sounds American or generic; a world with no menace; a story that is thin (X1).
- Patience: 15 minutes.
