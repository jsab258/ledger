## 1. The immersion fan (came from KCD2)
- Came for: a living, believable world (P3); period detail (P8); side stories that are whole stories (P5); choices that change what happens (P12).
- Punishes: a hollow world whose rules change on a whim (X15); bugs that break the illusion (X6); people who repeat themselves.
- Patience: 20 minutes of slow if the world feels real; 5 if it feels fake.
