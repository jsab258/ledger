## 3. The story-first player (came from Mafia)
- Came for: a story and characters worth caring about (P1); voice and performance (P10); the slow burn when it is earned (P13).
- Punishes: a story that is thin or clichéd (X1); an abrupt or forced ending (X10); dialogue that goes nowhere.
- Patience: 25 minutes, if the people are interesting.
