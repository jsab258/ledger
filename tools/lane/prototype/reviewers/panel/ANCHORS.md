# What the scores mean: anchors from KCD2 and GTA (lane task 4t, piece 6)

Each axis is scored 1 to 5 from what the frames and the log show. 5 means "as good as Kingdom Come: Deliverance II or GTA V at their best on this point"; 3 means "a decent small studio game"; 1 means "broken or empty". The anchors below describe what those games do, from public reviews and players' words collected in our research (PLAYERS.md, 9,335 Steam reviews of KCD2 and Mafia: The Old Country, read 18 Nov 2026; FEEL-REFERENCE-GAMES-2026-11-20.md). The judge is not shown those games' footage (we never download it); these words stand in for it, and the builder's own measurements of the reference games replace them from 23 Nov where they exist.

1. hands_busy (GTA's bar). 5: in GTA V the hands are almost never idle; between missions there is always a drive, a walk with somewhere to go or something to start within seconds. 3: something to do most minutes, with short stretches of walking or waiting that lead somewhere. 1: long stretches (over two minutes) of walking, waiting or reading with nothing to press that matters.
2. town_alive (KCD2's bar). 5: KCD2's people keep routines, notice the player and remember; players call it "alive, immersive, and grounded"; a witness's word comes back days later. 3: people move about and some react; the town feels like a set with a few live parts. 1: people stand still, walk into things, or ignore what happens in front of them.
3. people_and_talk (KCD2 and Mafia's stories). 5: a person reveals something in a few lines; talk sounds like that person and changes what happens next. 3: talk is clear and fits, but most of it is information, not people. 1: talk repeats, goes nowhere or makes no sense.
4. tension (both). 5: a moment where something can go wrong for the player, felt in the frames (a pursuer closing, a look held, money at risk), more than once in thirty minutes. 3: one or two tense moments, the rest calm. 1: nothing at stake at any point.
5. controls_and_camera (GTA IV and V on foot and driving; KCD2's readable blows). 5: the character starts, stops and turns without lag; the camera stays clear of walls and shows what matters; a blow coming is readable. 3: workable, with an awkward moment or two (a camera behind a wall, a slow turn). 1: the camera inside walls or the character stuck or sliding.
6. period_and_place (KCD2's period detail). 5: every frame says one British port town in 1990: the shops, cars, signs, clothes and words of the time, nothing out of place. 3: the time and place come through, with plain or generic stretches. 1: could be anywhere, or something clearly wrong for 1990.
7. knowing_what_to_do (KCD2's own weakness, PUNISH 14 and 20). The game shows no map marker or minimap on purpose, so getting lost is friction to report, not a fault in itself. 5: the player finds the way from what people say and what the street shows. 3: the player hesitates but gets there. 1: the player wanders with no way to work out where to go.
8. looks_finished (polish, PRAISE 18). 5: nothing broken or placeholder on screen. 3: plain blocks and flat colours, but nothing broken. 1: broken frames, things floating or passing through, words cut off.

Content rule for judges: the game shows no alcohol, gambling or children anywhere; any sign of them on screen is a fault to report.
