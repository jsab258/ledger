# The gate's hands-on route (part 3), about 20 minutes of play

Fixed, so rounds compare. Played through the real controls only: key presses sent to the page in headless
Chromium, the game running in real time (no LEDGER.goTo, no teleports, no setting state; reading LEDGER.read()
to know where Tom is is allowed). A picture every two minutes of play and at each numbered stop.

1. New game. Read the opening; follow the to-do line on screen to Mickey's on foot (W A S D, mouse or arrow keys
   to look). Note how long it takes and whether the way is clear from the screen alone.
2. On the way, walk into one wall and one corner on purpose; get away again.
3. At Mickey's: take every talk and written scene the game offers until the cab keys are Tom's.
4. Out to the cab. Get in (F), get out, get in again.
5. Take the first radio fare the game offers. Drive the passenger there, keys only. On the way, clip one kerb or
   wall at moderate speed, once.
6. Drop the fare; see that the money is shown.
7. Park; walk into the café (E at its door) when it is open; walk round inside; out again.
8. Open the Ledger (L); look at each part; close it.
9. Pause (Escape), wait, resume. Save (by the game's own way) and Continue from the title.
10. Wait (Z) to the evening if the game allows; take the night job if offered and do its first leg.

Stop at 20 minutes of play wherever Tom is.
