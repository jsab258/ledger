# The gate's hands-on route for the one-shot build (20 Nov 2026), about 35 minutes of play

The fixed route (HANDS-ON-ROUTE.md) first, then the stops below, which touch what the one-shot added. The same
rules: the real controls only, real time, no test functions that move Tom or change the game; reading
LEDGER.read() is allowed. A picture every two minutes and at each stop.

11. The start again, looked at as a newcomer: he arrives at the ferry landing. Is it clear from the screen alone
    where to go and what the keys are? Count the hints shown in the first five minutes; are any wrong or late?
12. On foot: the watch (N), the paper map (Comma), how things stand (Full stop), street manners near a person (M),
    sit or lean (B), walk slowly (Z held with W). Each one: does the screen say what happened, and does a second
    press or Escape put it away?
13. Ask somebody the way to a place (talk with E, then ask where something is). Does a goal appear and can it be
    followed?
14. Go into two shops when open. Buy one thing in each. Is the price shown before paying and the pocket after?
15. At the wheel: the horn (H), look back (B held), a second and third radio fare. Listen (read) for a fare with
    a story. Hit a lamp post at speed once; read what the game says the car can still do.
16. Knock a person with the cab at low speed (once, on purpose). A choice should come inside two seconds. Take
    "Stop and help". Read what follows.
17. After dark: walk the quay. If the constable comes, read the warning before he is close; let him challenge;
    take "Stand and be searched".
18. Look a place over (T at a shop front after dark, if offered), then try its way in (follow the prompts). Stop
    before taking anything if the prompt warns; else take one thing and walk away. Read what the Ledger says.
19. Sleep (the cot or bed at Mickey's, by its prompt) to the next morning. Read the morning's lines and the
    paper if offered. Is there something to do on day two?
20. Open the pause menu and work it with the keyboard only (arrows, Enter, Escape). Nothing should need the mouse.

Stop at 35 minutes of play wherever Tom is. Say for each stop: reached or not reached, and why not.
